## Art, geography and reading rules

Use bold black contours, heavier outer silhouettes, saturated flat fills, hard-edged two-step shadows, crisp white gutters, yellow narration boxes and uppercase comic lettering. No painted shading, dense hatching, watercolor, paper grain, glossy 3D or copied cover typography. Palette: ink `#11151B`, midnight `#102B56`, storm blue `#196FB4`, cyan accents `#22A9C7`, silver `#C9DBE6`, near-white `#FFFDF4`, yellow captions `#FFE783`; sparse blood red `#A92E39`, dawn peach `#F3B47D`. Skin remains natural. Dawn changes illumination, not the drawing language.

Pages read left-to-right, then top-to-bottom. Numbered panel order below is the only reading order. Typical gutters are 20–28 px, safe outer inset at least 40 px. Words start at 40–44 px on a 1024-wide page, with sufficiently wide boxes; never shrink to fit. Planned pages generally carry about 45–85 words; several action pages deliberately carry less. Caption totals and longest lines must be measured in the prepared layout before calls. Maximum two spoken balloons per panel. At approximately 390 CSS px wide, all required words, tails and actions must remain legible; zoom/transcripts supplement them. A material page split or changed count returns for a plan decision.

**Geography axis:** establish an oblique west-to-east view looking into the bay: ocean foreground/west, northern breakwater on image left, southern breakwater on image right, shallow sea shelf spanning their outer approaches, ridge on the far/eastern horizon, empty harbor road behind the southern breakwater. The fixed rupture is 20 km overhead. The storm advances east from the bay. Aerial plan views keep north at the top and east at the right and show an orientation cue through the actual coastline rather than lettering. Side views looking north put west/ocean at left and east/ridge at right. Any reverse angle first re-establishes named breakwater geometry. Do not turn the two breakwaters into two unrelated ports or the ridge into offshore mountains. Exact architectural forms are edition staging, not additional canon.

**Movement and effects:** a bell-step is one straight connection between two sounding chimes. Depict one solid Solstice at the destination plus a thin straight transit stroke; no flying duplicate bodies, curved teleportation or line from one endpoint only. Normal edict planes are fixed and nearly invisible: edges are disclosed by cut metal, rain or sparks, not permanent luminous force-field walls. Overloads briefly widen a plane. Royal-hearing information appears in yellow narration, never a new technological HUD. Enemy mesh pulses may be schematic dotted structure in a spatially grounded panel, with no living face or intelligence avatar. Do not show an attack traveling through air when its source mechanism is resonance through the mesh.

**Inherited state:** every panel inherits its page's entry state, the prior panel's changes, the accepted references and the global absences. Only explicitly listed changes occur. Off-frame chimes still exist; crop instructions never change their count. All wide count-bearing views must make the surviving individual chimes countable when reasonably large; distant formations are representative masses whose exact counts are stated in captions, not thousands of individually countable marks.

**Solstice:** one adult identity; long silver hair, white dress over an articulated silver/white armored bodice, silver pauldrons and forearm/hand armor. No exposed chest gem, head tiara or outfit swap. Thumb-sized resonator stays beneath sternum armor until the final hand-contact composition exposes only the necessary small recess. No invented undressing scene. Face/hair/armor geometry locked by R1. Initial white fabric becomes wet, torn and dirty and stays so. Source leaves injury sides unspecified; edition staging assigns the scored pauldron and rib impacts to her right, the later hip graze to her left, and the failed leg to her right. These choices remain consistent, without making medical claims beyond the source. Left-hand numbness on page 12 is transient. PG-13: a small nose bleed, bruised posture and narrow graze; no exposed bones, gore or sexualized injury.

**Global required absences:** no allied combatants, living donors, donor faces or spectral silhouettes, civilians in danger panels, aircraft, batteries, helper voices, souls, talking chimes, giant ships, extra chimes, extra moon/portal, autonomous spellcasting, second ultrahuman, new combat ability or false recovery.
