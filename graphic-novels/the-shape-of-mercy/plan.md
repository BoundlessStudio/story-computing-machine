## Adaptation decisions

The comic stays within Adrian's first-person knowledge. His initial ignorance is sincere; the family's reveal is testimony, not a remembered severance. Perceived connections are explicitly his subjective visual language, never ordinary physical ropes. The second cut ends on the remembered choice; the page turn goes to morning. Subsequent escape images are explicitly the witnesses' account, never recovered memory.

The 25 pages follow the actual information and action sequence: appointment (1), intrusion and coercive bond (2–3), rescue that traps everyone (4–5), perception and family revelation (6–11), competing parental commands and narrow choice (12–13), voluntary restraint (14), pre-act consent and witnesses (15–16), Garran's irreversible loss (17), approach to the cut (18), morning evidence (19–21), negotiated stewardship (22–24), damaged orchard and final signing (25). These separations protect lettering and do not manufacture new events.

Condense the opening siblings' earlier exploits into Adrian's caption and the crisis demonstrations; do not invent flashback battles. Omit the unwarded cup's survival gag and most scattered-silver description. Preserve the supper-knife exchange, Marcus's rescue joke, his bargaining joke and Phoebe's final physical warmth. The childhood fragment contains the wrist grip and caged fox, with non-graphic distress; testimony supplies the erased intervention. Condense repeated impact beats but retain failed wards, burning orchard, ice entrapment, redirected lightning, injuries and ruined exits. Preserve every ethical boundary of the record and morning terms. The burned orchard takes years to recover; Garran stays confined and cannot be restored; evidence cannot return Adrian's erased experience.

Applicable authority: `universe/README.md`, `universe/style-guide.md`, Bellweather entries in `characters.md`, `rules.md`, `factions.md`, `locations.md`, `timeline.md`. Canon ages are Adrian 19, Evelyn 26, Marcus 23, Phoebe 16; childhood fragment is ten years earlier (Adrian 9, Phoebe 6). The family doctrine about desire is local belief, not universal magic law. No cross-story mechanics, new named figures, geography, institutions or lineage facts are introduced.
