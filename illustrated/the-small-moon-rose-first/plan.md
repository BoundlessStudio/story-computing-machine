### Story and visual arc

Nell severs an endangered return path after 617 days with Bash in Ravel, arrives four months before his own return, and searches through false claims until private memories reconnect them. Reunion exposes her concealed decision. Chosen disclosure and a shared boundary allow continued friendship without resolving their disagreement about returning. On Nell's first anniversary home, practical company helps her face an ordinary threshold.

Themes: evidence versus appropriation, survival versus agency, knowledge offered rather than taken, recovery without cure. Tone: frightened wonder, guarded hope, sibling-like tenderness, difficult honesty. Nell and Bash are best friends with a sibling bond, never romantic. Three station images distinguish reunion, renewed fracture and chosen connection.

## Art direction

Default drawn anime novel illustration: expressive young faces, plausible child proportions, deliberate tapered pen contours, selective crosshatching, thin restrained washes and visible warm paper. Shadows remain drawn. Reject photographic skin, painterly oil, glossy 3D and chibi drift. Camera and value grouping supply cinematic clarity without lens effects.

Palette: navy, blue, tarnished copper, muted ochre, restrained red. Ravel concentrates blue charged glass against the black thread and living hands. Modern settings use cool supermarket light, kitchen amber/night or pale morning, stone-cream station daylight. Red belongs to clothing, never a magical cord. Station warmth is ordinary, not a healing aura.

Attach `source-cover` only to the first character sheet as subordinate palette/motif input, excluding its depicted contents and photoreal rendering. Once accepted, `nell-mercado` is the concrete rendering-style anchor. Its actual image guides Bash/Celia sheets without transferring identity. Every location attaches accepted child images as PRIMARY rendering-style inputs. Match actual contours, hatching, wash edges, paper and stylization, not just palette; do not copy figures or sheet layouts into environments. No generated sheet pixels yet exist to inspect.

Use human eye-level viewpoints and coherent sightlines. Negative space expresses hesitation; no inset memories, panels, embedded prose or reference headings in interiors. Small inscriptions/tap counts never carry required meaning alone.
