### Story movement and tone

Cal crosses with Tuck seeking a heartseed for his twelve-year-old sister. Wonder gives way to a memory toll, concealed costs and a bargain that would spend his family's memories without consent. Cal refuses; Tuck returns after nearly abandoning him, and their theft leaves the seed rooted in Cal. One monitored transfusion improves Lena without curing her. Their call restores ordinary sibling affection and her right to choose. The lost token then brings the dealer to the clinic door.

The pictures follow desire becoming responsibility, compromised loyalty and fragile relief: urban unease, expansive wonder, disorientation, refusal, danger, bodily consequence, humane quiet, then the knock. Separate refusal and phone-call images protect the emotional arc. Dry humor remains in expressions. Teen horror stays non-graphic, without gratuitous gore or spectacle of an injured human child.
