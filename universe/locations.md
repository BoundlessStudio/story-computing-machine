# Locations

## The world across eras

- Status: LOCKED
- Summary: Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world are not separate planets or
  planes; they are names associated with different eras of one physical world.
- First established: user decision, 2026-07-22 (`sources/decisions/2026-07-22-universe-grill.md` Q002–Q005,
  Q071)
- Aliases: Earth; Ravel; Glass-Sea world
- Notes:
  - The modern-like Earth label does not assert that this is literal real-world
    Earth.
  - The Glass-Sea world is later than the modern-like era.
  - Ravel's chronological position and its inhabitants' understanding of the
    world's nature remain unresolved.
  - This entry establishes continuity across eras, not geography within them.

## Unnamed western bay and rupture battlespace

- Status: CANON
- Summary: An evacuated bay in the modern-like Earth era was the site of
  Solstice Evening Bell's battle against a host descending from one stationary
  rupture twenty kilometers overhead.
- First established: stories/solstice-evening-bell/05-story.md
- Aliases: western bay; bay-to-rupture battlespace
- Notes:
  - The local geography includes an offshore sea shelf, northern and southern
    breakwaters, empty harbor roads, an eastern ridge separating the coast from
    inhabited land, cloud, the tropopause, and an autumn storm.
  - Eight landing routes targeted two sea-shelf sectors, both breakwaters, and
    four points along the eastern ridge. The ridge and inhabited physical world
    remained intact after the invasion failed.
  - `Earth` is the era-bound shared-world label and does not identify literal
    real-world Earth. No city, country, proper local place name, civil date, or
    Galactic Cycle coordinate is established.
  - The rupture's origin, builders, reach, destination, and wider crossing
    system remain unknown. It was sealed rather than established as a permanent
    portal site and has no demonstrated relationship to the Glass Gate, Queen
    Street seam, Route 0, Ruth's station, or another crossing or continuation
    mechanism.

- Status: CANON

- Aliases: None
- Notes:
  - Qarsis's walls, terraces, covered storage, still fields, cisterns, and
