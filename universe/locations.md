# Locations

## The world across eras

- Status: LOCKED
- Summary: Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world are not separate planets or
  planes; they are names associated with different eras of one physical world.
- First established: user decision, 2026-07-22 (`stories/_legacy/2026-07-22-universe-grill.md` Q002–Q005,
  Q071)
- Aliases: Earth; Ravel; Glass-Sea world
- Notes:
  - The modern-like Earth label does not assert that this is literal real-world
    Earth.
  - The Glass-Sea world is later than the modern-like era.
  - Ravel's chronological position and its inhabitants' understanding of the
    world's nature remain unresolved.
  - This entry establishes continuity across eras, not geography within them.

## The Glass Gate

- Status: CANON
- Summary: The Glass Gate is a local magical crossing system capable of linking
  different eras of the same world.
- First established: user decision, 2026-07-22 (`stories/_legacy/2026-07-22-universe-grill.md` Q040–Q041,
  Q044)
- Aliases: Gate
- Notes: Its access points, directionality, limits, paradox behavior, builders,
  and detailed operation remain story-specific and unresolved. It does not
  imply a global portal network or a common origin for other crossings.
