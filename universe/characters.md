# Recurring characters

## The Daughter

- Status: CANON
- Summary: Queen Aurelia's unnamed child survived the Day of Glass as an
  infant, remains alive four centuries later, and supplies living solar fire
  through witnessed, limited covenants rather than permanent rule.
- First established: stories/daughter-of-the-sun/05-story.md
- Aliases: Daughter
- Notes: Her personal name remains unrevealed. She refused both coronation and
  political withdrawal after the second dawn; those receiving her offered
  power govern their own bounded intake.

## Queen Aurelia

- Status: CANON

  cities were destroyed.
- First established: stories/daughter-of-the-sun/05-story.md
