## Rite of Deliverance

- Status: CANON
- Summary: The Glass-Sea capital annually commemorates Queen Aurelia's
  preservation of the city through the sacred Rite of Deliverance.
- First established: stories/daughter-of-the-sun/05-story.md
- Aliases: the Rite
- Notes: By the four-hundredth observance, its official prayer had been altered

  crowds, the Daughter's presence, and the shield-tower witness gave the rite

## Morrow kept-place tradition

- Status: CANON
- Summary: The Morrow household discreetly preserves an inherited kept-place
  supper tradition through teaching, archives, personal objects, yearly
  remembrances, obligations, and table custodianship.
- First established: stories/a-place-for-the-living/05-story.md
- Aliases: Morrow table tradition; kept-place supper tradition
- Notes:
  - The practice establishes no genetic magic, bloodline destiny, exclusive
    biological access, wider institution, or global necromantic culture.
  - A brass table key symbolizes future authority over the house, invitation
    preparation, remembrance choices, and access to family visits. Giving it up
    forfeits assured custodial authority and leaves future attendance dependent
    on the next custodian's invitation.
  - Aunt Zephra helped develop the supper as hospitality: the living offered a
    place, the dead chose whether to accept, and a tired guest was allowed to
    leave.
  - Davina Morrow converted that custom into partner vetting for marriage,
    residence, and future duty. Zephra rejected both the interview and the
    demand for a verdict, and Greer refused succession on those terms.

## Cinder Annex exhibition and access practice

- Status: CANON
- Summary: Cinder Annex holds a three-day annual Aptitudes Exhibition tied
  locally to advanced-workshop eligibility and changed its access and
  collaborator practices after one furnace emergency.
- First established: stories/the-friends-i-built/05-story.md
- Aliases: None
- Notes:
  - At the recorded exhibition, collaborative entries locked three days after
    announcement. Breaking an entry's glass scoring seal before judging ended
    voided its formal score, while Instructor Bexel could separately recognize
    demonstrated aptitude and collaboration for workshop access.
  - Before the incident, lesson and emergency alerts could be sound-only, team
    formation lacked an accessible adult-supervised path, and a proposed solo
    accommodation would have excluded Irix from the collaboration criterion.
  - Bexel then required future event alarms to include light and floor signals,
    required accessible adult-supervised team formation, and required living
    collaborators to be registered as participants and asked for consent.
  - The exhibition record recognized Irix, Knurl, and Thimble as three
    collaborators rather than one maker and two pieces of equipment. This was
    a local institutional decision, not a universal legal or ontological test
    for constructed people.
  - The household and school use sign, slates, visible lamps, word tiles,
    colored panels, diagrams, and tactile floor signals as meaningful channels.
    This establishes no universal infernal sign language or access standard.
  - With Irix's consent, Talvi, Belphegor, and Bexel made an adult-owned support
    agreement providing standing conveyance, adult-dispatch confirmation,
    backup notice and activation authority for Bexel, and adult-to-adult
    failure reporting. Irix does not maintain that system.

## Solstice's historical magical-girl circles and coronation compact

- Status: CANON
- Summary: Formerly independent magical-girl circles created one bounded
  command compact, accepted Solstice Evening Bell as their first monarch, and
  entrusted twelve freely made final spells to her nonliving crown.
- First established: stories/solstice-evening-bell/05-story.md
- Aliases: historical magical-girl circles; coronation compact
- Notes:
  - The circles argued for eleven days before allowing one of their own to
    command the rest. They later faded; the story identifies no predecessor,
    successor, present membership, large public population, or authority
    outside this tradition.
  - On the twelfth morning, the twelve strongest magical girls then living,
    all adults, knowingly and freely drove their transformations beyond bodily
    survival and placed one final spell each into silver. Their souls and minds
    passed into the underlying afterlife; the crown held only nonliving works.
  - The compact's local release clause allowed the monarch to release what the
    crown had joined. Solstice promised the crown would never own the donated
    works and fulfilled the clause by permanently expending them against the
    invasion.
  - This is one technical and political agreement. It establishes no universal
    sacrifice-powered magic, consent rule, contract magic, title-power,
    name-power, magical-girl hierarchy, godhood, worship, or relationship to
    another magical-girl or crown tradition.

## Unnamed modern-like hazard administration

- Status: CANON
- Summary: Unnamed public hazard bureaus maintain one era-local `ultrahuman`
  classification and passive exclusion-zone displays.
- First established: stories/solstice-evening-bell/05-story.md
- Aliases: hazard bureaus
- Notes:
  - The category covers a living individual able to exceed a strategic
    weapon's force while retaining the will and control to choose where it
    falls. Solstice was the one reported local asset before and after losing
    her crown.
  - The category is not a cosmological rank, universal authority, or complete

  - The displayed board, evacuation arrows, and other public instruments were
    passive. Solstice had conventional batteries and aircraft withdraw behind
    the ridge, and no civilian, force, observer, or instrument attacked,
    targeted, defended, healed, powered, or advised her during the battle.

## Qarsis civic water and fair-pay practice

- Status: CANON
- Summary: During the recorded glasswind emergency, Qarsis coupled rationed
  civic water accounting with recoverable hospitality and explicit purchase
  of private equipment, professional labor, and Urumet's chosen cold work.
- First established: stories/a-little-winter-for-sale/05-story.md
- Aliases: Qarsis waterkeeping practice
- Notes:
  - Emergency water could be issued before immediate payment as recoverable
    hospitality credit at the ordinary ration price, payable in coin or work.
  - The city bought Tair Ked's sunwheel core and separately covered its full
    replacement, delay, lost earnings, recutting work, and provisions. Urumet
    separately negotiated and paid Tair's northbound commission.
  - Need did not authorize confiscation or unsafe work. Yara asked for an
    informed sale, while Urumet retained its existing two-stroke contract,
    surcharge, inspection authority, and right to refuse any extension.
  - Receipts, ledgers, prices, consent, and purchase had ordinary civic,
    economic, evidentiary, and ethical force in this event. They did not fuel
    magic or create universal contract law. The small coins, wages,
    provisions, surcharges, and freight accounts establish no named currency
    or universal economic system.

## Ersene noble-dragon alliance law

- Status: CANON
- Summary: In Ersene, each noble family holds a reciprocal public-service
  alliance with a freely choosing dragon rather than owning, commanding, or
  weaponizing that partner.
- First established: stories/the-gentlest-terror/05-story.md
- Aliases: Ersene alliance law; noble-dragon alliance
- Notes:
  - The dragon watches the house's lands and may provide rescue. The human
    family protects agreed nesting or roost territory, provides injury and
    molting care, maintains agreed infrastructure, and represents the dragon's
    interests in human court. Allied dragons are not chained or saddled.
  - At a royal succession convocation, noble families and dragons renew one
    pair at a time before the royal dragon. The royal alliance must also be
    freely renewed; if it fails, the assembled houses may choose another heir,
    while witnessed assent confirms a successful royal heir.
  - Either partner may refuse renewal. Once the royal dragon witnesses the
    refusal whole, the alliance ends in law and in its local focus. The human
    title remains suspended until the breach is repaired and the same dragon
    freely returns, or another dragon independently chooses the partnership.
  - A former dragon ally may still protect local people by personal choice
    without restoring the house's title or duties. Service also obligates the
    human partner to provide care after danger rather than treating injury as
    the end of reciprocity.
  - The royal dragon's legitimate role is impartial witness, not commander.
    The formula `Asked first. Answered free. Witnessed whole.` places voluntary
    answer before royal witnessing.
  - This is local Ersene law and culture. It establishes no universal
    aristocracy, dragon behavior, consent metaphysics, succession rule, or
    relationship to Urumet and Qarsis.

## Sablewake Covenant civic culture

- Status: CANON
- Summary: For three centuries, Sablewake organized schedules, flood response,
  labor, deliveries, infrastructure spending, and expected casualties around
  the exact guarantees of the Covenant of Hours.
- First established: stories/the-future-that-refused-us/05-story.md
- Aliases: Covenant civic culture
- Notes:
  - Exact forecasts made duplicate gate crews, surplus boats, discretionary
    timber, early medicine, trained substitutes, and live tests of mechanisms
    appear wasteful. Scheduled deaths became accepted civic losses despite the
    named people receiving no choice.
  - Nine living readers renewed and maintained the Covenant. `first reader` was
    a lowercase civic office, not a separate identity or faction.
  - The breath registry recorded residents within the witnessed sequence and
    its guarantees. Civic bells transmitted scheduled instructions and drills;
    after the breach, unsynchronized bells began communicating observed needs
    rather than promised outcomes.
  - The city's post-breach governance, recovery, accountability process,
    infrastructure policy, and future reader practice remain unresolved.

## Lacebridge civic blade-yard practice

- Status: CANON
- Summary: Lacebridge maintains a municipal-scale blade yard with instructors,
  qualified officials, pupils, public trials, recorded results, and a trial
  badge.
- First established: stories/the-courtesy-of-blades/05-story.md
- Aliases: None
- Notes:
  - The local river patrol employs trained fencers, including Jasper Renlow;
    its wider organization, jurisdiction, and relationship to town government
    remain unresolved.
  - A courtesy bout is a mundane, nonlethal, first-to-three contest using
    flexible blunted chalk-edged blades and dark padded torso and upper-arm
    targets. Head, throat, hands, and joints are out of bounds; eye and throat
    guards are required.
  - A qualified official inspects equipment, refreshes chalk, checks the
    fencers, controls spectators, may call halt or no-point, accepts surrender,
    and may stop the bout. Clear visible touches are the official's judgment;
    when body position hides the order of a double touch, the fencers truthfully
    self-call which landed first for the ledger.
  - A mutually negotiated personal stake involving courtship grants the winner
    only the first invitation and choice of venue. The invitee may decline, and
    no company, contact, career or travel choice, later date, or dessert is
    owed.
  - These procedures have only local social, safety, and evidentiary force.
    They do not create ownership, romantic consent, universal law, or magic;
    neither a stake nor a self-call compels a person or animates equipment.

## Bellweather accountable severance practice

- Status: CANON
- Summary: After the succession crisis, House Bellweather adopted household
  safeguards for Adrian's memory-costing severance.
- First established: stories/the-shape-of-mercy/05-story.md
- Aliases: Bellweather severance safeguards
- Notes:
  - When possible, an unenchanted record begins before a cut and witnesses
    complete an account afterward. Adrian will not be tested without informed
    consent, and his history will not be concealed from him.
  - The ordinary record, signatures, physical consequences, and witnesses are
    evidence of a choice and outcome. They do not restore Adrian's erased
    autobiographical memory, compel belief, or create magical truth.
  - Adrian will not cut a person's whole faculty without consent except to stop
    immediate coercion or harm, and then chooses the narrowest effective
    connection. This is local negotiated household practice, not universal
    consent law, metaphysics, or authority over other severance users.
