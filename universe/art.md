# Collection artwork

Storytelling Machine maintains the collection's visual direction, illustration agent, reference studies, and cover workflow. The source of an illustration is the reviewed story at a specific Git revision and any original references approved for that edition. [Illustration and publication](https://github.com/BoundlessStudio/storytelling-machine#illustrate-and-import-a-story)

Historical images remain with their authored story packages. Shared story facts follow [universe authority](README.md). The writing workshop preserves supplied originals and hands off the reviewed text and rating. [Package guide](../stories/README.md#visualization-handoff)
