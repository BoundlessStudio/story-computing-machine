# Premise and foundations

## The deep-time shared world

- Status: LOCKED
- Summary: One physical world persists through deep time as countless
  civilizations rise, interact, collapse, and become archaeology for later
  cultures inside one coherent underlying cosmology.
- First established: user decision, 2026-07-22 (`sources/decisions/2026-07-22-universe-grill.md` Q002–Q005,
  Q009, Q018–Q020, Q107)
- Aliases: None universally; Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world are
  era-bound cultural labels for the same physical world.
- Notes:
  - The modern-like era associated with Nell and Bash resembles contemporary
    Earth but is not necessarily our literal Earth.
  - The Glass-Sea era occurs long after that modern-like era. Ravel's position
    relative to either era remains unresolved.
  - Other dimensions and timelines may exist, but none is required to connect
    these three eras.
  - Millions of years is the normal scale for civilizational layers, with no
    fixed maximum when a coherent story requires a greater span.
  - Original sources for most surviving systems are unrecoverable; provenance
    is layered through copying, theft, trade, reinvention, and misattribution.
  - Civilizational collapse is recurrent, not a law or synchronized cycle.
    Cosmic events, natural catastrophes, institutional failures, and individual
    acts may cause or combine in any particular fall.

## Memory, myth, and recoverable truth

- Status: LOCKED
- Summary: The central shared-universe tension is memory versus myth: evidence
  degrades, is controlled or distorted, and may be recovered only partially by
  people whose choices still matter.
- First established: user decision, 2026-07-22 (`sources/decisions/2026-07-22-universe-grill.md` Starting
  point, Q029–Q030, Q039, Q042, Q107)
- Aliases: None
- Notes:
  - Every civilization may eventually become archaeology, and later cultures
    may mistake machines for temples, rulers for gods, catastrophes for
    creation stories, or survival measures for sacred law.
  - Truth does not automatically produce justice. Provenance, competing
    evidence, material context, and witnesses remain important.
  - Ordinary lives and entirely non-supernatural stories belong to the shared
    universe and do not need to advertise the larger cosmology.

## Knowledge and connection

- Status: CANON
- Summary: Reality is coherent backstage, but cultures and characters may hold
  incomplete or incorrect models and may differ radically in awareness of the
  wider history.
- First established: user decision, 2026-07-22 (`sources/decisions/2026-07-22-universe-grill.md` Q006–Q009,
  Q012, Q037, Q039)
- Aliases: None
- Notes:
  - Some characters may recognize the larger setting while others never do.
  - No universal hidden authority regulates the impossible in every era.
    Institutions or networks established for recurring use must remain
    consistent in later stories.
  - Approved stories may add to the underlying cosmology, but additions must
    extend rather than contradict its established framework.
  - The project begins with multiple independent audience entry points and no
    official flagship.
