## Canon posture

The final story introduces Redwater, Harrowfast, the Black Reed League, and
their conflict in an unnamed fantasy era of the one continuous physical world.
It does not identify them with Ersene, the Crowned Ancestors' kingdom, the
former Sleepless Majesty polity, or any admitted sovereign, dragon, faction,
mechanism, place, or event. Their geography beyond the story's local roads,
marsh, border, and trade connection remains unresolved.

King Gorm is one living embodied ruler. His scar-lattice is presented as a
personal, costly use of his own heat and exertion, supported partly by reported
history and partly by one observed feat. The scars, table, ledgers, maps,
seals, notices, ration badge, and wedding chain are nonliving and never choose,
command, consent, or act as independent magical users.

The marriage negotiation and the military compact remain separate. Princess
Sigrun's refusal is effective; Redwater receives aid through a bounded compact
whose command, supply, revenue, tribunal, civilian-protection, and withdrawal
terms are carried out. The hopeful ending does not establish a marriage,
forgiveness, acquittal, dynastic settlement, permanent peace, recovered border,
or reform of Gorm.
