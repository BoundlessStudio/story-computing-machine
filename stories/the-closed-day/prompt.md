# Prompt

[WP] “The Imperial Guard does not exist to protect the Emperor from assassins. The Imperial Guard exists to protect would-be assassins from the Emperor’s wrath.”

## Audience and content

- Audience: broadly accessible adult and teen readers.
- Intensity: tense political violence and the threat of reprisal, without graphic injury.
- Content note: attempted assassination, past executions, threatened extrajudicial punishment.
- Supplied reference images: none.
