# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Queen Clarimond has retaken her capital but cannot enter the palace. At her childhood escape wall, former nursery servant Belda opens the service door and speaks. Deliver reunion's bodily shock and the harder pleasure of meeting someone whose life continued outside the queen's grief.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Clarimond wants Belda back with the hunger of the nine-year-old who fled. She has prepared for judgments from the dead, not another person's present. Belda wants to see the girl herself, discover whether exile contained anything good, and be recognized beyond her last terrible afternoon of service.
- Counterforce or complication: Clarimond offers to restore their former arrangement. Belda has a grown daughter, a household, and no wish to live in the palace again. Their escape contains an uncomfortable truth: after telling the child to run, injured Belda called her back in panic. Neither fits Clarimond's remembered sacrifice.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Clarimond. Let sound and involuntary physical responses precede interpretation. She remembers fleeing but never witnessed Belda's death; survival and subsequent life must come from Belda. No retrospective omniscience about the coup or war.
- Governing movement and time shape: Approximately an hour after surrender. Stay mostly at the wall and open doorway; brief sensory incursions of childhood interrupt present speech. Recognition expands into awkward conversation, contracts around the escape, then makes room for present attachment. The climax occurs outside. Entering the palace is a short consequence, not a ceremonial procession.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Entirely ordinary survival. Officers manage the secured palace and settled surrender. No new emergency forces emotional progress. Belda was rescued, concealed her former employment, and later married. Clarimond's whereabouts were secret until her claim became public eleven months ago; wartime contact then failed.
- Dialogue promise: Familiar affection made awkward by age, rank, guilt, and seventeen missing years. Allow impatience, failed reassurance, embarrassment, pleasure, and a little unplanned humor. Recognition does not make either speaker wise.
- Dialogic medium: The voice through a creaking door, face-to-face speech, misjudged closeness, hesitant touch, and unfinished answers. Silence changes who can continue without automatically signaling agreement.
- Dialogue engine: Each answer unsettles the identity the other expected. Questions shift from proving survival and obtaining absolution toward learning who remained alive and asking for affection without assigning a lifelong role.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Attend to the inconvenience of armored grandeur: stiff knees, caught fabric, a mouth too dry for a name. Present sensations interrupt rehearsed royal composure; memory arrives as short, incomplete bodily particulars.
- Conversational texture: Let a halting reunion acquire the uneven ease of old acquaintances. Belda supplies ordinary, sometimes rambling particulars. Clarimond can command fluently but asks personal questions badly. Keep practical speech available without turning the encounter into work.
- Rhetorical ownership: Clarimond organizes memory into debts and verdicts; these are her habits, not narrative truth. Belda remembers people, discomforts, and absurd social incidents. She may enjoy royal attention while resisting its implications. Affection permits irritation; neither monopolizes insight or humor.
- Pressure behavior: Clarimond becomes formally generous when afraid of rejection, then abruptly plain. Belda initially fills pauses and reaches for old habits; discussing her frightened call makes her hesitate and listen instead.
- Relationship movement: Clarimond wants proof she is still loved but offers employment. Belda wants access to the adult's missing years but fears intruding on a queen. Declining the old role, admitting fear, and receiving an unadorned request for closeness make a new relationship possible.
- Anti-default: Avoid the unflappable old servant who diagnoses a traumatized ruler, delivers forgiveness, and ushers her toward a better reign.

## Beats

1. Clarimond rests against warm, ivy-covered brick, crowned, sword lowered. Her escort waits out of earshot. The main entrance has defeated her; this door carries the memory of escape. Its creak and Belda's familiar voice precede explanation.
2. Recognition is physical and imperfect. Age has altered Belda's face; armor obstructs their first approach. Belda is startled herself. She came when surrender made the streets safe; former colleagues admitted her through the kitchens. Hearing the queen had slipped away, she tried the childhood refuge.
3. Establish the gap economically. Servants freed Belda's injured leg after raiders moved on. Clarimond was secretly taken abroad, believing the abandoned woman could not have survived. Belda hid her nursery service during reprisals and built another household. Two letters after the public claim went unanswered; neither knows where they stopped. Avoid an investigative subplot.
4. Let actual pleasure precede the difficult turn: a familiar mannerism, astonishment at height, Belda's unsolicited family news. Clarimond proposes rooms and a permanent place beside her. Belda's refusal is personal and slightly awkward; she came to see Clarimond and will return to her own home. The offer has revealed what the queen cannot ask.
5. Clarimond finally brings up hearing Belda call while she ran. Preserve the fact she fled from terror, not obedient heroism. Belda remembers calling: pain and fear briefly overwhelmed the instruction she had given. Let that admission cost Belda something. Neither supplies a general argument about what children owe adults. Clarimond must encounter Belda's frightened humanity as well as her survival.
6. Belda also needs something answered. Through tentative questions, discover an ordinary pleasure from Clarimond's exile, not merely battles endured or service performed. Clarimond begins answering as someone with a life. Allow a direct request for closeness and a sustained, mutually wanted embrace whose awkward armor remains real. Do not script reconciliation or certify that guilt has vanished.
7. They part with attachment continuing outside palace service. Keep the final exchange personal, vulnerable, and specific to their renewed relationship. Clarimond crosses the little doorway voluntarily after the emotional resolution; stop before throne room, proclamation, or explanation of the threshold.
