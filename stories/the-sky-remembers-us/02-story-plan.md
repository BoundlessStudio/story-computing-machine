# Story plan

## Premise and promise

In the modern-like low-magic trough, one coastal Earth government reluctantly permits a finite reconnection pilot proposed by a bounded repair compact from the wider magical universe. Naomi Basto, the government's ecological safety lead, believes measurable thresholds can make contact governable. Minister Ochoa expects the test to justify a moratorium; Veyru, the compact's living envoy, expects it to establish a duty to repair Earth's ancient wartime severance.

The eighteen-minute pilot verifies the severance without claiming Earth was ever literally empty of magic or that one cosmic system explains every anomaly. It also fails materially: living marsh ecology carries the restored practice beyond the engineered boundary and jams flood gates. Naomi must reject both Ochoa's lethal sterilization order and Veyru's unauthorized plan to widen the connection. Her risky Earth-side shutdown establishes a third course—limited, reversible reconnection under local authorization, public evidence, ecological review, mutual stop authority, and shared accountability. The crisis and political standoff resolve; Earth is neither globally transformed nor returned to isolation.

## Story controls

- **Length/shape:** Approximately 3,400 words, seven causal scenes, complete short story.
- **POV/tense:** Close third through Naomi, an Earth-based adult; past tense; no access to other minds.
- **Genre/tone/rating:** Contemporary political science fantasy; tense, humane, materially specific, ultimately hopeful; general adult/YA crossover; non-graphic peril.
- **Ending:** Procedural hope earned because government and restorers accept narrower power, not because risk vanishes.

### Compact story-specific style profile

- Filter attention through Naomi's work: flow, thresholds, wetland health, failure paths, and who bears risk. Use lucid procedural prose with dry impatience; move closest during her bodily contact with the pilot.
- Draw images from tide marks, salt in valves, root mats under dark water, strained breath, and pressure released through several small gates. Describe magic through temperature, labor, conductive sap, mineral growth, instrument drift, and altered sky color before terminology.
- Keep political/technical exposition contested and useful to immediate decisions. Dialogue runs on incompatible aims and tactic changes, not orderly explanation, policy slogans, villain speeches, prophetic riddles, or crisis banter.
- Allow denser procedural pacing during the failure for spatial clarity. Use only a few lyrical images at connection and shutdown; end on changed action/image, not a thematic summary.

## Character engine

- **Naomi Basto — protagonist:** Earth systems ecologist who helped restore the pilot marsh. **Want:** make contact governable, protect the low district, and retain power to stop bad science. **Need:** learn that accountability is not identical to institutional control or an inert kill switch; she must expose evidence, accept personal cost, and distribute authority. **Arc:** certifier of a proceed/halt binary -> witness that both sides have predetermined conclusions -> dissenter who rejects sterilization and unilateral expansion -> designer of a limited shared framework. Her career remains under review.
- **Minister Ochoa — government counterforce:** Has lawful authority over the island, flood works, and emergency response, not all Earth or every anomaly. Wants local veto, enforceable liability, and protection from ecological transformation and unequal exposure. Her risk forecast proves accurate. She orders the rehearsed destructive remedy, then earns her place in the resolution by changing from command to conventional crisis coordination when Naomi offers evidence of another path.
- **Veyru — living envoy/bridge:** Adult representative of one repair compact, not a god, sovereign, or universal regulator. Veyru and a finite remote chorus supply every magical action; inert apparatus only shapes it. Wants to discharge inherited responsibility before compact support collapses, but has incomplete records, cannot bind every wider-world actor, and pays measurable metabolic cost. Veyru moves from moral urgency to admitting bounded authority and following an Earth-shaped halt.

### Major-speaker dialogue profiles

- **Naomi Basto:** Wants usable numbers and the next action; short questions, conditional clauses, hydraulic/ecological diction. Hides how personally she takes risk to the marsh and residents. Resistance makes her blunt, then decisive. Rare dry humor when safe; no polished ethical summary.
- **Minister Ochoa:** Wants an identifiable responsible party; complete formal statements that turn uncertainty into jurisdiction, permission, or liability. Hides fear behind office language. Resistance moves her from narrowing the question to terse imperatives, then exact bargaining after the alternative works. Never claims every government agrees or rants about hating magic.
- **Veyru:** Wants recognition of repair as obligation; considered, concrete biological and temporal comparisons, with pauses caused by effort. Hides the compact's political impatience. Resistance moves Veyru from history, to admission of ignorance, to practical bargain. No oracle fragments or universal theory of magic.

## Causal arc

1. The compact refuses permanent silence but cannot safely attempt a large opening without data, so the government accepts Naomi's finite pilot.
2. Independent Earth geology corroborates Veyru's damaged record, proving severance while disproving any easy claim to present authority.
3. Reciprocal stop terms permit activation; a calibration response seeds that Earth life may act, not merely receive.
4. The pilot establishes real reconnection, then living marsh networks defeat its hard boundary and endanger flood infrastructure.
5. Sterilization and unauthorized expansion leave Naomi no neutral position; she makes evidence public and becomes a local living operator.
6. Divided agency—Naomi shaping, Veyru powering, Ochoa coordinating—ends the physical failure.
7. Public proof that the third method worked makes a limited charter possible and turns one controlled sky signal into the ending payoff.

## Setup and payoff

| Setup | Payoff |
| --- | --- |
| Conventional shutters cannot be certified as a magical kill switch. | The halt requires living Naomi and Veyru plus Ochoa's conventional response. |
| Core and damaged archive provide independent evidence. | War/severance are verified without making restorers omniscient or sovereign. |
| Calibration shows pressure seeking several living paths; staged shutters and three basins are mapped. | The ecology crosses the boundary; Naomi later uses those paths for prepared, not improvised, contraction. |
| Ochoa predicts infrastructure transformation and retains biocide. | Gates jam, validating the risk while leaving the destructive remedy contestable. |
| Public telemetry is reduced to an automatic safety feed. | Naomi triggers it, enabling evacuation and preventing either side from rewriting the failure. |
| Ochoa's jurisdiction and Veyru's compact are both bounded. | The final charter is reciprocal and local, resolving this conflict without deciding for Earth. |

## Canon constraints

- Earth is the modern-like name for the same physical world as other era-bound labels; Ravel and the Glass-Sea world are not separate external planes.
- Earth became effectively magicless in ordinary life, never literally empty. Do not erase existing exceptions or connect the pilot to named canon persons, crossings, anomalies, or systems.
- War, severance, compact, government, archive, pilot, and effects are bounded inventions; none explains every magical, technological, divine, or anomalous system.
- Veyru and the remote chorus open contact. Naomi and living marsh ecology can shape it only while connected. Archive, pylons, shutters, sensors, pumps, and gates remain non-agentic tools.
- Magic/technology are speaker categories, not a single objective taxonomy. Describe observable action.
- Veyru's compact has incomplete records, finite power, and no sovereignty. Ochoa represents one jurisdiction, not every Earth government or an existing hazard institution.
- Consent/public authorization govern this pilot ethically and politically but are not universal magical fuel. Personhood and consequences remain meaningful.
- Effects stay local, finite, costly, and partly reversible; the ending preserves the physical world and later chronology.
- Maintain close-third Naomi, past tense, non-graphic peril, humane complexity, and complete resolution.

## Proposed inventions

- Ancient cosmic war and a defensive Earth severance, with belligerents, date, motives, and full moral accounting unknown.
- Damaged compact archive plus independently dated basalt scar and diagnostic pattern that corroborate the severance.
- One bounded repair compact with incomplete knowledge, internal limits, inherited responsibility, and no Earth sovereignty.
- Veyru's living bridge physiology, finite remote chorus, and non-agentic focusing pylons.
- One unnamed coastal jurisdiction, research island, restored salt marsh, flood gates, and low district.
- A local ecological failure in which living roots/biofilm borrow conductive mineral practice and jam gates; not a rule for all Earth life.
- Naomi's temporary Earth-side operator method, which ends with contact and grants no permanent general power.
- The strictly local reconnection charter described in Scene 7.

## Failure modes to watch

- Do not caricature Ochoa: her narrow authority, forecast, and duty are real. Do not make Veyru omniscient or let responsibility become sovereignty.
- No inert device independently casts, decides, speaks, or terminates magic. No literal-total-magiclessness claim, universal origin, or merger with existing systems.
- Keep evidence contested and useful to decisions, not a lore lecture. Keep failure causal and spatially clear: living spread -> mineral accretion -> jammed gates -> rising water -> costly options.
- Seed Naomi's calibration contact, ecological expertise, basins, shutters, public feed, biocide, and widening proposal before the climax. No chosen-one power or effortless mastery.
- Dialogue must preserve speaker-specific aims, tactic changes, unequal fluency, and imperfect listening; no policy recital or crisis banter.
- Naomi's suspension/review remains possible. The charter stays local and provisional. End on the timed contour, not global transformation or thematic summary.
