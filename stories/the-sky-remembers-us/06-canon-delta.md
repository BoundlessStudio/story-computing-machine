## Canon posture

The final story occurs during the modern-like low-magic trough, but it does not
place its events before, after, or during any other modern-like story. `Earth`
is the viewpoint culture's era-bound name for the one shared physical world,
not a separate planet or plane from Ravel or the Glass-Sea era. The ancient war
predates every present government and the repair compact, but its date,
belligerents, decision-makers, and complete moral history remain unknown.

Earth's severance made sustained connection absent from ordinary life; it did
not remove every impossible person, incident, survival, anomaly, or crossing.
The severance and repair described here are not the source or explanation of
every magical, technological, divine, anomalous, portal, or cross-era system.

No character, location, faction, relic, crossing, practice, or incident from
prior canon is named or connected to this story. The repair compact and the
coastal government are new bounded parties, not aliases for existing
institutions. Similarities of light, geometry, contact, or crossing establish
no shared mechanism with prior canon.
