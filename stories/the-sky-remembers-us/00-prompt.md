## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] Long ago, the Earth was part of the greater magical universe, but were cut off due to a cosmic war, leaving them magicless. Recently, the magical universe has started to take action in restoring the Earth back to its former glory, much to the dismay of the government.**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Sky Remembers Us
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person through one Earth-based protagonist
- Tense: Past
- Tone and genre: Contemporary political science fantasy; tense, humane, and ultimately hopeful
- Audience/content rating: General adult/young-adult crossover; non-graphic peril
- Required elements: Earth was severed from a greater magical universe during an ancient cosmic war; the severance left Earth effectively magicless; representatives or forces of that universe are now actively restoring Earth's connection; an Earth government resists; the conflict reaches a complete short-story resolution
- Prohibited elements: None explicitly requested

## Assumptions

- Treat "the government" as one relevant Earth government or international authority rather than requiring every government on Earth to hold an identical position.
- Let government opposition arise from credible concerns about sovereignty, public safety, and uncontrolled transformation.
- Treat the magical universe's restoration as ethically complicated and materially consequential rather than automatically benevolent.
- Introduce no user-requested canon promotion; the completed story remains a non-canon candidate.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
