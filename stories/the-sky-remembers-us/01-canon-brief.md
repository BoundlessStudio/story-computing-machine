## Canon finding

The prompt can be planned without a retcon. Its required facts fit current authority if:

1. “Earth” is the modern-like era-bound name for the one shared physical world.
2. “Effectively magicless” means magic was absent from ordinary life and publicly treated as myth, not that no magic, magical person, or relic crossing existed.
3. The “greater magical universe” is a bounded community, network, or reachable wider cosmos, not a separate plane containing Ravel or the Glass-Sea world.
4. The ancient severance and present restoration are not declared to be the single origin or explanation of every magical, technological, divine, anomalous, or crossing system.
5. Restoration still requires living magical agency; nonliving devices may store, focus, transmit, or shape it but cannot independently use it.

No current authoritative source establishes the ancient cosmic war, the severance mechanism, the restoring coalition, its authority, or the resisting government. These are omissions available for bounded story invention, not pre-existing canon and not contradictions.

- One fictional, unnamed, or narrowly defined Earth government or international authority.
- A bounded government office created for the restoration crisis.

## Planning guidance

- Place the story in the modern-like low-magic trough, at the onset of an unprecedented public restoration.
- Use “Earth” as the viewpoint culture’s era-bound name without deciding whether the setting is literal real-world Earth.
- Let the protagonist initially understand “magic,” “technology,” and “cosmic war” through institutional language, then expose the limits of those categories through concrete evidence.
- Establish the severance through at least two independent evidence streams or one directly witnessed mechanism plus corroborating record. Avoid granting one envoy’s account automatic truth.
- Preserve existing magical exceptions by describing them as survivals, leaks, isolated living lineages, relic crossings, or other bounded exceptions only if the story itself supplies evidence. Do not assign a common origin to named canon systems.
- Give the resisting authority a defensible risk model: uncontrolled transformation, infrastructure failure, biological change, jurisdiction, unequal exposure, hostile follow-through, or inability to reverse the process.
- Give the restorers bounded knowledge and competing obligations. They may be responsible for repair without possessing sovereignty over Earth.
- Resolve the immediate political and material crisis through character choice and accountable terms, not through an omnipotent reveal or the automatic moral superiority of either side.
- Keep the global restoration incomplete enough to preserve future storytelling space, while making the story’s central decision and immediate danger conclusively resolved.
- Maintain the prompt’s hopeful ending through negotiated agency, shared evidence, or a deliberately limited restoration step rather than cost-free transformation.
