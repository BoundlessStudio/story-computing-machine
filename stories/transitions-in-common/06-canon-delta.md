## New characters or character facts

- **TIC-01 — Cody (proposed).** **Reusable fact:** Cody is the first-person
  narrator and a student who uses the stable name `Cody` and they/them pronouns in
  every body. Their school records and usual public presentation use one
  practiced masculine body; in private they can also choose practiced feminine
  and mixed human bodies. Masculine, feminine, and mixed bodies and clothing
  choices can each feel good to them, while their inward gender remains
  questioning or unlabeled; they consider `genderfluid` without adopting a
  final label. **Exact local scope:** Cody during the Friday-to-Monday incident
  in this story. **Constraints:** No body, clothing choice, voice, school record,
  ability, or pronoun mechanically proves Cody's gender; none of their forms is
  an original, truest, separate, or less accountable person. The story does not
  establish Cody's family name, assigned sex, origin, ability source, species,
  medical history, or relationship to another transformation mechanism.
  **Dependencies:** TIC-03 and TIC-07 through TIC-17. **Proposed disposition:**
  `PROPOSE ADD WITH IDENTITY-AND-LOCALITY QUALIFIERS`.
  **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/characters.md`.

- **TIC-02 — Hugo (proposed).** **Reusable fact:** Hugo is a student and
  transgender boy who uses the stable name `Hugo` and he/him pronouns. He

  PRIVACY-AND-NONUNIVERSAL QUALIFIERS`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/characters.md`.

- **TIC-03 — Cody and Hugo's repaired friendship (proposed).** **Reusable fact:**
  Cody and Hugo are close school friends. Their friendship is ruptured

  **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.

- **TIC-11 — Cody's full-change cost and cooldown (proposed).** **Reusable fact:**

  **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
