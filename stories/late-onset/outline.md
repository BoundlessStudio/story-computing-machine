# Late Onset — provisional direction

The intended pleasure is a woman with an established, liked adult life becoming a spectacular beginner in a culture that thinks beginners are fourteen. The tone is wry, bodily, and increasingly exhilarating; the scale grows from an alarming accident to an airborne rescue over the city. The central relationship is between fifty-three-year-old Delia Moss and Joss Bell, a nineteen-year-old professional hero who knows powers and has little practice teaching someone who can refuse him. The anticipated payoff is Delia making a consequential rescue possible, then choosing an adventurous use of the same power for herself. She does not have to despise her ordinary life to want the next part.

**Explicit:** mutation-associated powers normally emerge during puberty; the protagonist thought that window had passed and discovers otherwise during menopause. **Interpretation:** the reversal should be more than a hot-flash joke. An older beginner has accumulated preferences, adult authority, and an existing life that complicate the superhero-origin machinery. No particular power, relationship, scale, tone, or ending was requested. General is a starting intensity target, not a restriction on the action.

Provisional form: first-person past tense, roughly 3,000–4,000 words. Revised power: Delia can turn gravity off or choose its direction, at ordinary strength, for one discrete object she keeps touching, including herself, other people, and loose contents inside a vehicle. She can select herself alone instead; clothes travel with their wearer. Letting go restores ordinary gravity. She changes acceleration, not existing speed: stopping requires a deliberate opposite pull, and turning gravity off leaves an object drifting. Menopause is the unexpected activation window, not an emotional switch or something she must cure. Voluntary practice while cool and an ordinary flush that changes nothing will establish this distinction in events. The story will name menopause clearly without explaining real physiology.

- **A late first incident.** Returning from an appointment where her symptoms have been named as menopause, Delia rests a hand against a stopped bus while a sudden flush passes. The bus and its passengers rise a short distance; Delia is on the pavement outside. Her alarm breaks contact and the bus drops onto its tyres. Her handbag and everyone outside remain under ordinary gravity. Her positive mutation screening at thirteen had produced nothing. Relief had been real: teenage heroes were recruited into public lives before they could sensibly choose them. Her ordinary life has been liked and chosen, not wasted.
- **Beginner, with terms.** The agency has expertise and induction materials aimed entirely at children. Joss, a flyer who can carry one adult but cannot lift a vehicle, teaches her in a practice hangar near the ridge cable-car route. A weighted trolley establishes that its contents share its altered gravity. Delia sends it too fast toward a wall by expecting no gravity to mean no motion; opposite acceleration brakes it before the crash mats. Trying the power on herself introduces real vertigo. She asks for risks and declines publicity; he respects her authority after an awkward start, and she respects his flight expertise. Her booked six-week-away walking holiday across the ridge country is specific, wanted, and briefly uncertain because she cannot yet trust her own balance and control. Her adult daughter has her own plans; no family crisis must be solved before Delia may go.
- **She chooses the emergency.** A storm gust damages the cable-car hangers above the closed open-air arena beside the hangar. One occupied car hangs from its last emergency sling, roughly ninety metres above the field. Joss goes to it; Delia sees from his radio report that he cannot empty it before the sling fails and offers her demonstrated ability to remove its weight. He returns for her because she has a possible intervention, not because she is owed a lift. He puts her through its open service hatch. She secures her bare hand to an interior rail with a strap. The sling parts before she can cancel gravity; the car is wholly detached, with no surviving tether to confuse what she controls.
- **Steer, brake, land.** Delia selects the whole car and its contents. An upward pull first slows its fall; stopping consumes more height. She wants to cancel the pull too early because she feels weightless, and Joss shows her that the station roof is still rising past the window: they are still descending. He calls when the vertical motion reaches zero. Cancelling gravity now keeps its height but does not stop the wind from carrying it toward the arena grandstand. Delia must turn it toward the empty field, then reverse the pull early enough to brake its sideways movement, continually correcting for gusts. The same trolley mistake is now lethal. Shared gravity leaves Delia and the other occupants floating together rather than flinging them individually into walls; Joss gets people into seats/restraints, directs Delia against a fixed skyline, and calls distances while ground responders clear the landing space. Her strap preserves skin contact during weightlessness; her concentration and his concrete observations remain necessary. She then allows a slow descent, brakes it with upward pull, and restores normal gravity only when the car is on the ground. No passengers need ballistic launches or repeated catches; the emptied vehicle is safely landed too. Their complementary skills make the entire rescue possible.
- **A next life she chooses.** After the consequences and further practice, Delia keeps her walking holiday and adds a desire she did not have when she booked it. She negotiates instruction rather than accepting a heroic identity by default. End on a supervised flight on her own power toward a ridge or view she chooses for pleasure, with Joss as knowledgeable company. She can refuse public service and still choose to help; she can value her old plans and acquire an appetite for something new. The ending's action, rather than a speech, should carry this.

The gravity power replaces motion storage after the first discussion: the discarded passenger launches required unsafe mass ratios, a growing gravitational reserve, and an unresolved car destination. The cleaner rule keeps braking and contact binding while making the rescue possible to picture. Protect Delia's pleasure and choices from being consumed by either symptom comedy or endless duty. The direction remains provisional; the prose may discover a better route.
