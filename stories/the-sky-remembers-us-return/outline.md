# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **The Sky Remembers Us** is the opposite bookend to `NO MORE MAGIC`. Ages after the six systems end completely, their extinction-light reaches a living, magical realm beyond the sky—an adjacent dimension, not a second physical shared world. Keshen can reach toward the sealed world but not enter; magicless Dalo can answer but not complete contact. Their incomplete acts meet, originating reciprocal magic and a material awakening across both sides.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Dalo wants inherited loss to open onto wonder and wants younger sibling Miraq's trust. Miraq hides fear of losing Dalo behind mockery. Keshen wants the ancient light's people to answer without commanding their risk.
- Counterforce or complication: Extinction was absolute; the sides initially share neither language nor causality, and every one-sided attempt fails. A link shares possibility and consequence and cannot be opened, altered, or closed unilaterally. Nobody may accept for another.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Braided close third past: Seyathi briefly, then Dalo dominant with shorter Keshen passages. Each knows one sky; Miraq remains external. Embodied relays provide global scale, never dashboards. Ancient light reveals gestures, not motives.
- Governing movement and time shape: An outward-and-return arc: Ithrom's fall sends ordinary light outward; ages compress; one night expands through failed contact, recognition, and worldwide awakening. The decisive act stays intimate while consequences cross horizons. End with connection travelling outward from both worlds, not cleanup or resumed work.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Faris ended every capacity, store, crossing, relic, seed, and material restoration path of the six systems. Light carries evidence, never power. Keshen's unrelated living magic reaches the boundary only from an adjacent realm. New magic begins where living agents on both sides freely make compatible reaches; effect and cost touch both. It cannot restore the dead or past, reproduce old roots, operate alone, or be stored. Every later link needs its own participants.
- Dialogue promise: Sibling teasing, grievances, bad explanations, and protective interruptions meet cross-world misreading that turns from comic to consequential. Wonder grants nobody instant eloquence.
- Dialogic medium: Speech and sibling shorthand; gesture, sky images, shared sensation, displaced shadows, silence, and failed contact. Ancient light is not a ghost.
- Dialogue engine: Dalo and Keshen must learn what the other attempts and will share; Miraq must make fear legible without gaining veto. Corrections break contact, expose cost, or create compatible action.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move from bodily fall through immense compression into tactile contact. Register scale through sightline, balance, skin, animal response, and changing horizons—not explanation.
- Conversational texture: Dalo and Miraq overlap, needle, backtrack, and misuse shared history; jokes compete with fear. Cross-world contact remains effortful and misread.
- Rhetorical ownership: Dalo uses story and reckless association; Miraq owns literal objections, defensive humor, and reluctant listening. Keshen thinks through spatial images and tests, mistakes resemblance for understanding, then yields authority. Seyathi tracks betrayal; Faris tracks material consequence.
- Pressure behavior: Dalo accelerates and overclaims; Miraq goes blunt, then quiet; Keshen floods contact with images, then risks pauses. Failed listening changes bodies and link.
- Relationship movement: Dalo wants unasked-for trust; Miraq wants Dalo to stay without confessing dependence; Keshen wants an answer without demanding exposure. Contact turns fear into shared risk, gives Miraq influence without control, and binds Dalo and Keshen without instant friendship.
- Anti-default: No astronomers, engineers, or officials proving and governing an anomaly; no master switch, expert chorus, safe rollout, restored toolkit, or closing instructions. Keep magic bilateral and cosmically unruly.

## Beats

1. After `NO MORE MAGIC`, Seyathi's gesture remains powerless as she and Faris fall through Ithrom's wreck. Their angry, necessary, incomplete human reach escapes only as light. Preserve the betrayal, deaths, and perfect zero.
2. Ages pass. At Ithrom's footprint, Dalo brings Miraq beneath an unfamiliar brightening. Beyond the sky, Keshen receives the extinction-image; an answer stops at the world's boundary.
3. Dalo's answer makes no miracle; Keshen's unilateral reach makes distortion. Misread attempts reveal living counterparts. Miraq notices that every apparent gain also changes Keshen.
4. Images of Faris and Seyathi tempt Dalo toward restoration or absolution; contact offers neither. Miraq forces a distinction between wanting magic and accepting consequences that return both ways.
5. Dalo and Keshen make compatible embodied reaches without knowing the result; neither completes it first. Their overlap marks both and makes the skies mutually visible. Miraq's chosen contact alters what his link carries, without making him gatekeeper.
6. Relay choices through unnamed lives and places. Pairs produce different material impossibilities; refusals remain refusals. No old grammar returns. Bodies, distance, sound, and night change on both worlds.
7. The joined skies send a new wave outward in both directions. Leave the dead in the past; close on multiplying two-sided reaches across both sides, not triumph, custody, or an emblem.

## Continuity

- Canon used: One world persists through deep time and incomplete memory; unrelated systems may be called magic; only life uses it. A low-magic trough precedes later gods. Personhood survives lost power.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Extinction remains complete. New magic originates between the one physical world and an unrelated adjacent living realm; light is evidence only. No resurrection, copied person, recovered relic, hidden root, automatic consent, universal origin, or afterlife ruling. The adjacent realm's chronology stays unresolved.
