# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Expelled by an abusive master, an unnamed apprentice wins a trial placement with Kethra. Her kindness looks like a hidden test. During their first day, automatic assent makes a modest lesson unsafe; trying to prove their worth reveals the old treatment. Kethra's horror is unmistakable, while lenience becomes one credible choice rather than a cure.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The apprentice equates usefulness, endurance, and obedience with shelter. They want Kethra's approval but cannot admit pain or preference. Kethra wants a willing student whose refusal means something.
- Counterforce or complication: The former master stays offstage. The apprentice's survival logic makes every permission conditional and every stop a threat of expulsion; Kethra's rank and anger at the abuse can reproduce fear.
- POV, distance, and information limit: First-person past from the unnamed apprentice, sharing their euphemisms and calculations. Kethra remains external through sign, slate, expression, and action; the reader recognizes abuse first.
- Governing movement and time shape: One linear day: acceptance and a room key, an ordinary lesson, a failed call, disclosure, and the first night. Expand the workshop exchange; keep the old apprenticeship in concrete fragments. The inside bolt brackets events without proving recovery.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: In local callwright practice, Kethra shapes a low-risk cell; the apprentice authorizes it and calls its registered name on one breath. A broken or late call releases nothing but fatigues Kethra and strains the caller. Yes, wait, and stop depend on conduct, not magic.
- Dialogue promise: Awkward contact between a pupil who overanswers and a mentor using fluent sign, slate, diagrams, expression, and silence. The abuse account arrives as evidence of toughness, not confession; horror disrupts comprehension rather than producing instant eloquence.
- Dialogic medium: The apprentice's speech and roughening voice; Kethra's fluent sign and slate; shared field signs; diagram changes; contact with or withdrawal from the brass node; silence, distance, and the room's inside bolt.
- Dialogue engine: After failure, the apprentice argues they remain worth teaching. Kethra must test whether assent is free and show that refusal will not cost the placement. Each attempt changes magical risk and mutual understanding.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: The apprentice keeps tidy accounts of debts, rules, food, sleep, breakage, and permissions, noticing exits, keys, Kethra's hands, and tools. Physical evidence should undercut mild labels; loosen the accounting rhythm only slightly.
- Conversational texture: The apprentice is ceremoniously polite, too fast, and overanswers. Kethra alternates brisk sign-and-slate instruction, dry visual humor, blunt correction, and waiting. Keep mundane misunderstanding and irritation beside care.
- Rhetorical ownership: The apprentice owns bargains, rankings, euphemism, and poor causal explanations. Kethra owns spatial reasoning, expressive humor, social authority, and direct distinctions; her willingness to listen matters more than precision.
- Pressure behavior: Fear makes the apprentice agree sooner, speak faster, miss signs, and read silence as judgment. Horror makes Kethra's signs briefly rapid and sharp; she then slows, gives space, and lets action replace crowded explanation.
- Relationship movement: The apprentice wants permission to stay but cannot ask whether shelter survives failure. Kethra wants an honest stop but cannot command trust. Disclosure lets the apprentice risk one refusal and makes Kethra accountable for how authority lands.
- Anti-default: Avoid a saintly expert extracting a therapeutic confession, fixing trauma by protocol, or publicly exposing the old master. Keep Kethra fallible, the apprentice poorly explanatory, and the outcome smaller than cure or polished gratitude.

## Beats

1. Kethra accepts the rehearsed plea without vows or proof of suffering. A meal, free afternoon, and room bolted only from within feel suspicious; the apprentice tracks debts.
2. Through sign, slate, and demonstration, Kethra asks for a voice limit and reviews yes, wait, and stop. The apprentice claims no limit, refuses a break, and mistakes humor and choices for an opaque examination.
3. The apprentice assents before hearing the whole exercise. Kethra resets it. On retry, the apprentice forces a damaged voice through the call; it breaks, the cell collapses harmlessly, and Kethra bears the fatigue.
4. Expecting expulsion, the apprentice packs while defending their usefulness. Details surface incidentally: locked practice rooms, food and sleep withheld after errors, work through injury, punishment for stopping, and expulsion when their voice failed.
5. Kethra's horror is bodily and unmistakable. Furious signing outruns comprehension, and the apprentice mistakes its target. Kethra checks herself, stops only the lesson, clears the exit, and leaves key and college seal within reach.
6. Kethra shows through action that food, shelter, and placement do not depend on another call; she neither touches the apprentice nor demands more history. The apprentice refuses further magic and commits only to one night. Kethra accepts both limits without celebration.
7. The apprentice uses the room's inside bolt for privacy and finds that nobody tests it from the corridor. Preserve uncertainty and the fact that the next choice remains theirs.
