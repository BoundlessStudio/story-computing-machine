# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: On the morning Kemma turns eighteen, they leave Narrowhearth—the strongest-gravity settlement they have ever known and the place that ranked them last in every test that mattered locally—and join a lowland trading caravan before anyone can order them home. Trying to survive an informal first-day probation without attracting fresh ridicule, Kemma consolidates several freight bundles onto one carrying frame. Caravan leader Daret checks the manifest, finds that the quiet recruit is walking comfortably under exactly two hundred kilograms, and demands to know how in all that is holy this is possible. Fulfill the prompt by answering both the physical and personal question: Narrowhearth's inhabitants are adapted to a localized heavy pull that outsiders never climb into, so its weakest adult can be extraordinarily strong on ordinary ground; Kemma must then decide whether this discovery merely gives new people a more profitable way to use them or can become the first capacity whose terms they help set.
- Intended reader experience: Begin in the constriction of an escape that Kemma expects to fail, then deliver the pleasurable, almost comic recalibration promised by the two-hundred-kilogram reveal without turning it into triumphal revenge. Let the reader understand Kemma's strength slightly before Kemma trusts the evidence. The release should feel practical and protective: Daret believes the feat, but responds by unloading Kemma, checking the unknown limits, and offering paid work rather than admiration, ownership, or a demand for greater proof.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led reverse inventory. The load reaches its maximum before the central conversation; subsequent movements remove, weigh, compare, and selectively restore burdens while each comparison changes what Kemma thinks the village's verdict means. Progress comes from better questions and bounded trials, not larger feats.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: Kemma has built the escape around being useful enough to retain but unremarkable enough not to become a target. Their newly visible strength satisfies the first need and threatens the second. Daret needs reliable labor and an on-time caravan, but treats an unknown body as neither a draft animal nor a miracle that excuses unsafe loading.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: Kemma immediately wants to complete the day's probation and get beyond easy return distance. The competing want is to conceal anything that could invite mockery, forced labor, or being sent back as stolen village property. Their useful flaw is appeasement: they say yes before understanding an assignment, minimize discomfort, and hear safety questions as traps with a correct submissive answer. Their physical limit is equally consequential: lowland motion feels unnaturally light, so they overstep, grip fragile handles too hard, and do not yet know how long their adapted muscles, joints, and balance can safely manage ordinary-gravity loads.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: The primary pressure is incompatible uncertainty. Kemma needs the caravan to leave now; Daret cannot put a recruit with no trustworthy work history under an unprecedented load simply because the recruit reports feeling fine. Carried mass still bears on pack frames, planks, wagon beds, straps, and soft road even when Kemma can support it, while Kemma's lowland force calibration makes delicate freight and footing newly difficult.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Daret is a countervoice, not an antagonist. He wants the delayed caravan moving and recognizes the commercial value of Kemma's capacity, but a leader who lets a frightened eighteen-year-old conceal strain risks injury, ruined goods, and complicity in the same exploitation Kemma fled. He knows freight scales and roads, not Narrowhearth's interior, so he withholds certainty about cause while retaining the authority to cap the load. After the discovery, he still has a schedule and may offer only probation, not instant belonging.
  - Pressure or change created: Kemma initially treats every removed bundle as evidence of failure and tries to win it back by volunteering for more. Daret's insistence on unloading frustrates the urgent escape but creates the first encounter in which an adult's refusal to use all of Kemma's strength is not contempt. To keep the job, Kemma must disclose the home comparisons they have been trained to regard as proof of weakness and eventually state one limit without knowing whether it will cost them the caravan.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close first-person past through Kemma, intimate with bodily sensation and defensive social inference. Kemma can accurately compare loads, slopes, jumps, and grips but has no conceptual framework for different local gravity and habitually mistranslates surprise as ridicule. Daret remains exterior; distinguish him through ledger-minded questions, precise prohibitions, dry profanity, and a refusal to accept either boasting or self-erasure as safety information.
  - Time shape and compression: One continuous first road day, from Kemma's pre-dawn descent out of Narrowhearth through the caravan's evening halt. Render the joining, gradual freight consolidation, two-hundred-kilogram interruption, first comparisons, and private terms conversation in scene. Compress the middle miles into three concrete work trials whose purpose changes as Daret learns: carrying a normal pack, handling a fragile crate, and balancing an awkward but modest load. No flashback scenes; Kemma's remembered village judgments surface only when a present measurement contradicts them.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Give the reader early physical clues that lowland ground behaves wrongly to Kemma: the final downhill step lasts too long, an ordinary pack barely settles their shoulders, and a tossed peg travels farther than intended. Do not name the gravitational cause before Daret's question. After the manifest count, reconstruct it through specific comparisons—Narrowhearth's squat buildings and heavy well buckets, draft animals that refuse the upper track, Daret's freight weights, and Kemma's description of how the descent changed their stride. Daret may identify a localized stronger pull as the best working explanation, not prove an ancient source or declare a universal law. The village was truthful that Kemma ranked last among its adults; its cruelty lay in treating that relative result as total worth.
  - Governing movement: Leave on permission's first minute; descend too quickly; ask for any work; accept the smallest probation; find each assigned bundle oddly light; combine trips to protect the departure time; walk under the full frame; be stopped at two hundred kilograms; expect expulsion; be unloaded; compare familiar weights; repeat a modest load under observation; mishandle something fragile without catastrophe; learn that carried mass still endangers equipment and road surfaces; disclose what Narrowhearth's tests and bullying trained Kemma to hide; hear a bounded offer; ask what it pays and what the maximum will be; choose one second-day assignment rather than every available burden.
- Speculative surplus: Narrowhearth sits inside a stable, sharply localized stronger gravitational pull whose source and age remain unknown. Give the effect ordinary material and cultural consequences unrelated to social ranking: houses use short roof spans, bread rises shallow, nobody tosses tools, and the local pack stock is low and broad. On the lowland road, Kemma is distracted by domed bread and the slow high arc of spilled water or a tent peg. The field does not follow Kemma, reduce cargo mass, judge character, activate through confidence, or make bones, skin, balance, and equipment invulnerable.
- Structural distinction: A strength-reveal story organized by subtraction and calibration after an early maximum, rather than an escalating contest, rescue emergency, return-home confrontation, public technical proof, or procession of converted witnesses. The decisive work is a private employment conversation beside half-repacked freight; there is no single spectacular climax, no crowd acclaim, no village reform, and no closing image of Kemma carrying the greatest possible burden.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: When a crate handle cracks under Kemma's careful-seeming grip, they panic, offer to carry the entire damaged crate without pay, and insist they can take more weight. Daret stops the bargain and states the distinction Kemma has never been allowed: capacity is information, not consent, and an unknown maximum is not today's assignment. He offers ordinary recruit wages for a capped probation, with separately agreed pay if later load trials prove useful. Kemma nearly answers with automatic gratitude, then asks the load cap, distance, break rule, and whether refusing an extra bundle ends the job. Daret gives concrete answers and asks which of tomorrow's tasks Kemma wants. Kemma chooses one, preserving the escape while making the first work decision that is not simply “anything.”
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: Kemma has a place for the next leg and a plausible explanation for the impossible ease of the first load, but not a permanent home, a tested safe capacity, or freedom from the village's learned expectations. Their fine control remains poor, Daret's caravan is still an employer with needs, and later people may value the strength less carefully. Narrowhearth, its bullies, and its heavy pull remain unchanged; the story owes no revenge or institutional correction. What changes is narrower and usable: Kemma can distinguish being weak there from being worthless everywhere and has practiced attaching one condition to their labor.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Kemma treating the instant of their eighteenth birthday as permission to accept any burden that carries them away. End after the evening meal when Daret offers several ordinary next-day jobs and waits through Kemma's frightened pause; Kemma selects one and declines another because of the cracked-handle lesson. The opening makes departure depend on unconditional compliance; the ending keeps the road open after a specific refusal.

## Beats

1. Just after midnight on their eighteenth birthday, Kemma leaves Narrowhearth with a light personal bundle and a work slate that ranks them last. The descent feels increasingly strange—long steps, weak-seeming loads, overlong arcs—but urgency and fear of pursuit keep them from investigating.
2. At the caravan's lowland muster, Kemma asks Daret for any place at all. Daret grants one day's paid probation on unskilled camp and freight work; Kemma hears “probation” as a threat and “any assigned load” as a command never to admit difficulty.
3. A delayed departure requires cargo to be shifted among frames and wagons. Kemma combines assigned trips because every bundle feels trivial. Nobody dares or hazes them into it; the error grows from Kemma's need to be indispensable and from coworkers assuming Kemma knows their own limits.
4. Daret checks the visible tags before a vulnerable stretch of road, totals exactly two hundred kilograms on Kemma's frame, halts the caravan, and asks the prompt's astonished question. Kemma mistakes it for accusation, apologizes, and says they can carry more if that is what was required.
5. Daret orders the frame unloaded one piece at a time. Each removed mass prompts a practical comparison to home, revealing Narrowhearth's heavy well water, squat construction, non-throwing habits, and animals that will not climb the upper path. The working explanation becomes localized gravity and lifelong adaptation, not a new blessing received on departure.
6. The caravan moves with Kemma under an ordinary capped pack. Across the day's short trials, extraordinary carrying remains real while lowland coordination causes small, costly errors. Daret also shows that a bridge plank, strap, or crate still receives the full mass Kemma carries.
7. At the evening halt, the cracked crate handle triggers Kemma's offer of unlimited unpaid labor. Daret refuses, separates capacity from consent, and proposes defined probation and later paid testing rather than a spectacle.
8. Kemma asks concrete questions, accepts one assignment for the next leg, and declines a second until their grip and balance improve. Daret records the work choice without ceremony; the caravan will leave with Kemma in the morning, and the deeper limits remain to be learned on the road.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The story occurs in the one shared physical world at an otherwise undated era and location. The localized gravity is treated as one real bounded system/condition among many, with no claim that culture's label for it establishes an objective magical or technological category.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Kemma, Daret, Narrowhearth, its gravity field, and the caravan are proposed new story-local material. Do not connect them to Ravel, the Glass-Sea era, a Galactic Cycle coordinate, another village, established class/level systems, or any recurring person, place, faction, magic, artifact, bloodline, or ancient civilization. The source and creation date of Narrowhearth's pull remain unknown. It is non-sentient, does not make choices, and is not established as magic; if characters call it a curse or blessing, that is culture, not authority. Kemma's adaptation establishes no general superhuman biology, no immunity to injury, and no reduction of cargo mass. Exact and close-form searches found Kemma, Daret, and Narrowhearth available as new nouns; Daret is not Deryn Venn or Teren.
