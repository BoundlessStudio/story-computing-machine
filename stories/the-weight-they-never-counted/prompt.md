# Prompt

## Prompt

> **[WP]You are the weakest, the absolute bottom of the barrel in your Village. After joining a trading caravan the second you turned 18 to escape the constant bullying, the Leader of the Caravan asks you how in all thats holy you are lifting and comfortably carrying 200kg without even breaking a sweat.**

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
