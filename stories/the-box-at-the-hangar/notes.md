# Editorial notes

## 2026-11-21

**Codex promise keeper, request only.** “The distinctive tension is wanting what the Box could give you while knowing the exact odds of dying and having no say in which power you might get. The queue gives that private calculation a public scale.” The keeper read universal free access, a single regulated site with queue space, even odds of random powers or death by burning, and choice, pressure, and everyday consequences as explicit. It expected the payoff to show “how the choice changes an ordinary day for someone who has to live with it.” It left the lead, relationships, pressures, decision, ending, and precise rating open.

**Claude outline reader, request only.** “Someone decides, and the story makes us feel that decision and what it costs, whichever way it goes.” Claude expected pressure from outside as well as internal doubt, and ordinary consequences such as families of the burned or the newly magical returning to work. It read the hangar as an example of the required controlled site, inferred a sober human scale, and left entry, survival, refusal, relationships, and the source of pressure open. It read the rating direction as Mature intensity.
