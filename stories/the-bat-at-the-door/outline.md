# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Eleven-year-old Pippa has spent twelve days helping her wildlife-rehabilitator parent nurse an injured bat. On release night, strange-looking Olya and Corven claim he is their twelve-year-old son, Teyo, and ask to become Pippa's godparents. Turn the claim into evidence, let both children choose, define the offer, and settle departure and future contact without making either child a debt, prize, or pet.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The unnamed parent wants safe handling and proof before admitting strangers; Pippa wants adults to treat Teyo as able to answer and her impending goodbye as real. The couple want their missing son home, but urgency makes them mistake recognition for sufficient proof.
- Counterforce or complication: Teyo refuses their first family call and has only Pippa's limited care-board. A storm cuts heat to the flight room; its backup pad has under an hour. Underweight Teyo must be warm, awake, unrestrained, and willing to change, while each person holds incomplete evidence.
- POV, distance, and information limit: First-person past through Pippa's practical, protective parent. The narrator knows bat care and house risks but cannot interpret ultrasound, enter Teyo's mind, or verify the visitors' culture except through conduct. Teyo's complete account waits for a speaking body.
- Governing movement and time shape: One ninety-minute rainy evening moves by permissions from doorstep to flight room to kitchen: restricted entry, narrow questions, voluntary change, departure, then future terms. Keep the proof private and end with ordinary contact logistics, not ceremony or thematic summation.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: This family can voluntarily change between humanlike and bat bodies once between sunsets. Change requires three uninterrupted dark minutes, preserves identity, memory, hunger, and proportional injury, and spends body heat; no name, emotion, touch, or command can force it. Bat-form Teyo understands speech but has no human voice. Ultrasound conveys recognition and mood, not propositions; Pippa's learned board permits yes, no, stop, water, pain, alone, and letter selection. Establish no broader species rule.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move through safety checks, permissions, temperatures, and control of doors or touch. Notice mobile ears, light-catching eyes, breath, and odd clothing without making appearance moral evidence.
- Conversational texture: Use interruptible identification questions, care instructions, concrete child corrections, and imperfect cultural translation; misunderstandings require action or narrower questions.
- Rhetorical ownership: The parent reasons from risk and proof; Pippa from remembered behavior. Olya owns sensory family knowledge; Corven definitions and obligations. Teyo is spare, practical, and corrective.
- Pressure behavior: The parent turns procedural; Pippa repeats missed facts; Olya loses fluency and acts; Corven over-specifies, then answers plainly. Crowding makes Teyo signal stop or go silent.
- Anti-default: Avoid synchronized gothic speech, lore recital, miniature-therapist children, and polished moral consensus; let awkward verification and mundane boundaries carry meaning.

## Beats

1. Establish supervised care: the parent handles Teyo with gloves; Pippa keeps weights, water, food, quiet, and his signal board. Before his controlled release test, Olya and Corven arrive. Her mobile ear edges and dark-adapted eyes, his pulsing throat and wing-cut coat are kinship clues, not threat coding.
2. The parent separates claim from access. The couple know Teyo's age, wound, and a private habit matching Pippa's log. Their family call draws recognition, then Teyo's stop signal. Admit them only to the flight room: no touch, open exit, Pippa beside the parent.
3. The storm cuts power. Corven supplies the change limits; the parent tests them against Teyo's weight and recovery. Using one board choice at a time, Teyo confirms his name, parents, deliberate forbidden solo flight, and wish to change before choosing whether to leave.
4. Preparing safe warmth and darkness tests the offer's substance. Olya stops answering for Teyo, Corven accepts that custom grants no control, and the parent releases the carrier when Teyo refuses confinement. They combine household, care, and family knowledge; when Teyo signals stop, everyone stops.
5. Teyo changes voluntarily. His tender arm, shared features, recovery knowledge, and independent account verify personhood and kinship. He chooses to go home, asking for a medical check and food before the family addresses his unauthorized flight.
6. Define godparents as the closest English term for an optional pledge offered once when another household's child shelters theirs. Until Pippa is sixteen, the adults owe truthful warning about family-caused danger, up to three nights' emergency shelter, and practical aid. They gain no custody, access, obedience, secrecy, service, or claim. Refusal carries no offense; gratitude rests on adults, not either child.
7. Pippa chooses after private time with her parent. They accept a one-year trial, renewable only by their joint consent: no surprise visits, unsupervised outings, or hidden contact. Teyo exchanges details separately as a peer and leaves by choice. Finish while they test contact information and schedule one supervised visit.
