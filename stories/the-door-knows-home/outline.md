# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Apprentice locksmith Dessa restores the split recognition rail and weather-warped latch of an old freestanding traveling door while four adult relatives wait to see it reopen onto the house they inherited. It instead gives Sikra the room they are standing in, Lutik a cabin aboard his working mail boat, Mevia the rented rooms she shares above a bakery, and Jorren the kitchen of the spouse from whom he is separated. Fulfill the prompt by making all four destinations materially real and mechanically consistent: the repair has succeeded, but the family mistook a shared history for one present answer. The resolution must also preserve the distinction exposed by Jorren's test—sincerely recognizing a place as home does not establish ownership, welcome, or permission to cross its threshold.
- Intended reader experience: A warm, lightly wry domestic fantasy that begins with the satisfactions and nerves of a first solo repair, turns each test into a more intimate surprise, and lets wonder acquire social weight without becoming a lecture about what home means. The four openings should feel distinct in sound, air, clutter, and human response. End with a sound repair, an unsettled family, and several usable choices rather than a forced reunion, a verdict on the best home, or a sentimental proof that every attachment is reciprocal.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led four-crossing diagnostic cycle. Each relative repeats the same simple turn of the repaired key, but the resulting destination changes Dessa's working hypothesis—from dead mechanism, to wandering route, to user-specific accuracy, to the ethical and mechanical limit represented by a destination-side bolt. Movement comes from returning to the same door with less room to call the previous opening an error.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: Dessa has built her apprentice pride around the belief that correct workmanship produces one reproducible answer. She also values the locksmith's narrower duty to restore what a lock was made to do without claiming authority over the lives on either side. Sikra is attached to the old house as the family's remaining common address and has commissioned the repair partly to make that attachment portable; the functioning door reveals that kinship and a common past no longer generate one route.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: Dessa immediately wants to complete her first unsupervised traveling-door repair, earn the agreed fee, and leave a result her master cannot fault. Competing with that, she wants to preserve the century-old core rather than file it into a fixed-address gate and to keep accidental glimpses of four private homes from becoming evidence in a family quarrel. Her useful flaw is diagnostic neatness: she initially treats divergent testimony as contamination and keeps looking for the one output that will vindicate both her repair and Sikra's expectation. Her apprenticeship is also a real limit—she can restore the rail, latch, and living-current path, but cannot promise what a user's recognition will supply or safely redesign the door during a service call.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: Four legitimate attachments do not align with the family's inheritance plan. Sikra is paying for a common return route; the other relatives agreed to test an heirloom, not disclose their domestic lives. The door offers no explanation, only repeatable openings, and a user pressed to prove or disown a destination may become less sincere rather than more informative.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Sikra is a countervoice, not an antagonist. She has maintained the old house through illness and death, expects the scattered family to help clear it, and fears that without a reliable way back their promises to return will remain promises. Lutik has called his mail-boat berth temporary for years; Mevia has let the family dismiss her rented rooms as lodging; Jorren wants the door's recognition to mean that his estranged spouse must still regard him as belonging. Each protects something real, but none can make the door answer for another person.
  - Pressure or change created: Every correct destination makes Dessa look less competent to Sikra while making destructive recalibration harder to justify. Entering a revealed home risks weather damage, embarrassment, and intrusion. The fourth opening raises the stakes without widening them: Jorren's destination is correct, but his estranged spouse can shut and bolt the leaf from that side, so neither magic nor family pressure can convert his recognition into access.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close third-person past through Dessa, intimate with her hands, repair vocabulary, professional panic, and temptation to make the evidence tidier than it is. She can read wear, alignment, current, and ordinary locks, but not another person's sincerity or private history. Keep relatives' interiors closed; distinguish Sikra's language of deeds and obligations, Lutik's route-and-weather evasions, Mevia's precise plural domestic details, and Jorren's insistence on what he is owed.
  - Time shape and compression: One late afternoon into early evening in the nearly emptied old house. Open after the physical repair but before the first live test. Render all four openings in scene, with brief workbench intervals for Dessa to reverse pins, inspect the destination-side strike, and exhaust mechanical explanations. Compress only the relatives' arrival history and the repair already performed; no flashbacks or later public validation.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Establish that a living user's hand and recognition supply the current while the door only stores and shapes it. Let the reader initially share Dessa's expectation of a single ancestral destination. Sikra's same-room result can plausibly look dead; Lutik's moving cabin disproves that; Mevia's inhabited rooms show a stable, chosen domestic life; Jorren's barred kitchen supplies the decisive limit. Confirm correctness through repeated seating of the repaired latch, consistent destination-side strike marks, and ordinary inhabitants and objects—not a speaking door, infallible gauge, or external expert. Reveal only what each threshold exposes; do not make the door read memories, rank attachments, or report why a place counts.
  - Governing movement: Finish the physical repair; hear Sikra define success as one family address; test Sikra and mistake an ordinary swing into the same room for dead travel; reset the rail; let Lutik open onto his moving cabin and inspect a perfectly seated distant strike; return through boat-cold air; let Mevia open onto the bakery rooms and choose how much to tell the relatives waiting behind her; resist Sikra's request to file the route toward the old house; neutralize every mechanical variable; let Jorren open onto his estranged spouse's kitchen; see the spouse close the leaf and hear the inside bolt set; refuse Jorren's demand to turn repair into forced entry; recognize the common pattern across four apparently incompatible results; leave the core untouched; record four successful user tests and practical limits; let the family negotiate clearing, storage, and future invitations without the door settling those questions.
- Speculative surplus: A traveling door briefly admits the destination's pressure, temperature, scents, and loose sill debris before its weather strip settles. Owners use a catch strap and shallow tray; mail-boat cold, bakery warmth, flour dust, and a few rain beads make each crossing physical and explain the warp Dessa repaired. This effect can slam the leaf or dirty a floor but does not indicate sincerity, identify a destination, or solve the family conflict.
- Structural distinction: A recursive domestic test in which the same hand action opens four increasingly consequential private spaces. The story repeatedly leaves and returns to one nearly empty room, but the decisive action is Dessa's refusal at a barred household threshold, not a workshop confession, public technical proof, crowd conversion, institutional rewrite, or symbolic disposal of the heirloom. No destination is treated as the climactic true home, and the family does not become unanimous.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: Jorren's turn opens cleanly onto the familiar kitchen he has insisted is still his home. His estranged spouse, visible only briefly, tells him not to enter, closes the leaf, and sets the ordinary inside bolt. Jorren asks Dessa to pick it from their side. In tracing the load through her repaired latch, Dessa recognizes the evidence she has resisted: all four routes seated properly, and the fourth is proving two different things at once. She refuses the bypass, names the repair complete, and states only the door's bounded answer—it follows the living user's sincere recognition; it does not make the destination recognize the user back.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: Sikra receives no compulsory homecoming route and still must persuade the others to help clear the old house. Lutik and Mevia can no longer pretend their current homes are temporary for the family's comfort. Jorren must seek any return by ordinary invitation, and his marriage remains unresolved. Dessa keeps the core intact and bills for a successful repair, but her master may still question a job the client calls a disappointment. The family must decide where the physical door will be stored and what notice is required before any future crossing; no one solves that before the scene ends.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Dessa holding the repaired leaf on a wedge while Sikra asks which side will become home once the key turns; Dessa answers from habit in terms of keyhole, hinge, and expected address. End after Dessa removes the service wedge and packs her tools while the relatives argue, more quietly, about who will carry the door and who must knock first. The mechanism can move again, but Dessa leaves through the ordinary front entrance before the family chooses its next use.

## Beats

1. In the stripped front room, Dessa finishes aligning the recognition rail and replacing the weather-stiff latch. Sikra frames the test as a return to the one family house and ties Dessa's fee to that expectation; the other three relatives arrive with different degrees of reluctance.
2. Sikra turns the key. The door swings onto the same room as an ordinary leaf would. Dessa's current trace is clean, but she calls the result incomplete and resets the pins rather than contradict her client.
3. Lutik tests next. The threshold opens onto his cramped cabin aboard a moving mail boat: cold damp air, a rolling cup, and the door's distant strike seated exactly. Dessa crosses only far enough, with permission, to inspect the latch; Lutik admits that the berth has been his steady address longer than anywhere else.
4. Back in the old house, Sikra calls the route drift. Dessa reverses the hinge pins and removes every new part except the repaired rail, privately hoping one variable will restore a single answer. Mevia refuses a blindfold or interrogation and simply turns the key.
5. Mevia's opening reveals warm rented rooms above a bakery, occupied and unmistakably lived in by Mevia and her wife. Mevia limits what the family may see, returns, and shuts the door. Flour caught in the sill tray confirms travel without becoming proof of why the rooms count.
6. Sikra asks Dessa to bias the core toward the inherited house. Dessa lays out the cost: that would replace user recognition with a fixed route and could destroy the old core. She nevertheless performs one final neutral reset because she still wants a cleaner mechanical answer.
7. Jorren opens onto his estranged spouse's kitchen. His spouse refuses entry, closes the leaf, and sets the inside bolt. Jorren demands Dessa defeat it and treats the door's destination as a right to return.
8. Feeling the repaired latch take the load correctly, Dessa refuses. She compares the four tests without ranking their destinations: same restored current path, four stable strikes, no wander, and one valid local bar. The apparent failure is the family's single-destination assumption, not the repair.
9. Dessa records four successful user tests plus the weather and permission limits, leaves the recognition core untouched, and accepts whatever immediate dispute over the fee Sikra can credibly sustain. The relatives turn from blaming the mechanism to the practical questions it cannot answer—clearing the house, storing the traveling frame, giving notice, and asking before crossing—as Dessa departs by the ordinary door.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Only life can use magic; Dessa and each relative supply living agency, while the nonliving door stores and shapes that current. Portal and crossing capabilities are system-specific, with no universal network or rule.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Treat this traveling door as a new local crossing system with no established relationship to any established crossing, house, key, or recognition mechanism. The story may establish for this door that current sincere recognition selects a reachable domestic threshold and that an ordinary destination-side barrier can deny entry. It must not establish that recognition proves ownership, consent, emotional reciprocity, legal residence, bloodline, memory accuracy, a universal definition of home, or sentience in the door. Its maker, age beyond being a family heirloom, full range, failure behavior outside these tests, and wider prevalence remain unknown.
