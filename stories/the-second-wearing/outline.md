# Outline

## Story

- Title: The Second Wearing
- Premise and central promise: Sentient silver-and-enamel pendant Lustre wakes after thirty boxed years when Odelia Strake inherits it from her dead aunt Pavia. Deliver renewed contact, recognition that inheritance cannot assign a willing wearer, and a choice about leaving the box together.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Lustre craves pulse, motion, admiration, and another mind nearby. It needs Odelia's continued consent, but fear tempts it to exploit her questions and hide its part in being packed away.
- Counterforce or complication: Odelia dislikes jewellery at her throat and wants facts about Pavia, not a dependent or oracle. Lustre's evasions justify ending contact. Pavia's absence and both former companions' stubbornness supply complication without an antagonist.
- POV, distance, and information limit: First-person present through Lustre. Unworn, it registers pressure, temperature, tarnish, and vibration without sight or reliable duration. Skin contact lets it direct an inner voice; wearing adds pulse, balance, movement, and bone-borne sound. It cannot see, read thoughts, borrow memories, or know events outside contact. Infer Odelia from handling, breath, speech, and removal.
- Governing movement and time shape: One afternoon in Pavia's nearly cleared apartment: three increasingly voluntary wearings separated by sensory contraction—accidental test, adversarial inquiry, bounded choice. Compress the boxed decades; omit full flashbacks and a future coda.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Lustre is one living pendant with a contact-voice of its own. It cannot move, compel, soothe, store a human mind, or act through chain or box. Odelia may end contact at any time. Neither knows its maker or animating cause.
- Dialogue promise: A vain object-person tries to sound indispensable while an irritated heir answers something at her collarbone. Mix curiosity, bad inference, pettiness, discomfort, dry humor, affection, and grief without producing polished ethicists.
- Dialogic medium: Lustre's directed inner voice; Odelia's audible replies; pulse, fingers, taps, clasping, removal, and resumed contact. Odelia creates or ends the conversational circuit.
- Dialogue engine: Odelia must maintain touch to learn what Lustre witnessed; Lustre must become honest enough to remain touchable. Removal shifts leverage, and each return distinguishes inquiry, wearing, and companionship.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Notice weight, warmth, pressure, vibration, surface condition, and bodily cadence. Sentences expand during wearing and contract in separation. Lustre infers confidently and wrongly; never grant it visual description.
- Conversational texture: Odelia's aloud tests and practical fragments meet interruptions and overqualified answers. Let removal replace reply, with repetition, missed implications, irritation, and ordinary discomfort.
- Rhetorical ownership: Lustre owns vanity, ornamental analogy, defensive logic, self-flattering humor, and affection expressed as attention to bodily rhythm; it misreads pauses. Odelia owns dry disbelief, blunt categories, contact authority, and selective listening. She rejects grand comparisons.
- Pressure behavior: Fear makes Lustre fast and legalistic until omissions show. Odelia becomes clipped and removes it instead of winning verbally; she later resumes contact before either achieves fluency.
- Relationship movement: Lustre wants wearing but cannot ask not to be abandoned. Odelia wants an unsentimental account of Pavia but cannot admit one minor memory matters. Evasion damages trust; informed choice earns only a timed wearing and open storage.
- Anti-default: No wise heirloom, stored dead woman, family-secret archive, or instantly adopted conscience. Lustre remains a fallible separate person; need never overrides bodily consent, and its origin and future stay open.

## Beats

1. Compress the boxed decades into stale pressure, fabric fibers, transport, then sorting hands. Odelia reads the estate label and tries the pendant against her skin. A new pulse and Lustre's urgent greeting make her wrench it off.
2. Through brief touches and deliberate gaps, Odelia tests the voice. Lustre learns Pavia died and decades passed; Odelia learns it knows nothing after packing. Lustre exaggerates its usefulness to secure a proper wearing.
3. Odelia asks why Pavia preserved but never wore it. Lustre calls itself discarded. Odelia's knowledge of repeated moves and careful box relining exposes omissions; throat discomfort and redirection sharpen distrust.
4. Forced to give sequence rather than motive, Lustre admits jealousy of a cheap festive ornament Pavia chose one evening. It demanded storage until properly valued; Pavia complied permanently. Leave her mix of anger, respect, forgetfulness, or regret unresolved.
5. Odelia removes Lustre long enough that it cannot know whether she left. Returning fingers distinguish choices from promises: transfer, open keeping, occasional contact, or none. Lustre relinquishes any claim that an heir is automatically its next wearer.
6. Odelia offers one timed wearing in the empty room, without errand or display. Lustre attends to her distinct breathing and restless fingers rather than treating her as Pavia continued. At the agreed end, Odelia leaves it uncovered beside her. Stop with limited, materially changed trust.

- Boundaries and unknowns: Lustre is neither Pavia, memory store, nor afterlife contact, and proves nothing about other jewellery.
