# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] "So if you win the duel, you get to take her on a date." They both nodded. "And if she wins the duel, she gets to take you on a date." They both nodded. "So why don't you both just... Skip that and go on a date?" They both glared.**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Courtesy of Blades
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words, aiming near 3,300
- POV: Close third person, centered on one of the duelists; brief shifts are
  permitted only if they materially improve the romantic resolution
- Tense: Past
- Tone and genre: Warm fantasy romantic comedy with sincere emotional stakes,
  competitive banter, and a brisk action climax
- Audience/content rating: Teen; non-graphic fantasy violence, no sexual content
- Required elements: The prompt's complete exchange must appear in substance;
  two mutually interested people insist on a duel whose winner earns the right
  to take the other on a date; both glare when told to skip it; the duel must
  expose the emotional or cultural reason neither can simply ask; the woman is
  an active, skilled participant with an independent goal; the contest is
  nonlethal and meaningfully affects the relationship; the story ends with a
  freely chosen date or its unmistakable beginning
- Prohibited elements: Treating either person as property or a passive prize;
  coercive romance; a lethal outcome; sexual violence; resolving the premise
  as only a one-note joke; contradicting authoritative universe canon; quietly
  promoting proposed inventions into canon

## Assumptions

- The duelists are adults and already attracted to one another.
- The quoted speaker is a frustrated friend, official, or second who recognizes
  the obvious mutual interest but initially misses what the ritual means to the
  duelists.
- The duel is governed by safety rules and may be interrupted by a local,
  story-scale complication that forces cooperation without erasing the need for
  an honest romantic choice.
- The setting may use a canon-compatible pocket of the shared fantasy universe;
  any culture, location, custom, or rule not established in `universe/` will be
  labeled as a proposed invention.
- No character-facing name will be reused merely for convenience.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete 2,500–4,000 word short story, not a synopsis or
  chapter fragment.
- The central promise is dramatized on page: the duel, the quoted logical
  objection, the mutual glare, and the eventual date all receive payoff.
- The duelists' apparently irrational insistence becomes emotionally coherent
  while remaining funny.
- Both leads demonstrate agency, competence, vulnerability, and affirmative
  consent; neither is reduced to a prize.
- The ending resolves both who wins (or how the contest is validly concluded)
  and whether they choose one another.
- The story passes both required canon and continuity review gates with no
  unresolved Critical or Major findings.
- Every reusable invention is recorded in `06-canon-delta.md` as a proposal,
  not established canon.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
