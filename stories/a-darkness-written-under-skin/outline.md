# Outline

## Story

- Premise: In an era when every person is born with a visible, ability-bearing tattoo, eighteen-year-old Leora Sloane has spent her life as the first apparent exception. A routine black-light scan reveals a vast colorless tattoo beneath her skin, and contact with an active bearer shows that its ability is to receive, hold, and return other tattoo-born abilities.
- Character want and pressure: Leora wants the ordinary work credential repeatedly delayed while officials studied her supposed blankness. The revelation replaces exclusion with a more dangerous label: authorities see a tool that can disarm or appropriate anyone, while Leora fears that proving useful will surrender the self she built outside tattoo classifications.
- Turn: When the scan accident destabilizes the licensing hall and an official tells Leora to take powers from trapped applicants without permission, she refuses the command but accepts abilities that their bearers freely offer. By choosing the terms of contact, she changes her hidden mark from an apparent instrument of theft into a temporary refuge for power.
- Ending: Leora uses two voluntarily lent abilities to hold a collapsing gallery and quench an electrical fire, then returns both intact. Before consenting to any further study, she makes the registrar issue the ordinary credential she came for; the final image leaves her tattoo invisible in normal light but knowingly hers, neither an absence nor a public claim on her life.

## Beats

1. At a crowded licensing hall, close-third past narration follows Leora through her final competency appeal. Visible tattoos made from varied fluids advertise everyone else's likely ability; Leora's bare-looking skin has made her a lifelong specimen and blocked the work placement she has otherwise earned.
2. A registrar uses a newly added black-light scanner to close the file on possible transparent ink. Ultraviolet light exposes branching script over Leora's torso and limbs, written in a colorless fluid below the visible layers of skin; the room's curiosity instantly becomes institutional possession.
3. The registrar keeps an ordinary nonliving scan lamp on Leora while an examiner activates a silver-fluid suspension tattoo and touches her wrist. Leora's hidden lines fill with the examiner's bright pattern, the examiner loses the active ability, and a suspended section of scanning rig drops until Leora instinctively arrests it with the ability now held in her own living body.
4. Leora releases the examiner's pattern by breaking contact and willing it back, proving the loss was temporary, but the wrenching transfer has already torn the rig from a gallery support and ignited damaged wiring. Staff lock the hall in panic, leaving people beneath a sagging upper gallery while smoke spreads.
5. The registrar orders Leora to strip useful abilities from the nearest applicants. Leora refuses; her first accidental transfer felt like an invasion, and being tattooed does not give the registry ownership of her or anyone else. She instead calls to the trapped bearers and asks what they can safely lend.
6. An injured adult pinned beyond reach offers a load-bearing ability carried in dense resin ink; another applicant offers a brine-ink cooling ability. Through deliberate touch, Leora receives both patterns. The growing darkness beneath her skin brings severe pressure, cold, and narrowing concentration, establishing a bounded personal limit rather than a universal price for power.
7. Leora braces the failing gallery long enough for the crowd to clear, then draws heat from the damaged wiring and smothers the fire. She cannot solve every task alone: unpowered bystanders guide people out and the recovered examiner cuts the electrical supply, making the rescue collective rather than a display of supremacy.
8. In front of witnesses, Leora returns each offered ability to its original bearer unchanged. When the registrar reaches for a new containment classification, Leora makes the immediate choice concrete: issue her ordinary credential first, and any later examination will require her informed agreement.
9. Outside, normal light renders Leora's skin apparently blank again. She carries the credential and the felt boundaries of the hidden script, understanding that the black light revealed a capability, not a verdict about who she is or what she owes.

## Continuity

- Canon used: This is one unplaced era of the single deep-time physical world, during which the tattoo system is active planet-wide. The system is locally bounded: living bearers originate and use its abilities; tattoos are bodily channels or foci; the black-light scanner only reveals and measures; and Leora's strain, transfer rules, and consent practice establish no universal source, cost, or consent law. Leora's personhood and choices remain morally prior to institutional explanations of her anomaly.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Do not connect the tattoo system, Leora, or the unnamed licensing hall to existing characters, places, factions, powers, or historical events. Leave the era's position, the system's origin, inheritance mechanism, full ability taxonomy, and conditions beyond the witnessed transfers unresolved. “Everyone in the world” applies literally to people born in this tattooed era, not retroactively to other established ages of the shared world.
