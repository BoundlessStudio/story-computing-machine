# Prompt

## Prompt

> **[WP] Everyone in the world is born with a tattoo. The tattoo can be made from any kind of ink-like fluid and creates various possible abilities. You are the first to be born without one. Or so they thought until a black light was shined on you.**

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV and tense: choose from the prompt
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Avoid: none specified
