# Catch Me

Provisional direction: close third person through Nika, fifteen, a new magical girl who wants a turn making the team's decisions. Her teammate Mavis is forty-seven outside a transformation and looks thirteen inside it. The youngest, Alma, knows better than to make fun of the spare socks. Nika appreciates Mavis more than she wants to admit, but adult habits in a body younger than hers can make care feel like public fussing. Give this friction a specific shared evening and a comic object, probably the coat Nika said she would not need. Let Mavis already entrust Nika with something that matters; she is experienced, not obliviously overprotective.

An attack interrupts the evening. A living collector shaped like an enormous toy cabinet mistakes the girls' apparent ages and picks Nika off while she deliberately protects Alma. It traps her among its bright interior fittings, high above the street. Mavis stops sounding like a thirteen-year-old.
