# Content rating

- **Rating:** Teen
- **Content notes:** Non-graphic drowning and death; attempted resuscitation; bereavement; people endangered inside a fantastical bodily sea; choking, retching, and vomiting water. The cover depicts a dead adult and the sea visible through intact skin, without gore.
- **Reason:** The finished prose and selected cover sustain mortal peril, physical distress, and close attention to a drowned loved one's body. This intensity may be unsuitable for readers under 13. Death and the bodily transformation remain non-graphic; there is no sexual content.
