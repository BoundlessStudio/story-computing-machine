# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: During an at-home hair-dye night, a mother finally names what she knows: her sixteen-year-old daughter glimpses events before they happen. The daughter is already braced because the decision produced a glimpse. Resolve the confrontation through belief without proof and recognition of the cost of silence.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The mother wants honesty and protection, but also reassurance and absolution. The daughter wants acknowledgment without becoming the family's oracle, alarm, or scapegoat. She is angry, unfair at times, and still wants their ordinary intimacy.
- Counterforce or complication: Settled choices affecting the daughter trigger her faculty, so rehearsed questions reach her before speech. Revisions create new fragments; rapid branching brings nausea and confusion. The mother's testing and retreat keep reproducing this pressure. Hair processing supplies an ordinary clock and makes touch a live choice.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close first-person past through the mother, attentive to towels, stains, timers, and rationalized evidence. The daughter remains exterior; only her premature reactions, mistakes, strain, and incomplete account are available. The mother mistakes anticipation for omniscience.
- Governing movement and time shape: Roughly one hour: kitchen preparation, application, a hallway rupture, and the bathroom rinse. Memories appear only when reinterpreted. The recursive movement is intended question, premature response, misreading, revision. End privately before morning or any relationship forecast.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: A familiar person's sincere choice likely to affect the daughter soon may produce a short sensory fragment of its likeliest consequence. She cannot select subjects, hear thoughts, see causes, or know whether intervention has invalidated a glimpse. Reconsidered choices muddy or replace fragments; proximity increases frequency, not accuracy. This living faculty is local, fallible, and not fixed fate.
- Dialogue promise: An irritated, intimate mother-daughter collision where affection persists through habit and touch while deniability fails. The daughter is funny at bad moments, embarrassed, and petty—not serenely prophetic; the mother never becomes perfectly therapeutic.
- Dialogic medium: Speech, false starts, withheld questions, active silence, involuntary reactions, and gestures accepting or refusing touch. The dye task keeps bodies in the exchange without becoming a supernatural test.
- Dialogue engine: Naming the faculty forces them to renegotiate who carries family uncertainty. Each intended question can provoke an answer to an unspoken version, creating information and error together. The mother must surrender sequencing; the daughter must risk describing present experience instead of managing a predicted aftermath.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Concrete, close, domestically exact. The mother arranges evidence as neatly as hair sections, then admits omissions and mistaken motives. Keep glimpses outside narration; show arrival through the daughter's body and attention.
- Conversational texture: Overlap, unfinished questions, grooming logistics, stale family jokes, adolescent bluntness, and unsafe silences. Some remarks are merely annoyed or practical.
- Rhetorical ownership: The mother owns lists, qualifications, dry self-defense, authority, and control recast as care. The daughter owns sharp humor, sensory particulars, literalism, premature answers, and unfairness. Under strain, neither listens generously.
- Pressure behavior: The mother rehearses, retreats, and substitutes smaller questions. The daughter answers futures that have not occurred, repeats herself as fragments change, loses fluency with nausea, and refuses touch when speech fails.
- Relationship movement: The mother wants trust but cannot request it without burdening her daughter with fear. The daughter wants belief without usefulness but risks disbelief or exploitation by asking. Their exchange makes the ability shared truth while leaving its contents under the daughter's authority.
- Anti-default: Avoid an eloquent boundary negotiation, accuracy test, or death-warning thriller. Preserve pettiness, misreading, physical awkwardness, and unresolved consequences; no formal protocol, cleansing embrace, or polished lesson.

## Beats

1. Preparing the dye, the mother settles on confrontation. Her daughter reacts to its coming version before speech. The mother names clairvoyance plainly; the daughter blocks any retreat to euphemism.
2. They continue application. Premature answers expose intended proof questions and the mother's wish for reassurance. A small attempt at randomness repeats years of disguised tests and pushes the daughter from sarcasm into anger.
3. Their dispute reclassifies changed routes, preemptively moved objects, and avoided conversations. Neither owns the whole interpretation. The faculty's limits emerge through correction and sensory particulars, not a polished explanation.
4. Rapidly chosen and rejected questions produce overlapping glimpses. The daughter becomes ill, leaves the chair, and refuses help, breaking both the sequence and the mother's assumption that care grants access.
5. The daughter reveals that earlier near-confrontations made her glimpse questioning followed by withdrawal. The mother understands that private indecision repeatedly reached her daughter as rejection, without becoming malicious or wholly culpable.
6. The mother starts forming a self-serving question about future safety; the daughter's reaction shows the demand has landed. She abandons extraction, states only what she knows now, and leaves disclosure to her daughter.
7. The daughter offers one present-tense fact about the faculty and allows the rinse to be finished. End on that limited participation: changed trust, no prediction, agreement, proof display, or easy assurance.
