## Prompt

> [WP] Everyone thinks becoming the city’s youngest crime boss means money, power, and respect, but between preventing gang wars, managing corrupt officials, and keeping her own crew from betraying her, she hasn’t slept in three days—and tonight, someone has left proof that her empire is collapsing from within, it's time to take action.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
