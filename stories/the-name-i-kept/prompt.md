# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] You, the member of a superhero family, are talking with your older sibling, who was disowned for killing a supervillain, (had no choice). Shunned by heroes and family, you are the only one they can talk to. Also you are sick and tired of your parents calling them a failure and disgrace.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV and tense: choose from the prompt
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Avoid: none specified
