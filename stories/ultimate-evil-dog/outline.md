# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Open with older Tri-City Area superhero Brightguard correcting “Pickle” for skipping a beloved breakfast ritual while Maledict, awake inside Pickle’s dog body, targets her home command room. Across town, the butler protects dog-minded Pickle inside Maledict’s unharmed human body and counts down the expected fourteen hours. Braid both domestic farces until Maledict chooses luxury over reversal and must restore Pickle rather than steal Pickle’s body or identity.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Maledict wants Brightguard’s private response key and an unnoticed victory, but becomes attached to the heated bed, attentive care, snacks, walks, and freedom from villainous management. Brightguard wants to trust her dog while broken habits signal that Pickle needs help. The butler’s duty to Maledict yields to keeping bewildered Pickle safe and treated as a subject, not a malfunction.
- Counterforce or complication: Brightguard knows Pickle’s bowl ritual, favorite salmon crisps, dislike of the office floor’s hum, habit of fetching her wrist guard at alerts, and demand for a sofa blanket. Maledict’s nose-height text pad cannot compensate while comforts interrupt his plans. At the skyscraper, adult proportions, glass stairs, controls, and staff expectations endanger a dog mind. The butler has the manual but not the hidden override.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Braided close third past alternates between Maledict and the butler. Neither sees the other household. The butler infers needs but cannot give Pickle human thought or language. Brightguard remains externally legible through professional observation, aging-body routines, familiarity, and speech. The reader knows both halves immediately; Brightguard learns through discrepancies and the butler’s call.
- Governing movement and time shape: A sixteen-hour screwball braid organized by routines: breakfast, alert, walk, rest, contact, fourteenth-hour failure, then a two-hour resolution window. Recurring food, doors, furniture, and care expose wrong behavior or genuine preference. Join both threads privately in the skyscraper’s residential laboratory; end next morning on two separate dogs negotiating routine while the Tri-City Area regards Maledict as missing.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Maledict’s one-use device exchanges two living minds without blending memory, instinct, injury, or personhood. Default reversal occurs after fourteen hours; the animal-body occupant can reject it with a concealed collar override, preserving both occupants and opening a final two-hour window. The device then permits mutual return or recast: return each mind to its source body, then irreversibly reshape only Maledict’s consenting body into a distinct dog. No mind is copied or deleted. Expiry freezes the swap. This invention establishes no general rule.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Run a warm screwball braid, pivoting between tactical ambition and tactile household detail; notice routines, posture, surfaces, smells, aging gear, and care work. Jokes come from knowledgeable mismatch, never Pickle’s distress.
- Conversational texture: Brightguard moves between clipped field coordination and relaxed domestic prompting; the butler offers courteous choices and status reports; Maledict’s text-pad declarations shrink into distracted fragments. Preserve ordinary instructions and food talk.
- Rhetorical ownership: Brightguard owns practical inference and patrol analogies; Maledict owns conquest, rank, and entitlement; the butler owns timing, risk, and procedure. Pickle has no adult rhetoric—only movement, attention, refusal, and preference.
- Pressure behavior: Brightguard listens harder and becomes direct; the butler grows more exact without growing cold; Maledict repeats himself, abandons messages, and chooses immediate comforts; Pickle seeks predictable sensory anchors.
- Anti-default: Avoid polished quip-machine uniformity, an eloquent talking dog, therapy-language ethics, or a moral debate explaining what care and bodily choice already establish.

## Beats

1. Maledict-in-Pickle fails the first breakfast habit while plotting access; cut immediately to the butler cushioning a glass-and-steel suite as Pickle-in-Maledict explores by dog logic. Establish the white “sock” as natural forepaw fur, not clothing.
2. Attempts at the response key fail through personal tells. Brightguard offers ordinary choices whose history only Pickle knows. Comforts derail Maledict; the butler removes hazards, cancels meetings, and learns preferences without treating them as consent to remain human.
3. The butler finds the fourteen-hour instructions and calls Brightguard. On live video, she recognizes Pickle through embodied habits. Maledict’s text-pad denial collapses under evidence of his objective and another offered comfort.
4. At the skyscraper laboratory, Maledict knowingly activates the hidden hold at fourteen hours. The non-reversal is choice, not malfunction. Brightguard bars him from appropriating Pickle’s body, name, bed, or future; the butler uncovers recast before the final deadline.
5. Maledict chooses recast after its permanence and lost human privileges are concrete. Pickle returns to the original dog body, receives familiar-care verification and a veterinary check, and retains home, routines, belongings, and room to refuse the newcomer. Maledict becomes a distinct second dog under monitored care; the butler handles his affairs and restitution.
6. Next morning, Pickle resumes a known ritual and claims the favored place. Maledict is safe and pampered but waits within Brightguard’s boundaries. Close on breakfast management and his distraction while his disappearance remains an outside mystery—no confession, reconciliation speech, or moral line.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Personhood and moral consequence remain binding; transformation is mechanism-specific. The tonal range permits accessible contemporary superhero farce.
- Boundaries and unknowns: All four named nouns lack authoritative or passing-current matches and remain new, story-local proposals. The butler and skyscraper stay unnamed. Do not connect the device, powers, city, or villain operation to existing systems; give Pickle no adult reasoning or speech, and never let Maledict’s choice authorize Pickle’s displacement, suppression, copying, harm, or abandonment.
