# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: In a consent-bounded shared-memory session, a woman therapist follows a male client's panic backward through his unnaturally long life. Roughly twenty-five thousand years earlier, during a battle, he sealed a defensive passage while wounded survivors still approached. Resolve that discovery and why he buried it without making access a cure, verdict, or objective history.
- Focal pressure or attachment: He wants freedom from involuntary replays and control over a life others might exploit as an archive. She wants understanding without surrendering her right to stop a session whose risks he understates. Under pressure, he becomes useful and exact while disappearing from his own account.
- Counterforce or complication: Depth causes disorientation, while recurring sensations invite false equivalence between events. His concealed longevity invalidates their first risk estimate. He must disclose enough for renewed consent; she must not make access the price of belief.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through the therapist. She receives only what he sensed, noticed, or reconstructed—not unspoken motives, omitted space, or strategic truth. His present interruptions remain consequential, keeping him a participant.
- Governing movement and time shape: One private session descends recursively from a recent intrusion through older echoes into the battle. Layers shorten as time deepens and awareness of the room fades. The turn occurs inside the sealed passage; the outcome is a chosen return. End after identifying the source, without public proof or absolution.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Shared recall is a story-local living faculty requiring her attention and his active recollection; equipment is inert. He chooses each sensory handhold, every descent needs renewed assent, and either can exit independently through present sensation and released contact. Recall cannot change events, recover what he never perceived, certify dates, or compel truth. Depth brings nausea and time confusion; exit ends access, not distress. Continuous self-renewal and compressed memory are his individual condition, not universal traits or another person inside him.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Track concrete sensory recurrence while separating present observation, inhabited recall, and inference; compress syntax with depth without making memory dreamlike or omniscient.
- Conversational texture: Present protocol is practical and interruptible, with ordinary checks and unfinished answers. Battle speech is urgent, positional, and dependent on shared danger.
- Rhetorical ownership: The therapist owns bounded questions, not the thesis. The client owns durations and material detail while withholding interpretation. The commander reasons in tactical thresholds; survivors reason through bodies, routes, and dependents.
- Pressure behavior: The client becomes formally precise, repeats counts, and drops pronouns. The therapist speaks less and hears corrections. Ancient commands abbreviate; survivor voices overlap and spend words on possible action.
- Anti-default: Avoid polished confession, therapist-as-oracle, interchangeable aphorisms, an ancient exposition chorus, or a final exchange explaining an exit already chosen.

## Beats

1. Open late in a previously arranged session. The therapist verifies his starting memory, independent exit signals, and recording limit. A recurring alarm-and-pressure episode disrupts ordinary life; he asks to find its first occurrence, not erase it.
2. Enter a recent transit memory: closing doors, warning tone, bodies pressing forward. He counts capacity and places himself beside the mechanism. He redirects her interpretation toward a physical detail he can follow backward.
3. Cross two brief echoes—an evacuation decades earlier and a refuge centuries earlier. Similar sounds recur, but his different choices disprove simple repetition. Calendar fragments and continuous viewpoint expose his longevity. The therapist exits independently when the room nearly vanishes.
4. Make concealment consequential. She will not resume without the missing risk information. He protects his history from extraction; she protects her mind and boundary. They renew consent only for one descent: he controls the handhold and record, she whether to follow and when to leave.
5. Re-enter through pressure against a closing barrier. Assemble the battle from his station: shock through stone, obscured movement, route changes, incoming wounded, and a commander tracking an unseen breach. Keep factions and places unnamed.
6. Reveal the source through action. As a young defender, he was ordered to seal the passage before the opposing force reached it. Seeing survivors approach, he judged that delay could expose everyone behind him and closed it. The enduring wound is hearing and counting people beyond a barrier his hand made irreversible.
7. Keep remembered speakers distinct: the commander presses a tactical threshold; one wounded survivor directs others; another contests the timing; the younger client seeks one more interval. Their fragments embody competing survival claims, not a record able to prove whether the order saved more people.
8. The present client holds the moment seeking a missing answer and repeats the count that traps him in later emergencies. The therapist refuses endless reenactment and initiates her exit. He independently chooses the present handhold and leaves too.
9. Back in the room, both remain disoriented and his trigger remains. He identifies the battle as the source, limits the slate's record, and ends the session. Leave further access, corroboration, and his judgment of the ancient choice unresolved.

## Continuity

- Canon used: One world persists through deep time; memory yields partial evidence rather than automatic truth; personhood and consent survive speculative explanation. Violence stays non-graphic.
- Boundaries and unknowns: The battle occurred approximately twenty-five thousand years before the session, with no known-era placement or crossover. Recall cannot establish complete military history, casualty counts, or the order's necessity. His longevity and her faculty remain story-local and offer no diagnosis, clinical practice, or real-world therapeutic guidance.
