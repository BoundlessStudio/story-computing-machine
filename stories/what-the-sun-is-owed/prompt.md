# Original request

The sun doesn't kill vampires. It recognizes them. Long ago, the first vampire stole something from the sun, and every sunrise since has been an attempt to reclaim it. After centuries of hiding, a young vampire discovers she can hear the sun speaking her name. When she finally answers, daylight turns crimson, shadows begin moving independently, and the sun offers her a bargain: return what was stolen, or become the next star in the sky.
