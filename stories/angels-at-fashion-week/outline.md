# Provisional outline

## Promised experience

Three veteran Angels infiltrate Paris Fashion Week as international supermodels. A mysterious handler gives them exactly twelve minutes to identify an assassin walking the runway. Their extravagant gowns conceal experimental weapons, their towering heels spy technology, and the designers seem to have secrets. The user wants multiple outfits that match the supplied fashion images and all four women on the cover if all are important.

“Without breaking character, ruining their outfits, or letting the audience know” suggests a glamorous, funny and genuinely dangerous caper. The pleasure is watching a move succeed as both fashion and combat. The prompt specifies the team and handler. The women’s identities, private relationships, target, show and outcome are open. The remake comparison belongs to the handoff.

## Takes considered

- **The live duel.** One Angel is the target. Her team hunts the shooter through changes of clothes, then makes the closing photograph its trap. Chosen for the long scene where a murder attempt and the Angels’ best walk are the same choreography.
- **The lineup shell game.** Follow the Angel who works the rails. Rival houses doctor the run of show. The killer changes in front of two dressers who think they’re preparing different women.
- **The contact sheet.** An Angel works through the press burst in reverse the morning after. One misplaced hand leads her back to a live scene behind the pier. The opening tells us who lived.

## Provisional direction

Booked under established modeling covers for a three-designer show, the Angels are tracing a stolen experimental dart weapon small enough to pass as jewelry. At first call the handler says a runway woman has been paid to use it on Della at the finale photograph, exactly twelve minutes away. Della can withdraw and let the unknown shooter vanish with the weapon. She keeps her place and gives the other Angels control of the approach. Someone has put a shot at her in the show’s running order.

Della spots Mireille holding her right hand clear of two very different sleeves. Romi works out which jewelry came from the ateliers and which piece Mireille brought herself. Nari finds the firing port on that ring in a floor-level image from her platform toe. Mireille is both their shooter and the supermodel sharing their closing slot.

Romi shifts the final turn to spread the trains. She uses her cape for cover and sends the timing through her heel. Nari’s peacock sleeve fires a short binding filament between Della’s wrist and Mireille’s as their hands meet. Mireille fires anyway. Della turns the muzzle into the armored underside of her lacquer cuff and keeps walking. They take Mireille and the ring offstage in formation.

## Craft plan

**Teller.** Try close third past through Della, who reads what people arrange for others to see, and first-person Della accounting for a photograph she likes too much. She wants the portrait her real work has never allowed. She is accustomed to deciding what the other two can risk. She can sell a whole plan as advice about a pose and give an order that leaves friends room to disagree.

**Cast and originals.** Numbers follow the prompt inventory. Della and Mireille carry the duel and Romi has the third sustained voice. Nari helps prove the identification and seizes the gun hand in full view.

- **Della** enters hip first, plants, and lets people adjust around her. High black hair, silver crosses and a lowered stare carry from bone corset and buckled platforms (#9) to ember lacquer, teal spine and trailing sleeves (#1). The bones look dangerous in lineup. Her red platforms hold firm when the lacquer cuff takes the dart. Other fittings and earlier looks bring the silver-fuchsia serpent, peach dragon and blue jewel, teal-gold starbursts and dark starfield (#2, #3, #11, #14), with styled braids or waves.
- **Romi** tilts, gathers and glides. She draws people in with a warm story and lets them supply its last word. Her hands keep #4’s silver-lilac spill clear of heels. At the turn she uses the swing of a pendant to time her copper-blue sleeve, her dark braid on the opposite shoulder (#6, repeated in #15). The sage star train and icy beaded mini (#5, #7) are other looks.
- **Nari** frames her face with exact hands and finds the view others omit. She speaks in distances and useful corrections. The red fingers and black-and-wine webs of #10 put her in suspicion’s color palette. Her blue-black part, crossed stance and lacquered peacock sleeve drops (#8) carry through the heel-camera sightline and the short reach of the snare.
- **Mireille** offers both wrists and makes a room wait. Pearl white, jeweled waist and cross chains (#12) suggest ceremonial openness. One set of chains betrays the hand she holds apart. Red nails, lifted chin and wine-and-blue velvet (#13) turn the same assurance imperious. At the shutter her chin rises when her wrist cannot. She makes refusals sound like courtesies and cares fiercely about the clothes she uses to kill.

Keep the slits, high shoes and bold profiles, with opaque linings where needed. Give the additional reference looks movement in short passes or remembered fittings. The quartet’s changed silhouettes own the last walk.

**Place.** Galerie Basse and Sous-face 37, Palais de Tokyo. Wet late September, 20:48 to 21:00. The Grand Palais nave gives a long open sightline and the Musée des Arts Décoratifs nave puts its mosaic on view. Here slopes and a concrete pier force a turn in the temporary catwalk. Press cameras sit below it and the pier shelters the return. Callers work numbered look cards and dressers stage shoes in sequence. Cold seeps from the wall onto bare shoulders. Hairspray hangs behind the black drape, a bad rail wheel ticks, bass bounces off concrete, and platforms knock hollow on plywood.

## Scene briefs

1. **First call, 20:48–20:51 (20%).** At the rail and first exit Della wants the others to keep their places. Romi checks whether Della intends to run this alone. The handler cuts in as a dresser needs Della’s foot. Della walks instead of withdrawing. Skull chains knock as she reaches light. Give their years in the field one shared remembered maneuver.
2. **The returns, 20:51–20:58 (30%).** Between passes, three designers guard an erased cutter’s credit, borrowed jewels passed off as house work, and an exclusive press angle sold off the books. Romi talks a dresser through a sleeve and gets the jewelry list. Della matches Mireille’s hand across looks. Nari checks her image from the ramp. The ring’s aperture shows below a strand of jeweled crosses. Summarize other exits and fittings in the flow of changes.
3. **The closing photograph, 20:58–21:00 and the exit (50%, long).** Romi sells a shifted turn while Mireille argues for the original under the guise of protecting her velvet. Della needs her to commit and wants the shot as a model. Mireille forces the encounter a pace early. Stay close through the thumb moving, the heel planting and Della offering her wrist. The pose conceals the snare and dart. They carry the clinch around the pier.

**Ending.** Mireille leaves between the Angels while the room applauds. End on the press image of four women in red lacquer, peacock blue, copper and royal velvet. The hand Della initially guarded and the angle Romi bargained for now produce an apparent gesture of trust. Della wants a copy.
