# Provisional outline

## Promised experience

Three veteran Angels infiltrate Paris Fashion Week as international supermodels. A mysterious handler gives them exactly twelve minutes to identify an assassin walking the runway. Their extravagant gowns conceal experimental weapons, their towering heels spy technology, and the designers seem to have secrets. The user wants multiple outfits that match the supplied fashion images and all four women on the cover if all are important.

“Without breaking character, ruining their outfits, or letting the audience know” suggests a glamorous, dangerous caper with room for play. The pleasure is watching a move succeed as both fashion and combat. The prompt specifies the team and handler. The women’s identities, private relationships, target, show and outcome are open. The remake comparison belongs to the handoff.

## Takes considered

- **The live duel.** One Angel is the target. Her team hunts the shooter through changes of clothes, then makes the closing photograph its trap. Chosen for the long scene where the Angels work out who will shoot and use the walk to stop her.
- **The lineup shell game.** Follow the Angel who works the rails. Rival houses doctor the run of show. The killer changes in front of two dressers who think they’re preparing different women.
- **The contact sheet.** An Angel works through the press burst in reverse the morning after. One misplaced hand leads her back to a live scene behind the pier. The opening tells us who lived.

## Provisional direction

Booked under established modeling covers for a three-designer show, the Angels are tracing a stolen experimental dart weapon small enough to pass as jewelry. At first call the handler says a runway woman has been paid to use it on Della at the finale photograph, exactly twelve minutes away. Della keeps her place to draw the shooter out. Romi decides how to get close, and Nari controls when it’s safe to strike.

Mireille passes Nari’s shoe lens beside the dragon model. The image fits a red hand jewel with a dark slot and the underside of a silver wing. Only the dragon model’s look card lists that jewel. The house dressing Mireille left a second, nearly identical setting off her card to conceal a loan from its rival. Della spends her change hunting the dragon model and has to walk when called. Romi gets the lender’s list and puts both women back in play.

Romi uses the third designer’s clandestine camera deal to win a wider turn. The dragon model lifts her peach train with both hands and sweeps behind the pier before the closing quartet stops for the shutter. Mireille lets her velvet fall badly to keep her right hand clear, as she did in pearl white, then opens the ring a pace early. Nari holds the snare while a photographer has a view of the hands. Della offers her wrist anyway, drawing Mireille off Romi’s pinned train. Romi catches the freed cloth and screens the grip with her sleeve. Nari binds the wrists with an experimental filament from her peacock sleeve, and Della turns the muzzle into the armored underside of her lacquer cuff. They walk Mireille and the ring offstage in formation.

## Craft plan

**Teller.** Try close third past through Della, who reads what people arrange for others to see, and first-person Della accounting for a photograph she likes too much. She wants the portrait her real work has never allowed. She can sell a whole plan as advice about a pose and give an order that leaves friends room to disagree.

**Cast and originals.** Numbers follow the prompt inventory. Della and Mireille carry the duel. Romi has the third sustained voice. Nari sees the angles the others miss and decides when to use the snare.

- **Della** enters hip first, plants, and lets people adjust around her. High black hair, silver crosses and a lowered stare carry from bone corset and buckled platforms (#9) to ember lacquer, teal spine and trailing sleeves (#1). Her chains announce her in lineup. Her red platforms hold firm when the cuff takes the dart. The hidden armor is an addition.
- **Romi** tilts, gathers and glides. She can look as coolly appraising as #6 while drawing a dresser in with a warm story. The sage star train (#5) or icy beaded mini (#7) gives her a different span of motion in the earlier passes. At the turn she times the fall of a copper-blue sleeve by her pendant’s swing, a dark braid lying over her right shoulder (#6, repeated in #15).
- **Nari** frames her face with exact hands. She speaks in distances and useful corrections. She gives the opening cameras their idea of menace in #10’s red nails and black-and-wine webs, then corrects a friend’s footing with workroom precision. Her blue-black part, crossed stance and peacock sleeve drops (#8) carry into the low shoe-camera sightline and the short reach of the snare.
- **Mireille** offers both wrists and makes a room wait. Pearl white, jeweled waist and cross chains (#12) suggest ceremonial openness. The swing of the chains exposes the hand held clear. Red nails, lifted chin and wine-and-blue velvet (#13) turn the same assurance imperious. At the shutter her chin rises when her wrist cannot. She makes refusals sound like courtesies and cares fiercely about how the clothes fall.
- **The dragon model** is freckled, with high honey or copper braids. She advances as if entitled to the width of her wings. Her silver-fuchsia serpent and jeweled hand (#2) misdirect the hunt. The peach scales, blue jewel and sweeping dragon cape (#3) make her two-handed finale turn a chance to show off. She and the other passing models can stay unnamed. Keep #4’s pearly drapery and clear heels, #11’s teal-gold starbursts and #14’s dark starfield on other models. Use opaque linings where needed, keeping the slits, height and glassy profiles.

**Place.** Galerie Basse and Sous-face 37, Palais de Tokyo. Wet late September, 20:48 to 21:00. The Grand Palais nave offers an open sightline and the Musée des Arts Décoratifs nave puts its mosaic on view. Here concrete slopes force a bend in the temporary catwalk. The audience and low press bank face the outside of that bend. A pier shelters the return behind the final mark. Dressers stage shoes in sequence and the caller works numbered look cards. Cold seeps onto bare shoulders. Hairspray hangs behind the black drape, a bad rail wheel ticks, bass bounces off concrete, and platforms knock hollow on plywood.

## Scene briefs

1. **First call, 20:48–20:51 (20%).** Della checks the portrait monitor twice as a dresser asks for her foot. The handler comes through an earring on the local show relay, using look numbers and the unruffled courtesy of a caller sending someone out. Della walks instead of withdrawing. Her skull chains knock at the first step into light. Give their years in the field one shared remembered maneuver.
2. **The returns, 20:51–20:58 (30%).** The wing in Nari’s frame and the omission from Mireille’s card send Della after the dragon model. Her own cue pulls her back out in red with a loose sleeve she has to carry as if it were a stole. Romi talks a dresser through who will blame whom if a borrowed clasp goes astray and sees the two settings on the lender’s list. Introduce the sponsor angle in the same bustle. Give the passing looks a movement apiece.
3. **The closing photograph, 20:58–21:00 and the exit (50%, long).** Romi bargains for elbow space as if it were a matter of international protocol. Mireille counters with a case for the velvet. Stay with the women’s hands as they separate on the turn. Mireille steps on Romi’s train as a photographer starts to follow the grip. Romi sends the agreed heel cue. Nari holds her snare and signals Della to wait. Della brings the ring hand closer by offering her own wrist. When Mireille shifts her weight, Romi recovers the train intact and sweeps a sleeve across the sightline. Nari fires. Della catches the dart in the cuff. Their recovery reads as an encore before they take the clinch around the pier.

**Ending.** Mireille leaves between the Angels while the room applauds. End on the press image of the four in red lacquer, peacock blue, copper and royal velvet. The portrait Della watched for and the angle Romi bargained for show an apparent gesture of trust. The feeling on Della’s own face is real.
