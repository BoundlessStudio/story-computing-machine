# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Young adult friends Rumi and Emina treat a tiny terrace teahouse visit as a spare hour. Sharing its last strawberry cream cake, they repair several weeks of mutual absence through appetite, foolishness, and full attention. Brief future returns show this unannounced happiness becoming each woman's lifelong measure of being known; neither recognizes its importance before leaving.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Rumi wants proof that her unavailability has not thinned the friendship but seeks it by performing. Emina wants to be actively chosen and turns that want into petty cake rules. Both protect the ease that prevents them from asking for reassurance.
- Counterforce or complication: Sunset, closing, and the last convenient downhill bus bound the hour. Each has mistaken the other's silence for happy busyness; mention of a missed invitation briefly breaks their rhythm. No tragedy or villain certifies the memory's value.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past, changing between Rumi and Emina only at movement breaks. In the primary hour, limit knowledge to the current focal friend's guesses. Three compact proleptic glimpses may reveal only a sensory trigger and later recollection, not whole biographies.
- Governing movement and time shape: One sunset sitting moves from guarded arrival through playful accumulation and a small social snag into absorbed companionship. Cream and berries, a cooling cup, and amber roofs trigger early-adult, middle-life, and late-life glimpses. Return quickly after the first two; let the last widen without deathbed, reunion, or destiny claim.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Entirely ordinary world. Memory decays and diverges; no magic, prophecy, photograph, or keepsake preserves the hour. One cake slice must be divided, tea cools, daylight moves, and closing time cannot be negotiated away.
- Dialogue promise: Lived-in friendship at its easiest and least presentable: overlap, teasing unfairness, abandoned anecdotes, affectionate correction, awkwardness, and laughter without retrospective wisdom.
- Dialogic medium: Speech braided with eating, shared looking, cake-portion gestures, comfortable silence, and failures of attention that make renewed listening visible. Future returns are internal memory.
- Dialogue engine: Each embellishment invites correction, revealing how closely the other remembers. Cake division gives comic form to whether time and regard remain mutual; the missed invitation forces listening, evasion, and resumed play to change each friend's belief about the relationship.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Warm dual-close narration moves through hands, crumbs, porcelain, and changing light, with clean, sparse future cuts. Perfection must arise from particulars, never announcement.
- Conversational texture: Use overlap, unfinished stories, wrong recollections, scheduling, mouthful pauses, and laughter that outruns its cause. Keep the primary talk unaware of future significance.
- Rhetorical ownership: Rumi owns exaggeration, improvisation, affectionate noticing, and comic misreading; performance impairs her listening. Emina owns dry correction, fairness rules, small pettiness, and social authority over pace, but hides tenderness in precision.
- Pressure behavior: Rumi becomes overfluent, then loses her thread. Emina turns quiet and exact rather than reassuring. Recovery appears through slower listening and returning interruption, not polished directness.
- Relationship movement: Rumi wants absolution without admitting guilt; Emina wants to be chosen without pleading. Their exchanges replace mutual misreading with knowledge that both noticed the gap, restoring trust without guaranteeing availability.
- Anti-default: Avoid elegant co-therapy, fantasy spectacle, or aphoristic nostalgia. Keep appetite, distraction, unfair berry division, factual disagreement, and causeless laughter active.

## Beats

1. Emina claims the smallest terrace table in amber light. Rumi arrives mildly late; irritation remains too small for confrontation. They order the last strawberry cake slice, two forks, and separate teas.
2. Emina invents exact rules for dividing cream, sponge, and berries. Rumi cheats, is caught, and contests the final strawberry. Laughter restores ease before either tests what the missed weeks meant.
3. Errands and acquaintances give way to mutually corrected memories and incompatible stories about hillside windows. A similar taste years later briefly returns Rumi to this laughter, without summarizing her intervening life.
4. Scheduling exposes Emina's unanswered invitation and Rumi's mistaken belief that Emina preferred newer company. Neither account is noble. Emina's quiet and Rumi's failed comic recovery form the narrow social turn.
5. Rumi stops explaining and asks about the missed occasion; Emina answers with messy particulars. Rumi recalls one ignored-looking detail, and Emina yields the better cake portion. Trust shifts without a negotiated promise.
6. While the keeper closes shutters, they resume inventing hillside lives; one foolish addition breaks self-consciousness into their fullest laughter. A later cup ring returns Emina to being listened to here, not to a lesson.
7. They leave crumb-marked, nearly miss the downhill bus, and keep disputing something inconsequential. Their next plan stays casual. They name no threshold, preserve no object, and pose for no picture.
8. A late-life widening shows repeated returns and divergent details—berry count, weather, cause of laughter—while unguarded mutual attention endures. End amid ongoing life and recollection, not death, reunion, or thematic statement.
