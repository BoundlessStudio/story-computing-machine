# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] The kingdom calls her the Mother of Monsters, a cursed woman who commands beasts, goblins, and things that should never have crawled from the dark—but when humanity finally marches into her ruined sanctuary to destroy her, they discover the monsters are not her servants; they are refugees, and she is the only thing protecting them from mankind.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
