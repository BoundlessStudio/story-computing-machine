## Prompt

> [WP] For all time, you have tended the loom of the world, weaving civilizations, cities, seasons, and generations of families whose lives you follow through the threads. Now the gods have gathered at your loom to debate another change of Eras. Some insist the old must collapse before a better age can begin; others remember how long it took to recover from the last one. They turn to you for the deciding vote.
>
> This is story where the Tapissier is aware of the larger Shared Story Universe and can break the 4th wall.
> Also she is the Astral Valkyrie but that is secret even from the gods.
