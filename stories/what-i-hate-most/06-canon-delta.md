## New characters or character facts

- **WHM-01 — Queen Draxenne (proposed).** **Fact:** Draxenne is a
  thirty-nine-year-old woman, Queen of the Cinder March, a living cindercraft
  user, and the story's close-third viewpoint. She comes to Vantress to keep
  the March road and outer settlements defended, wins temporary control of the
  Ninth Conduit, relinquishes exclusive control when her plan fails, accepts a
  share of the ward burden, and asks to court Ombria. **Exact local scope:**
  Draxenne's identity, office, actions, and choices in this breach and its
  immediate aftermath. **Constraints and dependencies:** `Queen` is a local
  political title, not a divine classification; `crown` in the story refers to
  her authority and realm, not an established magical artifact. Her authority
  does not grant rights over Ombria. Depends on WHM-03, WHM-05, WHM-08,
  WHM-13 through WHM-16, and WHM-21 through WHM-25. **Provenance:**
  `stories/what-i-hate-most/05-story.md`, which remains non-canon unless
  explicitly promoted. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/characters.md`.
- **WHM-02 — Night-Sovereign Ombria (proposed).** **Fact:** Ombria is a
  forty-two-year-old woman, the council-appointed and publicly answerable
  night-sovereign of Vantress, and a living shadowcraft user who manages five
  district pressure signals. She chooses civilian protection over her tactical
  claim, honors Draxenne's temporary conduit command after losing it, later
  delegates two eastern stations without surrendering her office or veto, and
  freely accepts Draxenne's courtship and kiss. **Exact local scope:** Ombria's
  identity, local office, actions, and choices during this breach and its
  immediate aftermath. **Constraints and dependencies:** `Night-Sovereign` is
  a local political and work title; the story establishes no worship,

  relationship to another sovereign. Depends on WHM-03, WHM-04, WHM-07,
  WHM-08, WHM-10 through WHM-12, and WHM-21 through WHM-25. **Provenance:**
  `stories/what-i-hate-most/05-story.md`, authority none until an authorized
  promotion. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/characters.md`.
- **WHM-03 — Draxenne and Ombria's relationship (proposed).** **Fact:** The two
  adult rulers are old magical rivals who have fought beside one another,
  wanted one another through two years of interrupted visits and one
  almost-kiss, and use polished provocation to conceal fear and desire. After
  the crisis, Draxenne rejects ownership of Ombria's attention, asks for chosen

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **WHM-09 — Ombria's living shadow planes (proposed).** **Fact:** Ombria can
  extend the shadow cast by her own living body into independently shaped
  planes, connect them to Vantress's ward anchors, redirect wind and mineral
  grit, compress a cinder route, or angle the planes into baffles with measured
  gaps. She actively shapes every witnessed plane with her hands and attention.
  **Exact local scope:** Ombria's shadowcraft and its witnessed use at the
  Ninth Conduit. **Constraints and dependencies:** The planes do not enter a
  mind, compel a person, select targets, or continue after Ombria releases
  them. No wider shadow-magic class, inherited system, or connection to another
  shadow mechanism is proposed. Depends on Ombria's living agency under WHM-02,
  available shadow, the local ward apparatus and anchors under WHM-16, and the
  costs in WHM-10.
  **Provenance:** `stories/what-i-hate-most/05-story.md`, proposal only.
  **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **WHM-10 — shadowcraft capacity and cold cost (proposed).** **Fact:** Ombria
  can sustain four planes only at a serious bodily cost visible as cold skin,
  shaking breath, slowed or shortened speech, numbness, and gray fingertips.
  She identifies a fifth plane as a maximum-three-breath emergency reach; the
  witnessed fifth plane lasts two breaths, strips away her composure, and is
  released. Later she judges another fifth plane capable of stopping her heart.
  **Exact local scope:** Ombria's capacity during this crisis. **Constraints and
  dependencies:** Five is her demonstrated emergency maximum here, not a
  universal shadowcraft number; cold and attention are costs, not moral
  payment or romantic fuel. Depends on WHM-09 and Ombria remaining conscious
  and actively engaged. **Provenance:**
  `stories/what-i-hate-most/05-story.md`, authority none. **Suggested smallest
  topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **WHM-11 — shadow-plane release and overload behavior (proposed).** **Fact:**
  Ombria can release planes independently; a released plane becomes ordinary
  shadow immediately. She can taper a loaded plane through a counted lowering,
  while heavily loaded planes become visibly grainy and unstable at their
  edges. **Exact local scope:** The witnessed contest, transfer, and final ward
  shutdown. **Constraints and dependencies:** The story shows warning signs but
  no completed catastrophic snap, so no universal break pattern or exact
  damage radius is established. The coordinated safe-withdrawal procedure is

  WHM-06. The plane behavior itself depends on WHM-09 and WHM-10.
  **Provenance:**
  `stories/what-i-hate-most/05-story.md`, proposal only. **Suggested smallest
  topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **WHM-12 — Draxenne's living cindercraft (proposed).** **Fact:** Draxenne can
  drive directed blue-white cinder through mineral grit, stone cracks, and
  prepared ceramic. She can divide one path, sustain multiple streams, fuse
  airborne stormglass into heavy rounded beads, soften ceramic into a
  load-bearing brace, and direct the cast away from living bodies. **Exact
  local scope:** Draxenne's cindercraft as witnessed during this breach.
  **Constraints and dependencies:** The craft requires Draxenne's living
  attention, an available mineral path, and venting air; it does not create
  autonomous or living fire, compel anyone, or establish a universal fire
  system. Depends on Draxenne's living agency under WHM-01, the conduit stone
  and apparatus under WHM-06 and WHM-16, stormglass under WHM-17, and prepared
  ceramic under WHM-18. Associated bodily costs are inventoried separately in
  WHM-13. **Provenance:**
  `stories/what-i-hate-most/05-story.md`, authority none. **Suggested smallest
  topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.

  Cindercraft consumes Draxenne's body water and available breath, cracks her

  Draxenne's observed condition and
