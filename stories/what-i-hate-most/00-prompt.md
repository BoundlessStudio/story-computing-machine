## Verbatim writing prompt

## Story controls

- Working title: What I Hate Most
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person, centered on one of the two romantic rivals.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Dark romantic fantasy with dangerous competence, charged
  banter, luminous nocturnal imagery, and a playful fighting/flirting ambiguity.
- Audience/content rating: Adult characters; suggestive and sensuous but
  non-explicit; violence may be intense but not graphically gory.
- Required elements: The quoted exchange appears naturally on page; two adult,
  magically powerful rivals whose contest is also courtship; dark-queen or
  night-sovereign energy; a concrete reason one character resents having to
  "share" the other; consent and agency that keep possessiveness from becoming
  dehumanization; a decisive emotional and plot resolution.
- Prohibited elements: No person treated as a prize without meaningful agency;
  no coercive romance presented as healthy; no copied Suno lyrics; no ending
  that stops at banter without resolving the underlying rivalry; no silent
  contradiction of shared-universe authority.

## Assumptions

- The romantic leads are adults, mutually attracted, and original unless canon
  research identifies an especially strong compatible connection.
- "Share" may concern the rival's obligations, attention, allegiance, or public
  role rather than a third romantic partner; the story will make its meaning
  concrete and resolve the possessive tension through freely chosen reciprocity.
- The default ending is romantically affirmative and earned, with the rivals
  choosing one another without abandoning the duties that made conflict real.
- Low-impact setting, magic, and cast details may be selected during canon
  research and planning if they remain within current authority.
