# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **The Last Color Through** (proposed title). Two rival treasure-hunters find a black, lockless door fed by living colors. Deliver materially astonishing red, blue, and green worlds, then make unlimited possibility collide with the impossibility of visiting everything.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Nerith loves being the first to find something and tell everyone about it. She imagines selling access while reserving every destination for herself. Discovery excites her more than her commercial talk admits.
- Counterforce or complication: Bozren wants an extraordinary place to explore. He keeps taking turns she considers hers. Meanwhile, the colors are migrating into their dimensions; departed hues become unavailable. Neither hunter knows when the last will go.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Nerith's appetite, sensations, and unreliable estimates of Bozren. No inhabitants' thoughts or account of the door's origin. Narration may establish the prompt's world scales while keeping mechanisms bounded to observation.
- Governing movement and time shape: One encounter in an abandoned hilltop quarry, narrowing from abundant choices to separate departures. Expand openings and decisions; compress prior rivalry into disputed anecdotes. Target prose: 2,500–4,000 words.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Each living hue opens a different pocket dimension through the nonliving frame. Touch selects it; ordinary pigment cannot substitute. Shut the door before changing destinations. Passage works both ways while the current remains around the frame. Currents choose when to withdraw, leaving no crossing. Departure pauses while a body occupies the threshold, but a hunter cannot occupy it and explore. No remote return is promised.
- Dialogue promise: Competitive boasting becomes an argument about who experiences which impossibility, with amusement, jealousy, and temptation surviving separation.
- Dialogic medium: Speech, calls through the threshold, demonstrative touches, and the currents' approaches and withdrawals. Their movement communicates without a translated human language.
- Dialogue engine: Claims provoke demonstrations; disbelief costs turns; reservations meet refusal. Their final negotiation allocates worlds without agreement about the best choice.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Nerith notices prices, distances, and things she could carry until the worlds defeat those measures. Let sensory abundance stretch sentences; embarrassment and physical surprise can cut them short.
- Conversational texture: Familiar rivals interrupt, exaggerate exploits, dispute priority, and sometimes speak simply to entertain each other. Allow plain questions amid competition.
- Rhetorical ownership: Nerith turns uncertainty into confident salesmanship. Bozren punctures her claims but tells outrageous truths badly. His eagerness outruns his listening; neither owns the moral interpretation. The currents express impatience and preference physically.
- Pressure behavior: Nerith starts issuing demands when options vanish, then makes one unusually direct request. Bozren's teasing falls away when he must name an actual destination; he remains capable of impatience rather than sudden wisdom.
- Relationship movement: Each wants the other's astonishment and hates asking for it. Nerith wants Bozren to delay his departure; he wants her acknowledgment that his choice is his. The last exchange gives each a witness without binding their journeys together.
- Anti-default: Avoid an expert explaining three hazards to a novice, a mutual rescue proving trust, or a promised reunion resolving the encounter.

## Beats

1. **The find becomes a quarrel.** Nerith claims the door while Bozren examines the living river. Neither brought the other here. They challenge each other to find the better destination. Use Image #1's branching luminous currents, glossy darkness, depth, and stark black threshold; incidental geometry is nonbinding.
2. **Three impossible openings.** Red reveals a world of fire, moving flame forming landscape-scale ridges. Heat makes possession impractical. Blue opens above an endless ocean and enormous translucent swimmers; a wave wets their boots. Green gives a high perch above a planet-sized forest, its curvature visible through luminous haze. Bozren crosses onto a broad branch and returns with sap on his soles. Give these experiences space.
3. **The missing red.** Red withdraws through the frame and disappears. Touching red clothing cannot select it. Other hues begin leaving. A current avoids Nerith's collecting jar; an open route dims, waits for Bozren to clear the threshold, then vanishes. Establish the limit before either stakes a departure on it.
4. **Too much to choose.** Nerith bargains for another round while Bozren packs food and climbing cord for the forest. Violet opens onto low-gravity dunes of resonant mineral shells; a tossed pebble sets distant ridges sounding. Nerith wants to hear what walking there would do, but closes it to contest Bozren's departure before deciding herself. Her insistence spends remaining time.
5. **Separate choices.** Nerith asks Bozren to wait; he makes clear the cost and proceeds toward green. Their quarrel produces a specific allocation of remaining access, still colored by their wager. He enters with supplies; green withdraws after he clears the opening. Nerith selects violet and crosses with her own pack. End inside as her movement produces an unexpected physical result, without an explanatory line or guaranteed route home.
