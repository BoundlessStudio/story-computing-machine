# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: This city licenses living magic users as Studied, Inherited, or Gifted according to source. Pipe-mender Pelmara Jossan has Beral Quor's ten-year clay-softening gift formally withdrawn before her permit expires at noon. The withdrawal succeeds; then a different ceramic-stress faculty is born from changes long use made in Pelmara's hands. Assessor Zorevi Nask must recognize a fourth relation without mistaking one local case for cosmology.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Zorevi wants her first unsupervised certification exact and Pelmara kept in her trade. She initially treats another Gifted contract as mercy. Pelmara wants independence without being called fraudulent and refuses any category that misdescribes her body or Beral's clean withdrawal.
- Counterforce or complication: Ishket requires reproducibility before the Studied insure a worker; Uvena refuses hereditary status without developmental evidence; Beral needs an unambiguous end to a costly gift. Pelmara's locking fingers, one fragile coupling, the noon deadline, and demands for repetition make honest caution materially expensive.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Zorevi, attentive to hands, work damage, forms, and what each object actually establishes. She knows local definitions, not Pelmara's sensations or whether another such faculty exists. Conclusions remain bounded to this case.
- Governing movement and time shape: One morning in a private source room: withdrawal, failed old-category tests, discovery, disputed repetition, bounded license. Each handled object narrows the claim. End on departure for Pelmara's delayed repair, not public acclaim, a charter ceremony, or sequel promise.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Here, Studied magic is a repeatable technique another qualified living person can learn; Inherited magic appears during development with family recurrence; Gifted magic is supplied or transferred by a living giver. Beral's gift softened fired clay while costing Beral heat and Pelmara hand sensation. After complete recall, Pelmara cannot soften clay. Her new **Cultivated** faculty instead senses and redistributes existing stress: saving one ceramic section concentrates likely fracture elsewhere and stiffens her joints. Warmth and rest ease the cost; repetition risks lasting lost motion. Tools, records, seals, and ceramics remain inert.

## Beats

1. Zorevi lays out inert clay strips, fired rings, warm water, and Pelmara's cracked work coupling. Permit chatter establishes the delayed cistern repair and noon deadline. Pelmara declines Beral's sincere offer to renew the old gift.
2. Beral withdraws it through living touch and breath. Beral regains full softening; Pelmara's strip stays rigid. Their mutual relief prevents a punishment narrative.
3. Ishket guides the documented motions, but neither Pelmara nor Zorevi can reproduce the gift. Uvena confirms Pelmara had no childhood faculty. Object-bound questions eliminate easy labels without three sales pitches.
4. Pelmara senses hidden tension in the coupling. One permitted attempt moves the likely break from its load-bearing throat into a replaceable flange. The flange chips, throat holds, and Pelmara's fingers lock: damage moved rather than vanished.
5. Ishket requests repetition; Zorevi initially agrees. Unable to release the coupling, Pelmara directs Uvena to the fracture and Beral to the warm basin. Uvena rejects a lineage claim; Beral verifies complete recall; Ishket preserves his safety objection.
6. Pelmara refuses another use. Zorevi records **Cultivated** as a provisional fourth house: an embodied faculty grown through prior use, neither learned knowledge, birthright, nor present gift. She grants ceramic diagnosis and one redistribution followed by witnessed recovery.
7. They disperse without ceremony. Beral keeps the restored gift, Ishket keeps measurements, and Uvena carries the coupling. Zorevi carries Pelmara's crate toward the delayed job while Pelmara corrects route and workload; the fourth house begins as bounded work.
