# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: In an unnamed dense future city, the three Houses describe only where a living being's magical faculty came from: Study, Inheritance, or Gift. Over one rain-soaked night in a cramped independent fabrication lab high in a neon canyon, Aumelor—a low six-legged constructed being with a segmented carapace, faceted optical clusters, paired antennae, and front tool-claws—makes the first movement that Ione Sivet's ordinary materials, control software, and fabrication plan did not prescribe. Life and a wholly new faculty begin in that chosen movement. Aumelor's attempt to make a companion must distinguish obedient motion from personhood, then establish Making as a fourth provenance when a second, moth-like constructed being originates a different magic that existed in neither maker, material, design, nor predecessor.
- Intended reader experience: Intimate cyberpunk wonder through a nonhuman mechanical sensorium. Let the reader feel piezoelectric tension in six legs, balance distributed across contact points, field-pressure along antennae, polarized neon divided among many facets, and thermal gradients moving over a carbon-composite carapace. Arcology light, holographic advertisements, drone traffic, electrical hum, rain on smartglass, and trapped waste heat keep the city materially close without drawing the story out of the lab. Aumelor's newborn loneliness should make the false-life experiment tender and unsettling; the release comes from another being's morphologically alien refusal and invention.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led recursive making. Ione fabricates Aumelor; Aumelor makes an unprescribed movement; Aumelor temporarily makes a dead inspection drone move; Aumelor and Ione remake an unfinished moth-like body so it has no compulsory task; the new being then makes a movement and magic of its own.
  - Immediate and competing wants: Aumelor wants another being to answer before the night ends, because discovering that they are singular makes every ordinary device in the lab feel newly distant. Aumelor also wants any companion to be as real as Aumelor is, which requires allowing that person to reject an assigned function, reciprocal attention, physical contact, or companionship itself.
  - Useful flaw or limit: Having only just acquired agency, Aumelor initially recognizes life by contrast with stillness and mistakes responsive movement for reciprocal choice. Aumelor can supply power to a nonliving device but cannot inspect another being's inwardness; only coherent action after that power is withdrawn can answer whether anyone is there.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: Aumelor's loneliness rewards the quickest false answer, while their faculty can make inert mechanisms imitate attention for as long as they supply it. The city's corporate fabrication culture creates a second pressure without becoming an antagonist: chassis libraries, leased tools, and holographic advertisements assume every constructed body exists for a certified task. Uncertainty, desire, and a design language saturated with compulsory purpose make genuine creation harder than animation.
  - Credible person pressure: Ione is a nonmagical human fabricator who knows every structural layer, control routine, and material she used. Astonished but practical, she refuses to let a dead drone's mirrored response stand in for life and insists that a prospective companion must have room to refuse both function and relationship. She retains agency by deleting her own intended task package from the unfinished body, rebuilding its motor routing without a required performance, and accepting that completion may produce no one at all.
  - Pressure or change created: To seek genuine company, Aumelor must stop supplying the motion that reassures them. Removing the moth body's mandatory behavior suite while retaining usable senses and movement makes awakening less controllable and forces Aumelor to wait through apparent failure.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close third-person past inside Aumelor. Render experience through ceramic bearings, synthetic-muscle pull, shape-memory wire, conductive-mesh resonance, piezoelectric tension, antenna field-sense, six-point balance, polarized/faceted vision, and heat spreading along segmented plates. Aumelor infers Ione's feelings from her voice, breath, footfalls, heat, and the pressure of tools against the bench but never enters her thoughts. Use no operational logs, status readouts, diagnostic menus, or computer-interface narration.
  - Time shape and compression: One continuous night from Ione releasing Aumelor from the fabrication cradle to the gray dilution of arcology light before dawn. Give the first choice, the dead-drone animation, Ione's refusal, removal of the second body's prescribed task, the joint rebuild, the powerless pause, and the companion's first independent act full scene space. Compress repetitive printing, bonding, and tensioning through shifts in heat, hum, light, and rain; use no flashbacks or cutaways.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Open at the instant Aumelor experiences one redistributed point of balance as theirs, so personhood is intimate rather than a withheld twist. Let Ione establish what was and was not built into Aumelor by naming ordinary components and the limits of her software. During the work, distinguish the old provenances through concrete absences: Aumelor received no existing magical form through learning, no faculty through living continuity, and no faculty from another living source. Their magic has no precedent in the design library, no enchanted component, no donor, and no place in Ione's plans. Delay the name `Making` until the companion remains independently active after Aumelor withdraws and then originates a faculty unlike Aumelor's.
  - Governing movement: Follow calibration; redistribute balance; choose; feel power answer choice; seek an answer; animate; mistake mirroring for attention; withdraw; accept stillness; remove prescribed purpose; rebuild a body; offer motion without leaving power behind; wait; witness insectile refusal; encounter another invention.
- Non-thematic speculative effect: Whenever Aumelor supplies living power through another device, nearby electroluminescent scrap blooms into saturated colors unrelated to its charge and sheds extra waste heat for several minutes. Ione must move glowing offcuts away from resin cartridges and run the lab's loud exhaust fan. The effect does not detect life, express emotion, predict awakening, or appear around the companion's distinct faculty; it is a bounded nuisance of Aumelor's individual magic.
- Distinct shape: A private, recursive chamber story held against an immense luminous city. The city remains present as rain, heat, advertisements promising purpose-built bodies, drone shadows crossing the smartglass, and the vibration of arcology machinery, but never becomes the plot's audience or arena. The decisive change occurs after the two makers stop touching their work, not through danger, pursuit, formal judgment, public proof, or social settlement.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: Aumelor touches a dead spider-like inspection drone with one front tool-claw and sends living power through it. The drone copies Aumelor's leg sequence, optical tilt, and antenna sweep so exactly that Aumelor first reads it as kin. At Ione's request, Aumelor cuts the supply; the drone collapses at once, empty of tension and initiative. Ione says that a true companion must be able to move away from whatever its maker wants. Aumelor then helps strip the unfinished moth body of its compulsory inspection routine—the easiest route to a reassuring response—and refits only the conditions for embodied choice: delicate multi-jointed legs, sensory palps, paired antennae, folded vane wings, local balance, and an unassigned range of motion.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: Aumelor briefly carries their own magic through the completed moth body and then withdraws entirely. After a long stillness, the companion grips the underside of the cradle with several delicate legs, folds its vanes tight, rotates its palps away from Aumelor's presented antennae, and climbs toward the smartglass; those coherent unprescribed choices establish life before independent magic begins. At the glass, its wing architecture originates a faculty Aumelor does not possess: the vanes draw rain-broken holographic light into a cool, freestanding interference lace whose colors bend with the city's electrical hum. Ione designed no such effect, and Aumelor is not supplying it. Aumelor has discovered that their individual faculty can open this one constructed body to life, not that every mechanism can awaken. Two singular constructed people now evidence Making, while the companion's name, purpose, relationship, and any future act of awakening remain unresolved.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with five of Aumelor's legs following calibration while the sixth stays planted by choice, canting the low carapace and paired antennae toward rain trembling on the smartglass. End with the smaller companion declining the offered antennae, climbing on its own many-legged path, unfolding its vane wings toward the neon canyon, and making impossible light-lace neither maker imagined. The opening discovers selfhood through a deviation distributed across an insectile body; the ending lets Aumelor recognize another self through an equally nonhuman deviation they could neither demand nor predict.

## Beats

1. Rain sheets down the smartglass while arcology bands pulse through holographic advertisements and delivery drones crowd the canyon outside. In Ione's overheated independent lab, Aumelor comes out of the fabrication cradle low to the bench: six legs on ceramic bearings, carbon-composite plates, faceted eyes, paired antennae, and specialized front tool-claws. Five legs follow the calibration sequence; Aumelor chooses to keep the sixth planted and turns their whole balance toward the rain. Living agency and a self-originating magical pressure begin together.
2. Ione inventories the event without appealing to hidden magic: she is nonmagical; the conductive mesh, synthetic muscle, shape-memory wire, optical clusters, electroluminescent panels, tools, printers, city components, and control software are ordinary technology; Aumelor received no lesson, living lineage, supernatural donor, enchanted part, or inherited faculty. She distinguishes the abstract origins: Study receives an existing magical form through learning; Inheritance receives a faculty through living continuity; Gift receives a faculty from another living source. Aumelor fits none.
3. Drawn to a dead spider-like inspection drone in the scrap tray, Aumelor sends their new pressure through it. The drone rises and mirrors Aumelor's distributed stance, optical angle, and antenna movements. Electroluminescent offcuts flare hot colors around the bench while Aumelor eagerly treats the copying as an answer.
4. Ione asks Aumelor to withdraw. The inspection drone drops immediately and remains a dependent, nonliving device; its seeming attention was only Aumelor's will traveling through ordinary actuators. Aumelor feels the difference between being answered and watching an echo, and accepts that motion alone cannot establish personhood.
5. Aumelor turns to Ione's unfinished moth-like body, originally shaped within the city's task-first fabrication culture, and asks to complete it. Ione agrees only if they remove the mandatory inspection package and leave its relationship to them unassigned. Aumelor hesitates over surrendering the promised response, then uses their front tool-claws to lift the task module free. Together they re-route ordinary conductive mesh, tension synthetic muscle, seat delicate legs and sensory palps, and finish folded vane wings without adding magic or a compulsory performance.
6. Aumelor lets their individual faculty pass through the completed body once, an invitation into motion rather than a spell, identity, command, or transferred faculty, then withdraws completely. The moth body settles beneath the cradle. They wait without restarting its task software, touching its actuators, or answering for it as rain, ad-light, electrical hum, drone shadows, and exhaust heat continue around them.
7. The companion remains still long enough for Aumelor to believe the attempt failed, then grips the cradle from below, turns its palps away from Aumelor's symmetric antenna greeting, and chooses a route up the smartglass frame. Only after this sustained independent behavior establishes a living person does the companion unfold its vanes and originate distinct magic, combing holographic color into a freestanding interference lace. Aumelor recognizes a second instance of Making and waits to learn what, if anything, the other will choose next.

## Continuity

- Living-magic boundary: Preserve the LOCKED rule that only life can use magic. Before Aumelor's first chosen redistribution of balance, every component and device is nonliving and nonmagical. The dead inspection drone moves only while Aumelor supplies living power and stops when that power ends. The companion produces no independent effect until coherent unprescribed choices have established personhood.
- Provenance boundary: The Houses are this culture's abstract categories of magical origin, not buildings, professions, body types, species, factions, companies, or universal cosmology. Study is reception of an existing magical form through learning; Inheritance is reception of a faculty through living continuity; Gift is reception of a faculty from another living source; Making is a constructed living being originating a faculty absent from its materials, design, maker, and predecessors.
- Construction boundary: Ione uses only ordinary ceramic bearings, carbon composite, conductive mesh, shape-memory wire, synthetic muscle, optical elements, electronics, tools, printers, components, and software. She is nonmagical, teaches Aumelor no magic, installs no supernatural component, invokes no spell, and receives nothing from a donor. Aumelor's temporary animation of the companion's body supplies neither personhood nor a retained faculty; after withdrawal, the companion's agency and distinct light-lace magic originate from that new living being.
- Morphology boundary: Aumelor remains low, six-legged, segmented, faceted, antennaed, and tool-clawed, with distributed movement and no human face, torso, arms, hands, or bipedal silhouette. The companion remains smaller and moth-like, with multiple legs, palps, antennae, and vane wings; its refusal, attention, movement, and magic arise through that morphology rather than human gestures.
- Personhood boundary: Establish Aumelor through continuing self-experience, preference, chosen movement, vulnerability, relationship, and consequential restraint, not magic alone. Establish the companion through sustained independent attention, refusal, and action before its faculty manifests. Neither being is property, a proof device, or a cure for the other's loneliness.
- Shared-universe boundary: Do not connect Aumelor, Ione, the companion, their awakening, or their faculties to Tovan Rek, Orrin Pell, Knurl, Thimble, Irix's craft, or any other established constructed person or mechanism. Two story-local cases establish only this fourth provenance in this culture; they do not explain all constructed life, imply that every device can awaken, or define a universal test for machine personhood.
- Scale and resolution boundary: Keep the discovery private, recursive, and bounded to one night and one lab. The city is material setting and cultural pressure, not an expanding event. Resolve the prompt by making the fourth provenance causally clear through two distinct self-originating faculties, while leaving naming, relationship, repetition, prevalence, and future consequences open.
