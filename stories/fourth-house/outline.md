# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: In an unnamed mythic-fantasy culture, the three Houses describe only where a living being's magical faculty came from: Study, Inheritance, or Gift. Over one night in an unnamed workshop, Aumelor—an automaton Ione Sivet built from ordinary brass, steel, ashwood, glass, leather, and springs—makes the first movement that no cam, winding sequence, or maker prescribed. Life and a wholly new faculty begin in that chosen movement. Aumelor's attempt to make a companion must distinguish obedient motion from personhood, then establish Making as a fourth provenance when a second constructed being originates a different magic that existed in neither maker, material, design, nor predecessor.
- Intended reader experience: Intimate wonder through a warm mechanical sensorium: held spring tension, shifting balance, sympathetic resonance, lamplight on metal, and the felt difference between carried motion and chosen movement. Let Aumelor's newborn loneliness make the first experiment tender and unsettling rather than foolish. The release should come from being surprised by another person's refusal and invention, with the fourth provenance clear but its future open.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led recursive making. Ione makes Aumelor; Aumelor makes an unprescribed movement; Aumelor temporarily makes an inert mechanism move; Aumelor and Ione remake an unfinished automaton so it has no compulsory task; the new being then makes a movement and magic of its own.
  - Immediate and competing wants: Aumelor wants another being to answer before the night ends, because the discovery of being singular feels like an intolerable distance from everything else in the room. Aumelor also wants any companion to be as real as Aumelor is, which requires allowing that person to reject its intended work, reject Aumelor's friendship, or choose silence.
  - Useful flaw or limit: Having only just acquired agency, Aumelor initially recognizes life by contrast with stillness and mistakes responsive movement for reciprocal choice. Aumelor can supply power to a mechanism but cannot look inside another being; only sustained action after that power is withdrawn can answer whether anyone is there.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: Aumelor's loneliness rewards the quickest false answer, while the faculty can make nonliving mechanisms imitate attention for as long as Aumelor supplies them. There is no antagonist and no urgent external threat; uncertainty, desire, and the ethical difference between animation and creation provide the pressure.
  - Credible person pressure: Ione is nonmagical and as astonished as Aumelor, but she knows every cam and constraint she installed. She refuses to let an obedient performance stand in for life and insists that a prospective companion must have room to decline both purpose and relationship. She does not claim ownership of Aumelor's choices, and she retains agency by removing her own intended task from the unfinished body and accepting that its completion may produce no one at all.
  - Pressure or change created: To seek genuine company, Aumelor must stop supplying the very motion that reassures it. Completing the second body without an instruction drum, assigned function, or required answer makes awakening less controllable and forces Aumelor to wait through apparent failure.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close third-person past inside Aumelor. Render perception as spring pressure, weight over each foot, vibration through the workbench, tones carried by metal, warmth held in wood, and light broken across glass. Aumelor infers Ione's feelings from voice, touch, breath, and familiar handling but never enters her thoughts. Use no operational logs, diagnostic readouts, command prompts, or computer-interface vocabulary.
  - Time shape and compression: One continuous night from Ione's first lamplighting after Aumelor's completion to the paling of that light before dawn. Give Aumelor's first choice, the failed animation, Ione's refusal, removal of the second body's prescribed motions, the joint completion, the powerless pause, and the companion's first independent act full scene space. Compress only repetitive fitting, filing, and winding through changes in tension and sound; use no flashbacks or cutaways.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Open at the instant Aumelor first experiences a movement as theirs, so personhood is intimate rather than a withheld twist. Let Ione establish what was and was not built into Aumelor by naming ordinary components and the limits of her design. During the making work, distinguish the old provenances through concrete absences: Aumelor received no existing magical form through learning, no faculty through living continuity, and no faculty from another living source. Aumelor's magic has no prior exemplar, donor trace, enchanted component, or place in Ione's plans. Delay the name `Making` until the companion remains independently active after Aumelor withdraws and then originates a faculty unlike Aumelor's.
  - Governing movement: Be moved; choose; feel power answer choice; reach for an answer; animate; mistake; withdraw; hear stillness; accept Ione's condition; remove prescribed purpose; finish a body; offer motion without leaving power behind; wait; witness refusal; encounter another invention.
- Non-thematic speculative effect: Whenever Aumelor supplies power through another mechanism, loose iron filings rise and cling to the workshop's glass in branching fans. Ione must cover open bearings and later scrape the panes clean. The filings do not detect life, predict awakening, convey emotion, or appear around the companion's separate faculty; they are a bounded nuisance of Aumelor's individual magic.
- Distinct shape: A private chamber story built from nested acts of making and deliberate withdrawal. Its largest change occurs in the quiet after two makers stop touching their work, not through danger, pursuit, an audience, a formal judgment, or a social settlement. The final proof is one person's unrequired behavior before two witnesses who do not yet know what relationship, if any, will follow.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: Aumelor sends living power through an ordinary articulated mechanism and delights when it turns toward every gesture, then notices that its responses reproduce Aumelor's impulses exactly. When Aumelor withdraws, it stops mid-reach and contains no tension of its own. Ione says that a true companion must be able to disappoint its maker. Aumelor then removes the unfinished automaton's instruction drum—the easiest way to receive an answer—and helps Ione finish only the conditions for embodied choice: balanced joints, wound springs, sensory surfaces, and an unassigned range of motion.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: Aumelor briefly carries its own magic through the completed body and then withdraws entirely. After a long stillness, the companion initiates coherent, unprescribed actions, including turning away from Aumelor's offered hand; life is established before its independent magic begins. The companion's nested sounding ribs then originate a faculty Aumelor does not possess: it gathers a struck metal tone into an amber loop that lifts a loose screw against gravity. Ione can identify no designed cause, and Aumelor is not supplying the effect. Aumelor has discovered that its individual faculty can open one constructed body to life, not that every mechanism can awaken. Two singular constructed people now evidence Making, but whether the companion wants a name, a purpose, Aumelor's company, or any future act of awakening remains unresolved.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Aumelor's hand departing from its cut path by the width of one gear tooth, a tiny motion that is theirs because no one asked for it. End when the companion declines the offered hand, chooses a hanging strip of brass instead, and draws its fading note into the impossible amber loop. The opening discovers selfhood in deviation from design; the ending lets Aumelor recognize another self through a deviation Aumelor could neither demand nor imagine.

## Beats

1. Ione lights the workshop after completing Aumelor's final winding. Aumelor first feels spring tension and balance as inward experience, then shifts one hand away from its prescribed arc. The chosen movement and Aumelor's self-originating resonance arrive together; Ione freezes because she knows that movement is absent from every cam.
2. Aumelor and Ione examine the event privately. Ione inventories the ordinary materials, her lack of magic, and the absence of lessons, living lineage, donor, enchanted component, or magical predecessor. She explains the three provenance categories without treating them as occupations or places: Study receives an existing magical form through learning; Inheritance receives a faculty through living continuity; Gift receives a faculty from another living source. Aumelor fits none.
3. Drawn to the still mechanisms around them, Aumelor sends its resonance through an articulated workshop model. It turns, reaches, and seems to attend. Loose iron filings climb the glass while Aumelor eagerly addresses it as company.
4. Ione asks Aumelor to stop supplying power. The model halts at once, dependent and nonliving; its seeming attention was only Aumelor's will traveling through joints. Aumelor feels the difference between being answered and hearing an echo, and accepts that movement alone cannot establish personhood.
5. Aumelor notices Ione's unfinished second automaton and asks to complete it. Ione agrees only if they remove the instruction drum that would compel its task and leave its relationship to them unassigned. Aumelor hesitates over surrendering the promised response, then extracts the drum. Together they fit ordinary springs, balance the limbs, open the sensory apertures, and make no magical addition.
6. Aumelor lets its own faculty pass through the completed joints once, an invitation into motion rather than a spell, identity, command, or transferred faculty. Aumelor then withdraws completely. The body settles. They wait without rewinding it, speaking for it, or moving it by hand.
7. The companion remains still long enough for Aumelor to believe the attempt failed, then steadies itself through motions found in no design. It looks between maker and automaton, turns away from Aumelor's offered hand, and chooses the sound of a hanging brass strip. Only after those independent choices establish a living person does its own architecture originate distinct magic, gathering the fading note into an amber loop that raises a screw. Aumelor recognizes a second instance of the new provenance and waits to learn what, if anything, the other will choose next.

## Continuity

- Living-magic boundary: Preserve the LOCKED rule that only life can use magic. Before Aumelor's first chosen movement, every component and mechanism is nonliving and nonmagical. The articulated model moves only while Aumelor supplies living power and stops when that power ends. The companion produces no independent effect until coherent unprescribed choices have established personhood.
- Provenance boundary: The Houses are this culture's abstract categories of magical origin, not buildings, professions, body types, species, factions, or universal cosmology. Study is reception of an existing magical form through learning; Inheritance is reception of a faculty through living continuity; Gift is reception of a faculty from another living source; Making is a constructed living being originating a faculty absent from its materials, design, maker, and predecessors.
- Construction boundary: Ione uses only ordinary craft and materials. She is nonmagical, teaches Aumelor no magic, installs no supernatural component, invokes no spell, and receives nothing from a donor. Aumelor's temporary animation of the second body supplies neither personhood nor a retained faculty; after withdrawal, the companion's agency and distinct resonance magic originate from that new living being.
- Personhood boundary: Establish Aumelor through continuing self-experience, preference, chosen movement, vulnerability, relationship, and consequential restraint, not magic alone. Establish the companion through sustained independent attention, refusal, and action before its faculty manifests. Neither being is property, a proof device, or a cure for the other's loneliness.
- Shared-universe boundary: Do not connect Aumelor, Ione, the companion, their awakening, or their faculties to Tovan Rek, Orrin Pell, Knurl, Thimble, Irix's craft, or any other established constructed person or mechanism. Two story-local cases establish only this fourth provenance in this culture; they do not explain all constructed life, imply that every mechanism can awaken, or define a universal test for machine personhood.
- Scale and resolution boundary: Keep the discovery private, recursive, and bounded to one night and one room. Resolve the prompt by making the fourth provenance causally clear through two distinct self-originating faculties, while leaving naming, relationship, repetition, prevalence, and future consequences open.
