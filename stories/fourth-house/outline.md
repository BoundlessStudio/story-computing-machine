# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: In an unnamed culture that recognizes only studied, inherited, and gifted sources of magic, birth assessor Delka Por must keep the weak-breathing newborn Emri Aster safe while determining why every standard test goes blank. Emri's magic is neither possessed alone nor transferred: it arises only when two living participants respond to one another and makes a working neither could perform separately. Before the birth room empties, Delka must recognize that a fourth house has acquired its first member without turning the child into a public demonstration.
- Intended reader experience: Intimate diagnostic tension that gradually becomes tender astonishment; the reader should feel each familiar explanation narrow, then understand the new mode through a practical act of care rather than a lore speech.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led discovery contained within ordinary postpartum work.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: Delka's vocation joins care to classification, but the isolation she trusts as ethical, accurate procedure is exactly what makes Emri's relational magic disappear.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: Delka immediately wants to steady Emri's breathing and spare the exhausted Yune further strain; she also wants a truthful source finding so the family is not assigned to care that misunderstands the child. Her useful limit is a disciplined habit of testing one living source at a time: usually a safeguard against false claims, here a category error that worsens the infant's distress.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: Emri's irregular breathing, Yune's postpartum exhaustion, and a phenomenon that ceases under the culture's safe isolation procedure; no antagonist is needed.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Yune refuses more separations after noticing the pattern. She is protecting her child, not resisting knowledge, and continues offering exact observations Delka has discounted.
  - Pressure or change created: Delka must choose between repeating validated procedure and treating connection itself as the operative condition, while avoiding both a false house assignment and reckless testing on a newborn.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close third-person past through Delka. Her technical competence makes the three-house model concrete, while her isolation bias lets the reader witness the fourth effect before she can name its logic.
  - Time shape and compression: Roughly forty-five continuous minutes beginning just after delivery. Compress routine care; expand touch, breath, tool response, and the pauses in which Delka changes her hypothesis.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Introduce studied, inherited, and gifted magic through three small source tests, not historical exposition. Each test rules out one explanation but also erases the anomaly by separating Emri from a responsive partner. Let Yune's embodied observations precede Delka's recognition. A later working shared by Delka and Emri proves the effect is not merely a maternal faculty or a copied gift.
  - Governing movement: Isolate and lose; reconnect and alter; repeat with accumulating differences; finally test the relationship rather than either participant.
- Speculative surplus: During an active shared working, paired loose objects briefly mirror one another's motion—wash-water drops creep together, two blanket pins turn in matching arcs, and Delka must move metal instruments apart. This is a bounded practical nuisance and sensory signature, not an autonomous action by the objects or a metaphorical proof of the story's point.
- Structural distinction: A one-room concentric diagnostic loop. Three short failed tests return to the same mother-and-child contact with changed meaning; the fourth movement breaks the loop privately. No public venue, crowd conversion, institutional rewrite, or ceremonial naming scene.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: After Yune stops a fourth separation, Delka directs her familiar studied breath-count through Yune while Emri remains against her. A third rhythm answers between them, visibly different from Delka's technique and absent from either participant alone. Delka recognizes that isolation is not removing contamination but dismantling the user: this magic is co-generated by living response.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: Emri stabilizes through ordinary care aided by the bounded shared working, and a second, different response between Delka and Emri confirms the fourth source without establishing a general law. Whether it can be taught, inherited, repeated by others, or ever become a social house remains open; the fact of one new mode and one living founder does not.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Delka lifting Emri away from Yune because clean classification requires separation. End after she returns Emri and changes the care procedure to preserve responsive contact; when a fresh shared effect appears, she asks for another clean cloth and continues the work, making the unprecedented part of care rather than closing on a declaration or emblem.

## Beats

1. Immediately after delivery, Delka assesses Yune, lifts silent Emri to the warmed table, and begins ordinary measures to establish breathing. Emri improves in Yune's arms and falters when separated; Delka initially reads the timing as coincidence.
2. Delka's studied source-reading technique and its three inert foci establish the local houses in use: a repeated pattern indicates study, a family resonance indicates inheritance, and a donor aftertone indicates a gift. Delka supplies all magic used by the nonliving tools. Emri yields none of the three signatures.
3. Back against Yune's skin, Emri breathes more evenly. Yune reports that she can feel the child's next breath before it happens; paired water drops and blanket pins begin matching one another. Delka suspects an unrecorded inherited faculty or a dangerous gift drawing on Yune.
4. Delka repeats only the minimum safe distinctions. Each separation stops both the bodily foreknowledge and the material side effect; each reunion restores them in a slightly different form. Yune names the causal pattern and refuses another isolated test. Delka honors the refusal but fears leaving Emri without an accurate care path.
5. Delka changes method. Instead of aiming her studied breath-count at Emri, she offers its cadence to Yune while mother and child remain together. Emri answers with a third cadence that neither adult technique nor infant reflex produces alone; it lets Yune anticipate and support the baby's shallow breaths while Delka uses ordinary warming and positioning.
6. To distinguish relationship from maternal inheritance, Delka lets her technique lapse, takes Emri only with Yune's agreement, and responds to the infant's grip and breath without imposing a pattern. A new shared effect appears—different from the one with Yune, impossible for Delka alone—and ceases when she disengages. The evidence is enough; she does not push for spectacle or exhaustive proof.
7. Emri's breathing settles. Delka records observed limits rather than forcing one of the three source labels, returns the child to Yune, and revises the immediate care instructions around contact and consent. The story stops on continued birth-room labor as another modest fourth-source effect begins.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Only living beings use magic. Delka supplies the workings shaped by the nonliving diagnostic foci, and Emri, Yune, and Delka supply every observed fourth-source effect as living participants. The three houses and the new fourth mode are one culture's categories, compatible with the locked rule that magic, technology, and divinity are cultural rather than objective cosmological divisions.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Keep the era and location unnamed. Establish the three-house taxonomy, source tests, postpartum practice, and responsive fourth magic as bounded local inventions; assert no universal origin, cost, consent law, inheritance rule, or power taxonomy. Do not connect the houses, Emri's magic, or Delka's technique to House Bellweather, its first-desire doctrine, or any established faculty. Leave the fourth mode's wider recurrence, transmissibility, future membership, and institutional consequences unresolved. Yune controls nonessential testing on her newborn, ordinary medical care remains causal, and the explanation must preserve Emri's personhood.
