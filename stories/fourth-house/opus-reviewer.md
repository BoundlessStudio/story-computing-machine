# Opus review: The Fourth House

Independent audit for a later editing pass. It is not a `review.md` verdict,
does not change canon state, and does not authorize edits by itself.

## Verdict

- Dialogue: REVISE — the three officials and Beral all talk in Zorevi's scope-limiting, correcting register, and the naming climax and licensing coda run as a recap and a stated lesson.
- Prose: REVISE — the protagonist's trade and the stakes are cistern pipework (water-infrastructure policy); otherwise only minor tics.
- Continuity and prompt: PASS — the prompt's fourth house is born and written in on the page; two small logic loose ends.
- Edit scope: targeted-dialogue
- Existing review.md said: Verdict PASS, "Dialogue: PASS"; disagree on dialogue (officials converge; decisive exchange is procedural recap) and on the water policy, which postdates it.

This is one of the stronger dialogue stories in the collection. Pelmara has a real voice (tactile fragments, placement commands, dry family humour), and the discovery-to-redistribution run (L123–L283) is well staged, with interruptions, fluency breaking under pain, "Not now" said twice, and a practical murmur instead of speeches. The problem is that everyone at the table except Pelmara sounds like Zorevi. Ishket, Uvena and Beral answer with scope limits, rulings and corrections, so Zorevi's defining habit of narrowing her claims as her certainty drops no longer marks her, and the frame scenes fall into pairs of a statement followed by a narrower restatement. The story's two most important beats outside the workshop sequence are the only personal exchange between Beral and Pelmara and the naming of the fourth house. The first is carried by contract phrasing, the second by a round-robin recap, and the licensing coda then states its lesson through Uvena and a labelled emotion. The edit pass should leave the middle alone. It should re-voice the officials so that only Zorevi speaks in clauses, give Beral and Pelmara one audible want each, and let "Cultivated" arrive without the recap.

## Edit guardrails

- Prompt: three houses (Studied, Inherited, Gifted) and a fourth born today. "Cultivated" must still be written by hand into the house field of a form that prints only three, on this one morning. Keep the length within 2,500–4,000 words (now about 3,035). The story is broadly accessible, the active craft profile is prospective-2026-08-21, and the POV is close third past through Zorevi.
- Universe (LOCKED, rules.md "Living agency and magic"): only life uses magic. The register, seal, load frame, listening fork and clay stay inert, and the gift moves only through Beral's living touch and breath. Keep every "the tool is not magic" beat consistent with this even if some are trimmed (P2).
- Story facts the edits depend on: a ten-year gift that cost Beral heat and Pelmara sensation; the permit ends at noon; withdrawal at 9:52 and seal at 11:23; one authorized redistribution; the flange chips and the throat holds; Pelmara's fingers lock and recover only partly; the license terms at L333 (one redistribution, then witnessed recovery; diagnosis stops at stiffness; fired ceramic only; ten days); Ishket's safety objection is recorded; Beral does not offer renewal again.
- Water re-homing (P1) must keep ceramic couplings, fired pipe, the load-frame test and the "west stack". Move the trade to a non-water ceramic setting instead of rewriting the plot.
- Do not flatten Zorevi's formality. Her over-exact authorization (L217), her doomed definition (L315) and "A hair is not a unit" are her character. The fix is to take that register away from the others, not from her.

## Dialogue findings

### D1 · Major · Officials converge in one correcting register

- Where: L95–L99 (Gifted absence test), L117–L121 (Studied test), L293–L297 (insurance), L323 (naming); also L49 and L173
- Text:
> “The recall is complete. The absence needs a second observation.”
> “That conclusion concerns softening,” Ishket said.
> “There is no other faculty under examination.”
> “Then I cannot insure an unknown operation at all.”
> Ishket’s eyebrows drew together. “That is a provisional description, not a mechanism.”
- Problem: Identical Voices / correction ping-pong. The narration tells us Zorevi grows "more exact as certainty left her" (L217), but Ishket answers in the same way at almost every step: a claim is made and he returns a narrower version of it (recall vs. test, softening vs. faculty, provisional vs. mechanism, Studied vs. unknown). Beral's "The third is your language" (L49) and Uvena's legal tail at L173 do the same. With the tags covered, L99, L119, L297 and L323 could be Zorevi's. L119 has a second problem. Ishket signals that a second faculty exists before the reader has seen anything that would make him suspect one, because Pelmara's stillness is only reported afterwards (L123). The hint reads as the author setting up the reveal.
- Fix: Make Ishket's lines asks and pushes, the voice he already has at L109, L205 and L251. At L99 he just asks for the second press, with no correction of Zorevi's wording. At L119 either show him looking at Pelmara's hand before he speaks, or cut the line and let Pelmara's question at L127 break into Zorevi's record. At L297 give his objection a concrete risk (her hands on a live job, nobody able to repeat or undo it) rather than a second ruling. At L323 a question or a grunt is enough; Zorevi's hedge covers the rest. Leave Zorevi's own corrections at L29 and L233; they are earned and funny. Illustrative only: "Again. Harder, right in the middle."

### D2 · Major · Beral and Pelmara's only personal exchange is a contract routine

- Where: L37–L43 (renewal offer before the withdrawal); L105–L107 (after the recall)
- Text:
> “The offer remains until withdrawal.”
> “Then let it remain quietly.”
> Whatever she had wanted from that statement, if she had wanted anything, he did not add it.
- Problem: Negotiation/procedure routine plus Tennis Match with a last word. A ten-year arrangement that visibly cost Beral his heat (L35) ends in clause-talk, and neither line tells the reader why he is offering or why she refuses. The outline calls the offer sincere, but the page does not show it. At L107 the narration admits a missing emotional beat ("whatever she had wanted") without giving the reader anything to infer from, and "He did not offer renewal again" (L349) closes a thread the reader was never let into.
- Fix: Keep it short and reserved, and don't add a reconciliation speech. Give Beral's offer his reason in his own terms, such as her livelihood at noon or his own stake in the work. Point Pelmara's refusal at something concrete in front of her (his buttoned coat, his colour) instead of a rejoinder. Cut "The offer remains until withdrawal" and "Then let it remain quietly." At L105–L107, either give Beral one plain line to her rather than to the record, or cut the "Whatever she had wanted" sentence. Keep L349 as the thread's close. Illustrative only: "You'll be out of work by lunch."

### D3 · Major · The naming sequence is a round-robin recap

- Where: L305–L325, from Zorevi's residue question to the entry of "Cultivated"
- Text:
> Uvena held up the coupling. “It did not appear during development. Her history is ordinary until years into adult work.”
> Not inherited. Not given. Not studied.
> It establishes no general cause beyond the history we have.
- Problem: An explanatory chorus and procedure routine at the decisive exchange. The reader has watched every test. Here Uvena repeats her L173 finding, Ishket and Beral take turns delivering an exclusion, Zorevi restates all of them, and the narration lists them a third time as a triplet before the name. The name comes out of that triplet rather than out of anything said or seen. The closing hedge (L325) then states the story's point about bounded claims outright, echoing L173 and the license terms.
- Fix: Keep Zorevi's residue question and Beral's "I will not claim it to make your paper easier" (new information and character), and keep Zorevi's too-long definition with Pelmara's "Shorter." (a real payoff). Cut Uvena's L313 recap. Cut the triplet at L319 and let the word come from what Zorevi is actually looking at: the ten years in Pelmara's hands and the cloudy basin. "Grown, though no one had meant to grow it" can stay. Trim L325 to the act of writing. Ishket's objection and the provisional license already carry the limit.

### D4 · Major · "Ask the worker" states the lesson

- Where: L327–L329, before the license terms
- Text:
> “Ask the worker what she can safely do.”
> Pelmara looked surprised by the question, which shamed Zorevi more than the locked fingers had.
- Problem: The Philosopher, with a labelled emotion. Pelmara has directed every test since L143, has been asked about her perception and limits repeatedly (L161, L183, L201, L209), and set her own limit a minute ago at L299. A secondary character now tells the chair to consult her, and the narration assigns surprise and shame so that the moral lands. The line tells the reader what the scene has already shown, and the surprise is only weakly supported.
- Fix: Cut the shame sentence. Have Zorevi ask Pelmara directly as the next step in drafting the scope, because the license needs it, not as a revelation. Alternatively give Uvena a practical reason, such as the holder stating the scope in her own words for the record. Keep Pelmara's L331 answer exactly; "Don't write tile" is the best line in the coda.

### D5 · Minor · Second last-word closer in the coda

- Where: L345–L347, Ishket's objection
- Text:
> “Record it,” Zorevi said. “The license is limited because it remains.”
- Problem: An epigram used as a last word. It comes straight after "Complaining does not invalidate a signature" (L341), which earns Pelmara's smile, so the coda closes two exchanges in a row with a neat chiastic line.
- Fix: Keep L341. Make L347 a plain acknowledgement: Zorevi writes his objection in, or says only "Record it."

### D6 · Minor · Uvena's role arrives late and her voice splits

- Where: L31 (first appearance), L173 (Inherited ruling)
- Text:
> “Tea?” Uvena asked from the kettle.
> “That eliminates this case from Inherited certification. It says nothing about her future children, if any, or anyone else’s.”
- Problem: Voice and attribution. The reader meets Uvena as whoever is making tea and only learns she is the Inherited examiner from L173, so her knowledge of Pelmara at fourteen and sixteen (L165) arrives unsupported. She is the plain-word speaker at L87 ("Write heat"), then switches to legal clauses. The "future children, if any" tail is a policy hedge, and "if any" is needlessly sharp toward the woman next to her.
- Fix: Give her office one concrete cue at first appearance (L109 already implies that examiners wear house colours, or she could have Pelmara's file). Cut the second sentence of L173, or say it in her plain register.

### D7 · Minor · "You never withdrew it before" gives the act to the wrong person

- Where: L61–L65, just before the withdrawal
- Text:
> “You never withdrew it before.”
- Problem: Ground/referent. Beral is the one who withdraws the gift. The line is meant to explain his "Possibly" but names Pelmara as the agent. If it is meant as a dig ("you never ended it before"), nothing marks it as one.
- Fix: Make the explanation his own ("I've never taken it back"). If the dig is wanted, give it a beat that makes it legible, which would also help D2.

### D8 · Minor · Opening correction chain and a loose referent

- Where: L13–L29, the rings and the permit
- Text:
> Mark it thin on the east side.
> “Your permit expires,” Zorevi said. “Permits do not—”
- Problem: The first page is five corrections in a row (lies, measured, glaze, irregular, expires), which sets the house rhythm before any other rhythm appears. "East side" of a loose ring that Zorevi has just turned has no fixed referent.
- Fix: Keep "That one lies," the glaze, and Uvena's "Tea?" cutting Zorevi off. That interruption deflates her pedantry well. Tie the mark to something visible ("thin under the blister") instead of a compass point.

### D9 · Minor · Narration glosses lines just heard

- Where: L151, L169, L291, L315 (also L45, L107, L329 under D2/D4)
- Text:
> Her sentences were coming apart, but her placement commands were exact.
> Uvena ignored the edge in it.
> The word was not loud. It left nothing loose around it.
> She could hear herself building a definition too long to survive the page.
- Problem: Mechanics. Each sentence explains the line the reader has just read or is about to read. The L315 sentence steps on "Shorter." by making Pelmara's joke before she does. L217 ("Zorevi heard herself become more exact as certainty left her") does the same job once and does it well.
- Fix: Keep L217. Cut the L315 sentence so "Shorter." lands alone. Cut or reduce the others to a physical beat.

### D10 · Minor · "Not that stair" comes before anyone moves toward it

- Where: L363–L367, leaving the source room
- Text:
> Not that stair. South passage.
> “The west stair is shorter.”
- Problem: Ground/staging. Pelmara redirects Zorevi from a stair the reader has not seen her head for, so "that stair" only resolves after Zorevi names it.
- Fix: Add a half-clause before Pelmara's line showing Zorevi turning toward the west stair. Keep the exchange; "Crate clips the second rail" is a good ending line of practical knowledge.

## Prose findings

### P1 · Major · Water infrastructure is the trade and the stakes (policy)

- Where: L27, L57, L185, L199, L215, L339, L363, L369 (cistern keeper, cistern repair, "drain the line", the failed run)
- Text:
> The cistern keeper already thinks I’m inventing paperwork to avoid his west stack.
> It decided who might legally repair a cistern before lunch.
> If the cistern takes pressure, it opens inward.
> The keeper can drain the line while these rest.
- Problem: `universe/style-guide.md` "No default water infrastructure" (CANON; applies to authorized revisions) bars water works as a default occupation, stakes or texture, and the prompt does not ask for them. Here they are material: Pelmara's job, the waiting cistern, the keeper who must sign, and the closing image. The basin, hand-washing and warm water are ordinary water and can stay.
- Fix: Do one consistent sweep that re-homes the trade to non-water fired ceramics, such as flue or chimney stacks or kiln works. "West stack", "fired pipe", "couplings", "failed run" and the load-frame test all survive. A stack's weight on the coupling also suits the frame test better than water pressure. Rename the keeper to match (stack keeper, kiln keeper), change "drain the line" to letting the stack or kiln go cold, and change L57 and L369 to match.

### P2 · Minor · Negation template, especially the non-magic reassurances

- Where: L57, L71, L107, L125, L151, L197, L291, L319, L351
- Text:
> It had never performed magic.
> not magical, just thin brass meant to carry a fracture’s sound
> Nothing answered the pressure of the seal except paper.
- Problem: "Not X / only X / nothing but" is the default way the narration defines things. Four separate reassurances that tools are inert (L57, L151, L197, L351) repeat one point, and L319 turns the template into a triplet (D3).
- Fix: Keep L71 (the anti-spectacle withdrawal is the point) and one tool reassurance, and state the others positively or cut them.

### P3 · Minor · Ending restates the seal paragraph and closes on an emblem

- Where: L351 and L369
- Text:
> Behind them, the blank space on the next form remained blank.
> hands still learning how far they could close
- Problem: L369 restates L351's "The form still printed only three choices", and "blank space ... remained blank" is circular. It also blurs whether the house field is a blank (L301, L325) or three printed choices (L351). The last clause turns the story into a closing image.
- Fix: Decide what the form looks like (three printed choices with a write-in line, for example) and use it the same way at L301, L325 and L351. Cut the "remained blank" sentence. End on Zorevi following Pelmara, or keep the last sentence shorter and concrete.

## Continuity and prompt findings

### C1 · Minor · Zorevi's regret contradicts her own stop terms

- Where: L217 (authorization) and L263 (locked hands)
- Text:
> with immediate cessation upon pain, loss of sensation, tremor, joint stiffness, or altered
> Zorevi watched the water collect in the basin and hated that she had agreed before defining how they would stop.
- Problem: Zorevi did define when to stop, at length. What she failed to plan was how Pelmara would let go. As written, the regret reads as a contradiction.
- Fix: Make the regret specific: she defined when to stop, not how Pelmara's hands would release.

### C2 · Minor · The second-giver possibility is raised and never closed

- Where: L47–L53 (affirmation) and L315 (Zorevi's definition)
- Text:
> I don’t know every hand that ever touched hers.
> cannot currently be transferred by its former giver
- Problem: Beral's hedge is written into the record at Pelmara's insistence. The later exclusion of the Gifted category covers only Beral, so the certification's chain of reasoning skips a possibility the record itself raised.
- Fix: One short exchange in the naming sequence: Zorevi asks Pelmara whether anyone else ever gave to her hands, and she answers in her own terms. Alternatively Zorevi notes the gap as part of why the entry is provisional.
