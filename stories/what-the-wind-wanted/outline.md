# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Newly able to understand every language, Zerune expects a magnificent afternoon among foreign books. A sea breeze whispers from their fluttering pages and recruits her to carry it through a mountain tunnel to a courtship. Deliver the pleasure, bodily intimacy, and unnerving enlargement of comprehending an intelligence made of moving air.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Zerune loves deciphering inscriptions, overheard disputes, and annotations. Her new faculty feeds an existing appetite. The breeze offers something she cannot bring home between covers; curiosity can make her neglect fatigue and sensible reservations.
- Counterforce or complication: The breeze wants access to a sun-warmed basin before the afternoon currents change. It is vain, impatient, and attracted elsewhere. Zerune wants its undivided attention and an explanation worth the climb. Understanding its language supplies neither shared bodily experience nor agreement about an acceptable risk.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through adult Zerune. Translation arrives directly as meaning, with wind noises remaining audible. Distinguish the currents' statements from what she can verify. Leave the gift's origin unexplained.
- Governing movement and time shape: One afternoon opening outward: market, rising footpath, short rock tunnel, enclosed mountain basin. Expand the crossing into bodily seconds, then let the final exchange unfold across the landscape. Target 2,500–4,000 words.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Zerune understands every language she perceives, including unfamiliar writing and signing; she gains neither omniscience nor automatic fluency in producing languages. This living wind maintains a coherent circulating body, feeds on warmth, and communicates through patterned airflow. Cold still air can disperse it; warm humid lungs can sustain it briefly. It cannot carry Zerune, command weather, or cross the cold ridge intact. Her breath remains ordinarily limited. Its existing comprehension of local human speech comes from listening to people.
- Dialogue promise: Nonhuman small talk becomes startlingly personal. Preserve the breeze's vanity, distraction, and alien embodied meanings; comprehension permits argument and fascination, not instant intimacy.
- Dialogic medium: Human speech, translated whispers through leaves and clothing, demonstrative movements of air, and a brief internal flutter of comprehensible wind-language during the held breath. The answering basin current speaks through vegetation over a much larger area.
- Dialogue engine: Questions draw Zerune off her intended route. Disagreement over transport makes each participant disclose a vulnerability. Successful passage permits a courtship exchange whose changing speakers reveal more than explanations could.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Inquisitive narration follows moving edges: pages, sleeve openings, grasses, lips. Vary leisurely noticing with the bodily attention of carrying a breath. Let translated meaning sharpen physical sound.
- Conversational texture: Zerune asks digressive, sometimes embarrassingly eager questions. The breeze volunteers boasts and interrupts itself to taste passing warmth. Practical single-purpose speech belongs beside strange disclosures. Silence during transit has an immediate physical reason.
- Rhetorical ownership: Zerune owns literary comparisons, skeptical humor, and intrusive questioning. The breeze measures achievement by routes, things touched, and currents survived; it evades unflattering detail and listens best when its journey is threatened. The basin current owns selection and refusal, without becoming an explanatory chorus.
- Pressure behavior: On the climb Zerune grows terse while the breeze becomes expansive. Before entering her mouth their fluency reverses. Inside the tunnel neither can negotiate at length; establish the choice beforehand.
- Relationship movement: Zerune wants revelation; the breeze wants passage and cannot comfortably admit its physical smallness. Neither promises lasting companionship. Their bargain changes risk and access; arrival ends the breeze's dependence while intensifying her interest.
- Anti-default: Do not make the wind a wise guide correcting human attachment, or turn the courtship into a disguised human reconciliation. Its desire should remain inconveniently its own.

## Beats

1. Establish delight and inconvenience: Zerune understands an unreadable book, a vendor's foreign complaint, and customers signing, but cannot automatically answer in their languages. Incidental noises are not necessarily speech. A current whispers while worrying the book's leaves. She looks for a concealed speaker before addressing it.
2. Give first contact time. The breeze deliberately gathers spilled chaff into its rotating body, seeks warmth from sunlit stone, and recoils from a cold doorway. Establish it as a living participant, distinct from surrounding unvoiced air. It has heard people all its life; being answered is new. Their questions are unequally interesting to each other. Its candid attraction to a distant current replaces Zerune's initial expectation of ancient secrets.
3. Follow the route uphill, with breathlessness and a sea view. The breeze displays its courtship: accumulated smells, remembered paths expressed as turns, a boast Zerune finds unimpressive. It wants the basin beyond the ridge. It cannot survive the cold exposed saddle; a short, level pedestrian bore reaches the warm side. Establish both mouths and length before proposing transit.
4. At the tunnel, make the physical disagreement consequential. Zerune refuses to hold her breath for an undefined duration or run blind. She walks through and back without it, confirming the exit and distance. The breeze admits it may scatter if released inside. Her lungs offer available warmth and humidity, not a universal privilege of human bodies. They agree that she can exhale whenever she must; there is no magical contract. She chooses the attempt from appetite, fully understanding its purpose.
5. Carry the breeze. Render its entry as pressure, salt, and organized movement behind her ribs, without injury or erotic framing. The short passage feels enormous with another life inside her breath. A brief change in its airflow conveys fear. She reaches daylight and releases it into warmth. No collapse or rescue team.
6. Let the payoff occupy substantial scene space. The freed breeze addresses the basin current, which ignores part of its display and answers another part. Zerune understands the exchange while discovering what its references encompass. The currents choose to mingle; their speech acquires a new plural speaker, while both earlier voices remain audible within it. This establishes a witnessed transformation without certifying their entire biology. Zerune has accomplished the passage and been left outside the courtship. End amid her absorption in the widening conversation, with her descent still to come; no farewell lesson or scripted final line.
