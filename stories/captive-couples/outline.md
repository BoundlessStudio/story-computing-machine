# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Married heroes Renata Solis (Nearside) and Jonas Solis (Fixedpoint), already in a trust-and-command rupture, are trapped while stopping married villains Livia Kade (Brownout) and Xavier Kade (Downforce). Reveal the villains' marital competence through conduct, then make changed hero coordination necessary for escape without mistaking relational health for goodness or declaring the heroes repaired.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Renata wants equal field authority but concealed that an injury worsened her power's visual cost. Jonas found discrepancies, unilaterally added a veto to their patrol app, and reduced her to extraction duty. She withholds capacity; he turns questions into commands. Both want the partnership and the theft stopped, but distrust each other's risk decisions.
- Counterforce or complication: The villains designed the trap around those patterns and have forty-five minutes to drain a neighborhood heat-wave reserve into portable cells. Divided expertise and practiced correction make them harder to disrupt. They still choose abduction, grid damage, and danger to clinics and an assisted-living tower for profit.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Renata. She observes conduct but cannot know either villain's private motives or generalize from one night. Jonas's hand numbness and the villains' thresholds emerge only through necessary action and speech.
- Governing movement and time shape: Seventy-five minutes: failed entry, captive observation, capacity reckoning, timed escape, then hospital intake. Keep the climax in the unnamed transfer station; no public proof, crowd conversion, or institutional coda.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Nearside carries herself and one held person or object up to five metres along a visible clear line, three times per six hours; each transit compounds vertigo and lost depth perception. Fixedpoint grows two rigid planes within six metres of sight, each lasting thirty seconds; concurrent use numbs his hands. Brownout's living field drains active circuits within three metres into two finite cells, never bodies or disconnected storage. Downforce's battery harness multiplies downward load on three tagged nonliving objects for sixty seconds before cooling. Their gear stores or shapes power but never acts independently.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Renata's close narration moves by distances, exits, body orientation, and omitted capacity; spatial certainty frays into doubled edges as her cost rises, while marital inference remains tentative.
- Conversational texture: Hero talk has clipped operational shorthand and ordinary domestic familiarity damaged by missed answers. Villain talk mixes workmanlike task calls, irritation, brief checking, and silence; neither couple performs polished therapy. Any responder speech is minimal and logistical.
- Rhetorical ownership: Renata reasons through routes and usable seconds; Jonas through contingencies and failure trees. Livia owns electrical measures and hard thresholds; Xavier owns load, sequence, and physical fit.
- Pressure behavior: Renata first evades, then states exact limits and grows less fluent. Jonas overexplains, notices her withdrawal, and waits. Livia becomes curt; Xavier repeats sequences when rattled. Repair stays brief and task-adjacent.
- Anti-default: Avoid quip combat, synchronized villain spouses, charming manifestos, quote-card marriage advice, equal eloquence, or a final exchange that explains what coordinated action already showed.

## Beats

1. A staged outage draws the heroes underground. Renata rejects Jonas's altered route and spends two transits on an unagreed flank. Livia kills comms and lights; Xavier drops tagged shutters and anchor cuffs. Renata lands in a baffled cage; Jonas commits both walls to reaching her and is sealed separately. Damaged coordination enables a capable trap.
2. As the siphon starts, Livia owns charge and timing; Xavier owns prisoners, tags, and exit. She corrects his switch order from meter evidence without belittling him, and he adjusts. When she assumes he will hold an overheating tag, he refuses; she redistributes. They disagree, listen, set an abort temperature, and still choose the harmful theft.
3. Jonas seeks Renata's remaining count as if authorizing it; she minimizes symptoms and tests restraints alone. Livia snaps over a failing cell, then acknowledges the displaced blame and checks Xavier's burned palm without taking over his choice. Renata registers competent repair alongside deliberate danger to strangers, not moral redemption.
4. A harness cooling cycle creates one opening. Renata volunteers her true count and impaired-vision window, then proposes action. Jonas admits only one wall is reliable, supplies observations, and waits. His expanding wall wedges a cage baffle; when Xavier transfers a load tag to the cell sled, Renata uses her final transit through the exposed sightline.
5. Renata lands free but nearly blind. Jonas calls distances while holding one wall against a tagged shutter; she follows changing facts to his manual release. The villains counter independently, then coordinate over cells versus recapture. The heroes isolate the reserve and ground the siphon load. At their agreed heat limit, both villains abandon the charged sled and escape; their marriage helps them survive while their crime fails.
6. The heroes protect the reserve and injured utility staff rather than pursue. At intake, Renata gives the true symptom history; Jonas owns the app veto as his unilateral act. Avoid full confession or absolution. They remain married but uncertain about field partnership; the bounded movement is that immediate medical and reporting choices are asked and answered, not made for each other. End during assessment, not on a lesson.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The modern-like low-magic era resembles contemporary Earth. Magic, technology, and divinity are cultural labels; only life uses magic, while nonliving equipment may store or shape supplied power. Every capability here is local and finite.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Establish no link to existing heroes, magical-girl systems, factions, cities, power origins, or universal cost rules. The four adults retain independent judgment; healthy habits do not excuse criminal choices, and the rough patch excludes coercive romance or domestic abuse.
