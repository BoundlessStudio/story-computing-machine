# Editorial notes

## November 20, 2026

**Codex promise keeper, before seeing a plot.** The explicit relationship is the young conductor's work as a guide to lost spirits across the midnight storm crossing. The keeper expected “recognizable city transit doing work that regular service can’t do” and an arrival or attempted arrival that made the disappearance of a particular destination matter. “At midnight,” “heaviest rainstorms,” and “forgotten gods” suggested a hushed beginning with room for awe. The request leaves the scale, particular bonds, and ending open. In the reference the young rider looks “contained and intent,” alert to a change outside, with amber lamps reflected in the wet dark over a familiar green bench.

**Claude, before seeing a plot.** “The ordinary meeting the sacred” was the appeal, together with a destination the reader could see for themselves. Claude read the uniform as a suggestion of a daytime school life and inferred a quiet, intimate, possibly bittersweet ride. The request gives the job as a secret and leaves the conductor's daytime life, the size of the stakes, and the shape of the ending to the writer. Claude saw a “self-contained, tired, a little scruffy” rider at home on a familiar bench. The tram-like interior suggested how a city bus could look and feel.
