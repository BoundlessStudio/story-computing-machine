# All at Once

Provisional direction, replacing the five-anchor ensemble. The superseded draft is preserved at `tmp/ensemble-superseded-draft.md`; no new full prose yet.

The user-authorized lens is one newly awakened searching consciousness, an original telepath rather than a borrowed character or a machine. She is an early-morning local radio presenter. At the instant of the worldwide event, unbearable planetary input overwhelms her ordinary shift. Narrowing attention to a condition brings fleeting access to actual current minds, powered and unpowered. She has no census, coordinates or authoritative account of what she sees. Some matches are wrong, metaphorical or terrifyingly incomplete. Her microphone provides a limited ordinary channel, and the temptation to sound certain before she is. Her bodily frame stays brief. She is not the planet's coordinator or saviour.

Time moves through the first twenty-four hours. There is no calendar date or later coda. The reader enters many people's sensations, immediate wants and actions directly, sometimes for a sentence, sometimes for a sustained encounter. No fixed principal cast has privileged recurrence. Consequences, sensory details and changed questions connect the glimpses; lives continue after attention leaves them. The global birth remains the action, rather than a common evacuation crisis.

**First minutes:** a desperate narrowing toward people falling opens aircraft, ordinary stairs, involuntary levitation, somebody who can fly and somebody newly invulnerable who cannot. Other searches bring fire, phasing, shapeshifting, altered bodies and impossible strength as experiences people do not yet possess names for. Tiny wants make people particular: catch a hand, put down a stolen thing, keep a newly chosen face, get one breath. Delight and catastrophe coexist.

**Hours of naming and claiming:** she searches for people who know, hoping for an account she can give listeners. The condition returns conviction rather than truth. She passes on one frightening interpretation; a later mind exposes part of the resulting harm and something the confident source did not see. She cannot retract what people have already done. That error changes her questions from authority to experience. She enters frightened governments, rescuers, people founding territories and ordinary people refusing to leave one another. Standard and stranger gifts change work, hunger, ownership, bodies and violence, without one power supplying one approved moral. Joy need not be punished; mixed motives need not invalidate a rescue. No single hypothesis accounts for the birth.

**Middle hours:** an opening falling mind recurs while much of the outside day has passed. She initially treats it as intrusive memory or trauma. Searches prompted by that contradiction find unlike instances: someone has apparently lived far more time than the outside day, while another is still waiting through an ordinary interrupted moment. The reader may suspect temporal fracture before she knows what action it warrants. These encounters change her next conditions; simultaneity and human causes of later disasters do not substitute for mystery movement.

**Last hours:** at one unstable local building an outside demolition worker's drill or work light enters a delayed person's current experience, provoking a new action. She witnesses both the worker and the person through separate glimpses. There is no new two-way telepathy or repeated verification protocol. Material details in their current attention identify a reachable site; ordinary radio/telephone carries a warning. The provisional partial discovery is that some people are living different durations since the same onset, in both directions. This does not locate all missing people, return them, explain the source or turn powers into trapped companions. Resurrection varies, many deaths are real, and silence is not proof of death. Whether the fractures belong to the event itself or particular involuntary gifts remains uncertain, but unlike instances make the pattern planetary rather than one local rescue.

The warning forces an independent officer's choice: demolish a structure threatening an evacuation route, or delay and close that route, sending people through a dangerous alternative. Acting on imperfect evidence costs something real either way. Others refuse warnings, cannot be reached or have already acted. The lens cannot broadcast a solution to the whole planet. Simultaneously, powers elsewhere continue producing joy, coercion, war, intimacy, rescue and new geography that this finding cannot settle.

The ending belongs to the last hour and opens a new task rather than completing a universal rescue. The search condition has changed from escape from the noise to finding those not yet reached by the shared day. Many living moments continue beyond her attention; many cannot be recovered. The narrator can state the prompt's first-day scale without giving her impossible knowledge: seven billion at the start, 2.8 billion powers, 1.3 billion dead or missing. Unknown origin remains, but the pieces have altered what people dare do next.

Main risks for challenge: the search becoming a menu or ability catalogue; its lens becoming a hidden omniscient investigator; temporal mismatch being declared from insufficient mental evidence; a partial survival finding softening the mass loss; or the mystery swallowing the worldwide birth. These should be tested through scenes, not solved with a taxonomy or a procedure.
