# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Senior salvager Kedra learns that apprentice Beshan sold Cerdovan's civic name-strip to buy the brake leaf that saved their caravan. The buyer is already using it in the ruin's dissolution engine. Kedra must stop the dismantling, restore what remains of the name, and assign consequence without undoing either the saved caravan or damaged record.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Kedra needs her crew over the shelf road before closure, yet her cutter's authority makes the code enforceable. Beshan violated it to solve a danger she had deferred. Her power to dismiss or bind him sharpens every order.
- Counterforce or complication: Lurem bought a working authorization and committed credit and labor. Repaying him costs the caravan its haul and road. Keeper Sovet needs the strip returned in context while the engine tears other name-bearing plates free.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Kedra, who reads weights, cuts, custody, and competence better than motives. She learns the choice through missing inventory, partial overhearing, and Beshan's pressured account; Lurem's prior knowledge and Cerdovan's history remain unknown.
- Governing movement and time shape: One day: aborted departure, return, contained shutdown and bargain, then restoration at two anchors. Divide the climax between Kedra's material concession and re-seating; end next morning with altered assignments and a poorer caravan taking the low road.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Name-residue is any original surface bearing a person or place designation, or an identifying tally joined to one; adjacent metal may be removed only after it is exposed and supported. A city's name requires the same inscription at two public anchors with matching foundation teeth; copies do not qualify. Cerdovan's nonliving engine accepts either original strip as authorization to release tagged civic metal. Re-seating the intact strip against its mate reserves what remains but cannot restore crushed plates, position, or provenance. Nothing erases memory or speaks for the dead.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Follow Kedra's load-bearing attention—weights, cuts, fasteners, route time, and loose object versus evidence in place. Let manifests and handwork expose emotion first.
- Conversational texture: Use inventory calls, interruptions, task-bound complaints, exact bargaining, refusals, witnesses, ordinary irritation, and working silence.
- Rhetorical ownership: Kedra owns sequence, duty, and cost; Beshan failure contingencies and repair; crew loads, animals, meals, and road; Lurem custody and sunk expense; Sovet provenance among fragments.
- Pressure behavior: Kedra's commands shorten while judgment waits. Beshan begins fast and over-complete, loses fluency, then gains precision during restoration. Lurem repeats terms; Sovet withholds cooperation when rushed; crew talk splinters by role.
- Anti-default: Avoid master-apprentice ethics debate, villain buyer, sacred-name exposition, wise chorus, polished confession, or final code recital.

## Beats

1. At dawn weigh-out, Kedra finds the cistern strip missing from the leave ledger and an unbilled brake leaf on the lead wagon. Inventory calls and partial overhearing establish Beshan's trade; departure has under an hour of margin.
2. The cut proves deliberate. Beshan acted after the old brake split and Lurem refused other collateral; he meant to redeem the strip after crossing and concealed the sale to prevent another delay. Kedra turns the caravan around and suspends his cutting authority. Crew objections expose costs in water, wages, animals, and road time.
3. Lurem's hands are running Cerdovan's dissolution engine. Tagged beams drag lintel plates and mortuary indices toward rollers. Sovet's keepers mark losses but cannot cross the moving line. Kedra directs shutdown; Beshan's knowledge of the reader is necessary without returning command.
4. Lurem stops the feed but will not absorb his expense. Kedra cannot call a legitimate buyer a thief. She gives up the clean-metal haul and shelf-road bond for the strip, cessation, and labor returning pieces. The caravan keeps its brake but loses profit and must take the longer road.
5. A jam threatens to bend the strip. Kedra holds the release load while Beshan follows Sovet's alignment directions and crew separate tagged metal from scrap. Keep talk procedural and unequal; sacrifice of metal and time, not persuasion, resolves the immediate conflict.
6. They locate the second anchor, match teeth, shim and re-seat the strip, then collect separate witness marks. Sovet reconnects mapped plates; crushed or mixed pieces enter a context-loss lot. The engine reserves surviving civic metal without supplying judgment or history.
7. Kedra neither absolves nor expels Beshan. She removes his buying and cutting privileges, diverts his next share to keeper labor, and leaves him provisioned under Sovet until inventory ends; continued apprenticeship depends on this repair. The caravan departs poorer and late amid weight and water calls.
