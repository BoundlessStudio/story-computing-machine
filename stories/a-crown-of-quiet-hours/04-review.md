#### Continuity

**Finding P1-CON-01**

- **Severity:** Major
- **Location:** `03-draft.md`, first cavern count and transformation (lines
  69–71 and 89–122), compared with the later emergence and settlement (lines
  430–453, 539, and 555)
- **Evidence:** Istra enters a cavern and explicitly counts twelve dragons
  before separately seeing Queen Orvessa IV. Orvessa then transforms and says
  that the royal offices rotate “among twelve of us,” establishing Kharost as
  one of that twelve. The climax and charter instead use the intended count:
  Kharost plus eleven other dragons.
- **Why it matters:** The reveal moment produces thirteen members—twelve
  already in dragon form plus Kharost—while the plan, prompt implementation,
  climax, and resolution all establish a twelve-member collective. This is an
  explicit identity/count contradiction at the premise's central reveal.
- **Smallest effective fix:** Change Istra's initial cavern count to eleven,
  leaving Kharost's transformation to reveal the twelfth. If desired, add a
  brief recount after the transformation, but preserve Kharost plus eleven
  others everywhere.

**Finding P1-CON-02**

- **Severity:** Minor
- **Location:** `03-draft.md`, dragon descriptions and Kharost's fire injury
  (lines 81–82 and 446–449)
- **Evidence:** The opening assigns a shoulder scar to the largest black
  dragon. During the climax, the distinct red-gold Kharost catches a beam
  across one shoulder and a scale splits “along her old scar,” although no
  scar has previously been assigned to her.
- **Why it matters:** Reusing the specifically seeded shoulder-scar detail on
  a different-colored dragon creates a momentary attribute-transfer ambiguity
  in a story whose twelve bodies and identities must remain distinct.
- **Smallest effective fix:** Remove “old” and describe a new split scale on
  Kharost's shoulder, or seed Kharost's separate scar during her first
  transformation. Do not move the black dragon's established scar.
