# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.
Every item below is a proposed, chronologically unpinned, local fact from
`the-quiet-party`; none is authoritative merely because it appears in the
final story or in this delta.

## New characters or character facts

- **Merrit Loam** (`Merrit`; she/her) is a conscientious field registrar for
  the local Latchwater Contract Guild. She understands the mechanical service
  drawings she audits and begins the Mallowgate assignment convinced that
  audible readback is the most reliable proof of coordination. During the
  rescue, she authorizes a field variation, joins the four-position manual
  release, and recognizes that observable acknowledgement, correction, and
  integration can be audited across multiple communication media. She then
  certifies the Quiet Party's flawless work, records that coerced acceptance
  does not validate the guild's corrective premise, and accepts the loss of
  the quiet senior registrar desk she had sought. The guild assigns her to
  administer the resulting local communication-protocol portfolio.
- **Veda Crake** (`Veda`; she/her) is a silent structural defender,
  stoneworker-rescuer, and shield bearer with an excellent safety record. She
  reads load, fracture, and balance; becomes very still when she identifies
  structural danger; and communicates through a charcoal slate, diagrams,
  exact stress marks, demonstrations, gaze, and acknowledgement. Before the
  story, the local guild suspended her from civic rescue contracts after she
  declined oral debriefs. At Mallowgate she designs and maintains the
  structural brace and helps operate the release wheel.
- **Osket Firth** (`Osket`; he/him) is a silent rope scout and rigging
  specialist whose field outcomes are excellent. He communicates through
  placed equipment, physical demonstration, pointing, expressive attention,
  visible questions, and deliberate waits for acknowledgement. At Mallowgate
  he rigs the high traverse, rescue line, checkline, and nest sling; frees the
  reedmaw nest from three outer bindings; and relocates it intact.
- **Pim Fallow** (`Pim`; they/them) is a silent field medic and river
  naturalist whose local contract work funds a small field practice. They use
  waterproof written slips, demonstrations, face-checks, rephrasing, and
  deliberate pauses to verify comprehension. At Mallowgate they calm and treat
  an injured sluice keeper, identify and protect three young reedmaws, and use
  bitterroot to redirect the animals without harm.
- Veda, Osket, and Pim do not share a stated diagnosis, vow, origin, magical
  effect, telepathic link, hive mind, or prearranged private code. Their refusal
  to speak is a respected boundary distinct from refusal to communicate. Their
  successful coordination depends on ordinary, visible communication and
  active repair of uncertainty.
- The **unnamed guildmaster** and **two unnamed sluice keepers** receive no
  character-facing names or aliases. The guildmaster creates the compelled
  assignment and later accepts its administratively inconvenient result. The
  keepers are rescued from Mallowgate; one sustains a strained wrist with no
  break.

## New locations

- **Latchwater** is a chronologically unpinned town whose lower homes are
  protected by a reservoir and local relief works. No relationship to any
  established era or named canon location is proposed.
- **Mallowgate sluice** is an aging flood-control work in Latchwater's
  immediate district. It includes a reservoir feeder cut, gorge, stone
  gatehouse, relief gate, counterweight frame, service platform and catwalk,
  crest gauge, spill pool, and a four-position manual-release assembly. Its
  gate protects the lower Latchwater homes.

## New factions or cultural facts

- The **Latchwater Contract Guild** is a town- or district-scale adventuring
  bureaucracy with authority over its own local contracts only. It maintains
  charters, service drawings, field circulars, safety rubrics, civic rescue
  assignments, debrief customs, registrar posts, and contract-access records.
  It can suspend access to Latchwater contracts, but no broader jurisdiction is
  proposed.
- Before the Mallowgate event, the guild's custom treated oral debriefs and
  audible readback as the conventional proof of cooperation even though its
  rubric separately recorded an instruction sent, acknowledgement received,
  and correction integrated.
- The guild's local **corrective-composition rule** allowed leadership to
  condition continued contract access on completion of a compelled, graded
  joint assignment. In this case, refusal would have suspended each
  adventurer's access to Latchwater contracts for one month. The story and
  Merrit's report treat this coercion as ethically unsupported by the party's
  later success.
- Under the same local rule, a corrective unit that completes a graded civic
  rescue without safety demerits automatically becomes a standing local party,
  and its demonstrated field protocol enters the Latchwater field circular.
  The effect is administrative and contractual, not magical or inherent in
  names.
- After Mallowgate, local guild members request written briefings, diagram
  space, demonstrated readbacks, and equivalent acknowledgement options. The
  demand is sufficient to create a new administrative workload for Merrit; it
  does not establish a setting-wide reform.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Mallowgate operating mechanics:** The relief gate normally uses a toothed
  counterweight and relief chain. When the last counterweight link is lost and
  the release wheel is obstructed, the manual release requires four coordinated
  positions: directing animals away from the gate, controlling a nest sling,
  bracing the lintel while turning the wheel, and quarter-turning and pulling a
  recessed release pin. A high traverse, checkline, shield brace, safety hooks,
  and explicit load checks can keep the cracked structure and suspended loads
  controlled during the operation.
- **Reedmaws** are living local river animals with broad heads, fringed jaws,
  heavy tails, and young that make thin, reedy chirrs. They weave buoyant nests
  from reeds and debris; the observed nest has an inner bowl protecting its
  young and outer woven loops that can fasten it to branches. Adults flare
  their jaw fringes and may lunge away or thrash when startled by sharp noise.
  They follow bitterroot scent through water and can be redirected toward safe
  shallows without being handled or harmed. No personhood, supernatural power,
  or wider range is proposed for the species.
- **Bitterroot** has a sharp medicinal scent that carries in water and attracts
  or redirects the reedmaws observed at Mallowgate. The story establishes only
  this local behavioral use; it does not establish a magical property.
- The Quiet Party's local **observable-acknowledgement protocol** evaluates
  whether information is received, inspected or tested, corrected when needed,
  and integrated before action. Evidence may be supplied through writing,
  diagrams, gestures, demonstrations, object placement, eye contact, deliberate
  holds, or speech. The protocol preserves active communication and
  comprehension checks; it does not claim that speech or communication is
  unnecessary, or that one medium is always superior.
- Site-specific signals used during the rescue—Pim's written *HOLD*, *CLEAR*,
  and *STOP* slips and Veda's palm-down stroke—are proposed, positioned,
  inspected, and acknowledged in view of all four workers before use. They are
  not a hidden or pre-existing code.
- Merrit's adherence to accurate evidence has a concrete career cost: her
  truthful report removes the expected path to the quiet senior desk and gives
  her responsibility for a noisier, larger portfolio.

## Timeline events

- **Chronologically unpinned — the Mallowgate rescue:** During storm runoff,
  the guildmaster compels Veda, Osket, and Pim to undertake a joint graded
  rescue under Merrit's supervision. They evacuate two sluice keepers, separate
  and protect a reedmaw nest, survive an unforeseen waterlogged-trunk impact,
  and open the relief gate before the reservoir overtops. The lower Latchwater
  homes, the workers, and the reedmaws remain safe.
- **The following morning:** Merrit files a report finding flawless reciprocal
  coordination and no safety demerits, while also rejecting the coercive
  premise. The guild's own charter clause makes the Quiet Party a standing
  local party and places its demonstrated protocol in the Latchwater field
  circular. Merrit is assigned to administer the resulting requests and
  procedure.
