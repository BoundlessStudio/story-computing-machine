# Canon brief

## Hard constraints

- The prompt contract is controlling for this production pass even where the
  shared-universe notes are silent: the story needs at least three individually
  legible adventurers who never speak aloud, a guild-imposed team assignment, a
  real coordination test, no telepathic coordination or prearranged shared code
  that trivializes the achievement, and a comic resolution in which the
  punishment succeeds as an operational precedent.
  Silence must not be framed as a moral defect, and the story must not imply
  that communication itself is unnecessary. These are story requirements, not
  claims about universe-wide lore. (`stories/the-quiet-party/00-prompt.md`,
  headings `Story controls`, `Assumptions`, and `Completion tests`)
- Place the story somewhere in the one persistent physical world. It may be a
  stand-alone audience entry point and does not need to mention the wider
  cosmology, but it must not casually establish a separate world, plane, or
  timeline as the default setting. (`universe/premise.md`, heading `The
  deep-time shared world`; `universe/style-guide.md`, heading `Tonal range,
  scale, and connection`)
- Treat “magic,” “technology,” and “divinity” as vocabulary used by the local
  culture, not as objective cosmic categories. If a nonliving tool performs
  magic during the mission, it may store, focus, transmit, or shape power
  supplied through living agency; it cannot independently be a magic user
  unless the story establishes that it is alive. (`universe/rules.md`, headings
  `System categories` and `Living agency and magic`;
  `universe/glossary.md`, heading `Magic, technology, and divinity`)
- Do not give the guild setting-wide jurisdiction or imply that one universal
  hidden authority regulates adventurers or anomalous power in every era.
  The prompt's assumption that this is a local bureaucracy is compatible with
  canon and should remain local. Any institution established for recurring use
  must stay consistent in later stories. (`universe/premise.md`, heading
  `Knowledge and connection`; `stories/the-quiet-party/00-prompt.md`, heading
  `Assumptions`)
- Names are not universally magical. Any contract, roster, rank, or guild rule
  that binds consequences to a name must be explicitly local—legal, cultural,
  technological, or magical—rather than treated as a property of naming
  itself. (`universe/rules.md`, heading `Names and identity binding`)
- Take the ethical consequences of compelled team assignment seriously even in
  comedy. Canon supplies no universal metaphysical rule for consent, while the
  style guide protects personhood and moral choice from being erased by a
  convenient explanation. (`universe/rules.md`, heading `Costs, desire, and
  local mechanisms`; `universe/style-guide.md`, heading `Personhood and moral
  consequence`)
- Keep peril and fantasy violence non-graphic and consequence-centered,
  consistent with both the prompt's Teen / PG-13 control and the universe
  content ceiling. (`stories/the-quiet-party/00-prompt.md`, heading `Story
  controls`; `universe/style-guide.md`, heading `Audience and content
  boundaries`)

## Useful established context

- Warm comic fantasy, dry institutional satire, and a tightly local adventure
  all fit the universe's intentionally broad tonal and genre range. An ordinary
  or locally magical story belongs without a crossover, lore display, or
  supernatural hint beyond what its own mission needs.
  (`universe/style-guide.md`, heading `Tonal range, scale, and connection`;
  `universe/premise.md`, headings `Memory, myth, and recoverable truth` and
  `Knowledge and connection`)
- Many genuinely different systems can exist, interact, or be absent from a
  given era. The mission may therefore use a small local magical practice
  without connecting it to Ravel, the Glass Gate, gods, or any previous system.
  (`universe/rules.md`, headings `System categories` and `Artifacts, motifs,
  and folklore`)
- Characters and cultures may have incomplete or incorrect models of how their
  world works. The supervising official can be wrong about what effective
  communication looks like without the narration endorsing that belief.
  (`universe/premise.md`, heading `Knowledge and connection`)
- Precise, concrete action and varied prose are the established production
  default. That supports making the party's coordination readable through
  timing, body language, objects, writing, and tactical cause-and-effect rather
  than explanatory mind-reading. (`universe/style-guide.md`, heading `Prose
  defaults`; `stories/the-quiet-party/00-prompt.md`, headings `Story controls`
  and `Assumptions`)

## Conflicts or ambiguity

- No contradiction was found between the prompt and authoritative canon.
- The repository currently has no finalized story marked canon `yes`; every row
  in the index is `Canon: no`. Accordingly, no `05-story.md` is authoritative
  evidence for this brief, and candidate or legacy portrayals of guilds,
  adventurers, contracts, silence, or communication must not be imported as
  canon. (`stories/INDEX.md`, heading `Story index`;
  `universe/README.md`, heading `Authority order`)
- Era placement is ambiguous rather than contradictory. Canon orders the
  modern-like era before the Glass-Sea era and leaves Ravel's placement
  unresolved, but the prompt identifies none of them. If planning elects to use
  one of those labels, its established chronology becomes binding; otherwise a
  locally described, chronologically unpinned culture is safer.
  (`universe/timeline.md`, heading `Established era order`;
  `universe/glossary.md`, headings `Modern-like era`, `Ravel`, and `Glass-Sea
  world`)
- No canon ruling is required before planning. Status: **CLEAR TO PROCEED WITH
  LOCAL INVENTION**.

## Unknowns

- **Unknown:** The guild's name, location, age, membership model, ranks,
  leadership structure, disciplinary authority, contract law, funding,
  relationship to civil government, and whether it recurs outside this story.
  No authoritative universe entry mentions an adventurers' guild.
- **Unknown:** The mission site, objective, hazard, antagonist (if any), stakes,
  reward, and why guild leadership considers this assignment legitimate.
- **Unknown:** The adventurers' identities, number beyond the minimum of three,
  reasons for not speaking, specialties, histories, relationships, and chosen
  nonverbal media. Absence of speech is not evidence of a shared diagnosis,
  culture, vow, power, or origin.
- **Unknown:** Whether this culture has disability accommodations, sign
  languages, literacy norms, magical communication, or professional protocols.
  None may be presented as pre-existing universe-wide canon.
- **Unknown:** The story's era and its relation to the modern-like, Ravel, or
  Glass-Sea eras.
- **Unknown:** Whether magic appears at all. If it does, its local mechanism,
  limits, costs, and users must be established in-story and remain compatible
  with the locked living-agency rule.

## Safe invention space

- A single town, frontier district, ruin, road, cave system, or other
  story-scale mission site, left unconnected to named canon eras and locations.
- One local adventuring guild with a narrow charter, internal paperwork,
  assignment process, leadership committee, and comic institutional habits.
  Prefer claims about “this guild” or “this district,” not “all guilds” or
  “adventurers everywhere.”
- Three or more distinct silent adventurers, each with an observable specialty,
  motive, temperament, and preferred nonverbal medium. Their coordination can
  emerge from attention, trust, ordinary signals, writing, gesture, drawings,
  pointing, exchanged objects, and adaptive action.
- A bounded mission hazard and a bounded local magical technique, creature, or
  device, provided its causal rules are legible, it does not create a secret
  mind-link, and any magic use complies with living agency.
- Local professional norms about briefing, field safety, written reports,
  accommodations, and team formation. The successful party can become a useful
  precedent within this guild without establishing a universal reform.
- Local inventions intended to recur should later be recorded in
  `06-canon-delta.md`; until user-approved promotion, they remain proposed
  rather than established canon. (`universe/README.md`, heading `Authority
  order`; `universe/premise.md`, heading `Knowledge and connection`)

## Name constraints

- The prompt supplies no character-facing names or aliases, so there is no
  prompt-required reuse and no current collision to resolve. Every personal
  name, nickname, epithet, username, named companion/construct, and title used
  as a name must be checked after planning against `stories/NAMES.md`.
  (`stories/NAMES.md`, heading `Rules`)
- Avoid all reserved forms unless reuse is intentional, meaningful, and
  documented. The most relevant role-label trap for an adventuring party is
  `Captain`, already reserved as a named dog in legacy source `S06`. A party
  leader may be *a captain* as an ordinary lowercase role, but using `Captain`
  alone as that person's story-facing name would collide. (`stories/NAMES.md`,
  registry row `Captain`)
- Guild-official constructions must also avoid reserved full/title forms and
  aliases such as `Assessor Havil` / `Havil`, `Examiner Dovek` / `Dovek`,
  `Instructor Bexel` / `Bexel`, `Commander Kest` / `Kest`, `Night Clerk`,
  `Court Ghost`, `Rollcaller`, `Regent`, and `Keeper Below`. Generic role nouns
  such as assessor, examiner, instructor, commander, clerk, captain, or
  guildmaster are not automatically aliases when used descriptively, but they
  require a fresh registry check if capitalized or used alone as a
  character-facing label. (`stories/NAMES.md`, heading `Character name
  registry`, corresponding registry rows)
- Do not introduce the already unresolved forms `Lena`, `Mara`, `Nisha`, `Pell`,
  or `Voss`. Those collisions belong to distinct legacy identities and are
  warnings, not available shared identities. (`stories/NAMES.md`, heading
  `Known unresolved legacy collisions`)
- No close-match ruling can be completed before names are proposed. At planning
  time, compare new forms case-insensitively for exact matches, aliases, close
  spellings, reversals, cadence, and likely shortened forms against the entire
  registry—not only canon stories. Do not infer a crossover from resemblance.
  (`stories/NAMES.md`, heading `Rules`; `universe/style-guide.md`, headings
  `Prose defaults` and `Continuity and evidence discipline`)

## Required checks after drafting

- Search every silent adventurer's dialogue and action: none may speak aloud,
  including in the last line, and no narration may smuggle in telepathic
  coordination, a hive mind, a prearranged shared code that trivializes their
  adaptation, or unexplained perfect knowledge.
- Confirm each necessary tactical inference is observable to the supervising
  official and the reader through setup, attention, ordinary communication, or
  action. Preserve real communication; do not turn the success into a claim
  that communication is unnecessary.
- Confirm at least three adventurers remain individually distinguishable in
  motive, specialty, body language, and preferred medium rather than functioning
  as interchangeable “silent types.”
- Check that the guild deliberately creates the party as a corrective measure,
  the mission is real, the teamwork succeeds safely, leadership becomes
  comically annoyed rather than cruelly humiliated, and the success creates at
  most a local operational precedent.
- Check the viewpoint official's arc from institutional sympathy to revised
  judgment without treating silence or disability as a defect to cure and
  without erasing the ethical weight of coercive authority.
- Search for universalizing language about guild jurisdiction, adventurer
  culture, communication, disability, consent, names, magic, telepathy, and the
  story's era. Replace unsupported global claims with bounded local facts.
- If magic or an apparently autonomous enchanted device appears, verify its
  mechanism, limits, causal payoffs, and living source; verify that it is not a
  disguised mind-link.
- Recheck every final character-facing name and alias against
  `stories/NAMES.md`, including standalone surnames and capitalized ranks or
  titles. Document any deliberate reuse and its reader-disambiguation strategy;
  otherwise rename.
- Keep violence non-graphic, verify the comic resolution still respects
  personhood and consequence, and record every reusable new fact in the later
  canon delta as proposed rather than canon.
