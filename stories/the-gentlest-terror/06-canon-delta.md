# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.
Every item below is a proposal local to Ersene and this story's unnamed era.
Nothing in this file establishes a universal rule for dragons, magic,
aristocracy, consent, or succession.

## New characters or character facts

- **Princess Olyra / Queen Olyra** is King Ambric's daughter and chosen heir.
  She knows the royal dragon Nyrzath as a gentle friend but initially accepts
  the crown's use of his terrifying appearance. During her accession she
  recognizes Mossback's refusal, risks losing the throne by asking Nyrzath to
  choose her freely, enters the failing Flint quarry sluice, and helps divert
  the flood. After her confirmation, her first proclamation ends the Dread
  Passage and restores the royal dragon's role as an impartial witness.
- **Nyrzath, the Grief-Clad** is the royal family's centuries-old dragon ally
  and the largest, most frightening-looking dragon in Ersene. He has black
  plated scales, a crown of hooked horns, tusks, ragged-looking wing edges, one
  gold eye, one scarred milky-white eye, a voice like failing masonry, and
  controllable furnace heat behind his teeth. He can turn rain to steam and
  melt siege iron, but no broader dragon capability is proposed. He is patient,
  attentive, protective of small creatures, and unwilling to coerce another
  dragon's vow. He remembers every known nest in Ersene because he sends
  hatch-day gifts; examples include river glass, smoothed roof tiles, a wagon
  of pears, and six blue roof tiles for Mossback's clutch. At the Flint quarry
  he rejects a destructive blast, braces the collapsing wall with his body,
  suffers a severe left-wing injury, saves a swallow in his mouth without
  harming it, and freely renews his alliance with Olyra. **Nyr** is Olyra's
  private shortening of his name.
- **Lord Osric Fane** is a traditionalist court official who advises Olyra on
  accession procedure. He sincerely believes the Dread Passage and fear of
  Nyrzath keep Ersene's houses coherent, presses Olyra to establish command
  during the Flint crisis, and later presents Queen Olyra's first proclamation.
  He is not implicated in House Flint's breach.
- **Duchess Yselle Flint** represents House Flint at the accession convocation.
  Her house cut its quarry past the protected boundary beneath Mossback's
  nesting ledge after he warned of collapse, concealed the resulting
  instability, and tried to make renewal a condition of rescue. After the
  disaster she is sent before ordinary legal judgment for the illegal cut and
  concealment.
- **Mossback** is a broad, slate-green dragon with lichen along his spine. He
  watches the Flint valley, carries endangered quarry workers with deliberate
  gentleness, and nests above the villages. He refuses to renew with Duchess
  Yselle after House Flint violates the nesting boundary. He alerts the court
  to the flood while the old alliance is still active, guides Olyra to the old
  sluice after his exit, helps open its gate, rescues her from the water, and
  continues watching the valley's people by choice while remaining unallied to
  House Flint.
- **Lord Ruvik Cress** and **Larkspur** maintain a healthy reciprocal alliance.
  Larkspur is a large green-and-violet dragon who watches the Cress orchards
  and storm channels. Lord Ruvik's household clears her molting ground,
  rebuilds the upper cisterns, and maintains their gates, although three gates
  were still noisy at renewal. During the rescue, Lord Ruvik calls through
  their new focus and Larkspur freely empties the cisterns, clears debris from
  the channels, and repeatedly accepts the shared physical cost. Lord Ruvik
  later supervises the rescued swallow's safe shelter and return.
- **King Ambric** was Olyra's father and Nyrzath's former human ally. His death
  three weeks before the story ends their living alliance and leaves their
  scale focus inert. He taught Olyra that a crown sometimes needs a gentle
  friend to wear a monster's face, a belief she publicly rejects after her
  accession.

## New locations

- **Ersene** is a locally bounded kingdom in an unnamed era of the shared
  physical world. Every noble family in Ersene appears at the royal succession
  convocation beside a dragon ally. No proposed fact places Ersene in Ravel,
  the Glass-Sea era, the modern-like era, or any other named setting.
- The **royal convocation court** contains galleries, ceremonial banners, a
  witness stone, and a west arch rebuilt and widened after Nyrzath's former
  Dread Passages. Ersene displays Nyrzath's fearsome image over gates and on
  official wax.
- The **Flint valley** contains villages beneath House Flint's quarry and
  Mossback's nesting caverns. The quarry intersects a flood channel. Its north
  nesting cavern and ledge hold Mossback's clutch, and an old eastward sluice,
  human-width service passage, and outflow can divert water toward the Cress
  hills.
- The **Cress lands** include orchards, storm channels, hills, molting ground,
  upper cisterns, stone basins, and at least three maintained cistern gates.
  The emptied system can receive a flood diverted from the Flint quarry.
- Ersene also contains unnamed reed estates and cliff roosts watched by other
  noble-dragon pairs. These examples establish local variety without defining
  further houses or characters.

## New factions or cultural facts

- In Ersene, every noble family is paired with a freely consenting dragon ally.
  The alliance is a reciprocal public service, not ownership: the dragon
  watches the house's lands and provides rescue, while the human family
  protects agreed nesting or roost territory, provides injury and molting care,
  maintains agreed infrastructure, and represents the dragon's interests in
  human court. Allied dragons are not chained, saddled, or treated as weapons.
- At a royal succession convocation, noble families and their dragons renew
  one pair at a time before the royal dragon. The royal alliance must also be
  freely renewed. If it fails, the assembled houses may select another heir;
  the other pairs' witnessed assent confirms the successful royal heir.
- Either partner may refuse renewal. Once the royal dragon witnesses that
  refusal whole, the alliance ends in law and in its living focus. The human
  family's noble title is suspended until the breach is repaired and the same
  dragon freely returns, or a different dragon independently chooses the
  partnership. A former dragon ally may continue protecting local people by
  personal choice without renewing the house's title or duties.
- **House Flint** loses its noble title after Mossback's witnessed refusal. The
  title remains suspended at the story's end. Evidence, repair, and ordinary
  law—not a dragon's roar—govern judgment of its illegal quarrying.
- **House Cress** demonstrates the intended model: its human and dragon
  partners question one another, maintain reciprocal obligations, answer
  emergency calls without compulsion, and both knowingly bear the cost.
- The court had turned Nyrzath's entrance, heat, shadow, and roar into the
  **Dread Passage**, a succession performance intended to make dissenting
  dragons and houses fear the crown. Nyrzath's legitimate function is witness,
  not commander. Queen Olyra abolishes the threatening performance for future
  renewals.
- The local renewal formula is **“Asked first. Answered free. Witnessed
  whole.”** Its order expresses the cultural and legal requirement that a
  freely offered answer precede royal witnessing.
- Care after danger is treated as part of reciprocity: after Nyrzath is injured
  in service, Olyra personally ensures that his wing is freed, splinted, and
  cared for rather than treating his sacrifice as the end of the crown's duty.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- An Ersene alliance focus is created only by two living partners' spoken
  assent. The dragon freely sheds a scale, the human partner holds it, and the
  dragon warms it with living breath during renewal. The scale stores and
  channels the resulting connection but has no will and cannot choose or
  command.
- A deliberate call through a working focus communicates direction, urgency,
  and attention between the two partners. The focus does not compel the
  recipient; the recipient decides whether and how to answer.
- Each call drains body heat and stamina from both partners. Repeated calls can
  produce smoking breath, frost on skin or scales, trembling wings, weakness,
  and inability to stand. The cost is chosen anew with each call; it is not
  proposed as a universal fuel for magic.
- A focus needs two living endpoints. King Ambric's death makes the scale from
  his alliance with Nyrzath inert: it cannot point, carry a call, or draw heat
  from Olyra or Nyrzath. Olyra and Nyrzath require a new freely offered scale
  and new assent.
- A witnessed refusal ends the old connection immediately. After Olyra and
  Nyrzath witness Mossback's refusal, the Flint focus stops smoking, becomes
  inert, and can neither carry either former partner's call nor impose the
  shared physical cost. Mossback later guides Olyra by voice and claw signals,
  not by the expired focus.
- These focus-creation, cost, death, refusal, and expiry rules are proposed
  only for Ersene's alliance system. They do not make consent, scales, names,
  oaths, body heat, or death behave this way in other systems.
- Nyrzath's furnace heat, flight, great size, precise nest memory, and ability
  to brace the Flint quarry are individual realized capabilities, not
  universal dragon traits. Larkspur and Mossback also fly; Mossback is about
  half Nyrzath's measure at the quarry and cannot fit through the old sluice's
  first bend.

## Timeline events

1. King Ambric dies three weeks before Olyra's accession convocation. His
   alliance focus with Nyrzath becomes inert.
2. During rehearsal, Olyra frees a swallow tangled in Nyrzath's horn crown.
   Nyrzath agrees to enter and witness but explicitly refuses to force another
   dragon's vow.
3. At the accession convocation, House Cress and Larkspur renew freely and
   demonstrate the alliance focus. Mossback then refuses Duchess Yselle and
   exposes House Flint's quarry breach beneath his nesting ledge.
4. Before the refusal is formally witnessed, Mossback uses the still-active
   Flint focus twice to warn that stormwater has opened the quarry into the
   flood channel and threatens his clutch and the villages. Olyra recognizes
   his refusal, suspends House Flint's title, and ends its focus.
5. Olyra asks Nyrzath to choose her before the court. He freely renews on the
   condition that she remember witnessing is not commanding, creating their
   new living focus before they travel to the quarry.
6. At the Flint quarry, Nyrzath refuses a blast that would destroy Mossback's
   clutch and braces the wall instead. Mossback guides Olyra into the old
   sluice, Olyra and Mossback open it, and Mossback pulls her from the flood.
   Lord Ruvik calls Larkspur, who empties the Cress cisterns and clears their
   channels to receive the diverted water. Olyra's focus call lets Nyrzath
   shift his brace long enough to protect her, at the cost of further injury
   to his left wing.
7. The Flint villages and Mossback's clutch survive. Nyrzath catches the
   swallow unharmed amid falling rubble; once freed, he transfers it to Olyra,
   who sends it to a warm quarry shed under Lord Ruvik's care while his wing is
   treated.
8. Before sunset Mossback refuses House Flint again while affirming his
   voluntary protection of the valley's people. Duchess Yselle faces ordinary
   judgment, and House Flint's title remains suspended.
9. The convocation resumes without the Dread Passage. The remaining human and
   dragon pairs renew freely, their assent confirms Queen Olyra, and her first
   proclamation establishes the royal dragon's future entrance as impartial
   witness without threat. Nyrzath then takes the sheltered swallow back and
   publicly returns it unharmed to Olyra.

## New glossary terms or aliases

- **Ersene** — The local kingdom in which noble status depends on a reciprocal
  alliance between a human family and a freely consenting dragon.
- **Alliance scale / focus** — A freely shed dragon scale warmed by living
  breath during two partners' spoken assent. It channels deliberate calls but
  has no independent will. Death or a witnessed refusal makes the relevant
  focus inert.
- **Call** — The living partners' deliberate use of an alliance focus to share
  direction and urgency at a cost of heat and stamina.
- **Dread Passage** — The court's threatening performance of Nyrzath's royal
  entrance, later abolished by Queen Olyra. It has social force but supplies no
  magical right to command another dragon.
- **“Asked first. Answered free. Witnessed whole.”** — The Ersene renewal and
  refusal formula establishing offer, voluntary answer, and royal witnessing
  in that order.
- **the Grief-Clad** — Nyrzath's public epithet. **Nyr** is Olyra's private
  shortening for the same dragon. Neither form identifies a separate person.

## Possible conflicts or retcons

- **None identified.** The canon brief found no established dragon, kingdom,
  noble-alliance, or Ersene facts for these proposals to contradict.
- These proposals fill omissions rather than altering established history.
  Ersene remains in an unnamed era and has no proposed connection to Earth,
  Ravel, the Glass-Sea era, or another candidate story.
- No name or alias is reused across identities. Nyrzath and his forms remain
  distinct from the reserved dragon-associated names Kharost and Varkesh;
  Olyra remains distinct from other reserved royal identities.
- Promotion must preserve the local scope. It must not establish that all
  dragons fly, breathe furnace heat, form scale focuses, accept aristocratic
  alliances, or experience consent and death through Ersene's mechanism.

## Recommended promotions

If the user explicitly approves canon promotion, the following bounded bundles
are recommended:

1. **Ersene and its alliance law:** the kingdom's noble-dragon public-service
   model, reciprocal duties, succession renewals, voluntary refusal and exit,
   title suspension and restoration conditions, and the royal dragon's
   impartial witness role.
2. **The local focus mechanism:** living assent, freely shed scale, living
   dragon breath, directional call, voluntary response, shared heat and stamina
   cost, death expiry, and witnessed-refusal expiry. This should be labeled an
   Ersene-specific system.
3. **The named cast and houses:** Olyra, Nyrzath, King Ambric, Lord Osric,
   Duchess Yselle, Mossback, Lord Ruvik, Larkspur, House Flint, and House Cress,
   with the character facts and aliases listed above.
4. **The accession-day events:** the Flint breach and flood, the coordinated
   quarry-and-cistern rescue, Nyrzath's wing injury, House Flint's suspension,
   Mossback's continuing voluntary watch, Olyra's accession, and her
   proclamation abolishing the Dread Passage.

Incidental staging details—the rescued swallow, the widened court arch, the
exact hatch-day gifts, and the noisy eastern gate—are fully recorded above but
need not be promoted into high-level universe summaries unless later stories
reuse them. Until explicit approval and canon-steward maintenance occur, every
item in this file remains a proposal and `05-story.md` remains a candidate.
