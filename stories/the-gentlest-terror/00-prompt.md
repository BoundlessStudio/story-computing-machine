# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] Every single noble family in your kingdom has an alliance with a dragon that watches over them. The royal family's dragon, despite being the biggest and scariest by appearance, is a total sweetheart.**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Gentlest Terror
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person, centered on a royal-family protagonist whose public
  duty conflicts with private knowledge of the royal dragon.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Warm court fantasy with political tension, dry humor, and an
  earned hopeful ending.
- Audience/content rating: Teen / PG-13; non-graphic peril and intimidation,
  with no explicit sexual content or graphic violence.
- Required elements:
  - Every noble family in the kingdom has a meaningful reciprocal alliance with
    a dragon that watches over it.
  - The royal dragon is visibly the largest and most frightening dragon in the
    kingdom.
  - The royal dragon is genuinely and consistently a sweetheart, demonstrated
    through consequential choices rather than asserted as a joke.
  - The contrast between terrifying appearance and gentle character drives the
    central conflict and its resolution.
  - Dragons retain agency and are allies, not pets, heraldic ornaments, or
    weapons owned by human houses.
- Prohibited elements:
  - No reversal in which the royal dragon is secretly cruel, predatory, or
    manipulating the family.
  - No ending that depends only on other characters learning not to judge by
    appearances; the protagonist and dragon must make a costly, causal choice.
  - No contradiction of authoritative shared-universe canon.

## Assumptions

- The prompt establishes a particular kingdom and its noble-dragon alliance
  custom; where existing canon is silent, these remain local proposed
  inventions rather than universal facts about all dragons or governments.
- The royal family includes at least one person who knows the dragon's gentle
  habits intimately but must confront a court that values its terrifying image.
- Humor will arise from character and contrast without making the dragon
  childish, incompetent, or merely cute.
- The story will stand alone for a reader with no prior knowledge of the shared
  universe.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
- The royal dragon's sweetness changes the outcome through empathy, restraint,
  care, or another active virtue without erasing credible stakes.
- The noble-dragon alliance system is shown through at least two non-royal
  examples and materially affects the plot.
- The ending resolves both the immediate danger and the protagonist's conflict
  over how the crown presents and uses its dragon ally.
