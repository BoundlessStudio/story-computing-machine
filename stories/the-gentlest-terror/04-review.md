#### Continuity

**Finding 1**

- Severity: Major
- Location: `03-draft.md`, opening rehearsal (lines 51–70) and royal renewal
  (lines 299–326)
- Evidence: Olyra uses a scale “shed ... for her father's succession” to call
  Nyrzath before she and Nyrzath have chosen or spoken their own assent. The
  established local mechanism says the heir holds a newly shed scale while the
  two living partners assent, after which that focus connects those two allies.
  The later scene treats Nyrzath's new scale as the focus created by Olyra's
  actual renewal.
- Why it matters: The draft gives Olyra a functioning alliance focus before the
  freely chosen alliance on which her accession and Nyrzath's agency depend.
  That makes the system's endpoints and the legal/magical significance of the
  climax internally inconsistent.
- Smallest effective fix: Remove the pre-renewal magical call or explicitly
  establish a narrowly defined, mutually granted provisional call that is not
  an alliance and does not carry alliance duties. Keep the new scale as the
  first full alliance focus created by Olyra and Nyrzath's public assent.

**Finding 2**

- Severity: Major
- Location: `03-draft.md`, Flint emergency and rescue (lines 239–244, 281–291,
  and 452–454)
- Evidence: Mossback's old focus activates before Olyra recognizes his refusal,
  but it activates again in the service passage after Olyra has declared that
  he owes House Flint no alliance. The later call still exacts involuntary cold
  and pain from Duchess Yselle. The plan defines the focus as a channel between
  living allies, defines refusal as a valid exit, and supplies no continuing
  post-exit channel or consent rule.
- Why it matters: The climax uses an undefined magical remnant after the
  alliance has ended. Because one former partner can impose a physical cost on
  the other, the ambiguity also weakens the story's central promise that exit
  and dragon-human agency are genuine rather than merely legal language.
- Smallest effective fix: Make the first alert explicitly occur before the
  witnessed release ends the old focus, then remove the post-release call in
  the passage and deliver Mossback's warning by his already established voice
  and claw signals. Alternatively, define a consensual exit lifecycle that
  disables or mutually governs old focuses and apply it consistently.

**Finding 3**

- Severity: Minor
- Location: `03-draft.md`, quarry resolution through closing reveal (lines
  518–535 and 551–590)
- Evidence: Nyrzath catches the swallow in his mouth and is still holding it
  when he returns it after his wing has been splinted, Mossback's second refusal
  has been heard, the convocation has resumed, every remaining pair has
  renewed, and Olyra has read her proclamation.
- Why it matters: The unaccounted interval makes the bird's safe custody feel
  implausible and can inadvertently make Nyrzath's signature kindness seem
  careless, especially after the draft has established furnace heat behind his
  teeth.
- Smallest effective fix: Show Nyrzath transferring the bird to safe temporary
  care after the collapse and taking it back only immediately before the public
  return, preserving the frightening-jaws payoff.

**Finding 4**

- Severity: Optional
- Location: `03-draft.md`, arrival at the Flint quarry (lines 342–356)
- Evidence: Nyrzath, Mossback, and Larkspur fly from the convocation, while
  Duchess Yselle arrives “moments later” by quarry cart; the distance between
  the court and Flint valley is not established.
- Why it matters: A brief travel cue would prevent the cart from reading as
  accidental teleportation, though the text does not state a distance that
  makes the timing impossible.
- Smallest effective fix: Clarify that the quarry is close to the convocation
  court, give the flight and cart journey a plausible elapsed interval, or have
  Yselle transported with one of the consenting rescuers.
