# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **Where Sleep Takes You**. Cira Keln, an adult with narcolepsy and involuntary teleportation at every sleep onset, has one window to clear her closed shop's storage unit. Escalate one recovery problem while Cira retains authority over risk and loss; the condition is neither punishment nor gift.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Cira needs records and tools to settle the shop's obligations. Her knowledge is needed at the unit, but sleep may remove her; she wants trusted responder Pevin Ors's help without surrendering decisions.
- Counterforce or complication: At closing, a contractor takes the unit and bills Cira for clearance. Teleportations consume the buffer. Pevin cannot separate current client work from obsolete stock. The attendant enforces access and witnesses authorization but cannot extend time. None is an antagonist; narcolepsy and teleportation are non-moral and non-sentient.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Cira, attending to onset, secured belongings, clock loss, labels, and fatigued decisions. She knows her care plan but not her next destination, Pevin's work when video drops, or unspoken facility permissions.
- Governing movement and time shape: Two continuous hours. The first onset interrupts sorting; the second prevents Cira's return before closing and forces remote triage. Alternate her orientation and exit efforts with Pevin's work. Resolve privately at the loading bay through incomplete inventory and recovery steps, without public proof, reform, or thematic explanation.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Each actual transition into sleep, intended or involuntary, triggers one teleport; drowsiness, eye-closing, and cataplexy do not. Cira must wake before another onset. Her body, clothing, and zipped, buckled, or clipped objects travel; loose objects and other people remain. A destination is randomly selected from body-sized surfaces within 1.5 kilometres of departure. It cannot overlap solids or liquids, occupy a moving vehicle, or lack support, but roofs, locked rooms, wet or cold sites, and places beside ordinary hazards remain eligible. Arrival makes one pressure snap and pushes loose dust outward; neither trace carries direction. Range resets after arrival. Mood, intent, dialogue, training, medication, and technology cannot select, bias, or predict. Her secured wrist tag logs onset and, only after GPS displacement, transmits time and coordinates when signal permits; vibration and strobe aid post-arrival orientation. Ordinary care may affect narcolepsy symptoms but never explains, cures, or controls teleportation.

## Beats

1. At the unit, establish the deadline, secured tag and belt pouch, agreed stop conditions, and work split. The attendant records that Cira may authorize Pevin by live call but not through blanket disposal instructions. Cira chooses a client-by-client sort over his faster visual categories.
2. Feeling onset while holding a tray, Cira sets it down, clips the key into her pouch, and moves clear of boxes. Sleep takes her to a locked laundry-room floor blocks away. The tag sends coordinates but not floor level. Cira identifies the building and arranges her exit; Pevin continues sorting instead of pursuing without a plan.
3. Hired transport costs money and most of their buffer. Back at the unit, Cira corrects two misclassifications and continues within their rested agreement. She narrows the goal to client records, calibrated tools, and one personal box; shelving and the heavy bench become accepted risks.
4. At the second onset, Cira sits safely instead of fighting sleep, secures the ledger, and leaves a bulky tool case for Pevin. She arrives on an exposed service roof near range edge. Coordinates transmit, but weak data and controlled access prevent a return before closing.
5. Cira directs triage through intermittent video. Pevin's route questions clash with her categories until he changes camera order and waits. The attendant supplies countdowns, rules, and witnessed authorizations only. Cira makes item-level choices, rejects an unsafe extra trip, and accepts the fee and lost bench rather than ceding consent or risking Pevin's lock-in.
6. After building staff release Cira, they inventory: core records and tools survived, an obsolete crate displaced two fixtures, and the personal box is present. Preserve taxi cost, clearance fee, lost equipment, exhaustion, and unfinished client calls. Cira chooses recovery actions and where to await her ride; stop before the inventory is complete.
