## Story controls

- Working title: A Friendly Little Game
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close second person, centered on the unnamed viewpoint character.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Suspenseful shared-universe speculative fiction with a deceptive social game, mounting unease, and an emotionally satisfying turn.
- Audience/content rating: Teen; peril and restrained violence are acceptable, without graphic gore or sexual content.
- Required elements: A hooded stranger sits beside the viewpoint character at a bar while the rest of the viewpoint character's party is expected; the quoted invitation appears verbatim; the proposed game drives the central conflict; the significance of the arriving party is dramatized; the story resolves the game's outcome and its cost.
- Prohibited elements: No contradiction of current `LOCKED` or `CANON` authority; no unearned rescue, dream reset, or unresolved chapter-fragment ending.

- The bar may be an established or newly introduced location, but any new setting facts remain proposed rather than canon unless separately promoted.
- The prompt's second-person address is a deliberate narrative constraint, not merely a framing device.
