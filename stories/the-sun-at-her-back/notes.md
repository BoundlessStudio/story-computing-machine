# Editorial notes

## Request readings · 2026-11-11

The Codex promise keeper read the request and image before seeing a plan. It identifies the librarian as the decision-maker and the girl's ordinary life and the libraries as parts of one buried history. It anticipates that “the awe of a visible, ten-thousand-year-old presence turns intimate” through her memories, and wants to “feel the value of both outcomes and see what his decision actually costs.” It reads the woman's chin-in-hand pose as inward and human at an immense scale, the librarian as absorbed and at home among the worn books, and the open stone arch as a single sightline joining the book, woman, and inhabited city.

Claude, reading only the request and original, calls the contrast between “how vast she is and how ordinary she was” the core pleasure. It expects the library work to give the young man contact with her lost self and the name to matter as recognition. “Her freedom and personhood stand against a world that is real, loved, and hers.” Claude reads the woman's chin in her hand as the casual pose of a girl daydreaming on a step, with the librarian's informal posture and the warm room giving the reader a human scale. Her awareness, the librarian's answer, and the mechanism of the sacrifice remain open.

## Outline discussion

The keeper reads Iven as the decision-maker and the encounter as the dramatic center. It protects “the bell still sounding with no tower underneath it while Iven sees the girl and father on the stairs,” and asks for “one particular thing she wants from waking that no exercise of her dream can give her.” Claude reads the bell and the first dusk as a strong connection between the ordinary girl's life and the fate of the city, but finds that “the libraries-as-memories stop working after the setup.” It asks for the memories to exert pressure on Iven's decision, for worship to happen on the page, and for the first dusk to leave Bess's cost in view. Both favor the live window encounter over the split timeline or the retrospective fall.
