# Canon brief

## Hard constraints

- The story belongs to one physical world continuous across deep time. A new
  era and local culture are possible, but Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world
  are era-bound labels for that same world, not separate planets or planes.
  Ravel's chronological position is unresolved (`universe/premise.md` — `The
  deep-time shared world`; `universe/locations.md` — `The world across eras`).
- The class interface must be a particular local system, not the explanation
  for every anomaly. Many systems exist and may interact or resemble one
  another, but no single origin explains them all. Magic, technology, and
  divinity are cultural labels rather than objective cosmological categories
  (`universe/rules.md` — `System categories`; `universe/glossary.md` — `Magic,
  technology, and divinity`).
- If the class system uses magic, only life may use that magic. A nonliving
  device may store, focus, transmit, or shape magic supplied through living
  agency; an autonomous magical System must be established as alive, not
  assumed alive because it speaks or appears intelligent (`universe/rules.md`
  — `Living agency and magic`).
- Experience, levels, and abilities require defined local rules. Neither cost
  nor desire is a universal fuel, and even free power must obey the rules of
  its own system. This story cannot imply that lived experience universally
  converts into levels (`universe/rules.md` — `Costs, desire, and local
  mechanisms`).
- Control must preserve personhood and moral consequence. Canon gives consent
  no universal metaphysical behavior, so this local mechanism must handle it
  consistently and the story must take its ethical consequences seriously
  (`universe/style-guide.md` — `Personhood and moral consequence`;
  `universe/rules.md` — `Costs, desire, and local mechanisms`). This agrees
  with the prompt's ban on harmless coercive mind control
  (`stories/the-thirteenth-command/00-prompt.md` — `Story controls`).
- Connections require evidence: similar interfaces, numbers, titles,
  artifacts, portals, or imagery do not prove shared ancestry with another
  system (`universe/rules.md` — `Artifacts, motifs, and folklore`;
  `universe/style-guide.md` — `Continuity and evidence discipline`).
- Battle, peril, and death must remain non-graphic and consequence-centered.
  The prompt's Teen ceiling is within the universe's broadly accessible content
  boundary (`universe/style-guide.md` — `Audience and content boundaries`;
  `stories/the-thirteenth-command/00-prompt.md` — `Story controls`).

## Useful established context

- Civilizations and their systems can rise, interact, collapse, be copied,
  traded, stolen, reinvented, misattributed, and become archaeology. A local
  class interface may have incomplete provenance, provided the story does not
  claim an unsupported universal source (`universe/premise.md` — `The
  deep-time shared world`).
- Memory versus myth is a central tension. Evidence can degrade or be
  controlled, and recovering truth does not automatically produce justice.
  This can support uncertainty around "previous experience," but any answer
  used by the plot still needs concrete, causal evidence
  (`universe/premise.md` — `Memory, myth, and recoverable truth`;
  `universe/rules.md` — `Records, falsehood, and historical traces`).
- Cultures and characters may hold radically incomplete models of the wider
  world. No universal hidden authority regulates every anomaly, so a coherent
  local System need not explain the entire cosmology
  (`universe/premise.md` — `Knowledge and connection`).
- Systems can reproduce, substitute for, or hybridize effects that another
  culture labels differently; different systems need not be active in every
  era. A science-fantasy presentation is compatible when its local rules remain
  clear (`universe/rules.md` — `System categories`).
- The universe permits the prompt's progression-fantasy genre and
  close-third, past-tense approach; stories are connected by a coherent world,
  not a mandatory tone, scale, or degree of cosmological awareness
  (`universe/style-guide.md` — `Tonal range, scale, and connection`).
- If an established era is chosen, the modern-like era is a low-magic trough,
  the Glass-Sea era is much later, and Ravel's placement relative to them is
  unknown (`universe/timeline.md` — `Established era order`).

## Conflicts or ambiguity

- No authoritative contradiction currently blocks planning. No row in
  `stories/INDEX.md` is marked canon `yes`, so there is no promoted final story
  relevant to reconcile; the retcon log contains no approved change
  (`stories/INDEX.md` — `Story index`; `universe/retcons.md` — `Retcon log`).
- "System" is ambiguous. The prompt presents a capitalized interface voice,
  while canon uses *system* generically for many mechanisms. Canon does not
  establish that this System is magical, technological, divine, living,
  sentient, automated, or merely an interface to another actor. A local answer
  is safe; an objective universe-wide taxonomy is not
  (`stories/the-thirteenth-command/00-prompt.md` — `Verbatim writing prompt`;
  `universe/rules.md` — `System categories`).
- Autonomous magical operation is conditionally constrained: a nonliving
  System may operate technologically or shape magic supplied by living agency,
  but independent magical use requires it to be alive. The prompt does not say
  which case applies (`universe/rules.md` — `Living agency and magic`).
- "Host," "Class," "Combat Controller," "Level Up," and "Level 13" are
  prompt terms, not established glossary entries. Their ontology, scope,
  hierarchy, and capitalization remain local story decisions
  (`universe/glossary.md` — `Magic, technology, and divinity`;
  `stories/the-thirteenth-command/00-prompt.md` — `Verbatim writing prompt`).

## Unknowns

- Unknown: the era, Galactic Cycle coordinate, geography, culture, calendar,
  and relationship to the modern-like, Ravel, or Glass-Sea eras. Even the epoch,
  nominal Galactic Cycle length, and coordinates of known eras are unresolved
  (`universe/timeline.md` — `Universal chronology`).
- Unknown: who built, maintains, authorizes, or speaks through the System;
  whether it is alive or sentient; and whether it is magic, technology, a
  hybrid, or merely described using one of those cultural labels.
- Unknown: whether classes belong to one person, unit, institution, species,
  culture, place, historical layer, or broader region. There is no evidence for
  a global class network.
- Unknown: Host prerequisites, class selection, what a level measures, why
  thirteen matters, Level 13 capabilities, and all costs, limits, safeguards,
  error conditions, and failure modes.
- Unknown: how lived experience is detected, authenticated, translated, or
  distinguished from memory, records, simulations, inherited patterns, or
  copied information. Canon provides no universal memory substance or
  experience-to-power conversion (`universe/rules.md` — `Memory,
  transformation, copies, and branches`).
- Unknown: why the Host is waking, the sides and nature of the crisis, the local
  command structure, and the Host's relationships. No recurring characters or
  factions are established in the topical notes
  (`universe/characters.md` — `Recurring characters`;
  `universe/factions.md` — `Factions and cultures`).
- Unknown: whether "Host" and "Combat Controller" remain generic interface
  roles or become character-facing aliases in prose.

## Safe invention space

- Place the crisis in a new local settlement, battlefield, vessel,
  installation, or region in an otherwise unspecified era of the same world.
  Leaving its Galactic Cycle coordinate unstated is safe; using a named era
  requires preserving its established conditions and order
  (`universe/premise.md` — `The deep-time shared world`;
  `universe/timeline.md` — `Established era order`).
- Define a bounded class system with local access conditions, interface
  behavior, level criteria, abilities, limitations, information quality, and
  failure modes. It may be called magic, technology, or a hybrid by the
  viewpoint culture, but not made a universal rule
  (`universe/rules.md` — `System categories`; `universe/rules.md` — `Costs,
  desire, and local mechanisms`).
- Invent a concrete local method for recognizing transferable lived experience.
  It may be imperfect or context-sensitive; it must not establish that all
  systems or eras convert experience into levels.
- Invent Level 13 abilities that expose, filter, route, predict, prioritize, or
  visualize local combat information and help willing people coordinate.
  Establish range, sources, errors, cognitive burden, permissions, or other
  limits so this is neither omniscience nor harmless mind control.
- Invent local combatants, institutions, customs, ranks, equipment, objectives,
  and a contained crisis. Keep new factions and places local unless later
  proposed as reusable facts.
- If the interface channels magic, it may work through the living Host or
  another established living agent. Alternatively, establish a genuinely
  living System or use a nonmagical technological mechanism
  (`universe/rules.md` — `Living agency and magic`).
- Give the Host a local moral choice about coordination, delegation, risk, and
  consent. This fits canon when the coordinated people's agency and
  consequences remain real (`universe/style-guide.md` — `Personhood and moral
  consequence`).
- Do not connect the System to the Glass Gate, a named era, another artifact,
  or another candidate story merely because of similar language, numbers,
  combat roles, or motifs (`universe/style-guide.md` — `Continuity and evidence
  discipline`).

## Name constraints

- `stories/NAMES.md` is production memory, not canon. The prompt supplies no
  personal name, so no protagonist or supporting-cast name has cleared the
  registry. Check every future full name, given name, mononym, nickname,
  callsign, username, title used as a name, named animal or construct, and
  person-like System identity (`stories/NAMES.md` — `Rules`).
- Avoid all reserved forms unless reuse has intentional story value and a
  documented identity/rationale/disambiguation decision. Combat-facing labels
  need special care: `Captain` is reserved as a named dog; `Kest` and
  `Commander Kest` identify one released or non-canon character; `Court Ghost`, `the Court
  Ghost`, `Keeper Below`, `the Keeper Below`, `Regent`, `Hollow Regent`, and
  `the Hollow Regent` are reserved aliases or titles. Generic rank nouns remain
  usable, but using one as a person's name or sole identifier makes it
  character-facing (`stories/NAMES.md` — `Rules` and the corresponding
  character/entity rows).
- Do not repeat the unresolved collisions `Lena`, `Mara`, `Nisha`, `Pell`, or
  `Voss`; each already labels distinct in-progress story identities
  (`stories/NAMES.md` — `Known unresolved name collisions`).
- Watch close forms already called out by the registry: `Irix`/`Ilya`/`Ivo`,
  `Ekri`/`Eli`, `Talvi`/`Tavra`/`Tamsin`, `Marcus`/`Mara`, and
  `Oren`/`Orrin`/`Pell`. Prefer names distinct in spelling, cadence, and role
  (`stories/NAMES.md` — rows `Irix`, `Ekri`, `Talvi`, `Marcus Bellweather`,
  `Oren`, and `Orrin Pell`).
- `Host`, `Combat Controller`, `Controller`, `Class`, and `System` have no
  current registry rows. They may remain common interface terms. If prose turns
  one into a personal name, persistent alias, callsign, or person-like entity
  label, register it and distinguish the Host, class, and System identities
  (`stories/NAMES.md` — `Rules`;
  `stories/the-thirteenth-command/00-prompt.md` — `Verbatim writing prompt`).
- Any deliberate reuse must say whether identities are the same or distinct,
  why the reuse matters, and how readers can distinguish them; convenience is
  not a rationale (`AGENTS.md` — `Character-name discipline`).

## Required checks after drafting

- Verify the prompt's causal awakening chain: Host found, capabilities
  confirmed, class applications sought, Combat Controller found and applied,
  previous experience confirmed and applied, Level 13 reached, then the Host
  wakes. The experience must be concrete lived skill, not unexplained game
  credit (`stories/the-thirteenth-command/00-prompt.md` — `Verbatim writing
  prompt`, `Assumptions`, and `Completion tests`).
- Search for claims that turn this local System, classes, levels, or experience
  conversion into a universal mechanism; narrow them to the local rules
  (`universe/rules.md` — `System categories`).
- Confirm that Level 13 abilities have stable sources, scope, limits, errors,
  and causal effects. They must resolve the crisis without an effortless
  statistics-only victory (`stories/the-thirteenth-command/00-prompt.md` —
  `Story controls` and `Completion tests`).
- Confirm that Combat Controller coordinates people and information rather than
  overriding minds. Coordinated characters must retain agency, and mistakes,
  injury, death, consent, and the Host's consequential choice cannot be
  dismissed as interface artifacts (`universe/style-guide.md` — `Personhood
  and moral consequence`).
- If the System independently channels magic, identify the living user or
  establish that the System is alive; speech, automation, intelligence, or
  construction alone does not prove life (`universe/rules.md` — `Living agency
  and magic`).
- Verify that viewpoint vocabulary does not treat magic, technology, or
  divinity as privileged objective categories
  (`universe/glossary.md` — `Magic, technology, and divinity`).
- If the story names the modern-like, Ravel, or Glass-Sea era, preserve the
  established order and do not resolve Ravel's unknown placement by implication
  (`universe/timeline.md` — `Established era order`).
- Search for unsupported connections to the Glass Gate, named eras, recurring
  motifs, artifacts, or other story systems. Resemblance is not evidence
  (`universe/style-guide.md` — `Continuity and evidence discipline`).
- Confirm that battlefield peril and death remain Teen-appropriate,
  non-graphic, and consequence-focused
  (`universe/style-guide.md` — `Audience and content boundaries`).
- Register every character-facing form and run the story-scoped name check
  after planning, then reconcile it after final review. Include surnames used
  alone, titles, callsigns, aliases, usernames, named constructs, and a
  person-like System label; recheck prompt terms if they become personal
  (`AGENTS.md` — `Character-name discipline`; `stories/NAMES.md` — `Rules`).
- Record reusable class mechanics, abilities, System provenance, factions,
  places, characters, and chronology in `06-canon-delta.md`; none becomes canon
  unless explicitly promoted (`AGENTS.md` — `Canon discipline`;
  `universe/README.md` — `Authority order`).
