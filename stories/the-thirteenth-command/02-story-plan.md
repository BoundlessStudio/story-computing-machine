# Story plan

## Premise and promise

A damaged civic command lattice wakes Edda Rook beneath a besieged reservoir,
recognizes thirty-six years of floodworks dispatch as Combat Controller
experience, and raises her directly to Level 13; to save her former apprentice
and the water supply both armies need, Edda must decide whether command means
turning people into pieces or giving enemies enough shared truth to choose the
same dangerous plan.

The story promises a literal rendition of the System update, a surprising but
credible source for the Host's previous experience, clearly bounded tactical
abilities, an escalating reservoir battle, and a decisive Level 13 command
whose power comes from coordination and consent rather than domination.

Central dramatic question: will Edda use her unwanted authority to deliver the
watch captain's efficient victory, or accept the personal and strategic cost of
making a common objective visible to people on both sides?

## Story controls

- POV: Close third person through Edda Rook.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Tense, humane progression fantasy with a local
  science-fantasy interface; precise operational detail, restrained dry humor,
  and consequence-centered action.
- Length budget: 3,200–3,800 words within the prompt's 2,500–4,000 range.
- Audience/content boundaries: Teen; non-graphic injuries and battlefield
  deaths may be acknowledged, but no gore, torture, sexual content, or
  dehumanizing spectacle.
- Setting scale: Copperwell, one basin city in an unspecified era of the shared
  world. Its old command lattice, class language, factions, and water conflict
  are local proposed inventions, not universal cosmology.
- Interface convention: The story may render short all-caps System notices.
  The System is a non-sentient technological coordination engine powered by
  damaged local cells. It does not use magic, read thoughts, alter desires, or
  act as a person.

## Character engine

- **Protagonist — Edda Rook:** A sixty-two-year-old former chief floodworks
  dispatcher. She wants to get her former apprentice Sula out of the south
  pressure chamber and keep Copperwell's cistern intact. She left the dispatch
  board after obeying a closure protocol during an earlier surge that saved a
  district but killed two workers; she now mistakes refusal of formal authority
  for moral cleanliness. Thirty-six years of authenticated work logs and
  demonstrated skill—not combat, game play, or unexplained memory essence—are
  what the local System recognizes as prior Combat Controller experience.
- **External pressure — Harven Coil:** Copperwell's watch captain, physically
  brave and operating under a real deadline. He wants Edda to use the class
  lattice to trap the Drouth League fighters in a pressure gallery and flush
  them out before they seize the cistern. His plan can preserve the city in the
  short term, but it knowingly treats Sula's repair crew and the attackers as
  acceptable losses. He is opposition, not a caricature: if the cistern falls,
  Copperwell loses its water before the dry month ends.
- **Human stake — Sula Brant:** Edda's former apprentice and current south-gate
  shift lead. She is trapped with two repair workers beyond the disputed
  gallery. She trusts Edda's judgment but refuses to surrender her own; through
  a voluntary beacon link, she challenges Edda to explain risks rather than
  issuing a comforting lie.
- **Collective opposition:** Drouth League fighters have entered the cistern to
  force open Copperwell's ration gates for their failing east-bank settlements.
  They remain human agents with an intelligible objective. Their signal lead
  stays unnamed and is referred to by a descriptive role, never a
  character-facing alias.
- **Internal movement:** Edda begins determined to rescue Sula without becoming
  "the woman at the board" again. Failed half-measures show that abstaining
  merely lets Harven define the acceptable losses. She ends by accepting
  responsibility for a plan while making its objective, uncertainty, and costs
  visible to every voluntary participant—including the enemy—and accepting
  that some may refuse.

## Causal arc

1. **Beginning state:** Edda enters the old cistern command gallery during the
   assault to retrieve Sula's crew routes, is knocked unconscious by a pressure
   shock, and is found by the damaged class lattice.
2. **Inciting disruption:** The exact System sequence confirms Edda's
   capabilities, seeks classes, assigns Combat Controller, authenticates her
   civic dispatch history as previous experience, advances her through thirteen
   levels, and wakes her. Harven immediately needs the access only her new
   class can provide.
3. **First turn:** Edda uses consent-based links and a map overlay to route
   defenders around a League push. The success proves the class is useful, but
   the outdated map nearly sends a watch pair through a collapsed conduit.
   Sula's correction establishes that the System's forecasts are tools, not
   truth.
4. **Escalation:** Pressure telemetry reveals a structural cascade: continuing
   the firefight will rupture the main cistern within minutes. Harven argues
   for the standard victory template—seal the south chamber and scour the
   occupied gallery—because it ends the battle quickly. Sula and two workers
   would die, and the pressure wave may still poison the basin with silt.
5. **Crisis:** Edda discovers Level 13's `COMMON CAUSE` protocol. It can invite
   declared hostile beacons into a temporary shared tactical field only if
   each participant accepts one plainly stated objective. It reveals positions
   and hazard data symmetrically, burns the failing lattice's remaining power,
   and cannot compel acceptance. Invoking it sacrifices Copperwell's tactical
   secrecy and may leave the city defenseless if the League exploits the map.
   Harven orders her to run the kill-box template.
6. **Climax:** Edda refuses the efficient lie. She states the objective
   "Nobody drowns. The cistern holds," discloses the true rupture risk, and
   opens Command Thirteen to both sides. Sula accepts first; enough watch and
   League fighters choose it for Edda to delegate synchronized teams: former
   enemies brace a jammed gate, vent pressure in timed pulses, move wounded
   people through a neutral route, and keep one another from firing. Harven
   initially refuses, then freely joins when his isolated squad becomes the
   only team able to turn the manual spillwheel.
7. **Resolution and aftermath:** The cistern holds and the immediate assault
   ends in an armed, fragile ceasefire around jointly opened ration channels.
   Edda discards the System's class only after exporting the truthful incident
   log, preventing either side from claiming a bloodless victory. She accepts
   Sula's invitation to help build a civilian coordination board whose first
   rule is that every command carries its cost beside it.

## Setup and payoff

- The opening dispatch log shows thirty-six years of floodworks calls and two
  deaths under an old closure protocol; the climax proves why the System sees
  relevant experience and why Edda refuses concealed costs.
- The System's `RETENTION DECISION DEFERRED UNTIL STABILITY` notice addresses
  the unwanted application early; Edda later chooses to discard the class
  after using it, so the consent issue is neither ignored nor allowed to halt
  the crisis.
- The first successful route and near-fatal obsolete map establish both power
  and fallibility; later, Edda combines telemetry with Sula's current local
  knowledge rather than trusting an omniscient overlay.
- Short notices establish that links require beacon acceptance, show only
  transmitted position/voice/vital signals, and consume power; these limits
  govern the climax.
- Sula's demand for the honest forecast rehearses Command Thirteen's rule:
  people coordinate better when the risk is stated and the choice remains
  theirs.
- Harven's repeated physical willingness to enter danger prepares his late
  acceptance of the shared objective without making his earlier sacrifice plan
  harmless.
- The eleven-minute cell warning becomes the ticking clock; opening the
  hostile mesh spends the reserve that could have preserved Copperwell's
  tactical advantage.
- The old manual spillwheel and south-gate brace appear before the crisis, so
  the climax is solved through seeded infrastructure and coordinated labor, not
  an unannounced ability.
- The phrase "every command has a cost" begins as Edda's bitter memory and ends
  as the founding rule of the civilian board.

## Canon constraints

- Keep Copperwell, its conflict, the Drouth League, the class lattice, and all
  class mechanics explicitly local to an unspecified era of the one shared
  physical world.
- Do not call the System objectively magical, divine, universally
  technological, alive, or sentient. The story treats it as a local,
  non-sentient technological interpretation used by this culture.
- The System obtains data only from old structural maps, damaged physical
  sensors, authenticated civic records, and voluntarily accepted beacons. It
  cannot read minds, know unreported facts, guarantee predictions, or command
  bodies.
- Previous experience is recognized by this lattice through Edda's
  authenticated thirty-six-year dispatch record and a capability match; do
  not imply that experience universally becomes power.
- Class application changes interface permissions and information handling,
  not Edda's identity, desires, memories, strength, or personhood. Retention is
  reversible once stability returns, and the lack of initial consent has
  narrative weight.
- `COMMON CAUSE` requires an explicit shared objective and individual opt-in,
  shares declared tactical data symmetrically, permits refusal or withdrawal,
  and cannot make enemies agree.
- Maintain the cognitive and power costs, damaged/incomplete data, limited
  range within the cistern mesh, and real possibility of refusal.
- Do not infer links to the Glass Gate, a named era, candidate stories, or
  other systems from similar motifs, terminology, or the number thirteen.
- Keep harm non-graphic, coordinated people morally consequential, and the
  ending local: the immediate assault stops, but one operation does not resolve
  regional scarcity or produce automatic justice.

## Proposed inventions

- Copperwell, a basin city dependent on one main cistern during a dry month.
- The Drouth League, a local coalition of east-bank settlements using force to
  open Copperwell's ration channels during acute scarcity.
- An old, damaged, non-sentient technological class lattice embedded in the
  cistern command gallery, with local terms `Host`, `Class`, `Combat
  Controller`, levels, and deferred retention.
- This lattice's method of matching authenticated civic work records and
  demonstrated capabilities to class experience; Edda's direct advance to
  Level 13.
- Consent-based beacon links, sensor/map overlays, priority flags, delegated
  coordination nodes, short-range forecasts, cognitive load, limited battery,
  fallible maps, and voluntary withdrawal.
- Level 13 `COMMON CAUSE`, also rendered as Command Thirteen: a time-limited
  protocol that lets self-identified hostile participants opt into one declared
  objective and symmetric tactical data without compulsion.
- Copperwell's pressure galleries, south chamber, ration gates, manual
  spillwheel, and the contained cistern crisis.
- Edda's work history, moral injury, temporary class use, truthful incident
  log, and later civilian-board commitment; Harven's and Sula's roles and
  choices; the fragile post-crisis water-sharing ceasefire.

All are proposed, local, and non-canon pending the completed workflow and any
later explicit promotion.

## Failure modes to watch

- Do not let the opening become an extended status screen. Preserve the prompt's
  update sequence, compress Levels 1–12, emphasize Level 13, and wake Edda into
  immediate physical pressure.
- Do not make thirty-six years of dispatch work a joke or a semantic loophole;
  show concrete skills—prioritization, uncertainty handling, delegation,
  infrastructure knowledge, and communicating risk—causing the outcome.
- Do not turn Edda into a battlefield omniscient. Every useful conclusion must
  come from identified sensors, maps, records, spoken reports, or her lived
  knowledge, with errors and gaps preserved.
- Do not personify the System, give it motives, or imply independent magical
  agency. Its notices can be responsive without becoming a character.
- Do not allow `COMMON CAUSE` to force agreement, reveal thoughts, erase
  hostility, prevent betrayal mechanically, or make refusal morally unreal.
- Do not flatten Harven into a cowardly villain or the Drouth League into
  faceless monsters. Both sides have urgent material stakes and remain
  responsible for violent choices.
- Do not solve the climax with rhetoric alone. The shared objective enables
  difficult, seeded physical work performed by several agents under time
  pressure.
- Track the eleven-minute cell limit, obsolete corridor, south crew position,
  pressure cascade, spillwheel, link acceptances/withdrawals, Edda's cognitive
  symptoms, and who knows which risk at each turn.
- Keep Sula agentic: she corrects the map, demands unsoftened information,
  accepts or refuses links, delegates a repair node, and materially shapes the
  plan.
- Do not erase casualties or political consequences with the ceasefire.
  Resolve the cistern assault and Edda's choice while leaving regional scarcity
  and future justice open.
- Register only character-facing forms actually planned or used; if drafting
  adds a callsign, person-like System label, named construct, surname label, or
  name for the League signal lead, stop and validate it before finalization.
