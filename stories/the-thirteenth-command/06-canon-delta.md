# Proposed canon delta

This file inventories reusable facts proposed by `05-story.md`. Every item is
**local to Copperwell, non-canon, and pending explicit approval**. Nothing here
establishes a universal class system, a named era or cross-story connection, or
personhood for the System.

## New characters or character facts

- **Edda Rook** — Proposed local character. Exact final forms: `Edda Rook`;
  `Edda`. `Rook` is not used alone. She is an older former floodworks
  dispatcher with thirty-six years of authenticated work involving flood
  calls, gate orders, pump failures, evacuation grids, prioritization,
  delegation, uncertainty, and risk communication.
  - During an earlier great surge, Edda obeyed a closure protocol that saved
    the western district but trapped and killed two workers. She gave them the
    approved reassurance before their beacons failed. Her moral injury led her
    to leave the board and confuse refusal of formal authority with freedom
    from responsibility.
  - In the current crisis, the lattice matches that lived work to Combat
    Controller experience. Edda initially conceals a forecast from Sula, then
    adopts informed participation: sources, uncertainty, alternatives, and
    costs stated plainly. She refuses Harven's scour order, invokes Common
    Cause, exports the incident log, discards the class, and agrees to help
    shape a civilian board.
- **Harven Coil** — Proposed local character. Exact final forms: `Harven Coil`;
  `Harven`. Neither `Coil` nor `Captain` is used alone. Copperwell's watch
  captain is brave and willing to enter danger, but his proposed scour plan
  treats Sula's crew and League fighters as acceptable losses. He withdraws his
  own link, cannot decide for his squad, later accepts Common Cause separately,
  and helps turn the manual spillwheel.
- **Sula Brant** — Proposed local character. Exact final forms: `Sula Brant`;
  `Sula`. `Brant` is not used alone. Edda's former apprentice is trapped with
  two unnamed repair workers in the south pressure chamber. She corrects stale
  data, demands an unsoftened forecast, supplies a current measurement, chooses
  the waste-vent route, accepts Common Cause first, takes the delegated south
  node, and controls brace and pressure-pulse timing.
- Two repair workers, watch members, League fighters, and the League signal
  lead remain unnamed. `watch captain`, `League signal lead`, and `signal lead`
  are lowercase descriptions, not names or aliases.

## New locations

- **Copperwell** — Proposed local city in an unspecified era of the one
  shared physical world. During a dry period, it depends on a main cistern and
  controls ration channels capable of sending water toward east-bank
  settlements.
- **Copperwell cistern complex** — Proposed local civic infrastructure:
  - an old command gallery with a physical dispatch board, civic-seal access,
    archived routes and protocols, damaged cells and conduits, structural
    sensors, and a cistern-range beacon mesh;
  - a southern pressure chamber, south bulkhead and automatic seal, twisting
    south gate and lower hinge, service crawl, and recently widened but
    uncertified waste vent;
  - upper and lower galleries, west gallery and controls, west filter, lower
    door, east spur and stair, filter stair, north walk, lower stair and drain,
    neutral maintenance walk, and a narrow waste-channel hatch absent from the
    main survey;
  - ration controls and channels, scour gates, gallery sluice, main baffle,
    cistern wall, and a manual spillwheel whose recurring jam is remedied by
    lifting before pulling.
- These places and designs are Copperwell-specific and have no proposed link to
  another era, location, artifact, or story system.

## New factions or cultural facts

- **Drouth League** — Proposed local coalition of water-starved east-bank
  settlements. League fighters enter the cistern with rifles to force open
  Copperwell's ration channels because their settlements have days rather than
  weeks of water left. Their need explains but does not excuse the assault.
- **Copperwell's watch** — Proposed local civic defense force led in this
  crisis by Harven. It initially holds the upper gallery while the League holds
  the west ration controls. Each watch member controls their own lattice link
  even while following physical orders.
- Proposed local civic practices include floodworks dispatch, civic seals,
  authenticated work archives, beacons, repair certification, ration
  stewardship, and incident records.
- After the cistern is stabilized, the two sides keep separate positions but
  enter a fragile armed ceasefire around one jointly opened narrow ration
  channel toward the east bank. They negotiate its duration; no peace treaty,
  settled ration policy, or resolution of regional scarcity follows.
- **Civilian coordination board** — Proposed but not yet built. Sula suggests a
  board involving repair crews, ration stewards, and east-bank signals if they
  choose, using current maps and shared costs. Edda's proposed first rule is:
  every command carries its cost beside it.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Lattice nature and provenance** — Proposed local mechanism. The old class
  lattice is embedded in Copperwell's cistern board and infrastructure. It uses
  damaged power cells, physical conduits, archived protocols, local sensors,
  and civic-record access. It is treated as a non-sentient technological
  coordination engine, not a mind, living being, magical user, god, named
  construct, or character. Its builders, exact age, original scope, and wider
  ancestry remain unknown.
- **Local vocabulary** — `System`, `Host`, `Class`, `Combat Controller`,
  `Controller`, `previous experience`, `Level Up`, `Level 13`, `temporary class
  session`, `mesh`, `beacon link`, `node`, `stability threshold`, `permanent
  profile`, `Common Cause`, interface form `COMMAND 13`, and prose form
  `Command Thirteen`. These are interface, class, protocol, or role terms, not
  personal names.
- **Authenticated-experience matching** — After finding Edda unconscious, the
  lattice tags her civic seal, confirms capabilities, seeks a class
  application, and matches thirty-six years of authenticated floodworks work
  and demonstrated skill to Combat Controller experience. It applies thirteen
  levels by indexing and routing abilities she already possesses. It does not
  add memories, alter identity or desire, increase strength, or imply that
  lived experience universally becomes levels.
- **Data sources** — Old maps and structural surveys; damaged physical sensors;
  authenticated work logs, maintenance records, and inspection reports;
  position, voice, and pulse data voluntarily transmitted by beacons; current
  human reports such as Sula's work-slate measurement and the League hand
  gauge; and Edda's lived infrastructure knowledge. The lattice cannot read
  thoughts, know unreported facts or motives, guarantee a forecast, or command
  a body.
- **Consent and individual link behavior** — The lattice applies Edda's
  initial permissions while she is unconscious and defers retention until
  stability; the unwanted application remains an ethical injury. Edda accepts
  the temporary session after waking. Ordinary links appear as requests,
  require individual acceptance, and transmit only permitted categories.
  Participants may accept, refuse, or withdraw separately; a commander cannot
  choose for a squad. Withdrawing removes that person's live position.
  Delegated nodes govern only information a participating group chooses to
  route.
- **Errors** — The system can offer a collapsed corridor as open, omit
  uncertified changes, use an obsolete gate profile, lose beacons in sensor
  shadow, and receive physical readings that run high or low. Forecasts carry
  confidence grades, source ages, ranges, and uncertainty. Human reports can
  correct stale data but remain sourced and potentially unverified.
- **Costs and limits** — The mesh reaches only through the Copperwell cistern.
  Its damaged cells begin with roughly eleven minutes at the initial load;
  Common Cause spends the operational reserve, leaving nineteen seconds after
  stabilization. Concurrent voices, routes, priorities, and forecasts cause
  Edda mounting pressure behind the eyes, darkened peripheral vision, hand
  tremor, missed cues, and a lost timing thread. Delegation reduces but does
  not remove the burden.
- **Retention, discard, and export** — At stability, Edda may retain Combat
  Controller as a permanent profile or discard it. She discards it, ending the
  overlays. First she exports a symmetric incident log to the civic archive and
  every beacon that accepted Common Cause. It records the assault, scour order,
  refusal, map errors, uncertainty, withdrawals, wounded people, and current
  battle's one death. The record is evidence, not automatic justice.
- **Common Cause / Command Thirteen** — Proposed local Level 13 protocol:
  - It requires one declared objective; Edda states, “Nobody drowns. The
    cistern holds.”
  - It can invite beacons tagged hostile within lattice range. Before an
    individual accepts, the invitation contains only the objective and
    host-published structural hazards, static routes and control locations,
    uncertainty, and strategic cost. No live position crosses the field.
  - After individual acceptance, accepted positions, voices, hazard readings,
    and published routes are shared symmetrically. Participants may separately
    accept, refuse, or withdraw.
  - It cannot compel agreement, alter desire, prevent lies, betrayal, or
    gunfire, or make refusal unreal. It exposes Copperwell's static tactical
    infrastructure and consumes all operational reserve.
  - Its success depends on voluntary physical work: bracing the gate from both
    sides, turning the spillwheel, opening a ration channel in measured
    increments, moving wounded people through the neutral walk, and timing
    pressure pulses.

## Timeline events

- **Copperwell cistern assault** — Proposed local event in an unspecified era.
  Drouth League fighters enter the cistern during acute east-bank scarcity.
  Gunfire and opposed gate operations create a pressure cascade threatening the
  main cistern. Individually consenting people from both sides use Common Cause
  and coordinated physical work to vent the pressure; the wall flexes but
  holds.
- **Exact current-battle death count:** one unnamed person dies before Common
  Cause opens. Other people are wounded, with no exact reusable count. This is
  separate from the two deaths in Edda's earlier surge history.
- The immediate assault ends in the fragile armed ceasefire described above.
