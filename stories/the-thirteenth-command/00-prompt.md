# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] System Update: Host found - Host's Capabilities confirmed - Seeking Class applications - Class found - Applying Class: Combat Controller - previous experience confirmed - applying previous experience - Level Up - Level Up - ... - Level 13 reached with previous experience in Class - waking Host**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Thirteenth Command
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person centered on the Host.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Tense, humane progression fantasy with science-fantasy elements; tactical rather than power-fantasy triumphant.
- Audience/content rating: Teen; non-graphic battlefield peril and death, no sexual content.
- Required elements: The prompt's system update appears as an awakening sequence; the Host is assigned Combat Controller; thirteen levels arrive because the system recognizes real prior experience; the prior experience has a concrete, surprising source; controlling combat means coordinating people and information rather than overriding minds; the Level 13 abilities materially affect a present crisis; the Host must make a consequential choice that resolves the immediate conflict.
- Prohibited elements: No synopsis or chapter-fragment ending; no effortless victory produced only by inflated statistics; no coercive mind control treated as harmless; no unbounded universal rule added merely to service this story; no canon promotion.

## Assumptions

- "Previous experience" means transferable lived skill acquired before the Host ever saw a class interface, not a hidden stash of game points.
- The class system will be treated as a bounded phenomenon unless canon research establishes a broader mechanism.
- The Host wakes into an urgent conflict whose human cost cannot be solved by optimization alone.
- The repeated Level Up sequence may be compressed on the page, but Level 13 and the reason for reaching it must be explicit.
- The story may introduce local characters, a local crisis, and limited system terminology where canon is silent; reusable additions will be listed in `06-canon-delta.md`.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
- The reader understands why the system classified the Host as experienced, what Combat Controller can and cannot do, and why thirteen levels are both useful and dangerous.
- The ending resolves the immediate battle and the Host's choice about what kind of controller to become.
