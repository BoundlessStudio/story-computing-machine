# Prompt contract

> Contract status: CAPTURED
> Story slug: `the-five-minute-warranty`
> Captured: 2026-08-02

## Verbatim writing prompt

> [WP] Goblin's aren't terrible engineers, they can make anything work. And I mean anything. Just... not for very long.

## Story controls

- Working title: The Five-Minute Warranty
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person, selected during planning.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Warm, high-pressure comic fantasy with sincere stakes.
- Audience/content rating: Teen; danger and non-graphic injury are acceptable.
- Required elements: Goblins are demonstrably capable engineers; they can make an
  apparently impossible system work; its sharply limited operating life creates a
  visible deadline; the temporary solution enables a durable human choice or
  consequence; the plot reaches a complete resolution.
- Prohibited elements: Treating goblins as stupid, making the failure purely random,
  or using the prompt only as a closing punchline without dramatizing the engineering.

## Assumptions

- “Goblin's” is preserved verbatim above; the story may use the plural “goblins.”
- The prompt permits an original cast, location, and mechanism within current shared-
  universe authority.
- “Anything” is interpreted as extraordinary repair and recombination under pressure,
  not unrestricted reality alteration.
- “Not for very long” will be an engineering constraint with understandable causes,
  tradeoffs, and consequences rather than proof of incompetence.
- No canon promotion is implied; successful completion produces a non-canon candidate.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The temporary machine's operating window is legible enough that readers can track
  the escalating deadline.
- Goblin ingenuity materially saves people or preserves something worth saving.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
