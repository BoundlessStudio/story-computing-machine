## Canon posture

The final story supports one emergency involving Kezli, Olbit, their unnamed
local workshop, Director Nalda Vick, and the North Rise in one unnamed quarry
city during an unplaced fantasy-industrial era. It establishes no relationship
to an admitted story, established location, named era, or dated chronology.

Goblin engineering remains rigorously local to these two goblin engineers,
their workshop conduct, and the reputation attributed to that workshop in this
city. The story does not establish a biological goblin talent, a universal
goblin temperament, a world-spanning goblin culture, or a fixed lifespan for
goblin work. “Anything” is local hyperbole immediately narrowed by Kezli to
making these damaged parts agree under a declared load for five minutes.

The North Rise and the emergency rig are mundane, nonliving, non-agentic, and
nonmagical machinery. Coal, steam, friction, stored mechanical motion, springs,
and gravity supply or transmit the observed work. No machine casts, chooses,
consents, suffers, or supplies living agency.
