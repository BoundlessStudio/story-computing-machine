- Keep in substance: Caleb is a lost incoming Brightward College student; he spots Mirelle, runs to her, embraces her, and the room goes silent because the students know and fear her as the Blizzard Baroness.
- Keep in substance: Brightward is a strange superhero college discovered through a campus tour, including contradictory navigation, hazardous institutional absurdity, and students who keep their distance from Mirelle.
- Keep in substance: Mirelle is a genuinely formidable cursed cold prodigy who is cold and blunt with most people but affectionate with Caleb; affection neither cures nor disproves her power or reputation.
- Keep in substance: A student tries to use Mirelle as a cold source or test resource, exposing exploitation beneath the campus fear.
- Keep in substance: The crowd does not convert, Mirelle keeps her boundaries and agency, and the tour resolves with chosen public affection and a warm title payoff.
- Keep in substance: Caleb owns expansive self-mocking humor; Mirelle owns narrow refusals, literal distinctions, dry humor, and social authority.
- Change or replace: Make Caleb's discoveries concern Brightward, the Blizzard Baroness title, the students' fear, rumor, avoidance, and exploitation, and the social meaning of Mirelle's campus persona—not previously unknown fundamentals about Mirelle herself.
- Change or replace: Make the opening embrace recognizably familiar and welcome between partners. The room's silence and tension should arise primarily from the students witnessing someone freely hug the feared Blizzard Baroness, not from Caleb discovering that touching or startling his girlfriend violates an unknown rule.
- Change or replace: Establish that Caleb already understands Mirelle's curse mechanics, warning signs, physical costs, ordinary touch preferences, and private temperament before the story begins. He may be surprised by the scale of Brightward's institutional response or by how much control she must display there.
- Change or replace: Reframe the relationship pressure: Caleb mistakenly tries to normalize, soften, or translate Mirelle for frightened students because he wants them to see the person he knows. He must learn that knowing her does not authorize him to manage her campus reputation or revise her refusals.
- Change or replace: Rebuild the private conversation so Mirelle explains the place, title, students, and usefulness or cost of their fear rather than explaining herself to her boyfriend. Preserve emotional movement without making the established couple renegotiate whether they know, trust, or desire each other.
- Change or replace: Make Mirelle's final public affection reveal an existing tenderness to Brightward while confirming Caleb has learned how to stand beside her in this setting.
- Remove: The stairwell tutorial in which Caleb says he thought Mirelle merely made things cold and learns the basic three-metre startle pulse, redirection rule, and numbing cost for the first time.
- Remove: The second startle-and-freeze demonstration on the back stair.
- Remove: Any implication that Caleb does not know whether Mirelle likes his touch, is unsure whether she loves or welcomes him, or must newly learn the basic terms of being her boyfriend.
- Remove: The accusation and apology arc centered on Caleb grabbing Mirelle without permission; retain only setting-specific accountability for speaking over her or trying to soften her no.
- Remove: Any framing in which the Blizzard Baroness is a hidden true Mirelle that displaces the girlfriend Caleb already knows.
