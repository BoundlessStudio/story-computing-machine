# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: For ages, elven guardian Avarra has ridden the giant white elk Winterbough along Elderweald's inner edge. When he crosses its ancient stones for the first time, she follows through forgotten kingdoms and discovers the change: abandoned outer land has become woodland, its youngest roots joining Elderweald. The old boundary no longer describes the forest.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Avarra loves Winterbough but has fused guardianship with a fixed line, familiar circuit, and belief that she guides him. She wants him safely back and the crossing to mean a danger she can oppose. Following risks admitting that his silence has always contained choices she missed.
- Counterforce or complication: Winterbough cannot translate his purpose, and Avarra treats refusal as alarm or stubbornness. Outside, the life-sustaining seasonal pulse is intermittent, old roads imitate boundaries, and layered ruins support incomplete explanations. She may return at each night's halt, but cannot compel him.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Avarra. Notice Winterbough's pale shag under her hand, muscle and breath, bare feet against bark or soil, shade, root tension, and missing landmarks. She cannot enter his mind, hear a forest consciousness, or recover ruined kingdoms' histories; the answer accumulates from material succession and the local pulse.
- Governing movement and time shape: Three outward days, each allowing return. A familiar loop breaks into a crossing, a traverse through cleared land gone wild, then a widening walk through young forest over masonry. Resolve privately where old and new growth join, ending on a changed choice between rider and mount rather than public proof or a replacement boundary.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Elderweald's connected living roots share a slow seasonal rhythm with Avarra and Winterbough through physical contact, explaining their local longevity without granting memories, speech, command, or species-wide immortality. Winterbough senses continuity through his hooves; Avarra feels a dim pulse through touch. Inert stones mark where roots once ended. Ordinary growth can extend the connection.
- Dialogue promise: An affectionate ancient partnership is tested when Avarra's fluent one-sided companionship stops passing for mutual understanding. Keep communication intimate, irritated, and sometimes embarrassing without making Winterbough secretly human in thought or speech.
- Dialogic medium: Avarra's speech meets Winterbough's pace, breath, ear and antler orientation, kneeling, refusal, shared touch, silence, and the root pulse. Failed commands count as exchanges; narration supplies no translation.
- Dialogue engine: Avarra needs explanation and return; Winterbough can only lead, wait, reject direction, or offer contact. Each attempt changes their route and her reading until trust requires meeting him on terms other than command.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move at hoof and walking pace through weight, weather, fur, roots, and worked stone. Let Avarra compress kingdoms without omniscience; widen only as the former outside becomes legibly wooded.
- Conversational texture: Avarra's talk is a domestic stream of observations, misplaced certainty, small complaints, and repeated questions. Winterbough answers through sparse physical decisions open to misreading.
- Rhetorical ownership: Avarra owns duty language, historical analogy, dry humor, evasion, and premature conclusions. Winterbough owns timing, refusal, directional authority, affection, and willingness to listen through altered pace rather than eloquence.
- Pressure behavior: Fear makes Avarra talkative and commanding, then embarrassed when commands fail. She shifts toward fewer words, longer waits, and attention to weight.
- Relationship movement: Avarra wants reassurance that Winterbough needs her but cannot ask whether he has been guarding her. He needs her to follow and notice without stating either need. Exchanges redistribute route authority, knowledge, and trust while leaving his full intent uncertain.
- Anti-default: Avoid immortal experts decoding a ward, a speaking-animal oracle, a hostile kingdom, or triumphant border redrawing. Keep Avarra fallible and awkward beyond habit; let ecological accumulation and private trust resolve the story.

## Beats

1. Establish the ancient circuit and bodily ease: dark-haired adult Avarra rides without controlling harness, hand in Winterbough's dense pale fur, through dim forest he physically navigates.
2. Winterbough crosses the lichened stones. Avarra tests danger, coaxes, commands, and considers dismounting; his refusal to turn and patient contact make her crossing voluntary.
3. Outside is empty rather than attacked. She seeks smoke, banners, axes, or broken magic, finding mossed roads, thicketed walls, and differently aged trees while Winterbough directs attention downward.
4. At the first halt, the root pulse weakens but persists. Avarra can return alone or stay without proof. She stays; discomfort and poor sleep keep the choice bodily.
5. Layered ruins show no single catastrophe: palaces became quarries, roads drainage, orchards woodland, and later settlements also vanished. Avarra's remembered names match nothing.
6. Winterbough reaches a newly broken foundation band where fresh outer root tips meet Elderweald's older web. Through him, Avarra feels one pulse: a sudden crossing enabled by centuries of regrowth.
7. Higher ground reveals young canopy across the former outside. Accepting that the stones mark history, Avarra declines Winterbough's offered back for now and walks beside him, leaving direction to him.

## Continuity

- Canon used: One physical world persists through deep-time civilizational rise and collapse; evidence degrades; magic is local and only life uses it. No recurring noun or era is proposed.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Keep the era and kingdoms unidentified. Do not connect Elderweald, its stones, elves, or white elk to existing systems. Longevity is local; roots share rhythm, not thought or history; stones neither command nor imprison. Image jewelry, clothing, flowers, and sparks remain optional atmosphere.
