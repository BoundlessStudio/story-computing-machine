# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: While clearing his late mother’s apartment on a sleeting night, Tobin Lerrow hears a fall next door. Aldith Gant—the black-clad, apparently twenty-something witch treated as the town’s oldest creditor—has bought an adult tenant time through a respiratory crisis and is aging rapidly. She claims Tobin’s mother owed one safety watch. Resolve Aldith’s youth, the townwide debts, and whether Tobin can help without inheriting one.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Tobin wants to finish packing and leave town, but cannot ignore a sick neighbor. He needs Aldith to separate an emergency request from a family claim. Aldith needs a witness before sunrise yet trusts account language more than a request someone may refuse.
- Counterforce or complication: Delay worsens Aldith’s condition. Her records mix current agreements, promises that died with their makers, duties descendants freely assumed, gifts misfiled as payment, and her own debts. She usually accepts refusal but encouraged hereditary shorthand because it made help predictable; she is neither predator nor perfect guardian.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close first-person past through adult Tobin, noticing pulse, temperature, timers, faulty hinges, wet shoes, and tenancy papers. He knows gossip, not Aldith’s history; tonight’s records, interruptions, and bodily evidence limit inference. Her exact age and old crises remain uncertain.
- Governing movement and time shape: One night in two neighboring apartments, counting down from ambulance departure to sunrise: refusal, assessment, selective record checking, two interruptions, a failed solo reset, and a chosen watch. Keep the climax at Aldith’s kitchen table, not in public. End after medical follow-up is secured, with tonight bounded and the ledger unresolved.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: With a willing living adult’s consent and touch, Aldith can suspend one acute bodily failure for twenty minutes by carrying its strain; she cannot cure its cause, replace emergency care, or work remotely. The strain ages her. Before sunrise, her living magic can return her toward an adult baseline, but a consenting witness must touch, observe, and interrupt unsafe drift. The witness supplies judgment, not youth or fuel, and may stop. Restoration costs food, sleep, and cumulative injury; missed strain becomes permanent. Records, timers, mirrors, and tokens are inert aids, not agents or truth machines.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Tobin narrates by triage—objects, measurements, minutes, corrections—before admitting human implications. Gothic textures are maintained domestic choices amid radiators, groceries, pills, and boxes; bodily evidence carries wonder.
- Conversational texture: Talk is interruptible and apartment-sized: pulse-check questions, revised instructions, a delivery, one phone refusal, and stretches when touch or handwriting works. Keep ordinary single-purpose speech.
- Rhetorical ownership: Tobin owns troubleshooting, tenancy boundaries, and plain request-versus-duty distinctions. Aldith owns dates, categories, conditional precision, and passive constructions that hide desire. Residents speak only from immediate errands or limits.
- Pressure behavior: Tobin shortens and repeats, then listens before fixing. Aldith’s formal syntax sheds qualifiers into fragments; she evades need through procedure. Refusal need not become eloquent.
- Anti-default: Avoid flirtatious immortal banter, an omniscient gothic hostess, grateful-town chorus, courtroom neatness, and consent slogans. Preserve awkward dependence, compromised bookkeeping, and tonight’s help as distinct from inherited duty.

## Beats

1. Packing his mother’s kitchen, Tobin watches paramedics take an unnamed tenant away. A crash reveals Aldith aging inside her open doorway. He checks danger before considering her claim that his family owes a watch.
2. Canned soup, repair notices, medical supplies, and a faulty kettle undercut Aldith’s legendary apartment; only the indexed cabinet feels ceremonial. Tobin refuses his mother’s obligation. Aldith explains the deadline yet still calls her need an account.
3. Tobin finds heterogeneous entries without touring family histories. Across two centuries, Aldith accepted later favors for magical aid and ordinary work as midwife, translator, lender, and sitter. Intermarriage and help to newcomers put every established household under a heading. Marks distinguish fulfilled, assumed, disputed, lapsed, and reciprocal entries; family indexing proves no inheritance.
4. One household delivers agreed groceries; another adult refuses a dead relative’s task. Aldith accepts refusal but calls it an adjustment. Tobin finds favors Aldith owes and instances where she let useful ambiguity survive its maker.
5. Aldith marks Tobin’s mother’s promise lapsed, then tries restoration alone. The change becomes uneven without living feedback; she stops rather than compel Tobin or pretend objects can substitute. He must choose after leverage is gone.
6. Tobin accepts only tonight’s watch. Draft restoration through food, timed observation, touch, pauses, and stop-or-continue decisions. Dialogue must change usable information and control; avoid confession, apology, or reform plan.
7. Aldith returns toward her youthful baseline but retains exhaustion, tremor, and cumulative damage. Tobin secures ordinary medical follow-up and returns next door without taking her cabinet or promising future watches. They are two neighbors after one bounded choice; other accounts remain unsettled between living people.
