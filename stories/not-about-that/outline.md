# Outline

## Story

- Title: Not About That
- Premise and central promise: In an era crowded with magical girls, teenage Lowen secretly activates a family focus, accepts its required feminine body, and finds flying easier than asking his sister Sarena to believe he remains her brother. Resolve whether he can keep magic and stated boyhood without treating Sarena's transness as the outcome to fear.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Lowen wants Sarena's respect and another flight. He enjoys the feminine form but fears that admitting pleasure will let others overrule his account of himself.
- Counterforce or complication: Lowen took an unassigned focus before their supervised trial. Showy use prevents reversion until he grounds its lift. Sarena resents the risk and quoted denial; Lowen hears hurt and instruction as diagnosis.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Lowen, attentive to altered balance, clothes, wind, delight, and embarrassment. Never enter Sarena's mind or certify Lowen as cis, closeted, or destined for another label.
- Governing movement and time shape: One late-afternoon practice spiral from roof to courtyard and back by dusk. Each landing attempt changes both remaining lift and a misreading. Sarena's last wind-knot and Lowen's practiced landing make the private turn; chosen images, not technical proof, close it.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: The nonliving focus answers only Lowen's deliberate living agency, gives him one stable feminine form, and makes brief footholds redirecting his momentum. Used lift requires a controlled landing before reversion; panic spoils aim but cannot choose or define him. Nothing classifies another system.
- Dialogue promise: Embarrassed sibling comedy: teasing, interruption, wrong guesses, ill-timed safety calls, and one prompt-required outburst whose harm cannot be repaired by assigning Lowen a different label.
- Dialogic medium: Face-to-face speech, overlap, silence, pointing, demonstrated footwork, chalk marks, a thrown wind-knot, shouted or gestured corrections, camera framing, and selective image keeping.
- Dialogue engine: Lowen needs Sarena's anchor, instruction, and belief but cannot ask without admitting fear. Sarena needs him to acknowledge both unauthorized risk and the insult without seeming to recruit him into her story. Each attempt changes danger and listening; the photograph makes self-presentation an explicit choice.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Agile close third tracks weight, clothing, edges, and the magical skyline. Sentences open with exhilaration, then double back when embarrassment distorts Lowen's hearing.
- Conversational texture: Sibling talk moves among petty scores, teasing, overlap, sulking, clipped safety calls, and unfinished questions. Preserve badly timed humor and remarks serving only the moment.
- Rhetorical ownership: Lowen owns categorical denial, over-explanation, defensive jokes, and suspicious misreading. Sarena owns shorthand, sharper teasing, selective silence, and social confidence; trans experience gives stakes, not authority over his label. Affection appears through attention, anchoring, and image care.
- Pressure behavior: Lowen stutters, talks past instructions, and turns fear into argument. Sarena's humor dries into literal brevity. Wind and height break both speakers' fluency, making listening physically consequential.
- Relationship movement: Lowen wants belief, welcome, and rescue but cannot ask for all three. Sarena wants trust and recognition that she is not his humiliating counterexample. Exchanges move them from assumed access through rupture and accepted tether to Lowen trusting her with a chosen record of his magical form.
- Anti-default: Avoid an egg-crack reveal, tidy lesson, or polished trans spokesperson correcting a cis boy. The body proves nothing; keep Lowen's present claim authoritative, Sarena's hurt specific, and future understanding open.

## Beats

1. Magical girls commute, practice, quarrel, and miss landings above an ordinary block. Lowen activates the focus knowing its form is feminine; delight in air-stepping arrives before explanation.
2. A copied flourish overshoots the chalk circle, leaving lift active and reversion unavailable. Sarena returns, prevents a worse fall, and confronts the unauthorized trial. Her unfinished question touches Lowen's fear; he delivers the prompt's quotation verbatim. Her withdrawal makes its insult land without a speech.
3. Lowen insists on grounding alone. Comic failures carry them between roofs and courtyard: he copies strangers, argues through instructions, and misses Sarena's consistent name and pronouns. She makes a bad call and laughs at the wrong instant, keeping expertise imperfect.
4. Dusk wind erodes Lowen's last footholds. He must separate the practical request from smuggled reassurance; Sarena must separate belief in his stated boyhood from pretending he did not wound her. Let sparse words, changed positions, and willingness to listen carry this turn, not confession or protocol.
5. Sarena anchors and throws her last wind-knot. Lowen accepts the tether and lands by applying the embodied footwork learned through prior failures. Grounded and free to revert, he explicitly chooses to have Sarena photograph his feminine magical form for himself. Sarena keeps both an awkward image and a laughing one under his chosen limits. End on that selective image-sharing and keeping, not another practice promise or focus handoff.
