# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.
Every mechanism and practice below is local to this story unless a later,
approved canon decision gives it wider scope.

## New characters or character facts

- **Tair Ked** (story-facing forms: `Tair Ked`, `Tair`) is a solitary route
  surveyor who has spent five years recording bearings in a calibrated
  brass-and-silver sunwheel. Tair reaches Qarsis after four desert days, sells
  the sunwheel's bimetal heart to the city for an emergency repair, and learns
  to name and accept the full replacement, delay, lost-work, recutting, and
  provision costs rather than treating unpaid self-sacrifice as morally
  cleaner. Tair then accepts Urumet's separate commission to carry a letter
  north and, after receiving a replacement sunwheel, survey a route for light
  freight.
- **Urumet** (story-facing form: `Urumet`; no title or nickname) is a
  slate-blue, hoarfrost-edged ice dragon living voluntarily in the Coldmouth at
  Qarsis. Urumet came from an unnamed ice shelf far to the north, left it by
  choice, enjoys its precise paid work in Qarsis, and can also miss its
  northern home. It sets prices and surcharges, checks and stamps receipts,
  keeps and spends its own earnings, accepts or refuses work, and will not use
  unsafe equipment or exceed its local cold limit. Its story-shown compensation
  includes small coins and pepper sweets, and its accounts track northbound
  freight. Urumet remains in Qarsis after the story and commissions a commercial
  route that may carry letters, light freight, visitors, or a future visit; the
  commission is not a rescue or immediate return home.
- **Yara Mesk** (story-facing forms: `Yara Mesk`, `Yara`) is Qarsis's civic
  waterkeeper. She manages water accounts and rations, extends recoverable
  hospitality credit, recognizes the sunwheel's usable bimetal core, obtains
  Tair's informed sale, cuts and calibrates the replacement regulator tongue,
  and refuses to confiscate a traveler's property even during a water crisis.

## New locations

- **Qarsis** is a local desert city of roughly five hundred residents built
  against the lower face of the Black Shoulder, far from potable surface
  water. Its walls and terraces occupy the mountain's shadow, and its water
  works combine several limited sources rather than relying on a single
  miracle.
- **The Black Shoulder** is the dark mountain against which Qarsis is built. It
  has rare runoff, a deep brine seep, and the cave called the Coldmouth.
- **The Coldmouth** is Urumet's cave and the site of Qarsis's regulator and
  brine exchanger. It is a place name, not a person-like entity.
- An existing, marked north caravan road reaches a freight shelter beside an
  otherwise unnamed split-stone well about eight days from Qarsis. Tair's
  replacement sunwheel is contracted for delivery there before any new
  precision surveying begins.

## New factions or cultural facts

- **New named factions:** None.
- Qarsis maintains civic water accounts. Emergency water may be issued before
  immediate payment as recoverable hospitality credit at the ordinary ration
  price, payable in coin or work.
- Qarsis's practice during this event is to purchase private equipment,
  professional labor, and Urumet's cold through explicit terms rather than
  seize them under emergency need. The city separately compensates Tair for
  the sunwheel, delay, lost earnings, recutting work, and provisions; Urumet
  separately hires Tair for the northern commission.
- Receipts, ledgers, consent, and purchase are civic and ethical practices
  here. They have no proposed universal binding or magical force.
- **New named currency or universal economic system:** None. The story uses
  unnamed local small coins, wages, provisions, surcharges, and freight
  accounts.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- Qarsis's local mixed water system uses solar stills, rare mountain runoff,
  cistern carryover, household storage, a deep brine seep, and freeze
  separation powered by Urumet's chosen cold. In ordinary conditions roughly
  five hundred residents receive about twelve litres each per day, an
  approximate civic need of 6,000 litres. Stills and runoff supply about half
  an ordinary day; Urumet's six exchanger batches yield roughly 400 litres
  each, or about 2,400 litres; stored carryover closes the remaining variation.
  These are local working figures, not global hydrological constants.
- Urumet locally sells at most six four-count **strokes** in a day. Each
  controlled stroke chills one loaded exchanger batch. The cold comes from
  Urumet alone. The process causes heat to accumulate in Urumet as visible
  hoarfrost recedes; after six strokes, it sheds that heat and recovers
  overnight. Urumet may still refuse fresh work after recovery until the
  equipment has been cleared and inspected. The limit and recovery apply to
  Urumet as portrayed here and make no species-wide claim about dragons.
- Payment, pleasure, need, and consent do not generate Urumet's power. Payment
  compensates work Urumet chooses and enjoys; the six-stroke ceiling is a
  physical local limit that higher payment and emergencies do not override.
- The **regulator** and **brine exchanger** are nonliving civic
  mechanisms. A bimetal tongue meters the duration of cold supplied by Urumet,
  while copper ribs and gates direct that supplied cold through brine so clean
  ice separates from concentrated bitter water. The mechanisms do not decide,
  cast, breathe, or initiate magic. A replacement regulator tongue can be
  bench-calibrated without a fresh magical discharge, using warm return brine
  and stored ice from an already completed batch.
- A **glasswind** is a severe local desert storm carrying glassy grit. In this
  event it buries Qarsis's exposed solar still field and drives a stone through
  the Coldmouth's outer screen hard enough to shatter the regulator.
- **New universal rules of magic, dragon biology, payment, names, or
  personhood:** None.

## Timeline events

- In an unnamed era, after four days beyond the last mapped cistern, Tair
  reaches Qarsis as an early glasswind approaches.
- On a day when Urumet has completed four of its six contracted strokes, the
  glasswind buries the solar stills and shatters the regulator.
  Qarsis has about 4,200 litres in emergency reserve plus household jars; the
  final two batches can add roughly 800 litres, enough for a reduced one-day
  ration of about ten litres per person and for the clearing crews.
- Tair agrees to sell the calibrated heart of the sunwheel at a stated
  replacement-and-delay price. Yara uses it to repair and nonmagically
  calibrate the regulator. Urumet accepts the repair for the two already
  contracted strokes and performs exactly the fifth and sixth strokes, with no
  diagnostic cold, seventh stroke, or emergency exception.
- Urumet completes its heat-shedding recovery by dawn, then withholds new work
  until the exchanger intake is cleared and inspected. The glasswind ends the
  following afternoon; Qarsis clears the stills, restores distillation, and
  survives the bridge day without cutting the old, sick, or young below the
  reduced safety ration.
- Qarsis contracts to deliver Tair's replacement sunwheel to the known freight
  shelter after the road reopens. Tair departs along the marked eight-day
  caravan leg with Urumet's paid letter and first commission installment, and
  will begin new precision surveying only after the replacement arrives.

## Possible conflicts or retcons

- **Direct conflict with current authoritative canon:** None identified in the
  canon brief or draft review.
- **Approved retcon required:** None.
- Promotion must preserve the local scope of Urumet's cold, capacity,
  heat-shedding recovery, preferences, and northern origin. Current canon does
  not define dragons generally; these facts must not become claims about every
  dragon, all cold-working beings, or a universal magical category.
- Promotion must preserve Urumet as the only living initiator of cold. The
  regulator and exchanger are nonliving tools that meter and shape a living
  agent's supplied magic.
- Promotion must not imply that payment, pleasure, consent, contracts, names,
  receipts, or civic need fuel magic. Their role in Qarsis is economic,
  ethical, and personal.
- Qarsis, the Black Shoulder, the Coldmouth, and the northward route remain
  local to an unnamed era and have no proposed identity, ancestry, or crossover
  connection to another story's city, dragon, artifact, faction, or magical
  system.
