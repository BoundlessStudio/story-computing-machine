- **Severity:** Minor

  **Evidence:** Urumet says it sheds the accumulated heat overnight, but after
  the storm ends the following afternoon and the stills restart by sunset, the
  closing image describes it as sleeping off the same six strokes.
  **Why it matters:** This creates mild ambiguity over whether recovery takes
  one night or extends into the next evening, and whether Urumet was available
  for a new day's bounded work.
  **Smallest effective fix:** Clarify that the closing sleep is ordinary rest
  after completed overnight recovery, or adjust the stated recovery interval.
