# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Six weeks after saving a kingdom, five retired companions share Manydoor Hall with three campaign intimates and Brum, a white wolf. Dabria stages a full-house library evening to prove peace has not dissolved fellowship. Ornamented blue Oruvane lies amid readers, lovers, crafters, talk, and small magic, yet each occupies a separate cluster. His door maze exposes privacy wounds, Cavren's departure, and Dabria's broken final-stair promise. Can chosen return and incomplete repair constitute friendship?
- Focal pressure or attachment: Dabria wants proof of togetherness, but confuses attendance, access, and usefulness with intimacy. She must risk invitations that can be refused.
- Counterforce or complication: Courtship, solitude, movement, play, and acknowledgment compete without a villain. Oruvane's poor bids disrupt; Dabria assigns solutions.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Dabria. Her tactical eye counts bodies, doors, and tasks while misreading motives; others register through talk, touch, work, silence, and Brum's loyalties.
- Governing movement and time shape: Expand one compulsory gathering, compress six questing days, expand the seventh-night maze and predawn departure, then circle back three days later without solving every relationship.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Only library and roof court fit Oruvane; human corridors make privacy resemble disappearance. He may leave freely. Living practitioners supply minor magic; objects never act alone.
- Dialogue promise: Overlapping talk, couple-rhythm, stale jokes, missed bids, firm boundaries, and a grievance resisting reconciliation. The chorus interrupts rather than explains.
- Dialogic medium: Speech, roster and quest cards, touch, distance, Brum's body, task gestures, living-made light, silence, failed listening, and thresholds.
- Dialogue engine: Dabria seeks commitment; the lovers privacy; Cavren acknowledgment without management; Oruvane chosen company and freedom without asking plainly. Communication rearranges rooms, reveals travel, and enables departure or return.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Warm and spatially alert; move from Dabria's satisfied inventory into cluster gaps. Domestic friction punctures grandeur; tactical noticing fails when the house refuses formation.
- Conversational texture: Layer remarks, interruptions, couple-rhythm, mistimed humor, and talk aimed at occupied listeners. Uneven listening matters more than wit or subtext.
- Rhetorical ownership: Dabria owns plans and authority but misreads refusal. Cavren uses facts and repetition, listening after defense stops. Oshara owns boundaries and dry impatience; Torvik affection, play, and evasive jokes. Oruvane magnifies bids into quest rhetoric, then grows plain. The chorus teases and changes subjects.
- Pressure behavior: Dabria grows fluently managerial; Cavren shortens; Oshara stops cushioning; Torvik's jokes fail; Oruvane escalates spectacle, then silence. Brum and chorus reposition, not mediate.
- Relationship movement: Dabria wants chosen presence but cannot ask if they want less of her. The lovers cannot request privacy without seeming disloyal. Cavren wants the stair named, not tidied. Oruvane wants to be missed, not required, but hides the ask in play. Exchanges change privacy, trust, and freedom, not forgiveness.
- Anti-default: Resist a generic cozy tableau or articulate household meeting. Preserve play, pettiness, embarrassment, partial listening, separation within warmth, and the unresolved grievance; no roster, meal, or polished apology solves them.

## Beats

1. In the literal cover-defining gathering, huge deep-blue Oruvane lies gold-chained and tracery-painted among books and firelight. Four companions and three adult campaign intimates cluster to read, court, stitch, talk, and hand-make lights. Brum crosses between dragon and Cavren; Cavren refuses Dabria's help with travel straps, making old distance visible. Dabria interrupts the lovers and converts Oruvane's evident boredom into scheduled recreation.
2. For six days Oruvane turns chairs, ropes, cards, bells, and hinges into domestic quests. Some housemates play and others retreat; Dabria adds rules. Genuine fun curdles when everyone treats him as entertainment.
3. On the seventh night, removed doors form a library maze around Oruvane. The chorus protects books and frees outer doors while the five enter. Brum exposes Cavren's packed chest and stays.
4. Moving the lovers' door makes Dabria hear how battlefield vigilance became trespass. When a brace shifts, she commands Cavren; he refuses and names her unfulfilled relief promise on the final stair. Preserve his east-road plan and her failed defense. She does not barter acknowledgment for forgiveness.
5. Past the maze, Oruvane has travel bags but no saddle or harness. His quest tested whether absence matters. Dabria asks rather than assigns; he leaves alone without route, date, command, or obligation. Cavren may still leave, and the grievance stays open.
6. Three evenings later, an optional gathering forms without a roster. The broad household occupies shifting clusters; Cavren's chest remains. Oruvane returns by choice without ceremony. Brum makes room beside him, and Dabria offers attention without counting heads or closing every distance.
