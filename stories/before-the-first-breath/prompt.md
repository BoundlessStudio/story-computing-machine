# Original request

The first humans awoke together on a small island surrounded by a perfectly still lake. Beyond its shores stretched an untouched paradise of green valleys, towering mountains, and magnificent sunsets. Yet something about the world was wrong. Paths already wound through the hills, ancient stone monuments stood along the ridges, and the remains of enormous structures could be seen beneath the lake's clear waters. The first people had no memories of their creation, but the world around them bore unmistakable evidence of a civilization that had existed long before humanity's first breath.
