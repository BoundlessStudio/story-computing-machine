# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Fugitive clairvoyant Philomena has seen that former performance partner Dalen is the only reachable person who could leave her free after dawn: his puppet cart passes the ward gate unsearched. In his shuttered booth she tries money, shared history, predictive leverage, and an unpreviewed request. He refuses. The failure and consequence are genuine, never a disguised rescue.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Philomena fled the odds house holding her exploitative service bond after four years of forecasting. She wants escape and proof that Dalen's old affection survived; her flaw is rehearsing people into roles before they choose.
- Counterforce or complication: Dalen protects a precarious livelihood and young daughter. Years ago Philomena followed a favorable branch out of a failed confidence game, never seeing his arrest. Helping now risks the life he rebuilt. His refusal has value beyond obstructing her.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close first-person past through Philomena. Previews feel lived, but returns to the booth stay legible. She perceives only her future senses—not probability, private thought, offstage costs, or events beyond her death or horizon. Evasions and mismatched recollections reveal the old betrayal.
- Governing movement and time shape: One predawn hour, recursively interrupted by three futures reaching days or weeks ahead. Each return subtracts a tactic and some of Dalen's willingness to listen. The refusal stays private. Philomena finally exits through the public entrance to draw approaching searchers away from the booth.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Before asking a particular person for one concrete act, Philomena may hold a breath and experience up to four structurally different answer-branches, through her own senses, up to ninety days. They are possibilities, not ranked probabilities. Rephrasing returns the same broad branches. After three previews in an hour, details bleed together and vertigo makes further use unreliable. The listener can answer differently, lie, delay, or refuse; the faculty neither compels nor reads minds and establishes no universal prophecy rule.
- Dialogue promise: Former partners retain damaged comic timing while disputing whether remembered closeness creates present obligation. Rehearsed seduction gives way to awkward asking; refusal remains refusal, not reconciliation.
- Dialogic medium: Speech braided with Philomena's internal previews of unsaid conversations, Dalen's puppet repair, one domestic interruption, and silences where a predicted answer fails to arrive.
- Dialogue engine: Philomena needs voluntary help. Each preview informs her only about herself, yet makes Dalen feel managed. His answers remove leverage, expose unseen costs, and force a request without clairvoyant rehearsal.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Lunge into sensate futures, then return to neglected booth details—paste, torn paper, cold tea, a child's blanket. Fray Philomena's confidence through sequence mistakes and correction, not prophetic haze.
- Conversational texture: Old-act shorthand, petty corrections, unfinished questions, practical interruptions, stale jokes, and handwork; socially awkward, never a clean ethical debate.
- Rhetorical ownership: Philomena owns hypotheticals, patter, anticipatory humor, and outcome-based evasions; affection appears as overprecise memory. Dalen owns present costs, dry pettiness, and the booth's social authority. He listens until she presumes consent, then turns spare.
- Pressure behavior: Philomena grows too fluent, repeats details from unlived futures, then loses words without sight. Dalen repairs props to pace himself; fear shortens his speech, while his daughter's needs interrupt anger.
- Relationship movement: Philomena wants escape and complicity but cannot comfortably request danger to Dalen's child. Dalen wants his unseen life acknowledged but will not barter help for remorse. Her leverage falls; his boundary hardens; no alliance or trust returns.
- Anti-default: Avoid serene oracle, expert foresight debate, persuasive conversion, or polished reconciliation. Keep shabby theatrical humor, embarrassment, misremembering, and the fact that optimized language cannot manufacture consent.

## Beats

1. Open in the attractive preview: Philomena rides hidden beneath puppet scenery and passes the gate. A sensory break returns her to before her knock, establishing rescue and limited sight.
2. Dalen admits her only to prevent noise. The booth and sleeping daughter reveal the life she omitted. Her practiced warmth feels like stage business; he asks what followed her.
3. Philomena packages escape as paid transport. Dalen refuses the money and detects preview-derived knowledge. An old comic reflex briefly survives without restoring closeness.
4. A shared-history appeal previews Philomena safe weeks later, with Dalen's cost invisible. In actuality he raises the night she abandoned him; their memories differ because she recalls only her branch.
5. His daughter wakes needing something mundane. Dalen's attention leaves the negotiation. Philomena almost treats the interruption as leverage, then recognizes she is assigning the child a role.
6. Searchers approach. Philomena discards a forecasted appeal that exploits Dalen's affection because she cannot answer what happens to him after her safe arrival.
7. Without previewing, she asks plainly. Dalen refuses without betraying her or joining her; no alternate offer preserves her rescue.
8. Philomena exits through the front, drawing pursuit away from the booth. Remain in the unrescued present as the safe branch leaves reach; stop with failure enacted, not reinterpreted as victory.
