# Proposed canon delta

user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

## New locations

- **The unnamed city of eleven foundations (proposal):** A colorful local city
  of unspecified name, era, and wider-world location occupies an
  amphitheater-shaped basin beside a river. Eleven wedge-shaped districts step
  down toward a low river edge. Their adjacency does not imply a common origin
  or literal descent from any real-world architectural history.
- **Eleven districts and foundations (proposal):** Each district has a distinct
  palette, structural language, foundation behavior, and infrastructural role:
  Gothic basalt, vaults, buttresses, and through-flow cisterns buffer one
  overflow branch; Chinese-derived timber frames, mortise-and-tenon joints,
  dougong bracket sets, raised stone plinths, screens, glazed-tile roofs, and
  stepped courtyards tolerate settlement and convey the other branch;
  Egyptian battered sandstone walls and high stepped basins bear lateral load
  and settle grit; Greek limestone colonnades, stoas, graded rills, and
  removable stylobate plugs distribute flow; steampunk copper boiler courts
  and reciprocating pumps supply the route's only lift; cyberpunk sealed
  composite foundations and passive cyan-and-magenta pressure strips exclude
  water and display pressure; biopunk roots, grown cellulose, reeds, fungi, and
  permeable membranes stabilize and filter; dieselpunk steel sheds, elevated
  compression engine, flywheel, and river works supply torque and the normal
  outfall; raypunk freight tubes and tall pressure-balancing shafts vent
  trapped air; solarpunk planted terraces and linked receiving cisterns delay
  runoff and store filtered water; and a Brutalist concrete center with deep
  caissons, shear walls, and a floodable undercroft provides low emergency
  volume. “Eleven foundations” is local to this city and carries no proposed
  cosmological or universal significance.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Shared drainage and isolation system (proposal):** Eleven radial drains
  enter one buried foundation service ring and normally discharge through a
  single low outfall in the dieselpunk river works. At district boundaries,
  high-water floats actuate linkages that drop sector bulkheads closed; the
  bulkheads must lift to reopen. The fixed mechanism protects against ordinary
  local backflow or contamination but can divide a citywide overload into
  unequal pressure compartments.
- **Local storm failure (proposal):** A cloudburst over the basin and its short
  upstream catchment drives branches, roof grit, reeds, and silt against the
  exterior outfall screen while river pressure closes the check gate. Continued
  runoff, automatic bulkhead closure, trapped air, district-specific resistance
  to water, and transferred loads produce hydraulic hammer, foundation scour,
  uneven settlement, and imminent rupture of the ring crown. This is a local
  mechanical and hydrological failure, not sabotage, magic, or a global event.
- **Corrected emergency topology (proposal):** Anonymous inhabitants lift the
  bulkheads, open the central flood doors and lower raypunk shutters, remove
  distribution plugs and grilles, expose a pre-existing diesel/steampunk
  coupling, and open surface spill routes. The Brutalist undercroft accepts the
  first unequal surge and is the only pump intake; raypunk shafts vent trapped
  air; the dry diesel engine is disengaged from the blocked discharge pump and
  mechanically drives the steampunk reciprocating pumps. Those pumps make the
  route's sole lift from the undercroft to the high Egyptian settling basins.
  Every later stage is gravity-fed: Egyptian settling descends to Greek
  distribution, which divides into a Gothic branch and a Chinese-derived
  branch. The Gothic branch buffers water in deep vaulted cisterns until it
  reaches marked working-level throats in their downhill walls, then exits
  through falling masonry channels. In parallel, plinth channels and stepped
  courtyards carry the Chinese-derived branch. Both branches reunite above and
  pass through biopunk reed and fungal filters, after which the filtered water
  descends directly into solarpunk receiving cisterns below the filter outlet
  but above the river works. Cyberpunk pressure strips passively display the
  resulting equalization. No post-filter water returns to a pump and no second
  lift exists.
- **System and personhood boundaries (proposal):** The city, districts,
  service ring, gates, pumps, pressure strips, vents, and other mechanical
  infrastructure are nonliving, non-sentient, non-magical systems with fixed
  physical responses. Biopunk roots, reeds, fungi, and grown cellulose are
  alive but non-sentient and non-magical; they swell, anchor, flex, filter,
  grow, fail, or require replacement through ordinary biological processes.
  Architectural agency in the prose is metaphorical.
- **Costs and post-crisis seams (proposal):** Survival leaves the undercroft
  mud-striped, settling basins and filters silted, Gothic basalt tide-marked,
  Greek paving and a column base displaced, timber screens lost, and a plinth
  undermined; the blocked outfall still requires later clearing. Repairs retain
  visible bronze position marks, liftable cistern grilles, rehung spill panels
  over new channels, an accessible diesel/steampunk coupling, external
  raypunk-shutter releases, cross-border spillways, and low equalization slots.
  Sector bulkheads rest raised over those slots until high-water floats actuate
  and drop them closed.

  bulkhead`, `equalization slot`, and `receiving cistern` remain lowercase
  functional descriptions rather than proper names.
