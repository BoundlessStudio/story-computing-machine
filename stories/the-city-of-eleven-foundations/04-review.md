#### Continuity

**Finding P1-CON-01**

- **Severity:** Minor
- **Location:** `03-draft.md`, service-ring setup and closure/opening sequence
  (lines 29, 43, and 115)
- **Evidence:** The setup says “a float could lift a linked bulkhead and
  isolate one sector,” but the inciting failure says a rising float's linkage
  “dropped a sector bulkhead” to close it. The rescue then opens the route when
  “the first sector bulkhead began to lift.”
- **Why it matters:** “Lift” is assigned to both the isolating action and the
  opening action, contradicting the gate's physical state at the story's
  central setup. The later scenes consistently establish that lowered gates
  isolate and raised gates equalize, so this is localized but conspicuous.
- **Smallest effective fix:** In the setup, replace “could lift a linked
  bulkhead” with wording that says the float actuates or drops the bulkhead.
  Keep the later close-down and lift-open sequence unchanged.

#### Causality and character

**Finding P1-CAUS-01**

- **Severity:** Major
- **Location:** `03-draft.md`, climax hydraulic route (lines 127–145)
- **Evidence:** The reciprocating lift pumps explicitly draw mud-heavy water
  from the brutalist undercroft, and their “first discharge” reaches the open
  Egyptian basins. The flow then loses pressure while descending through
  settling steps, open Greek grades, Gothic cisterns, timber courtyards, and
  biopunk filters. After emerging from those filters, however, it enters
  “upper mains and climbed on the repeated stroke of the coupled pumps” into
  the uphill solarpunk cisterns. No second-stage intake, return connection, or
  retained pressure path links the post-filter water back to those pumps.
- **Why it matters:** The climax depends on all eleven districts forming one
  complete hydraulic sequence. Once the first pump discharge enters open
  settling basins and gravity channels, its pressure head is spent; the already
  established pump intake at the undercroft cannot also lift the downstream
  filtered flow without another shown connection or stage. The water therefore
  cannot causally reach the final storage that proves the city has survived,
  so the resolution is presently incomplete rather than merely
  under-explained.
- **Smallest effective fix:** Seed and show a second lift stage or intake after
  the biopunk filters, driven by the same diesel-to-steampunk transmission, and
  distinguish it from the bank drawing out of the undercroft. Alternatively,
  revise the elevations so the filtered water can gravity-feed the final
  cisterns and remove the claim that it climbs on the pumps. Use one coherent
  topology and establish it before the payoff.
