# Story plan

## Premise and promise

**One-sentence premise:** When a cloudburst blocks the only low outfall beneath
an unnamed city of eleven architecturally incompatible districts, the sealed
foundation compartments that were meant to protect each style begin to destroy
one another, and the city can survive only by accepting a controlled flood
through its most distinctive spaces.

**Story promise:** A colorful architectural suspense story in which load,
water, air, heat, silt, and material flexibility carry the action. Every
required design language supplies a legible structural or infrastructural
behavior, and the climax makes those behaviors operate as one temporary
hydraulic sequence without blending the districts into a generic hybrid.

**Central dramatic question:** Can a city built to keep eleven foundations
separate become permeable before trapped water turns their differences into a
chain of structural failures?

**Meaningful change:** The city begins as a collection of proud, self-contained
districts whose protective gates make every boundary a refusal. It ends with
those boundaries retained but crossed by visible spillways, couplings, and
repair seams: difference survives because it can share pressure.

## Story controls

- **Working title:** *The City of Eleven Foundations*
- **POV and tense:** Third-person omniscient, past tense, with city-scale
  architectural focalization. The narration may metaphorically describe what
  walls “refused,” roofs “offered,” or the city “wanted,” but it must never
  imply literal awareness, speech, intention, or magical agency.
- **Tone and genre:** Lyrical speculative architecture with civic and
  hydrological suspense. Wonder should come from precise material behavior,
  not unexplained enchantment.
- **Target length:** 3,600 words; acceptable range 3,000–3,800.
- **Audience/content boundary:** General audience / PG. Flooding, evacuation,
  cracking masonry, structural peril, and recoverable property damage; no
  graphic injury, visible death, or human panic as spectacle.
- **Setting scope:** A new, unnamed city in an unspecified place and era of the
  persistent shared world. Do not call its design languages by literal
  in-world historical origins. “Gothic,” “Chinese-derived,” “Egyptian,”
  “Greek,” and the various “punk” labels are reader-facing planning terms.
- **Architectural protagonist:** The city's linked built fabric, treated
  metaphorically rather than as a person. Its concrete dramatic “desire” is to
  remain habitable without surrendering the eleven distinct ways it carries
  weight, weather, water, and power.
- **Human-presence rule:** No named humanoids and no individual human
  viewpoint. Inhabitants appear only through traces: worn thresholds, warm
  windows, maintenance marks, evacuation shutters and empty transit, tools and
  pins left where anonymous hands used them, then reopened doors, footprints,
  and renewed occupancy after the crisis. No dialogue.
- **Physical-crisis rule:** A violent local cloudburst sends a silt-heavy river
  surge against the city's single low outfall. A debris mat jams the screen
  just as the river check gate closes against backflow. With rain still entering
  the buried foundation service ring, high-water floats actuate and drop sector
  bulkheads closed, isolating eleven compartments and turning uneven water
  levels into destructive pressure. The bulkheads must lift to open. No magic,
  sabotage, prophecy, sentient system, or new information causes or solves the
  crisis.
- **Ending shape:** Costly survival, not restoration to the opening state. The
  low floors and sacrificial basins remain water-marked, some finish materials
  are lost, and the outfall requires later repair; the city preserves its
  distinct districts by making their controlled interdependence permanent and
  visible.

## Character engine

### Metaphorical protagonist: the city's linked built fabric

- **Status:** Nonliving, non-sentient, and unnamed. “Protagonist” describes the
  story's focal and causal center, not a constructed person.
- **Concrete desire:** Remain standing and inhabitable while preserving eleven
  legibly different architectural systems.
- **Why delay costs:** Water continues entering the buried ring throughout the
  cloudburst. Each closed compartment raises a different head of water; after
  the first foundation seam lifts, every additional pulse transfers load to a
  neighboring district and shortens the time before ring rupture and cascading
  settlement.
- **External stakes:** Loss of the low districts, collapse at three vulnerable
  boundaries, contamination or loss of the receiving cisterns, and eventual
  uninhabitability even in districts the closed bulkheads appear to protect.
- **Opposition:** The blocked outfall, rising water and trapped air, abrasive
  silt, incompatible material responses, and the city's own isolation
  bulkheads. Weather supplies force; the opening design supplies the dangerous
  pathway.
- **Blind spot / internal tension:** Each foundation was optimized to survive
  alone and to exclude a neighbor's fire, vibration, runoff, and pressure. The
  same defensive logic makes a citywide overload impossible to distribute.
- **Strength:** The eleven districts possess complementary capacities—mass,
  flexibility, ventilation, sensing, filtration, mechanical power, lift,
  distribution, and storage—that no single one contains.
- **Vulnerability:** Those capacities terminate at closed borders. Interfaces,
  rather than monumental centers, are the weakest and most neglected parts of
  the city.
- **Meaningful choice:** At the crisis, the built situation presents two
  options: preserve dry district interiors behind closed sector bulkheads until
  the common ring bursts, or open every boundary and deliberately surrender
  sacrificial courts, undercrofts, screens, and finish surfaces to a controlled
  flood. Anonymous operators choose the second course. Show that collective
  decision only as architectural evidence—withdrawn pins, raised gates,
  opened shutters, engaged coupling teeth—not as a human scene. The story may
  call this “the city's choice” only as clear metaphor.
- **Internal movement:** Colorful separation → reflexive self-protection →
  mutually amplified failure → costly permeability → articulated
  interdependence.

### Human and living presence

- There is **no named cast** and no stable unnamed person who becomes a
  protagonist by accumulation.
- Anonymous inhabitants are first legible as scale and use: worn stair centers,
  steam on glass, laundry removed ahead of rain, hand-polished rails, a cart
  left under shelter.
- Evacuation is expressed by descending window light, open fire doors, stopped
  lifts, empty platforms, chalk water marks, and dry footprints giving way to
  wet ones. Avoid fear-centered crowd description.
- Maintenance and the climactic decision are expressed through altered
  objects. A wrench, a severed seal, stacked gate pins, a new shaft coupling,
  and propped-open spill panels may imply coordinated work, but no worker
  receives dialogue, an epithet, or repeated individualized traits.
- The biopunk district's roots, reeds, fungi, and grown cellulose are living
  infrastructure, not aware characters. They respond through defined
  biological processes only.

## Causal arc

### Beginning state

After a long dry interval, the city's colors are brightest at its borders and
its water infrastructure is least tested. Eleven wedge-shaped districts step
down an amphitheater basin toward a river. The buried service ring and
brutalist undercroft occupy the low collecting level; the diesel and steampunk
works reach the lowest river edge. The emergency surface route begins only
after a mechanical lift to the Egyptian high shoulder, then descends through
the Greek distribution level. Its Gothic branch first buffers water in deep
cisterns, then spills through working-level throats into downhill masonry
channels; its Chinese-derived branch descends through courtyards. The branches
reunite at the lower biopunk filter level before entering solarpunk receiving
cisterns on the next lower terrace. Those cisterns remain above the river-edge
works. Each district rests on its own foundation logic, but all eleven radial
drains meet the service ring and normally empty through the diesel district's
low outfall. High-water floats actuate bulkheads downward to seal each sector
from its neighbors. They are presented as prudent protective architecture.

Traces of ordinary use establish inhabitation without introducing cast. The
solarpunk receiving-cistern gauges show unusually low levels below the dry
biopunk filter outlet; dried silt stripes in the high Egyptian settling stairs
show their intended hydraulic use; a painted-over mechanical coupling bridges
the diesel/steampunk boundary; raypunk pressure shafts exhale faintly when
pneumatic freight passes; Greek rills disappear under removable stylobate
plugs; Gothic cistern grilles, working-level overflow marks, and downhill outlet
channels are clear but dry; Chinese-derived plinth channels step toward the
same biopunk beds. These details and their visible downhill order seed the only
available resolution.

### Inciting disruption

A cloudburst strikes both the city and the short upstream catchment. The river
rises faster than the storm drains can discharge. At the diesel outfall, a
mat of branches, roof grit, and river silt jams the exterior screen; river
pressure closes the mechanical check gate exactly as designed. The diesel
discharge pump begins cavitating against the blocked mouth. Rainwater continues
to enter the common foundation ring from eleven radial drains.

The first high-water float actuates and drops a sector bulkhead closed. Its
closure prevents backflow into one district but throws the next pulse against
the adjacent compartment. Other high-water floats actuate and drop their
bulkheads closed in sequence. The buried ring ceases to act as a shared
equalizing volume and becomes eleven unequal pressure vessels.

### Escalating turns

1. **Defense becomes transfer.** The waterproof cyberpunk foundation admits
   almost no water, so its trapped compartment sends the rising head through
   narrow utility penetrations toward the biopunk boundary. Cyan and magenta
   pressure strips climb like a diagram made visible. Saturated living root
   rafts swell into the constricted throat, slowing flow and magnifying the
   pressure behind them.
2. **Mass protects one edge by endangering another.** Water seeks the next weak
   joint and pushes against the Egyptian district's battered stone base. The
   massive walls barely shift, but their resistance redirects lateral force
   under the neighboring Greek stylobate. Fine white dust falls; one line of
   slender columns loses true alignment. The first visible architectural wound
   proves that dry buildings can be endangered by water they have excluded.
3. **The cascade reaches unlike structures.** A pressure pulse crosses the
   Greek compartment and scours gravel from beneath Gothic buttress feet.
   Colored panes distort as one wall settles outward. Across the next boundary,
   the Chinese-derived timber halls survive the same small movement through
   mortise-and-tenon give and dougong load distribution, but water begins
   washing fines from below their raised stone plinths. Flexibility buys time;
   it does not remove the water.
4. **Local fixes cannot answer a shared load.** The steampunk lift pumps stand
   ready but their low boiler court floods and quenches their heat. The diesel
   engine still turns above the waterline, yet its connected discharge pump
   can only grind against the blocked outfall. Raypunk shafts remain shut at
   their lower freight shutters, trapping pockets of compressed air; each
   pressure pulse now strikes the ring like a hammer. Meanwhile the brutalist
   undercroft and the dry, downstream solarpunk receiving cisterns remain
   unused capacity behind closed borders.
5. **Evacuation empties the architecture without solving it.** Light withdraws
   uphill, transit halts, fire doors stand open, and movable contents vanish
   from low rooms. The city can be made safer for people, but continued
   compartmentalization will still break the place to which they would return.
   Passive pressure bands mark three simultaneous danger points: the Gothic
   buttress line, the Greek colonnade, and the ring crown beneath the central
   court.

### Crisis

The storm reaches its hardest interval. The ring crown begins lifting the
central brutalist pavement; if it ruptures, uncontrolled scour will attack all
eleven foundations from beneath. Keeping the bulkheads shut preserves dry
interiors for a few more minutes but guarantees cascading settlement. Opening
them will immediately flood the brutalist undercroft and deliberately route
muddy water through prominent courts, galleries, cisterns, planted beds, and
finish surfaces.

The meaningful choice appears as a change in material state. Warning seals
break. Locking pins are withdrawn and stacked above the waterline. Lower
raypunk tube shutters open. Stylobate plugs, courtyard spill panels, and
cistern grilles are removed. No operator is centered; the architecture records
that anonymous inhabitants have chosen a damaged, connected city over eleven
pristine failures.

### Climax

The bulkheads rise open in a physically necessary order:

1. The brutalist undercroft opens first and takes the initial unequal surge.
   Its deep caissons and shear walls hold while the extra volume lowers the
   pressure at the ring crown.
2. Raypunk pressure shafts vent the compressed air released from adjoining
   compartments. Glass blocks fog from within and then clear as the hydraulic
   hammer diminishes.
3. At the diesel/steampunk boundary, the painted-over coupling is exposed.
   The diesel engine is disengaged from the blocked discharge pump; its
   flywheel drives the steampunk reciprocating lift pumps, whose low intake
   draws directly from the brutalist undercroft. This is a mechanical transfer
   of torque, not a fusion of systems or an autonomous decision.
4. In the route's only lift stage, the pumps raise the mud-heavy undercroft
   water to the Egyptian district's high stepped basins. Battered walls take
   the new lateral load; successive terraces slow the current and let abrasive
   grit settle. The pump discharge ends at this high settling head.
5. Clarified overflow begins a continuous gravity descent into the Greek
   district immediately below. Removed stylobate plugs expose the stoas' slight
   grades, dividing flow into two branches rather than letting it attack the
   already shifted column line.
6. One branch descends through the Gothic district's gargoyle channels into
   deep vaulted cisterns, where the first volume is stored long enough to blunt
   each pump-fed surge and relieve the scoured buttress feet. As the cistern
   reaches its marked working level, water enters the pre-seeded low overflow
   throat in the downhill wall and leaves through a falling masonry channel
   whose outlet remains above the biopunk beds. The other branch crosses the
   Chinese-derived district in stepped courtyards and plinth channels;
   sacrificial screens release, while primary timber columns and bracket sets
   accommodate the remaining settlement.
7. Still descending, the Gothic channel outflow and the Chinese-derived
   courtyard branch visibly reunite in the biopunk district's reed galleries
   and fungal beds. Roots hold eroding soil and living filters catch the fine
   silt that would clog the receiving lines. The filter outlet lip is visibly
   higher than the solarpunk terrace below it.
8. The brutalist undercroft buffers each pump-intake stroke, cyberpunk pressure
   strips display the falling head in every compartment, and the raypunk shafts
   keep the moving water vented. Filtered flow crosses the biopunk outlet lip
   and descends by gravity—without returning to any pump—into the dry
   solarpunk receiving-cistern chain. Those cisterns lie below the filters but
   above the low diesel/steampunk river edge, and their planted terraces
   continue delaying new rain.

The climax succeeds because opening the borders makes all eleven capacities
available in sequence. It uses one lift from the undercroft to the Egyptian
head and gravity for every stage after that. No single district saves the rest,
and no new mechanism appears after the crisis choice.

### Resonant aftermath

The rain eases. Water levels meet across the opened ring instead of battering
its divisions. The river eventually falls below the check gate, exposing the
blocked outfall for ordinary clearing; do not make that later cleanup the
climax.

The city bears damage: a Greek column base is reset, Gothic lower courses keep
a dark tide line, several courtyard screens are gone, reed beds are choked
with caught silt, and the brutalist undercroft smells of river mud. Repairs do
not hide what happened. New spill channels cross the colored border bands;
the diesel-steam coupling remains accessible; and the bulkheads, lifted open
during the emergency, return to a raised/open resting position with low
equalization slots that remain clear until high-water floats actuate and drop
the bulkheads closed.

Return is shown through traces: shutters reopen by slope, ventilation resumes,
footprints cross still-damp courts, and warm window light occupies the colors
again. End at one border where runoff moves through a new narrow joint from
one material language into another without making either indistinct. The city
has not learned or awakened; its rebuilt form embodies a human choice that can
be repeated.

## Failure modes to watch

- **Architecture-as-scenery failure:** Every major paragraph must track force,
  space, material response, water route, ventilation, storage, or repair. Do
  not interrupt the causal line for a decorative district tour.
- **Inventory opening:** The first scene must move along shared runoff and the
  buried ring, pairing details with future hydraulic functions. Avoid eleven
  equal mini-essays or an aerial brochure.
- **Humanoid-protagonist drift:** No named people, dialogue, repeated individual
  gestures, private thoughts, leadership scene, heroic engineer, or rescue
  subplot. If one anonymous figure accumulates traits or decisions, redistribute
  the traces back into collective architectural evidence.
- **Literal city sentience:** Words such as “wanted,” “remembered,” “chose,” or
  “learned” may appear only as unmistakable architectural metaphor supported
  by physical states. The city does not speak, perceive, calculate, use magic,
  or operate its own gates.
- **Biological personhood drift:** Roots, fungi, reeds, and grown structural
  material swell, flex, filter, and anchor. They do not feel fear, volunteer,
  sacrifice themselves knowingly, communicate, or become a collective mind.
- **Autonomous-system solution:** Every bulkhead lifted open, pin removed, plug
  lifted, shutter released, pump coupled, or engine redirected must bear a
  trace of anonymous human manipulation. High-water floats may automatically
  actuate and drop bulkheads closed according to their seeded mechanical rule,
  but no network invents the rescue route.
- **Vague disaster mechanics:** Preserve the chain: cloudburst and river surge
  → jammed outfall plus closed check gate → continued inflow → sector bulkheads
  drop closed under high-water-float actuation → unequal pressure and trapped
  air → load transfers through specific seams → imminent ring rupture. Do not
  substitute “the storm grew worse” for causal escalation.
- **Single-district rescue:** The climax must remain sequential. The brutalist
  undercroft buys time and supplies the sole pump intake; raypunk shafts vent;
  diesel drives the steampunk lift to the high Egyptian head; Egyptian basins
  settle; Greek grades split the gravity flow; the Gothic cistern branch
  buffers, reaches its working-level throat, and flows downhill while the
  Chinese-derived branch descends separately; those outflows reunite in the
  biopunk filters and continue into the lower solarpunk receiving cisterns;
  cyberpunk strips verify. Omitting one weakens the title promise.
- **Unseeded solution:** Establish every capacity in scene 1 or during its
  failure before it becomes useful. In particular seed the low undercroft and
  pump intake, the high Egyptian silt stairs and pump discharge, the downhill
  Greek plugs, Gothic grilles, working-water mark, low overflow throat and
  falling outlet channel, Chinese-derived plinth channels, biopunk beds and
  outlet lip, solarpunk receiving cisterns below that lip, the diesel/steampunk
  coupling, raypunk lower shutters, and cyberpunk strips.
- **Broken hydraulic topology:** The pump bank draws only from the brutalist
  undercroft and discharges only at the Egyptian high head. After the open
  settling basins spend that pressure, every later movement is gravity-fed:
  Egyptian → Greek → (Gothic cistern buffer → working-level outlet channel) /
  Chinese-derived branch → biopunk reunion → solarpunk. Never let the Gothic
  branch terminate in storage and then appear unexplained at the filters.
  Never claim that post-filter water climbs, returns to the pumps, rides their
  repeated stroke, or reaches storage through an unseeded second lift.
- **Dead-end cistern:** Do not treat “cistern” as self-explanatory continuity.
  Show the Gothic branch fill to the marked working level, enter the low
  downhill-wall throat, pass through the falling masonry channel, and emerge
  above the biopunk beds before it reunites with the Chinese-derived branch.
  Preserve both functions: temporary buffering/footing relief and through-flow.
- **False engineering precision:** Keep the hydraulics internally coherent but
  avoid invented equations, exact pressure values, or claims that invite
  technical scrutiny the story cannot support. Use relative elevation,
  visible gauge states, flow delay, and material consequence.
- **Chinese-derived flattening:** Do not reduce the district to red paint,
  dragons, lanterns, or exotic atmosphere. Its plot function must follow from
  timber framing, mortise-and-tenon joinery, dougong bracket sets, raised stone
  plinths, courtyards, screens, roof drainage, and glazed tile.
- **Facade-only “punk” districts:** Steampunk, cyberpunk, biopunk, dieselpunk,
  raypunk, and solarpunk need structural and infrastructural roles, not only
  gears, neon, greenery, engines, fins, or solar panels as visual garnish.
- **Homogenizing resolution:** Interdependence does not mean blending all
  materials into one style. Preserve distinct colors, structures, repair
  techniques, and spaces; only the crossing seams and emergency route are
  shared.
- **Costless ending:** Retain flooded interiors, silted filters, lost screens,
  tide-stained stone, displaced column work, and a still-damaged outfall.
  Anonymous return should be possible because the choice cost the city
  something, not because the storm leaves no mark.
- **Lore inflation:** Do not date the era, name the city in-world, establish
  literal real-world architectural history, define a global technological
  sequence, or connect the city to Ravel, the Glass-Sea world, the modern-like
  era, the Glass Gate, a recurring faction, artifact, or character.
- **Name drift:** Keep all humans, animals, districts, machines, organisms, and
  structures unnamed. Avoid capitalized role labels or person-like epithets,
  especially any reserved form in `stories/NAMES.md`. Any new
  character-facing form requires a fresh registry check before drafting.
- **Weak ending image:** End on an inhabited physical seam—water, footprints,
  light, or repaired load moving between unlike materials—not a narrator's
  abstract declaration about unity and not a human speech explaining the
  theme.
