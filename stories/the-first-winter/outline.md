# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **The First Winter.** On the morning an eighteen-year-old decides to leave the autumn valley, Besset reveals the approaching murderer and the day she keeps taking away from him. Make protection visible, then defeat its cause before releasing the world.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The unnamed narrator loves Besset, wants unfamiliar weather and people, and resents being someone who can only be carried to safety. Besset still sees the infant she escaped with.
- Counterforce or complication: A solitary living blood-hunter bound his failing life to the newborn as his intended donor. He can follow that living person's direction; to steal their remaining years he must physically kill them and take their heart. He values his own survival and actively chooses murder. Age, disguise, and relocation cannot hide the donor. He remains physically mortal.
- POV, distance, and information limit: First-person past, close to the narrator's impatient, sheltered attention. No hunter interiority, distant world survey, or enacted mother flashback. Besset's account acquires corroboration through pursuit and attack.
- Governing movement and time shape: Two dawns enclosing one final day: witnessed reset, preparation, diverted pursuit, unrepeated dawn. Briefly extend into the first advancing winter. Target prose: 2,500–4,000 words.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Besset's one working returns the whole material world to one fixed dawn every twenty-four hours. She carries herself, the child, and worn or held belongings through it, so their bodies and memories accumulate; everything else, including the hunter's progress and discoveries, resets. Autumn's weather, fruit, and fallen leaves repeat. The starting dawn follows the mother's death. The blocked short pass forces the hunter onto an approach slightly longer than a day: each reset takes away the last distance he needs. Besset cannot rewind either protected person's injuries, aim the working at one enemy, or reach a date before its establishment. She must renew it at dawn; her death ends it. Killing the hunter and then renewing would restore him. Release must follow his confirmed death in the final interval.
- Dialogue promise: A necessary explanation changes a departure into a dangerous shared undertaking without settling the betrayal of eighteen years' concealment.
- Dialogic medium: Speech within ordinary hearing, interrupted by observed movement, recognition, and urgent calls across uneven ground.
- Dialogue engine: Answers determine where the narrator dares stand; contested plans change who faces the hunter. The hunter's demands reveal what he needs physically and what he will risk to obtain it.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Familiar paths and autumn details receive impatient compression. Unprecedented movements draw slower attention. Let bodily fear interrupt interpretation; avoid explaining an image after it lands.
- Conversational texture: A practiced household shorthand becomes inadequate. Allow blunt questions, partial answers, mistaken timing, and ordinary requests. The hunter speaks to obtain access, distract, or frighten; he delivers no cosmological lecture.
- Rhetorical ownership: The narrator reasons from routes walked, sometimes mistakes defiance for evidence, and owns brittle humor. Besset evades responsibility through practical instructions; affection appears in involuntary attention. The hunter uses proprietary certainty and listens for weakness. No shared stock of polished metaphors.
- Pressure behavior: Besset loses explanatory fluency when the narrator steps toward danger. The narrator repeats an unanswered question before acting. During combat, speech contracts to necessary information and failed contact.
- Relationship movement: The narrator wants usable truth and shared risk; Besset wants obedience but cannot comfortably ask to keep raising a child forever. Acting together transfers responsibility without instant forgiveness. The hunter wants surrender and cannot accept his intended victim's acquired will and competence.
- Anti-default: Do not resolve danger through reassurance, a semantic bargain, or the discovery that the pursuer meant well. Practical cooperation can coexist with durable anger.

## Beats

1. **Departure interrupted.** Before dawn, the narrator takes the downhill path. Besset catches up in windblown blue layers. From a sheltered overlook, expose a man approaching below. When the narrator moves into view, his attack makes Besset's warning immediate. Retreat behind rock; she does not allow an avoidable wound as instruction.
2. **The lost approach.** Dawn restores the disturbed path, interrupted birdsong, and gold leaves; the hunter disappears back to the beginning of his route. The narrator's displaced belongings and bodily fright remain. Besset explains the world-wide extent, then the heart theft. Establish why the broken short pass leaves him unable to reach their concealed refuge before renewal.
3. **What the mother bought.** In a short necessary account, the mother held that pass while Besset carried the newborn clear and completed the working; its collapse forced the long pursuit route. The mother died before the fixed dawn. Her resistance supplied the indispensable minutes. Besset's own lasting injury from an earlier attempt to confront the hunter supports the limit of her protection. She has no combat spell, and dying while attempting another attack would surrender the child.
4. **A second participant.** The narrator still intends to leave. Ordinary hunting and valley knowledge now supply a capable partner. They plan an interception late in the interval, while renewal remains available if they retreat alive. Establish their physical advantage and retreat route before use; prepare a simple ambush.
5. **The hunter answers.** Intercept him before the final ascent. His recognition of the adult donor corroborates the blood connection; his attempt to secure the narrator for killing substantiates the exact danger. Moving prey changes his choices, so the expected route fails them. Give him an intelligent counteraction. Besset and narrator must each expose themselves and physically overcome him; neither a new power nor an automatic reset wins. Keep violence consequential and non-graphic. Confirm death before dawn.
6. **Time allowed through.** Besset withholds renewal beside the dead pursuer. His body remains where it fell; their injuries remain. No hunting organization inherits the threat. Allow the narrator's actual departure as weather changes toward winter. The mother remains dead; affection does not cancel secrecy. End within the changed life, without a forgiveness speech or maxim.
