# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: World-traveling hero Tarmel reaches Varrowick as a landslide reservoir rises above its river capital, Lowharbor. He reads outbound carts as panic and offers to blast the slide. The kingdom instead plans to open old spillways, lose its lower capital, and spare inhabited terraces and downstream settlements. Resolve its refusal through action: Tarmel must choose between imposing his familiar spectacular rescue and helping residents execute a costly survival they chose. His expertise frees a failed spillway and trapped crew, but Lowharbor floods as planned without uncomplicated gratitude.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Tarmel measures himself by arriving where ordinary capacity fails; refusal feels like abandonment. He wants no preventable death, yet wrongly treats buildings, government, and population as one object called a kingdom. Residents disagree over acceptable loss, and the crown cannot consent for every household.
- Counterforce or complication: The slide will overtop after dark. Queen Nadrys’s plan depends on evacuation counts, three aging spill shutters, and the eastern terrace staying dry. Some soldiers want Tarmel to defend the walls; displaced residents resent their sacrifice; workers want both groups out of the way. A jammed shutter traps two channel workers and threatens the terrace. Channel-master Sarto knows the buried works; Tarmel recognizes structural failure under unfamiliar load. Neither has a complete answer.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Tarmel, attentive to routes, loads, bodies, and precedents. Reveal local priorities through what people carry, abandon, dispute, and request. He cannot infer whether any refusal is courage, coercion, pride, or fear without listening; his fame cannot authenticate his geology.
- Governing movement and time shape: One rainy afternoon into early night, moving countercurrent. Tarmel descends while residents climb, works below street level as water arrives, then returns uphill after the lower city is beyond saving. Render the checkpoint, Nadrys’s mobile inspection, the walk with Sarto, and the spillhouse failure. Keep the climax with a small crew, not a council or crowd. End amid camp accounting and unresolved property anger.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Use no operative magic. A rockslide dam will eventually breach uncontrolled. Three manual spill channels can release an earlier, smaller surge through Lowharbor’s sacrificial basin; holding water against the wall or blasting the slide redirects uncertain force toward occupied terraces. A timber shutter is warped in its guide. Freeing it requires Sarto’s local map plus Tarmel’s wedges, rope practice, and recognition of vibration and seepage. These are local physical facts.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Follow Tarmel’s rescue attention through weight, exits, hands, water sounds, and tasks before explanation. Shorten his confident analogies as Varrowick’s choices contradict them.
- Conversational texture: Mix route checks, cart disputes, instructions, interrupted objections, and brief negotiation. Most people need one immediate thing; no local chorus educates the hero.
- Rhetorical ownership: Tarmel reasons by precedent and decisive intervention. Nadrys names jurisdiction, populations, and losses she may authorize. Sarto tests gradients, mechanisms, and crew positions. Residents argue from particular property and family stakes.
- Pressure behavior: Tarmel grows terse and stops hearing qualifications. Nadrys drops ceremony for bounded decisions. Sarto becomes exact until danger forces gestures and single-step orders; bystanders repeat their nearest concrete loss.
- Anti-default: Avoid a smug anti-savior parable with uniformly enlightened locals, a foolish hero, and a polished lesson in the final exchange.

## Beats

1. Tarmel approaches Lowharbor against carts and stripped doorframes. Checkpoint guards decline his battle plan, clear his horse from the uphill lane, and ask him to move a stalled medicine wagon. Practical refusal unsettles him more than hostility.
2. Nadrys inspects evacuation markers outside a granary. She rejects blasting the slide and defending the lower wall by naming who each option endangers. Officers and residents press incompatible alternatives; urgency grants Tarmel no authority.
3. Nadrys lets him observe under Sarto. As they descend, his imported solutions meet different terrain. Work talk and passing encounters show support, resentment, opportunism, and people concerned only with moving family or tools.
4. The central shutter remains closed and two workers are cut off below. Tarmel’s quick cut would undermine the terrace wall. Sarto refuses it and rigs a slower approach through a maintenance slot.
5. Tarmel recognizes altered vibration and muddy seepage from another failure. He offers the observation, not a verdict. Sarto checks it against the buried channel and changes the sequence; his wedges and rope work serve joints she identifies.
6. Rising water makes the shutter machinery unrecoverable. Tarmel holds the rig while the workers cross and lets Sarto call the irreversible release. The surge takes the lower streets and palace basin; the occupied terrace holds.
7. Camp survival does not settle blame. Residents dispute counts, compensation, and surrendering the capital; Nadrys offers no medal or verdict. Tarmel accepts a bounded task in the unfinished accounting rather than owning the outcome.
