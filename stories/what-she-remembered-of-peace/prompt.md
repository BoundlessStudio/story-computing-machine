# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] You've taken in an android that was originally built for war to prevent her from being scrapped. You rehabilitated her, gave her a home, and taught her how to integrate in peacetime society. Now with a new war at your doorstep and the world collapsing around you, she draws on old programming.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
