# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **What She Remembered of Peace**. Years after Olyne Orret prevented adult war-built android Anvek's scrapping and supported independent rehabilitation and integration, shelling traps their block above a shelter. Anvek deliberately reopens retained combat functions. Resolve one evacuation and defense; peace supplies knowledge and attachments, not weakness.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Olyne wants eleven residents out before fire reaches the batteries but again mistakes protection for authority over Anvek. Anvek shares the goal while choosing her own objective, risk, and limits. Olyne knows residents and routines; Anvek knows the cargo cradle, threat geometry, and her body.
- Counterforce or complication: The stair and networks fail. Only a rooftop produce cradle can lower residents two terraces, and its manual brake strands an operator above. A reconnaissance drone marks movement for follow-up fire while smoke, a fraying cable, unequal mobility, and partial responder reports close the window. Disabling it cannot change the war.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Olyne, restricted to sightlines, familiar tells, and protective bias. She cannot read Anvek's processes, see through cover, or know beyond two terraces. Anvek's choices remain legible through action, bounded reports, and direct consent.
- Governing movement and time shape: Forty continuous minutes moving upward against evacuation: impact, resident sweep, roof climb, repeated descents, one defensive shot, separate escapes. End in the lower shelter with headcount and injury assessment while distant fighting continues—not victory, death, restored normality, or thematic closure.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: A military ranging burst only flags Anvek's sealed combat library; she must set a local objective and unlock it. For eight cumulative minutes it fuses existing hearing, optical sight, and thermal readings to track six visible or audible threats within 150 metres, project three seconds of motion, and permit ninety cumulative seconds of high torque. It cannot see through cover, identify unseen people, hack, invent data, command machines, or repair sensors. Heat narrows fine movement and speech timing; excess torque can permanently damage actuators. Her transmitter sends only fixed identification or distress bursts. Rehabilitation preserved memory and personality: at Anvek's request independent technicians severed her coercive receiver and restored calibration and voluntary task selection while leaving this non-coercive library. Olyne provided housing, practice, advocacy, and local knowledge, not Anvek's autonomy.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Follow Olyne's practical attention in connected paragraphs: names, loads, smoke, familiar gestures, and the instant care becomes attempted control. Render Anvek's precision through observable selection, not diagnostic overlays.
- Conversational texture: Keep talk domestic and task-bound—counts, keys, pain, route disputes, clipped status, unfinished ordinary remarks. Anvek uses contractions and neighborhood shorthand when bandwidth permits; never generic robotic deadpan.
- Rhetorical ownership: Olyne reasons through relationships and exceptions but overstates protection as permission. Anvek owns quantified limits, conditional plans, exact corrections, and plain preferences. Gess supplies building facts; Naro supplies protocol and partial radio knowledge.
- Pressure behavior: Olyne interrupts, then becomes usefully specific. Anvek delays speech while sensing and shortens choices without losing agency; she seeks confirmation when another body or risk is involved. Gess repeats headcounts; Naro stays inside his sightline.
- Anti-default: Avoid uniform competence, combat quips, apology loops, personhood monologues, and any final claim that peace made Anvek human or love defeated code.

## Beats

1. A ranging pulse and impact interrupt kitchen repairs. Anvek bounds the signal; Olyne starts her resident list, then orders Anvek to remain. Anvek refuses without debating personhood.
2. Warden Gess Miro supplies keys and occupancy but wrongly trusts the stair. Anvek rejects it through ordinary hearing and tactile checks. Residents consent individually to harnesses; amber/blue lights coordinate hold/move only.
3. On damaged radio, Naro Tule confirms the shelter and drone-marking pattern but cannot see the roof or promise rescue. Olyne gives him their sole portable battery.
4. Anvek voluntarily unlocks the library with a civilian-corridor objective. Olyne supplies what it lacks: mobility needs, load pairings, Gess's weak grip, and an empty awning safe to sacrifice.
5. Anvek times visible search arcs across descents; Gess signals and corrects headcount. Smoke blinds one sector. Olyne tries taking the brake, then accepts resident preparation.
6. A fragment parts the cable as the drone marks an occupied cradle. Anvek chooses high torque to hold the brake and one calculated maintenance line-driver shot at its exposed rotor. The local defense ruins the tool and damages her shoulder and fine control.
7. Once residents are below, Anvek sends a fixed distress burst to draw attention toward the empty awning while Olyne takes the service ladder. Anvek drops the counterweight to block pursuit, then follows before fire consumes home and hoist.
8. In shelter, Naro can cool but not replace the actuator; Gess confirms the count. Olyne asks before touching Anvek's shoulder and follows her triage priority. Survival remains bounded by lost housing, damage, and uncertain movement.
