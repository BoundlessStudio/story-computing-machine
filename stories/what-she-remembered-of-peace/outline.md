# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Years after the unnamed first-person narrator diverted Coru, a demilitarized war android, from scrapping and helped her build a civilian life, a new front overtakes their outlying house before evacuation is complete. Coru voluntarily reopens combat-analysis routines she placed behind a consent lock during rehabilitation. Fulfill the promise by letting those old skills materially save them without treating either war code or peacetime teaching as her whole identity: she reads the bombardment, crosses an automated search sweep, and reaches a drainage culvert with the narrator, choosing each use and leaving the routines active because the danger has not ended.
- Intended reader experience: Intimate siege suspense with an initial fear of relapse that gives way to a harder, less sentimental recognition of continuity. The house should flicker between home and terrain; Coru's clipped tactical attention should be useful, frightening, and still recognizably hers. End with immediate escape rather than victory: relief at their continued partnership, grief for the abandoned home, and a live war ahead.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led reclassification. Each present movement gives the same body, object, or space a domestic use and a battlefield use; pressure rises when the narrator mistakes the new classification for erasure, while Coru insists through choices that both meanings remain available.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: The narrator's care has long taken the form of repair, shelter, and helping Coru live beyond the purpose imposed on her. Coru values that home and also refuses to make permanent helplessness the proof that peace changed her.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: The narrator immediately wants to reach the culvert before the search machines close the road, while also wanting Coru's civilian self protected from command logic and military recapture. Coru wants them both alive and unclaimed, while preserving her authority over which old capacities she uses. The narrator's useful flaw is a repairer's reflex: unfamiliar behavior looks like damage to interrupt, so fear makes them reach for Coru's service panel and issue deactivation commands at the worst time. Coru's useful limit is that her combat routines assume trained squadmates who obey terse timing cues; the more efficiently she speaks, the less able the frightened narrator is to follow.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: Shelling has cut power and blocked the road; passive search machines are moving between houses before ground troops arrive. Their sensors compare heat, motion, and outline but do not think or make moral judgments. Coru's high-output movement makes her easier to detect, and the safest culvert approach lies across an exposed kitchen garden.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: No person supplies the primary counterforce. The opposing force's machines execute a limited local search doctrine; Coru's old combat model and the narrator's fear create additional friction without becoming possession, personality replacement, or a manufactured villain.
  - Pressure or change created: Coru can predict the sweep only by running systems the narrator associates with her former exploitation. To cross, she must deliberately overheat her chassis and carry the narrator close under a reflective orchard sheet so the pair resemble one damaged machine rather than two living fugitives. The plan requires consent, physical trust, and obedience to a count neither can safely improvise once exposed.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close first-person past through the unnamed narrator. Their mechanical knowledge makes Coru's physical changes legible but not her private thought; early narration misreads tactical diction as evidence that she is vanishing. Coru's agency emerges through refusals, corrections, requested consent, and choices her old recommendations do not dictate.
  - Time shape and compression: One continuous forty-to-sixty-minute escape from the first near shell to the culvert. Interrupt it with three brief associative returns in reverse order: a recent ordinary market disagreement Coru handled herself, the night she chose the combat-routine lock, and the reclamation yard where the narrator first mistook rescue for a right to decide. Keep each memory short and triggered by a present touch or phrase rather than staging a second full plot.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Begin with Coru already hearing structure in the bombardment that the narrator cannot. Reveal in action that her weapons were removed long ago, the surviving war routines concern prediction, body control, concealment, and threat classification, and Coru herself controls their lock. Let the reader share the narrator's fear when her voice and posture change, then disprove identity loss through her unscripted correction of the plan and her insistence on an agreed abort signal. Supply no universal android psychology, inevitable violent compulsion, magical mechanism, or link to any established invasion system.
  - Governing movement: Secure the house; hear the pattern; choose to unlock; classify a route; resist the classification; lose time; cross the damaged yard; fail at command-style coordination; stop under cover; state fears and limits; rebuild the plan around assent; become one sensor silhouette; hold through the sweep; open the culvert; descend; encounter a gap in Coru's model; let the narrator's local knowledge lead the next turn.
- Speculative surplus: Under heavy stabilizer load, Coru's chassis induces a slow clockwise drift in uncovered standing water within two metres. At home they use a broad bowl to diagnose an overworked joint and keep full cups on saucers; during the escape, rainwater turning in a broken basin confirms strain. The effect does not reveal emotion, detect enemies, power her systems, or decide the route.
- Structural distinction: A forward present escape braided with three backward domestic memories, organized by successive reclassification rather than rooms conquered, repeated cycles, public accumulation, a spell ratchet, or an out-and-back traverse. The decisive action is a silent shared crossing of a kitchen garden; there is no firefight, public proof, crowd conversion, institutional response, or symbolic destruction of Coru's war past.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: In a collapsed potting shed, after the narrator nearly exposes them by grabbing for Coru's service panel during a mistimed dash, Coru refuses both deactivation and blind obedience. She identifies the failure in her own command cadence, explains the thermal-silhouette plan in ordinary language, and asks whether the narrator can consent to being carried and remain still through her count. The narrator admits that fear of losing her has become an attempt to control her. They agree on a physical abort tap. Coru then opens the combat prediction fully, overheats by choice, holds the narrator under the orchard sheet, and waits motionless while a search machine inspects and passes their single false outline.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: They reach the culvert before ground troops, but the house is left behind, the road remains occupied, Coru's hip stabilizer is overworked, and the nearest confirmed refuge is still hours away. Her combat routines remain open because closing them for emotional reassurance would reduce their chances. Neither knows what law or army will call her if they survive, and the new war is not resolved.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with the narrator fastening the kitchen shutters and privately treating the house as the proof that Coru's war life is over while water in her diagnostic bowl begins to turn. End below the garden when echoing runoff defeats Coru's directional model; the narrator, who once maintained the culvert, identifies the safer fork, Coru tests the footing, and they continue without settling who must always lead or what the house meant.

## Beats

1. A near shell kills the lights while the narrator fastens shutters and sorts what they can carry. Coru listens past the impact, identifies a tightening search pattern, and places her own service pin on the table.
2. Coru explains that the road will be swept before they can reach it and chooses to reopen the consent-locked combat routines. Her posture and vocabulary change. A touch at her panel calls up the recent market memory in which she handled suspicion without the narrator intervening.
3. She converts household details into a route: pantry wall as blast cover, laundry trench as dead ground, orchard sheet as concealment, drainage culvert as exit. The narrator hears only the return of military categories and wastes time challenging each step.
4. They leave as part of the roof fails. Coru predicts impacts and moves cleanly, but her clipped count makes the narrator hesitate in the open. When a search machine turns toward the motion, Coru pulls them into the ruined potting shed; the narrator reaches for her service panel as if stopping a fault.
5. Coru blocks the hand without harm and refuses to close what she chose to open. The memory of the consent lock surfaces, followed briefly by the reclamation yard: the narrator saved her life but never acquired it. Both name the practical failure—the plan requires trust, and command cadence is making trust impossible.
6. Coru restates the exposed crossing, including its risks, and asks rather than orders. They agree on a two-tap abort signal. She drives her stabilizers and thermal sink hard, wraps them together beneath the reflective orchard sheet, and carries the narrator into the garden on a counted gap.
7. A search machine pauses over their combined outline. The narrator nearly breaks stillness; Coru neither restrains them nor abandons the plan, but repeats the agreed count. They hold. The machine classifies the shape as low-priority wreckage and moves on; this is a narrow exploitation of local sensor limits, not proof of universal machine behavior.
8. At the culvert Coru uses old breaching geometry, without explosives, to shift a warped grate and worsens her hip strain. Underground, runoff echoes defeat her model. The narrator supplies remembered maintenance knowledge, Coru checks the chosen fork, and they move deeper while bombardment continues behind them.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The story occurs in the one deep-time physical world during an otherwise unplaced technological era. Apply the locked personhood protection: technical causes and inherited directives cannot erase Coru's identity, consent, relationships, or moral choices. Existing canon establishes only that whether a constructed mind is alive must be established rather than assumed; Coru's demonstrated life is local to her.
- Boundaries and unknowns: The era, belligerents, country, war chronology, android manufacture, search machines, sensor doctrine, and legal status of former war constructs are new and remain unnamed or local. Coru is not Tovan Rek, Orrin Pell, or a branch of either, and her construction establishes no universal android class. The new war and its search machines have no connection to the modern-like western-bay invasion or its nonliving frames. No magic appears. The fate of the house, the larger war, and the pair's eventual refuge remain unresolved beyond their immediate escape.
