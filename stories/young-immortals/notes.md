# Editorial notes

## Request readings, 2026-11-14

- **Codex keeper.** The prompt explicitly links young immortals' risk-taking to physical invulnerability and older immortals' caution to the time that invulnerability can keep them trapped. The keeper inferred that the pleasure is “watching one fact make two opposite choices feel sensible.” “Rather sticky situations” suggests wryness, while “VERY long time” can carry dread and gives the story its likely scale. The expected payoff is a concrete predicament that makes an older immortal's caution feel earned. The cast, relationship, trap, duration, and possibility of escape stay open.

- **Claude.** Invulnerability, as opposed to agelessness alone, produces both the swagger and the predicament. “The joke and the horror are the same idea” because the expected end of the fall, sinking, or burial “just *continues*.” Claude hears a comic edge in “rather sticky situations” and expects a concrete ordeal, experienced or vividly conveyed, that makes the old fear legible. An illustrative personal scale and an old/young pair were suggestions. The prompt leaves the narrative register, relationship, and outcome open.
