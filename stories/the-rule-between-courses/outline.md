# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Mekko expects supper with old friend Talja to restore their closeness. Talja chose this catfolk restaurant to announce that she is leaving the district without him. Her departure must remain a departure rather than become the movement that turns inherited hunting reflexes into a chase.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Mekko wants Talja to stay and their friendship to survive. His cheetah-line body reads retreat as movement to close upon, while his habit of following her withdrawals helped drive her away. Instinct, anger, care, and choice must remain distinct.
- Counterforce or complication: Talja’s secrecy makes the restaurant feel like a trap; hurt impairs Mekko’s listening; cleared plates remove food’s anchor. Other diners’ different ancestral impulses sharpen around sudden movement. Nobody is an antagonist, and the ancient rule works only because people honor it.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Mekko, registering scent, tempo, exits, other bodies, and his gathering muscles before named emotion. Talja’s intent appears only through uneven talk, correction, silence, posture, and her choice of restaurant.
- Governing movement and time shape: One set-menu sitting: arrival, two courses, two cleared-table intervals, then an expanded final interval. Warm comic awkwardness tightens into held stillness, not action spectacle. Stop once Talja is beyond pursuit and Mekko has chosen not to follow.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Catfolk inherit strong but non-compulsory instincts from wild feline ancestors; appetite, motion, proximity, and sound intensify them without erasing responsibility. Satisfying courses and soft live strings regulate the room. During the plate-cleared truce, a guest may leave, but no diner may rise or follow; staff withhold the next course until the guest clears the entrance. The rule is cultural, not enchanted.
- Dialogue promise: Stale teasing, food talk, remembered habits, wrong assumptions, and unfinished questions expose an old friendship and the embarrassment of learning why Talja chose this house. Directness stays fragmentary; no polished farewell.
- Dialogic medium: Speech, menu text, shared food, live strings, ears and tails, chair scrape, the proprietor’s hand signals, eye contact and its refusal, silence, and failed listening.
- Dialogue engine: Talja must make leaving intelligible without seeking permission; Mekko must learn whether she wants distance from the district, from him, or both. Her reason for choosing the restaurant changes trust; standing activates the rule; his response determines whether the room receives an exit or a hunt.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Warm sensory attention shifts from fish and candlelight to rhythm, exits, feet, and distance as Mekko’s appetite becomes vigilance. Keep instinct bodily, not decorative metaphor or moral diagnosis.
- Conversational texture: Old friends use food observations, stale teasing, corrections, overlap, and differently interpreted silences. Staff communicate economically without explaining the world.
- Rhetorical ownership: Mekko owns nervous humor, speed-and-distance thinking, affection, and premature conclusions; hurt makes him mistake explanation for permission. Talja owns concrete plans, dry correction, omission, and remembered preferences; she listens, but not indefinitely. The proprietor’s timing and gesture carry social authority.
- Pressure behavior: Mekko repeats questions, stops hearing answers, then forces quiet. Talja overexplains logistics, loses fluency when standing, and will not soothe him. Other diners orient before deliberately looking away.
- Relationship movement: Mekko wants proof he matters but cannot ask without making departure negotiable. Talja wants safe non-pursuit but cannot admit she chose the house because she doubts him. Disclosure damages ease; his final restraint changes risk and preserves limited trust, not reconciliation.
- Anti-default: Avoid competent crowd-control procedure, a chase, or eloquent breakup debate. Let food, embarrassment, pettiness, affection, and chosen stillness hold pressure; end on non-pursuit, not a speech, healing dessert, or applause.

## Beats

1. Mekko enters the low-ceilinged, amber-candlelit restaurant and finds Talja waiting. Varied ears, tails, stripes, rosettes, and heavy coats suggest mixed bloodlines. An unnamed musician plays soft strings; the proprietor indicates the old menu rule as ordinary custom.
2. The first course offers safe occupation. Flavor and rhythm soften Mekko; stale jokes and Talja’s memory of his preferences suggest recovered ease. Her packed bag and evasions disturb that hope.
3. During the first clearing, another guest makes a slow permitted exit. The room involuntarily orients, then deliberately returns attention to candles, hands, and music. The moment demonstrates risk and choice; Talja watches whether Mekko understands.
4. They divide a whole roasted fish during the second course. Talja reveals her move and why she selected this restaurant. Humiliated, Mekko treats logistics as an argument to defeat while she keeps correcting what she has and has not asked.
5. Plates vanish and Talja stands. Her chair scrapes; different bloodlines answer in ears, claws, breath, and posture. Music holds its quiet pattern. Staff remain unobtrusive, and diners exercise restraint rather than becoming a faceless pack.
6. Mekko nearly rises from inherited pursuit and the personal urge to stop her. Their brief, imperfect exchange separates his wish that she stay from any claim that she must, while Talja struggles to repeat what the restaurant choice already said.
7. Mekko signals the proprietor to hold the waiting course, extending the truce. He looks from Talja to the candle and remains seated until the door closes and her steps pass beyond useful pursuit. End inside that hungry interval, with music continuing and the friendship unresolved.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The deep-time world permits independent cultures. The unenchanted rule requires living choice, preserving personhood and moral consequence under instinct.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Keep catfolk biology and the house custom story-specific; connect them to no existing species, system, or era. Instinct varies, excuses no harm, and does not determine personality. Leave the rule’s origin unknown. Use the reference only for an intimate wooden room, amber candles, varied feline traits, whole fish, and soft live guitar—not its signage, language, clothing, apparent gender mix, or incidental background.
