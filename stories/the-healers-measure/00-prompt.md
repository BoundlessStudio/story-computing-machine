# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] witchknights are not only master mages, but are also the most capable warriors. When one chose you to become their apprentice you were confused; You trained to be a healer. Apparently that's why they did.**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Healer's Measure
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person centered on the healer-apprentice.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Character-driven secondary-world fantasy; tense, humane,
  and ultimately hopeful without making violence consequence-free.
- Audience/content rating: Teen; non-graphic battlefield injury and peril.
- Required elements: Witchknights are credible masters of both magic and
  martial combat; a witchknight deliberately selects a trained healer as an
  apprentice; the reason is revealed through consequential action rather than
  explanation alone; the healer's existing vocation remains essential rather
  than being discarded for combat prowess.
- Prohibited elements: No effortless competence, sadistic mentor dynamic,
  destiny-by-bloodline explanation, or implication that healing is lesser work.

## Assumptions

- The user supplied no preferred names, setting details, POV, or ending, so
  those choices may be made within current canon and the repository style guide.
- The story may introduce a local witchknight tradition if canon permits it;
  all proposed additions remain non-canonical until separately promoted.
- The prompt's second-person phrasing is treated as direct address to the
  protagonist, not as a requirement for second-person narration.
- The story will aim for one complete apprenticeship crisis rather than a broad
  training chronicle.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The mentor's selection of a healer has a concrete tactical, ethical, and
  magical payoff in the climax.
- The protagonist succeeds through integrated healing and witchknight training,
  not by abandoning one discipline for the other.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
