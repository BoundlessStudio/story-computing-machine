## Canon posture

The final story supports one trained healer six weeks into an apprenticeship,
her witchknight mentor, an unnamed injured attacker, one unnamed regional
witchknight practice, one locally bounded healing working, and one roadside
infirmary crisis. Every fact remains limited to the observed people, place,
working, and incident.

All magic in the story originates in living practitioners. Iron blades,
bracers, a clasp, chalk, cloth, bandages, buildings, and spell paths are
nonliving and non-agentic: they focus, carry, mark, resist, or receive forces,
but they never choose, diagnose, consent, cast, or sustain a working by
themselves. The story supplies no named region, calendar date, official era,
order name, bloodline, universal magical taxonomy, or connection to an
established system or story.
