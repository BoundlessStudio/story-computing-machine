## Prompt

> [WP] The museum's new wing commemorates the ancient Battle of the Bay: one queen, alone, against an invasion the plaques round to "countless." You have read every caption. The count was 131,072. She never carried a sword. And the audio guide keeps mispronouncing your name.
>
> (Solstice Evening Bell, centuries on — memory-versus-myth with the last living witness as its own subject.)

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
