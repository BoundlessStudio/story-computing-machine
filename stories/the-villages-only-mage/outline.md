# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Three traveling mages cross a dangerous pass because an impossible aura rises beyond the mountains like weather. They expect a breach or uncontrolled prodigy; instead they find an older village mage happily spending pinpricks of power on hinges, splinters, and cradle stones. After asking exactly, `where is the one with the absurdly massive amount of magical energy`, they diagnose catastrophic leakage and offer suppression training. Reverse that diagnosis: the aura is only what escapes restraints he has maintained since childhood. He is a self-taught master of suppression and fine control, though no one can measure or safely explore the void-like reserve. Snow closes the pass, turning intervention into reciprocal winter study.
- Focal attachment and contradiction: The village mage genuinely loves being useful; service is practice, affection, and chosen work. He knows his power only through embodied habits devised alone. The travelers possess theory, terminology, wards, and knowledge of other traditions, but initially cannot recognize mastery unlike their own. Each side needs what the other knows without becoming master in every domain.
- Counterforce or complication: The reserve appears bottomless to available measures, but literal infinity remains unproved. He maintains layered suppression and releases minute effects with extraordinary accuracy; he cannot inspect the source alone or know whether external wards will cooperate with restraints built into his breath and body. The sounding becomes dangerous when scale destroys the examiner's sense of direction. The storm strands three strangers among finite winter stores, while applying their suppressive equipment on a false diagnosis could cause harm.
- Narrative design:
  - POV and information limit: Open in close third past through the exacting teacher during the trio's final approach; the aura overwhelms instruments, but she cannot identify its source. At the villagers' pointing gesture, break cleanly into close second-person past through the unnamed village mage for the remainder. He reads the visitors only through action, dialogue, and their living contact during the sounding. One pivot gives the travelers' alarm authority without head-hopping.
  - Governing movement and time shape: Move from arduous approach and misdiagnosis, through a private sounding that reverses examiner and examined, into forced proximity and exchange. Render arrival through the first night; compress several early-winter days through changed domestic routines; end on the first deliberately mutual lesson. Avoid montage, public appraisal, spectacular release, village meeting, or departure.
  - Information strategy: Let the aura support the leak theory and establish fine control through useful work before explaining suppression. The sounding evokes depth and pressure without making the reserve evil, sentient, divine, or hungry. When the examiner loses her returning strand, he guides her out. Only afterward reveal his practiced restraint. Leave origin, capacity, and literal limit unknown.
- Operative speculative constraint and surplus: Reserve, suppression, release, and precision are distinct. Vast reserve does not grant vast safe output, and measurement does not confer command. Sounding is a living connection; nonliving instruments only register or shape supplied magic. Magic passing through his hands briefly accelerates nearby yeast, so villagers routinely cover and move starter crocks. The nuisance neither measures the reserve nor resolves the plot.
- Decisive turn and ending shape: Once snow prevents departure, the teacher retracts her assumption that he needs basic suppression and asks how his restraints move; he asks for her terminology, records, and collaborative safeguards. The road mage contributes ward geometry and the youngest replaces appetite for scale with attention to precision. Individual villagers cover a few jobs and share supplies, giving help without a collective conversion. End privately: formal wards define a stopping point, he demonstrates one outer restraint rather than opening the reserve, and studies their notation while coaching a precision exercise. The imperfect result establishes mutual apprenticeship, not mastery of the abyss.

## Beats

1. From the teacher's viewpoint, the trio sees an aura beyond the mountains that their instruments cannot frame. They force a late crossing while the road mage reads the storm and the youngest speculates; apparent leakage makes delay feel irresponsible.
2. After the POV pivot, show the village mage enjoying three small helps, with the yeast nuisance treated as familiar domestic inconvenience. The exhausted trio arrives, asks the prompt's exact quoted question, and every villager points to him.
3. Indoors, the travelers warn that he is leaking enough mana to be seen across mountains and prepare to teach suppression. He accepts a cautious living sounding to understand their alarm, not because he doubts his competence.
4. The examiner encounters void-like depth, loses the path back, and drags the sounding out of shape. He stabilizes her strand and guides her out, reversing their roles without humiliating her or creating public proof.
5. When the trio insists the leakage must stop, reveal that it is residue beyond lifelong restraints. Their external method conflicts with his embodied one; all four stop before harm. The pass closes, making them winter guests.
6. Across a few compressed days, pair lessons: the teacher names an effect he discovered; he corrects her pressure-heavy suppression; the road mage adapts wards around his moving restraints; the youngest learns precision through a repair. Neighbors take on tasks or bring food so he can participate without ceasing to help.
7. In a private first formal session, they agree to examine only an outer boundary. The wards supply shared limits, he controls the contact, and each side catches something the other would miss. Close on alternating study and instruction while the deep reserve remains unopened.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The setting may contain an independent local system called magic. Only living beings originate its use; sounding, wards, route instruments, stones, and tools merely receive, register, store, focus, or shape living magic.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Keep the village, range, era, travelers, and tradition unnamed and unconnected to established canon. His kindness neither creates nor fuels the reserve. Do not establish literal infinity, divinity, destiny, universal mana ranks or suppression, or a duty to provide power. No named person or place is proposed, matching the passing current inventory and legacy baseline.
