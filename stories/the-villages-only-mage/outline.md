# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: In remote Lanternscrape, Aumiri has happily used their unique magic for small jobs: holding a cistern seam, truing a mill bearing, warming sick kids, drawing goats home, and steadying ladders. Three traveling mages follow an impossible reading and ask, “Where is the one with the absurdly massive amount of magical energy?” Their test synchronizes Aumiri’s unfinished favors into an overload. Resolve it while preserving Aumiri’s desire to help.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Aumiri likes making hard days easier and leaves a faint return-thread on each working so it can be adjusted later. They remember people better than tasks still drawing on them. Severing everything would protect Aumiri but abruptly fail water, milling, heat, and access.
- Counterforce or complication: Torrigan reads one vast source; Velshia sees a containment emergency; Nimoret suspects a distributed system. Their standard pulse, supplied by Torrigan through inert instruments, made every thread answer together. The interval shortens toward involuntary discharge. Villagers resist blanket cuts for specific needs but know physical substitutes outsiders cannot see.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close second-person past through Aumiri, whose attention goes to waiting hands, broken things, and the background tug of favors. Aumiri cannot initially distinguish hundreds of threads. The visitors cannot identify any line’s purpose without its local keeper.
- Governing movement and time shape: One afternoon into night, paced by the shortening pulse: cheerful errands, disruptive arrival, walking inventory, then rolling shutdown. End next morning with one bounded favor, not recruitment, public proof, or a lesson speech.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Aumiri can lend material one small, specific intention through touch and attention. No working exceeds its task, anchor, or Aumiri’s modest live output. It persists through a living return-thread; nonliving anchors and instruments only store, focus, or transmit. Years of open threads create a huge circulating reading, not extractable power. Each must be closed by Aumiri at its anchor. Remote severance risks recoil and simultaneous failures; unchecked synchronization will overload Aumiri and shed workings unpredictably.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Close second person follows Aumiri’s task-first noticing—waiting hands, crooked hinges, animal breath, flour dust, and background tugs. Reveal scale by accumulation and miscount, not spectacle.
- Conversational texture: Village talk is familiar, interrupted, and single-purpose: requests, corrections, workarounds, incomplete receipts. Visitors talk across specialties. Preserve practical silence while hands are occupied.
- Rhetorical ownership: Aumiri uses eager procedural shorthand; Torrigan comparative readings; Velshia failure modes and safe cuts; Nimoret relationships among anchors. Beskaret and other villagers own sequence, wear, animals, and muscle-powered fallbacks.
- Pressure behavior: Aumiri’s answers shorten into repetition and mishearing. Velshia grows directive, Torrigan defensive, Nimoret tentative. Villagers interrupt theory with immediate consequences.
- Anti-default: Avoid awed experts and a quippy hidden prodigy. The measurement has a mistaken model; competence stays distributed, and decisive talk depends on the work in hand.

## Beats

1. A circuit of ordinary help shows Aumiri’s pleasure and overlapping obligations: a mill adjustment, returned warming blanket, goat-finding tug, cistern visit, and deferred request. Beskaret’s incomplete mill tally hints that neither Aumiri nor villagers know what remains open.
2. The dusty trio arrive after their measuring needles bent toward Lanternscrape. They ask the required question. Assuming they need help too, Aumiri consents to one limited locating test.
3. Torrigan’s pulse makes every thread answer: the mill surges, water overtops, old stones heat, and Aumiri feels the village at once. A shortening echo gives them a hard deadline. Velshia proposes severance; Nimoret notices anchors answering in learned order.
4. Aumiri refuses a blanket cut but agrees to close any line safely replaced. Walking the village, ordinary speakers supply partial markers and task knowledge: an obsolete oven charm, a needed pen latch, a manually stoppable mill, a bridge requiring props. Their corrections expose convenience, forgotten endings, and true dependencies without making them a needy chorus.
5. Torrigan tracks which closures lengthen the interval; Velshia isolates clusters against recoil; Nimoret pairs anchors with local keepers. Aumiri’s fluency deteriorates until Beskaret’s concrete mill instructions interrupt the wrong task. The “massive energy” resolves as accumulated open-ended help, not limitless talent.
6. The cistern, bridge, and mill remain phase-locked. Villagers choose labor and inconvenience: props, a water line, unloaded millstones. On their calls, Aumiri closes each favor while Velshia contains recoil and Torrigan cancels the query. The last pulse leaves fatigue, cold water, and repairs, but neither magical loss nor upgrade.
7. Measurements now show several bounded workings. The trio leave anchor tags and fault-reading methods, not authority or an invitation. Each continuing favor gains a visible end condition, physical fallback, and local keeper. Next morning Aumiri chooses one prepared, time-limited task while other repairs proceed without magic.
