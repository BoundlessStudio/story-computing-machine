# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Four sisters—Princesses Ilvara, Mirdakai, Meraine, and Nuvaya—were hidden separately in kingdoms their former court calls “lowly.” Months later, each receives an authenticated summons to Foldgate Relay, forged by Kalmid Osreth, their former tutor and pursuer. Sisters and hosts must expose the lure, turn its route against him, and end his hunt without making the princesses cargo or their protectors scenery.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Separation keeps the sisters alive but makes promised reunion irresistible. Ilvara wants a defensible plan; Mirdakai a testable mechanism; Meraine the sender’s intention; Nuvaya immediate action. Each host defends a sanctuary compact that fails if one small kingdom surrenders another’s guest.
- Counterforce or complication: Kalmid helped topple an extractive crown and sees every surviving princess as a future general’s banner. He knows their cipher and family habits and assumes poor kingdoms can be frightened or bought, but not the local relays’ physical checks. The sisters must respect those checks.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Braided close third past, rotating through the four sisters over one night. Stay inside one refuge and host conversation at a time. Only packets, route marks, omissions, and delayed receipts cross distance; no voices, thoughts, or access to Kalmid’s mind.
- Governing movement and time shape: Dusk to dawn: four summonses, four examinations, one misidentified reply, and a Foldgate interception. The sisters never reunite. End with Kalmid and his stolen shard secured and each sister choosing continued local sanctuary.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Paired duskglass transmits light/dark bits that workers convert into Nalevane’s tide mirrors, Rusk Hollow’s two bells, Oriel Strand’s woven vanes, or Ternhold’s lamp shadows. A palace shard forges royal authentication but not tide cadence, bell rebound, wind spacing, or shadow orientation. Relays are manual, range-bounded, delayed, and fallible.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Cross-cut one packet as glare, vibration, cloth pull, and shadow. Keep mechanics tactile and subordinate to choice; contradict “lowly” through skilled work.
- Conversational texture: Mix royal shorthand, craft correction, complaint, and incomplete receipts. Hosts interrupt and refuse without becoming rustic sages; no bantering quartet.
- Rhetorical ownership: Ilvara owns contingency; Mirdakai timing; Meraine motive and omission; Nuvaya routes and risk. Hosts own their media and sanctuary decisions. Kalmid reduces people to outcomes.
- Pressure behavior: Ilvara becomes formal; Mirdakai floods speech before choosing one test; Meraine turns quiet and indirect; Nuvaya interrupts toward action before waiting for receipt. Hosts stay fluent in familiar work.
- Anti-default: Avoid interchangeable clever princesses, a joint declaration, villain monologue, or crowd conversion. Identity emerges through local knowledge and unequal responses.

## Beats

1. At dusk, Breqen stops Ilvara forwarding a court-perfect summons because its spacing claims a Nalevane tide that never occurred. Ilvara defends the cipher, then accepts Breqen’s control of the mirror route and asks for one bounded check.
2. Mirdakai and Brikka find that the packet lacks Rusk Hollow’s mountain bell rebound. After burying the moment in measurements, Mirdakai selects one challenge whose answer can locate the intruder without exposing a sister.
3. Meraine notices the summons promises reunion in birth order but ignores the hosts; Rhevos finds an edited wind gap in Oriel Strand’s record. They infer a sender who knows royal longing but imagines the kingdoms as containers, and withhold acknowledgement.
4. Nuvaya sees riders already taking Ternhold’s dry path to Foldgate. Ullet stops her running a warning and makes her map their sightlines. She identifies where a reversed shadow packet can seem to originate from her refuge while drawing pursuit away.
5. Findings cross by local route marks. Ilvara keeps the sisters separated; Mirdakai supplies false timing; Meraine selects credible bait; Nuvaya chooses its visible route. Hosts control equipment and watches, then send an acknowledgement apparently exposing Nuvaya at Foldgate.
6. Kalmid accepts because he expects the youngest to break hiding first. Three kingdoms maintain false echoes while Ullet’s small watch uses shuttered lamps, terrain, and a disabled bridge span to isolate him. Nuvaya times the last reversal from safety.
7. Kalmid’s stolen shard proves his access; his resistance frames heirs as wars, not people. Hosts secure him under their compact. The sisters decline reunion and turn to tide reset, bell inspection, vane repair, and lamp oil—affection present in separate receipts without a shared statement.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The deep-time world permits an independent, era-local civilization; concrete causality, personhood, and broadly accessible violence boundaries apply.
- Boundaries and unknowns: All nouns and duskglass are new and local. The non-agentic signal craft is unrelated to canonical bells, crowns, glass systems, or shadow faculties. “Lowly” is a court slur, not objective fact. There are exactly four sister-princesses; introduce no fifth. Kalmid’s capture resolves this pursuit and immediate survival, not succession or the wider postwar settlement.
