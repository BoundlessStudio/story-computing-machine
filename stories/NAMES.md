# Character name registry

This is persistent production memory, not canon. It maps character-facing names
and aliases to the stories or legacy sources that use them so new work can avoid
accidental reuse.

## Rules

- Search this file before choosing a full name, given name, mononym, nickname,
  alias, username, title used as a name, or named animal, companion, construct,
  or person-like entity.
- `Reserved forms` lists every form that should collide case-insensitively. A
  surname is listed separately only when the prose uses it alone as a character
  label or an existing continuity review has identified it as a collision.
- `unique` means no known other identity reserves that form. `deliberate` means
  reuse is intentional and the rationale explains its story value and reader
  disambiguation. `unresolved` means the overlap is accidental or undecided and
  must not be copied into new work.
- A deliberate crossover or recurring identity may reuse its own names. Two
  distinct identities may share a name only when the reuse materially matters
  to the story and readers can distinguish them. Convenience is not a reason.
- Legacy, abandoned, and non-canon names remain searchable reservations unless
  a row explicitly marks them released. Listing a name here never promotes it.
- The primary story coordinator updates this table after planning and reconciles
  it again after final review. Specialist agents read it but do not edit it.
