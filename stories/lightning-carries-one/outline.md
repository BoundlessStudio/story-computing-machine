# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Witnesses see Hazel Stroud vanish in lightning and appear where she means to go. Eight years after carrying younger brother Tobin left him older and burdened by years of disordered memories, he brings a weathered notebook in both their hands and asks her to retrace that instant without lightning. Two later passengers suffered likewise. The route through places nested inside Hazel's idea of *home* must reveal the power and make her recognize Tobin's unseen life without curing or reuniting them.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Hazel wants a rule that contains guilt and preserves solo freedom. Tobin wants his years recognized but cannot ask her to grieve siblinghood she cannot remember. Her investigative habits threaten that acknowledgment.
- Counterforce or complication: Tobin's chronology loops, Hazel's is blank, and the notebook persuades without explaining. Fatigue, resentment, thunder, and Hazel's option to skip away limit his willingness. There is no villain.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Hazel, sharing impatience with distance and bodily recognitions. Tobin remains external through pages, disrupted speech, humor, anger, and boundaries. Evidence confirms the operative truth, not its ultimate cause.
- Governing movement and time shape: One afternoon into stormy evening: lightning arrival, then bus and walking through three former homes to the original destination. Each leg releases pages and shifts control; recognition stays private.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Lightning joins Hazel to a desired place in an outer instant, but desire is not a coordinate. Inside, places contributing to its meaning become continuous terrain. Alone, she lives that elastic duration but arrives restored and forgetful. A companion anchors shared time: Hazel still forgets, while they retain incompatible years and a fraction of bodily aging, producing lasting temporal and perceptual disorder. Three attempts show no safe exception; no fourth occurs.
- Dialogue promise: Estranged sibling talk brings stale shorthand, pettiness, failed jokes, mistaken expectations, and uneven chronology. Hazel's neat questions fail; listening matters more than eloquence.
- Dialogic medium: Speech, withheld pages, handwriting, sketches, gesture, silence, body cues, sensory fragments, and choosing not to initiate lightning.
- Dialogue engine: Tobin controls pages and stops; Hazel controls whether travel stays ordinary and he becomes testimony or specimen. Exchanges change route, evidence, leverage, and willingness to continue.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Hazel's attention leaps between objectives and treats roads as waste; ordinary travel forces it into footsteps, waits, weather, and gaps. Intrusions stay brief and concrete, never a cosmic dream-tour.
- Conversational texture: Sibling speech is clipped, familiar, intermittently childish, and correction-heavy. Mix route talk, hunger, irritation, failed jokes, repetitions, and unfinished explanations with consequential disclosure.
- Rhetorical ownership: Hazel owns tidy causality, logistics, and dry evasive humor. Tobin owns associative geography, barbed affection, inconvenient specifics, and chronological misreadings; experiential authority does not grant fluent explanation. Hazel's willingness to listen is earned.
- Pressure behavior: Hazel accelerates into interrogation until guilt breaks her fluency. Tobin overconnects times, repeats, or goes silent when categorized; thunder makes him anticipate actions she cannot recall.
- Relationship movement: Tobin wants recognition but cannot request love from the stranger replacing his remembered sister. Hazel wants a map and absolution but cannot ask whether her forgotten self harmed him. Pages shift leverage; bodily recognition shifts knowledge; final restraint permits limited trust, not restored intimacy.
- Anti-default: Avoid a lucid traumatized oracle guiding a competent investigator to a clean mechanism. Preserve confusion, pettiness, exhaustion, partial evidence, and Hazel's wish for a fix. No cure, public proof, safe protocol, repeat attempt, or polished moral.

## Beats

1. Hazel arrives by lightning at the disused departure rooms. Tobin flinches, withholds most of the notebook, and sets terms: ordinary travel to the family house, his stops, no lightning.
2. On the bus, surface the three attempts through conflict. Tobin was first; Hazel recruited two consenting adults to test whether he was exceptional. All returned disordered and older. His first page bears her hand across impossible dates, but he resists authentication instead of hearing.
3. A former rental lies off the direct route but inside Hazel's meaning of home. Tobin knows a private detail and anticipates a calming wrist cue she performs unknowingly. Humor fails when she labels it a variable.
4. Tobin's directions and chronology unravel. Hazel takes control and loses the associative path. A sketch and fragment of stilled rain, worn shoes, and older Tobin make fabrication untenable without restoring her lost life.
5. Under shelter, Hazel demands sequence until Tobin withdraws. He withheld the notebook because she answered his return by risking others. He needs neither certification nor every year reconstructed, but acknowledgment that the harm is not only inside him.
6. Hazel lets Tobin choose what stays unread. Selected pages show each meaning nested in her destination becoming lived terrain; her forgotten self documented the pattern. Include quarrels, care, boredom, and mistakes, not only explanation.
7. Near the childhood house, the storm offers Hazel an exit. She initiates no strike and reaches for no passenger. Notebook, body memory, and landscape confirm the rule. Tobin sets future contact and keeps most pages; Hazel takes ordinary transport for now, without cure, reunion, proof, or absolute vow.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Reality is coherent despite incomplete models. Hazel's independent faculty must preserve personhood, consent, grief, and responsibility; treatment stays accessible and consequence-focused.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Establish only Hazel's solo rides and three companion attempts. Do not connect them to Bellweather lightning, the stormhart, Eiren's locked crossing, the storm engine, or any other route system. Origin and wider behavior remain unknown. Tobin's disorder is real but neither erases agency nor vanishes through belief. Other passengers remain unnamed; no fourth attempt occurs.
