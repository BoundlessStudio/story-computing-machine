# Outline

## Story

- Title: A Note of Spite
- Premise and central promise: At Clorinda's fiftieth-birthday reception, Lavinia admires her friend's new protective perfume, then discovers it blocks her customary nuisance hex. Deliver fashionable magical defenses, sharp social comedy, and an evening whose amusement changes ownership.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Lavinia wants to remain Clorinda's indispensable accomplice. They built intimacy by making occasions ridiculous; she mistakes change for dismissal. She enjoys Clorinda and being the funnier woman.
- Counterforce or complication: Clorinda wants to sing one unspoiled song. She has enjoyed their mutual pranks, but her elaborate protection now embarrasses Lavinia. Her pleasure in surprising her friend is not entirely innocent. Neither is a professional performer.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Lavinia's incomplete social readings. Discover Clorinda's preparation and private musical pleasure through conversation and performance, never another viewpoint.
- Governing movement and time shape: One reception, from fresh top notes through warmer perfume. Circulating compliments become a blocked prank, attempted recovery of control, then listening through a song. No crowd conversion follows.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Aromatic oils focus magic supplied by the living wearer's activation and breathing. This blend intercepts voice-twist hexes, releasing a bitter-green note. It cannot identify motives, stop words, improve singing, or prevent embarrassment. Evaporation limits duration; fresh perfume renews the coating. Bottles and oils possess no agency.
- Dialogue promise: Cultivated compliments contain curiosity, affection, vanity, or challenge. Friends misjudge when amusement stops being mutual; genuine wit coexists with tactless repetition and ordinary party talk.
- Dialogic medium: Spoken conversation, scent recognition, offered wrists, a handwritten entertainment card, failed magical interruption, and an amateur song answered by listening and laughter.
- Dialogue engine: Lavinia must discover Clorinda's arrangements without admitting exclusion. Clorinda wants participation without control. Attempts to recover their usual rhythm change who directs each encounter.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Lavinia notices scent before dress and audience before meaning. Allow lush, discriminating sensory sentences; shorten when another person refuses her interpretation. Her amusing judgments should sometimes expose her own smallness.
- Conversational texture: Flowing reception chatter surrounds uneven private exchanges. Compliments may be sincere, questions merely curious, and interruptions badly timed. Save verbal brilliance for moments Lavinia actually earns.
- Rhetorical ownership: Lavinia owns swift comparisons, tactical praise, and evasive joking. Clorinda owns literal corrections, stubborn repetition, physical affection, and occasional ponderous humor. She controls the guest list but is no sage; Lavinia listens well only after losing her prepared answer.
- Pressure behavior: Lavinia repeats a joke after its occasion passes and overexplains attempted kindness. Clorinda becomes blunt about small matters, then loses fluency when asking for something she expects to be mocked.
- Relationship movement: Lavinia wants proof she is still wanted but cannot comfortably request reassurance. Clorinda wants attention without interception but dislikes acknowledging how much Lavinia's opinion matters. Their exchanges relinquish one shared habit without settling the whole friendship.
- Anti-default: Avoid giving both women polished speeches about vulnerability. Preserve their vanity, pleasure in display, unequal comic timing, and ability to be irritating after a meaningful change.

## Beats

1. **An entrance worth smelling.** Lavinia expects their familiar conspiracy against dull guests. Establish defenses as desirable grooming: throat and wrist application, fashionable blends, discreet renewal. One incidental ward turns a candle spark; aromatic oil threatens a pale cuff. Clorinda's pear-and-violet fragrance earns an admiring inquiry.
2. **The joke catches.** Lavinia sends one familiar, minor voice-twist while Clorinda welcomes someone. Instead of the expected squeak, the new perfume gives its bitter-green flash. Clorinda continues speaking. Lavinia recognizes the ward's narrow function; guests do not automatically identify an attacker. Clorinda declines to expose her publicly, which Lavinia initially reads as another move in their game.
3. **An occasion already arranged.** The entertainment card reveals Clorinda's solo. Lavinia assumes parody, then offers to join and improve it. Clorinda has continued singing privately after Lavinia assumed they had both abandoned a youthful enthusiasm. Keep the discovery concrete: practice, a difficult passage, anticipation.
4. **An unwanted rescue.** Away from the immediate audience, Lavinia proposes making the performance deliberately ludicrous before anyone can laugh at it. Clorinda refuses that protection too. Let Lavinia misunderstand once, become petty, and encounter a plain limit rather than a confession. Clorinda still wants her present; Lavinia must choose whether presence requires authorship. No bargain or perfect apology is necessary.
5. **What protection permits.** During the song, Lavinia has an ordinary spoken joke ready that no ward could stop. Her effort to follow Clorinda's actual performance interrupts the opportunity. The voice wavers; listeners fidget, enjoy passages, or miss things. Keep the scent physically present without making its expiration a technical emergency. Clorinda reaches a ridiculous turn she chose herself, using her imperfect voice with unexpected pleasure.
6. **A different laugh.** Lavinia's laughter answers that invitation instead of preventing the song. Clorinda completes it without becoming accomplished or universally admired. Resolve the promise in its imperfect reception: no duet, public repentance, generalized praise, restored old arrangement, or concluding moral. Leave the women capable of further friction.
