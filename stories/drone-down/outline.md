# Outline

## Story

- Proposed reader-facing title: *The City Keeps Score*.
- Premise and central promise: In a future city where police drones routinely scan, command, and stun civilians, teenagers compete for ingenious drone “drops.” Across three escalating rounds, Raska must recover from freezing at a warning tone, defeat rival Kero, and ground an active incapacitation unit. Deliver daring play, then reveal that drones count teenage associations as relentlessly as teenagers count falls.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Raska wants rival treatment, not damage management, and converts that want into risk. Kero wants their competitive ease back but watches her too closely. Sevi wants to stop being the protected witness and lookout.
- Counterforce or complication: Patrol logic, appetite for witnessed novelty, and mistrust of protection reinforce one another. A grounded drone can still record and shock. Sevi hides increasing patrol attention, then creates a challenge the older teens misread.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Raska, attentive to routes, movable street matter, score arithmetic, and Kero’s possible pity. The warning tone narrows perception. She cannot know Kero’s motives, Sevi’s classification, or retained drone data before action or a display reveals them.
- Governing movement and time shape: Three evening rounds over ten days, each entered through a disputed chalk-board addition. Compress the first two; give the last night half the story. Escalate from play, through relational failure, to three-way improvisation on shuttered streets. Stop before they settle the score.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Drones follow a visible observe-command-incapacitate cycle and build fallible association tallies from faces, voices, proximity, and interference. Grounding stops flight, not the reserve camera, speaker, contact charge, or queued upload. Digital evidence is forbidden; a fall counts only when witnessed and when Sevi reads the unit mark.
- Dialogue promise: Friendship, rivalry, fear, and status surface through petty rulings, interrupted taunts, bad explanations, single-purpose warnings, and costly moments of directness. Care usually masquerades as an objection to method or score.
- Dialogic medium: Spoken challenge, erasable chalk marks, improvised hand signs beneath drone microphones, canned drone commands and displays, and consequential misreadings.
- Dialogue engine: Witnesses and Sevi’s ruling make communication necessary, while speech helps drones connect the teens. Sevi’s misread marks summon the last round; their authority later forces Raska and Kero to notice the city’s tally. Each contact changes position, target priority, trust, or risk.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Kinetic but not sleek. Track Raska’s counting alongside cracked shutters, laundry rigs, and delivery rails repurposed as play. Shock-memory breaks the rhythm into sensory gaps, then it returns imperfectly.
- Conversational texture: Use overlap, heckling, score-law pedantry, sulks, warnings, clumsy jokes, repeated questions, purposeful quiet, and ordinary complaints amid danger.
- Rhetorical ownership: Raska owns precision, rules, and late listening, mistaking concern for contempt. Kero uses throwaway humor and demonstration; affection drives evasion until fear makes him blunt. Sevi holds authority over points, exaggerates when embarrassed, and catches contradictions. Drone language stays repetitive and indifferent.
- Pressure behavior: Raska repeats counts and overexplains rules until the warning tone takes her words. Kero drops jokes and reasons. Sevi overtalks to seem older, then uses an ambiguous sign that worsens the approach.
- Relationship movement: Raska wants Kero’s trust but cannot request less protection; Kero wants her alive without surrendering rivalry. Sevi wants participant status but hides being targeted. Exchanges expose Kero’s fear, make Sevi a risk-sharing actor, and force Raska to share the decisive choice.
- Anti-default: Resist a clean hacker team or articulate junior activists. Keep methods physical and improvised, competence uneven, mistakes consequential, motives mixed, and the ending limited to changed friendship and an unresolved score—not exposure, reform, or a slogan.

## Beats

1. A drone delays a street queue and advances toward stunning a slow civilian. Raska redirects it into an improvised fall; Kero calls the method derivative, and Sevi awards a partial point. Bystanders disperse before retrieval.
2. At the hidden board, establish Raska one point behind Kero and angry at his caution since her stunning. Sevi asks to compete; both dismiss them. Raska resolves to ground an active incapacitation unit cleanly.
3. Raska and Kero converge on another drone. Its warning tone freezes Raska’s counting; Kero abandons his attempt to finish the fall and shield her. Sevi awards a shared point, but Raska gives Kero full credit, converting protection into humiliation.
4. Patrols multiply on Sevi’s route while Raska and Kero avoid each other. Sevi hides the attention and chalks a final opportunity. Each mistakes the marks for the other’s challenge.
5. They find Sevi’s solo trick holding an incapacitation unit low, not grounded. Incompatible approaches, a bad sign, and an ill-timed taunt let it reorient and advance its cycle.
6. Its display prioritizes Sevi through a tally built at earlier falls. Raska draws priority; Kero recognizes her intent; Sevi corrects their timing. Their messy combined action grounds it before anyone is stunned.
7. The live drone keeps recording, its mark beside the charged frame. A claim risks another shock and clarifies responsibility in the queued record. As retrieval approaches, they leave together without proof or a settled winner. End on changed conduct, not a new rule, erased board, declaration, or promised movement.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The deep-time shared world permits an ordinary technological civilization in an unspecified era; no existing person, place, faction, or system is imported. Locked evidence, chronology, personhood, and content boundaries remain.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Leave the city, country, calendar, era’s relation to established eras, drone maker, government, police institution, and wider resistance unnamed. Association tallies are local and fallible, not omniscience. Keep takedowns vivid but non-instructional and incapacitation consequential but non-graphic.
