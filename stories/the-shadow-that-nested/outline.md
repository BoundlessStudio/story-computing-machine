# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Surface observers report a forked lunar shadow. An orbital repair crew traces the second track to a forgotten sunshade caught broadside, then finds a vacuum-faring migration brooding across its attitude machinery. Complete one repair operation that prevents the next shadows from dangerously overlapping without killing or conveniently relocating the migration.
- Focal pressure or attachment: EVA lead Adal wants old machinery returned to its designed state. A full drive cycle would be clean and lethal: nest fibers bridge stationary bearings and the moving collar. Adal must accept a partial, uneven repair dependent on colleagues' estimates and behavior nobody can certify.
- Counterforce or complication: Tugs cannot tow the shade; cutting it loose creates debris and an uncontrolled occultation. Suit time, propellant, comm lag, and the approaching overlap restrict experiments. Vibration makes the creatures tighten their structures, while their launch pattern may clear only some sockets. Deadline, ecology, and mechanism—not a villain—supply resistance.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Adal from approach through EVA. Render geometry through handholds, load paths, suit contact, and visor shadow maps. Surface facts arrive late. Wing position, light, and structure-borne vibration support only Pado's provisional interpretations; builders, animal minds, origins, and unseen public response remain unknown.
- Governing movement and time shape: One approach-to-eclipse operation over roughly an orbit: identify the artifact, inspect the jam, reject impossible options, observe two behavioral cycles, then hand-feather selected shutter sectors as a natural clearing wave reaches them. Keep the climax on the maintenance rim and end after a survivable shadow pass, before public explanation or full restoration.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Eight overlapping shutter sectors share an attitude collar, but maintenance dogs can isolate and hand-jack each sector. Opening four balanced sectors a few degrees fragments one umbra into narrow bands that surface reserves can bear; portable gear cannot turn the whole array. The creatures ride solar pressure and magnetospheric currents, brood on warm stationary housings, and slacken crossing fibers before launching along the light edge. Those signs are behavior, never language or assured cooperation.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Stay tactile and maintenance-close: torque through gloves, shadow geometry on a visor, animal scale as motion across machinery too large to see whole. Let each observation revise Adal's working model.
- Conversational texture: Use checklists, repair calls, lagged confirmations, interruptions, clipped single-purpose speech, and ordinary irritation. Secondary ground voices contribute isolated data or corrections.
- Rhetorical ownership: Adal owns load paths and executable imperatives; Bero, conditional orbital thresholds; Pado, competing behavioral hypotheses and uncertainty; Enver, surface consequences, timing changes, and plain omen reports.
- Pressure behavior: Adal repeats confirmations; Bero speeds up but stays exact; Pado slows and retracts overclaims; Enver interrupts when margins change. Lag creates overlap and missed listening without false hostility.
- Anti-default: Avoid captain-engineer banter, science-versus-superstition debate, save-them-versus-save-us speeches, comms exposition, and thematic debrief. Put the ethical result in which clutches move, which stay locked, and the imperfect shadow produced.

## Beats

1. During an expected lunar transit, Enver sends a revised map: a straighter second track will merge with the moon's on the next pass. Storage can bear either darkness, not both. Omen-driven closures matter because abandoned charging queues shrink that margin, not because belief needs refuting.
2. Bero pilots toward a nearly invisible lattice off the moon's apparent limb. Regular ribs, inspection marks, and shutter bands establish an engineered sunshade. Solar pressure has overcome its failed collar and is turning it broadside.
3. Adal and Pado reach the rim expecting seized bearings. Lamps reveal brood cups on fixed housings and fibers spanning collar, sockets, and rollers. A cautious vibration test makes adults clamp tighter; a normal cycle would sweep through the nests.
4. Towing, severing, waiting, and driving through the nests fail mechanically or ethically. Adal finds maintenance dogs; Bero determines four isolated, balanced sectors can break the umbra. No single expert owns both discoveries or the answer.
5. Pado compares two light-edge passages, wing formations, and vibration timing. Adults slacken crossing fibers before launching, while brood cups stay fixed. This yields a fallible sequence of temporary socket openings, not evacuation, translation, or consent.
6. They improvise one pass: Bero holds reaction torque; Enver calls shrinking margins through lag; Adal and Pado disengage and jack a sector only after its span slackens. The first advance triggers renewed clamping, costing time and propellant. Adal slows the cadence.
7. One balanced target remains occupied. Bero recalculates an asymmetric set while Enver's next update interrupts Pado's uncertain forecast. Adal chooses the less efficient clear sector over forcing the ideal one. They advance in pulses, pausing whenever fibers tighten; the densest nesting sectors never move.
8. The moon's next shadow is followed by narrow shade bands instead of a second umbra. Enver confirms pumps and storage remain within buffers. Adults resume their launch sequence around the altered shutters while the crew secures its jacks and leaves the isolated collar for slower work.

## Continuity

- Canon used: One world persists through deep time; technology can become archaeology or omen; knowledge stays partial. The shade is an independent artifact, not proof that recurring sun, moon, or shadow motifs share an origin.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Do not identify this moon with Ravel's witnessed moons or fix the era. Builders, date, full purpose, migration origin, and species intelligence remain unresolved. Machinery is nonliving and nonmagical; animal behavior stays uncertain. Partial feathering solves the immediate danger without promising permanent control or effortless coexistence.
