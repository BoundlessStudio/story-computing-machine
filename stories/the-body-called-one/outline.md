# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Rebel pilot Meyda Varr has forced her way out of Unit 02, the regime combat frame she stole and crashed beyond its retrieval corridor. Its damaged recorder reports an unresolved dependency on a missing predecessor. She follows it to Orison Hollow expecting an earlier machine whose control maps might help the rebels disable captured frames. Fulfill the reveal materially and personally: Unit 01 designated Meyda’s human body before Unit 02 was built around it, and Unit 01 “went missing” when she first escaped into rebel protection.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Meyda wants useful machinery, not an origin. Her continuous memory begins with rebels teaching an injured, nameless pilot to move without a frame. She made herself valuable by treating pain and bodily hesitation as cockpit warnings to override. Now her posture, reflexes, scars, and lost childhood—not merely her flight skill—prove to have been the program’s prototype. Knowledge competes with escape as she decides how much recovery risk her history is worth.
- Counterforce or complication: Unit 02’s crash beacon will trigger Orison Hollow’s purge before dawn. The empty installation obeys reunification protocol: after recognizing Meyda, it closes doors, cues involuntary corrections through dormant interface nodes in her body, and routes the wreck toward her. No officer need appear. Meyda’s procedural thinking is the useful limit; she misreads human restraints as maintenance fixtures and her reactions as damage while the exit window contracts.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Meyda, intimate with weight shift, phantom cockpit geometry, and her conversion of sensation into instrument language. She knows rebel salvage and Unit 02’s recent behavior, not her earlier life or the facility’s purpose. Absent technicians stay exterior, and the facility has no personality. Sparse records contribute evidence; no single file or recovered-memory monologue explains the truth.
- Governing movement and time shape: One night: descend from the open wreck through increasingly body-scaled rooms to a diagnostic chamber, then climb out by another service route before the purge. Early motion is confident and machine-oriented; each lower level slows as the architecture fits Meyda. Render the wreck exit, first interface response, childhood-scale equipment, diagnostic recognition, and choice of what to carry. Compress corridor searches. End before the rebel rendezvous, with immediate pursuit escaped and disclosure unresolved.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: This Unit program is local technology. It mapped Meyda’s living nervous system and trained Unit 02 around her accumulated reflexes; implanted nodes can cue muscles but cannot create a mind or make the frame alive. Suppression damaged autobiographical recall but left procedural skill, scars, records, and recognition responses. Longitudinal medical matches verify Meyda as the continuing person designated Unit 01, without restoring an exhaustive childhood or establishing a universal theory of souls or identity.

## Beats

1. Meyda leaves Unit 02’s split cockpit with its recorder. The dependency error points to Unit 01 at Orison Hollow; she chooses the possible control map over a direct rebel return.
2. The freight entrance ignores the recorder but accepts Meyda’s palm and gait. An involuntary hand correction completes its access pattern, showing that surviving nodes can cue her muscles without choosing for her.
3. The machine bay narrows into braces, pressure clothes, food dispensers, and rigs sized for a growing child. Meyda translates them into prototype parts until wear patterns match her favored knee.
4. Inventories reorder the lineage: Unit 02 is an enclosure and amplifier; Unit 01 required nutrition, sleep, wound care, and growth adjustments. Authentication shortens the recovery clock.
5. A diagnostic scan aligns Meyda with childhood-to-adult records through a healed fracture, dental anomaly, interface topology, and registration filament beneath an old scar. Unit 01 disappeared just before the rebels found her. Sensory fragments confirm procedures, not a complete former self.
6. Reunification seals the doors, routes Unit 02’s wreck inward, and cues Meyda toward enclosure. Unable to copy the full archive before purge, she uses Unit 01 authorization to reach a breaker and cuts the motor link at the wall.
7. Meyda takes the longitudinal record and limited disabling data, abandoning the larger archive. She exits through a waste route during the purge and sends a bare survival signal, leaving later disclosure to a choice made beyond this story.
