# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Rebel pilot Loxan Vythe has torn her active mind from Unit 02, a combat chassis presented as a vehicle, into a short-lived emergency shell. She seeks Unit 01 for flight architecture that might free other pilots. Evidence at Harrowstar Annex reveals Unit 01 as her first organic body and the series as numbered embodiments. Complete the search without reducing either body or present self to equipment.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Unit 02's diagnostics called unseen scars “baseline,” and supposedly learned maneuvers felt remembered. Loxan wants the precursor's control map, but fears being declared derivative once her origin is known. Her clumsy emergency shell has only hours of support.
- Counterforce or complication: Signal technician Mursa Glint needs the rumored command seed before Directorate retrieval craft arrive. She can guide Loxan only through narrow audio bursts and cockpit glyphs. Harrowstar recognizes Unit 02's calibration signature but classifies Loxan as loose program material, sealing routes and attempting storage. The reveal changes what must be rescued and consumes copying time.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Loxan, intimate with false cockpit sensations, latency, vibration, and poor proprioception. She cannot enter Unit 01's mind, determine whether awareness remains, or verify claims about continuity. Mursa understands signals, not embodiment. Show only evidence Loxan physically triggers and compares.
- Governing movement and time shape: One flight and descending ten-hour search, narrowing from debris lanes into body-sized maintenance spaces. Each clue replaces machine history with embodied evidence. End after retrieval, before public proof, other rescues, or a theory of which body is “real.”
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: A local Directorate program mapped one pilot's living sensorimotor pattern into successive grown combat chassis. Each body has local memory and active neural tissue; there is no remote hive mind. A destructive service transfer moves the current pattern into a compatible emergency shell and leaves the prior chassis insensate, without proving soul or perfect continuity. Unit 01's preserved tissue remains the biometric root. Records use retinal footage, bone-conducted audio, vestibular plots, muscle traces, and maintenance color fields. The archive is not conversational, and its labels do not establish personhood.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Keep the narration embodied but initially miscalibrated: Loxan reads roll, thrust, latency, temperature, and phantom scale before emotion. Let evidence accrue through action as human anatomy enters the vocabulary.
- Conversational texture: Mursa's rare audio bursts are practical and interruptible; Loxan answers while working, sometimes by steering or glyph acknowledgment. Allow ordinary checks and failed hearing.
- Rhetorical ownership: Loxan reasons through motion and bodily correspondence. Mursa owns signal inference, contingency, and resource arithmetic. Archive labels are terse classifications, not villainous eloquence.
- Pressure behavior: Loxan grows quieter; overload fragments her reports. Mursa becomes more explicit as delay worsens, then listens when her command-seed assumption fails.
- Anti-default: Avoid a talking-machine exposition scene or polished arguments about authentic identity; let changed priorities reveal belief.

## Beats

1. In a stolen courier, Loxan stabilizes after severing from Unit 02. Mursa confirms retrieval craft are approaching. Loxan chooses the search over the rebel rendezvous because the precursor may release other pilots.
2. Obsolete guidance lights answer Unit 02's signature. Loxan anticipates their turns, then feels a flinch around an absent limb—too specific for generic flight software. Mursa still reads it as inherited machine calibration.
3. The annex opens to Loxan's neural contact. Diagnostics arrive as color, machine audio, and balance pulses. An “airframe” diagram resembles machinery until a blink disrupts its horizon.
4. Maintenance rooms are scaled for cradles, not aircraft. Loxan matches Unit 02's baseline to a retinal scar, hearing loss, and muscle responses. Mursa finds that production numbers belong to embodiment records, without yet knowing what survived.
5. The archive classifies Loxan for re-integration and closes partitions. She predicts the level maintenance route from Unit 01's recorded balance error; when speech fails, Mursa converts pressure alarms into cockpit arrows.
6. Loxan finds her preserved organic body, bearing every calibration asymmetry. Tissue maintenance shows no reliable awareness. Logs prove extraction and reuse but not transfer, reconstruction, or branching. The “command seed” is Unit 01's biometric response; copying it perpetuates the body's use.
7. As retrieval craft enter the debris lane, Loxan abandons full extraction and disconnects Unit 01. Mursa guides departure by glyph and broken audio. Loxan keeps only an index toward other pilots. Mursa adjusts the rendezvous for two bodies and one speaking person, practical accommodation rather than metaphysical verdict.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The deep-time world permits an independent technological era and partial recovery of controlled evidence. Personhood protection applies: construction, extraction, labels, and uncertain continuity do not make Loxan or a potentially living body equipment. The mechanism remains local.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Make no connection to Tovan Rek, Orrin Pell, Nina and Anin, the Regent identity system, or the afterlife. Do not settle Unit 01's awareness, soul continuity, or broader reproducibility. Unit 02 remains insensate after severance. Retrieving the body completes this search; freeing other pilots remains future work.
