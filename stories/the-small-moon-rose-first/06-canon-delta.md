# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

## New characters or character facts

- **Nell Mercado:** A thirteen-year-old modern-like-era girl who remembers 617
  days in Ravel after only eleven seconds pass at her February 18 departure
  point. She returns in her departure-aged body with healed Ravel scars, a
  617-notch belt, and half of Trenn's copper token. Celia is her mother. Nell
  and Bash regard each other as family; her arc moves from unilateral secrecy
  toward chosen, mutual trust without resolving their disagreement about the
  Glass Gate.
- **Sebastian “Bash” Wren:** A twelve-year-old modern-like-era boy, cellist, and
  Latin student who shares Nell's 617 Ravel days. He returns twelve seconds
  after his own June 7 departure with his scars, restored red scarf, and the
  other half-token. He wants to preserve the possibility of reaching those
  left in Ravel and agrees with Nell that neither child will act on a route
  alone.
- **Celia:** Nell's mother. She witnesses Nell's eleven-second disappearance,
  supports the search while protecting her from harassment, and gives Nell
  room to settle her Ravel conflict with Bash herself.
- **Trenn:** A deceased Ravel road guide who teaches Nell and Bash to disclose
  only one usable identity line at a time. He hides a supposedly lucky silver
  button inside his collar, dies behind the broken primary ledger, and leaves a
  sun-scratched copper gaming token that the children split into matching
  crescents.
- **Saint:** A gray mule left in Tavra's care in Ravel. His biting of a customs
  priest over Bash's red scarf becomes a private authentication memory.
- **Tavra:** A young Ravel ally who leads Saint away as Nell and Bash attempt
  their crossing and remains in Ravel afterward.
- **The Hollow Regent:** A living Ravel antagonist whose officials use a
  three-line identity system, stored records, and stolen identity-lines for
  coercion. After the resistance breaks the primary ledger, a surviving living
  identity-thread attempts to follow Bash through the Gate.
- **Willa Corst:** A Ravel resident whose given name, family name, and recognized
  home are extracted and recited by a house compelled through a living Regent
  officer's magic. The violation does not erase her identity or personhood.
- **Indexer Prax:** A living Regent official who offers Nell passage through the
  Gate in exchange for Bash's family line and recognized home.

## New locations

- **Ravel's witnessed sky:** A small blue moon rises eleven minutes before a
  larger gold moon on the night of the severed crossing. Their wider history
  and any chronological implication remain unresolved.
- **Drosswall:** A Ravel place with broken roofs, glass arrows, and walled dark
  spaces where Nell and Bash use their tap code.
- **Marn's Bridge:** A Ravel bridge above a cache of thousands of stoppered jars
  holding stolen identity-lines and the stored magic of living victims. Nell
  and Bash carry the jars into daylight.
- **The eastern salt cuts:** A Ravel district below Willa Corst's red-doored
  recognized home.
- **Modern-like home anchors:** Nell recognizes a narrow rowhouse in Frankford,
  Philadelphia, as home; Bash recognizes a second-floor apartment above a
  violin repair shop in Cambridge as home. Their reunion occurs at 30th Street
  Station. These names locate their era without establishing that it is literal
  historical Earth.

## New factions or cultural facts

- A living Ravel resistance opposes the Hollow Regent, breaks his primary
  ledger, and supplies the living magic stored and shaped by the Glass Gate's
  copper-and-glass apparatus.
- The Regent's administration includes living clerks, officers, and indexers
  who enter and search identity-lines through nonliving copper fittings,
  ledgers, houses, and jars.
- Ravel travelers under identity threat may use road names, disclose one usable
  identity line at a time, and withhold family and recognized-home lines as a
  practical defense.
- Gate Cant includes a rehearsed severance command used with living breath and
  stored charge. The phrase is a bounded operating form, not evidence that
  language or names are universally magical.
- Ravel has customs priests; one attempts to take Bash's red scarf and is bitten
  by Saint.
- Ravel food includes root rounds, remembered by the children as a rough
  comparison for modern pizza toppings; no recipe or wider custom is
  established.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- The Glass Gate can store and shape magic supplied by living travelers and
  living operators; the Gate, rails, panes, ledgers, houses, and jars are not
  established as living magic users.
- At this crossing, each traveler's living memory of departure provides a
  private temporal anchor. Nell and Bash therefore return from one shared
  Ravel departure to different modern-like dates. This is Gate-specific and
  separate from the Regent's identity system.
- The Gate restores each child to their departure age, body size, and clothing
  while preserving memory, healed Ravel scars, and small carried or worn
  objects. Restoration does not erase either child's Ravel personhood or
  trauma.
- The Gate Cant command “Living road, release the held path” can sever a charged
  crossing path when a living speaker supplies the final push. It cannot aim a
  traveler, select a date, or repair the path; severance can separate travelers
  and close their known route.
- A surviving living identity-thread can use a caught traveler and that
  traveler's remembered anchor as a route through the charged Gate. Nell's
  severance prevents the Hollow Regent's thread from following Bash.
- The Hollow Regent's bounded identity system distinguishes given name, family
  name, and recognized home as three lines. A living user can narrow a search
  with one line and coerce more deeply with all three. Nonliving records only
  hold or transmit what living users place in them.
- Nell and Bash's elapsed 617 days coexist with eleven- and twelve-second
  absences at their separate departure anchors. The cost includes separation,
  temporal mismatch, persistent scars, trauma, grief, and uncertainty rather
  than a clean reset.

## Timeline events

- Nell and Bash survive 617 days in Ravel, learn Trenn's identity defenses,
  endure Drosswall and ash-hound injuries, free stored victims' identity jars
  from beneath Marn's Bridge, and join the resistance's attack on the Hollow
  Regent's primary ledger.
- On Day 617, Trenn dies, the resistance breaks the primary ledger, Tavra takes
  Saint away from the Gate, and a remnant identity-thread catches Bash. Nell
  uses the severance command; the children's joined path breaks while each
  carries one half of Trenn's token.
- Nell returns to her February 18 departure after eleven seconds and creates an
  anonymous search post titled “The Small Moon Rose First.” She narrows it after
  false claims and harassment expose the danger of public identity clues.
- Bash returns to his June 7 departure after twelve seconds, finds Nell's
  four-month-old post, and authenticates himself through private memories.
- Nell and Bash reunite at 30th Street Station, exchange full identities
  voluntarily, and agree that neither will open, enter, or cut a future route
  without the other's knowledge.
- One year after Nell's return, both children remain in therapy and continue to
  carry trauma habits and an unresolved Gate dispute, but they re-enter the
  supermarket together.
- Ravel's placement relative to the modern-like and Glass-Sea eras remains
  unresolved; none of these events assigns it a universal date.

## New glossary terms or aliases

- **Gate Cant:** A Ravel operating language whose witnessed severance line is
  “Living road, release the held path.”
- **Temporal anchor:** For this Gate, a living traveler's private remembered
  departure to which the crossing may return that traveler.
- **Identity-line:** One of the Regent system's separately searchable identity
  components: given name, family name, or recognized home.
- **Road name:** A deliberately limited character-facing identity used while
  traveling under threat from the Regent's search system.
- **Ash-hound:** A dangerous Ravel creature that leaves lasting scars; no wider
  biology is established here.
- **Three-pause-two / one:** Nell and Bash's private tap code for “Still alive?”
  and “For now.”

## Recommended promotions

- **Promote — Ravel moons:** On the witnessed night, a small blue moon rises
  eleven minutes before a larger gold moon. Defer their history and any wider
  chronological implication.
- **Promote — Gate temporal anchors:** The Glass Gate can return travelers to
  separate private remembered departures, producing different arrival dates
  from one shared Ravel departure.
- **Promote — Gate restoration and carry-through:** This crossing restores
  departure-aged bodies and clothing while retaining memories, healed scars,
  and small carried or worn objects.
- **Promote — Gate living-input boundary:** The Gate stores and shapes magic
  supplied by living agents and has a bounded, costly severance operation; it
  is not established as alive or as part of a universal network.
- **Promote — Regent identity infrastructure:** The Hollow Regent's local system
  binds given name, family name, and recognized home as distinct searchable
  lines, with deeper coercive access when living users hold all three.
- **Promote — Ravel identity-defense practice:** Road names and deliberate
  withholding are used to resist that bounded infrastructure.
- **Story-local — people, places, and events:** Keep Nell Mercado, Sebastian
  “Bash” Wren, Celia, Trenn, Saint, Tavra, the Hollow Regent, Willa Corst,
  Indexer Prax, Drosswall, Marn's Bridge, the eastern salt cuts, the Day 617
  assault, the separated returns, the search, the reunion, and the anniversary
  aftermath attached to this story unless later continuity requires reuse.
- **Defer — unresolved mechanics and history:** Do not establish Ravel's date,
  the moons' history, the Gate's builders or full limits, a general rule for
  time travel, or a connection between these motifs and any other system.
