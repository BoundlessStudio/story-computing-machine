- This is an ordinary in-progress, non-canon story; its creative-input history creates no lifecycle, review, publication, or promotion exception. (`story.json`; `stories/INDEX.md`.)
- Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world are era-bound names for one physical world, not planets or planes. Ravel's chronological position remains unresolved. (`universe/premise.md`, “The deep-time shared world” [LOCKED]; `universe/locations.md`, “The world across eras” [LOCKED].)
- The modern-like Nell-and-Bash era may resemble contemporary Earth without establishing literal real-world Earth. Real place names may remain texture but cannot assert cosmological identity. (`universe/glossary.md`, “Modern-like era” [CANON].)
- The Glass Gate is one local crossing system linking eras of the same world. Its access, limits, directionality, builders, and paradox behavior are unestablished; adapted mechanics must remain Gate-specific and not imply a universal network. (`universe/rules.md`, “Portals and crossing systems” [CANON]; `universe/locations.md`, “The Glass Gate” [CANON].)

- Only life can use magic. Wards, ledgers, jars, doors, roads, bells, and the Gate may store, focus, transmit, or shape magic from living agency but cannot independently use it unless alive. (`universe/rules.md`, “Living agency and magic” [LOCKED].)
- Identity loss, stolen memories, reconstructed bodies, and altered selves must preserve personhood, grief, consent, relationships, and consequence. (`universe/rules.md`, “Memory, transformation, copies, and branches” [CANON]; `universe/style-guide.md`, “Personhood and moral consequence” [LOCKED].)
