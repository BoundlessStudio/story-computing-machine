## Canon posture

The final story takes place in one chronologically unresolved culture of the
shared physical world. Orison's exact era, altitude, builders, construction
date, and relationship to any established era or story remain unknown. The
Inner Sea is a new bounded local geography, not an alias for the Glass Sea or
another admitted location.

Orison's school teaching says magic died before human workers raised the city,
and residents regard Orison as their greatest wonder. These are local cultural
claims, not narrator-confirmed universal history. No magic returns during the
catastrophe. The six reactors, electrical bus, control systems, lift rings, and
records are nonliving, nonmagical, and non-agentic technology.

No character, location, faction, artifact, magical system, or event from prior
canon is named or connected to this story. Similarities involving cities,
gravity, rings, falling, seas, ancient machinery, or civic disaster establish
no shared origin or mechanism.
