# Outline

## Story

- Title: The Dress They Brought Her
- Premise and central promise: After the western-bay battle, a recovery unit brings a sparkly pink dress and wand to an unnamed tattooed inmate whom a damaged packet identifies as the surviving magical girl. She discovers why and breaks the match without becoming magical, accepting succession, performing publicly, or locating the real survivor.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Possible escape tempts her, but she will not let strangers annex her chosen body and history. Dark messy hair, dull grey eyes, tattoos, and piercings express self-authorship, never guilt or occult power.
- Counterforce or complication: The suited officer has urgency, an anonymous hazard count, and a sealed packet whose provenance looks stronger than its contents. Procedure treats her silence and intake photographs as confirmation.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past, limited to the inmate. Reveal the join through tags, thumbnails, and sequence. Never enter the officer’s mind or cut to Solstice; disclose no survivor identity, location, or plan.
- Governing movement and time shape: A two-hour loop from cell through conditional transfer and intake to a property-counter reversal. Each step toward extraction grants access to the imposed identity; stop before any public venue.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Post-battle systems retain one anonymous era-local `ultrahuman` count, not identity or location. An evacuated station scanned a heat-damaged magical-girl packet immediately before prison files. Its unreadable end separator made the reconciler absorb her first intake sheet as the identity anchor. The apparent biometric proof is her prison body map and property number, not evidence inside the sealed fragments.
- Dialogue promise: One confident misidentification meets an inmate who refuses explanation. Screens and custody movement assert authority; she withholds expected compliance and arranges evidence until the officer observes it.
- Dialogic medium: One spoken sentence, then smoke, gaze, gesture, silence, tablet prompts, property seals, thumbnails, and an intake scanner.
- Dialogue engine: The unit needs recognition; she needs property and source records before denial matters. Mistaking her actions for assent gives the officer’s evidence back to her and stops the transfer.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Close, dry, materially alert; move from ash, glitter, seams, metal, adhesive, and scanner light into inference. Withhold her offense; show selfhood through choices.
- Conversational texture: The sole spoken line is unnaturally complete against gestures, failed prompts, waiting, and physical replies. Silence is strategic, never mystical.
- Rhetorical ownership: The inmate owns gallows humor, suspicion, embarrassment, and intake knowledge. The officer owns certainty, misreading, and social authority but can listen with his eyes. The guard’s cigarette favor supplies compromised affection.
- Pressure behavior: Urgency makes him repeat options and rush; she slows down, violates expected order, and risks the transfer. Neither becomes eloquent.
- Relationship movement: He wants confirmation; she wants recognition without pleading. Evidence shifts leverage from custodial assumption to observable doubt, stopping transfer without trust.
- Anti-default: Avoid policy debate, public proof, magical awakening, or tattooed-danger shorthand. Preserve temptation, pettiness, costume awkwardness, unequal power, and a victory limited to rejecting misidentity.

## Beats

1. In her cell, the inmate drags on a cigarette bummed from the guard. A man in a suit enters, hands her the sparkly pink dress and wand, and says the only spoken sentence: "The other magical girls are dead, you are the last one."
2. She weighs cheap fabric and inert wand without performing recognition. The officer shows the packet joined to her portrait and the count of one. Escape tempts her until a thumbnail reveals an intake scale, stored piercing, and exact booking photograph.
3. A source strip shows sealed fragments and her intake sheet scanned consecutively. She indicates that transfer requires her property bag. Mistaking this for cooperation, they escort her to intake while she carries the dress badly folded.
4. At the property counter she scans her original receipt, then the displayed packet code. Raw lineage resolves both anchors to her property number and exposes the missing separator.
5. She withdraws from the transfer reader, returns dress and wand, and reruns her barcode with the packet detached. Her custody record remains; the magical packet becomes unassigned; the independent count stays one.
6. The officer preserves the scan and stops extraction. He leaves with dress and wand; she remains incarcerated under her own record. The tablet loses its human match while the passive count persists, leaving Solstice alive offstage without disclosing where.
