# The Long Way Through

The request explicitly brings our world and the world of magic into recent contact, gives both an existing love of racing, and puts Magic against Steel in a vehicle triathlon final. The short sentences suggest the pace and scale of a championship race. I want the lead to feel earned and precarious across three different surfaces. The pleasure is in watching the two sides do things the other thought impossible and recognize a good piece of driving on sight. The request leaves personal relationships and the result open.

## Provisional direction

Ione, a local champion, wants the first cross-world trophy on her own course. Corl, the visiting Steel finalist, wants it just as plainly. Three vehicles apiece, one driver per team, one running clock. The start and finish share a grandstand overlooking the road, lake and pinnacle gates. Since the crossing opened a few months ago, visitors and locals have found almost nothing they can do together without an interpreter. In the grid scrum, both racers catch a child pushing a toy through the corners on a folded course map and lean the same way as it turns. Ione recognizes the visitor's hunger for the race before they can say much to each other.

- **Land.** Ione's runners lash invisible lines to solid anchor points and haul the sled forward. The anchors shine under load. On the broken hillside she can pull up a grade that makes Corl's driven wheels scrabble. At the last hairpin she hauls straight into a stone on the inside. Corl's car gives up the inside and comes in wide, then straightens early enough to get full power down while she is still pulling herself around. He reaches the launch racks ahead.
- **Water.** Corl's powered hydrofoil skims away across open water, where Ione's skiff has no stone to pull against. Ione takes an alarming outside swing from a rock at the shore. For an instant the line pulls her away from the next marker, but she uses it to wheel her bow into the rock channel at speed. In that maze she can throw lines from rock to rock and gains on him. Corl retracts the foil to skim on his hull through a short, shallow slot, then drops it again in deep water and accelerates. He reaches the wing racks a breath ahead. Ione has a useful discovery about what the angle of the pull buys her.
- **Air.** Her wing climbs from pinnacle to pinnacle by the same magic. Ione comes level with Corl, high above him. Corl dives early into the open approach, where his propeller gives him a direct run at the gate between the final two spires. The short approach buys him first crack at the gate, which he can bank through and power out of. Ione recognizes the land hairpin. Her customary inside anchor would pull her through quickly but leave her facing rock, with a second swing to make before she runs out of anchors. She spends her altitude on a wide dive, using her lake-tested swing to arc through the gate pointed straight down the final drop. She cuts her line at the instant her wing is aimed at the finish. The dive carries her beyond the last reachable stone as Corl's powered exit eats up her advantage. Ione takes the flag by a wingtip. Corl traces her absurdly wide entry on the wing fabric afterward, then marks a line a shade wider still. She sees exactly where he means to beat her next time.
