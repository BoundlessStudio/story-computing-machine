## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] You purchase a crumbling, run down pub in rural country side, determined to bring it back to its once grandiose ways. But the pub hides an ancient secret..**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Hollow Cask
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person, centered on the purchaser-restorer.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Rural gothic fantasy with tactile restoration detail, gathering unease, and a humane resolution.
- Audience/content rating: Teen and adult; non-graphic peril.
- Required elements: A genuinely crumbling countryside pub; a protagonist who chose the purchase and personally commits to restoring it; convincing evidence of the pub's former grandeur; an ancient secret integral to both the building and the restoration; a complete resolution that tests what "bringing it back" should mean.
- Prohibited elements: A purely mundane "secret"; an ending that is only a sequel hook; treating ownership of the property as automatic moral ownership of any person or sentient being found there.

## Assumptions

- The setting may occupy an otherwise unnamed rural place and era compatible with current canon rather than attaching itself to an established location without need.
- The protagonist's gender, trade background, motive for buying the pub, and the nature of the ancient secret are open story decisions.
- "Once grandiose" means the pub had real communal importance and striking craft, not necessarily aristocratic luxury.
- Minor punctuation and phrasing irregularities in the prompt are preserved above but do not require imitation in the prose.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
