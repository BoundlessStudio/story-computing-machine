## Canon posture

The final story occupies an unnamed rural district in a chronologically
unresolved era of the one shared physical world. It names no country, kingdom,
established settlement, Galactic Cycle, or absolute date. The Hollow Cask, its
cellar colony, and its restoration remain bounded local inventions.

Only the living colony called the Underbreath supplies supernatural force. The
pub, cask-shell, hollow dowels, gutter, grille, clay branches, bellows shutter,
joinery, deed, food, smoke, and maintenance book remain nonliving materials,
routes, resources, cues, or records. The story neither calls the whole building
alive nor establishes the colony's exact degree of personhood.

No admitted canon character, location, faction, artifact, magical system, or

choice establish no crossover or common origin.
