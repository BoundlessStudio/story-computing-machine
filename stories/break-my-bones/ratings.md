# Content rating

- **Rating:** Teen
- **Content notes:** Repeated non-graphic magical bone-breaking, bodily distortion and self-restoration; threats of serious injury; a brief struggle, hooding, and restraint.
- **Reason:** The comic tone and painless recovery keep the violence restrained, but repeated skeletal collapse and threatened injury may be unsuitable for younger readers. The finished cover depicts calm wrist-setting without blood, exposed bone, or gore and does not raise the prose's intensity.
