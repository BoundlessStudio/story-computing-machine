# Editorial notes

## Request readings

**Codex promise keeper, independent.** Explicitly, “She makes both the technical intervention and the art.” It inferred the pleasure of watching a street artist “turn the most expensive surface in the city into her canvas,” with tension from secrecy, the strangeness of rediscovery, and a citywide change people can share. It read the tone as “defiant and awestruck.” The woman looks at home on the roof and the companion comfortable with her silence. Their bond, the corporation’s response, the duration of the intervention and the fate of the actual sky are open.

**Claude, independent.** The core pleasure is “a simple, real thing returned to a world where it has become a product.” The moon merits the set piece, and the city has to look up for “humanity has forgotten” to matter. Claude read “wonder and longing” in the request and a “hushed and triumphant” mood in the original. The companion reads as a familiar witness. His identity, the cost of the hack and its consequences remain open. The image contains no written instructions.

## Outline

**Codex promise keeper, first reading.** “The corporation’s sale of the sky supplies the opposition and the opportunity.” The birch and kelp strips, rollback, and held moon give Nima the choices that drive the scene. “The Needle Bend earns its place” through vibration, sightlines, and carriage windows. The keeper wanted a choice in the actual drawing and a more particular encounter with forgotten nature. It asked to establish the closed shades on a passing train and include the designer constellations in Daymark’s wares.

**Claude, first reading.** The strongest evidence was “the climax overwrites the corporation’s highest-priced moment with a real, scarred moon visible to everyone, and the ending is the city answering it.” Claude called the proof of a held image well planted and warned “the conflict could end up being about access, not about the sky.” It asked to make the top lot a smooth designer moon so the hand-painted crater field directly answers a sale. It also wanted the company’s tower in the view and the painting itself to get time in the long scene.
