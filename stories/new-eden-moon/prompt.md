# Prompt

In the sprawling metropolis of New Eden, the night sky is controlled by a corporation that sells artificial sunsets, designer constellations, and moonlight to the highest bidders. A young street artist secretly hacks the atmospheric projection network, replacing advertisements with visions of the beautiful natural world humanity has forgotten. She paints a gigantic full moon above the city
