## Canon posture

The final prose presents one wholly standalone terminal-era event in which six
distinct magical systems exhaust every kind of magic active in the present
material world. The event is undated and geographically unplaced relative to
all admitted stories. It names no admitted character, location, artifact,
institution, title, system, crisis, or historical episode, and it establishes
no crossover or shared origin.

The story's terminal claim is exact but bounded. Living capacity, active
workings, stored charge, magical structures, material-world crossing anchors,
faculties, hybrids, reserves, relics, inheritance, recoverable patterns, and
every material restoration possibility end permanently. Earlier history
remains real. The prose makes no claim about the universal afterlife, other
realities, earlier eras, alternate timelines, or destinations beyond closed
material-world crossings.

The Ithrom Array, eight relics, six sockets, world-curves, and terminal
junction are non-character mechanisms. Living authorities arm the six
system-specific conditions while magic is active. Faris's spoken declaration
supplies witness, not power; his ordinary cotter pull releases the already
armed conditions. No object chooses, consents, judges, casts independently, or
survives as a magical seed.
