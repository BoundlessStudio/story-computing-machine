# Prompt contract

> Contract status: CAPTURED
> Story slug: `all-accounts-due`
> Captured: 2026-08-05

## Verbatim writing prompt

> [WP] You have no magic, but as you've helped others you've amassed tons of favors, artifacts, etc. Today everyone woke up to find thier max mana is zero.
> - *House of M* - "No More Mutants" style of event but for magic instead.

## Story controls

- Working title: All Accounts Due
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Chosen during planning for strongest dramatic effect.
- Tense: Chosen during planning for strongest dramatic effect.
- Tone and genre: Character-centered crisis fantasy with urgent, consequential stakes.
- Audience/content rating: Teen.
- Required elements: A protagonist who has never had magic; a history of helping
  others that has yielded favors, relationships, and artifacts; a sudden event
  in which everyone wakes with maximum mana at zero; and a climax in which the
  protagonist's accumulated nonmagical capital matters decisively.
- Prohibited elements: No direct use of Marvel characters, settings, or plot;
  the *House of M* note controls the event's scale and emotional premise only.

## Assumptions

- "Everyone" is treated as the people reached by the story's bounded setting
  and evidence, not as license to declare an unverified universe-wide rule.
- The cause of the zero-mana event may remain unknown if explaining it would
  require inventing global canon.
- Artifacts may retain stored charge or useful nonmagical functions only if
  current canon permits it; they do not become autonomous magic users.
- The protagonist's decisive advantage should come from earned trust,
  practical knowledge, and reciprocal obligations, not a concealed magical
  awakening.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The zero-mana event has immediate practical and emotional consequences rather
  than functioning only as background exposition.
- At least one favor and one previously acquired artifact pay off causally.
- The protagonist remains genuinely nonmagical through the ending.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
