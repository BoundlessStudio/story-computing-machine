# Story plan — All Accounts Due

## Story promise

When every reachable magic user in flood-prone Gannet Reach wakes with a locally measured maximum mana of zero, lifelong nonmagical fixer Faris must keep the failed lift-pumps from drowning Lowbank. The rescue pays off the ordinary trust, favors, access, and odd artifacts she earned by helping people before anyone knew help would be scarce. She never gains or channels magic; she wins time for one district through practical knowledge and relationships whose owners remain free to refuse.

## Story controls

- Target: 3,300 words within the 2,500–4,000-word contract.
- POV/tense: close third person, past tense, fixed on Faris.
- Genre/tone: character-centered infrastructure-crisis fantasy; urgent, tactile, wry under pressure, hopeful without promising recovery.
- Rating: Teen. Flood danger, panic, and non-graphic injury risk; no graphic harm.
- Scope: one morning in the new settlement of Gannet Reach, chiefly its Lowbank catchment, in an unnamed era. No established location, faction, character, artifact, or chronology is imported.
- Required resolution: stabilize Lowbank before the noon river rise. Do not restore magic, identify the outage cause, or establish its reach beyond local evidence.
- Prohibition: no Marvel characters, setting, language, or plot analogue beyond the captured mass-loss premise.

## Bounded mana model and evidence

- **Local term:** Reach practitioners call the highest amount of power a living user can presently supply to their local conductive workings “maximum mana.” It is a municipal calibration category, not an objective description of all magic.
- **What zero means:** Under every Reach test shown, no tested person can supply accessible power. The story cannot determine whether capacity is gone, blocked, suppressed, disconnected, or merely inaccessible to these methods.
- **Evidence at dawn:** the municipal account board shows zero for every connected resident gauge; twenty-seven people from five locally distinct trades repeat zero on independent hand testers; Tycho cannot produce a regulated test pulse; healer, lampwright, and pump workings all fail when their living users attempt them.
- **Evidence limits:** a board or gauge could fail, so independent attempts matter; magical long-distance relays are dead; an upstream flag station reports dark public workings but no capacity data. “Everyone” in character speech means every reachable/tested Reach practitioner, not every being or system elsewhere.
- **Immediate consequences:** lift-pumps stop, lamps and clinic sterilizers lose magical functions, powered lifts stall, and magic users suffer fear and loss of practiced identity. Mundane gravity, fire, tools, boats, muscles, records, and mechanical devices still work.
- **Cause/duration:** unknown at opening, climax, and ending. No theory is endorsed and no recovery occurs on-page.

## Freely chosen favors

- Favors are remembered acts and invitations, never contracts or magical debts. Faris asks plainly; each person may refuse, renegotiate, or cap the risk.
- **Tycho:** Faris once kept falsified pump readings from being blamed on him. He freely supplies engineering knowledge and labor, but first refuses an untested gate opening.
- **Uma:** Faris testified when a toll clerk tried to seize her barge. Uma refuses to enter the submerged lower channel, then offers her barge, capstan, and crew from the safe upper quay.
- **Zeke:** Faris organized hand-carried cold boxes during a clinic breakdown. Zeke will not abandon patients; he instead creates an evacuation chain, loans mundane medical gear, and sends off-duty volunteers who individually agree.
- Payoff principle: no single favor or artifact solves the crisis. Faris’s decisive competence is matching people, limits, access, map, rope, boat, and timing into a workable response.

## Character engine

- **Faris — protagonist:** lifelong nonmagical neighborhood fixer and courier. Want: keep Lowbank alive and prove she is useful when the magical order fails. Need: stop treating favors as accounts that make asking safe; accept that trust must be renewed by free choices. Pressure: water rises while people who once patronized her now expect certainty. False tactic: present every request as repayment so refusal cannot feel personal. Arc: collector of obligations → coordinator who asks honestly → community member willing to owe and be helped.
- **Tycho — pump adept:** his authority, craft identity, and confidence vanish with his reading. Want: diagnose and preserve the system before Faris improvises around it. Need: separate expertise from magical capacity and follow a nonmagical lead without surrendering judgment.
- **Uma — barge captain:** values crew safety above civic prestige. Want: help Faris without letting gratitude become permission to gamble her crew. Her negotiated boundary makes the rescue safer.
- **Zeke — clinic steward:** triages people before machinery. Want: keep the clinic staffed and move vulnerable residents uphill. His refusal prevents Faris from treating helpers like pieces, while his volunteer chain supplies the necessary hands.

## Story-specific style profile

- **Dominant voice:** practical, observant, dryly self-protective. Faris notices watermarks, leverage, worn fasteners, routes, and who has previously carried what for whom. Syntax tightens during physical risk; longer sentences hold competing tasks and her private accounting.
- **Distance/register:** close enough to inherit Faris’s ledger metaphors and omissions, drawing closest when she must ask without leverage. Compress travel and repeated recruitment; expand choices, failures, and the manual gate operation.
- **Dialogue pressure:** conversations negotiate pace, authority, risk, and whether a favor grants control. Ethical meaning appears through refusals being honored and plans changing, not policy speeches.
- **Image sources:** rising water against old marks, hands on tools, unequal weights, knots, doors, and crossed-out accounts. Use one restrained account/ledger motif; do not turn social trust into literal magic.
- **Speculative texture:** show failed magic through attempted work and material consequence before terminology. Keep the crisis technical but legible.
- **Intentional departure:** withhold cosmic explanation and magical restoration. The earned ending is local survival plus a changed relationship to reciprocity, not reassurance about the world.

## Dialogue profiles

- **Faris:** wants agreement quickly; bargains with remembered specifics, short clauses, route/tool vocabulary, and dry understatement. Refuses to say she is frightened of being useless or refused. When leverage fails, becomes bluntly honest and offers alternatives. With old friends, jokes can mask a request; she would not naturally deliver an abstract speech about community.
- **Tycho:** wants orderly diagnosis and retained authority; speaks in measured conditional chains and names tolerances, pressures, and failure states exactly. Keeps his fear of permanent ordinariness vague. Resistance shifts him from credentialed correction to sharp questions, then concise collaboration. He would not promise safety he cannot calculate.
- **Uma:** wants a bounded risk; uses concrete verbs, distances, load limits, and decisive pauses. Avoids sentimental acknowledgment of what Faris did for her. Pressure makes her more exact, not louder; once a boundary is honored, she volunteers more than requested. She would not call recklessness courage.
- **Zeke:** wants bodies moved before arguments finish; terse triage questions, counts, and names of ordinary supplies. Hides exhaustion and affection behind assignments. When pressed, he refuses once without softening, then offers a narrower option. He would not frame patients as acceptable losses or favors as ownership.

## Causal scene sequence

### 1. Zero morning — 450 words

- Open on the pump house bell clanging mechanically while the lift-pumps remain silent and runoff climbs the Lowbank wall.
- Faris’s personal gauge reads its lifelong zero; Tycho’s and every connected account now match it. Independent hand tests and failed workings establish the bounded evidence.
- Faris spots that the catchment will overtop before noon. Tycho wants diagnosis and official authorization; she insists the physical water will not wait.
- **Incompatible aims/tactic change:** Faris wants immediate manual action; Tycho wants controlled testing and to protect the pumps. She shifts from citing the waterline to asking him for one falsifiable deadline calculation; he shifts from rank to calculation and confirms the emergency.
- **Turn:** Tycho’s result makes waiting untenable, but the usual magically driven drain has no user power.

### 2. What still works — 500 words

- In Faris’s crowded storeroom, she and Tycho test items individually rather than assume a class rule. The whisper bead and sling enchantment fail; the plate, key, and sling’s base fiber remain useful within stated limits.
- The survey plate shows an abandoned gravity-relief sluice from the catchment to a lower river outfall. It was sealed after powered pumps made it obsolete.
- **Incompatible aims/tactic change:** Faris wants to treat each gift as a possible tool; Tycho wants to discard “dead” magical objects and return to familiar diagnostics. Faris stops defending the collection and makes him assess one mechanism at a time; Tycho shifts from ridicule to engineering annotation.
- **Turn:** they form a viable manual plan, but it needs river access, a capstan, medical evacuation, and many hands.

### 3. Calls without compulsion — 550 words

- Faris asks Uma and Zeke separately, naming old favors as context but giving each a real exit. Inert communications force face-to-face requests and mundane runners.
- Uma refuses the submerged route, then offers a safer upper-quay pull if Faris accepts her load ceiling. Zeke refuses to leave the clinic, then organizes uphill evacuation and invites off-duty volunteers without ordering them.
- **Incompatible aims/tactic changes:** Faris wants maximal help now; Uma wants a crew-safe boundary and changes refusal into a counteroffer after Faris accepts “no.” Faris wants Zeke at the gate; Zeke wants patients prioritized, and Faris shifts from repayment language to asking what help he can freely spare.
- **Turn:** the limits initially feel like less help, but produce a safer barge position and protect residents before the gate attempt.

### 4. The obsolete throat — 650 words

- The team crosses a ward of stalled lifts and bucket brigades. The brass key opens the upper inspection throat; the plate supplies elevations and keeps them out of the flooded lower chamber.
- Tycho discovers the gate can move mechanically, but the counterweight is seized and the designed magical assist cannot engage. Water is now entering the clinic’s lower street.
- Faris proposes a direct capstan pull. Tycho calculates it would shear the gate and block the sluice permanently—the major reversal.
- **Incompatible aims/tactic change:** Faris wants motion before the deadline; Tycho wants no irreversible structural failure. She begins issuing instructions, then yields the calculation and asks him to design within Uma’s load limit; he stops using impossibility as a shield and identifies a two-stage lift-and-release.
- **Turn:** success now requires the stormweave sling placed by hand around the counterweight and coordinated human releases inside the throat.

### 5. All accounts due — 800 words

- Uma rigs the sling as ordinary fiber and stations the barge capstan; Tycho calls measured pulls; Zeke’s volunteers hold bucket lines, carry an injured worker out, and maintain evacuation. Faris enters the cramped throat to set the mechanical dog and clear grit with hand tools.
- First pull slips because the sling cannot magically self-tighten. Uma halts rather than exceed her boundary. Faris admits she has no hidden leverage and asks everyone whether they will try Tycho’s slower re-rig.
- Each chooses again. Uma knots a mundane choke, Tycho abandons magical terminology for clear mechanical timing, Zeke rotates exhausted hands, and Faris uses the plate’s depth marks and key as a temporary alignment gauge—not as spell components.
- **Incompatible aims/tactic changes:** Faris wants one fast all-in effort; Uma protects load and crew, Tycho protects the gate, Zeke protects bodies. Faris shifts from calling debts to distributing vetoes; their counteroffers become a staged pull with stop signals and rotations.
- **Climax/turn:** people, capstan, map, access, sling, timing, and trust open the relief gate. Gravity takes the floodwater. Faris contributes no magic and receives none.

### 6. The open account — 350 words

- Lowbank stabilizes; damage and uncertainty remain. All repeat tests still read zero, distant scope is unknown, and public works must operate mundanely for now.
- Faris tries to strike the favors from her notebook as paid. Uma objects that she helped by choice, Tycho asks Faris to help redesign the bypass, and Zeke sends her uphill to eat and rest.
- **Incompatible aims/tactic change:** Faris wants clean closure that leaves her owing no one; the others want continuing reciprocal relationship. She deflects with accounting humor, then accepts food, future work, and an unbalanced page.
- **Ending image:** beside the falling waterline, Faris closes the notebook on accounts that will not balance while ordinary hands keep the manual gate turning.

## Stakes, reversals, and ending

- **Immediate stakes:** Lowbank homes and clinic flood by noon; stalled lifts and dark services multiply injury risk.
- **Personal stakes:** Faris fears the newly powerless will still see her as lesser, while Tycho fears losing the identity that made him authoritative.
- **Moral stakes:** treating favors as collectible debts would reproduce coercion during crisis; honoring refusal must materially alter the rescue.
- **Reversals:** zero is corroborated rather than a board fault; several magical artifacts fail while passive/mechanical features survive individually; helpers refuse Faris’s first requests but offer safer help; the direct pull would destroy the sluice; the sling slips without living mana and requires a freely renewed plan.
- **Resolution:** the team prevents a local flood through gravity and coordinated labor. The event remains unexplained, unresolved, and unbounded beyond evidence.
- **Arc/ending:** Faris remains genuinely nonmagical and moves from insulating herself with accounts to accepting uncertain reciprocity. Final image is practical work continuing, not a thematic epigram.

## Canon constraints and local inventions

- Binding: only life uses magic; no artifact originates, chooses, or casts. Each artifact’s function and failure is individual.
- Binding: favors remain ordinary, voluntary relationships. No debt, consent, sacrifice, trust, or desire becomes fuel.
- Binding: `maximum mana` is Reach terminology; no universal mana ontology, shared source, or universe-wide conclusion.
- Binding: no resemblance licenses use of Adrian’s severance, Solstice’s crown, the Glass Gate, Fenwick watch, Ersene focuses, Aven’s harness, Sablewake, or any named canon mechanism.
- Local inventions only: Gannet Reach and Lowbank; municipal user gauges and conductive test practice; magically assisted lift-pumps; obsolete gravity-relief sluice; the four artifacts; the characters and their prior ordinary favors.
- Unknowns preserved: event cause, geographic/cosmological reach, permanence, effect on untested systems or beings, and whether zero reflects loss or inaccessibility.
- Later gates: coordinator registers all planned names before drafting; final prose receives name reconciliation and current-authority continuity review.

## Drafting failure modes to watch

- Do not turn Faris’s zero into immunity, special power, destiny, or concealed magic.
- Do not let stored charge, generic artifact rules, or unexplained autonomous effects solve the gate.
- Do not claim literal universal scope or answer the outage mystery in narration, theory, or final image.
- Keep hydraulic action spatially causal and load limits consistent; expand the failed pull and changed plan, compress errands.
- Let refusals cost time and improve the plan; do not make helpers compliant after token objections.
- Avoid exposition relays, policy-shaped dialogue, uniform wit, or a closing speech that states the lesson.
