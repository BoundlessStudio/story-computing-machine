# Story plan — All Accounts Due

## Revision mandate and story promise

This plan wholly replaces the rejected Gannet Reach version. Exactly nine years after the Glass-Sea second dawn, every living user of magic in the present material world loses all magical capacity, permanently. Lifelong nonmagical Faris has spent those nine years carrying records, repairing mundane routes, honoring revocations, and accumulating favors and artifacts across the seven independent solar branches. When the last magical crossings, wards, archives, waterworks, and habitats fail together, she must call those relationships in without treating them as debts. Her choice determines whether the first nonmagical civilization inherits distributed water, seed, routes, memory, and accountable public authority—or repeats Queen Aurelia's ancient choice by preserving one capital at everyone else's expense.

The victory is not restoration. No magic survives in a person, god, focus, artifact, reserve, hidden system, or autonomous substitute. Faris's ordinary network gets enough people and material knowledge moving that the world has a first future after magic rather than only an ending.

## Story controls

- **Target:** 4,500–6,000 words; planned draft about 5,400 words.
- **POV/tense:** close third person, past tense, fixed on Faris. The final scene may widen for one brief proleptic paragraph that reports later material consequences without entering another mind.
- **Genre/tone:** world-ending epic fantasy with an elegiac, cumulative pressure; intimate labor carries civilizational scale. Awe comes from irreversible loss and the weight of prior ages, not spectacle alone.
- **Rating:** Teen. Mass danger, displacement, ecological loss, and non-graphic deaths may be acknowledged; the camera stays on choice, labor, and aftermath.
- **Chronology:** opens at sunrise on the exact ninth anniversary of the second dawn and runs for thirty-one hours. It is later than all canon deliberately placed in the Glass-Sea past. No claim is made about the order of undated stories.
- **Terminal scope:** all magic used by life throughout the present living material world ends forever. Earlier eras and acts remain real. The story makes no claim about alternate timelines, other worlds or planes, the universal afterlife, or the condition of destinations beyond inert present-world crossing endpoints.
- **Cause:** unknown and not secretly recoverable. The narration authoritatively establishes permanence; characters do not derive a universal ontology from one measurement.
- **“Maximum mana”:** the seven branches' familiar capacity language and Faris's lifelong gauge reading, not a universal substance or explanation. Other cultures experience their differently understood systems ceasing without adopting the term.

## Character engine and arcs

- **Faris — lifelong nonmagical protagonist:** She has never had magic, was not severed from it, and is neither immune nor chosen. Want: prove that the ordinary redundancies and relationships she built matter when every grand system fails. Fear: that her usefulness is only revenge on people who once discounted her. False tactic: invoke old help as an account now due so refusal cannot wound her. Need: ask plainly, accept limits, and relinquish ownership of the network she made. Arc: keeper of private obligations -> emergency coordinator tempted by singular authority -> author of a distributed plan that no one, including her, can own.
- **The Daughter:** Her living solar fire ends without killing her, undoing her scars, absolving Queen Aurelia, or erasing four centuries of choices. She moves from being treated as the missing power source to choosing ordinary witness, carrying, and care. Worship may persist, but no claim of godhood can rest on power after zero; the story does not retroactively settle whether she was taxonomically a god.
- **Tovan Rek:** The local expression loses branch/archive access but retains embodied memory, agency, and his claimed identity. He refuses to speak for the six other diverged expressions. His arc is from feared reduction to “the last complete archive” toward publishing one bounded, incomplete person's testimony while omissions remain visible.
- **Orrin Pell:** His living pale-metal interfaces fall silent; his knowledge, body, choice, and ordinary tool use remain. He refuses both an old central-service logic and Faris's first overly commanding request, then freely chooses manual work whose authorship is his.
- **Sister Calvea Dorn:** Dusk no longer answers her, but old costs and scars remain. She proves her consent practice was never merely a spell component by rebuilding it as public allocation procedure after magic is gone.
- **Seris Venn:** Supporting institutional counterweight. She uses temporary stewardship to prevent a capital seizure, then makes her own authority expire under witnessed terms rather than becoming the new permanent center.

## Story-specific style profile

- **Dominant voice:** Faris notices loads, temperatures, missing names, road grades, who carried what, and which promise included an exit. Her ledger habit colors selection without turning friendship into literal currency.
- **Narrative distance:** close and dryly practical during triage; closer still when she risks refusal or feels the shame of being glad she knows how to live at zero. Distance widens briefly for the terminal event's global reach and the final prolepsis, while never entering another character's private thought.
- **Rhythm:** longer cumulative sentences hold centuries of infrastructure and simultaneous failures; short sentences are reserved for zero, refusals, and irreversible releases. Avoid a chain of polished epigrams.
- **Dialogue pressure:** arguments concern pace, jurisdiction, whose evidence counts, and whether catastrophe cancels prior limits. Ethics appear in stopped actions, narrower asks, public counts, returned credentials, and accepted refusals—not policy speeches.
- **Image sources:** ordinary sunlight versus vanished living fire; warmth leaving glass and stone; water finding gravity; names surviving on paper and steps; seven unequal routes; hands formerly luminous now blistered by ordinary work.
- **Epic/elegiac departure:** allow restrained ritual repetition around the six restored city names and the first/second dawn imagery. Keep mechanisms tactile, casualties non-graphic, and grief mixed with administrative nuisance and labor.
- **Ending discipline:** no explanatory theory, restored spark, secret archive pulse, or consoling hint. The final image changes an established image—open hands once offered solar fire; now they plant and carry under an ordinary sun.

## Major-speaker dialogue profiles

- **Faris:** Wants a usable answer and defaults to bargain, remembered detail, and clipped route/tool diction. Keeps her fear of refusal and momentary vindication indirect. Resistance first makes her sound like a collector; then she names the choice, cost, and exit. With the Daughter she drops dry humor. She would not naturally call reciprocity a form of magic.
- **The Daughter:** Wants people to stop treating capacity as permission. Measured, formal clauses shaped by covenants, heat, hands, and inherited blame; silence precedes direct refusals. At zero her speech becomes plainer, not grander. With Seris she allows unfinished questions. She would not claim powerlessness absolves her mother or herself.
- **Tovan:** Wants exact scope and continued recognition as one divergent person. Uses archival qualifiers, source distinctions, and corrections; under fear he over-specifies gaps. Resistance shifts him from citation to a personal “I know / I do not know.” He never naturally speaks for the other expressions or calls incompleteness death.
- **Orrin:** Wants chosen work separated from directive. Concrete maintenance vocabulary, short diagnostic clauses, little ornament; becomes painfully literal when a request echoes compulsion. After refusal is honored, he offers alternatives without ceremony. He would not call himself a tool, even metaphorically.
- **Calvea:** Wants risks and authority named before action. Deliberate questions, household counts, physical limits, and pauses that force others to answer. When urgency is used against her, she refuses; when Faris accepts delay, she accelerates the public process. She would not equate consent with magical efficacy.
- **Seris:** Wants protection without replacing protected people's authority. Terse conditional commitments, time limits, witness names, and status vocabulary; she initially interposes herself physically, then changes to procedural leverage. With the Daughter, touch and withheld touch carry old intimacy. She would not promise permanent stewardship.

## Causal scene sequence

### 1. The ninth second dawn — 700 words; sunrise to minute fifteen

- In the Crown of Noon chamber, now opened once yearly as a witnessed record hall rather than a command center, the seven branches mark exactly nine years since decentralization. Faris is present with her route fan, jack, tally, and a battered personal ledger; one local Tovan stands beside six branch-presence windows. The Daughter begins the same bounded one-hour greenhouse covenant that followed the second dawn.
- At sunrise, every capacity slate in the branch vocabulary falls to zero. The Daughter's living fire vanishes; Calvea cannot call dusk; Tovan's six windows cut off; Orrin's interfaces go silent; the black-sun key becomes cold inert matter. An unnamed visiting practitioner from another tradition fails by that tradition's own test. A same-era material-world fold-door collapses into an ordinary stone arch, stranding travelers without implying any link to the Glass Gate, another era, or another plane.
- Physical consequences arrive rather than a spectacle montage: shield cooling stops, the greenhouse temperature begins falling, lifts freeze, cistern gates stay shut, medical workings cease, and a transformed visitor's existing material body and old scars remain even though no further working answers.
- Faris's gauge still reads the zero it has read all her life. This is no immunity or clue; for the first time, everyone shares her limit.
- **Incompatible aims / tactic change:** Faris wants immediate mundane inventory; the Daughter and Tovan each need one final verification before surrendering lifelong categories. Faris stops saying “nothing works” and asks each for one bounded test; after those fail independently, they stop testing and report exact losses.
- **Turn:** the story's narrative frame states that the silence is world-wide and final even though no character can yet prove forever. Magic has ended; infrastructure has not finished failing.

### 2. What the world was leaning on — 650 words; minute fifteen to second hour

- Orrin maps the causal deadlines: six hours before the Ninth Hollow seed-house drops below its safe range; four before magical pumps leave upper cisterns inaccessible; one tide before stranded crossing traffic blocks the last ordinary roads. Tovan retains yesterday's embodied memory but cannot query the archive or other expressions.
- Last mechanical signal flags and arriving runners bring separate present-world evidence: a weather-working coast has lost storm control, a distant heat-dependent refuge is evacuating, a living healer can no longer close wounds, and another material crossing endpoint is inert. These reports remain culturally distinct, establish consequence rather than common origin, and do not name unresolved-era casts.
- Capital continuance stewards propose locking the central water and seed reserve behind guards until authority can be restored. Their fear is materially credible: distributing now may shorten the capital's survival. Seris accepts stewardship only until the third public bell, preventing seizure while a witnessed plan is heard.
- Faris lays out the alternative: open gravity feeds, copy verified memory, divide seed, mark nonmagical routes, and dispatch the same five-part survival account—people, water, seed, routes, and witnesses—from seven independent branches. It must leave today or cascading failures will make choice irrelevant.
- **Incompatible aims / tactic change:** Faris wants immediate distributed release; Seris wants a legitimate interval; the stewards want centralized certainty. Faris shifts from “you owe the outer branches” to a deadline-and-cost demonstration; Seris shifts from guarding the door to granting a revocable hearing.
- **Turn:** Faris has a world-scale design but no authority to compel the four people and many communities it requires.

### 3. One voice, not seven — 600 words; second to fourth hour

- With magical archive access gone, officials try to appoint local Tovan as the “complete” Keeper Below. The pressure threatens his personhood and would turn his partial record into singular authority.
- A compressed memory of Faris's year-two paper circuit seeds their relationship. Present Faris asks too broadly; Tovan refuses to represent other expressions. She apologizes without smoothing the emergency and narrows the request.
- Tovan freely dictates only what he personally retains: copied witness overlaps, the six restored city names, branch dependencies, and routes current as of the previous night. He marks conflicts and blanks. Faris uses the route fan to separate magical shortcuts from physical roads and gravity water.
- Ordinary semaphore confirms only that three other expressions remain alive; none is shown taking command from him. The remaining expressions are unconfirmed, not declared dead.
- **Incompatible aims / tactic change:** Faris wants complete actionable data; Tovan wants bounded identity and epistemic honesty. She moves from urgency to source-by-source questions; he moves from formal refusal to first-person testimony.
- **Turn:** the memory packet can travel without magic because incompleteness is preserved, but the physical seed and water stores are still locked.

### 4. The hollow keeps its no — 750 words; fourth to ninth hour

- Faris, Orrin, Calvea, the Daughter, and unnamed witnesses descend by stairs to the Ninth Hollow; dead lifts and the inert fold-door make the route slow. The greenhouse is cooling. A bounded magical vine loses its light and wilts while ordinary seed remains viable if moved now; this case does not settle every magical species' biology.
- Calvea's failed dusk working leaves old burns, dimmed sight, and grief intact. She refuses Faris's first demand for immediate release because households have not consented to surrender winter water and seed.
- While Calvea convenes a fast public count, Orrin tests the seed-house gate. His interface gives no answer. Faris's demand that he “open the system” echoes the Crown directive; he refuses. She names the mechanical job, offers him the outside position, and crawls under the gate herself.
- The split-shaft jack's dead self-centering collar slips. Nothing catches it magically. Orrin resets it by hand, Faris takes the load, wedges follow each ratchet tooth, and household witnesses halt the work once to revise how much water may leave.
- **Incompatible aims / tactic change:** Faris wants maximum release before cooling; Calvea wants current household authority; Orrin wants chosen bounded work. Faris stops treating limits as delay, separates three asks, and accepts a smaller initial allotment. Calvea changes from refusal to expedited count; Orrin changes from withdrawal to precise manual leadership.
- **Turn:** the gravity bypass opens and seed crates emerge, but the capital stewards move to seize the outgoing road before the third bell.

### 5. Authority after power — 650 words; ninth to twelfth hour

- At the surface dispatch yard, frightened worshipers ask the Daughter to try harder and capital guards ask Seris to extend her stewardship. The Daughter demonstrates once—no solar heat, no hidden ember—then refuses further self-harm. Her loss is neither death nor absolution; Queen Aurelia's choice, the six cities, and the second dawn remain historical facts.
- Faris invokes the year-eight testimony circuit. The seven-mark tally opens only mundane dispatch drawers; it cannot command the guards or certify truth. Seris reads its conditional authority and requires branch witnesses to countersign each release.
- The Daughter rejects an offered emergency crown and joins the seed line. Calvea treats powerless practitioners as residents with skills and needs, not failed instruments. Orrin insists that his hands are still his. Tovan removes the religious title from the packet's authority line and signs as himself.
- **Incompatible aims / tactic change:** Faris wants Seris to stop the seizure outright; Seris wants authority to end on time; the Daughter wants room to grieve without being made symbol or battery. Faris shifts from asking for protection to proposing a witnessed transfer; Seris lets her temporary office expire into that process; the Daughter shifts from refusal to ordinary participation.
- **Turn:** no central office owns the release. This legitimacy costs speed, leaving one daylight interval to activate the physical carrier network.

### 6. All accounts due — 1,250 words; twelfth to twentieth hour

- The climax braids five simultaneous, spatially clear tasks at the dispatch yard, cistern gate, copy room, signal tower, and departing roads. Faris moves between them but does not personally perform everyone's work.
- **Artifact payoffs:** route-fan rubbings identify seven viable land/water corridors and gravity-fed wells; the jack remains in the converted hand gate while teams fill water casks; the tally opens each copy drawer and is dismantled mark by mark as local witnesses assume control.
- **Relationship payoffs:** Tovan dictates bounded memory and refuses invented certainty; Orrin supervises the manual gate; Calvea and households set water/seed limits; Seris makes the handoff public; the Daughter carries the first crate. Unnamed carriers and signal wardens whom Faris helped over nine years are asked, not ordered. Some refuse dangerous routes, and their safer counteroffers change loads and destinations.
- Major reversal: the fastest route sheet still assumes two fold-doors. Faris's first dispatch would strand seed at dead arches. She catches the dependency in Tovan's marked blank, accepts the lost hour, and substitutes slower salt roads from the copper elevations. A second reversal follows when the capital can retain either its exclusive winter reserve or enough gravity head to send water outward, not both.
- Faris is offered continuing custody of the tally and authority to choose alone. Her old ledger of favors could make the claim plausible. She instead reads each prior help as context, explicitly releases every debt, and asks for renewed choices. This is not magic and does not make agreement unanimous.
- The irreversible decision is made under witness: open the gravity head, divide seed stock, publish the incomplete archive, and send route packets beyond all seven branches. The capital loses monopoly and some short-term security; outer settlements receive a chance rather than a guarantee. Once water drops, crates depart, tally marks separate, and copies leave by different roads, no crown or single destroyed packet can recall the decision.
- Ordinary flags propagate the five-part account to visible stations; runners and caravans carry it farther. Each recipient is instructed to correct locally, preserve earlier versions, name witnesses, and copy outward. This makes the response capable of world-scale spread without instant communication or universal command.
- **Incompatible aims / tactic changes:** Faris wants one fast coordinated answer; every helper protects a different limit—source truth, mechanical safety, household consent, temporary authority, or bodily agency. Faris changes from collecting compliance to distributing vetoes. Resistance changes the others from refusal into bounded counteroffers; no one recites a shared lesson.
- **Climactic turn:** the first seven convoys leave with people, water, seed, routes, memory, and accountable procedures. Magic remains wholly absent. Faris's decisive power is giving away access, credit, and centrality.

### 7. The first ordinary dawn — 800 words; twentieth to thirty-first hour

- Overnight, consequence stays causal and embodied. Clinics work by lamplight and trained hands; shield districts cool; crossings remain stone; travelers become road crews and carriers; focus stones are labeled as inert material; the greenhouse loses heat-dependent growth but saved seed reaches ordinary soil. Death and displacement are acknowledged without graphic inventory.
- Political and spiritual changes begin rather than resolve cleanly: former power-based offices lose leverage; worshippers may keep prayer and memory, but no powerless being satisfies godhood through power; Seris is replaced on schedule; allocations and errors remain public. The story does not declare the Daughter once was or was not a god.
- Identity survives faculty. Calvea remains Calvea without dusk. Orrin and Tovan remain living persons, not broken tools or dead data. The Daughter's earlier miracles, scars, relationships, and responsibility remain real. The Mother-fragment does not return. No prior casualty, transformation, rescue, or regime is rewritten.
- A brief proleptic paragraph follows the physical packets through copying, correction, famine, migration, rebuilt wells, seed exchanges, road cultures, changed borders, and memorial practice. Some communities fail; many survive because records and resources were divided. Inert crossing arches become landmarks on long roads. The afterlife and any unconnected realms remain unclaimed. The narration states without qualification that magic never returns to the material world—no reserve, birth, relic, god, machine, or future discovery restores it.
- Faris closes her private favor ledger but leaves the survival account open for corrections. She keeps no tally mark. Under the first wholly ordinary sunrise, the Daughter's once-luminous hands press seed into cold soil while wagon wheels carry copies past the steps bearing the six restored city names.
- **Final conversational pressure / tactic change:** Faris wants to call the account settled; the Daughter rejects settlement that erases ongoing obligation. Faris tries one dry accounting joke, lets it fail, and changes from closing the public record to adding a blank page.
- **Ending image:** open powerless hands, ordinary seed, old names still legible, and seven roads carrying unequal loads away from a dead Crown.

## Stakes, reversals, and causal resolution

- **Immediate:** hours before water becomes unreachable, seed dies in cooling stores, clinics and shelters fail, and dead crossings choke ordinary roads.
- **Civilizational/world:** every culture must replace magical medicine, climate control, defense, communication, transport, prophecy, and material processing while systems fail at different physical rates.
- **Personal:** Faris must resist turning lifelong exclusion into entitlement; every former practitioner must survive loss without having personhood reduced to function.
- **Political/moral:** centralized certainty can save more of one capital by sacrificing unknown others; distributed uncertainty preserves agency, evidence, and wider chances at real cost.
- **Reversals:** zero is not a board fault; iconic Glass-Sea systems offer no reserve; Tovan cannot be one archive; consent slows then improves allocation; the jack loses magical alignment but works manually; the fast routes depend on dead crossings; the capital reserve cannot both stay exclusive and flow outward; Faris must surrender the credential that makes her plan possible.
- **Resolution:** physical distribution plus replicable records establishes the first nonmagical survival network. It changes who lives, what history survives, how water and seed move, which routes replace crossings, and whether emergency power remains contestable. It does not prevent all loss or make Faris sovereign.

## Canon constraints, chronology boundaries, and local inventions

- Preserve the one deep-time physical world and the established order: modern-like low-magic history precedes the Glass-Sea era; this story is exactly nine years after second dawn. Earlier magic and its consequences remain true.
- The terminal event reaches all living magic in the present material world only. It does not retroactively alter earlier eras or establish effects in alternate timelines, afterlives, planes, other worlds, or chronologically unresolved Realms/Glass Gate localities.
- Exclude live unresolved-era crossovers: no Solstice or Northbridge cast, Ravel traveler, Queen Street/Deepmarket cast, afterlife or Cinder Annex cast, Ersene/Qarsis/Bellweather/Sablewake/Copperwell/Lacebridge/eleven-foundation cast, or other unplaced identity appears.
- No bounded prior mechanism—severance, Gate Cant, crown, watch, breach, central release, oath, glass, or artifact—is scaled into the cause. Resemblance is not connection.
- Only life used magic; no nonliving object chooses, casts, awakens, transmits a surviving reserve, or becomes an autonomous replacement user. Mundane mechanisms and records work only through ordinary people and physics.
- Tovan's expressions remain seven diverging, mutually non-commanding persons. Orrin's and Tovan's loss of access cannot invalidate their personhood. The Daughter's power loss is not death, coronation, absolution, or proof that the Mother-fragment was meaningless.
- Local inventions, proposal only: the annual Crown witness gathering; Faris's nine-year mutual-aid history; same-era unnamed fold-doors unrelated to the Glass Gate; capacity slates as branch vocabulary; ordinary semaphore/runners; the three audited artifacts; the five-part survival account; unnamed contemporary visitors, stewards, witnesses, surveyors, carriers, and households. These do not establish a full world map or universal magical taxonomy.

## Drafting failure modes and acceptance checks

- Do not shrink the event into a regional outage: the Glass-Sea crisis is the viewpoint spine, while the narrative frame, distinct failed systems, physical reports, world-spreading packets, and prolepsis establish permanent material-world scope.
- Do not use generic montage in place of causality. Every consequence must change a deadline, route, allocation, relationship, authority claim, or physical action.
- Do not imply suppression, unknown duration, faulty measurement, restoration, last spark, stored-charge escape, future magical birth, autonomous artifact, or technical substitute that is magic under another name.
- Keep Faris genuinely ordinary. Her expertise comes from nine years of mundane work; her favors remain freely renewed and her climax gives power away.
- Track the jack's load, wedges, manual resets, gravity head, seed temperature, route substitutions, witness countersigns, and convoy departure clearly.
- Preserve the eight consequence classes in scenes: civilizational, ecological, political, spiritual, identity, crossing, material, and historical.
- The final prose must explicitly state that magic never returns while preserving the afterlife/other-realm boundary and every prior person's identity and consequence.
