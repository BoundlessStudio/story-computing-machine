>
> Make the new execution less engineered and more lived. Reduce narratorial explanation of the consent/access theme so the climax carries it. Make the practical incompatibility Emet feared concrete with one concise fact about what the twenty-year shelter circuit required and what a shared life would have asked of either former partner, without excusing his lie. Keep their responsibility asymmetrical: Emet lied and concealed; Lacrixa did neither, though she may own a distinct prideful choice.
>

Establish the climax’s spatial geometry once and clearly. Order the coda chronologically, retain real consequences for Emet’s field trust, keep one restrained ensemble-tenderness beat per companion, and let the ordinary-door image close without thematic bookkeeping.
>
> The former relationship must materially cause both danger and solution rather than serve as a punchline. Do not treat Bosk’s sexuality as the joke, equate infernal nature with irredeemable personhood, divide fault falsely, restore the romance, or resolve the conflict through consequence-free killing.
