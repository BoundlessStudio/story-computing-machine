# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: At a roadside sanctuary, Lacrixa arrives through an illicit crossing with “Hello lover!” The party looks at Bosk; Emet answers, “Actually, my ex is referring to me.” His concealed courtesy-hinge leaf is exposed. The exes must dismantle three borrower loops through old bodily fluency, then end access without restoring romance.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Emet wants his vocation and the belief his lie spared Lacrixa a choice. The stolen leaf makes that evasion dangerous while he wants her trust.
- Counterforce or complication: An unknown rig redirects the paired leaves; occupants crowd both approaches, each removal destabilizes the next, and disclosure ruins needed trust.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Emet, attentive to construction, weight, and Lacrixa’s movement. Never enter her thoughts; show hurt and lost control through heat, timing, and refusal.
- Governing movement and time shape: One night into cool morning: comic arrival, diagnosis, three removals across one threshold, separation, then chronological accountability. Keep the climax local.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: A courtesy hinge has two living-bearer-keyed leaves. One mounted leaf is inert; both bearers near their leaves supply power. The rig redirects but never chooses or creates power. Establish one rectangle: sanctuary near side, old room and kiln court far side, three loops nested toward the threshold plane. Remove them near-jamb-to-plane; severing the leaves while anyone straddles the plane endangers them.
- Dialogue promise: Mistaken expectation, damaged familiarity, and involuntary shared shorthand create romantic-comedy timing. Emet names his choice; Lacrixa refuses reunion; cooperation never becomes repair.
- Dialogic medium: Speech, drum and foot counts, touch cues, posture, missed timing, silence, visible leaves, and proper-name goodbyes.
- Dialogue engine: Coordinating bodies and clearance removes each loop. Disclosure changes Lacrixa’s cooperation, Udren’s field trust, and whether both leaves are destroyed.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Brisk, tactile narration notices hinges, braced feet, kiln heat, shield weight, and Emet’s evasive clerical categories; under strain it contracts and stops explaining what action proves.
- Conversational texture: Overlap, work calls, dropped thoughts, ill-timed familiarity, and gaps. Humor comes from assumptions and damaged shorthand, never Bosk’s sexuality.
- Rhetorical ownership: Emet owns institutional evasion and belated precision; Lacrixa dry humor, material judgment, court authority, and refusal of abstraction. Bosk supplies play and count; Udren concrete questions and unsparing listening.
- Pressure behavior: Emet over-explains, then loses fluency. Lacrixa misses a beat and vents heat into objects. Bosk sheds wit after losing his drum; Udren grows quieter and direct.
- Relationship movement: Emet wants help, forgiveness, and reunion but cannot ask honestly. Lacrixa wants truth and destroyed access, not comfort. Bosk and Udren need an open choice but cannot restore trust; exchanges change leverage, risk, and commitment.
- Anti-default: Avoid articulate professionals debating consent while cleanly fixing a device. Let embarrassment, damaged muscle memory, strain, poor explanation, and refusal carry the hinge.

## Beats

1. Land the exact opening lines quickly. The joke is the party’s assumption, not Bosk’s desire. Heat and kiln-court voices create evacuation pressure; Emet recognizes his supposedly destroyed leaf in the rig.
2. Lacrixa’s far leaf remains openly mounted in their old room, inert alone. Bosk finds three loops and drums the count. Establish geometry and rules once.
3. Emet and Lacrixa remove the near loop while Udren clears the sanctuary. Involuntary old shorthand swaps their lead; efficiency followed by irritation makes the relationship palpable. The crossing contracts dangerously.
4. Exposure of the old room and retained access breaks Lacrixa’s control visibly: heat flares, glaze cracks under her hand, and she misses a count. Bosk’s drum is dragged away; he continues by voice and heel.
5. Before the middle loop comes free, Emet admits the House never forbade them. Its circuit required twenty road years, with no posting beyond one season; shared life meant Lacrixa repeatedly leaving her court or Emet relinquishing the circuit. He chose it and lied. Udren grounds him and asks his present choice without taking it away.
6. At the inner loop, Emet and Lacrixa hold opposite sides; Udren braces him while Bosk counts residents through. Repeat “He held.” as physical endurance. “Both feet clear.” confirms safe clearance. Add no rules.
7. Emet frames retained access as protection; Lacrixa answers, “You do not keep me safe by keeping access.” She refuses reunion. Each destroys their own keyed leaf in time; “Emet withdrew.” ends the magic. They exchange restrained proper-name goodbyes.
8. In order: tend casualties; Emet gives a full account; Udren removes him from solo threshold and route decisions until field trust is rebuilt. She rewraps his hand; Bosk sits beside him with water. At dawn, an ordinary sanctuary door is propped open to cool morning, without summation.
