## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] A demon appears before your party, exclaiming “Hello lover!” Everyone looks at the bard, but then the cleric speaks up. “Actually, my ex is referring to me.”**

## Story controls

- Working title: Hello, Lover
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person, centered on the cleric.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Character-driven fantasy adventure with romantic-comedy timing, infernal danger, and an emotionally sincere core.
- Audience/content rating: Teen / PG-13; no explicit sexual content or graphic gore.
- Required elements: A demon appears before an adventuring party and says “Hello lover!”; the party initially assumes the bard is the addressee; the cleric identifies the demon as their ex; the former relationship materially drives the conflict rather than serving only as a punchline; the immediate danger and the exes' unfinished business both receive a satisfying resolution.
- Prohibited elements: Treating the bard's sexuality as the joke; equating infernal nature with irredeemable personhood; resolving the central conflict through an unearned reunion or a consequence-free killing.

## Assumptions

- The story is a stand-alone entry in the shared fantasy universe and need not reuse an existing cast.
- The cleric and demon are adults whose past relationship was consensual.
- The prompt's quoted dialogue may be punctuated to fit the surrounding prose while preserving the wording and comic reversal.
- Low-impact setting details may be invented where current canon is silent, but no new shared-universe fact becomes canon unless separately promoted.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
