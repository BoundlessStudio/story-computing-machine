# Prompt

[WP] The great waterfall beyond the palace flows backward for exactly one minute every hundred years. During that minute, anyone standing on the western balcony may send a single message into the past. You have spent your entire life preparing the words that could prevent your kingdom's destruction. But as the water begins to rise, a letter appears in your hand, written in your own handwriting: "Whatever you do, let it burn."
