# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] An entire mountain village goes to sleep beneath a strange aurora and wakes millions of years later inside an archaeological excavation. The future knows them only as the mythical First Kingdom; the villagers know themselves as an unimportant trading stop with overdue taxes, mediocre beer, and no idea how to get home.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
