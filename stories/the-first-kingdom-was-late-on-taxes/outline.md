# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Kettlebridge wakes on its old bedrock inside the First Kingdom Excavation, millions of years after its mountain vanished. Brew-house keeper and tax reeve Ormela must help the village survive institutions treating their homes as founding artifacts. Resolve safety, custody, status, and a chosen interim home without recovering the mountain or complete history.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Ormela's overdue tax bundle inventories households. It can establish who and what survived, but officials mistake beer tallies and trade debts for royal record. Using it protects people; yielding its meaning turns it into scripture.
- Counterforce or complication: Odan must protect people, site, and evidence while a preservation order claims buildings and goods. Incomplete translation, dwindling water, inheritance claimants without proven descent, media, and officials invested in the myth create incompatible duties rather than a villain.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Ormela, who notices households, casks, obligations, and repairable value. Future history and law arrive through Sevri's tentative translation, procedure, and competing meetings; she cannot speak for every villager or certify the missing ages.
- Governing movement and time shape: One daylight into pre-dawn, through three disputed inventories: living needs, objects and remains, then households and destinations. Move from village lane to access deck to Freight Hall Nine. Turn on a household-built manifest, not public proof or consensus speech.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: A story-local aurora enclosed one settlement volume and returned it once to the same bedrock coordinates after no subjective interval. Excavation removed overburden; warnings cleared machinery before the volume displaced air and rainwater, preventing fusion. Houses, residents, animals, stores, and furnishings return; the outer spring, road, fields, and cemetery do not. The aurora closes without a route back or connection to another temporal system.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move through counts that become people: cistern depth, animal noise, lift capacity, household marks. Ormela notices value and obligation before what her accounting cannot contain.
- Conversational texture: Layer interrupted translation, work calls, private refusals, competing small meetings, and ordinary complaints about water, latrines, food, beer, children, and delay.
- Rhetorical ownership: Ormela reasons through tax, trade, brewing, and households; Belkin through water and waste; Aruz through symptoms and consent. Odan owns evidence, custody, and safety conditionals. Sevri exposes linguistic uncertainty; officials use polished founding categories.
- Pressure behavior: Ormela lists faster and turns blunt; villagers remain a role-bound chorus, not one voice. Odan compresses options but hedges claims. Sevri stops and corrects. Silence and refusal survive translation.
- Anti-default: Avoid primitive-versus-sterile-future comedy, universal fluency, heroic archaeological rescue, or a final debate converting myth into settled truth.

## Beats

1. Before the public opening, warnings clear the trench and Kettlebridge settles onto exposed foundation cuts. Ormela counts houses through ward runners, not a resident catalogue, and finds finite water, distressed animals, unsafe fires, no spring, and excavation walls where the road was.
2. Odan makes contact without entering homes or touching people. Sevri builds vocabulary from objects, gestures, names, and correction; safety instructions remain fragmentary. Belkin rations water and sites lined latrines. Aruz negotiates specific medical checks while households refuse unsolicited scans.
3. Public identification as the First Kingdom misfires against Ormela's expectation of a tax collector. Media and officials demand a founding appearance. Ormela produces the overdue bundle for the headcount, then withholds it when ceremonial titles overwrite household entries.
4. Competing meetings sort worn objects, tools, livestock, casks, homes, and stores by use and memory. Excavators separate previously lifted artifacts from returned property. Cemetery boxes from outside the envelope remain sealed: no display, sampling, kinship ruling, or reburial before villagers choose several representatives and cultural claimants are heard without becoming automatic descendants.
5. A halting emergency hearing grants a narrow provisional order: villagers are people outside artifact custody; occupied homes and household goods stay with them; documentation requires consent; excavated material and remains receive neutral care; land title stays unresolved. The founding ceremony and exhibition pause for review, without declaring either myth or ledger complete history.
6. Officials offer museum quarters or separated medical housing. Household, trade, care, and livestock groups instead choose Freight Hall Nine, a disused concourse with water, ventilation, loading access, animal bays, and partitionable rooms. Some adults remain as rotating site stewards; others delay or refuse movement. Choices stay individual and revisable.
7. At the first lift, an official inventory calls the tax chest and tools regalia. Ormela makes the bundle only a loading aid: households confirm, correct, or decline entries; Belkin changes water priorities; Aruz holds back a patient; brewers contest casks; Sevri preserves uncertainty. Odan accepts the slower manifest and releases people and property without claiming it.
8. Food, sanitation, medical space, livestock transport, privacy screens, counsel, and interpretation follow the order. The first convoy reaches Freight Hall Nine while chosen stewards retain site access. Ormela gets the bundle back, accepts that the mountain cannot return, and joins the practical allocation of beds, water turns, and mediocre beer.

## Continuity

- Canon used: One physical world persists through deep time; civilizations become archaeology and myth. Records and artifacts can be misread, and recovered truth remains partial rather than automatically just.
- Boundaries and unknowns: The aurora, future law, excavation, media, language, and First Kingdom institutions are story-local. Do not connect the event to the Glass Gate or generalize time travel, translation, ownership, or ancestry. Nothing inside the envelope is duplicated; outside material became remains. Return is impossible, claimants gain no custody by default, and testimony cannot settle every question.
