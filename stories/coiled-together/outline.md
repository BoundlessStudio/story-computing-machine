# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: An adult caravan outrider wakes tightly wrapped by the feared serpentfolk queen. She releases them when asked and says they begged not to be dropped during her flood rescue. Her wish to “keep” them becomes a paid six-day offer to test surface warnings, with private quarters and an unrestricted right to leave once the upper path opens.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The outrider wants bodily freedom, missing memory, and the human rope party above to survive, but fear recasts every hold as predation. The queen wants one conscious surface expert who can explain why humans mishear her warnings; she values the outrider's competence but resents proving harmlessness while water enters her home.
- Counterforce or complication: Storm runoff is filling a low basin with air that confuses humans before unconsciousness. A rescue party mistakes the queen's retreat from falling stone for abduction and forces a narrow signal fissure, destabilizing the ledge. Haste, acoustics, and incomplete recall keep both accounts uncertain until renewed danger tests them.
- POV, distance, and information limit: First-person past through the unnamed outrider's breath, balance, touch, and fearful inference. They cannot know the queen's motives or tail signals. Sensory fragments return under matching motion, but no perfect recollection or witness settles old disappearances.
- Governing movement and time shape: One storm-dark hour advances through changed holds and sightlines: restraint, release, solo verification, rupture, freely requested contact, escape, and a quiet offer. Keep the decision inside the cave and the aftermath private; the rescuers remain divided.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Violent rain makes this cave's low basin collect cold, carbon-heavy air; humans become clumsy, confused, and amnesic, while serpentfolk detect it sooner but remain vulnerable. The queen is roughly seven metres head-to-tail, with two arms and one muscular lower body. She can carry one adult or brace a gap, not both indefinitely; her strained flank can fail. Common speech works nearby, but flooded throats shear consonants from distant warnings. Floor taps carry only rehearsed safety signals, never thought.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: The outrider tests frightening impressions against breath, leverage, sound, and route geometry; memory returns as sensory correction, not lyrical revelation.
- Conversational texture: Practical questions about pressure, exits, injuries, rope, and timing mingle with unfinished accusations; irritation and task calls interrupt premise-heavy talk.
- Rhetorical ownership: The outrider reasons through trail risk; the queen uses bounded royal precision; her attendant tracks bodies and supplies; the cousin speaks in commands and kinship claims; the rope carrier thinks in loads.
- Pressure behavior: Fear makes the outrider repeat questions and miss answers. The queen grows shorter, not seductive; the cousin talks over others; the attendant works silently; the rope carrier interrupts rank over safety.
- Anti-default: Avoid a velvet-voiced monster seducing a captive through polished consent doctrine, or a chorus that trades species aphorisms until prejudice dissolves.

## Beats

1. The outrider wakes unable to breathe fully inside warm coils on a dry crown ledge. Their requests to loosen and then release are obeyed immediately, in stages that prevent a fall. The queen separates the past emergency plea from present permission and answers practical questions without asking for trust.
2. Free on a folded hide, the outrider finds bruises, the queen's torn flank, and the snapped belt loop they remember gripping. The attendant treats injuries and wet bedding. The queen limits her claim: a storm-whistle drew the outrider toward bad air; they collapsed, woke during the vertical carry, and panicked when her grip shifted.
3. After making the six-day offer, the queen lets the outrider crawl alone through the human-width signal fissure. Its acoustics turn clear speech behind them into the lure-like sibilance the rescuers heard. Their cousin and a rope carrier saw neither the collapse nor rescue; certainty ends with their sightline.
4. The cousin tries to haul the outrider out. A loaded rope breaks the fissure lip; runoff divides the party and crumbles the queen's ledge. She retreats to protect her injured attendant, not contest the freed outrider. The outrider chooses to redirect the rope toward them.
5. The queen can brace the washed gap for her attendant and one human, but cannot also carry the dizzy outrider; the cousin cannot reach without overloading the anchor. As symptoms return, the outrider requests one coiled crossing, with breathing room and release specified. The same shift restores a sensory fragment of their own repeated plea. Her strained compliance does not make coils inherently safe or intimate.
6. The attendant guides by minimal floor taps while the rope carrier refuses an unsafe pull. The outrider selects the upper chimney; the queen gets her attendant across and abandons the ledge. Their incompatible expertise brings all four adults above the bad-air line before flooding, without a unified account.
7. At the upper mouth, the cousin still calls the queen dangerous; the rope carrier claims only what they witnessed. The outrider chooses one recovery night and the paid trial, contingent on a doorless sleeping niche and daily mouth access. The queen accepts without touch or triumph. Captivity and danger resolve; wider prejudice remains.
