## New characters or character facts

- **Cal Mercer (proposed):** Lena Mercer's older brother and a first-time
  Runner. He enters the Queen Street seam to obtain a heartseed for Lena,
  refuses a contract that would violate Lena's and their mother's memories,
  and steals the seed. After it roots in him, he accepts monitoring and agrees
  that any further donation requires Lena's assent, their mother's consent,
  and medical approval. The story does not establish Cal's age, long-term
  prognosis, or permanent control over the seed.
- **Lena Mercer (proposed):** Cal's twelve-year-old sister, whose worsening
  cardiac condition has placed her on a transplant path. One screened
  transfusion of Cal's heartseed-altered blood improves her measurements and
  breathing but does not cure her or establish how much time she gains. She
  insists on a voice in any future transfusion or attempted miracle.
- **Tuck (proposed):** An experienced, mononymous Runner guide who conceals
  that Cal's cash covers guidance rather than a heartseed. He admits having
  abandoned a living friend for the friend's boots, later rejects a clear
  chance to abandon Cal, returns part of his fee, and commits to helping close
  the breached route. His past abandonment remains morally consequential.
- **M. Voss (proposed):** A licensed Exit Witness who registers Cal's entry,
  closes Tuck's returned token, arranges quarantine and monitored care after
  the crossing, and accepts responsibility for containing the open follow
  trail. Her given name, employer, jurisdiction, and wider history remain
  unknown.
- **Priya (proposed):** A person from Cal's past who gave him his first kiss.
  Deepmarket's entry gate removes Cal's lived episode of that kiss while
  leaving only sparse semantic knowledge that Priya and the event existed.
  No surname, present-day status, or further connection is established.
- **GutSaint72 (proposed):** The username of an old Runner-forum account that
  claimed salt discouraged “wet trouble.” The story does not establish the
  account holder's identity, reliability beyond this one useful claim, or
  whether the holder is alive.
- An unnamed Deepmarket gatekeeper is a living, warm-smoke being that
  states and collects Deepmarket's entry toll. It selects one emotionally
  weighted autobiographical memory only after an entrant knowingly crosses
  its collection line.
- An unnamed living root shop uses a child-shaped dealer avatar that
  is an avatar of a living shop organism rather than an ordinary child. The
  organism tunes and protects heartseeds, proposes predatory contracts, reads
  locally marked route names, and retains Cal's unstamped token at the end of
  the story. Its origin, full reach, and relationship to Deepmarket's larger
  living substrate remain unknown.

## New locations

- **Proposal — Queen Street return tunnel:** An abandoned tunnel below Queen
  Street contains one living white portal seam, an Exit Witness's registration
  station, and an adjacent secured quarantine clinic. The story does not state
  the locality's exact calendar date, establish that it is literal real-world
  Earth, or connect this seam to the Glass Gate or any other crossing system.
- **Proposal — red-grass approach:** The Queen Street seam opens onto a region
  of red grass, a white road, and a sky crowded with drifting doors. The story
  does not establish whether the region is another place, era, constructed
  enclosure, or something else.
- **Proposal — Deepmarket:** A market-city of copper towers and crooked awnings
  built on the shell of a vast sleeping living being. Its merchants and
  organisms trade in locally weighted wants, memories, years, and stranger
  goods. Its ultimate location, age, origin, and relationship to the wider
  world remain unknown.
- **Proposal — shame-gated heartseed shop:** A living root structure within
  Deepmarket that opens after sincere admissions of shame, stores heartseeds,
  and manifests the dealer avatar. It is one local organism, not evidence that
  every market or doorway has the same rules.

## New factions or cultural facts

- **Proposal — Runner practice:** Runners cross local anomalies with guides,
  trade incomplete field knowledge through forums, and use slang such as “the
  Realms run on want.” The story establishes no unified Runner faction,
  universal licensing body, or reliable knowledge of every crossing.
- **Proposal — Exit Witness practice:** Licensed living witnesses can register
  entrants with the Queen Street seam, maintain timed return windows, close
  used tokens with a stamp, and access a quarantine clinic. The institution's
  name, jurisdiction, staffing, authority, and operation beyond this crossing
  remain unknown.
- **Proposal — Deepmarket trade culture:** Guides may charge ordinary money for
  passage knowledge even when Deepmarket goods demand nonmonetary prices. The
  living market's merchants recognize and exploit expressed desire, but no
  shared government or universal law is established.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Proposal — living Queen Street seam:** A living organism embedded in the
  tunnel wall produces upward-falling black rain and operates the local portal.
  A living Exit Witness may prompt it to mark a route, but cannot command it.
  Nonliving disks merely store marks supplied by the seam.
- **Proposal — route tokens:** For the witnessed crossing, the seam marks a
  metal disk with an entrant's declared name, entry route, and active return
  window. A token remembers a route rather than an owner. Losing it before an
  exit opens strands the entrant as “local”; separation after opening can
  still allow momentum to carry the entrant through. If the disk is not then
  stamped closed, its holder retains one usable follow trail. Cal crosses only
  after his disk has opened the threshold; the dealer-root tears it away
  before closure. Tuck returns independently on his own disk.
- **Proposal — bounded want-pricing:** Deepmarket's living substrate senses
  expressed desire and weights local prices accordingly. Tuck's claim that
  “the Realms run on want” is fallible Runner shorthand, not a universal law of
  magic, desire, or every destination beyond the doors.
- **Proposal — stated memory toll:** The living smoke gatekeeper charges one
  emotionally weighted episodic memory after stating the terms and obtaining
  consent through crossing a visible collection line. The witnessed
  extraction removes the lived sensory and emotional episode while leaving
  sparse semantic knowledge and independent historical traces intact.
- **Proposal — local name access and shame gate:** The Queen Street seam binds
  Cal's declared name to his route mark. Within the living shop, roots can read
  that local mark; this is not universal name omniscience. The shop's entrance
  opens when its root network detects sincere shame, a local access condition
  rather than a universal truth test.
- **Proposal — relational-memory contract:** With an accepted bargain, the
  shop claims it can remove linked people's lived memories of the payer and
  bias their attention so surviving evidence feels mistaken. The effect would
  not rewrite history, photographs, birth records, hospital records, or other
  independent traces. Cal refuses because Lena and their mother did not and
  could not consent through him.
- **Proposal — shop defense and ejection:** The shop's living roots can attack,
  move shelves, use the dealer avatar, and eject a thief and anyone holding the
  thief toward their most recently opened marked route. This is a defensive
  behavior of one organism and does not grant arbitrary portal travel.
- **Proposal — local salt vulnerability:** Ordinary salt draws moisture from
  the dealer avatar's wet conductive tissue and nearby exposed roots, briefly
  disrupting them. No universal anti-magic property is proposed.
- **Proposal — heartseeds:** Heartseeds are living symbiotes kept dormant in
  nonliving jars. The dealer's organism can tune a seed's repair output toward
  a patient's damage by using records, an image, expressed desire, and a living
  relational link. Tuning does not choose the host: after release, the seed
  roots through the first fresh blood it contacts. Once rooted, removal would
  probably kill host and seed. Cal's seed produces a limited surplus of
  repair-bearing blood factors tuned to Lena, permitting one beneficial
  screened transfusion without guaranteeing a cure.
- **Proposal — heartseed cost and hunger:** Donation depletes Cal, while the
  rooted seed responds with heat, a second rhythm, and hunger. Any repeat use
  requires medical monitoring and shared consent. The seed's long-term effects,
  removal options, nutritional needs, lifespan, and danger to Cal or Lena
  remain unknown.
- **Explicit unknown — Realms ontology:** The drifting doors and reachable
  regions are not identified as parallel universes, separate planes, distinct
  worlds, or time routes. No connection to the Glass Gate is proposed.

## Timeline events

- **Proposal — undated modern-like-era event:** During one timed Queen Street
  return window, Cal and Tuck enter Deepmarket. The gate takes Cal's episodic
  memory of his first kiss with Priya; Cal refuses the dealer's
  relational-memory bargain; he and Tuck steal Lena's tuned heartseed; and the
  shop ejects them through their marked return routes.
- **Proposal — immediate aftermath:** The heartseed's jar breaks in the return
  tunnel and the organism roots through Cal's cut palm. At the linked clinic,
  Lena and her mother agree to one screened transfusion, Lena's measurements
  improve without becoming normal, and Cal commits to monitored, consensual
  care. Tuck and Voss commit to containing the breach.
- **Proposal — unresolved pursuit:** Cal's token remains with the dealer rather
  than crossing for closure. After the siblings' immediate arc resolves, three
  knocks and the dealer's voice at the clinic door demonstrate that the open
  follow trail has been used. The confrontation and its outcome remain
  unresolved.

No calendar year, Galactic Cycle coordinate, or placement relative to Ravel or
the Glass-Sea era is established.

## New glossary terms or aliases

- **Proposal — `Realms`:** Runner shorthand for the poorly understood places
  or enclosures reachable through anomalous doors. In this story the term does
  not establish a multiverse, a separate plane, or one universal network.
- **Proposal — `Runner`:** A person who enters local crossing systems; the term
  does not by itself imply membership in a single organization.
- **Proposal — `Exit Witness`:** A licensed living witness who registers and
  closes crossings at the Queen Street seam. `Licensed Exit Witness` is a role
  description on M. Voss's tag, not an additional character name.
- **Proposal — `exit token` / `route token`:** A nonliving marked metal disk
  that stores one entrant's local route and return window under the bounded
  Queen Street seam mechanism.
- **Proposal — `Deepmarket`:** The market-city reached during this story.
- **Proposal — `heartseed`:** A living, tunable symbiote whose repair output,
  first-blood rooting behavior, and costs are described above.
- No additional aliases or universal technical terms are proposed.
