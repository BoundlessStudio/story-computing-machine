## Hard constraints

- Preserve the recognizable premise: Cal enters a dangerous portal-linked market seeking a heartseed for his sister, with the central Cal/Lena and Cal/Tuck relationships, first-person past tense, and the existing emotional promise. The archived record is creative evidence only; all setting rules introduced here remain proposals until promotion. (`stories/realms/00-prompt.md`; `sources/records/s03/Realms.md`.)
- This is an ordinary in-progress, non-canon story. Its archived creative input creates no lifecycle, publication, review, or promotion exception. (`stories/realms/story.json`; `stories/INDEX.md`.)
- The setting is one physical world persisting through deep time. Queen Street, Toronto Island, Canadian dollars, and the 2021 reference may remain viewpoint-local, but must not settle whether the modern-like era is literal real-world Earth or turn Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world into separate planes. (`universe/premise.md`, “The deep-time shared world” [LOCKED]; `universe/locations.md`, “The world across eras” [LOCKED].)
- The Queen Street portal and Realms must be a story-local crossing system, unrelated to the Glass Gate absent approved evidence. Define access, directionality, exit-token behavior, and crossing consequences. (`universe/rules.md`, “Portals and crossing systems” [CANON]; `universe/locations.md`, “The Glass Gate” [CANON].)
- “The Realms run on want” must describe a bounded Realms/Deepmarket mechanism, not universal cosmology. Costs, desire, names, memory, identity, records, transformation, and consent gain metaphysical force only through explicit local rules. (`universe/rules.md`, “Costs, desire, and local mechanisms,” “Names and identity binding,” “Memory, transformation, copies, and branches,” and “Records, falsehood, and historical traces” [CANON].)
- Only life can use magic. Any apparently active portal, market, shop, roots, heartseed, or map must be alive, channel a living agent, or be non-agentic storage. (`universe/rules.md`, “Living agency and magic” [LOCKED].)
- Memory extraction and threatened erasure of Cal from Lena's life must retain their personhood, relational, consent, and moral consequences. (`universe/style-guide.md`, “Personhood and moral consequence” [LOCKED].)
- Keep violence and body horror non-graphic under the Teen ceiling. Resolve Cal's central emotional and causal arc before any dealer-pursuit or hungry-heart sequel hook. (`stories/realms/00-prompt.md`; `universe/style-guide.md`, “Audience and content boundaries” [LOCKED].)

## Useful established context

- A modern-like, low-magic era resembling contemporary Earth exists; relic anomalies may survive while ordinary society treats magic as myth. The exact era coordinate is unresolved. (`universe/timeline.md`, “Established era order” [CANON]; `universe/glossary.md`, “Modern-like era” [CANON].)
- The universe supports many distinct systems with no single origin for every anomaly, so Deepmarket's crossing, heartseed, tolls, salt vulnerability, and door effects may be one bounded system or a clearly defined family of local mechanisms. (`universe/rules.md`, “System categories” [LOCKED].)
- Characters may hold incomplete operational models while backstage reality stays coherent. Runner folklore and Voss's advice need not be omniscient cosmology. (`universe/premise.md`, “Knowledge and connection” [CANON].)
- The memory-versus-myth theme supports the memory toll and threatened erasure if evidence, causality, and choices remain consequential. A standalone entry point needs no crossover exposition. (`universe/premise.md`, “Memory, myth, and recoverable truth” [LOCKED]; `universe/style-guide.md`, “Tonal range, scale, and connection” [CANON].)

## Conflicts or ambiguity

- No authoritative canon conflict requires a user ruling.
- Exit-token logic is internally inconsistent in the creative input: Voss says losing one prevents return, yet Cal and Tuck return while Cal's is missing, and the dealer apparently follows it. Planning must establish when it leaves Cal, why return succeeds, whether it is person-bound, and what access it grants.
- The heartseed is labeled for Lena but roots in Cal after blood contact. Define its host-selection trigger and how Cal's rooted seed can benefit Lena through transfusion, with bounded risks and limits.
- The dealer's proposed payment might alter memory, records, identity binding, history, or a branch. Choose one bounded mechanism; contradictory records cannot themselves prove changed truth.
- The Realms' ultimate ontology may remain unknown, but the story cannot imply a multiverse or universal portal network as established fact.
- The current knock and second heartbeat are hooks, not resolution. Cal's choice, cost, Lena's meaningful but bounded outcome, and the changed relationships must resolve first.

## Safe invention space

- Keep the crossing, Runner system, exit tokens, Deepmarket, Bone Orchard, and regulation local. Define the plot-critical operational rules while leaving the Realms' ultimate cosmological classification unresolved.
- Treat want, memory payment, name sensitivity, market bargains, salt, shadows, and heartseeds as bounded local mechanisms; never generalize them to all magic, names, memory, or healing.
- Give heartseed rooting a concrete trigger and Lena's benefit specific limits and costs while honoring living agency and personhood.
- Retain Toronto-facing details as local geography without an omniscient literal-Earth declaration.
- Complete the immediate arc, then optionally retain the dealer or hungry-heart hook.
- Rename colliding character forms during planning; no seed name is required by the prompt.
