# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: An unnamed mountain sorcerer seeks immortality by braiding many people’s blood into one self-renewing magical pattern. Small, consensually donated samples instead form repeatable compatibility groups. Resolve the failure causally: he recognizes stable human blood types, cancels the pooled working, and leaves a bounded basis for investigation rather than a joke, miracle cure, or finished medical science.
- Focal pressure or attachment: He wants enough life to finish every theory and uses intellectual certainty against his fear of death. First snow threatens his planned convergence date. Accepting categorical differences destroys years of universal-blood theory; proceeding would misuse volunteers’ trust after his own trials warn against it.
- Counterforce or complication: The valley healer who controls consent and records refuses to let him dismiss irregular results, while a recovering resident wants an account of distress caused by an earlier, non-transfer blood-linked charm. They protect people and evidence, not ignorance. Sample age, cold, diet, kinship, and uneven spell control remain rival explanations until repeated blinded comparisons remove them.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close first-person past through the sorcerer, attentive first to ratios and grand inference. Other motives stay external until he stops interrupting. He can observe magical reactions and ordinary records, but cannot know modern antigen science, map every rare type, or derive universal medical guidance.
- Governing movement and time shape: A recursive fortnight of four experiment-and-interview cycles before heavy snow. Each return to the bench removes one assumption; descents to the healer restore the people hidden by numbers. The private turn is stopping the convergence and accepting the bounded external comparison. End with individual disclosure and returned samples, not public proof, lecture, cure, or emotional victory over mortality.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: The sorcerer’s living pulse can make samples show magical coherence or rejection. Blood, labels, bowls, cooling stone, and tables are inert conduits; none diagnoses or casts. Stable biological differences cause the groupings, while magic is only a story-local detection method. No blood enters another body, the working treats nobody, and drafting should omit collection technique, transfusion procedure, and medical advice.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Let the sorcerer narrate in narrowing spirals: monumental theory gives way to bench-scale recurrence, bodily unease, and finally the people behind the marks. Keep material notices precise without procedural detail.
- Conversational texture: Mix clipped scheduling, consent checks, weather, storage limits, meals, and symptom descriptions with occasional theoretical dispute. Conversations should often serve one immediate task.
- Rhetorical ownership: The sorcerer owns immortalist analogy, elegant classification, and defensive abstraction. The healer owns sequence, exceptions, permissions, and practical consequence. The resident owns concrete sensation, interruption, and the limits of what explanation can repay.
- Pressure behavior: Under challenge the sorcerer accelerates and answers questions not asked; evidence makes him repeat terms, then shorten. The healer becomes slower and more exact. The resident withholds cooperation when talked over and grows direct once heard.
- Anti-default: Do not make everyone polished experiment-explainers or end on a science lecture. Preserve unequal fluency, ordinary logistics, incomplete knowledge, and the sorcerer’s unresolved fear of dying.

## Beats

1. During convergence preparations, the healer delivers separately sealed volunteer samples and checks their authorized uses. He treats the ledger as obstruction and the pooled working as immortality’s final step. Establish his living agency and inert apparatus.
2. The first pairing fails in a pattern: some samples sustain coherence, others extinguish it, and repeats preserve the split. Without expanding consent, he tests cold, age, health, and handling as explanations.
3. At the healer’s rooms, donor follow-up and supply talk puncture his abstractions. The recovering resident describes the older charm’s consequence and rejects being called impure. The healer shows strong and frail donors in every cluster. He separates variation from moral worth or health but defends his immortality premise.
4. The healer relabels an authorized subset. Across days, kinships, and sample ages, compatibility families recur. Keep one ambiguous result so the discovery remains provisional. His notes shift from ranking potency to mapping types while he still prepares convergence.
5. Heavy snow arrives early. Alone with the prepared pool, he sees that combining everything would force incompatible patterns through his living spell. He stops before activation and performs only the final consented external comparison needed to show that the groups predict earlier failures.
6. He records that human blood is not interchangeable, stable types exist, his immortality design is invalid, one result remains unresolved, and no treatment follows. He gives the healer and resident an account rather than a lecture. They separately decide what records and samples may remain; the rest follow prior consent.
7. The next morning he accompanies the healer downhill for individual disclosures. The apparatus remains unused and his fear of death unresolved. He has abandoned the harmful premise and accepted a finite, accountable investigation.

## Continuity

- Canon used: One world permits many local systems, and only life uses magic. The sorcerer supplies all magical agency; tools, labels, vessels, samples, and records remain passive.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Link this to no existing era, place, person, bloodline, or system. Blood types are biological, but their taxonomy, inheritance, history, and medical use remain unknown. Consent is procedural and ethical, not magical fuel. Keep collection consensual and non-graphic, physical transfer absent, and ordinary care with the healer.
