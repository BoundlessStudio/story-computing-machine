The Captain's arrival is a real destructive event: his approach breaks the station's antenna and sends glass into the empty street. His interception knocks Mara off her route. He catches the two together in the same carrying posture he taught her.

The rescue is not an automatic pardon or the end of his rage. With Mara hurt in his arms and Vane still within reach, the Captain has to make a separate choice. He lowers them onto the evacuated roof instead of killing Vane. The man gets his arrest and medical transport; Mara cannot immediately use the arm or fly.
