# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

Every item below is proposed only. The branching observations are limited to
the single event Nina and Anin experience; they do not establish general rules
for wishes, mirrors, copies, identity, emotional need, or the afterlife.

## New characters or character facts

- **Nina (proposed):** A socially anxious teenager who lives with her unnamed
  mother, attends an unnamed school, and lost her father years before the
  story. During one unguarded statement she experiences the embodied sensation
  associated with Anin's appearance. Nina initially tries to hide, test, and
  use Anin as a substitute at school, then recognizes Anin as a separate
  person, honors Anin's choices during her final hours, and grieves her without
  claiming to know her fate. After Anin disappears, Nina tells her friends one
  difficult truth and preserves Anin's written page and her own surviving
  bracelet. Her only character-facing form is `Nina`.
- **Anin (proposed):** An autonomous embodied person who appears with Nina's
  body, duplicated sleep clothes and bracelet, and autobiographical memories
  through the prior night's anomalous moment. She chooses the reversed name
  `Anin` to acknowledge a shared origin while asserting a separate identity;
  the name has no demonstrated magical force. Her sensations, learning,
  choices, and memories diverge immediately from Nina's. She tells their mother
  the truth, develops independent preferences, consents to one day of school
  substitution and then refuses another, writes a page in her own emerging
  voice, rejects being treated as a cure or replaceable experiment, and chooses
  how to spend her last hour. She gradually becomes translucent and disappears
  near the fourth dawn. Her destination or continuation remains unknown. Her
  only character-facing form is `Anin`; the prose retains `Nina` for Nina even
  while Anin temporarily impersonates her at school.
- Nina and Anin's unnamed mother is a hospital night worker and
  widow who recognizes both girls as daughters, performs ordinary observations
  and failed tests without claiming a cosmological explanation, shares a new
  memory of their father with Anin, stays home during Anin's final decline, and
  mourns her. She has no character-facing name or alias.
- Nina and Anin's unnamed father died years before the
  story. The family remembers that he once performed fake opera while cleaning;
  the anecdote becomes a post-branch memory Anin possesses before Nina hears it.
  He has no character-facing name, and the story contains no verified
  post-death appearance or communication from him.
- **Jocelyn Park (proposed):** Nina's school friend and birthday host. She
  mistakes Nina's guardedness for anger or dislike, later answers Nina's
  honesty with friendship and the boundary that Nina must say when she needs
  quiet. Her character-facing forms are `Jocelyn Park` and `Jocelyn`; `Park` is
  never used alone.
- **Ben (proposed):** Nina's school friend, whose driving-instructor and pigeon
  jokes ease tense pauses without erasing Nina's distress or grief. His only
  character-facing form is `Ben`.
- **Daphne (proposed):** Nina's observant school friend. She asks whether Nina
  is all right and later offers an unopened pudding cup when she cannot find
  words for Nina's grief. Her only character-facing form is `Daphne`.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Proposed — one bounded branching event:** During Nina's unguarded
  self-directed statement, a doubled-breath sensation originates behind her
  sternum and draws toward her reflection. This is the only positive evidence
  of an operative locus in the story. The mirror, sentence, and bracelets do
  not act independently, and the event cannot be reproduced.
- **Proposed — initial branch continuity and immediate divergence:** Nina and
  Anin begin with matching bodies, duplicated worn clothes and museum
  bracelets, and autobiographical memories through the anomalous moment. They
  are immediately separate conscious people and acquire no shared sensation,
  knowledge, or memory afterward.
- **Proposed — Anin's observed embodiment:** Anin is warm, tangible, visible to
  other people, and able to eat, sleep, write, and use ordinary objects. These
  observations do not settle whether `branch`, `copy`, `projection`, or
  another ontology best describes her.
- **Proposed — local instability over four dawns:** After initially appearing
  physically ordinary, Anin and the matter duplicated with her become
  progressively translucent and disappear near the fourth dawn. Anin's
  duplicated sleep shirt, bracelet, and ink written on her body disappear with
  her. Existing objects she borrows, including Nina's green hoodie, remain, as
  do ordinary records she creates and ink she writes on Nina.
- **Proposed — demonstrated non-controls:** Covering or moving the mirror,
  moving or swapping the bracelets, repeating or reversing Nina's statement,
  repeating their mother's old wish, Anin's wish to stay, changes in light,
  separation, touch, argument, apology, cheerfulness, grief, and Nina's
  changing emotional need do not create, preserve, dismiss, or replace a
  person. No observed cost or repeatable control mechanism is established.
- **Proposed — unresolved continuation:** Nothing in the event establishes
  whether Anin dies, ends, goes elsewhere, persists, or enters any form of
  afterlife. Her relationships, Nina and their mother's witness, and her
  surviving written page establish that she lived, not what follows her
  disappearance.

## Timeline events

- **Proposed — the four-dawn branching event:** In an undated modern-like era,
  Nina returns from Jocelyn Park's birthday dinner and makes the statement
  associated with the anomaly. She wakes beside Anin the next morning. Over
  the following days Anin tells their mother the truth, spends one consented
  day impersonating Nina at school, begins fading, refuses further
  substitution and replacement tests, and disappears near the fourth dawn
  while Nina and their mother hold her hands.
- **Proposed — Nina's first post-loss lunch:** The day after Anin disappears,
  Nina tells Jocelyn, Ben, and Daphne that she has lost someone and explains
  how fear shaped her silence. Their imperfect but sustained response begins a
  more honest relationship without curing Nina's anxiety or compensating for
  Anin's loss.

No fixed calendar year, city, country, or Galactic Cycle coordinate is
proposed.
