## Hard constraints

- This is an ordinary in-progress, non-canon story; its creative-input history creates no lifecycle, review, publication, or promotion exception. (`stories/self-reflection/story.json`; `stories/INDEX.md`.)
- The setting belongs to one physical world persisting through deep time. A contemporary-looking environment may be in the modern-like era without asserting literal real-world Earth. (`universe/premise.md`, “The deep-time shared world” [LOCKED]; `universe/locations.md`, “The world across eras” [LOCKED].)
- If Nina's duplication is magical, operative agency must be living. A mirror, bracelet, sentence, or other object may focus or participate but cannot independently use magic. (`universe/rules.md`, “Living agency and magic” [LOCKED].)
- Nina's lonely wish, joke, or desire cannot establish universal manifestation. Desire has force only where a bounded local system establishes it. (`universe/rules.md`, “Costs, desire, and local mechanisms” [CANON].)
- Anin is a person with agency, consent, relationships, and morally meaningful loss, not a disposable symptom, tool, or meaningless error. (`universe/style-guide.md`, “Personhood and moral consequence” [LOCKED].)
- Shared memories do not decide a universal copy ontology. Keep copied or branched identity specific to this mechanism. (`universe/rules.md`, “Memory, transformation, copies, and branches” [CANON].)
- Reflection, reversal, resemblance, and recurring imagery do not establish connection to any other artifact or system. (`universe/rules.md`, “Artifacts, motifs, and folklore” [CANON].)
- Keep treatment broadly accessible and non-explicit, centering grief and distress on character and consequence rather than spectacle. (`universe/style-guide.md`, “Audience and content boundaries” [LOCKED].)

## Useful established context

- The modern-like era is a low-magic trough where ordinary life generally treats magic as myth, though isolated anomalies may occur. Nina, her family, and classmates need not understand the wider cosmology. (`universe/timeline.md`, “Established era order” [CANON]; `universe/glossary.md`, “Modern-like era” [CANON]; `universe/premise.md`, “Knowledge and connection” [CANON].)
- Entirely local, ordinary-scale stories belong in the universe and require no crossover or large reveal. (`universe/premise.md`, “Memory, myth, and recoverable truth” [LOCKED]; `universe/style-guide.md`, “Tonal range, scale, and connection” [CANON].)
- A universal underlying afterlife exists, but a temporary or copied person's relationship to it is mechanism-specific. This matters only if Anin's disappearance is positively characterized as death or continuation. (`universe/rules.md`, “Afterlife and continuation” and “Memory, transformation, copies, and branches” [CANON].)
- No universal authority regulates all anomalies, so no supervising organization is required. (`universe/premise.md`, “Knowledge and connection” [CANON].)

## Conflicts or ambiguity

- No authoritative conflict blocks planning; metadata agrees the story is in progress and non-canon.
- The creative input associates Nina's mirror remark with Anin's appearance and Anin's fading with Nina's reduced need. Preserve their emotional and apparent causal relationship without turning either into a universal law.
- Canon does not decide whether Anin is a branch, bodily copy, projection, temporary construct, or another kind of person. The story may choose or deliberately limit the answer while preserving her personhood.

## Unknowns

- The exact year, town, and placement within the modern-like era.
- What creates Anin, why it acts on Nina's statement, and whether the mirror is causal or contextual.
- Whether Anin's copied clothing and bracelet are new matter, projections, or temporary extensions.
- Why Anin fades and whether emotional independence causes, accompanies, or is unrelated to it.
- What happens after Anin disappears. Absorption, annihilation, survival, or passage into the afterlife cannot be asserted as established canon without a coherent local mechanism.
- Whether comparable duplications have occurred elsewhere.

## Safe invention space

- An undated school, neighborhood, hospital job, and family history in a contemporary-looking part of the modern-like era.
- One narrow anomaly involving Nina's living agency, spoken desire, and a temporary autonomous duplicate, without making wishing universal magic.
- Story-specific rules for shared pre-appearance memories, post-appearance divergence, bodily presence, duplicated possessions, fading, and disappearance.
- A deliberately limited character-level explanation that leaves the larger cause unknown while keeping events causally and emotionally coherent.
- Local friendships, routines, and family consequences without recurring institutions or global anomaly policy.
- Mirror and reversal imagery as motif rather than proof of linkage.

## Name constraints

- `Nina` is unique for this story but visually close to reserved `Nilo`; retain clear context.
- `Anin` deliberately reverses `Nina`. They are distinct people; the plan must document why the reversal matters and how narration and dialogue distinguish them after memory divergence.
- `Nisha` collides with an existing reservation and is easily confused with Nina within this story. Rename this character before prose to a form clearly distinct from Nina, Anin, and all registry reservations.
- `Jocelyn Park`/`Jocelyn` and `Ben` are unique reservations for this production.
- Any newly named relative, teacher, school, companion, or person-like manifestation requires a fresh registry check. (`stories/NAMES.md`.)

## Required checks after drafting

- Never state or imply that lonely wishes generally create people; trace all magical action to living agency.
- Give Anin meaningful choices, interests, and fears beyond Nina's therapeutic need, and mourn her as a person.
- Track which identity attends school, does work, speaks to each friend, and learns each fact; shared memories must not silently continue after divergence.
- Track both bracelets, clothing, transparency, touch, and disappearance consistently.
- If the ending explains Anin's fate, check it against personhood, copy, and afterlife rules; otherwise make uncertainty explicit.
- Replace `Nisha`, document Nina/Anin's intentional reversal, register every final form, and pass the strict scoped name check.
- Do not use reflection or duplication imagery as evidence of another established system; preserve the Teen ceiling and complete emotional arc.
