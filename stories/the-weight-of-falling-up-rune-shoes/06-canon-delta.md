## New characters or character facts

- **Juni Wex:** An apprentice at the Collegium of Nine Eaves who makes
  temporary drawn workings. She devises paired shoe-mounted wind channels,
  passes her aerial qualification after an unsafe choice and a competent
  free-fall repair, and remains eligible for the rooftop-survey practicum.
  - **Evidence:** Dovek calls her full name at launch; Juni draws, charges,
    controls, diagnoses, redraws, and modifies the wind curls; Dovek records
    the pass and confirms her qualification in `05-story.md`.
  - **Boundary:** New local identity. No relationship to any established or
    legacy character is proposed. Her wider biography, age, era, and later
    history remain unspecified.
- **Examiner Dovek:** Administers Juni's aerial assessment. Dovek recognizes
  the shoe method's limitations, offers a scored repair pause, prepares to
  accept a declared stop without intervening, and awards a qualified pass
  based on diagnosis, power control, repair, disclosure, and controlled
  landing rather than novelty. Dovek is referred to with singular `they` /
  `them` pronouns.
  - **Evidence:** Dovek's launch questions, one-point pause offer, warning
    during Juni's corkscrew, and final scoring discussion in `05-story.md`.
  - **Boundary:** New local identity and role at Nine Eaves. No universal
    examining authority or wider institutional rank is proposed.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

All mechanics in this section belong to Juni's local drawn-shoe practice. They
do not define rune-writing, broom flight, mount flight, wind, or magic
elsewhere.

| Proposed fact | Evidence in `05-story.md` | Scope boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Juni draws a mirrored three-stroke **wind curl** in black ink directly around the heel and underside of each ordinary leather shoe, then supplies living warmth through her hands to charge the completed lines. | The launch-roof drawing and charging of both shoes; the in-air redraw, crown addition, and palm activation. | The shoes, ink, and marks are nonliving focuses. They do not choose, target, balance, repair, or activate themselves. |
| When Juni supplies living power through an intact curl, it shapes a narrow stream of magical wind from that shoe. Sustained output drives a continuous climb or crossing; short pulses produce abrupt corrections. | Juni's launch, climb, bank, stationary attempt, descent, recovery, and final approach. | The story proposes a local magical-wind response. No technological, combustion, or universal flight mechanism is asserted. |
| Each foot aims its own wind stream. With both soles pointed down and output matched, Juni rises; directing both heels behind her drives her forward; swinging both heels ahead turns the jets against her motion to brake. | The launch climb, forward acceleration, moderate early brake, hard late brake, and conservative final landing. | These controls belong only to Juni's paired marks. |
| The two jets must be balanced. Extra output on one side rolls and yaws Juni toward the weaker side, while matched thrust steadies her. If the right curl fails during reverse braking, the commanded left jet drives her left side backward and drops her right shoulder into a rightward corkscrew. More left power tightens the rotation; cutting all power stops adding torque but does not erase her existing spin or fall. | The early overstrong left burst, its correction, the right-rune failure, Juni's worsening attempts, her diagnosis, and the unpowered free fall. | No autonomous stabilization or shared field is proposed. Each shoe produces only the output Juni commands through its intact physical channel. |
| Every activation scours a little ink from Juni's curls. Smooth sustained output wears slowly; rapid alternating microbursts and reverse braking roughen the channels faster. A fully broken curl produces no jet until a living user redraws and recharges it, while its intact partner continues to respond. Sustained output also tires Juni's concentration and legs. | Black specks appear during the first brake; the stationary microbursts visibly thin the favored right curl; the hard reverse brake removes its last line; the living redraw restores output. | These are costs of Juni's chosen medium and practice only, not a universal price of magic. |
| A shallow **three-point crown** drawn over an intact base wind curl is a one-use modifier. On living activation, it broadens that shoe's narrow stream into a plume centered beneath Juni's body. The plume dampens rotation and opposes a fall but cannot steer. | Juni explains the omitted crown before launch, draws it over the repaired right curl during free fall, and uses its broad plume in the recovery. | The crown has a fixed local response and no judgment. Juni must aim her body, supply power, and decide how to use the interval. |
| Once a crowned curl is activated, one visible point burns away per breath. The complete cycle lasts **exactly three of Juni's breaths** and consumes the crown. Juni must supply power throughout; cutting power early aborts the plume but still consumes the unused points, so the crown cannot be paused, saved, or restarted. When the third point disappears, the intact base curl remains and again produces its ordinary narrow jet on living input. | Dovek's launch questions establish the full lifecycle. In the crisis, breath one damps the corkscrew, breath two checks the fall and exposes the left heel, and breath three lets Juni redraw and charge the left curl. The last point disappears, the broad plume narrows, and both base curls resume balanced output. | Exact one-use lifecycle for Juni's local crown modification. It should not be generalized to other marks, media, or practices without later evidence. |
