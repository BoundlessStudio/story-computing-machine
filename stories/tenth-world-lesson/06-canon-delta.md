## Canon posture

The final story supports a narrowly local character history, one living-agent-operated crossing mechanism, and one town's storm-engine system. Ryo and Eiren use world as practical assignment language; the destinations' objective nature, count, location, and relationship to established shared-world eras remain unresolved.

Eiren is a living door-steward. People in at least one unspecified place called her a goddess after seeing her open exits, and frightened people prayed for an exit. Those cultural observations do not establish the continuing conjunction of worship, power, and authority required for objective godhood. Her taxonomic divinity status remains unresolved. The story establishes no relationship between Eiren and Queen Aurelia, the Goddess of Time, any divine government, or any established crossing system.

The door-filament and storm-engine mechanisms below are strictly local. They do not define universal rules for portals, magic, sacrifice, usefulness, praise, consent, storms, or the afterlife.
