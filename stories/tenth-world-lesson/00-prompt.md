# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] The purpose of Isekai is to reform the previously shut-in heroes into people that believe in their own importance and self. It’s clear that you haven’t learned your lesson yet, because you’ve been sent to your 10th world and that goddess is getting exhausted.**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Tenth World Needs No Hero
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person, centered on the repeatedly isekai'd hero.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Character-driven portal fantasy with dry divine comedy, emotional honesty, and a hopeful ending.
- Audience/content rating: Teen; no graphic sexual content or gore.
- Required elements: This is the protagonist's tenth assigned world; the goddess is visibly exhausted; earlier worlds demonstrate that competence and repeated heroism have not produced self-worth; the tenth world's conflict forces the protagonist to value themself apart from usefulness, victory, or external praise; the goddess has an interior motive beyond impatience; the protagonist makes a costly self-directed choice; the emotional lesson is dramatized rather than delivered only as exposition.
- Prohibited elements: A solution based solely on defeating a dark lord; a romance that substitutes another person's love for self-worth; treating shut-in behavior as moral failure; instant therapeutic cure; lore that silently contradicts current shared-universe authority.

## Assumptions

- The hero's original shut-in isolation is approached with compassion rather than ridicule.
- The ten worlds may be structurally separate from existing shared-universe locations unless current canon research identifies a compatible connection.
- Low-impact details not fixed by the prompt may be chosen during planning, with a preference for a coherent 2,500–4,000 word standalone story.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The tenth assignment changes what the protagonist chooses, not merely what they can defeat.
- The goddess's exhaustion matters causally to the climax and is not only a recurring joke.
- The ending shows durable first steps toward self-belief without claiming total recovery.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
