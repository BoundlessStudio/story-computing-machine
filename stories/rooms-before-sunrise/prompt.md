## Prompt

> [WP] In a coastal city grown from engineered coral and muscle, residents lease symbiotic apartments instead of owning them. A municipal pruning worker discovers that one housing tower has quietly grown illegal rooms around people erased from the census. The building cannot speak, and nobody knows whether it is protecting its residents or responding to something they placed inside it. The worker has until sunrise to classify the new growth as housing or disease.
