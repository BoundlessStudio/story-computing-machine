# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Forty-three minutes before sunrise in Aqessa,
  municipal pruning worker Mavek puts a diagnostic blade into what Mast Nine's
  lease map calls an empty service muscle and hears a spoon strike a bowl on
  the other side. The tower has grown a hidden spiral of rooms around seven
  people whom the census no longer recognizes. Before the tower's dawn
  circulation carries the anomalous tissue into its structural ribs, Mavek
  must make the required binary judgment: housing, which cannot be cut on
  sight, or disease, which must be excised immediately. Resolve the promise
  with Mavek filing one classification before sunrise while leaving the
  building's motive and the ultimate safety of the growth genuinely unknown.
- Intended reader experience: Begin in tactile body-maintenance unease, narrow
  into the intimacy of homes discovered inside a suspected lesion, and keep
  both interpretations materially plausible. The pressure should come from
  incompatible risks rather than a villain: a wrong cut may open occupied
  rooms and kill the tower, while a wrong reprieve may let diseased tissue
  spread. End with a private, accountable choice and continued practical
  uncertainty, not public proof, moral applause, or a repaired census.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led real-time inspection shaped as an inward spiral through the
    illegal rooms, followed by a shorter outward return in which the same
    evidence changes meaning without becoming conclusive.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: Mavek cares for living buildings by
    cutting early and cleanly; restraint can feel to her like neglect. Yet the
    tissue under her blade is already doing the intimate work of sheltering
    people whom her instruments insist are absent.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: Mavek immediately wants to
    stop any proliferative growth before the dawn pulse, preserve Mast Nine,
    and finish a classification she can defend. She also wants not to turn
    seven residents into collateral damage merely because they lack valid
    lease signatures. Her useful limit is a diagnostic habit of trusting
    tissue maps and touch before testimony: it makes her a precise pruner but
    leads her to begin a cut before asking who might be inside, and it gives
    her no practiced way to weigh inhabited function against an illegal cause.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: The tower's sunrise circulatory shift; a binary municipal form;
    simultaneous signs of reciprocal housing and host-consuming overgrowth;
    and a salvaged nursery plug the residents placed in the service muscle to
    coax one emergency room, after which the tower grew several more.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Imro, the resident who placed the plug,
    wants the rooms spared and initially hides it because she expects any
    admission of tampering to make the verdict automatic. She also lives in
    Mast Nine and does not want it sickened; once Mavek identifies growth
    radiating from the innermost room, Imro discloses the plug and is willing
    to remove it if that is the only way to save the tower. She cannot promise
    removal will not collapse the rooms around the other residents.
  - Pressure or change created: Every observation supports both readings. The
    rooms regulate breath, warmth, fresh water, and waste around particular
    bodies, but they have no lease nodules, draw mineral faster than adjacent
    legal apartments, and branch from tissue that resembles an early tumor.
    Cutting the plug is the fastest experiment and the least reversible act.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close third-person past through
    Mavek, intimate with pressure, smell, vibration, and trained hand judgment.
    The building never speaks, receives interior viewpoint, or performs an
    action that proves intention or sentience. Imro's motives become available
    only through conduct and speech; the other residents remain people with
    possessions and choices, not specimens in Mavek's test.
  - Time shape and compression: One continuous predawn hour. Let the inward
    inspection occupy most of the story, with the sunrise countdown marked by
    the tower's accelerating pulse and the changing pressure in Mavek's tool.
    The return through rooms already seen is brief and perceptually revised.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Establish through Mavek's ordinary work
    that Aqessa's residents lease responsive rooms through body-linked nodules
    rather than owning apartment tissue. Reveal occupants first, their
    census absence second, the rooms' mixed biological signs through the
    spiral, and the nursery plug only near the center after Imro's evasions
    become untenable. Do not reveal why the census erased these particular
    people beyond a bounded failed relocation reconciliation, and do not solve
    whether the plug, the residents' bodies, or the tower's own adaptive
    response generated the rooms. Separate legal status from biological
    classification only when Mavek needs to make the final choice.
  - Governing movement: Locate; begin to cut; hear; enter; count people the
    city does not; inspect inward; uncover the residents' intervention; refuse
    a destructive proof; retrace inhabited exchanges; classify; return to the
    day's ordinary work.
- Speculative surplus: Aqessa's apartment walls shed a clear salt film before
  the morning tide; it loosens glue and tarnishes fine metal, so tenants use
  barbed bone pegs instead of adhesive hooks and pruning crews wax their tool
  seams nightly. In the hidden rooms, improvised pegs and one ruined spoon give
  this biology domestic and occupational consequence unrelated to the central
  classification question.
- Structural distinction: A two-person predawn inspection that moves inward
  and then partially repeats itself outward. Its decisive action occurs in an
  occupied threshold, not a hearing, laboratory, control room, public crisis,
  or collective turn. There is no demonstration of the building's mind, crowd
  conversion, census correction, eviction ruling, or institutional rewrite.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: At the innermost room, Imro offers
  to pull the nursery plug so Mavek can watch what dies. Mavek stops her: the
  experiment would use seven occupied rooms and the tower itself as disposable
  evidence. Sitting briefly in the newest unfinished room, Mavek feels it open
  breath pores, wick sweat, and shape a water blister around an unregistered
  body—hers—while its mineral draw continues to strain the surrounding rib.
  The response proves neither protection nor cause. It does make the tissue's
  present function undeniable. On the outward walk, she confirms that the
  dangerous draw can be isolated and watched without the immediate full cut,
  then checks `housing` on the binary field report and separately flags the
  nursery plug and rib for non-destructive dawn follow-up.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: The classification prevents immediate
  pruning but does not grant leases, restore census entries, guarantee against
  eviction, or show that Mast Nine is healthy. Mavek attaches her name to the
  call and remains for the first daylight circulation; Imro keeps the plug in
  place under an isolation sleeve. The rooms hold through the first pulse, but
  the stressed rib still registers a slow mineral loss that will require
  another decision.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Mavek's blade teeth resting against a
  warm wall she has already named disease. End after sunrise on the outer ledge
  when the wall's ordinary contraction pinches her safety tether; Imro, still
  inside, taps the practical release rhythm through the tissue. Mavek backs up
  one foothold, frees the tether, and moves on to the next item on her route.

## Beats

1. Forty-three minutes before sunrise, Mavek finds an uncharted bulge in Mast
   Nine's service muscle. Its heat, fast mineral uptake, and lack of a lease
   nodule satisfy her first disease screen. She sets her blade, and a spoon
   clatters beyond the cut line; she stops before breaching the inner membrane.
2. Mavek opens a narrow maintenance fold instead. Imro confronts her from a
   furnished room and admits that seven people live in a chain of new chambers.
   Mavek's census reader returns no residents and her tower map returns no
   rooms. She imposes a no-touch interval until she completes the inspection;
   sunrise is now close enough for the wall pulse to be felt underfoot.
3. Moving inward with Imro, Mavek passes sleeping niches, water blisters, waste
   cilia, and heat folds adjusted to individual habits. Residents quietly pack
   what they can carry rather than treating Mavek as a rescuer. Between rooms,
   Mavek finds blind buds and mineral-starved structural tissue that resemble
   the early disease she was trained to remove.
4. The growth pattern converges on an unfinished central room. Imro first says
   the tower simply made it; when Mavek finds a graft seam, Imro produces the
   sleeved nursery plug salvaged after the failed relocation that also erased
   the residents from the census. They asked it for one room. Neither of them
   knows why Mast Nine continued growing.
5. With minutes remaining, Imro offers to remove the plug. Mavek recognizes
   that collapse, further growth, or no change would all arrive too late to
   save anyone from the experiment. She refuses the irreversible test and sits
   in the newest room long enough to experience its immediate exchange with
   her own unregistered body, alongside the continuing strain on the rib.
6. Mavek retraces the spiral and distinguishes treatable strain from grounds
   for emergency excision: she sleeves the plug in place, marks the stressed
   feeder for daylight monitoring, and records every occupied chamber without
   supplying the missing census history the system asks for.
7. Before sunrise, Mavek checks `housing`, signs the report, and tells Imro
   exactly what the decision does and does not protect. The tower's first dawn
   pulse passes through the rooms without resolving whether their growth is
   adaptation or pathology. On the exterior ledge, a routine contraction
   catches Mavek's tether; Imro's taps guide her free, and Mavek continues her
   route.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The story occupies one otherwise unspecified era of the locked
  deep-time shared world. Local knowledge may be incomplete, and altered civic
  records may function as oppression without becoming automatic truth. The
  census deletion does not erase the residents' identities or moral standing.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Aqessa's grown architecture, lease biology, nursery
  plugs, pruning classifications, and census system are local nonmagical
  technologies, not universal rules and not established as connected to
  Deepmarket, heartseeds, constructed Crown participants, or the unnamed city
  of eleven foundations. Mast Nine's sentience, intent, reason for growing the
  rooms, and long-term health remain unresolved. The story establishes no civil
  date, Galactic Cycle coordinate, wider geography, or chronology relative to
  another setting; `housing` resolves Mavek's required immediate classification
  without settling ownership, lawful tenancy, or future municipal action.
