## Prompt

> [WP] The age of starlight brought with it the advent of life, later evolving to intelligent civilization. The cosmos would give birth to many. Most were peaceful, one was not. Facing an enemy of universal proportions, the stars turned to the oldest among them. The first people. Humanity!
>
> Some context to think about: The Fermi Paradox stems from a philosophical question regarding why there appear to be no aliens in the universe that we have seen, given how many Earth-like planets exist in just our region of space alone. One potential answer to the problem is that Earth is simply the first planet to give rise to life, and – given time – many more worlds will one day follow.
