# Request

Humanity has lived within the colossal mountain city of Caelorum for as long as its histories remember. Generation after generation, its inhabitants have constructed increasingly magnificent buildings, bridges, and monuments, following architectural instructions engraved into the cavern walls. Nobody knows who wrote them, only that disobeying the designs brings disaster. When an apprentice architect notices that the city's structures form an enormous mechanism, she realizes the builders have spent thousands of years unknowingly assembling something. And now with it almost complete, she must discover whether their ancestors intended to build it or not.
