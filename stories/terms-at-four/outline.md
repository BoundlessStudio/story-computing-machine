# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: At four, a guest attends an abundant tea hosted by their cute, impeccably composed adult former colleague, co-designer of an orbital defense platform. Amid several teas, layered cake, cookies, and warm pie, the guest learns she controls its armed mode and holds six security officials elsewhere. The Prime Minister calls. Resolve the comic menace and credible danger through government acceptance, irreversible satellite disarmament, and every hostage freed.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The guest wants everyone alive, hoping their former colleague has not made violence her only language. Technical knowledge makes them an independent verifier; smoothing over her choices risks complicity. The hostess wants a detained civilian maintenance crew delivered to counsel and its refused arming orders filed in court. She wants the guest to witness that she keeps terms, not absolve her methods.
- Counterforce or complication: The Prime Minister must protect captives and population without mistaking promises for disarmament; the hostess will not surrender leverage before the crew is safe. One hostage needs medication, both remote sites are visible only through limited feeds, and orbital blackout constrains confirmation. Acceptance is coerced, not moral conversion.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close second-person past through the unnamed adult guest. Notice steam, porcelain, appetite, serving gestures, and audio delays. The guest understands status indicators but cannot know government deliberations, the hostess's contingencies, or anything outside the feeds. Her composure may be interpreted but never mind-read.
- Governing movement and time shape: About seventy minutes through successive courses: discovery at first pour, verification over cookies, negotiation with cake, reciprocal action during pie, then consequence. Keep the decisive action at the private table with two remote windows. End after disarmament, release, and the authorities' arrival, without speech or policy coda.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: The story-local satellite has one armed control lattice. Safe mode destroys its targeting key and ejects the lattice inert; shared telemetry confirms both, and ground control cannot restore them during the story. A separate release provides continuous video of all six hostages reaching waiting medical staff. Prerecorded images fail agreed live checks, but no feed proves motive.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Second-person closeness moves by servings, glances, status lights, and verifiable facts, letting sweet abundance and coercive machinery occupy the same attention without coy winks.
- Conversational texture: Tea talk stays practical—preferences, portions, cooling cups—while the former colleagues use unfinished references and careful corrections. Remote negotiation is slower, procedural, and tied to observable actions; captive speech is brief and need-led.
- Rhetorical ownership: The guest thinks in failure paths and moral distinctions; the hostess uses hosting logistics, exact conditions, and selective memory; the Prime Minister speaks in sequencing and state obligation. Captives do not comment on the premise.
- Pressure behavior: The hostess becomes formally courteous and misses listening cues; the guest interrupts and grows direct; the Prime Minister shortens clauses and repeats dependencies. Verification silences outweigh polished menace.
- Anti-default: Do not make everyone an arch, quotable supervillain, turn cuteness into childishness, or let cake jokes trivialize captives. Keep lines context-bound, unevenly fluent, and often single-purpose.

## Beats

1. The guest expects a strained reunion and finds multiple teas, tall cake, abundant cookies, and covered pie. Establish the hostess's adult charm, precision, and shared history. A display among the dishes reveals the satellite link before she explains the stakes.
2. Over tea and cookies, the hostess states her demands and permits inspection of telemetry and a live hostage feed. The guest detects one captive's medication need and makes access a condition of continuing as witness; she arranges it without turning care into a debating point.
3. As she cuts cake, the guest tests for loops, blind angles, and hidden armed pathways. Intimate subtext comes through remembered preferences, avoided history, and the guest's refusal to soften judgment. A hostage speaks only to confirm identity or need. The threat, people, and safe mode prove real; future good faith cannot.
4. The Prime Minister calls while cake remains on the table. Negotiation concerns sequence, not speeches: counsel transfer, court filing, key destruction, lattice ejection, door release, and medical retrieval must overlap. The guest rejects conditions that leave captives disposable and wording that substitutes intent for action.
5. During pie, feeds show the maintenance crew reach counsel and the filing accepted. The Prime Minister explicitly confirms the government has met the demands. The hostess begins safe mode; an ambiguous indicator makes the guest distinguish blackout from abort. She commits to irreversibility, and telemetry confirms disarmament.
6. The hostess releases the locks. Hold on all six hostages emerging, being counted, and reaching medical staff; permit relief, anger, and disorientation rather than gratitude. The Prime Minister confirms release and ends the call without moral agreement. Verification closes only after both outcomes are complete.
7. Authorities arrive; immunity was never offered. The hostess gives no manifesto or claim that arrest proves virtue. The guest refuses absolution but stays for a factual statement. End with the tea party over and every promised action resolved; prosecution and later court outcomes remain bounded consequences.
