# Outline

## Story

- Proposed reader-facing title: *Terms at Four* (provisional; the prompt remains authoritative).
- Premise and central promise: An unnamed adult narrator accepts a four-o'clock reconciliation tea from Clemency Pruett, a very cute adult former friend whose ribbons, exquisite manners, and excellent baking are genuine. Once the narrator has tasted the tea, cake, cookies, and pie, the Prime Minister calls: the government will grant Clemency safe passage, amnesty for three arrested adult accomplices, and release of their frozen defense fund if she disarms a hijacked orbital mirror and frees six adult officials held in the house's service wing. Clemency must decide whether to accept after realizing that her plan also depends on the narrator voluntarily leaving with her. She disarms the satellite and releases the hostages; the government honors its terms; the narrator refuses to go.
- Intended reader experience: Move from sensuous, overmanaged comfort through comic shock into dry suspense, with every courtesy readable as care, control, or both. Keep the food delicious and Clemency charming, frightened, capable, and culpable—not a disguised monster or innocent mascot. End in relief and personal loss, not applause for either side.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led subtraction in near-real time. The bargain advances only as Clemency relinquishes the armed ground track, locked hostage doors, the narrator's usefulness as cover, and her belief that choices offered inside her arrangement are free.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: The narrator hopes the invitation means their old friendship survived; Clemency wants the one person who knew her before the crime to choose her afterward. She can prepare for every appetite except an answer she cannot control.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: The narrator wants the hostages freed and Clemency kept alive, while also wanting proof that the invitation was friendship rather than recruitment. Appeasing politeness buys time but lets Clemency count silence as consent. Clemency wants the bargain, protection for her accomplices, and the narrator in the escape car, while retaining leverage against betrayal. Her limit is choreographed generosity: she sincerely mistakes good options inside boundaries she alone sets for freedom.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: Mutual distrust under a thirty-five-minute firing window. The repurposed agricultural mirror is nearing the one ground track from which its focused light could ignite a fuel depot near the capital. The Prime Minister will not send Clemency's car until all six doors report open and the mirror is feathered; Clemency fears that acting first leaves her defenseless inside an armed perimeter.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: The unnamed Prime Minister protects the hostages, public, and officers and offers narrow concessions because the threatened harm exceeds their cost; distrust follows from Clemency's crimes, not hidden villainy. Clemency protects herself and three accomplices she believes the government would discard, but that fear does not make confinement benign. She retains the agency to leave under the bargain without confessing or reforming.
  - Pressure or change created: Clemency planned to take the narrator as an apparently willing witness and quiet human shield. A refusal forces her either to create a seventh hostage or relinquish leverage without the validating companionship for which the tea was staged.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close first-person past through the unnamed narrator, intimate with sensory pleasure, old soothing habits, and dawning recognition of coercion. Keep the perimeter, hostages, Prime Minister, accomplices, and Clemency's fallback plans exterior. Clemency uses diminutives and hostess questions when frightened, then orbital precision when control slips; the Prime Minister speaks in plain deadlines. The narrator reads social pressure better than engineering and cannot disarm the mirror.
  - Time shape and compression: Roughly forty continuous minutes, from the first cup just before four until Clemency's transport leaves. Render the required foods, call, hostage check, private pause, refusal, compliance, and departure in scene; compress repeated status checks through a clock and ground-track display.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Establish Clemency as an adult and the prior friendship before the call. Reveal the satellite, hostages, demands, and conditional agreement early rather than saving the prompt as a final twist. Show all six hostages alive on a silent feed with concrete needs and anger, never as proof of Clemency's kindness. Then reveal the mirror's agricultural use and the passport, coat, and escape seat prepared for the narrator. Leave later legal consequences uncertain, not whether Clemency complied.
  - Governing movement: Taste the immaculate spread; take the Prime Minister's call; verify the hostages; hear the government reject Clemency's automatic-exchange script; discover the narrator's packed escape place; understand the tea as farewell and audition; ask what a refusal would mean; stop smoothing the conversation and refuse; walk toward the unlocked front door; remain nearby for the hostages instead of joining Clemency or fleeing; watch her release the six people and feather the mirror before the car arrives; hear the government confirm its concessions; decline one last invitation; turn toward a released hostage's immediate need.
- Speculative surplus: The orbital mirror normally adds moving bands of winter light to greenhouse country. Growers publish mirror-time beside weather reports, produce ripens in strips, and moths sometimes emerge at the wrong hour. During the hijack, one scheduled growing cycle is lost. None of this authenticates the bargain or solves the standoff.
- Structural distinction: A real-time etiquette scene organized by relinquishments, not escape attempts, public technical proof, crowd conversion, or institutional reform. The national apparatus remains one voice and a few indicators; the turn is a refused invitation and Clemency's response, not a mechanical demonstration or food symbol.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: The narrator finds the prepared passport wallet and travel coat. Clemency calls the second seat a choice but cannot say what happens if the answer is no. The narrator says that beautiful treatment inside a sealed plan is not freedom, refuses the car, and walks toward the front door. Clemency reaches for the lock control but stops. Without the approval she wanted, she releases the six hostages and disarms anyway.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: The hostages survive, the strike is prevented, and the government honors the narrow concessions. Kidnapping, possible later liability outside the agreement, the accomplices' choices, and Clemency's future remain unsettled. She leaves alive and unrepentant but alone; the narrator remains to help and testify, with no promised reconciliation.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Clemency offering abundant decorative choices after arranging every consequential condition. End after she accepts the narrator's meaningful no, as the narrator turns from her departing car to ask an exhausted released hostage what they need first.

## Beats

1. Just before four, the narrator enters an unnamed hill house through a visible security cordon. Clemency's cute adult presentation and lavish homemade spread revive old affection; shielded windows and locked interior doors seed unease.
2. The narrator tastes the tea, cake, cookies, and pie while avoiding direct questions. Clemency repeatedly offers small either-or choices and calls the tea a reconciliation.
3. On speaker, the Prime Minister accepts Clemency's demands if she disarms the orbital mirror and releases all six hostages within the approaching firing window.
4. A silent room-by-room feed shows six living, angry adults with food and medication. Clemency refuses an audio channel that might carry instructions; practical care has not made them free.
5. The Prime Minister rejects Clemency's simultaneous-exchange program and promises the concessions on the authenticated call, but no car until the doors and mirror are safe. Neither side gets certainty.
6. During the muted pause, the narrator finds a second travel coat and passport wallet. Clemency admits the tea was meant to end with them leaving together and to prove that someone stayed willingly.
7. The narrator says caring about Clemency does not excuse the hostages or imply consent to leave. When asked what happens after a refusal, Clemency offers more cake instead of an answer.
8. The narrator refuses and walks toward the front door. Clemency's hand reaches the lock control, then stops. The narrator remains in the entry hall for the coming release rather than fleeing or joining her.
9. Clemency opens all six rooms, waits for clear door sensors, and feathers the mirror. Keep the operations brief; the action is relinquishing control. The Prime Minister confirms the concessions and dispatches the promised car.
10. Clemency leaves after one last unembellished invitation. The narrator declines, then turns to the first released hostage's practical need; stop before debriefing, public reaction, or judgment of the deal.

## Continuity

- Canon used: An otherwise unspecified modern-like, low-magic era on the one physical world; it may resemble contemporary Earth without being literal real-world Earth. The orbital mirror is story-local technology. No magic, divinity, known ultrahuman, or universal hidden authority is required.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Keep the country, capital, hill house, Prime Minister, hostages, and accomplices unnamed. Clemency Pruett is a new adult person with no connection to Solstice Evening Bell, the era-local `ultrahuman` category, or the unnamed hazard bureaus. Do not imply that the government, mirror network, bargain, or legal terms recur beyond this story. Engineering and later consequences may remain local and unresolved, but the mirror's one dangerous focusing limit, deadline, release, disarmament, and government fulfillment must be causally clear.
