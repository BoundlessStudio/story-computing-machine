# Prompt

[WP] They call her Onyx, the last daughter of a people who turned their bodies to living stone rather than kneel. Every wound leaves a vein of gold across her skin—and after a 100 years of war, she is tried of being a weapon and want to retried to peaceful life.

- Audience: broadly accessible adult and teen readers.
