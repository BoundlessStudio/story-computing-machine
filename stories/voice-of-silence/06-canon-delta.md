# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.
Every item below is local to this story's unnamed callwright culture and era
unless its scope says otherwise.

## New characters or character facts

- **VOS-01 — Kethra (proposed).** **Fact:** Kethra is a mute callwright who
  hears, communicates fluently through sign, slate, expression, and field
  diagrams, supplies living power as a shaper, designed the voiced lattice, and
  earns the local office of archmage through an emergency rescue. **Local
  scope:** Kethra and the unnamed college witnessed in this story only.
  **Constraints:** Her muteness has no stated cause, is not cured or bypassed,
  and is not an intellectual, ethical, emotional, or magical deficiency; she
  cannot release these local spells without a consenting living audible caller.
  **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-14 and VOS-20 through VOS-22.
  **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/characters.md`.
- **VOS-02 — Ulan (proposed).** **Fact:** Ulan is one living caller who works
  with Kethra, communicates in speech and sign, states and enforces a two-call
  safety limit after the opening demonstration, calls `Airclear` and
  `Safe Descent`, uses **wait**, and gives a fresh **yes** before the final call.
  **Local scope:** Ulan's participation in this college examination and rescue.
  **Constraints:** He does not shape, target, or power Kethra's spells; his
  voice, consent, labor, fatigue, and later choice to rest remain his own.
  **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-13, VOS-17, VOS-19, and VOS-20.
  **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/characters.md`.
- **VOS-03 — Dorram (proposed).** **Fact:** Dorram is the college's chief
  examiner, a living shaper and caller who self-authorizes and releases
  `Roofstay`, recognizes his dangerous load-targeting error, yields incident
  command to Kethra, voluntarily calls her `Crown Release`, and applies the
  local archmage criteria without pity. **Local scope:** Dorram and this one
  examination/rescue. **Constraints:** His staff only marks geometry; his spell
  is powered and sustained by him, and his error does not establish general
  examiner hostility or disability prejudice. **Dependencies:** VOS-06 through
  VOS-10, VOS-16, VOS-18, VOS-20, and VOS-21. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/characters.md`.

## New locations

- **VOS-04 — unnamed callwright college and examination tower (proposed).**
  **Fact:** An unnamed college in an unnamed fantasy-era region teaches the
  local callwright practice. Its examination tower has occupied galleries, a
  high crown, a central exit, a lower open court, brass nodes, and speaking
  rails; during Kethra's examination, an old western joint fails, the crown is
  deliberately separated, and the court becomes the rescue and recovery area.
  **Local scope:** This one college site and incident; no real-world location,
  calendar date, polity, or relationship to another magical institution is
  asserted. **Constraints:** The tower, rails, and masonry are nonliving and do
  not choose, consent, power, sustain, or cast. **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through
  VOS-20. **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/locations.md`.

## New factions or cultural facts

- **VOS-05 — local callwright practice and work roles (proposed).** **Fact:** At
  this college, a `shaper` supplies power and direction, a `caller` supplies the
  required living audible spell name, and a prepared one-spell pattern is a
  `cell`; one participant can occupy both living roles if the same authorization
  procedure is completed. **Local scope:** The unnamed college's callwright
  practice only. **Constraints:** These are lowercase work roles and a bounded
  activation grammar, not character aliases, universal magical categories, or
  evidence about other cultures. **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-10.
  **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/factions.md`.
- **VOS-06 — local archmage office (proposed).** **Fact:** This college awards
  the lowercase office of `archmage` for original spell architecture, precise
  control of power and consequences, a method others can learn safely, and
  discretionary command that values life above prestige, property, and the
  candidate's own work. **Local scope:** This college and its examination only.
  **Constraints:** The office is not a universe-wide rank or power class; the
  panel recognizes demonstrated competence rather than conferring new magical
  capacity. **Dependencies:** VOS-05 and VOS-20 through VOS-22. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/factions.md`.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **VOS-07 — local living shaper/caller activation (proposed).** **Fact:** A
  spell in this callwright practice remains incomplete until a living shaper
  supplies power, pattern, target, and direction and an authorized living
  caller audibly speaks the full registered spell name. The call closes the
  incomplete pattern but does not take ownership of its shaping. **Local
  scope:** This college's callwright spells only. **Constraints:** A spoken call
  is causal, not ceremonial; neither participant is equipment, and no rule for
  other magical practices is proposed. **Dependencies:** A living shaper,
  living caller, authorization under VOS-08, and valid call under VOS-09.
  **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-08 — one-spell call-link (proposed).** **Fact:** Shaper and caller touch
  the same brass node, the shaper marks one registered spell, and the caller
  signs **yes** to authorize that caller for that spell and cell. The link lasts
  at most thirty breaths, ends when either participant breaks it, and is spent
  by its release. **Local scope:** This college's callwright procedure only.
  **Constraints:** Each cell requires separate authorization; earlier assent is
  not permanent, and the nonliving node does not choose or consent.
  **Dependencies:** VOS-07 and VOS-14. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-09 — ready window and sound path (proposed).** **Fact:** After the
  shaper's two-finger ready sign, the full registered name must be spoken in
  order on one living exhalation within twelve heartbeats, either directly into
  the shaped acoustic field or through an intact speaking rail. Conversational
  volume with clear syllables is sufficient in the witnessed `Weightward`
  release. **Local scope:** This college's callwright procedure and witnessed
  castings only. **Constraints:** Writing, signing, thought, or sound outside
  the active field does not substitute for the audible living call.
  **Dependencies:** VOS-07, VOS-08, and VOS-14. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-10 — invalid calls and collapsed patterns (proposed).** **Fact:** A late
  call, broken syllable, silence, or severed sound path produces no spell; the
  incomplete pattern collapses and still fatigues the shaper. Stored commands,
  recordings, and later echoes cannot complete a current living link.
  **Local scope:** This college's callwright practice only. **Constraints:** No
  synthetic-voice rule beyond the witnessed rejection of nonliving stored sound
  is proposed. **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-09 and VOS-14.
  **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-11 — shaping and calling costs (proposed).** **Fact:** Strong or failed
  shaping causes fatigue, tremor, pain, slowed movement, loss of precision, and
  possible collapse; three major releases in rapid sequence leave Kethra unable
  to remain standing. Calling consumes breath and can roughen or exhaust a
  throat, so a caller may set an individual call limit. **Local scope:** The
  witnessed callwright work and participants only. **Constraints:** These are
  practical local costs, not a universal emotional, moral, or metaphysical
  price for magic. **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-10. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-12 — consent and field-sign protocol (proposed).** **Fact:** Callers use
  **yes**, **wait**, and crossed hands for **stop**; stop cancels the relevant
  cell immediately without penalty or argument, wait delays action, and fresh
  assent is requested when circumstances materially change. **Local scope:**
  Kethra's college procedure and rescue command only. **Constraints:** The
  mechanism does not establish a universal ethics rule or magically compel or
  certify consent; agency is preserved through participant choices and conduct.
  **Dependencies:** VOS-08 and shared visual communication. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-13 — voiced lattice (proposed).** **Fact:** Kethra's original voiced
  lattice lets one living shaper hold at most three isolated spell cells,
  aligned to separate callers and sound paths, while retaining each spell's
  power, map, and target. **Local scope:** Kethra's demonstrated design at this
  college. **Constraints:** Every cell still needs its own authorization,
  ready sign, audible living call, breath, and working sound path; holding cells
  is physically demanding, and a failed cell can jeopardize the sequence. The
  design does not give Kethra a voice. **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-12
  and VOS-14. **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`.
  **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-14 — brass speaking rails and nodes (proposed).** **Fact:** Brass rails
  and nodes mark spell geometry and carry or modestly amplify a present living
  voice into a shaped acoustic field. A small node can be removed and carried
  into direct speaking range while its shaper maintains the target geometry.
  **Local scope:** This college's callwright apparatus. **Constraints:** The
  apparatus is nonliving; it cannot store a valid call, consent, choose, supply
  power, target, sustain, or cast, and a severed rail carries no call.
  **Dependencies:** A living shaper and caller under VOS-07 through VOS-09.
  **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-15 — `Weightward` (proposed).** **Fact:** This local spell moves an
  existing mapped load along grounded rails into sound foundation. **Local
  scope:** The two witnessed releases at the examination tower. **Constraints:**
  It creates no support, and a bad map can redirect weight dangerously.
  **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-09 and sound load-bearing geometry.
  **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-16 — `Roofstay` (proposed).** **Fact:** This local spell temporarily
  holds selected roof geometry while its living shaper sustains it. Dorram
  authorizes, shapes, calls, powers, and sustains the witnessed release himself.
  **Local scope:** Dorram's witnessed emergency use at this tower. **Constraints:**
  The staff only marks geometry; the spell creates dangerous transferred load
  when aimed without a complete structural map, and ending it out of sequence
  would drop the roof. **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-09 and a living
  sustaining shaper. **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`.
  **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-17 — `Airclear` (proposed).** **Fact:** This local spell clears dust
  from one mapped breathing corridor. **Local scope:** Kethra and Ulan's one
  witnessed release at the tower. **Constraints:** It does not clear the whole
  tower and consumes shaper strength and one of Ulan's safe calls.
  **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-09 and an intact short rail.
  **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-18 — `Crown Release` (proposed).** **Fact:** This local spell separates
  selected masonry joints along Kethra's exact mapped rails, allowing the tower
  crown to peel outward. **Local scope:** Kethra and Dorram's one witnessed
  release at the tower. **Constraints:** It is destructive, cannot safely
  separate the joints while `Roofstay` pins them, and destroys Kethra's ornate
  lattice with the crown. **Dependencies:** VOS-07 through VOS-09, grounded
  gallery load under VOS-15, and Dorram ending VOS-16 first. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/rules.md`.
- **VOS-19 — `Safe Descent` (proposed).** **Fact:** This local spell slows a
  falling platform and nearby crown stones for a few breaths while Kethra
  steers their descent. **Local scope:** Kethra and Ulan's one witnessed release
  at the tower. **Constraints:** It slows rather than stops, cannot levitate
  indefinitely, needs the active sound path restored within the twelve-heartbeat
  window, and requires continued shaping and steering. **Dependencies:**
  VOS-07 through VOS-14 and the prior separation under VOS-18. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/rules.md`.

## Timeline events

- **VOS-20 — examination-tower rescue (proposed).** **Fact:** During one
  undated archmage examination, an old western joint fails under the occupied
  gallery's ordinary weight. Kethra honors an apprentice caller's **stop**,
  diagnoses the dangerous transfer caused by Dorram's `Roofstay`, dismantles
  her masterwork, and commands the ordered sequence `Weightward`, end
  `Roofstay`, `Crown Release`, and `Safe Descent`; the people escape with
  bruises and shallow cuts while the tower crown is lost. **Local scope:** One
  undated event at VOS-04 in an unnamed era. **Constraints:** No wider disaster,
  public knowledge, calendar coordinate, or institutional recurrence is
  established. **Dependencies:** VOS-01 through VOS-19. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/timeline.md`.
- **VOS-21 — Kethra qualifies as archmage (proposed).** **Fact:** After the
  rescue, Dorram and the panel find Kethra qualified under all four local
  criteria because of her architecture, diagnosis, teachable protocol,
  sacrifice, sequencing, and incident command. **Local scope:** The immediate
  aftermath of VOS-20 at this college. **Constraints:** The ruling does not
  cure Kethra, credit her with the callers' labor, or establish rank in any
  other tradition. **Dependencies:** VOS-01, VOS-02, VOS-03, VOS-06, and
  VOS-20. **Provenance:** `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:** `universe/timeline.md`.
- **VOS-22 — post-rescue safety roster and briefing (proposed).** **Fact:** The
  next morning, Kethra begins her first safety briefing as an archmage using a
  caller roster with voluntary assignments, individual voice limits, and the
  signs **yes**, **wait**, and prominently placed **stop**. **Local scope:**
  Kethra's first post-examination teaching action at this college. **Constraints:**
  The story does not establish adoption beyond the gathered students or a
  permanent college-wide reform. **Dependencies:** VOS-01, VOS-06, VOS-12,
  VOS-20, and VOS-21. **Provenance:**
  `stories/voice-of-silence/05-story.md`. **Suggested smallest topical target:**
  `universe/timeline.md`.
