# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] In a world where people must call out the names of their spells to use them, a mute person has managed to become an archmage.**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Voice of Silence
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person, centered on the mute archmage.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Character-driven fantasy with tension, wonder, and a decisive climax.
- Audience/content rating: Teen; non-graphic fantasy danger.
- Required elements: Spellcasting genuinely depends on a spell name being called out; the protagonist's muteness remains a material constraint; their archmage status is earned and demonstrated through consequential action; the mechanism respects current shared-universe authority; the story reaches a complete resolution.
- Prohibited elements: A last-minute cure for muteness; treating muteness as intellectual or moral deficiency; resolving the premise as an effortless technicality; contradicting `LOCKED` or `CANON` authority.

## Assumptions

- "Mute" means the protagonist cannot produce ordinary spoken language; it does not by itself define hearing, cognition, literacy, or magical aptitude.
- The protagonist remains mute at the end unless current canon requires a narrower interpretation.
- Low-impact worldbuilding details may be invented when canon is silent, but they remain local to this story unless separately promoted.
- The default deliverable is a coherent 2,500-4,000 word standalone short story.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
