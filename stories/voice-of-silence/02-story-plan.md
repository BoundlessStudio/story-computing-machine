# Story plan

## Premise and promise

In one unnamed fantasy-era culture, every spell in the local callwright practice remains inert until its registered name is audibly spoken by a living caller. Kethra, a hearing woman who cannot produce ordinary spoken language, has reached the final archmage examination by becoming an exceptional shaper, designer, teacher, and incident commander. Her original **voiced lattice** lets one shaper prepare several bounded spell cells for separate consenting callers, but it does not give Kethra a voice, make recordings magical, or remove her dependence on other people.

A ceremonial examination becomes a structural emergency. Kethra must abandon the masterwork meant to win her title, honor callers who withdraw, recruit and coordinate replacements, and choose a destructive rescue over prestige. Three voluntary living calls are causal to the climax, while Kethra's diagnosis, power, sequencing, and judgment make them effective. She earns the local office of archmage without cure or hidden magical speech.

## Story controls

- **Length:** 2,500–4,000 words; six planned scenes totaling about 3,150 words.
- **POV/tense:** Close third on Kethra; past tense.
- **Tone/rating:** Character-driven fantasy, tense examination drama and wonder; teen, with dust, falling stone, bruises, fear, and temporary voice strain but no graphic injury.
- **Scope:** An unnamed callwright college in one region of the persistent shared world. Never claim that this practice governs other cultures or systems.
- **Communication:** Kethra hears and uses a fluent local sign language, slate, expression, and pre-agreed field signs. Her muteness has no stated cause and is never a proxy for cognition, virtue, courage, or aptitude.
- **Ending:** People escape, the tower crown is deliberately sacrificed, and Kethra earns the local archmage office. She remains mute and the audible-name rule remains intact.

### Exact local spell controls

1. **Living shaper:** A living practitioner supplies power, holds a learned pattern in body and attention, and selects target and effect. No diagram, rail, rod, staff, or tower can begin, choose, power, or sustain a spell.
2. **One-spell call-link:** Shaper and caller deliberately touch the same brass node while the shaper marks one registered spell and the caller signs **yes**. The link authorizes only that caller, spell, and cell. It expires on use, when either participant breaks it, or after thirty breaths. It transmits no thoughts and cannot force breath or speech.
3. **Ready window:** After the shaper gives a two-finger ready sign, the full registered name must be spoken on one living exhalation within twelve heartbeats. The caller must be in the shaped acoustic field, directly or through an intact speaking rail.
4. **Audibility:** Conversational loudness at the node is enough; accent and pitch may vary, but every syllable must be intelligible and ordered. Background speech cannot fire an unprepared cell, and a linked caller can decline.
5. **Release:** The live sound closes the shaper's incomplete pattern. The shaper remains power source and director; the caller is a causal living participant, not equipment.
6. **Failure:** Silence, a partial or wrong name, a late call, or a call that cannot reach the node produces no spell. The pattern collapses and still fatigues the shaper. A recording or later echo fails because it is not a present living caller completing an active link.
7. **Costs:** Strong shaping causes Kethra progressive shaking, slowed movement, and lost precision; three major releases in succession put her near collapse. Calling strains breath and throat. Dust or repeated calls may leave a willing caller unable to complete a name.
8. **Consent:** Callers retain signed **yes**, **wait**, and crossed-hands **stop** choices. Kethra drops a cell immediately on stop. The mechanism does not magically certify ethics; Kethra's protocol and conduct preserve agency, and no one is punished for withdrawing.
9. **Voiced lattice:** Kethra can hold at most three isolated cells, each separately authorized to one caller and spell. Each still needs its own ready sign, living audible name, breath, and sound path. One failed cell drains her and can spoil the whole sequence.
10. **Speaking rails:** Brass tubes and nodes carry or modestly amplify live voice. They are nonliving transmitters only and cannot store a valid call, consent, decide, or cast.

## Character engine

### Kethra

- **Want:** Earn the archmage office under the existing examination, proving that her mastery is not someone else's casting.
- **Need:** Stop treating solitary performance as the only defensible competence. Claim ethical coordination, preparation, and judgment as her own mastery without pretending dependence has vanished.
- **Pressure:** Every release visibly needs another voice; a collapse removes prepared callers, sound paths, and rehearsal. Delay threatens lives, while preserving her showpiece threatens the exit.
- **Strengths:** Structural diagnosis, spell architecture, exact preparation, fluent visual command, respect for consent, and stamina to shape three cells.
- **Vulnerability:** Fear that accepting help lets examiners credit the voice instead of the mind and power directing it.
- **Arc:** She begins making interdependence look like solo casting. She ends by openly honoring refusal and commanding a collaborative rescue. The title validates judgment, not performed independence.

### Ulan

- **Want:** Help Kethra receive fair recognition.
- **Need/pressure:** Treat his voice as his contribution, not a debt or part of Kethra's equipment. He states a two-call limit, uses **wait**, and later freely chooses the final call after rest and a new consent check.
- **Function:** Makes voice cost and partnership concrete. He is trained to call but does not shape or power Kethra's spells.

### Dorram

- **Want:** Test whether a candidate preserves life when collaborators fail.
- **Error:** He equates resilience with self-sufficiency and casts a familiar `Roofstay` before reading the whole load.
- **Turn:** Kethra's diagnosis makes him yield incident command, end his own spell on her signal, and voluntarily call hers.
- **Function:** A demanding, corrigible examiner rather than a disability caricature. His final ruling cites evidence witnessed on page.

### Stakes and local archmage criteria

Immediate stakes are the gallery occupants, callers, blocked exit, and Kethra's failing stamina. Personal stakes are her title and fear of misattributed mastery. Institutional stakes are whether the college recognizes disciplined coordination as command.

The office is local. A candidate must demonstrate: (1) original spell architecture; (2) precise control of power and consequences; (3) a method others can learn safely; and (4) discretionary command that puts life ahead of prestige, property, and the candidate's own design. The emergency tests all four; the panel confirms rather than manufactures her achievement.

## Causal arc

Kethra's opening `Weightward` proves that Ulan's audible living call releases a spell she shapes and powers. Because Dorram doubts whether her lattice survives caller loss, he retains a resilience trial. Because Kethra enforces consent, she records Ulan's limit and lets an apprentice withdraw when the tower cracks. That leaves her without the rehearsed sequence. Dorram fills the gap with a personally shaped and called `Roofstay`; it stops immediate collapse but transfers lethal force to the exit column.

Kethra sees that tower and exit cannot both be preserved. She dismantles her examination piece and converts its rails into a rescue lattice. Because callers cannot be swapped casually, she must explain each cell, gain separate assent, and accept Ulan's **wait**. `Weightward` first grounds the gallery load; only then can Dorram end `Roofstay` and call `Crown Release`; only after separation can Ulan call `Safe Descent`. A broken rail forces Kethra to reposition the final cell while holding it, so muteness and dependence remain materially dangerous. The rescue succeeds because callers choose and complete their names and because Kethra correctly authors, powers, orders, and adapts the effects.

## Setup and payoff

- Live voice travels through brass in Scene 1; broken rails in Scenes 3 and 5 prove stored sound is no substitute.
- The twelve-heartbeat rule becomes the final countdown.
- **Yes / wait / stop** is rehearsed, then the apprentice's stop and Ulan's wait cost time and are honored; his last yes is demonstrably chosen.
- Ulan's two-call budget pays through `Airclear` and `Safe Descent`; afterward he chooses non-vocal communication.
- `Crown Release` and `Safe Descent` appear on the Scene 2 emergency slate, preventing last-minute rules.
- The hairline crack primes Kethra's faster diagnosis without making her infallible.
- Dorram's independence challenge causes the trial; his successful but badly targeted solo spell reveals solo speed's limit. His later caller role completes his reversal.
- Kethra treats the ornate lattice as proof she deserves recognition. Destroying it proves the judgment the office requires.
- The final seal marks earned status; the consent roster shows neither muteness nor collaboration's constraints vanished.

## Canon constraints

- Scope every rule to this local callwright practice. Never say `all magic`, `every mage`, or a world-spanning law.
- Keep the system independent of Irix, Cinder Annex, House Bellweather, Nine Eaves, Northbridge, Solstice, Ravel, Glass Gate practice, Calvea's Calling the Name, Sablewake, and Ersene calls.
- Spell labels are local activation grammar, not personal true names or universal name-power.
- Kethra is living shaper and power source for her spells. Callers are living, consenting causal participants. Rails, nodes, diagrams, staff, masonry, and slate only focus, target, or transmit.
- `Roofstay` is visibly shaped, called, and sustained by living Dorram; its danger comes from target judgment, not autonomous magic.
- Recordings, synthetic voices, later echoes, written names, signs, thoughts, lip movement, and silent exceptions never release spells. Every successful release includes its audible call on page.
- Kethra remains unable to produce ordinary speech. No cure, borrowed mouth, possession, magical voice, or claim that silence casts.
- Treat sign and slate as full communication. Access friction may add pressure but never imply ignorance, passivity, purity, or deficient personhood.
- `archmage` is a lowercase local office, not a universe-wide power class.
- Apply caller consent consistently. Callers have limits, choices, safety, recognition, and care; none is a familiar, construct, battery, compelled choir member, or person-like tool.
- Keep danger non-graphic and consequence-centered. Dorram may be wrong without becoming a sneering abuse spectacle.
- All inventions remain local and proposed unless later promoted.

## Failure modes to watch

- **Universalization:** Keep every magic claim local.
- **Cosmetic calls:** Put every successful name audibly on page; signs and writing never substitute.
- **Apparatus as caster:** Rails transmit live sound only and never replay a valid call, decide, or become person-like.
- **Effortless workaround:** Preserve authorization time, twelve heartbeats, rail breaks, fatigue, voice limits, and inability to cast when nobody can or chooses to call.
- **Caller as equipment:** Give limits, opt-in, wait/stop decisions, consequences, thanks, and care. Kethra never presumes consent.
- **Agency by magical fiat:** The link cannot compel or certify consent; choices and conduct do.
- **Hidden cure:** No lattice voice, ventriloquism, recovered speech, or non-vocal loophole. Kethra remains mute.
- **Archmage by applause:** Title follows the shown rubric and Kethra's invention, power, diagnosis, sequencing, teaching, sacrifice, and command.
- **Self-sufficiency moral:** Collaboration is real mastery, not consolation; dependence remains consequential.
- **Disability shorthand:** Do not conflate muteness with deafness, explain its cause unnecessarily, romanticize silence, or make access a joke.
- **Examiner caricature:** Dorram's standard has safety logic; he is corrected by evidence, accepts command, takes caller risk, and awards without pity.
- **Causal blur:** Preserve climax order: `Weightward` grounds the gallery; Dorram ends `Roofstay`; `Crown Release` separates the unloaded ring; `Safe Descent` lowers it.
- **Name drift:** Use only `Kethra`, `Ulan`, and `Dorram` as personal forms; keep roles lowercase and re-run the name gate for additions.
