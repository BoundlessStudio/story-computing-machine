# User request

In a realm where everyone can see the supernatural, the dead walk openly among the living, distinguished by the skeletal faces they acquire upon death. One extraordinary woman has spent three hundred years painting a skull upon her perfectly living face, disguising herself as one of the dead. She has danced with forgotten kings, attended the funerals of civilizations, and become a beloved figure in the underworld. Nobody has questioned her existence until a newly appointed Reaper discovers her secret. When he tries to claim her soul, he learns why she has hidden among the dead for centuries.
