## Verbatim writing prompt

> [WP] Life with a Girlfriend with Shrinking Powers

## Story controls

- Working title: Life with a Girlfriend with Shrinking Powers
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close third person, centered on the non-shrinking partner.
- Tense: Past.
- Tone and genre: Warm romantic speculative fiction with domestic comedy,
  escalating physical stakes, and an emotionally sincere resolution.
- Audience/content rating: Teen / PG-13.
- Required elements: An established adult couple; shrinking as a repeatable
  power rather than a one-off accident; ordinary shared life reshaped by the
  power; a consequential crisis that tests both partners; consent, agency, and
  mutual problem-solving; a complete ending.
- Prohibited elements: Graphic sex; fetish framing; infantilization of the
  shrunken adult; non-consensual use of the power; an ending that treats the
  girlfriend as a possession, pet, or problem to be cured against her will.

## Assumptions

- Both romantic partners are consenting adults who already live together.
- The power changes the girlfriend's physical size without changing her age,
  intellect, identity, or legal personhood.
- The prompt leaves the cause and mechanics open; the story may use the
  shared universe's established miracle rules so long as current authority is
  respected.
- The power is useful enough to be part of daily life but costly or risky
  enough to generate meaningful choices.
- The title is a working title and may remain the final title if no stronger
  title emerges during editing.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The relationship is shown through concrete domestic routines as well as the
  central crisis.
- The girlfriend remains an active decision-maker whose power creates both
  opportunities and hazards.
- The climax depends on trust and a choice by both partners, not only on a
  mechanical rescue.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
