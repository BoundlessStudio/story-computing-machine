## Conflicts and ambiguities

- **Contradiction:** None in the corrected captured prompt. The story may proceed without a user canon ruling.

- **Conditional contradiction:** Declaring that all transformation, all bodily magic, or all “miracles” obey this shrinking system would contradict the established plurality and local scope of canonical mechanisms.

- **Conditional contradiction:** A magical nonliving device independently deciding when, why, or how to shrink someone would contradict `Only life can use magic` unless the device is established as alive. A passive focus responding to living agency is compatible.

- **Conditional contradiction:** Treating the shrunken girlfriend as a lesser person, dependent child, possession, pet, duplicate, replaceable body, or extension of her partner would contradict the prompt and the locked personhood constraint.

- **Conditional contradiction:** Connecting the power to Northbridge transformations, Crown-era dragon guises, the Sleepless Majesty, Solstice’s tradition, heartseeds, or another existing system solely because the effects look similar would violate continuity and evidence discipline.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** Canon defines no shrinking power, size-changing culture, established shrinking user, universal mass-conservation rule, or standard social response to miniaturized adults.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** Canon does not decide whether shrinking is voluntary, reflexive, scheduled, emotionally triggered, externally mediated, or subject to occasional failure. The prompt requires consent and agency but leaves the exact trigger open.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** Canon does not decide whether clothing and carried objects change with the girlfriend, where displaced mass or energy goes, how strength scales, or what happens to heat, respiration, hearing, voice, balance, impact tolerance, hunger, fatigue, healing, or medication.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** Canon does not decide the minimum and maximum size, transition speed, duration, recovery interval, reversibility, interruption behavior, or consequences of becoming stuck between states.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** Canon does not establish whether the couple conceals the power, whether friends or institutions know, whether size-changing has legal accommodations, or whether any public community recognizes it.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** “Established miracle rules” in the prompt is permissive rather than a reference to one canonical miracle system. Current authority supplies general system boundaries, not a ready-made shrinking mechanic.

## Safe local invention space

- Create a standalone adult couple in an unnamed modern-like or other local era, with no connection to established characters, factions, locations, or mechanisms.

- Define one intrinsic or locally mediated shrinking power with stable, observable rules. Make the girlfriend the living source and decision-maker if it is magical, or clearly define the separate technical mechanism if it is not.

- Choose a limited, story-manageable size range and specify enough scale behavior for the crisis to remain causally legible. Exact real-world physics may be selectively stylized, but the chosen behavior must remain consistent within the story.

- Establish repeatable household protocols: visual or tactile signals, designated safe surfaces, clothing or equipment, no-go zones, appliance checks, carrying consent, emergency stop procedures, and rules for doors, drains, water, ventilation, pets, vehicles, or sleeping.

- Use domestic comedy arising from logistics, imperfect coordination, adapted routines, and the couple’s familiarity. Do not make the girlfriend’s adult body, vulnerability, helplessness, or diminished reach the object of humiliating or sexualized comedy.

- Give shrinking genuine usefulness in ordinary life while retaining meaningful tradeoffs such as fatigue, heat loss, limited duration, communication difficulty, recovery time, reduced leverage, environmental exposure, transition uncertainty, or dependence on an interruptible protocol. These are available inventions, not established canon.

- Build the crisis from rules and routines introduced earlier. Both partners may make mistakes, but the resolution should require the girlfriend’s expertise and chosen use of her power as well as the viewpoint partner’s trust, restraint, or coordinated action.

- Allow the emotional conflict to concern overprotection, risk tolerance, concealed fear, unequal responsibility, protocol drift, or the difference between helping and controlling. Love should motivate attention without becoming permission.

- Let the girlfriend voluntarily revise how she uses the power or what risks she accepts. Do not make restoration to permanent ordinary size the prize for good behavior or romantic commitment.

- Keep any reusable power rule, accommodation protocol, organization, location, or recurring character fact narrowly local in the later proposed canon delta. This research establishes none of those facts.

## Concrete canon recommendation

Proceed as a standalone domestic transformation story in an unnamed modern-like locality. Make shrinking an intrinsic, self-directed ability of the girlfriend or a bounded system she knowingly operates. Give it a small number of concrete rules covering activation, reversal, size range, bodily cost, clothing or carried matter, communication, and the environmental hazard used in the climax.

Show that the couple already has practical routines and boundaries, then let the crisis expose one assumption those routines failed to address. The non-shrinking partner’s arc should move from protective control or private fear toward informed trust, while the girlfriend’s decisive action demonstrates expertise rather than reckless self-sacrifice. End with a revised mutual protocol and an intact adult relationship, not a cure, ownership arrangement, or promise that love removes risk.
