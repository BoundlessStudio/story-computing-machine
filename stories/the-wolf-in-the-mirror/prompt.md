## Prompt

> [WP] You were fully prepared to watch over your cursed partner while they transform into werewolf on the full moon. What you were not prepared for was them chasing their tail for 2 hours and then barking at themselves after walking in front of mirror.

- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
