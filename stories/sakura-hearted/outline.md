# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: For their eighth wedding anniversary, Celestine restores the absurd two-seat moon-ride from their first date and builds a private planetary tour around it in their apartment. Discover the loving attention of this quiet genius wife through wholehearted adult marital delight, ingenuity, beauty, and shared play.
- Focal pressure or attachment: He wants every detail before the surprise ends; she wants fresh happiness together. Their different memories of being nervous, hungry, and attracted produce discoveries within secure intimacy.
- Counterforce or complication: The preserved coin timer allows ninety seconds per ride. Recollection, laughter, and touching repeatedly distract them from the display. Celestine knows they can run it again; the evening becomes less orderly and more enjoyable than her intended reveal.
- POV, distance, and information limit: First-person past through the unnamed thirty-eight-year-old husband, close to bodily delight and imperfect memory. Her preparations become known through visible effects and questions. No access to her thoughts.
- Governing movement and time shape: One evening: arrival, three flexible ninety-second circuits, and substantial pauses. Each reveals another part of the space and another recollection. Compress restoration history; end inside an ongoing chosen ride.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Celestine miniaturized a workshop tractor emitter into a sakura pink raygun fitting one gloved hand. It moves inert objects fitted with receiver discs within the room and a modest load limit; discs retain a timed hover. Charge and clearance matter. It cannot lift people. The heavy ride uses its floor-mounted motor. Suspended planets obstruct cupboards, supplying ordinary inconvenience.
- Dialogue promise: Affectionate married communication: interrupted memories, admiration, minor disagreements about the date, and quiet physical familiarity. Neither spouse needs correction into a better partner.
- Dialogic medium: Speech, tinny recorded ride announcements, gloved touch, demonstrated objects, and remembered gestures requiring explanation.
- Dialogue engine: His questions change which surprise she reveals; differing recollections redirect the next circuit's attention. Decorative details disclose private associations. Requests for another ride or closer contact lead to action.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Warm, physically present recollection; chrome against an elbow, a cramped knee, light through pink plastic. Let his admiration vary between appetite, amusement, and astonishment rather than recurring superlatives. Shared history enters through particular objects.
- Conversational texture: His curious runs of questions meet her low-volume, comfortable answers. She sometimes becomes expansive about a remembered pleasure. Include ordinary logistical remarks and laughter that interrupts actual sentences.
- Rhetorical ownership: He embellishes his younger self's bravery and readily concedes evidence. She owns affectionate factual corrections, quietly ridiculous design choices, and precise demonstrations. Neither owns every joke. Familiar touch carries affection without making her an enigmatic woman he must decode.
- Pressure behavior: The timer makes him rush and point badly; she pauses the explanation, enjoys his distraction, and restarts when he asks. Mild embarrassment produces laughter and a changed subject, not polished confessions.
- Relationship movement: He wants the story behind her effort but hesitates to interrupt the surprise. She wants his uninhibited delight and finds outright solicitation of praise embarrassing. Specific questions and spontaneous affection let both requests become easy; knowledge deepens an already secure attachment.
- Anti-default: Do not turn her invention into a city-saving crisis, her quietness into damage, or his love into a lesson about letting competent women rest.

## Beats

1. He arrives carrying anniversary flowers and encounters a small ringed planet obstructing the coat cupboard. Celestine moves it with her pink raygun, kisses him, and makes room for his coat. Establish her adult age, thirty-six, and their lived ease. Her brown upswept hair and white headband complement a pink-and-white retro-futuristic dress, white elbow-length opera gloves, circular-buckled white cinch belt, and knee-high white go-go boots.
2. Reveal the battered moon-ride, recognizable by an upholstery seam; adult-sized seats remain inconveniently close. Show her genius through the compact emitter and moving models. She explains how she located the discarded machine without named intermediaries.
3. First circuit: physical absurdity, tinny narration, recognition. Memories compete over an ordinary first-date mishap. The timer stops before they attend to the moon. He requests another circuit; establish enough coins for repetition.
4. During the pause, handmade details disclose holidays and household jokes. She explains particulars he asks about. His flowers join the set through one receiver disc, clear of machinery. She enjoys his specific recognition.
5. Second circuit: a passing model eclipses her face at precisely the wrong moment for his attempt at a kiss. Their amused adjustment disrupts her planned sightline again. A recollection reveals something small she wanted on that first date and he never noticed; she can comfortably ask for its present equivalent now.
6. Final movement: they deliberately spend a third fare, already knowing the scenery. Celestine settles close enough to get what she asked for; he lets an unexamined detail pass. End in the ride's sensory, affectionate present, with the artificial moon moving past the apartment window. No maxim, repair assignment, breakfast, or dance.
