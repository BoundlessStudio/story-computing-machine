# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Cyril Nabe, the most powerful living man in this era's world, keeps modest accounts for local businesses and the superhero Daybreak. After recognizing him during an anonymous intervention, she seeks his help freeing abducted people. His defense of concealment meets a need he could effortlessly answer; preserving his secret afterward becomes the harder act of restraint.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Cyril likes being useful at a scale where a dissatisfied person can replace him. He also hungers for the gratitude Daybreak receives. An earlier anonymous intervention became a prolonged exercise in deciding what other people should be allowed to do; he withdrew when he caught himself enjoying obedience. His obscurity denies both applause and easy companionship with anyone who knows him fully.
- Counterforce or complication: Daybreak has located eight abductees in a remote experimental compound. She could defeat its guards but cannot simultaneously protect dispersed captives from retaliation. They move tomorrow. Her courage and assessment are sound. She wants a colleague; Cyril hears the beginning of unlimited jurisdiction. Her knowledge of his identity creates a real exposure risk that cannot be abolished without controlling her.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Cyril's temptation and irritation. Establish his unmatched present capacity literally. Her judgments remain conduct and speech; neither knows every consequence.
- Governing movement and time shape: One evening: an after-hours appointment, an abrupt journey into the compound, then return to the same office with its familiar exit newly consequential. Target 2,500–4,000 words. Compress the spectacular rescue; expand the decision about the witness afterward.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Cyril can relocate matter, fold distance, and arrest physical forces at planetary scale. He must specify actual targets; power supplies neither omniscience nor judgment. He cannot read or edit minds, reverse time, or restore departed consciousness. Daybreak flies and spreads impacts across luminous planes, but sustained loads exhaust her and she cannot protect separate rooms simultaneously. Both are living magical agents. Cyril still has to understand receipts; Daybreak's expensive harness distributes the ordinary strain of carrying people.
- Dialogue promise: A familiar client's increasingly concrete demand punctures an accountant's impressive defense of privacy without making the working hero foolish or the hidden man merely cowardly.
- Dialogic medium: Office conversation, short spoken directions during extraction, captives' ordinary questions, and an obstructed departure.
- Dialogue engine: Daybreak keeps specifying people and actions where Cyril generalizes obligations. His explanation changes what she fears from him; her refusal to guarantee secrecy puts his claimed restraint into immediate practice.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Patient prose gives vast interventions little extra ceremony. Cyril notices wear, prices, and expressions he cannot reliably interpret. His self-flattering classifications meet inconvenient bodies.
- Conversational texture: Begin with the familiarity of recurrent appointments. Daybreak can be impatient, grateful, distracted, and professionally commanding; Cyril can be evasive, expansive, petty, or simply helpful. Preserve logistical speech without polishing it into argument.
- Rhetorical ownership: Cyril owns broad comparisons, qualifications, and the temptation to win by defining the question. Daybreak owns remembered particulars and authority earned under danger. She may mock his evasions. Captives want recognizable things, not thematic commentary.
- Pressure behavior: Cyril's fluency grows with command, then fails when the office door exposes his conduct. Daybreak becomes economical under physical risk and angrier afterward, when she can understand it.
- Relationship movement: She wants dependable help and cannot comfortably ask him to become another person's last resort. He wants her admiration without her claims upon him. Their exchanges produce gratitude, alarm, and consequential knowledge; neither acquires ownership of the other.
- Anti-default: Do not turn rescue competence into proof that everyone should accept Cyril's retirement, or finish with recruitment, improved listening, and reassuring friendship.

## Beats

1. Daybreak arrives after hours. Establish ordinary familiarity through an expense correction and her discomfort sitting in damaged equipment. She recognized him when he carried her and falling wreckage through folded space during a recent rescue; smoke concealed him from other witnesses. She has kept that recognition private.
2. She describes the abductees and tonight's opportunity. Cyril initially tries to finish their accounting business. Her challenge that he could do considerable good with his powers draws his sincere counterclaim that keeping those powers hidden from prying eyes is itself heroic. Give this argument room, but make her resist its use as an answer to eight actual people.
3. A brief recollection of extending an intervention beyond rescue exposes his pleasure in obedience. She distinguishes tonight's request from governing the world. He accompanies her because the immediate request survives his objections.
4. At the compound, Daybreak moves first and protects the first room. Cyril makes the entire occupied complex weightless, separates its walls without displacing inhabitants, and holds every fired round motionless. The scale and ease establish genuine supremacy. Daybreak identifies captivity, reaches frightened people, and directs their voluntary passage to safety; he cannot substitute mass handling for that knowledge. Preserve an individual's hesitation or inconvenient belongings.
5. He nearly expands extraction into permanent confinement of the operators. Daybreak interrupts while securing captives and evidence for ordinary responders. His irritation reveals assistance becoming possession. Finish with all eight safe; her competence remains essential to humane execution.
6. Back in the office, Daybreak thanks him and rejects the idea that tonight proves all further refusal heroic. She will not promise never to tell anyone what she knows. As she leaves, Cyril folds the short passage back upon itself: a nonviolent, effortless confinement. She encounters the same door again and understands. Make this brief physical action the decisive breach, not an illustrative hypothetical.
7. He restores the exit before she submits or bargains. He can protect perfect secrecy only by becoming what he fears; instead he lets an independent witness leave with the truth. Her decision about future disclosure remains hers. End on the immediate consequence of her free departure and his exposure, allowing anger, relief, or shame without a prescribed final speech or emblem.
