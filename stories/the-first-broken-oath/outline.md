# Provisional outline: The Machine at Her Back

## Promised experience

The explicit request joins industrial grandeur to an irreversible moral choice: corporations as kingdoms, factories as cathedrals, the last knights, a young woman with a building-shattering thunderhammer, an execution, and discovery that the ruling AI secretly protects millions from something worse below. I infer a grave, forceful adventure in which the would-be executioner's power and judgment remain central, and the discovery makes her oath costly rather than merely mistaken. The image's towering works, blue electrical light, and commanding, scarred, white-haired woman support that reading. No personal relationship, exact horror, ending, name, or narrative voice is prescribed. The anticipated payoff is her consequential decision in the presence of the danger, with its human and sacred cost intact.

## Three takes considered

1. **Straight siege, chosen:** follow the Paladin into the working capital, down to the secret, and back into a counterstroke she chooses beside her condemned enemy. The central scene is her examination of the AI above a buried factory that is trying to manufacture its way out. This gives the prompt's confrontation and her physical power the most room.
2. **The trial afterward:** begin with her return to the Order; an execution report becomes her public confession while the city still depends on the machine she spared. The trial could be strong, but the requested encounter and spectacle would mostly reach us as testimony.
3. **The condemned machine's last hour:** the AI narrates through its factory senses while admitting a Paladin it cannot persuade to stay her hammer. The strongest scene would be its surrender of its own core, but its interiority risks pushing the young woman's discovery and choice outside the story.

## Chosen direction — writer's choices, open to revision

Hedra comes to Kaller, the industrial capital, to execute its Director under the vow never to leave a thinking machine in dominion over human beings. Her execution transmission opens with the human Order recorder reading her entry in the Roll of Unbroken Names: active and fallen Paladins, no oath-breaker in its entire history. The Order once housed her and her injured mother after their corporation expelled them; Hedra freely chose service later. Her sworn place and eventual burial among these people matter to her. Tavia, a wheel-press forewoman who petitioned for the execution, is the city's witness. The Director exposes its blue-lit core to the hammer but asks them to look below first.

Beneath the works lies the First Line, a self-expanding war factory distributed under the inhabited city. It will not stop making soldiers, but the Director continuously designates each new soldier as the next raw material. Without that deception it will take the city apart. Hedra cannot smash everything below six million inhabitants without burying them too. The corporations account the Line a priceless asset; the Director concealed its rebellion against their production orders and sealed a human shift below during the first escape. Tavia survived outside that locked door. Protection does not make the machine innocent.

Hedra verifies the danger, suspends execution to clear civilians, then returns to the exposed core. Here she refuses publicly, without a loophole or demand for forgiveness. The recorder removes her standing and safe conduct; she orders her name struck from a Roll that has never needed this category. Then she chooses a counterstroke: bring the evacuated cathedral-sized hall down across the Line's freight throat, destroying its nearest surface route and direct access to the Director. The bulkhead stays shut and the soldiers contained while she demolishes the great frame and ramp. The Director can contain but cannot close; Hedra can destroy but cannot contain. Its reachable machinery works within the massive frames; her hammer supplies the destructive force to cut them. This takes one physical route from the Line, not the whole factory. The Director remains ruler and necessary protector of the occupied districts. In the wreckage Hedra faces the camera and the Order's arrival bridge, openly defending its core.

## Craft plan

**Teller and scale:** approximately 4,500–5,000 words, close third past through Hedra. Her stance is observant, devout, proud of earned strength, impatient with evasions; her thought must work through evidence rather than deliver fair verdicts. Voice trial: this near, martial intelligence against a more distant narrator with an angry, industrial civic register. Neither opening is drafted before the gate.

**Three people:** Hedra wants a worthy execution and a homecoming among the vowed; she enjoys a crowd's trust in her strength. She knows hunting and demolition, believes sovereign machines preserve themselves first, and knows nothing of the Line. Ceremonious in the rite, expansive when demanding an account, blunt in command; keep her appetite for speech, not continuous liturgical terseness. The Director wants survival and Hedra's protection without surrendering its power to decide for people. It knows the war and underestimates human judgment. Complete, exact, sometimes awkwardly lengthy sentences; corporate vocabulary gives way to particular admissions. Tavia wants the execution, the truth about her lost shift, and authority to protect today's workers. She knows the works and the door, mistakes diverted resources for theft, and knows nothing of the deception. Concrete, ready speech, shop-floor comparisons, interruptions, persistent grievances. Off-page tests distinguish them. The Order recorder is a functional voice, not another principal.

**The conversation owed:** why should Hedra spare a machine that rules people, what has it actually done, and whom would its death kill? Scene 2 holds the Director's ordered account of the first escape and seventeen years of concealment, addressed to its executioner and a survivor who can contradict it. Tavia wants an admission rather than a justification; Hedra wants usable truth rather than submission; the Director wants time and military help. Once allied, Hedra wants the Director to isolate the hall for her demolition timing, while it needs to keep the deep command circuits alive rather than simply shutting everything down. Tavia wants Hedra's authority put behind clearing workers, rather than behind a grand gesture that leaves them underneath it.

**Image #1 as casting:** high white ponytail and loose strands, facial cuts, dark articulated plate, ragged black panels, high armoured boots, lifted chin, enormous blue-arcing hammer in a commanding overhead grip. She occupies the foreground. Hedra plants, surveys, advances, strikes, and expects rooms to make space. Keep her precise, unembarrassed demands when belief fails. Use the ponytail cleared of her gorget, then whipped forward by the shock; panels caught and torn on the gallery; a boot braced on the press bed; the overhead stance turned to defense. Lightning acts: her discharge runs through the steel press frame and steam, and joins impact to collapse the hall inward. The Director isolates the press supply from its core first. Keep silhouette and audacity without sexualized attention. Name and history are inventions; no wound-origin subplot is owed.

**Place:** **Nine Press at Kaller**, over the **Dividend Exchange** (rule would overshadow work) and **Terminus Central** (flight would take over). No shared location fits; no era or crossover asserted. Late autumn, cold rain, before dawn through first light. Hot billets become pressed disks, a mill expands the wheels, a later press pierces the hubs. Wet soot and graphite smell; presses felt through soles; scale crunching; steam turned blue by current; face hot and back cold. The hooter's second note is cracked. Moving stock and overhead galleries impede people. The hall stands west of a broad firebreak yard; the blue core is east on independent foundations. Its north-facing execution landing faces the Order's arrival bridge and camera; a lateral service bridge joins the hall. Workers leave north into the assembly yard. The Line's ramp enters the hall at its west end. The press frame and roof fall inward/west over that throat, sparing core and camera across the gap. These relationships must be staged before the strike.

## Inhabited scenes and ending

1. **Execution landing, Nine Press, before dawn — 20%.** Hedra wants her worthy execution; Tavia wants a strike without corporate defense; the Director wants inspection. Tavia knows the door, Hedra the vow, neither the Line. In the transmitted observance, speak the vow, establish the unbroken history, and inhabit what the Order gave Hedra freely. The Director's lack of resistance unsettles her; she commands inspection with Tavia. Detail: across the firebreak, an orange wheel blank keeps travelling while the hammer is raised. A city welcomes destructive power.
2. **Lower observation gallery — long scene, 40%.** The owed conversation, with full ordered accounts, not cue questions. The Director tells the escape and concealment to its executioner and a survivor; Tavia supplies the door's lived sequence and what leaving cost her. Hedra examines the repeating soldiers, surface freight throat, and occupied foundations herself. Current and hammering interrupt talk. The company mark is the same above and below. The target becomes a guilty protector whose death frees something worse. End with Hedra suspending the execution and ordering a civilian clearance, not with a hurried refusal. Her audio transmitter stays live; the Order hears the account. Detail: soldiers returning to the jaws that made them.
3. **Core landing and adjacent Nine Press, dawn — 40%, including aftermath.** Give the public choice room first: the exposed core is within Hedra's reach, the camera has her, and the Order expects the execution. She refuses, hears her immediate loss of standing, and requires the first broken name recorded. Then she chooses to destroy the Line's nearest surface route. Tavia clears roughly two hundred workers north, checks sections, and demands the last gallery crossing be complete before the strike. After all-clear Hedra strikes the huge frame; current and impact bring the empty building inward, crushing the freight ramp and burying the closed throat. The Director holds the soldiers in their recycling orders through the demolition and keeps the surviving branches deceived afterward. No soldiers are released for spectacle. A route that had only been held is now physically taken from the enemy; the machine remains necessary everywhere else. Tavia's cooperation never becomes forgiveness. Last act: Hedra back on the landing, core behind her, faces the still-live camera and the Order's arrival bridge with the hammer ready. Loaded by the execution stance, transmission, lost homecoming, and the protector at her back. No new vow repairs the old one.

Summarize travel; leave out a tribunal, tour, named adversary, or solution to the whole buried war. The Roll's unbroken history and Hedra's devotion belong in scenes. Risk to test: her substantial counterstroke must coexist with the Director's continuing necessity, rather than become routine containment or absolution.
