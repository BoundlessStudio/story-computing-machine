# Content rating

- **Rating:** Teen
- **Content notes:** Non-graphic account of forty-three workers killed behind a sealed industrial door; coercive labour and confinement; machines targeting humans and being crushed in breakers; large-scale building destruction; minor non-graphic injury, bereavement and loss of belonging. The cover depicts an armed adult Paladin, electrical discharge and industrial ruins.
- **Reason:** The finished prose and inspected cover contain sustained industrial menace, threatened mass death and non-graphic violence that may be unsuitable for readers under 13.
