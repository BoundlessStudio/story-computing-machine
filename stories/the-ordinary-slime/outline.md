# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: At Nacre Spill, you pick up the plum-sized slime that, moments earlier, sent a pulse through runoff and made two dozen scattered slimes move in timed waves, rolling a jammed pilot stone free before a sluice release drowned their nesting shelf and swept you away with it. In your hand it is ordinary in every visible respect: cool gel, mild digestive film, no core, no hidden object, little strength, and the same blunt reflexes as any ditch slime. Resolve the contradiction in two linked disclosures. The slime is Feskarn Veylor, your dead former waterworks mentor, whose same continuing soul has been reincarnated from birth in this living body. His emerging ability is not a concealed organ or stolen trait but the power to place deliberate pressure cadences into contiguous water. During the story he develops that single-note talent into a responsive braid that lets the local slimes coordinate without erasing their separate impulses, enabling the cluster to reach a permanent seep before the next release.
- Intended reader experience: Comic tactile disbelief gives way to the tender shock of recognizing Feskarn's private work rhythm, then to the pleasure of a compact progression-fantasy first quest. Familiar hydraulic intelligence must discover an unfamiliar body, learn one ability's hard limits, and protect a small community with puddles, glazed sample dishes, and many unimpressive slimes. End with one earned advance and a clear invitation toward larger waterways, richer communication, and further slime communities.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led first-quest progression. Each crossing attempt tests a different assumption: cadence is not language, range requires continuous liquid, one repeated signal creates congestion, and other slimes answer with simpler rhythms. Feskarn advances by learning to receive and sequence those replies, not by consuming an enemy or acquiring a second power.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: You mourned Feskarn as the person who made dangerous water legible. Finding him offers impossible relief, but your wish to get him back can make you address the remembered mentor while ignoring his present body's limits, choices, and attachment to the slimes that sustained him after hatching. Feskarn wants recognition without abandoning the life and community he has now.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: You want to identify the extraordinary slime, understand what you saw, and leave before the next release; once Feskarn identifies himself, you want to carry him home. Feskarn wants to move the whole cluster to the upper seep first. Your useful flaw is specimen thinking intensified by grief: you search his body for the exceptional part, treat carrying him as rescue, and hear every pulse as meant for you. Feskarn's useful limit is a former foreman's habit of sending one clear cadence harder when others do not follow, which dehydrates him and bunches the slimes instead of organizing them.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: No antagonist. A damaged timing wheel will release another wash within ninety minutes. The upper seep lies beyond a dry maintenance lip that breaks Feskarn's water-borne pulses; you have one canteen, six shallow glazed sampling dishes, and a long walk out. The slimes respond to attractive and warning vibrations but do not understand human plans, and some are feeding, resting, or guarding translucent egg clutches.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Feskarn protects the cluster because they surrounded his hatch-pool, shared moisture, and drew him toward food before he could interpret his new senses. He cannot compel them, read minds, or make them sapient; any slime may ignore or leave a cadence. You are right that immediate evacuation matters, while Feskarn is right that saving only the being you recognize would abandon the community through which his present life became possible.
  - Pressure or change created: Every experiment spends moisture and time. A straight dish-chain can carry one signal across the lip but uses most of the canteen and produces a single crowded route. Carrying Feskarn alone would solve your emotional emergency and the least important physical problem. Moving the cluster requires a two-way relay and accepting a drier return without captive proof of reincarnation.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close second-person present through the unnamed picker, preserving the prompt's direct physical stance. Stay intimate with gel moving over knuckles, taps felt in wrist bones, thirst, wet stone, and your habit of hearing Feskarn's old corrections in competent acts. You cannot enter Feskarn's mind; identity and intent emerge through timed pulses, a few letters shaped in silt, private details, and present choices that exceed a memory quiz.
  - Time shape and compression: One continuous ninety-minute interval between sluice releases. Open after the first extraordinary coordination, identify Feskarn within the first third, and spend the larger middle and ending on ability discovery and the crossing rather than making reincarnation a final twist. Use brief memories of Feskarn teaching the five-beat gate cadence and dying in a winter culvert; do not stage his whole former life, afterlife passage, or hatching separately.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Establish that examination is honestly ordinary. Feskarn repeats the private five-beat cadence through water on your palm, answers bounded yes/no taps, writes `FESKARN` by pulsing a droplet through silt, and identifies the hidden flaw in your first repaired gate. He remembers dying, an interval he can describe only as being carried without a body, and waking at this body's first awareness. Demonstrate limits: cadence needs unbroken liquid, loses precision with distance, costs body water, and influences other slimes only when it matches signals they already use. Reveal reply pulses after his failed one-way attempt; the decisive advance is listening and braiding, not a stronger secret mode.
  - Governing movement: Lift; examine; find nothing exceptional; feel the five-beat pulse; recognize Feskarn without reducing him to a quiz; hear his request; offer to carry him alone; be refused; inspect the dry lip; lay a direct dish-chain; watch Feskarn overdrive one signal and cause a jam; notice replies you assumed were echoes; give him connected water and stillness; rebuild two staggered lanes; alternate gather, hold, and release around local replies; move offered egg clutches; survive the wash; reach the seep; choose the next task.
- Speculative surplus: Ordinary ditch-slime gel removes berry dye while leaving fired glaze untouched, so local waterworkers use unpainted leather and glazed dishes around them; during examination, Feskarn bleaches half the blue tally mark on your glove. This nuisance gives slimes occupational presence but does not prove identity, transmit cadence, increase power, or solve the crossing.
- Structural distinction: A progression-fantasy origin compressed into one observed first quest: intimate examination becomes recognition, then a small hydraulic relocation whose failures define the ability. Advancement appears as richer communication and coordinated movement rather than levels, combat, devouring, mimicry, public proof, or promotion. The decisive space is a dry lip between puddled shelves, and the community is present from the beginning rather than converted by a climactic speech.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: The straight dish-chain fails. Feskarn repeats his foreman's cadence until his body thins; leading slimes crowd the first dish while egg guards stay put. You begin to scoop him up, promising to return, and he strikes a broken rhythm against the glaze. You stop translating it as damaged human speech and notice matching replies from three puddles: feeding movement, aversion, eggs. You reshape the dishes into staggered lanes with gaps where slimes choose when to bridge. Feskarn listens between pulses, then braids his gather beat with each local reply, holding one group while releasing another. The slower responsive sequence carries bodies and offered clutches across without a pileup. His new capability is earned adaptation to the colony, not a sudden grant.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: The wash scours the abandoned shelf but misses the cluster around the upper seep. Feskarn is dehydrated and can make only one weak tap; he must recover with food and clean water. The braid is repeatable but not mastered, limitless, or useful over dry ground. You accept Feskarn as a continuing person whose changed body and consent matter, while neither of you knows why the reincarnation occurred, whether it can recur, or how stable his human memories will be. The next goal is practical: bring shallow glazed channels, enlarge the pulse vocabulary, and map a route toward another slime patch Feskarn sensed downstream.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with you turning Feskarn over in one hand, looking for the extraordinary thing inside an ordinary slime. End at the seep with him too depleted for spectacle: he nudges the bleached glove mark, taps the old five beats once, adds a new colony reply, and indicates six dish-shaped intervals. You leave to fetch channels before dark, not with a specimen or a restored mentor, but with a collaborator, his community, and a next quest.
