# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: An unnamed picker lifts and assays a plum-sized green slime after seeing it animate a severed gauntlet and punch a cutting engine through painted scenery. Every test says the creature is weak, mildly acidic, and utterly ordinary. Inside it, however, the dead stage rigger Feskarn Veylor has awakened with his memories intact and a body whose blunt survival impulses interrupt his thoughts: down, dark, hide, run, bite. When Papermantle Yard's verifier identifies him as an unauthorized moving decoy, the picker drops him and flees. Feskarn must cross the abandoned yard alone while one continuous mechanical system pursues him with larger responses. He survives by diving into successively larger hollow props, learning each body while already being chased.
- Intended reader experience: A fast, spatially clear chase comedy told from inside the slime. The pace comes from pursuit rather than a catalogue of rewards. Each shell is an increasingly absurd hiding place chosen under pressure; it solves the immediate danger, is difficult to enter and operate, preserves prior damage, increases control lag, and causes the verifier to deploy a larger threat. The internal comic duel is between Feskarn's theatrical confidence and his new body's terse appetite for darkness, moisture, and immediate escape. There is no speaking companion, crowd scene, or outside character arc.
- Generating force: Feskarn needs to reach the drainage arch beyond the far service gate before the verifier catches, cuts, burns, or compacts him. His slime body always wants the nearest safe opening; his remembered rigger mind wants to understand and control the yard. Papermantle's old rehearsal-assembly lane runs toward the same gate and is marked by white registration crosses. Its shells increase in size along the route because they were built and tested by nesting smaller rehearsal bodies inside larger ones. Feskarn does not know that plan at first; he recognizes it one mark and one emergency entry at a time.
- Operative rules and accumulating cost: Feskarn can animate only an empty, nonliving, hollow shell entered through a real opening. A claimed shell may claim a larger shell only when the complete current body fits inside. Each layer multiplies delivered force but retains its breakage, awkward joints, fire risk, and weight distribution. Sensation and intention must travel from slime to thimble to every outer layer, so response delay worsens with scale. Leaving a layer makes it inert. The verifier classifies the moving outer silhouette and answers at the same scale. The body's instinctive fragments are part of Feskarn's one living consciousness, not a displaced second person, tutorial voice, or separate source of magic.
- POV and voice: After the prompt's literal opening action, use close third-person present entirely through Feskarn in the slime. The picker is only glove, lens, smell, and danger before disappearing in the first movement. Feskarn thinks in stagecraft, rigging loads, entrances, marks, and offended professional judgments. Slime impulses appear as brief sensory imperatives rather than polished speech. Their disagreement makes physical comedy and bad decisions; their eventual cooperation supplies the climax. Explain the speculative rule only through failed or successful use.
- Governing movement: One continuous run from the assay bench to the far gate. Keep the destination or next landmark legible, and make every transition a consequence of the previous pursuit. The thimble rolls into the intact gauntlet's cuff when a cutter overturns the assay tray. The gauntlet must open the armor's jammed breast grille before the cutter severs its fingers. The damaged armor follows the registration lane until scissor-hounds drive it into the parade dragon's low chest hatch. Ram cranes strike the fleeing dragon through a storage wall and bodily lodge it inside the battlefield giant's rehearsal bay. The giant uses a useless painted sword as a lever against the mobile forge; the impact peels the apparent hillside open, revealing the false mountain's marked service gate. Fire and the advancing press force the giant wholly through that gate.
- Decisive turn: As the false mountain, Feskarn can see the drainage arch but cannot reach it before the authenticity press closes. Abandoning the nested bodies would leave the slow naked slime in the press's proving lane. He throws a panicked mountain punch; the delayed layers answer out of order, miss, and nearly fold his own arm. Heat enters the seams. His slime instincts feel the press's vibration sooner than his remote mountain senses, while his rigger memories recognize the registration crosses as receipt marks: each layer must accept a movement instead of anticipating it. Feskarn stops arguing with the body's immediate warning, uses its flinch as the first cue, and passes one controlled backhand outward through thimble, gauntlet, armor, dragon, giant, and mountain. The aligned palm reverses the stamping ram into the verifier's hopper, causing the fixed machine to compact itself.
- Aftereffect and ending: Feskarn survives with damaged nested bodies and reaches the drainage arch without being adopted, appraised, or explained by anyone else. The chase has taught him both the reach and the cost of the power: larger shells are protection that can become traps, and his smallest body's quick senses are essential rather than embarrassing. A plate uncovered by the impact identifies the mountain as a one-to-one-hundred rehearsal body and points toward six full-size storage sites. Feskarn's ambition and the slime body's refusal deliver the final comic disagreement.

## Beats

1. A picker examines Feskarn after his accidental gauntlet strike; every assay confirms an ordinary slime. The returning cutter makes the picker drop him and run.
2. Feskarn hides in a thimble. A blow rolls it into an intact gauntlet, teaching him that complete nesting extends control. He escapes along the marked assembly lane.
3. The cutter damages the gauntlet as Feskarn uses its remaining fingers to release an armor grille. He enters, learns a reversed knee while running, and triggers scissor-hounds.
4. The hounds herd and dismantle the armor toward the parade dragon. Feskarn pulls its hatch chain, dives inside, and gains speed, delayed steering, and a useless reservoir of old confetti.
5. Ram cranes pursue the dragon down the same lane. Their strike drives it through a wall into the giant's open rehearsal bay; the second impact seats the whole dragon inside and wakes the giant.
6. A mobile forge blocks the far gate. Feskarn learns the giant while fleeing fire, levers the forge aside with a prop sword, and exposes the false mountain gate beneath peeled scenery.
7. The giant enters the mountain before the forge recovers. The final authenticity press seals the route to the drainage arch. A rushed mountain blow fails because every nested body answers at a different time.
8. Trapped between fire and compaction, Feskarn trusts the slime body's early vibration warning and applies his knowledge of registration marks. One timed movement travels outward and turns the press's ram against itself.
9. In the smoking quiet, Feskarn crawls the mountain toward the arch and discovers the full-scale storage plate. His two modes of thought disagree about whether a larger dark opening is opportunity or an obviously terrible idea.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The universal underlying afterlife permits story-specific reincarnation. Feskarn is the same continuing person after death, not a copy, possession, or replacement of another person. His slime embodiment contributes appetite and reflex without establishing a separate erased consciousness. Only living Feskarn supplies magic; every shell and verifier machine remains nonliving.
- Boundaries: Keep the shell ability local to Feskarn and the yard's history unresolved. Do not establish a universal slime trait, transferable reincarnation method, autonomous magical object, visible progression system, or relationship to another continuation mechanism.
