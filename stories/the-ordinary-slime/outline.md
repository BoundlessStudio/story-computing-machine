# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: In Papermantle Yard, you pick up a plum-sized slime and examine it after watching it climb into a severed iron gauntlet, animate the fingers, and backhand a three-legged cutter machine through two painted castle walls. Naked in your palm, it is trash-tier by every honest measure: ordinary gel, mild acid, no core or hidden relic, almost no lift, and barely enough speed to menace a mushroom. The impossible feat belongs to Feskarn Veylor, a deceased stage-effects rigger reincarnated as the same continuing person in a slime body, and to a new magic that works only through hollow shells. He can pilot one empty nonliving shell as his body; if that shell fits wholly inside a larger empty shell, he can claim the next layer, multiplying force and scale while retaining every layer's structural weaknesses and control lag. Fulfill the prompt by rocketing him in one frantic episode from naked slime to thimble, gauntlet, armor, parade dragon, giant battlefield decoy, and finally a false mountain weighing more than a million cartloads, then use the whole ridiculous stack in one precise, spectacular blow to defeat the yard's escalating verifier machinery.
- Intended reader experience: Fast, bright progression-fantasy comedy whose “surely that is the ceiling” feeling survives for no more than a page at a time. The fun comes from clear rules producing increasingly unreasonable consequences: a thimble scratches a name, armor runs with one knee backward, a dragon coughs centuries-old confetti, and a mountain must be coached through a punch it is too large to aim. Keep danger kinetic and broadly accessible, banter brisk, scale immediately legible, and Feskarn's personhood matter-of-fact rather than ponderous. End on decisive victory, absurd retained power, a new picker/slime partnership, and a final reveal that makes the mountain look like beginner equipment.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led nested escalation. Every claimed shell solves the current-sized attack, triggers the yard's next larger verifier response, and provides access to the next still larger hollow decoy. The sequence is causal rather than a catalogue: Feskarn must enter each shell to survive long enough to reach the next, while you identify openings, registration marks, structural flaws, and safe routes.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: You make a living separating useful salvage from theatrical junk and trust mass, material, and contained power. Feskarn's naked body is worthless by those measures, but discarded emptiness is exactly what lets his power compound. Feskarn has just escaped the helplessness of dying under scenery and wants the biggest available body immediately; he also needs a spotter who will stop him from treating every larger disaster like opening night.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: You immediately want to bottle the anomalous slime, recover valuable yard salvage, and leave before the verifier finishes waking. Competing with profit and self-preservation is the need to stop the machine you and Feskarn activated from reaching the occupied salvage camp outside the yard. Your useful flaw is inventory certainty: if a lens finds no stored force, you assume none can be generated through relation or use. Feskarn wants a voice, mobility, and enough power never to be crushed again; his showman's flaw is “bigger before better.” He claims shells faster than he learns their balance, ignores rehearsal marks, and mistakes spectacle for control until his first mountain-sized swing nearly destroys what he is saving.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: Papermantle Yard's nonliving verifier system was built to destroy battlefield decoys that moved without authorization. The gauntlet punch wakes hand-sized cutters; armor movement wakes scissor-legged hounds; the parade dragon wakes ram cranes; the giant decoy wakes a mobile forge; claiming the false mountain releases the final valley-spanning authenticity press, whose furnace mouth and stamping ram advance toward the yard entrance. It has no will or moral position, only an intact ancient routine.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: No personal antagonist. The verifier's escalating response matches the silhouette and movement of each unauthorized decoy, so Feskarn's growth creates its own counterforce. The evacuated-looking yard is not harmless: an unnamed salvage camp beyond its gate cannot move before the final machine arrives, and you know your earlier entry disturbed the verifier's balance keys.
  - Pressure or change created: A shell must be nonliving, hollow, unoccupied, entered through a real opening, and large enough to contain the full current stack. Feskarn cannot claim living bodies, solid objects, occupied machines, or several separate shells at once. A breached layer drops out of the stack; outer scale multiplies force but also sensory delay and imprecision. Each victory therefore makes him stronger, clumsier, more flammable, and visible to a larger response.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close second-person present through the unnamed salvage examiner. Begin literally with the slime's weight in your palm and keep scale tactile by comparing each new body with your hand, wagon, buildings, yard wall, and surrounding ridges. You cannot enter Feskarn's mind. At thimble scale he scratches terse words; armor gives him a booming stage grille and a comic voice; larger shells make speech slower and louder as control lag grows.
  - Time shape and compression: About twenty-five continuous minutes from gauntlet wreck to final press impact. Give the examination, first written exchange, armor claim, first shell breach, dragon launch, false-mountain awakening, failed giant swing, and decisive nested punch full scene space. Compress only short races between aligned storage sheds. No extended former-life flashback: Feskarn's name, death beneath a collapsing stage palace, bodiless afterlife interval, and awakening as a slime arrive in four clipped answers while cutter machines close in.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Make every ordinary examination result true. Reveal the magic by action: Feskarn jumps from your palm into an abandoned thimble, moves it, and scratches `FESKARN / DEAD / SLIME / YES` into assay wax. State the rule only as each boundary matters. He cannot move your solid knife, cannot take a live snail's shell, can animate an empty gauntlet, and can claim the armor only after the gauntlet is wholly inside its chest. Show multiplication on your burden lens: less than one cartweight naked, twelve in armor, hundreds in the dragon, tens of thousands in the giant, then past its million-cartload limit in the false mountain. Do not add levels, menus, devouring, trait absorption, mimicry, a tutorial voice, or a secret second power.
  - Governing movement: Pick up; assay; disbelieve; watch the thimble write; duck the first cutters; claim a second intact gauntlet; enter empty armor; run badly; punch well; lose one arm to a hound; dive armor-first into a parade dragon; sneeze confetti at ram cranes; crash through its storage wall into the giant decoy; use the giant's painted plank-sword to pole-vault the mobile forge; enter the false mountain's service gate; stand up as terrain; swing too early; expose the camp; accept your stage cues; align every nested registration mark; propagate the opening backhand from thimble to mountain; drive the authenticity press's stamping ram into its own hopper; survive self-compaction; discover the next scale.
- Speculative surplus: Claimed shells inherit the slime's harmless dye-bleaching at exposed seams. Red paint fades toward mint as each shell joins the stack: the dragon's ferocious mouth becomes pastel, the giant loses one painted trouser leg, and a mint rain runs from the false mountain. This escalating visual nuisance ruins camouflage and annoys Feskarn but does not increase force, reveal the next shell, disable the verifier, or resolve identity.
- Structural distinction: A Russian-doll action climb through one abandoned decoy yard, with scale and slapstick doubling together. The same simple gesture recurs at widening magnitudes, but each repetition changes the mechanical problem rather than rehearsing an ethical lesson. There is no training montage, monster-consumption ladder, guild appraisal, throne claim, crowd conversion, institutional rewrite, or rescue that proves a public doctrine. The climax is a mountain-sized piece of stage blocking executed by two people under severe control lag.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: Feskarn rises inside the false mountain and reads beyond your burden lens's million-cartload maximum. He throws a celebratory hook at the incoming authenticity press, but seven layers answer at slightly different times; the fist misses, strips a ridge of fake trees, and sends debris toward the camp. The press clamps his shoulder and lights its furnace. Feskarn finally stops reaching for another size. You spot the small registration marks painted inside each shell and use the examiner's signal mirror to cue them from innermost to outermost. He rotates thimble, gauntlet, armor, dragon, giant, and mountain through the same backhand he used in the opening, letting each layer receive rather than anticipate the motion. The synchronized mountain palm catches the press's stamping ram, reverses it into the open hopper, and triggers the machine's own compaction cycle. The verifier folds itself into one enormous but inert cube before its furnace reaches the camp.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: The camp survives; the verifier system is broken locally; no one has converted, promoted Feskarn, or explained reincarnation. Feskarn retains the false mountain and all surviving inner shells, but fine control still worsens with scale, fire can destroy the outer painted skins, and losing the nested alignment drops his output sharply. He and you agree on a practical partnership: you assess openings, ownership, structural load, and bystanders; he supplies mobility, terrible ideas, and force beyond the lens. Inside the mountain's prop office, a brass inventory plate identifies this million-cartload body as a one-to-one-hundred rehearsal model and diagrams six full-scale hollow decoys stored elsewhere. The completed episode stops the immediate threat while launching an unapologetically larger next quest.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Feskarn naked in your palm while your lens reports an utterly ordinary slime and you try to reconcile that with one impossible gauntlet strike. End seated in the false mountain's city-block-sized palm, pointing the same lens at him until its needle snaps past one million; Feskarn asks through a voice cone whether he still looks normal. Before you can answer, the rehearsal-scale plate drops open between you. He reads it, pauses, and asks where anyone would store a full-size mountain.

## Beats

1. Open in the wreckage of two painted castles. Pick up and examine the slime ejected from the shattered gauntlet that just punched a cutter machine across the yard; every physical and magical reading says ordinary, weak, and cheap.
2. The slime jumps into an abandoned thimble, walks it on its rim, and scratches `FESKARN / DEAD / SLIME / YES` into your assay wax. Under pressure from incoming hand-cutters, establish Feskarn's same-person reincarnation and the first shell rule through terse written answers.
3. Insert thimble and slime into a second intact gauntlet. Feskarn repeats the impossible backhand, then crawls the gauntlet through an empty armor's breast opening and claims the whole suit. He gains a stage-grille voice, runs with one backward knee, and destroys the first response while waking the next tier.
4. Scissor-legged hounds breach the armor's arm. Feskarn and the surviving stack dive into the chest cavity of a twenty-foot parade dragon. The dragon lurches awake, coughs ancient confetti instead of flame, battering-rams the hounds, and crashes through the next storage wall as ram cranes arrive.
5. The dragon fits inside a hundred-foot battlefield giant built around a rehearsal bay. Feskarn claims it, discovers its sword is a painted plank, and uses that useless-looking prop as a pole to vault the mobile forge. The landing exposes the service gate in what both of you thought was a hillside.
6. Enter the hollow false mountain before the forge burns through the giant. Feskarn stands the mountain up on concealed fortress legs; your burden lens crosses a million cartloads. Fake trees shake loose, red scenery bleaches mint, and the final valley-spanning authenticity press wakes and turns toward the gate and salvage camp.
7. Feskarn's first celebratory mountain punch misses because seven layers lag, damages scenery near the camp, and lets the press clamp and ignite his shoulder. Bigger cannot solve aim. You identify nested registration marks while he holds the press off without another wild strike.
8. Use the signal mirror to cue the registration marks from thimble outward. Feskarn propagates the opening backhand through every shell; the mountain palm reverses the press's stamping ram into its hopper, causing the machine to compact itself into an inert cube before the fire spreads.
9. Confirm practical costs and form the partnership amid the ridiculous aftermath. The lens needle is broken, Feskarn remains a walking mint-streaked false mountain with nested weaknesses, and the camp is safe. End when a prop-office plate reveals the mountain is a one-to-one-hundred rehearsal model for six larger hollow decoys.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Reincarnation is possible within the universal underlying afterlife, and Feskarn's same-person continuation has a story-specific mechanism: his conscious soul passed through that afterlife after human death and entered the new slime life at its first awareness. He is not copied memory, possession, a branch, or a displaced prior slime mind. Only living Feskarn supplies magic; claimed shells remain nonliving extensions that cannot choose, cast, or move magically without his continuous occupancy. The verifier is nonliving automated technology following fixed instructions, not an independent magic user or person.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Keep nested-shell claiming local to Feskarn. A claim requires one empty nonliving hollow shell fully containing the current stack; it cannot target life, occupied objects, solid matter, separate swarms of objects, memories, souls, abilities, or traits. Force multiplies with nesting, while structural weaknesses, fire risk, sensory lag, and precision loss accumulate. Breaching a layer removes it; leaving the stack ends its animation. Do not establish a universal power system, visible levels, transferable reincarnation method, slime species trait, or connection to another continuation, construct, transformation, relic, Gate, automaton, or living-building mechanism. Papermantle Yard's era, builders, war, full-scale decoys, and wider geography remain unresolved.
