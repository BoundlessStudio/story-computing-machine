[WP] The royal blade dancers possess a secret ability: they can step outside time for exactly one heartbeat, moving through a world frozen around them. But every stolen heartbeat must eventually be repaid, and the accumulated debt can claim years of their lives in an instant. During a performance before the royal court, a silver-haired elven princess attempts a forbidden dance that requires her to steal three consecutive heartbeats. When she completes the movement, seventeen years have passed.
