# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: On the departure day of Latsara, a migratory
  city made from linked gardens, workshops, and sail-homes, young navigator
  Nevenra carries an automated climate archive's recorder from the city's
  trailing gardens to its lead sail. The archive has demonstrated exact local
  forecasts and will provide them for every future migration if Nevenra sings
  the complete route corpus and authorizes one standardized edition as the
  only navigational version. Latsara's established singers have deadlocked and
  invoked the ordinary custom that leaves tool and route authority to the
  active route-holder, so Nevenra cannot defer the offer to a wiser assembly.
  The walk forces her to hear how apparently technical variations preserve
  incompatible accounts of an ancestral abandonment. Resolve the promise with
  an unambiguous choice: Nevenra refuses authorization, loses access to the
  forecasts, and leads Latsara onto a slower, less certain route whose first
  cost is real but not catastrophic.
- Intended reader experience: Begin with sensuous relief at precision: a cold
  current arrives on the archive's promised beat, and ordinary preparations
  become effortless. Let that seduction narrow into discomfort as a clear,
  beautiful standard converts testimony into error without ever becoming
  technically false. The final refusal should feel neither triumphant nor
  foolishly pure; end with people doing extra work because of Nevenra's choice,
  while the reader still understands why Caldren would have chosen safety.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led one-way traversal from the trailing archive dock through
    Latsara's gardens and workshops to the lead navigation sail. Each physical
    section supplies another part of the recording and changes what
    `standardization` means, without a public debate or emergency spectacle.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: Nevenra loves navigation because a
    voice can hold a whole moving city together, but she is ashamed of the
    uncertainty and disagreement inside the songs. The archive offers the
    clean correctness she thinks an adult navigator should possess; accepting
    it would make the living chain that taught her into source material for an
    edition no singer may dispute.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: Nevenra immediately wants
    a perfect forecast for her first migration as route-holder, both to keep
    thousands of linked homes safe and to avoid freezing again when two songs
    give different instructions. She also wants Caldren's minority verse—and
    the dead and absent people it names—to remain usable testimony rather than
    a catalogued mistake. Her useful limit is a hunger for one defensible
    answer: it makes her disciplined at pitch, timing, and sail coordination,
    but she treats unresolved variation as personal failure and initially
    welcomes the archive's corrections.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: The archive's exchange is indivisible and time-bounded by
    Latsara's ordinary departure. Its climate forecasts are genuinely perfect
    within the migration waters and horizon it states; declining them restores
    real exposure to cold shears, stalled currents, damaged gardens, and human
    error. The singers' prior deadlock makes Nevenra's route-holder authority
    decisive rather than ceremonial. The archive's nonliving program can
    retain all source recordings, but its contract certifies only one route
    edition, trains against that edition, and labels future deviations as
    errors until the archive approves a new standard.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Caldren, Nevenra's senior teacher, wants
    the exchange accepted. An unforecast cold shear scarred his lungs and cost
    Latsara a season of food; he will surrender even his own accusatory version
    rather than make later crews bear preventable danger. He does not deny the
    historical cost, coerce Nevenra, or become converted by her refusal. Once
    she decides, he requires her to choose the first route and share the work
    her uncertainty creates.
  - Pressure or change created: The archive does not threaten, lie, or reveal a
    hidden flaw. It produces a standard that is navigationally coherent and
    faithfully stores the variants, making refusal harder to defend. What it
    cannot preserve is their status as live corrections to one another: the
    majority verse says trailing gardens separated voluntarily during a famine;
    Caldren's line names lead gardeners who cut them loose; a third says a
    sudden current made the severing unavoidable. None can be proved complete.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close third-person past through
    Nevenra, intimate with breath placement, remembered corrections, rigging
    strain, water temperature, and her urge to hear one right line. Caldren is
    known only through speech, pauses, damaged breath, and work. Render the
    archive through neutral prompts, mechanisms, and verified outputs; give it
    no interiority, desire, or person-like agency.
  - Time shape and compression: One linear departure day. Give the archive's
    forecast demonstration, the disputed recording, Caldren's private case for
    acceptance, and Nevenra's authorization choice full scenes. Compress the
    intervening walk and less consequential songs through changing districts,
    sail sounds, and short archive corrections. Add a brief noon-after coda on
    the first forecastless leg so consequence, not argument, closes the story.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Establish the forecast's perfection and
    the all-or-nothing terms early; there is no late exposure of fraud. Let
    Nevenra first experience standardization as helpful removal of duplicated
    directions and obsolete timing. Reveal the tail-severing dispute in stages:
    an archive confidence flag, three sung wordings, the people and blame each
    wording preserves, then Caldren's willingness to let his version lose
    navigational standing. Make clear that a stored recording is not the same
    as a song future navigators must answer and vary. Do not decide which
    historical account is true.
  - Governing movement: Verify; accept the recorder; walk and sing; enjoy
    correction; encounter an irreducible variation; hear the material case for
    safety; distinguish storage from living transmission; refuse; choose a
    route without certainty; bear its first cost.
- Speculative surplus: Latsara's warm-water sailcloth grows a translucent salt
  nap whenever it crosses a sharp temperature boundary. The harmless growth
  jams hinges and pulley teeth, so households keep broad bone combs beside
  doors and children earn breakfast by grooming workshop rigging. This remains
  an occupational nuisance of the migrating city, not evidence for either side
  of the archive choice.
- Structural distinction: A one-directional walking negotiation whose scale is
  felt through the connected city's changing work sounds rather than a widening
  cast. The decisive act occurs at the exposed lead sail after the recorder has
  crossed the whole city, not in an archive chamber, hearing, control room, or
  disaster site. There is no technical debunking, crowd conversion, vote,
  institutional rewrite, preservation ceremony, or public declaration.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: At the lead sail, the archive plays
  its finished route edition. Its voice is clean, its directions are safe, and
  its historical annotation accurately lists Caldren's version as a minority
  source. Caldren says to accept; he would rather be footnoted than see another
  garden freeze. When the archive asks Nevenra to attest that its edition may
  define future navigational correctness, she understands that she is not
  being asked which history is true. She is being asked to remove the right of
  later singers to keep that question active. She refuses the exchange and
  instructs the archive to release rather than retain the provisional
  recordings. No better forecast appears to reward her.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: Latsara takes Nevenra's wider traditional
  lane. It avoids immediate danger but reaches the warm current late, using
  enough stored heat that workshops bank their furnaces and gardeners move
  temperature-sensitive seedlings into occupied homes. The archive departs
  with its forecasts and no song corpus. Caldren remains disappointed, works
  beside Nevenra, and makes her name every uncertain cue instead of treating
  tradition as instinct or virtue. Future voyages may vindicate or condemn the
  choice; this leg does neither.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Nevenra watching the archive's trial
  forecast spare everyone a choice: the temperature turns at the exact sung
  beat, and Latsara adjusts before anyone needs to judge conflicting signs.
  End at noon the next day with Nevenra off the lead sail helping carry chilled
  seedlings through narrow homes because the wider lane cost the city heat.
  When the current finally warms, she returns to watch and resumes the route;
  the story stops in accountable work, without an emblem, applause, or claim
  that uncertainty is harmless.

## Beats

1. At Latsara's trailing dock, the archive predicts the arrival, temperature,
   duration, and exact rigging effects of a cold filament. The forecast proves
   correct to the beat. Nevenra feels the relief of acting before doubt and
   reviews the indivisible exchange: complete recording, one certified
   standard, perfect migration forecasts, and no further appeal from the
   singers who placed the deadlocked choice in her hands.
2. Nevenra accepts the provisional recorder and starts the one-way walk toward
   the lead sail with Caldren. Through the trailing gardens, easy redundancies
   disappear from the archive's draft; its cleaner phrasing delights her and
   exposes her wish never to hesitate in front of a crew again.
3. Workshops and sail-homes show the city's ordinary interdependence while
   residents comb salt nap from cold-stiffened rigging. Brief fragments of
   person-to-person teaching make the songs practical work rather than sacred
   performance. Caldren's broken breath also keeps the danger of missed weather
   physically present.
4. At a linkage once replaced after famine damage, the archive halts on three
   incompatible versions of the tail-severing verse. Nevenra sings each as she
   learned it. Their directions can be reconciled for current use, but their
   accounts of choice, blame, and named responsibility cannot.
5. In a quiet sailmaker's passage, Caldren explains what his minority line
   accuses the lead gardens of doing and why he still wants the standard. He
   rejects Nevenra's hope that every version can remain equally operational:
   perfect forecasting requires the archive's single certified vocabulary, and
   he considers lost historical standing an acceptable price for fewer ruined
   homes.
6. Near the city's front, the archive plays interim phrases and labels the
   minority lines as preserved deviations. Nevenra recognizes that their words
   will survive while their capacity to interrupt teaching and route judgment
   will not. Her own remembered freezing during a novice disagreement makes
   acceptance remain tempting rather than obviously corrupt.
7. At the lead sail, with departure preparations proceeding below rather than
   gathering as an audience, the archive plays the finished edition and asks
   for Nevenra's authorization. Caldren plainly advises yes. Nevenra asks one
   final operational question—whether future singers may vary the certified
   history without being marked wrong—then refuses when the mechanical answer
   is no and requests release of the provisional recordings.
8. Caldren neither congratulates nor abandons her. He makes Nevenra select the
   wider, slower lane from fallible gauges and disputed songs, state her
   confidence aloud, and lead Latsara away while the archive withdraws with the
   exact forecast she chose not to buy.
9. By noon on the first leg, the delay has consumed stored heat. Nevenra helps
   move chilled seedlings while furnaces are banked, then returns to the lead
   sail when warmer water finally reaches the root mats. The story ends before
   the voyage can turn her choice into either vindication or disaster.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The deep-time shared world permits independent civilizations and
  technologies in undated eras. The locked memory-versus-myth foundation
  supports partial, disputed evidence whose recovery does not automatically
  settle truth or justice.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Nevenra, Caldren, Latsara, the migratory culture,
  its sail and garden technology, its songs, and the automated climate archive
  are story-local additions and are not recurring canon. The archive's
  prompt-authorized perfection is limited to stated climate conditions,
  migration waters, and forecast horizons; it is not omniscient, cannot judge
  historical truth, and establishes no universal predictive rule. Keep the
  archive nonliving and non-sentient. Establish no connection to any canonical
  prophecy, lattice, sea, archive, climate, navigation, or infrastructure
  system. The story's exact era, geography, archive provenance, and the truth
  of the tail-severing accounts remain deliberately unresolved.
