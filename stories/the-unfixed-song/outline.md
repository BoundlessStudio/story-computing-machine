# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **The Unfixed Song**. An unnamed sea-city migrates as linked sail homes, gardens, and workshops. At a warm-current fork, young navigator Mazhira can obtain exact forecasts only by authenticating one standardized form of every variable route song. The disputed fork song carries incompatible history and directions.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Mazhira wants to carry the entire linked city through a closing warm corridor without treating her teachers as bad data. Her dead mother taught Zulmec's accusatory variant; Heket's competing version is also embodied expertise. Mazhira's watch authority binds the fleet to its route source, so delay is a choice too.
- Counterforce or complication: The archive is valuable within its design. Its sensor mesh predicts local conditions exactly for eighteen hours, but calibration cannot distinguish duplicated observations when variants assign one measure to different positions and causes. Both elders preserve useful readings and interested history. Linked craft turn slowly; outer gardens chill first.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Mazhira, noticing pitch against rope strain, water warmth under bare feet, sail-color relays, and command lag. She cannot establish the old crossing, see beyond instruments and testimony, or know either elder's motives. The archive reports inputs, limits, and outputs, never moral truth.
- Governing movement and time shape: One afternoon-to-night passage through rehearsal, archive ingestion, and live navigation. Keep the decisive exchange among five workers on the lead deck. End next morning in changed teaching work, without public ratification.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Buoyant hulls, towlines, hinged bridges, gardens, workshops, and sail homes form a nonmagical articulated city. Songs encode landmarks, current temperatures, cloud order, elapsed beats, damage, and attributed causes; person-to-person variation carries observation and revision together. The automated archive is fixed, nonliving infrastructure with sensor buoys, audio intake, and visual shutters. Calibration requires each observation mapped to one place, time, and sequence; parallel variants become duplicate voyages and contradictory positions. Exact service requires all variants recorded, collapsed to one authorized form, and used for future reports. Other versions may remain private memories but cannot remain taught navigation authority without breaking calibration and ending the service. Living people, not the machine, impose the social cost.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move by embodied listening—cadence against swell, rope load, temperature, and delayed color relays. Render the vast city through local work; keep history partial.
- Conversational texture: Mix clipped sail calls, interrupted singing, repetition requests, hauling corrections, and ordinary concerns. Not every utterance carries heritage.
- Rhetorical ownership: Mazhira tests conditions; Heket reasons through sequence and stewardship; Zulmec through remembered harm and omissions; Brannic through loads and hands; Kuvesh through protocol boundaries. Archive responses stay fixed status language.
- Pressure behavior: Wind shortens Brannic and Mazhira. Heket grows exact but stops listening; Zulmec repeats the contested measure; Kuvesh becomes literal. Mishearing forces practical repetition.
- Anti-default: Avoid lyrical consensus, heritage-versus-machine speeches, or a debating archive. Let routing work, safe passage, and narrowed teaching carry the choice.

## Beats

1. While rear gardens join towlines, Mazhira rehearses with Heket and Zulmec. Heket's held note routes above a cold backwash and says rear families cast loose voluntarily; Zulmec's repeated beat marks a submerged shelf and names abandonment. Brannic's rigging calls keep dispute inside work.
2. Kuvesh connects the archive's audio horn and shutters. A bounded forecast exactly times a gust, rain edge, and temperature stripe, proving value. Kuvesh shows one measure landing on two positions and supposed voyages; the fixed system withholds the route forecast.
3. Mazhira tests both forms. Heket's upper approach protects towlines but exposes gardens to cold. Zulmec's lower approach uses the shelf to turn but risks hulls if depth changed. Corrections reveal knowledge and interested history. Brannic reports falling temperatures and needs a course.
4. Parallel tracks and annotations fail the ingestion rule. Kuvesh states without advocacy that future access requires reports in the selected form; teaching both as route authority ends exact service. Mazhira cannot preserve the navigational tradition while taking the forecast.
5. Current readings support Zulmec's extra shelf beat. Mazhira authenticates his full variant rather than pretending to separate cadence from its unresolved accusation. Heket refuses the clean ingestion; Mazhira performs it, and the archive returns a narrow warm corridor requiring immediate sail reduction.
6. Wind shreds phrases and one color relay is misread. Mazhira repeats a plain turn command; Brannic reallocates crews; Heket supplies a needed correction despite opposing the standard. Every section clears the shelf inside the predicted corridor. Safety and surrendered authority are real.
7. Next morning, forecasts precisely organize repairs and garden warming. When a deck worker needs the return route taught, Heket declines the official lesson and Zulmec offers only the authenticated cadence. Mazhira teaches the fixed form, including the historical claim she cannot prove. End in altered work, not blessing or reconciliation.
