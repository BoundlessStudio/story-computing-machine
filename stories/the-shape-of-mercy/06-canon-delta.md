# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

## New characters or character facts

- **Adrian Bellweather (proposed):** Nineteen-year-old third child of House
  Bellweather and first-person protagonist. He was raised to believe he had no
  magic, but possesses severance: the ability to perceive and permanently cut
  a selected magical connection. At the story's end he accepts appointment as
  steward of Bellweather Hall under explicit safeguards governing his gift.
- **Evelyn Bellweather (proposed):** Twenty-six-year-old eldest Bellweather
  sibling and keeper of ashfire. She can reduce forests to ash, once stopped a
  wildfire by burning an untouched tract ahead of it, bears scars beneath her
  left glove, and exercises voluntary restraint during the succession crisis.
- **Marcus Bellweather (proposed):** Twenty-three-year-old older Bellweather
  sibling and wielder of abyssal cold. He has frozen an ocean reach solid
  enough for an army to cross. During the crisis he creates and then
  deliberately lowers an ice wall, records Adrian's pre-act consent in mundane
  ink, and witnesses the severance.
- **Phoebe Bellweather (proposed):** Sixteen-year-old youngest Bellweather
  sibling. Her beast affinity is empathic and non-dominating: she can feel a
  creature's intentions, make room for its distress, distinguish its own wants
  from imposed commands, and guide it without overriding its agency.
- **Lady Rowena Bellweather (proposed):** Mother of the four siblings, head of
  House Bellweather, and a living ward-maker who channels her power through
  silver into objects and structures. She concealed Adrian's gift and history
  for ten years out of both fear and love, then accepts the post-crisis
  safeguards.
- **Garran Bellweather (proposed):** Estranged father of the four siblings and
  former maker of command-bonds. Adrian permanently severed Garran from that
  faculty ten years before the story. Garran returns with a stormhart still
  carrying one of his old bonds, attempts to use it against the family, and is
  confined after the bond is removed. His irreversible loss and grief remain
  morally real without excusing his coercion.

## New locations

- **Proposed — Bellweather Hall:** The seat of House Bellweather and site of
  its succession appointments. Its formal spaces use Rowena's living wards,
  while its household ledger and ordinary ink become the durable evidence for
  Adrian's consent and act.
- **Proposed — the living orchard at Bellweather Hall:** An orchard cultivated
  by generations of Bellweathers to flower in every season. Evelyn burns a
  crescent through it during the crisis, Marcus freezes its ornamental pond and
  surrounding ground, and part of its wall is broken by the freed stormhart.
  The damage will take years to mend.

## New factions or cultural facts

- **Proposed — House Bellweather:** A locally renowned hereditary mage lineage
  in an unnamed high-fantasy era. Its public reputation rests on spectacular
  family faculties, and it marks succession through formal household
  appointments.
- **Proposed — first-desire tradition:** House Bellweather holds that a child's
  magic follows the shape of an early ruling desire. This is a local household
  belief and model, not a universal rule of magic. The family associates
  Evelyn with wanting warmth, Marcus with wanting stillness, Phoebe with
  wanting to understand a trapped animal's pain, and Adrian with wanting harm
  or magic to stop.
- **Proposed — accountable severance practice:** After the crisis, the family
  agrees that severance will use an unenchanted record begun before the act
  when possible and completed by witnesses afterward; Adrian will not be
  tested without informed consent; his history will not be concealed from him;
  and a person's whole gift will not be cut without consent except to stop
  immediate coercion or harm, using the narrowest effective connection.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Proposed — Evelyn's ashfire:** A living faculty capable of forest-scale
  destruction and precise firebreaks. It can burn its wielder, and releasing
  its magical heart can leave ordinary fire that still requires care.
- **Proposed — Marcus's abyssal cold:** A living faculty capable of freezing an
  ocean reach and shaping floodwater into defensive ice. Its ice can trap
  allies, redirect or multiply lightning, and injure its wielder when the
  battlefield turns the cold back on him.
- **Proposed — Phoebe's beast affinity:** A consensual, empathic connection
  that shares felt intention rather than compels obedience. It can expose
  Phoebe to overwhelming pain but lets her distinguish a creature's own desire
  from a foreign command.
- **Proposed — Rowena's wards:** Living protections supplied by Rowena's breath
  and pulse and channeled through silver into objects, clothing, stone, and
  panes of force. The silver and warded objects are conduits, not independent
  magic users.
- **Proposed — Garran's command-bonds:** Connections created by Garran's former
  living will that can force a living creature toward a repeated command. An
  old bond may persist through the living creature that bears it even after
  Garran loses the faculty to create new bonds. A bracelet can focus and
  tighten such a bond but supplies no magic of its own.
- **Proposed — Adrian's severance:** Adrian perceives magical connections as
  distinct, vulnerable edges and can cut a chosen connection. A broad cut can
  permanently remove a person's whole faculty; a narrow cut can remove one
  imposed command while preserving a creature's innate magic and other
  voluntary bonds. Severance does not erase a soul, name, identity, mundane
  skill, arbitrary memory, or all magic by default.
- **Proposed — exact severance cost:** Once Adrian completes a cut, he loses
  autobiographical memory of performing that specific severance and of the
  moments inside its active interval. Knowledge, love, identity, earlier life,
  and consent recorded before the cut begins remain. Questions and agreements
  made only after a test cut was already underway fell inside the erased
  interval. Mundane evidence, physical consequences, and other people's
  memories persist.
- **Proposed — stormharts:** Living antlered magical beasts whose bodies
  generate wind and lightning. A stormhart's storm, fear, anger, and desire to
  run are its own. The specimen in this story remains unnamed.

## Timeline events

- **Proposed — childhood severance, ten years before the story:** Garran tried
  to force six-year-old Phoebe's emerging beast affinity into a dynastic
  weapon by making her share a captive fox's pain. Nine-year-old Adrian
  intervened and reflexively severed Garran from his entire magical faculty.
  Adrian lost his memory of making the cut. Rowena, Evelyn, and Marcus concealed
  the truth, and Garran left the household.
- **Proposed — Bellweather succession crisis:** Garran returns to Bellweather
  Hall with a stormhart compelled by a decade-old command-bond. Evelyn's
  firebreak and Marcus's ice protect guests but also pen the stormhart in.
  Phoebe distinguishes its innate storm from the foreign order. After signing
  pre-act consent, Adrian severs only the command, preserving the stormhart's
  magic, Phoebe's bond, and his siblings' faculties. The stormhart escapes into
  open country, and Garran is confined while his focusing bracelet is retained
  as mundane evidence.
- **Proposed — post-crisis settlement:** The next morning, the family gives
  Adrian the signed pre-act consent and witness account. Adrian accepts
  stewardship only under the accountable severance practice, and Rowena,
  Evelyn, Marcus, and Phoebe explicitly agree.

## New glossary terms or aliases

- **Proposed term — ashfire:** Evelyn Bellweather's destructive fire faculty.
- **Proposed term — abyssal cold:** Marcus Bellweather's ice faculty.
- **Proposed term — beast affinity:** Phoebe Bellweather's empathic,
  non-dominating connection with magical beasts.
- **Proposed term — command-bond:** Garran Bellweather's coercive magical
  connection to a living will.
- **Proposed term — severance:** Adrian Bellweather's faculty for cutting a
  selected magical connection at the cost of remembering the act.
- **Proposed term — stormhart:** A living antlered beast species that generates
  wind and lightning.
- No new personal aliases are proposed.
