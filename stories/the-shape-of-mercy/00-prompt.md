# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] You are a child of a great mage lineage, your eldest sister could turn forests into ash with her flames, your older brother capable of freezing oceans over, your younger sibling, a tamer of magical beasts, but for some reason, everyone fears you the most, and you have zero clue why.**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Shape of Mercy
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: First-person, tightly limited to the feared sibling
- Tense: Past
- Tone and genre: Character-driven high fantasy; wry mystery that deepens into
  emotional and morally serious family drama
- Audience/content rating: Teen / PG-13; magical peril and non-graphic injury
- Required elements: A renowned mage lineage; an eldest sister whose fire can
  reduce forests to ash; an older brother able to freeze oceans; a younger
  sibling who tames magical beasts; a protagonist feared more than all three;
  the protagonist's sincere ignorance of the reason; a concrete, dramatized
  revelation; and a choice that resolves what the protagonist will do with the
  truth
- Prohibited elements: Treating the mystery as only a baseless
  misunderstanding; making the protagonist secretly omniscient; gratuitous
  cruelty; a lore dump in place of a climax; contradicting authoritative canon;
  or ending before the central revelation and choice

## Assumptions

- The protagonist is the third of four siblings and is old enough to act
  independently but still shaped by the family hierarchy.
- The siblings' spectacular powers are real, though the short story may show
  their scale through consequences and credible threat rather than destroying
  an ocean or forest on-page.
- The family's fear has a real magical and emotional cause. The protagonist's
  ignorance is itself part of the causal mystery, not mere social obliviousness.
- The family can be frightened, loving, compromised, and wrong at the same
  time; no relative must become a one-note villain.
- Any setting additions remain local, limited, and explicitly proposed in
  `06-canon-delta.md` rather than treated as established universe facts.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- All three siblings' signature powers matter to the action or the
  protagonist's central choice.
- The reveal explains both why others are afraid and why the protagonist did
  not know, with setup that makes the answer feel earned.
- The ending resolves the immediate threat and the protagonist's relationship
  to their feared capability without requiring a sequel.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
