  - **Severity:** Minor

    `03-draft.md:353–357`

    which reads as the whole cream ticket changing color. Once Sabine appears

    black `HELL`.
  - **Why it matters:** The ticket's visible state is part of the local
    mismatch mechanism, so an unclear or apparently reversed transformation
    makes the transfer rule harder to track.
  - **Smallest effective fix:** Specify exactly which part of the ticket
    darkens during the seizure and carry that same visible state into the
    hell-train window scene.

- **Finding STB-D3**
  - **Severity:** Minor
  - **Location:** `03-draft.md:142–144`, `03-draft.md:263–271`,

    still awaits Ruth's ticket.

    Ruth presents a ticket, or add one brief clause explaining why it is open
    and why presenting Ruth's ticket still matters.
