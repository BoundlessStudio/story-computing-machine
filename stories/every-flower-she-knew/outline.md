# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **Every Flower She Knew**. Safra enters the wild valley alone and returns that night with correctly separated flowers for a fever cot, a depleted storm-channel crew, a millwright ordering memories he already has, and a woman exhausted by night watches. Deliver wonder beside known mortal danger, practiced competence, and baskets that genuinely but boundedly help all four needs.
- Focal pressure or attachment: After years of supplying the village, Safra wants every harvest to remain trustworthy: right flower, stage, dose, and recipient. Complete knowledge cannot enlarge two baskets, delay a rising ford, or make blooms open early.
- Counterforce or complication: Early rain carries known glass-nettle sap downhill, invalidating a familiar ambercup bed. Successive bloom windows, separated compartments, weight, and incompatible flowers make each harvest close later options.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Safra, attentive to route, petals, basket balance, odors, sounds, moisture, and labels. She knows every valley species; the healer alone knows current symptoms, and no viewpoint reveals other interiors.
- Governing movement and time shape: One day framed by village conversations: pre-dawn triage, a solo climb as each collection narrows later choices, then a return against water and darkness. Resolve in the herb room through allocation, not public proof or symbolic closure.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Non-sentient valley flowers hold distinct effects in petal sap. One ambercup petal eases one feverish night; another to the same patient that day causes shaking, and it cannot mend injury or poison. Half a redspur stamen restores spent strength for two hours without repairing damage or preventing later fatigue; it stays viable three dry days. One clearblue filament sharpens access to formed memories for forty minutes, never adding facts or truth, and dulls at midnight. One dried duskbell petal permits about six hours of peaceful, readily broken sleep without treating its cause. Mirrored leaves, metal-sour odor, and motionless dew identify touch-killing glass-nettle; wet runoff wicks its sap through flowers and basket weave. Redspur heat spoils adjacent duskbell. These are story-local magical effects, not real-world claims.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move by selection and exclusion: what Safra smells, hears, weighs, leaves, and rechecks. Keep wonder exact without luxuriating in poison or lecturing.
- Conversational texture: Brief, work-worn bookends over labels, symptoms, petals, water height, weight, and time; ordinary confirmations and corrections, not polished allocation debate.
- Rhetorical ownership: Safra owns species, route, freshness, dose, and separation. The healer reasons from symptoms and triage; the channel forewoman from bodies, loads, water, and hours.
- Pressure behavior: Fatigue clips Safra and makes her repeat verification. The healer slows and listens harder; the forewoman's quick estimates fragment into concrete counts.
- Anti-default: Avoid herbalist-lore cadence, plant-name exposition, aphorisms about knowledge or nature, miracle speeches, and a final exchange explaining sacrifice or community.

## Beats

1. Before dawn, Safra fits dividers and tags into both baskets. The healer updates needs; the channel forewoman supplies storm timing and carrying limits; Safra identifies separation and freshness constraints. Their expertise overlaps without making anyone a foil. Safra leaves alone.
2. At the valley threshold, Safra reads wind and the mirrored underside of a known glass-nettle verge, rejects its shortcut, and takes a dry ledge without contact. Known checks show years of competence amid beauty.
3. Clearblue opens at first sun. She takes mature filaments for the millwright and leaves expiring surplus. A fading petal tone reveals frost-reduced potency.
4. In the lower meadow, she takes exact ambercup and redspur counts, insulating the warm redspur and preserving space for duskbell. Weight, use, and patch recovery govern what stays rooted.
5. Early rain falls. Metal-sour runoff and clinging dew show glass-nettle sap crossing the familiar ambercup bed. Safra rejects everything below the seep, marks three dry days before reuse, and climbs toward a known clean shelf.
6. Cold leaves many ambercups beautiful but inactive. Bright inner veins and a clear rim note identify enough for the fever allotment, with no surplus. Safra leaves optional redspur uncut to balance both baskets for descent.
7. As the ford rises, Safra reaches duskbell during its closing interval, dries and seals the petals, shifts redspur outward, and follows the longer familiar ridge home. She returns after dark with both baskets intact; danger was avoided, not defeated or misunderstood.
8. In the herb room, the three unload by label. Symptoms revise an ambercup portion; redspur goes to the most depleted workers; clearblue is used before midnight; duskbell reaches the night watcher. The fever eases, the crew clears the threatened channel, the millwright orders a remembered repair sequence, and the watcher sleeps while others cover her work. Safra records the compromised bed and next safe harvest date.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Binding style only; no shared botany rule. Safra, the unnamed village and valley, and lower-case flower names are story-local, unconnected to Flint valley or Bellweather's orchard.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Safra knows every dangerous bloom and avoids them; pressure never comes from a secret species, amnesia, foolish contact, or false lore. She travels alone, collects, and returns with plural useful flowers as she has for years. Plants have no voice, morality, intention, prophecy, or universal mechanism. Ordinary care, finite supply, later fatigue, sleeplessness's causes, memory uncertainty, and future harvest risk remain; today's allocations are resolved, not every patient or season.
