# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] She ventures alone into a wild valley where beautiful flowers can heal the sick, restore strength, sharpen memory, or bring peaceful sleep—and others can kill with a touch. She knows every dangerous bloom and carefully avoids them, returning to her village with baskets of wondrous flowers that have helped her people for years.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
