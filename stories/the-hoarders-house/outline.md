# Outline

## Story

- Proposed reader-facing title: *The House of Possible Uses*.
- Premise and central promise: Emrys secretly asks the Museum of Safekeeping for help, and two agents arrive at the dangerously crowded house he shares with Wystan to find a brass artifact lost among eighteen years of purchases and repairs. Their adult niece comes to prevent a covert clear-out but learns that the Maybe Scale has turned imagined future uses into weight borne by the house. Fulfill the promise through a hazardous search requiring museum procedure, the uncles' object-memory, and the niece's trust. The Museum takes the Scale; the family survives, but the ordinary hoard remains.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The unnamed niece loves both men and knows surprise disposal makes Wystan barricade himself. She has also stopped visiting rather than admit their paths are unsafe. She wants to prevent seizure without protecting the fiction that nothing must change. Emrys wants intervention but concealed his call; Wystan has real evidence that moving things can hurt the house.
- Counterforce or complication: No villain. Linet Borne must recover a building-scale hazard, yet her search vocabulary sounds like dispossession. Pascal Redd's readings show the stairwell nearing failure, limiting consultation time. The agents need the uncles' private geography; Emrys has secretly disturbed it, while Wystan sometimes offers possible uses instead of locations. Moving an object can return lethal weight, but leaving all untouched keeps the joists overloaded.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close first-person past through the niece. She understands household shorthand but cannot know which uses are sincere, what is charged, or whether Museum assurances deserve trust. The agents know only instruments, partial provenance, and protocol. Reveal the rule through weight and structural effects; the Scale cannot measure love, illness, usefulness, or truth.
- Governing movement and time shape: One late afternoon, moving inward and upward through narrow paths: porch, front room, kitchen choke point, stair landing, box room. Each movement shifts authority among agents, uncles, and niece. Render the first weight return, landing failure, Scale recovery, and transfer; compress repeated tests. Keep the decisive turn private on the landing, with no public proof or institutional coda.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: The nonliving Maybe Scale responds to a living keeper imagining an object's possible use. It diverts most resulting anomalous weight into the building, leaving the object deceptively light. Deliberate surrender or removal returns stored weight over several seconds; casual doubt does not. Moving the Scale above a containment case's limit releases every charge at once. A braced cradle can accept one return at a time. The uncles' hoarding predates its effect; it judges nothing and has no will.
- Dialogue pressure: Linet needs permission for controlled surrender; Wystan needs her to admit the Museum cannot make the house safe today. Emrys withholds moving the Scale. The niece uses household locations as a proxy for trust. Linet speaks in loads and reversible steps, Wystan in future uses, Emrys in minimizing practicalities, and Pascal only to name physical limits.

## Beats

1. At the blocked porch, Emrys calls the visit an appraisal and Wystan refuses collection. A load pin registers impossible shifting weight; a lamp becomes too heavy for its table when Wystan agrees it can go.
2. Linet explains the immediate limit. The niece brokers entry: nothing moved without a reason, every return tested on the cradle, and no ordinary possession taken. Wystan navigates by object associations rather than room labels.
3. The front room and kitchen reveal charged useful objects, implausible projects, and ordinary obstruction. Emrys's directions fail where he shifted stacks after calling. One controlled surrender crushes sacrificial blocks but lowers the joist reading.
4. A necessary handhold shifts a charged stair carton. The landing drops; Pascal braces it while the family chooses reachable objects to release. Wystan supplies histories instead of blanket refusal, and the niece separates a sentimental possession from a replaceable stockpile.
5. They find the Scale beneath Emrys's relocated magazines. Its charge exceeds the case limit. Wystan authorizes enough staged releases to make transfer possible; Linet allows one genuinely near-term-use item to remain until shoring. Choices remain practical, partial, and pressured.
6. Along the braced route, each person has necessary work: carry, monitor, guide, surrender, or prevent indiscriminate clearing. The Scale enters containment below threshold. The Museum takes only it and knowingly released charged objects. The uncles accept temporary lodging and inspection, not a cure. Stop outside the still-cluttered, officially unsafe house as concrete next steps begin.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Independent local systems and incomplete institutional knowledge; a nonliving artifact may shape magic supplied through living agency. Cost and consent behave only as this mechanism establishes.
- Boundaries and unknowns: The Museum is not a universal authority, the hazard bureaus, or a Rollcaller collection authority. The new local Scale has no implied link to other artifacts or systems. Leave its maker, age, prior path, and other Museum holdings unresolved. Its effect intensifies danger but does not explain away hoarding, attachment, consent, family history, or responsibility.
