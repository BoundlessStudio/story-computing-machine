# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Enna Laird persuades her uncles Tobren and Sael to admit Palinode Museum agents Kezra Vonn and Daskar Avel, who trace an unregistered dangerous artifact to their crowded house. Poking through its rooms wakes a collector's seed that folds the building into repeating storage. The five must identify the overlapping object-signals, stop the fold, and contain the danger.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Enna wants blocked exits and unstable piles made safe without having her uncles treated as contaminated inventory. Tobren preserves repairable goods after years of scarcity; Sael preserves a spatial system of useful stock and family records. Their accumulation is hazardous, but their knowledge remains necessary.
- Counterforce or complication: Kezra protects provenance; Daskar prioritizes containment. Their survey lantern makes three unrelated artifacts answer through sound, light, and vibration, which the seed mistakes for a collection needing rooms. Each person first trusts the evidence mode they understand while the geometry narrows.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close first-person past through Enna, who knows the old floor plan, family tensions, and uses attached to piles but not artifact mechanics. Signals never become speech; causes emerge only by blocking one modality at a time.
- Governing movement and time shape: One late afternoon advances room by room: bounded inspection, accumulating signals, repeating thresholds, separation across false rooms, coordinated silencing, containment. End at the next morning's limited sorting session, with safety work begun but the household neither cured nor emptied.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: A whisper-vane sounds near hidden openings, mothglass flashes near sudden heat, and a step-stone pulses when enclosed volume changes; none knows people or language. Three recurring modes activate the collector's seed, which grows storage rooms but cannot distinguish possessions from people. It stops only when fewer than two modes remain and it enters a dark, sound-damped vessel held off the floor. Bare movement accelerates folding. The survey lantern can isolate outputs, not locate the seed without the family's house map.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Enna measures usable space against remembered domestic purpose: a shoulder-wide path, buried table edge, wrong distance between familiar doors. Wonder arrives through geometry, not lore exposition.
- Conversational texture: Permission, provenance, family correction, and terse safety calls are interrupted by tones, blocked sightlines, and floor pulses.
- Rhetorical ownership: Enna owns routes and relationships; Tobren repair histories and possible use; Sael counts and exceptions; Kezra classification and custody; Daskar immediate hazards and confirmation.
- Pressure behavior: Enna drops cushioning; Tobren's defensive origins shorten; Sael grows quieter and exact; Kezra admits uncertainty; Daskar repeats receipts and rejects assumptions.
- Anti-default: Avoid witty agent banter, a translated artifact voice, or a debate judging hoarding; let unequal knowledge and interfering signals cause misunderstandings.

## Beats

1. Enna clears the agreed entry lane, exposing a blocked side door and the brothers' different systems: Tobren sorts by possible use, Sael by remembered position. Each uncle excludes a different room before consenting to a bounded inspection.
2. Kezra follows the museum record to a stone among drawer pulls. Daskar's lantern tests heat, resonance, and motion; a whistle, red light, and floor pulse answer elsewhere. Each person blames a different object.
3. Moving a cabinet reveals a second pantry where the yard should be. Sael proves shelves duplicated; Enna proves the wall did not move. The front-door route now returns them to the stairs.
4. They block one modality at a time: cover mothglass, brace the step-stone, seal air around the whisper-vane. Individual signals stop, but the active fold continues. Kezra infers the threshold without identifying the core.
5. Another fold separates Tobren and Kezra from Enna, Sael, and Daskar. Sound rounds false corners, color crosses a nonadjacent door, and directionless floor pulses cause a dangerous misunderstanding about who is trapped.
6. Enna requests evidence, not interpretations. Tobren reconstructs the drawer-pull box's history; Sael identifies an item that moved before the inspection; Kezra narrows the trigger; Daskar marks handling limits. Plumbing and wall scars lead Enna to the original pantry.
7. They clear a straight lane while suppressing all three signals. The uncles choose which obstructing goods may be broken or abandoned. Kezra prepares the vessel, Daskar suspends it, and Enna extracts the seed after the signal count falls; false rooms collapse empty.
8. The agents separately contain four artifacts and document ownership. The house remains overfull and unsafe. Next morning, the brothers begin one chosen route and category, holding disputed objects for later rather than accepting indiscriminate removal.
