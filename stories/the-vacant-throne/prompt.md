# Prompt

## Prompt

> **[WP]Fifteen years ago, the human king and the demon queen fought a legendary battle that resulted in the king's death. The throne remained vacant until a half-demon child appeared bearing the royal sword.**

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV and tense: choose from the prompt
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Avoid: none specified
