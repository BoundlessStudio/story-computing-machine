[WP] Perched upon the weathered stone battlements of an ancient watchtower, a young princess gazes across her kingdom as thousands of lanterns illuminate the streets and colorful fireworks bloom against the moonlit sky. In the distance, her family's magnificent royal castle rises above the city, its towers glowing warmly against the night. Below her, musicians play, children dance, merchants celebrate, and families gather for the annual Festival of Lights. A cherished tradition passed down through generations. Having quietly slipped away from the palace celebrations, the princess enjoys a rare moment of solitude, watching her people laugh and dance without knowing she is among them. Yet as she observes their happiness, she finds herself wondering what life might be like beyond the expectations of royalty — and whether she could ever experience the simple freedoms enjoyed by the people she will one day rule.
