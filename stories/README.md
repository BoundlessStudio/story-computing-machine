# Stories

A new `[WP]` package uses `prompt.md`, a short provisional `outline.md`, `story.md`, `notes.md`, and `ratings.md`. The prompt preserves the user's request; the outline gives readers a direction to challenge before drafting and may be left behind as prose improves. The story is the reader-facing prose. The notes preserve the editorial conversation and why important changes were made. A cover follows by default once the prose is ready, unless the user asks for prose only. Supplied original images live in that story's `art/references/`; accepted character and location art lives in its `art/` directories. Keep rejected art candidates outside the story package. Additional generated art is optional and should earn its place.

Each finished story also has `ratings.md`, using General, Teen, Mature, or Explicit for the strongest material in the prose and its cover. It gives a brief reason and specific content notes. General is the starting target for new stories without a requested audience; it is not assigned automatically to finished work. See the [content boundaries](../universe/style-guide.md#audience-and-content-boundaries) for the definitions.

Older `PG`, `YA`, and `R+` labels in preserved prompts describe earlier directions or publication history. `ratings.md` is the current classification of the finished story.

Older packages keep their existing four-file or bundle layout. Their `outline.md` and `review.md` remain history; do not rewrite them just to match a new template. Add or update `notes.md` for an authorized prose change that alters meaning, action, or voice; a purely mechanical correction does not need it. Current stories mark canon in `story.md` frontmatter; bundles use `story.json`. Check the marker before editing. See [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for permissions and [story-create](../.agents/skills/story-create/SKILL.md) for the editorial practice.

`NAMES.md` is older name memory. It can help avoid confusing reuse, but neither it nor an old review establishes a universe fact. Shared facts belong to [universe/](../universe/README.md).

For collection visual direction and art provenance, see [universe/art.md](../universe/art.md). The [repository README](../README.md) gives a short introduction; [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) governs permissions and working practice.
