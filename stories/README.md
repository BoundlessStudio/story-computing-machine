# Stories

A new `[WP]` package uses `prompt.md`, a short provisional `outline.md`, `story.md`, `notes.md`, and `ratings.md`. The prompt preserves the user's request; the outline gives readers a direction to challenge before drafting and may be left behind as prose improves. The story is the reader-facing prose. The notes preserve the editorial conversation and why important changes were made.

Once the prose is ready, make and inspect multi-view reference sheets for the principal characters and reference paintings for the story's important distinct locations first, then make the cover using the relevant accepted references, unless the user asks for prose only. Include important locations beyond the cover scene: a story with multiple important settings needs at least two distinct location references; a story confined to one setting needs only that place. Alternate views or corrections of one location do not count as multiple locations. A scene portrait does not replace a character sheet; an ensemble sheet must give each principal character full studies and clear labels. Supplied original images live in that story's `art/references/`; accepted character art lives in `art/characters/`, and location paintings in `art/landscapes/` or `art/interiors/`. Name final art for its character or place, without paired “Original” and “Selected” location copies or a new selection manifest. Keep drafts, superseded candidates, and rejected art under the worktree's `tmp/`. Additional generated art is optional and should earn its place.

Each finished story also has `ratings.md`, using General, Teen, Mature, or Explicit for the strongest material in the prose and its cover. It gives a brief reason and specific content notes. General is the starting target for new stories without a requested audience; it is not assigned automatically to finished work. See the [content boundaries](../universe/style-guide.md#audience-and-content-boundaries) for the definitions.

Older `PG`, `YA`, and `R+` labels in preserved prompts describe earlier directions or publication history. `ratings.md` is the current classification of the finished story.

Older packages keep their existing four-file or bundle layout. Their `outline.md` and `review.md` remain history; do not rewrite them just to match a new template. Add or update `notes.md` for an authorized prose change that alters meaning, action, or voice; a purely mechanical correction does not need it. Current stories mark canon in `story.md` frontmatter; bundles use `story.json`. Check the marker before editing. See [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for permissions and [story-create](../.agents/skills/story-create/SKILL.md) for the editorial practice.

`NAMES.md` is older name memory. It can help avoid confusing reuse, but neither it nor an old review establishes a universe fact. Shared facts belong to [universe/](../universe/README.md).

For collection visual direction and art provenance, see [universe/art.md](../universe/art.md). The [repository README](../README.md) gives a short introduction; [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) governs permissions and working practice.
