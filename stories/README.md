# Stories

Every story has its own directory: `stories/<lowercase-kebab-slug>/`.
Directories beginning with `_` are support directories, not stories:
`_template/` is the production template and `_legacy/` is the non-canon source,
research, decision-provenance, and adaptation-backlog archive.

The numbered files form a durable chain from source prompt to final prose:

1. `00-prompt.md` — verbatim prompt plus explicit story contract.
2. `01-canon-brief.md` — researched constraints and safe invention space.
3. `02-story-plan.md` — causal, scene-level architecture.
4. `03-draft.md` — complete working prose.
5. `04-review.md` — preserved draft and final-story review passes, with the
   current certification naming the artifact it covers.
6. `05-story.md` — polished reader-facing story.
7. `06-canon-delta.md` — reusable facts proposed for canon promotion.

Only `05-story.md` is reader-facing. Nothing in a story directory becomes
shared canon until the index says canon `yes`. A completed candidate must have
a latest `04-review.md` certification that names `05-story.md` and has verdict
`PASS`; review of `03-draft.md` alone is not sufficient.
