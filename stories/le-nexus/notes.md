## Line edit

The editor heard a voice “dry, courtly and exact about the body” in Keva making dance corners around the kitchen table. The writer gave the run of the floor a longer sentence and the three knocks their own space. “I felt both knocks in the heel of the hand I was holding the wrist with” became “The answer came up through the steel into the heel of my palm.” The rehearsal’s “For as long as I leaned against it she kept it there” became “She straightened up, took my steel hand off her waist and put her own hand flat at my side.” That keeps the change visible and saves the catch. In the pause after the knocks, “Her eyes lifted from the thumb to find mine” gave way to a smile Keva is close enough to see tremble into place. The original’s red wiring returns through the light and movement, and Odile speaks Keva’s name in the dance. The writer let the catch stay quiet so Keva can feel Odile’s work under her hand.
