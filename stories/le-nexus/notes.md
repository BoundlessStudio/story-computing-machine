# Editorial notes

## Request readings · 2026-11-14

**Codex keeper, before seeing a plot.** The prompt requires repeated reaches toward a stranger at Le Nexus, a physical pin, a message in a code the wearer never learned, and a reply after the red cocktail goes down. “The distinctive pleasure is a bodily breach that becomes a private, deliberate exchange in public.” The keeper expects the arm's history to affect a present choice and reads an intimate, poised charge in the opening, with mechanism, scale, and any romance open. The original casts a guarded dark-haired woman at ease with the room and an openly amused woman capable of setting the pace, among crowded elbows and neon.

**Claude outline reader, before seeing a plot.** “A conversation runs through the protagonist’s own body, and they’re shut out of it.” Claude anticipated enough of both messages and the arm’s prior life to change what it means for the wearer to use it. It saw the “you” as placing the reader in the wearer’s position, private intrigue in a crowded neon bar, and several possible stakes beyond that opening. It read the pictured pairing as seasoned skepticism meeting a warm, knowing ease. An old owner and a romantic turn were inferences.
