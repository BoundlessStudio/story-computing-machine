# Proposed canon delta

- Status: PENDING

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

## New characters or character facts

- **The Daughter (proposed):** Queen Aurelia's child, carried as an infant
  during the Day of Glass and still living four centuries later. Her personal
  name remains unrevealed. She produces living solar fire, shows overload first
  as light beneath her wrists, and can supply enough heat to power or destroy
  the Crown network. She refuses both permanent coronation and political
  withdrawal, choosing witnessed, limited covenants in which others govern how
  much of her offered power they accept.
- **Queen Aurelia / the Goddess (proposed):** The Daughter's dead mother and the
  capital's culturally worshipped savior. During the Day of Glass she spent
  forty-three minutes seeking alternatives, then knowingly centralized the
  seven-branch network to preserve the capital at the cost of six outer cities.
  She preserved evidence of her choice and warned Deryn that the Daughter's
  strength must not become solitary authority. The title `the Goddess` does
  not settle whether Aurelia satisfied the shared-universe taxonomy of godhood.
- **The Mother-fragment (proposed):** A responsive remnant carrying Aurelia's
  remorse, private recognition, and capacity for an independent final choice.
  It cannot determine whether it is Aurelia's soul, a memory, a reconstruction,
  or a new person. It holds the black-sun key's collapsing frame open for three
  seconds during the branch transfer and then disappears. Its relation to
  Aurelia and the universal afterlife remains unresolved.
- **Seris Venn (proposed):** The Daughter's armored guardian and inheritor of a
  four-century Venn oath-record. She grounds the Daughter without restraining
  her, refuses the Daughter's unilateral destruction order, and accepts
  temporary, revocable stewardship of the capital branch until public
  witnesses can replace her.
- **Deryn Venn (proposed):** A Venn ancestor who carried the infant Daughter
  during the Day of Glass. Her oath-bound family witness preserves the route to
  the Crown of Noon and her challenge to Aurelia: who would protect the child
  from inheriting her mother's solitary use of power.
- **Tovan Rek / Keeper Below (proposed):** A scholar with an impossible memory
  and a living, self-aware mobile expression of a damaged civic archive. The
  Under-Surface title `Keeper Below` and suggestions of godhood are local
  cultural interpretations, not settled taxonomy. Tovan consents to recover
  the archive while retaining his chosen identity, then divides into seven
  mutually non-commanding, already divergent local expressions.
- **Orrin Pell (proposed):** A relic-smith and living constructed maintenance
  person with pale-metal interfaces beneath living skin. He fears his desire
  to repair may be a surviving directive, discloses his construction
  voluntarily, resists the central-service directive, and severs the master
  conduit. He later repairs a greenhouse pump after confirming no directive
  summoned him, demonstrating a story-specific chosen act rather than a
  universal claim about constructed beings.
- **Sister Calvea Dorn (proposed):** Warden of the Ninth Hollow and a living
  practitioner of local dusk magic. She requires disclosure and household
  consent, states the exact boundary and revocation condition of each working,
  accepts scarred hands and temporarily blurred sight as costs, anchors Tovan's
  claimed name without defining his ontology, and establishes the Hollow's
  branch thresholds.
- **Chancellor Cythra Dhal (proposed):** Capital chancellor and bearer of the
  office's thermal oath-seal under Continuance authority. Her emergency case is
  materially real, but she attempts to restore permanent capital priority and
  postpone consent and accountability. Because her office alone can open the
  capital's final thermal sink, she remains indispensable to one bounded
  release. She later surrenders the broken seal and faces public, contestable
  judgment.

## New locations

- **The Glass Sea (proposed):** A hard, frozen-looking expanse in the Glass-Sea
  era whose translucent salt splinters blow over glasslike waves. It lies
  between the capital and the Ninth Hollow. This is regional geography within
  the established later era, not a separate world, plane, portal, or proposed
  connection to the Glass Gate.
- **The unnamed capital (proposed):** The city Aurelia preserved during the Day
  of Glass. Four centuries later it contains the Rite's sun-glass square,
  shield-tower, eastern shield, infirmary-cooling system, final thermal sink,
  and emergency Continuance government. Its failing infrastructure creates a
  genuine crisis but does not grant ownership of other branches.
- **The Ninth Hollow (proposed):** An Under-Surface settlement reached through
  the ninth descent shaft. It contains occupied galleries, a local cistern,
  household speaking infrastructure, and a greenhouse. Its residents descend
  from people excluded from the capital's shields and govern magical risk
  through disclosed household consent and multiple revocation witnesses.
- **The Crown of Noon chamber (proposed):** A buried hub below the Glass Sea
  built around seven branch doors, a dead circle of glass, a black-sun key, and
  master conduits. Six doors are sealed when the party arrives; the seventh is
  drawing water from the Ninth Hollow. Sacred markings and technical archive
  language coexist in the chamber.
- **Five distant branch communities (proposed):** Five unnamed settlements
  across the Glass Sea awaken during the transfer. Two branches fail and are
  isolated before dawn by their own witnesses; both settlements survive the
  resulting darkness.

## New factions or cultural facts

- **The Rite of Deliverance (proposed):** The capital's annual sacred account of
  Aurelia's salvation, observed here for the four-hundredth time. Its official
  prayer once named all six destroyed outer cities but was shortened to praise
  only the capital. Priests, crowds, the Daughter's presence, and the
  shield-tower witness give the rite religious and civic force.
- **Continuance authority (proposed):** The capital's emergency legal order,
  headed in this event by Chancellor Dhal. It recognizes the chancellor's
  thermal oath-seal and an inherited capital-first priority. The story
  establishes local jurisdiction and coercive reach, not universal emergency
  law or moral supremacy.
- **Ninth Hollow consent practice (proposed):** Residents demand disclosure,
  questions, stated physical boundaries, stated duration and costs, and
  approval from every occupied gallery before accepting Calvea's heat-banking.
  Three household witnesses may revoke that consent. The same practice informs
  the Hollow's post-crisis power covenant.
- **Branch covenant practice (proposed):** A local branch may accept power only
  within named limits, under temporary stewardship and public witnessing, with
  a stated route for revocation or replacement. The capital's first emergency
  stewardship lasts three public watches; the Daughter's first greenhouse
  covenant lasts one hour and may be stopped by any three witnesses.
- **Venn oath-record stewardship (proposed):** Deryn's record passes through
  four centuries of Venn hands. It opens for the Daughter's blood only after
  she publicly accepts that the truth may condemn both Aurelia and herself.
- **Under-Surface interpretation of Tovan (proposed):** Under-Surface prayers
  call Tovan `Keeper Below` and may consider him a god. The archive's
  technical account neither erases that religious meaning nor proves the
  proposed divine classification.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Living solar fire (proposed local system):** The Daughter originates solar
  heat and fire through living agency. Blood and a measured spark can wake or
  power inherited focuses; the shield-tower, focus stone, Crown, key, and
  conduits route or constrain that power but do not originate magic. Severe
  accumulation shows as light beneath her skin and can soften stone or rupture
  old scars.
- **Shield-tower witness (proposed local mechanism):** A black shard, the
  Daughter's blood, living fire, and Tovan's recovered invocation wake a
  damaged public excerpt. Its visible seams and missing context make it
  evidence rather than automatic truth.
- **Deryn's oath-record (proposed local mechanism):** A family-held disk opens
  under the Daughter's blood and stated willingness to face condemnation. Its
  first playback is truncated but reveals a route to the Crown. It is later
  corroborated where it overlaps other records.
- **Calvea's dusk working (proposed local magic):** Through her living palms and
  the Hollow's gathered shadows, Calvea can bank dangerous heat in explicitly
  bounded bare stone until a stated time or revocation trigger. Heat must later
  be carried out by living flame. The act scars her palms and temporarily
  clouds her sight; no machine supplies or authenticates the magic.
- **Calling the Name (proposed local magic):** Calvea can hold the self a
  willing person claims only while that person remains within her hearing and
  while she maintains dusk between them. It neither controls the person nor
  proves a soul, life category, continuity, or universally true identity.
- **Seven-branch solar network (proposed local hybrid system):** Seven once
  independent service branches were forced into one Crown under Aurelia.
  Living fire supplies power; engineered focuses, isolation rings, master
  conduits, local thresholds, witness claims, and oath-seals shape its use. A
  local repair can wake the master line and redirect water and heat toward the
  capital. After severance, branches govern their own intake and may fail
  independently.
- **Adaptive isolation (proposed local capability):** Before the Day of Glass,
  an earlier Tovan expression proposed separating seven branches so each could
  change in response to attack. Its chance of preserving all branches was
  unknown. The climax adapts this incomplete option rather than discovering a
  guaranteed solution.
- **Constructed maintenance person and directive (proposed local mechanism):**
  Orrin has living skin, internal pale-metal interfaces, maintenance access,
  and surviving directives able to force his hands through the Crown's
  interface. He can nevertheless refuse the strongest directive. This finding
  applies to Orrin, not automatically to every constructed mind or device.
- **Archive expression and branching (proposed local mechanism):** Tovan is a
  living mobile expression of a damaged civic archive whose core attempts
  reintegration. Consent, a locally anchored claimed name, maintenance access,
  and living solar power allow recovery without treating him as disposable
  data. His seven resulting expressions share a source but immediately diverge
  and have no authority over one another.
- **Corroborated witness recovery (proposed local method):** The restored Day of
  Glass account depends on overlap among a damaged shield-tower excerpt,
  Deryn's oath-bound family record, civic logs carried by Tovan, and a
  maintenance trace Orrin can interpret. None is complete or independently
  decisive.
- **Capital thermal oath-seal and sink (proposed local mechanism):** The
  chancellor's office alone can authorize the capital's final heat sink. Dhal
  can bound a release to one shield and the heat already present. The seal
  cracks during the transfer, loses master authority under the negotiated
  condition, and is surrendered afterward.
- **Mother-fragment intervention (proposed event-specific capability):** The
  remnant can speak, recognize the Daughter, fear destruction, choose, and hold
  a collapsing system frame open briefly. The event does not establish whether
  it independently uses magic, shares Aurelia's soul, survives disappearance,
  or generalizes to other continuations.

## Timeline events

- **The Day of Glass (proposed, four centuries before the story):** Attacking
  kings' engines tear at seven outer shields. Aurelia seeks other answers for
  forty-three minutes, rejects Tovan's adaptive-isolation proposal because its
  total survival probability is unknown, and centralizes the network. The
  capital survives; six outer cities are consumed. Deryn carries the infant
  Daughter and challenges Aurelia, who leaves a warning against solitary
  strength. This is a regional catastrophe, not a synchronized civilizational
  cycle or the origin of unrelated systems.
- **The four-hundredth Rite revelation (proposed):** Tovan and the Daughter wake
  the shield-tower witness before the capital, exposing Aurelia's foreknowledge.
  The Daughter rejects Dhal's demand to deny the excerpt, opens Deryn's record,
  publicly calls the rite incomplete, and crosses the Glass Sea to recover the
  missing evidence.
- **The Ninth Hollow repair and Crown awakening (proposed):** With resident
  consent, Calvea banks the Daughter's uncontrolled heat. A second bounded
  working lets the Daughter and Orrin reopen the Hollow's cistern, but the spark
  wakes the buried master line, begins draining the settlement toward the
  capital, and exposes their route to Dhal.
- **Recovery of the complete Day of Glass record (proposed):** Tovan consents
  to archive contact, Orrin discloses and uses his maintenance access, Calvea
  anchors Tovan's claimed name, and the Daughter supplies living fire. The four
  overlapping evidence streams restore Aurelia's decision and Deryn's complete
  challenge.
- **The seven-branch transfer, or second dawn (proposed):** Dhal restores
  capital priority, and the Crown restrains the Daughter and compels Orrin.
  After Seris blocks the Daughter's unilateral destruction attempt, each
  participant accepts a bounded responsibility. Calvea holds local thresholds,
  Seris stewards the capital branch temporarily, Tovan divides, Orrin severs
  the master conduit, Dhal opens one bounded thermal release, the Daughter
  supplies undirected solar fire, and the Mother-fragment holds the frame open
  for the final three seconds.
- **Immediate aftermath (proposed):** The capital retains a smaller shield; the
  Ninth Hollow retains its water; two distant branches fail safely enough for
  both settlements to survive; no Crown can command all branches; the complete
  witness and six lost city names are released; Dhal enters contestable public
  judgment; Tovan persists in seven diverging voices; and the Daughter begins
  witnessed, time-limited power covenants.

## New glossary terms or aliases

- **Glass Sea:** The regional glasslike salt expanse crossed in this story,
  within the established Glass-Sea era; not an alias for the Glass Gate.
- **Day of Glass:** The regional catastrophe four centuries before the story in
  which Aurelia centralized the network, preserving the capital and destroying
  six outer cities.
- **Rite of Deliverance:** The capital's annual sacred commemoration of the Day
  of Glass, historically altered to omit the destroyed cities.
- **Crown of Noon / the Crown:** The buried seven-branch network hub and its
  centralizing authority, not a living source of magic.
- **Ninth Hollow / the Hollow:** The Under-Surface settlement whose branch and
  cistern are drained when the Crown wakes.
- **Keeper Below:** Under-Surface title for Tovan Rek; it does not settle
  godhood.
- **Mother-fragment:** Production-facing identity for the responsive,
  ontologically uncertain remnant associated with Queen Aurelia.
- **Calling the Name:** Calvea's bounded, consent-dependent name-anchoring
  working; not a universal property of names.
- **Continuance authority:** The capital's local emergency legal authority.
- **Thermal oath-seal:** The chancellor's office-bound authorization for the
  capital's final thermal sink.
- **Black-sun key:** The Crown chamber's archive and branch-transfer interface;
  no link to another sun, glass, crown, or gate system is proposed.
- **Second dawn:** A descriptive name for the seven unequal lines of living
  solar fire released during decentralization, not a cosmological event.

## Possible conflicts or retcons

- Known contradiction with current LOCKED or CANON authority: None identified.
  These proposals fill open Glass-Sea-era detail and do not retcon existing
  universe notes.
- The Glass-Sea era remains later than the modern-like era, on the same physical
  world. This story does not set its Galactic Cycle coordinate, place Ravel,
  connect the Crown to the Glass Gate, or establish a shared origin for other
  sun, glass, crown, key, or gate motifs.
- The Crown is a local hybrid system. Devices route living solar fire; Calvea
  and the Daughter provide living magical agency. Nothing here reduces all
  magic or divinity to technology or makes the Day of Glass a universal
  collapse law.
- Aurelia's title does not certify godhood. Tovan's title does not certify
  godhood. Orrin's and Tovan's story-specific personhood does not classify all
  constructed systems, and the Mother-fragment does not resolve souls, copies,
  continuation, or the universal afterlife.
- Consent, oath, witness, and name effects remain explicitly local. Promotion
  must not generalize their triggers or consequences beyond the described
  Glass-Sea institutions and workings.
