- The Glass-Sea era is a later era of the same physical world as the modern-like and Ravel eras, not another planet or plane. Ravel's position relative to it remains unresolved. (`universe/premise.md`, “The deep-time shared world” [LOCKED]; `universe/locations.md`, “The world across eras” [LOCKED]; `universe/timeline.md`, “Established era order” [CANON].)
- Civilizational collapse may recur but is neither a synchronized cycle nor a universal law. The solar network cannot become the origin of every system or anomaly. (`universe/rules.md`, “System categories” [LOCKED].)
- Magic, technology, and divinity are culture-bound labels for real systems. Systems may hybridize, but a technical reveal cannot reduce all magic or divinity to machinery. (`universe/rules.md`, “System categories” [LOCKED]; `universe/glossary.md`, “Magic, technology, and divinity” [LOCKED].)
- Only life can use magic. A nonliving device may focus power supplied through living agency; an independently acting constructed mind, projection, fragment, or artifact must first be established as alive. (`universe/rules.md`, “Living agency and magic” [LOCKED].)
- Canonical godhood requires continuing worship, power, and authority. Construction neither grants nor disqualifies it, and cultural titles such as Goddess do not settle the taxonomy. (`universe/rules.md`, “Divinity” [CANON].)
- Technical explanation must preserve the identity, grief, consent, relationships, and moral choices of constructed, copied, projected, branched, or reconstructed people. Any soul fragment or continuation must remain coherent with the universal afterlife without being assumed identical to it. (`universe/style-guide.md`, “Personhood and moral consequence” [LOCKED]; `universe/rules.md`, “Afterlife and continuation” and “Memory, transformation, copies, and branches” [CANON].)
- Names are not universally magical, and consent has no universal metaphysical behavior. Duskkeeping, oath-binding, Calling the Name, and consent-sensitive thresholds must be bounded local mechanisms with consistent limits. (`universe/rules.md`, “Costs, desire, and local mechanisms” and “Names and identity binding” [CANON].)
- Witness memories and archives require provenance, limits, and causal mechanisms. No artifact is automatically decisive, and myth or memory cannot arbitrarily patch contradictions. (`universe/rules.md`, “Records, falsehood, and historical traces” [CANON]; `universe/style-guide.md`, “Continuity and evidence discipline” [LOCKED].)
- Shared sun, glass, crown, or gate imagery does not prove shared origin. Do not connect the Glass Sea, Crown of Noon, or black-sun key to the Glass Gate, Ravel, or another system without evidence. (`universe/rules.md`, “Portals and crossing systems” and “Artifacts, motifs, and folklore” [CANON].)
- Follow the Teen ceiling and non-explicit, non-graphic treatment of intimacy, violence, horror, abuse, and death. (`stories/daughter-of-the-sun/00-prompt.md`; `universe/style-guide.md`, “Audience and content boundaries” [LOCKED].)
