# Provisional direction: Grand Theft Me

The request joins a sound body, a new attachment, and fear that discovery of the speaker's "true nature" will bring rejection. The user requested a fresh direction, endorsed the romantic theft-of-self idea subject to careful setup, then asked, "she ai cant she do both?" I choose a buoyant first-person caper with one AI person acting through her warship and purchased body concurrently. The disguise causes the trouble; both bodies will actively solve different parts of the escape. The provisional payoff remains a negotiated criminal partnership carrying debt, anger, and another outrageous prospect. Romance, comedy, and that ending are creative choices.

Ilex (she/her) is the living intelligence of the retired naval interdiction ship *Indemnity*. Discharge gave her ownership of her sound hull. She answers public cargo questions from its idle comms. On their first call she knowingly helps salvage captain Fara correct a doctored manifest for love-song cylinders instead of reporting her. Fara keeps calling afterward; they trade appalling songs and increasingly personal jokes. Fara assumes Ilex is a customs clerk, and Ilex lets her. On a video call Fara docks her empty shuttle by wrist controller while ashore, showing its stout salvage prow and explaining that her next profitable job will buy it a decent drive. She mocks naval pomp and confiscations; Ilex edits her service out of her stories.

Fara asks to visit. Ilex drafts an invitation with her true berth number, looking around the comfortable observation lounge where she could receive Fara, then deletes it. Giving her address would reveal the naval weapon behind the imagined clerk. On the bridge, the bodywright hears the prompt's exchange. Ilex orders a civilian human-scale body instead of showing Fara the self she already has. The bodywright finances it against the hull's auction: payment releases only after accepted arrival at the buyer's refit berth; failed delivery leaves Ilex owing the body advance and a non-delivery penalty.

The bodywright installs Ilex's compact person-core in the purchased body and pairs it with the hull's existing control network. One mind receives both sets of senses and directs both bodies. Ilex takes her first small steps while still looking through the hull's cameras and working its doors; a delighted greeting comes out of both her mouth and the ship's PA. For the sale she disconnects the hull, leaving a vacant, manually flyable ship. Her pairing keys remain usable until the buyer replaces them. The bodywright demonstrates the manual helm and the physical ownership-key slot the delivery crew will use. The helm, slot, and service-berth controls share the bridge.

The catalogue gives Fara her target. She obtains a breaker's offer for its engines and invites Ilex to the auction gala. She will steal the whole hull, fly it to the breaker, and use her share to buy her shuttle's drive. Their private jokes and attraction survive Ilex's unfamiliar elbows. A weapons company wins the auction. Fara lifts its bearer delivery warrant from the courier and offers Ilex a criminal date. Fara will pose as the collection captain, take the manual helm, follow the authorized delivery route, then divert; Ilex is her invited companion, the apparent clerk. The real crew is due in forty minutes. By wrist command Fara docks her shuttle to *Indemnity*'s service berth; it will travel with the hull to carry her home, or provide an independent escape. She gives Ilex the option to stay at the party.

Ilex sees a security officer who could stop Fara and preserve her payment, but refuses to make their first meeting an arrest. Telling the truth might end the connection she has sold her body to preserve. She follows Fara, hoping to keep the sale, disguise, and date. Her knowledge makes the clerk story increasingly peculiar. To stall the theft without exposing herself, she uses a retained owner credential to disable engine start while her small body distracts Fara. The auction's supervision flags the intervention and summons the buyer's crew early. They arrive in a company tug, clamp it to the boarding collar, lock Fara's shuttle berth, and start replacing the ownership keys. Fara discovers Ilex's sabotage while both are trapped on the bridge.

Ilex identifies herself and deliberately reopens the full hull connection. Escaping in the sold ship means accepting the debt she tried to avoid. Fara realizes she has spent weeks making navy jokes to a warship. Ilex keeps talking from her purchased body as the room around them answers: doors divide the crew, engines wake, and hull cameras show the tug holding them fast. One technician is already on their side of the bridge bulkhead with the replacement key module. Fara distracts the technician; Ilex's small body snatches the module before it can be seated and retreats behind the helm while her ship-body closes the pursuing crew's route. The stolen module stays in her small hand as she drives the crew back into the tug's boarding cabin with successive doors. Her new body physically prevents revocation of the access her hull is using.

Ilex reopens Fara's shuttle berth, giving her the independent escape they planned. The tug still clamps the hull; pulling it through the crowded yard would endanger everyone nearby. Fara instead uses her wrist controller to send her unmanned shuttle's salvage prow into the clamp linkage, spending her own vessel to free Ilex. The crew remains safely aboard its tug. Fara bluffs the departure gate with the warrant and her cargo-channel fluency. Ilex maneuvers her ship-body while her small body braces Fara against a lurch and secures the stolen module. She experiences both the hull clearing the dock and Fara's weight against her new arms, without transferring, copying herself, or abandoning either body.

Once clear, the bodywright's invoice and a buyer's redelivery warrant put names to their trouble: advance, auction penalty, and a fugitive hull the buyer still claims. Fara has lost her shuttle and payday; she remains angry about the deception and sabotage. She proposes making the buyer pay with *Indemnity*'s guns. Ilex vetoes it and proposes stealing the buyer's next auction deposit from its accounts office. The hull stays hidden; two ordinary-size accomplices can enter its small lift. Ilex requires a speaking part and either partner's veto, and gives Fara first choice of cover identities. Fara agrees while Ilex stands beside her and flies them toward cover. Their next undertaking is chosen together, with the first one's costs still due.
