# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

## New characters or character facts

- **Gideon Tolland / Night Clerk:** Gideon is a teacher at Northbridge
  Secondary and a volunteer emergency-radio operator. Six months before the
  showcase incident, he answered an anonymous request from three magical girls
  for logistical and safeguarding support; on their voice-scrambled relay he
  operates as Night Clerk. He later inferred the girls’ civilian identities
  from cumulative mundane evidence but keeps that knowledge from them because
  their anonymity is a condition of accepting his help. His role is supportive
  and has no command authority.
- **Drita Ademi / Kiteglass:** Drita, fifteen, is the trio’s tactical planner.
  As Kiteglass she creates angled translucent barriers that redirect force.
  Sustained use drains body heat and aggravates her bruised wrist. During the
  showcase incident, she chooses to guard the evacuation route instead of
  pursuing the Rollcaller and later encloses its exposed living body.
- **Bryn Ahn / Pulsewire:** Bryn, sixteen, is a systems-minded member of the
  trio who habitually identifies multiple routes. As Pulsewire she senses and
  severs active magical connections at close range; severance can cause
  tremor, headache, sensory halos, and temporary sound sensitivity. She
  identifies the Rollcaller’s bridge into the team relay, requests the full
  blackout, and performs the final severance after both teammates consent.
- **Cassia Dominguez / Mothlight:** Cassia, fifteen, is a quick-witted member
  of the trio who uses grounding counts to manage anxiety without treating
  fear as incapacity. As Mothlight she produces moving luminous silhouettes
  and displaced voice echoes. Numerous decoys cost glucose and concentration.
  During the showcase incident, she asks for one breath and then creates the
  decoy field that exposes the Rollcaller.
- **The Rollcaller:** A single living, sheet-thin local anomaly with ink-dark
  filaments. It imitates voices, extends its own living tissue through
  connected equipment, and seeks the attention surge produced when a crowd
  recognizes a concealed identity. It learns identities only through
  observation, inference, elicited reactions, and ordinary evidence; names and
  records do not reveal truth to it. Its later disposition remains unresolved.

## New locations

- **Northbridge Secondary:** A secondary school in an unnamed city during the
  modern-like low-magic era. Relevant local features include ordinary network
  and livestream infrastructure, paper backup attendance, an auditorium and
  rehearsal corridor, a local electrical breaker, an acoustic fire partition,
  and a service exit outside camera sightlines. These details make no claim
  about other schools or cities.

## New factions or cultural facts

- **New formal factions:** None.
- Gideon and the three girls maintain an informal, mutually anonymous
  four-person support arrangement; it is not part of a wider magical authority
  or organization.
- Their local practice treats operational consent as procedural: Night Clerk
  may state risks, recommend retreat, and offer support, but does not order a
  transformation or control a participant’s body. After the showcase incident,
  they add a darkness protocol requiring him to state the facts once and then
  disconnect when the team requests an operational blackout.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Local transformation system:** Drita, Bryn, and Cassia transform
  voluntarily through personal nonliving focus tokens. Each girl supplies the
  living magical agency; a token focuses power but neither acts by itself nor
  compels transformation.
- **Local power set:** Kiteglass creates angled barriers and redirects force;
  Pulsewire senses and severs active magical connections at close range;
  Mothlight creates luminous moving silhouettes and displaced voice echoes.
  These are capabilities of this trio, not general rules for magic.
- **Costs and recovery:** Their power use consumes calories, body heat, and
  concentration and may worsen existing injuries or produce sensory overload.
  Food, water, warmth, injury checks, rest, and each girl’s own report are the
  group’s practical recovery measures.
- **Refraction veil:** While transformed, the girls can sustain a
  living-user-powered blur over face and voice in live sight and ordinary
  recordings. The veil does not erase memories, rewrite records, conceal a
  witnessed transformation, or prevent deliberate correlation of civilian
  and magical identities.
- **Handler communications:** The encrypted relay and voice scrambler are
  ordinary technology. Health and location telemetry is opt-in and may be
  withheld or disconnected. The Rollcaller can compromise the relay only by
  physically bridging connected equipment with its living tissue.
- **Compromise check:** Night Clerk never orders a transformation. The trio
  therefore treats any such order as proof that the communications channel is
  compromised.
- **Rollcaller limits:** The Rollcaller can copy voices and operate equipment
  it has physically threaded, but the equipment itself is not alive or
  magical. It cannot counterfeit the team’s shared consent history, acquire
  truth from a spoken or written name, or make a record inherently reliable.
- **Containment:** Once Pulsewire severs the Rollcaller’s external filaments
  and Kiteglass encloses its body, the trio can maintain short-term containment
  in an unplugged metal equipment case. This does not establish permanent
  containment or a larger collection authority.

## Timeline events

- **Six months before the story:** Three anonymous magical girls request
  practical help over a local emergency-radio frequency. Gideon answers and
  becomes Night Clerk under a mutually anonymous support agreement.
- **Before the story:** Gideon infers the girls’ civilian identities from
  repeated, ordinary observations but does not disclose that knowledge.
- **Night before the showcase:** The trio fights the Rollcaller. It survives,
  retains blurred street-camera evidence, and follows them to Northbridge
  Secondary.
- **Northbridge showcase incident:** The Rollcaller observes and probes the
  trio, steals a page from the ordinary attendance ledger, begins a premature
  guardian livestream, and publicly juxtaposes three civilian names with
  blurred transformed images. The girls reject its copied transformation
  order. At their request, Gideon cuts local power, stopping the stream and
  cameras before they transform. The trio contains the Rollcaller while Gideon
  evacuates the class through the service corridor.
- **After the incident:** No clear transformation or identity link reaches the
  public; ordinary witnesses understand the event as a corrupted presentation
  and electrical failure. The girls choose food, injury checks, and two
  mission-free nights, establish the darkness protocol, and leave the
  Rollcaller’s long-term disposition for a later decision. Gideon replaces
  verbal attendance with silent self-check-in.
