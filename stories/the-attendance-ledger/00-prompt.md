# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> **[WP] A school teacher moonlights as a handler for magical girls, looking after their wellbeing in battle and the classroom, even protecting the girls' secret identities without alerting them.**

## Story controls

- Working title: The Attendance Ledger
- Target length: 3,200–3,800 words, within the repository's 2,500–4,000-word
  default
- POV: Close third person centered on the teacher/handler
- Tense: Past
- Tone and genre: Tender, sly, and suspenseful magical-girl urban fantasy;
  school-story care work with moments of dry humor and genuine danger
- Audience/content rating: Teen / PG-13; non-graphic magical combat, injury,
  anxiety, exhaustion, and threatened exposure
- Required elements: The protagonist works openly as a school teacher and
  covertly as a magical-girl handler; the same girls are under the teacher's
  care in class and in battle; care includes physical, emotional, academic, and
  operational wellbeing; the teacher actively protects their secret identities;
  the girls remain unaware that the teacher has identified them; the girls
  retain agency in battle and in decisions affecting their lives; a crisis
  makes the classroom and battlefield responsibilities collide; the ending
  resolves the immediate danger while preserving the secret
- Prohibited elements: Sexualization of minors; graphic violence; teacher-
  student romance; treating the girls as weapons or incompetent children;
  resolving the plot through an unearned secret-identity reveal; contradicting
  authoritative universe canon; canon promotion without explicit user approval

## Assumptions

- "Handler" means a support, logistics, safeguarding, and communications role,
  not ownership or coercive command.
- The teacher already knows the girls' identities when the story opens, while
  the girls believe their civilian and magical lives remain compartmentalized.
- The story may invent a local school, local threat, and small-scale magical
  support practices where canon is silent, provided all reusable inventions are
  recorded in `06-canon-delta.md`.
- No external legacy story is being adapted.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise is dramatized in both a classroom sequence and a battle
  sequence, with consequences moving between them.
- The teacher protects at least one girl's wellbeing in a way that helps her as
  both student and magical combatant without exposing the teacher's knowledge.
- A concrete identity threat is defeated or contained without the girls
  learning that their teacher is their handler.
- The ending resolves the central dramatic question and leaves the girls with
  meaningful agency rather than merely being rescued by the adult.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
