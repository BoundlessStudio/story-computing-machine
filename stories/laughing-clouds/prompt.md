# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] Once a year, a solarpunk city celebrates inventions that failed at their intended purpose but became useful in unexpected ways. A painfully serious young engineer enters a compact water condenser, only to discover that it produces tiny personal clouds which rain whenever someone laughs nearby. Determined to correct the defect before judging, she reluctantly accepts help from a rival who thinks the clouds are already perfect.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
