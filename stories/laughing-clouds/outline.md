# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: At the annual festival for inventions that failed into usefulness, young engineer Ianthe enters a compact water condenser. During setup it instead raises tiny clouds above nearby laughers and rains on them. Before judging, she reluctantly accepts help from Rasim, the rival who calls them perfect. Fulfill the promise by proving one bounded use, correcting hazards without erasing the defect, and entering it honestly in the festival’s intended category.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Specifications protect Ianthe from being dismissed as young; a funny, low-yield machine feels like evidence against her competence. She wants potable-water performance restored and every signal understood, but needs Rasim’s user observations. His help must materially change her work without replacing her judgment.
- Counterforce or complication: Rasim has seen the clouds cool people and revive plants, but mistakes delight for consent and usefulness. Raw mode wastes water, wets electronics and paving, excludes quiet occupants, and fails in wind. The judging clock, heat, finite reservoir, and dwindling battery constrain safe tests. Neither rival is an antagonist.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Ianthe, intimate with quantities, fault trees, surfaces, and machine signals. She cannot read Rasim’s motives or visitor needs from logs. Keep his observations fallible and reveal utility through material results, not endorsement.
- Governing movement and time shape: One festival afternoon tightens through bench diagnosis, live mapping, a constrained repair-court trial, and classification at judging. Each test turns embarrassment into data but exposes a limit. End on their next task, not applause, romance, or a lesson about failure.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Solar-chilled mesh condenses humidity into a finite clean reservoir. A firmware error makes ultrasonic ranging treat laughter’s broadband pulses as dense moisture, aim chilled microjets above each source, and coalesce their aerosol into brief rain. Up to four armspan clouds form within four metres while water and charge last; wind scatters them. Colored cloud-edge light and three descending tones signal target, supply, and cutoff states. Device, clouds, display, and logs are nonliving.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move through Ianthe’s rapid selection of temperatures, wet footprints, spectral bars, and leaf response; let human details intrude until she includes them. Keep wonder sensuous but mechanically legible.
- Conversational texture: Use clipped bench coordination, interrupted observations, a child’s single-purpose questions, and a judge concerned with classification and safety. Gestures toward signals and wet objects should carry unstated meaning.
- Rhetorical ownership: Ianthe owns thresholds, failure modes, and causal distinctions. Rasim owns observed use, bodies in space, and premature generalization. The judge owns scope; visitors report immediate inconvenience or pleasure.
- Pressure behavior: Ianthe becomes over-specific and stops answering the social question; embarrassment shortens her listening. Rasim stays fluent until a near-slip makes him pause, count exclusions, and become concrete. Their best coordination is sparse.
- Anti-default: Avoid quip-banter between dour genius and whimsical truth-teller, joke sequences used as rain switches, speeches about useful failure, or a final exchange naming a change their work already shows.

## Beats

1. During potable-yield checks amid repaired shade, reclaimed parts, and planter channels, laughter flashes an impossible acoustic profile. A cloud forms, rains, and ruins Ianthe’s calibration sheet. She starts a reset before judging.
2. Rasim points to cooler skin, settled dust, and revived seedlings. Ianthe shows him low yield and exposure risk. A cloud rains on an unwarned stranger: delight is not consent. She accepts Rasim’s offer to map users while she traces signals.
3. They correlate audio input, digital logs, colored cloud edges, water mass, and battery tones. Rasim tracks movement and landing surfaces; Ianthe changes one variable per cycle. They identify the fault and learn that restoring potable flow would consume their remaining time and charge.
4. A child laughs after fixing a stubborn wheel. Rain cools the shaded repair court and fills a dry planting wick, but an adult recoils toward slick tile. Ianthe cuts power before a fall; Rasim’s certainty finally breaks.
5. They define a safe trial: absorbent beds, a visibly marked rain field, one-cloud cap, and wind and charge cutoffs. The laughter trigger remains. This can cool playful courtyards and irrigate nearby plants, but cannot schedule watering, produce potable water, or cool everyone equitably.
6. Ianthe changes the entry classification and records the limits. During judging, signals, wet catch media, temperature readings, and varied reactions supply evidence; Rasim supports the narrower claim. The judge asks practical questions without a crowd conversion or invention speech.
7. Accepted in the proper category, the rivals spend the remaining charge measuring quieter opt-in triggers for a future version. They relocate the unit away from foot traffic and follow its descending battery tone into cleanup and another experiment.
