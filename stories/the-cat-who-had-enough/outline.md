# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Ten months after inheriting Mizzle, an advice-speaking cat, an unnamed adult narrator has turned complaining into a substitute for choosing. On the morning their older sister needs a final answer about a bakery partnership the narrator proposed and then delayed, the narrator complains of being cornered. Mizzle answers with the promised sustained, furious lecture: she calls the narrator a waste of talent, favors, money, and counsel, reconstructs emergencies their avoidance created, and identifies them as the common source. Keep the attack forceful but fallible. Resolve the deadline through argument and an in-person choice that costs trust and money while preventing further harm.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The narrator wants another mind to choose so failure will belong to the advice, while preserving a self-image of independence. They care about their sister and once wanted the bakery, but cannot admit that desire has changed. Mizzle wants to stop serving as a judgment machine and be treated as a dependent companion. The narrator must separate her accurate causal record from her cruel verdict on their worth.
- Counterforce or complication: At four o'clock, part of the sister's holding payment becomes unrecoverable and her alternatives narrow. The narrator disputes one detail until a whole pattern disappears, then turns the dispute into another complaint that obliges Mizzle to answer. Mizzle knows only what she witnessed or was told; she cannot know the sister's finances or choose the narrator's future. The sister needs a usable answer and will not let late honesty erase costs already incurred.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close first-person past through the narrator's self-editing. Early narration blurs agency through circumstances and passive constructions; keys, forms, amounts, and chronology make choices harder to disguise. Mizzle remains exterior except through speech and behavior. No voice supplies an objective verdict on the narrator's worth.
- Governing movement and time shape: One workday narrows diffuse complaint to one answer: deadline message and lecture in the apartment; attempted rebuttal through rooms marked by unfinished tasks; a walk with unsigned papers and shop keys; confrontation in the empty storefront; brief return home. Movement comes from lost plausible deniability and one bounded choice, not a self-improvement montage.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: One sincere complaint directly addressed to Mizzle permits one spoken reply of any length she chooses. Questions, praise, and invented grievances do not; she cannot volunteer speech between complaints. The faculty grants language, not mind-reading, prophecy, compulsion, or perfect judgment. Mizzle remains a living cat with ordinary needs and limited firsthand knowledge. The lecture is one extended reply, not a new power.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move from comic causal fog toward exact verbs, times, keys, forms, and amounts; keep cat observation and domestic inconvenience sharper than self-description.
- Conversational texture: Use interruptions, failed restarts, practical questions, and silence around one asymmetrically long lecture. The sibling exchange is shorter and deadline-bound.
- Rhetorical ownership: Mizzle reasons by accumulated sequences and sensory evidence; the narrator uses exceptions, softened labels, and hypothetical futures; the sister owns dates, costs, and commitments without diagnosing anyone's character.
- Pressure behavior: Mizzle becomes cumulatively fluent; the narrator interrupts and manufactures complaint-triggers before losing fluency; the sister grows sparer and waits through evasion.
- Anti-default: Do not make the cast equally aphoristic or Mizzle an omniscient self-help oracle. Preserve her overreach, valid objections, logistics, and an ending without a polished lesson.

## Beats

1. Establish the routine through a small complaint and Mizzle's short advice. The sister's message states the deadline and money at risk. The narrator recasts repeated delay as sudden pressure and complains to Mizzle.
2. Give Mizzle the long lecture in a continuous scene. From firsthand receipts she reconstructs several causal chains, names advice-collecting as avoidance, calls the narrator a waste, and identifies them as the recurring source. Anger makes her unfair at the edges without weakening the attack.
3. The narrator tries to discredit the whole through complaints about individual errors. The trigger mechanic sustains a tense exchange until the narrator exposes the withheld fact that the bakery is no longer wanted. Mizzle distinguishes inference from observation, refuses to choose for them, and withdraws.
4. The narrator nearly signs merely to prove change, recognizes another outsourced choice, and carries the unsigned papers and keys to the storefront rather than sending a vague message.
5. The narrator begins with circumstances; the sister answers with promises, expenses, and missed chances. The narrator declines and admits how long they knew. The sister switches to a smaller plan, takes the keys, and leaves repayment and trust unresolved.
6. Before four, the narrator files the withdrawal and accepts their share of the loss. At home they seek neither verdict nor absolution, meet Mizzle's ordinary needs, and begin one delayed job for a bounded period. End with effort underway and both relationships unsettled.

## Continuity

- Canon used: An otherwise ordinary modern-like period in the single shared world; genuine magic may be locally rare, and Mizzle supplies living agency rather than functioning as an object.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Leave Mizzle's origin, lifespan, and trigger unexplained. Establish no class of talking cats, transferable inheritance magic, universal truth in advice, hidden organization, or canon link. Her lecture is fallible: avoidance causes the immediate losses, but hardship remains real and personhood is not reduced to being a problem.
