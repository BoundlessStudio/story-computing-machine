# Content rating

- **Rating:** Teen
- **Content notes:** Human extinction and non-graphic mass death; a child in mortal danger; war and a burning library; endangered hospital patients; discriminatory denial of medical care; bereavement, prolonged family separation, and guilt. The cover depicts a frightened child reaching home, without injury detail.
- **Reason:** Sustained apocalyptic dread and explicit knowledge of irreversible deaths may be unsuitable for younger readers. Violence and medical danger remain non-graphic; the finished prose and cover contain no sexual content or graphic injury.
