[WP] Millions of years after humanity’s extinction, two researchers reconstruct the fragmented diaries of Sarah Elizondo, a mysterious young girl who wandered through human history without aging, slipping between centuries and accompanied by a strange consciousness she called her 'ghost'. Her writings reveal fifteen events leading to humanity’s destruction—and a horrifying paradox: every attempt she made to prevent the apocalypse helped bring it about. Now, after countless millennia of searching, a signal matching Sarah’s has finally appeared. The researchers have one last chance to bring her home, because the girl responsible for humanity’s extinction is their daughter.
