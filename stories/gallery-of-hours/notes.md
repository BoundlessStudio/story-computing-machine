## Outline

The keeper found a genuine surrender of control in that counteroffer. My November 19 comparison rests on who needs the sitting and who can afford it. Dolly’s work makes a clear face photographable against a dark wall while the lead keeps it alive. Her wages and stairs have already made sleep at work the affordable choice. She is entitled to sell and to ask for her own exposure. Iria can buy four years now and more whenever she likes. Only a living work could make Iria’s counteroffer, and only an audience with money could have brought it about. The private portraits deepen the class bargain through Dolly’s appetite for a good picture. The revised closing moment holds both that appetite and the cost in her hands.

## Line edit

The editor heard Dolly’s voice most clearly in a thumb mark, a dish towel and her hand already reaching for Loane’s money. It asked for a particular action wherever Dolly summarized a want. “He put a price on the set” became Loane’s “I could pay four hundred for a set. Two hundred to each of you,” and “Iria wanted more than anything to take back the phone” became “Did I blink?” as her hairpin falls. Dolly recognizes her hand in a dozen unnamed photographs. The writer declined to name a manufacturing firm there. A half-past-three pipe, a thread of light still running after the shutter, and the pin ticking on the floor carry the appointment through in real time.

## Rewrite requested November 19, 2026

The user rejected the first version and requested Mature intensity and a technician external to the system. The former outline and final Yes decisions evaluated the earlier direction only. In the keeper’s new request-only reading, “Calling that same seller a contractor would leave the central choice intact.” The technician’s work can give them access to the exhibited people and a difficult decision, while someone else’s body and years are at stake. The keeper emphasized the gap between an ordinary collector’s view and what a technician experiences at a connection. The kind of contract, source of intensity and ending remain open.

Claude separately understood the correction as keeping the technician’s own “body and years off the ledger.” It saw the warm glass people in everyday clothes, the sole dark onlooker and the privately focused faces as an invitation to witness what pretty things cost. It inferred that a service provider from outside the institution could satisfy the economic, institutional and physical readings together. Who bought or sold, among those depicted, remains an authorial choice. It read Mature as room for the human consequences of ownership to be felt on the page.
