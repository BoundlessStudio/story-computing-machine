# Promised experience

An exclusive room full of beauty that depends on a bargain somebody poor has made. The request puts a struggling technician within arm’s reach of extracted, conscious people in transparent bodies, each on an overhead supply, offered as living art to wealthy collectors. “Purchase immortality” and “sell years of their lives to survive” put the two ends of the bargain in different hands. I read wonder, unease and a wish to know what the technician sees from there in the image’s warm people and cool, expensive room. The particular relationship, mechanism, scale beyond the gallery and outcome are open.

## Takes considered

- A dispersal auction. The technician must unhang a group of immortals who have lived within sight of one another for sixty years. The scene to keep would be the last pair watching the size of the room double between them.
- A slow exposure. Once a year a sitter moves her finger a fraction across her glass phone. A succession of technicians grows old during the taking of a single photograph. It makes time palpable, though each worker gets little of the page.
- A double portrait. A preserved photographer wants the technician to sell her some years in front of a collector. In the sitting both women have a claim on the camera. This lets the same act be a sale of actual life, a day’s skilled work and the making of a picture somebody might want.

## Provisional direction

Dolly Soper, the gallery technician, and Iria Senne have a private set of photographs of Dolly after her sales of years. Dolly asked for the first one. Her old work badge was getting her stopped at her own door. She has since made occasions of the pictures, sometimes coming to Iria in a good dress. Iria paid for her extraction decades ago. The woman looking down at a phone is famous as a piece, and rather less famous as a photographer. She shows a collector their pictures. He offers the two women a sitting fee to watch one last portrait made during a transfer, with sufficient years changing hands to see the difference. The seller physically ages by the term sold. For somebody who can go on purchasing such terms, life in glass can keep going indefinitely. The overhead lead powers the body and carries new years into it.

Dolly has lost a callout fee to the stairs at her lodgings. Last night she slept downstairs at work. The four years and her fee would get her the deposit on a ground-floor room. She wants that money and likes some of those pictures much more than she’ll say. She will sell Iria the four years if she can take a picture of Iria receiving them. The collector who wanted the sight of one woman growing old must decide if he will pay for both pictures. He pays. Iria usually decides what she gives a roomful of strangers to see. This time Dolly chooses the instant and keeps the exposure. The picture she takes is of the pleasure with which Iria receives the years. Dolly discovers she wanted it for reasons besides making Iria answer for taking them.

## Craft plan

Try Dolly’s first person, a professional with a good opinion of her eye and a difficult time admitting what things cost her, against close third as amused by her vanity as invested in her getting the room. Three principals. Dolly talks in practical proposals and stories of things that happened on this floor. She knows every reach of cable and thinks she has seen all Iria’s expressions. Iria confides, directs and cajoles in the same expansive breath. She has mistaken Dolly’s pleasure in their pictures for consent to a public sitting. Bevis Loane collects people’s work, looks carefully and speaks in terms of what a person can see from where he stands. He has assumed the hardest thing to obtain will be a visible change of age. The long encounter owes all three a plain negotiation about what he came to see, who gets to look and which woman is paying in flesh.

The original supplies the group as well as Iria. The glass clothes, a seated figure intent on a phone, an upturned curly head above backpack straps and an open raised palm give strangers distinct occupations even when still. The bun, loose fall of trousers, phone held close and absorbed downward gaze are Iria’s. She can spend a whole conversation composing a shot, talking out loud as she moves things in her view. The man with the backpack follows the moving lead with his chin. Later he decides for himself where to look. At the turn Iria must part with the thing she has arranged to look at for decades. Neither the image nor the prompt names anyone.

Choose the Sill Street Sample Room over the curtained Cresset Club or Fallow Station’s private exhibition hall. In this former plate-glass showroom the buyers and the people on low black plinths can see the full length of the same floor. November rain, late afternoon into dark. Staff pay out cable, set the viewing angles, buff the reflected light off shoe marks. Wet wool at the door, a warm collar of light in a clear shoulder, the little drag of the ceiling reel, the heater ticking, plane trees beyond a blackening end window. On this floor Dolly can bring a sitter off her base without breaking her supply. She knows the band of dark concrete that will give the woman’s transparent face its edges.

## Scenes and ending

1. **Before the appointment, a quarter.** Dolly deals with the evidence of her night spent on the floor and pays out a stiff lead while Iria shows her a photograph she has put forward for purchase. Dolly’s ordinary familiarity with all these extraordinary bodies includes the unflattering angles she spares them. Iria wants her to dress up for a sitting. Dolly wants to see the offer and learns that Loane expects to witness a real sale. Summarize their years of pictures here. Keep the illicit pride Dolly takes in a good one.
2. **The private showing, the long scene, about two fifths.** Loane comes up the row, has eyes for a piece Dolly usually walks past and can see art in a worker’s old badge portrait. He wants to buy the final image and witness an exposure. Dolly makes him and Iria say who will receive the life being sold. Iria tries to direct her friend as usual. Dolly brings her down to the black wall on a length of warm flex and asks for the phone. Iria offers to pose for her there and then, while she can still practice the face she wants. Stay through Dolly’s refusal of the substitute, her reasons for wanting that picture and the difficulty of taking her friend’s assent at its word. They agree to make both pictures. The sale is still ahead of them.
3. **The sitting, the final third.** Four years pass between living bodies through the overhead lead. Give the transfer its own felt duration, including the physical work of holding and handing over the phone in the middle of it. While the buyer watches Dolly grow older, one of the works on the far plinths turns to watch the woman receiving the years. Dolly sees a very young, unguardedly pleased face. Let her take her photograph, then let both women want the first look while her body is still coming through the change. Leave the move to the new room and the exhibition’s later reputation offstage.
