# Prompt

[WP] Four young witches arrive in a market town by the sea with little more than scarlet gowns, marigolds in their hair, and a handful of half-formed gifts—Nort can speak to crows and roots, Mysie can summon fire and make rivers glow, Elspie commands wind and rain, and you can read the sky and awaken what sleeps beneath the earth. What begins as a joyful midnight dance of shared learning soon becomes something far more dangerous when the four of you discover your magic does not merely grow when practiced—it multiplies when shared, awakening ancient forces hidden under the town. As more girls are drawn into your circle and the old words begin to ring across the land, the town must decide whether you are the start of a golden age of wonder or the first spark of a power no one will be able to control.
