## Provisional direction

Make clear whose choices control the conflict and climax, what supplies the main tension, and what the ending rewards. Readers will compare those beats with their independent understanding of the request before drafting; the promised-experience paragraph is a claim for them to test. Keep the direction short and open to discovery: the user's requirements remain authoritative, and the prose may find a better route. This is a thinking aid, not a scene quota or a checklist.
