# Promised experience

Briefly state the distinctive pleasure, tone, scale, central relationship, and anticipated payoff of the complete request, including later user directions. Distinguish explicit requirements from interpretations; leave genuine uncertainty visible.

## Provisional direction

Identify concrete scenes that could deliver those promises through action, choices, turns, and an ending. Keep the direction short and open to discovery: the user's requirements remain authoritative, and the prose may find a better route. This is a thinking aid, not a scene quota or a checklist.
