# Promised experience

Describe the experience the complete request asks for, including its tone, scale, and any specified relationship or payoff. Use the user's words for requirements. Leave the writer's choices open.

## What the scene trials showed

Name the approaches tried and the moment in the prose that made the stronger one worth following. Record any failure that changed the character, situation, or plan.

## Provisional story

Name the teller and the place. Give the scenes in a few lines each, following what people want, what they do, and what that makes possible next. Keep the decisive scene in view. If it turns on dialogue, identify what each person needs from the other and what each can know.

## Ending

Describe the intended last turn or image and what the earlier scenes give it to work with. Revise the plan when the draft discovers a better ending.
