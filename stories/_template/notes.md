# Editorial notes

Keep the decisions a future writer needs: both outline readers' short independent understandings of the request before seeing the outline or editorial framing, their accounts of what the proposed beats would actually deliver, useful reader effects and their causes, what changed, what rereading showed, and the coordinator's reasons for resolving objections or preserving dissent. Before drafting, preserve the coordinator's concise comparison of the request, the outline's actual dramatic center, concrete scene and ending evidence for fulfillment, and the resolution of material mismatches. Distinguish explicit requirements from interpretations, and preserve reasons when an interpretation changes. Use concise, dated, plain-language entries; this file is not a checklist, scorecard, separate process report, or transcript.
