# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Ivrine's neck-pouch spider stops eating flies, grows fat, and scarcely moves. Someone has been feeding it witchcraft until its protection fails. Discover the animal's condition and the household danger through its behavior; carry that knowledge into an actual escape.
- Focal pressure or attachment: After their mother's funeral, Ivrine intends to leave the family inn with her half of the portable savings. She has enjoyed being looked after by her older brother Pelset again. Her familiar spider, raised from a juvenile, belongs to that recovered feeling of safety.
- Counterforce or complication: Pelset kept the failing inn and their mother alive while Ivrine lived elsewhere. He wants her money to preserve the place and regards her departure as abandonment. His labor is real; she resists acknowledging its extent. He has secretly used small spells to exhaust her protection before compelling her to surrender the money.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Ivrine's hands, neck, habits, and fallible family readings. Neither the spider's intentions nor Pelset's private reasoning is accessible. Observation, bounded expertise, and conduct establish the threat.
- Governing movement and time shape: One day and the following departure morning, contracting from an errand into the inn's kitchen and upstairs room, then opening onto the road. Suspicion accumulates before a short confrontation at the loading door.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Living spiders consume witchcraft directed at their wearers when their ventilated pouches touch bare skin. They are the culture's best defense, including against harmless workings, but sustained magical feeding leaves them inactive for hours. Insect meals do not produce this saturation. Local human workings require directed breath and close proximity; warmth is easy, while imposing one simple bodily action requires sustained effort. They cannot erase memory or command someone remotely. Hungry spiders arrest such workings before completion. Pelset exploits prolonged household access. Pouches have no independent power.
- Dialogue promise: Familiar sibling hospitality becomes unreliable as ordinary questions test what the other knows. Preserve irritation, family shorthand, and misplaced gratitude.
- Dialogic medium: Conversation during animal handling, kitchen talk interrupted by service work, overheard breathing, and a final exchange beside departing luggage.
- Dialogue engine: The poucher's questions change the meaning of Ivrine's symptoms. Pelset's requests for money and proximity acquire coercive stakes; Ivrine's withheld discoveries let her test her suspicion and leave.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Notice friction, weight, compressed legs, stale fly wings, and household sounds before interpreting them. Remembered comfort complicates sensory alarm.
- Conversational texture: A poucher's concrete questions and impatience; siblings who omit shared history, interrupt over tasks, and mistake customary access for agreement.
- Rhetorical ownership: Ivrine uses small jokes to evade indebtedness. Pelset owns household accounts and damaging family comparisons; his solicitude can be sincere and instrumental. The poucher corrects husbandry without diagnosing relationships. The spider supplies no human commentary.
- Pressure behavior: Ivrine initially overexplains harmless choices, then becomes selectively uninformative. Pelset repeats requests with decreasing courtesy when familiar cues fail. Avoid an eloquent villain's argument.
- Relationship movement: Ivrine wants welcome without surrendering her future; Pelset wants her money and dependence but frames both as duty. Discovery turns gratitude into concealment. His failed command ends his presumption of intimate access.
- Anti-default: Avoid a benevolent expert translating the spider into a moral lesson followed by siblings agreeing on fair support. Preserve the animal's indifferent appetite and the consequences of broken trust.

## Beats

1. **An inconvenient pet.** During preparations for the already hired cart, establish pouch life: skin chafing, ventilation, fly feeding, and replacement expense. Pelset handles breakfast and luggage solicitously. Ivrine mistakes her spider's swollen stillness for illness; a briefly warm patch beneath the pouch has seemed another symptom.
2. **Examination.** A poucher finds feeding signs. With Ivrine's agreement, a mild warming attempt reaches her through the inactive spider; a hungry shop spider stops the repeated working. The poucher can identify magical overfeeding, not its author or purpose. Ivrine obtains a loan spider while keeping her familiar one to recover separately.
3. **The expensive welcome.** Pelset presses his claim on the savings amid ordinary work. His account discloses care Ivrine has minimized. She almost explains the examination, then notices his disproportionate interest in whether her spider is eating. This suggests danger without proving it.
4. **Watchfulness.** Ivrine conceals the hungry replacement against her skin and leaves the familiar outer pouch visible. Feigning sleep, she witnesses Pelset directing a small working toward her. The alert spider intercepts it. His fatigue and repeated private visits now fit deliberate saturation. She prepares to depart without seeking a confession.
5. **The loading door.** When Ivrine carries out the money, Pelset tries to make her hand release it. Use the established bodily onset, sustained breath, and spider response to make the failed compulsion legible. He physically intercepts her; the carter's arrival to lift her trunk gives her an ordinary opening to leave. Pelset disguises the encounter as a family quarrel, preserving his public standing.
6. **Distance.** Pelset seeks her destination under a family pretext. She withholds accurate information. Finish during departure: she has the savings and both living spiders, but familiarity no longer feels safe. Let her altered response to the animal's movement carry consequence without a concluding explanation or guaranteed reconciliation.
