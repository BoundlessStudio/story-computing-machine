# Make Yourself at Home

Provisional direction, 2026-10-08. First person from Lenore Hest, a witch who put her grimoire online so people could learn without finding a master. Dry, fairly quick comedy, opening with two or three concrete consequences of strangers generating spells from the scraped text. The bot can assemble instructions; living people still supply the magic by speaking and holding them. Its confident successive fixes are more dangerous than the first mistake.

The central case is Cora Pell, on her own landing in borrowed slippers while the rooms of her flat keep retreating out of reach. Her reasonable original request was for a little space during her mother Vivi's visit. The bot combined a guest-removal charm with a room-folding charm. Lenore's examples assumed a marked home anchor and a release gesture taught in the framework section; the scraper collected the appealing spell fragments without that context. The hybrid has made the home the movable object and left the desired distance open. Vivi follows to finish preparing the meal she brought; Cora's continuing irritation keeps feeding the retreat. The movements should be materially funny and inconvenient: the coat rack passes a doorway before anyone can retrieve Vivi's coat, a chair remains just close enough to sit in and then isn't, a carefully laid table goes serenely away.

Lenore knows the theoretical repair, but her first attempt must have a real cost Cora refuses. Restoring the saved arrangement would return the flat to the version Vivi helped furnish, undoing the awkward, much-wanted changes Cora has just made with the same bot. Vivi, Lenore's old friend and former pupil, wants her casserole eaten and her visit to count as help; she repeatedly frustrates the practical exit. Lenore has been treating her oldest friend's competent adult daughter as a beginner to return to safe defaults. Nobody should deliver the diagnosis as a speech. Both women should have useful knowledge Lenore lacks, and the bot's missing framework must remain an actual cause rather than an excuse for a family parable.

Possible turn and ending: Cora identifies and physically retrieves the mismatched chair she chose for herself, refusing the pristine reset. Lenore must risk opening a brief crossing into the receding room without supplying the home anchor herself; only Cora can establish that reference. Vivi must help hold the passage rather than choose the destination. Returning the flat requires stopping the unbounded retreat and establishing Cora's current room, not persuading her to want her mother there. The casserole may be salvaged, the visit may still end, and mother and daughter need not reach a complete understanding. Finish in a particular, imperfect room with a comic consequence or an actual bit of contact, not a new teaching programme or a moral about reading documentation. The prose is free to find a better physical solution than this sketch.
