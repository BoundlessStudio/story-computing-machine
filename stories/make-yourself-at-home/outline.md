# Make Yourself at Home

Provisional direction, revised after outline discussion, 2026-10-08. First person from Lenore Hest, a witch who put her grimoire online so people could learn without finding a master. Dry, fairly quick comedy, opening with two or three concrete consequences of strangers generating spells from the scraped text. The bot can assemble instructions; living people still supply the magic. Its confident successive fixes are more dangerous than the first mistake.

The central case is Cora Hobden, on her own landing in borrowed slippers while the rooms of her flat keep retreating out of reach. Her mother Vivi is stuck in the receding kitchen. Cora's reasonable original request was for a little space during the visit. Reconstruct three actual bot generations: useful help rearranging the flat; a repeat to keep Mum from putting the furniture back; then a patch to stop the operation moving people. The bot wraps a furniture-moving operation in a room-fold, preserving its unbounded repeat. Lenore's example fragments assumed the framework's fixed object, reference, and stopping mark; the scraper lost that setup. The home is now the movable object and no distance is enough. Cora's mood is not magical fuel. Vivi's attempt to put a kitchen chair back under the supper table triggers further movement. The movements should be materially funny and inconvenient: the coat rack passes a doorway before anyone can retrieve Vivi's coat, a carefully laid table goes serenely away, someone cannot quite step from one ordinary room to the next.

Lenore knows the conventional reset, but its cost is substantial to Cora. Vivi installed a move-in marker while helping furnish the flat. Resetting to that arrangement would close the little print studio Cora has folded out of a linen cupboard using the bot, ejecting the secondhand press and her supplies onto the landing. The press otherwise dominates the living room. Reset preserves the objects but takes back the usable home Cora has made. Vivi, Lenore's old friend and former pupil, wants her casserole eaten and her visit to count as help. Lenore has been treating her oldest friend's competent adult daughter as a beginner to return to safe defaults. Nobody should deliver that diagnosis as a speech. Vivi knows where the old marker is hidden, in the outer wall under the coat hooks; Cora knows the current layout and that her deliberately mismatched chair was expressly excluded from the bot's whole project: leave this chair exactly here. Its front foot stays level with a chipped threshold tile within reach of the exterior landing while its rug and the rest of the room slide away. These are particular kinds of useful knowledge Lenore lacks.

Possible turn and ending: Cora identifies the chair as a stable physical reference, refusing the reset. Lenore can hold the outer threshold open or enter, not both; Cora goes in because she knows her own altered room and can locate the chair. The folding doorway is becoming less passable with successive repetitions. A first interrupted-fold test should establish the modest danger of ending with a doorway opening onto plaster. Vivi must leave the meal and use her technical competence to steady the inside edge. The old peg supplies the reference for isolating the latest room-fold from the existing studio folds; a line at the stable chair supplies a stopping mark. Lenore can bring the room back toward the peg until the table reaches the line indicating Cora's present layout, then release the latest charm without resetting everything. No magical rule says only the rightful owner can anchor a home; the three people solve a physical problem with distinct knowledge, imperfect coordination, and continuing disagreement. Returning the flat requires bounding the unending operation around the current arrangement, not persuading Cora to want her mother there. The casserole may be salvaged, the visit may still end, and mother and daughter need not reach a complete understanding. Finish in a particular, imperfect room with a comic consequence or an actual bit of contact, not a new teaching programme or a moral about reading documentation. The prose is free to find a better physical solution than this sketch.
