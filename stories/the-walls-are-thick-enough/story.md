# The Walls Are Thick Enough

"I will keep the observance until the first kitchen bell," she said.

The younger attendant glanced toward the garden gate. "Majesty, the garden cools quickly."

"Bring cushions to the prayer chamber. Both of you. If anyone asks, I am finishing the seventh bridal meditation and forbade you to interrupt me."
