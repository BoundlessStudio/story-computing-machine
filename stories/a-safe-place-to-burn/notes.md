# Editorial notes

## 2026-10-19

Codex promise keeper, independent request reading, 2026-10-19:
The distinctive pleasure I anticipate is magical-girl power pushed far beyond its ordinary limits, with the spectacle made personal by a farewell to someone beloved.

Explicitly, ordinary use is around 10–20%; the speaker is at 195% and still rising. She intends to get the addressee and their crew to safety, then find somewhere safe to burn up. Her declaration of love belongs to that decision.

I infer urgent, frightening grandeur joined to intimate tenderness. The excess power should materially affect the rescue and make her planned departure credible. “The crew” suggests a shared undertaking and relationships beyond the central pair. The beloved might be a romantic partner, but the request does not specify that.

The anticipated payoff is the collision between extraordinary capability and a terrible personal cost: what she can accomplish now, and what leaving means to those she saves. Death appears expected by the speaker; the prompt does not explicitly require that she ultimately dies. A survival or reversal would need to preserve the farewell’s emotional and causal weight.
