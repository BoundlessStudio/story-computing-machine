# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **The Astral Valkyrie.** As celestial debris annihilates an island civilization, its former war singer competes to become its surviving witness. Deliver the descent, meaningful selection, irreversible civilizational death, and memory reaching another age.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Nacorra wants future people to hate Theskar. Its rulers executed her dissident lover; before that, her glorious songs helped make its conquests beloved. She craves an audience and misses the culture whose destruction she once wished for.
- Counterforce or complication: Vasketh, an elderly historian, knows vastly more, including the crimes Nacorra's performances concealed. The Valkyrie hungers for experiences unavailable to a visitor arriving only at endings; spectacular memories attract her dangerously.
- POV, distance, and information limit: First-person past, Nacorra writing in the next age. Establish her involvement early; uncertainty concerns its emotional extent. No remote casualty omniscience or selector interiority. Reader address recruits judgment, then exposes her efforts to manage it.
- Governing movement and time shape: One final night, descending from ceremonial heights into the doomed city; a forward passage across centuries and brief later testimony. Offered memories interrupt present action. Target prose: 2,500–4,000 words.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: The living Valkyrie can sustain one other embodied soul across centuries on this same world. She cannot shelter another, divert impacts, or retrieve the dead. Her touch makes offered memories perceptible to everyone touching her; breaking contact ends reception. She cannot obtain unoffered recollections. Halo and wings focus her living agency.
- Dialogue promise: Rival witnesses offer incompatible inheritances to an inhuman listener; the chosen witness subsequently contests the reader's appetite for a beautiful catastrophe.
- Dialogic medium: Spoken appeals, sensory memories offered by touch, interrupted song, bodily withdrawal, and the narrator's address to an unanswered future reader.
- Dialogue engine: Offerings risk exposure. Vasketh's corroboration prevents Nacorra from making private grief her entire history; her disclosures make the reader reconsider earlier invitations.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Sensuous, embittered recollection with a performer's attention to appetite and timing. Let long remembered festivities collide with blunt bodily facts. Mark conjecture about the Valkyrie.
- Conversational texture: Ceremonial petition deteriorates into partial memories, practical speech, accusation, and unfinished singing. Ordinary requests for shade or a drink coexist with cosmic stakes.
- Rhetorical ownership: Nacorra owns seduction, obscene humor, self-serving omission, and affection expressed through remembered particularities. Vasketh dates and identifies, but speaks clumsily about his own fear. The Valkyrie attends intensely to sensations and sometimes misses their social meaning; beauty interests her without settling her judgment.
- Pressure behavior: Nacorra grows fluent while manipulating and loses that fluency when caught enjoying the past. Vasketh's recall remains strong while breath fails. The Valkyrie's questions become fewer; sustained attention replaces reassurance.
- Relationship movement: Nacorra seeks the Valkyrie's election and the reader's condemnation, but cannot comfortably expose how much she enjoyed belonging. Vasketh wants to survive and distrusts her. The Valkyrie wants a witness capable of bringing her beyond the moment of extinction; selection commits her to an account that may accuse its audience.
- Anti-default: Avoid a fair-minded tribunal whose winner teaches everyone how remembrance should work. Keep rivalry, compromised pleasure, and the rejected candidate's legitimate claim alive.

## Beats

1. **Arrival above the burning sea.** Weeks of impacts have destroyed Theskar's outlying settlements and ships; its survivors occupy the final island refuge. Nacorra reaches the roof prepared to offer an indictment. Descending between burning fragments, the Valkyrie has Image #1's long indigo hair, luminous violet eyes, crystalline halo, gold wings, and dark clothing. Her radiance shadows injured bodies. Establish the final approaching impact and her carrying limit before petitions.
2. **A substantial rival.** Vasketh offers languages, crop knowledge, children's games, and massacres through memories of life and study. His knowledge is extensive, imperfect, and candid. Nacorra offers the execution she witnessed, soliciting the reader's anger. Vasketh recognizes her and identifies the victorious procession for which she composed the marching song. Both receive each other's offerings through the Valkyrie's touch.
3. **The descent through what remains.** The roof starts breaking; the three descend through a feast hall used as a shelter. An injured veteran recognizes Nacorra and tries to sing her march while someone struggles to keep him breathing. She reflexively supplies its rhythm. The Valkyrie touches the remembered feast Nacorra offers: peppered fruit, dancers, a lover's hand, the immense pleasure of being applauded. Keep the culture materially alive beyond its crimes. The selector's absorption tempts Nacorra to substitute this seductive inheritance for the indictment.
4. **An offering she cannot control afterward.** Nacorra interrupts contact before the feast's captured enemies enter. Vasketh recognizes the context and challenges the omission. She voluntarily reopens that memory: humiliation of prisoners, her profitable song, her exhilaration. Beauty and culpability coexist. She names her participation without retracting her grievance. The Valkyrie selects her for continuing past the omission that protected her own story, while Vasketh remains alive and willing. This is the selector's consequential judgment, not an objective ranking of souls.
5. **The unchosen die.** Vasketh disputes the selection, then returns to people calling from the shelter; no blessing or reconciliation is required. The Valkyrie lifts Nacorra as the impact destroys the last refuge. Allow bodily horror at human distance before the rising view makes it terrifyingly beautiful. Nacorra tries to keep individual lives in view and cannot. There is no surviving enclave or secret second passenger; the world beyond Theskar continues.
6. **The next age receives her.** Confirm Nacorra's bodily arrival alone, centuries later on the changed coast, still before magic's terminal extinction. The Valkyrie never appears to this living civilization. Compress years spent learning another people's language. Her written account becomes the transmission the reader has already received. Return to one pleasure from the feast whose full context prevents innocent nostalgia. Resolve preservation without declaring forgiveness, redemption, or control over future readers.
