For eternity, the gods have worshipped it, fought over it, and constructed entire religions explaining its purpose, despite secretly knowing absolutely nothing about it. Then a young goddess, mocked for having no domain, no worshippers, and no meaningful power, announces that she intends to activate it simply because no one has ever dared to try. The pantheon threatens to destroy her, not because they know what will happen, but because the greatest terror of an all-powerful god is discovering something beyond their understanding.
