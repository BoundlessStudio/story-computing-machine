# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: After thirteen years of moving through other
  people for no more than a day at a time, Eamon Huxley enters Nadira Whitlock and knows
  from an old broken toe, a buried tooth chip, and the body's deep proportions
  that the search is over. Nadira's own memory begins on the morning Eamon lost the
  body, and she has spent the intervening years aging it, caring for it, and
  building a life in it. Fulfill the prompt by confirming that this is Eamon's
  original physical body, letting Eamon discover whether returning defeats the
  one-day limit, and resolving who controls it after that answer: when the
  expected ejection does not occur, Eamon can reclaim the body indefinitely but
  voluntarily releases it to Nadira.
- Intended reader experience: Begin with visceral relief and the uncanny
  pleasure of bodily recognition, then let certainty become intimate moral
  pressure without turning the story into a courtroom argument. The day should
  feel tactile, awkward, occasionally dryly funny, and increasingly difficult
  as Eamon's remembered anatomy carries Nadira's calluses, strain, tastes, habits,
  and obligations. End with loss and genuine resolution together: Eamon no
  longer has a body or a search, Nadira is not erased, and neither person can
  pretend the encounter restored the life that was interrupted.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led disputed workday. Each ordinary use of the recovered body
    separates three things Eamon has treated as one: its original anatomy,
    Nadira's thirteen years of maintenance and learned skill, and the right to
    decide what it does now. The twenty-four-hour boundary turns that growing
    distinction into a choice with permanent force.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: Eamon has survived by treating the
    original body as the one possession that could end all borrowing, yet the
    person most able to understand that longing is Nadira, whose entire remembered
    life depends on not being expelled from the same body. Each can truthfully
    call the loss of it catastrophic without either truth cancelling the other.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: Eamon immediately wants to
    stay for the full day, prove the body's identity, and learn whether it will
    keep them after the usual limit; Eamon also wants to stop taking unwilling
    days from people and cannot accept making Nadira the final victim. Their useful
    flaw is evidentiary hunger: thirteen years of searching by scars, gait, and
    bone has trained them to read bodies as clues and to avoid learning more
    about temporary hosts than survival requires. Nadira immediately wants motor
    control returned, while also wanting the first credible account of why her
    life began without a past; she refuses to let panic cost a promised repair
    or make her bargain away tomorrow before the limit is tested.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: The counterforce is an incompatible pair of legitimate continuities
    under a possession mechanism that gives Eamon all voluntary movement for the
    day. Nadira remains conscious, remembers the whole interval, and can speak to
    Eamon within their shared attention, but cannot move the body or surrender
    her learned motor skill as transferable memory. Eamon receives current
    sensation without access to Nadira's memories, habits, or private thought.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Nadira is a countervoice, not an antagonist.
    She has thirteen years of relationships, work, bodily care, and promises to
    protect; Eamon's proof explains her missing past but grants her no safe place
    to go. She insists on completing one modest bookbinding repair through
    Eamon's hands because a frightened stranger's claim cannot cancel her day.
    After the limit fails, she is prepared to offer Eamon one more day rather
    than disappear immediately, a choice made under real pressure rather than
    free consent; she retains agency by naming that pressure and later setting
    terms for any future contact.
  - Pressure or change created: Eamon can force the body through remembered
    motions but cannot do Nadira's present work well, while obeying Nadira's precise
    instructions makes Nadira's life more legible and Eamon's intended permanent
    claim harder to sustain. The deadline supplies no external rescue: if the
    body accepts Eamon past twenty-four hours, only Eamon can decide to leave.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close first-person present through
    Eamon, intimate with the shock of recovered proprioception and initially
    overconfident in what bodily familiarity proves. Eamon controls movement and
    narration but cannot inspect Nadira's memory or silence her voluntary inner
    speech. Nadira's objectives emerge through exact work instructions, refusals,
    corrections, and the bodily maintenance Eamon would otherwise overlook.
  - Time shape and compression: One continuous twenty-four-hour possession,
    from an early-morning entry in the building hallway to the same minute the
    next morning. Render entry and recognition, Nadira's demand for release, the
    apartment tests, the binding repair, the first direct account of the lost
    morning, and the final hour in scene. Compress the afternoon's smaller
    negotiations and rest into a short accumulation of changed bodily facts;
    let the last ten minutes dilate around the expected ejection.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Open inside the suspected body and confirm
    physical identity quickly through private anatomical knowledge rather than
    delaying it as a twist. Establish the local rules through Nadira and Eamon
    testing what each can feel, remember, say, and control. Reveal that Nadira's
    first memory and Eamon's loss share the same date, but provide no causal
    explanation for Nadira's arrival or Eamon's displacement. Withhold only the
    original body's exception to the day limit, which neither person can know
    until the boundary passes. Do not let a record, expert, recovered memory,
    soul taxonomy, or hidden culprit decide personhood or rightful control.
  - Governing movement: Enter; recognize; claim; hear refusal; compare old body
    facts with new wear; learn Nadira's beginning; agree to wait out one day;
    attempt Nadira's work from verbal instruction; fail delicately; slow down;
    complete the repair without acquiring her skill; inventory what thirteen
    years changed; admit what other hosts paid for the search; prepare for
    ejection; pass the limit; feel the available permanence; decline Nadira's
    pressured bargain; release voluntary control.
- Speculative surplus: While Eamon controls a body, sweet and sour reverse only
  in Eamon's taste: jam bites like acid and pickling brine tastes syrupy, while
  the conscious host tastes the same mouthful normally. Eamon therefore eats
  bland food and does not season for hosts; during this day Nadira has to stop them
  from ruining paste and lunch by trusting the wrong taste. The inversion does
  not identify bodies, measure consent, expose emotion, predict ejection, or
  help resolve the claim.
- Structural distinction: The recovered body is both setting and disputed
  instrument: the plot moves through uses of one pair of hands rather than a
  chase, repeated possession catalogue, public investigation, rescue, or
  widening emergency. The decisive venue is Nadira's quiet workroom after its
  morning task is already complete; the climax is the absence of an expected
  event and one person's private relinquishment. There is no crowd conversion,
  institutional rewrite, technical proof, culprit reveal, or polished symbol
  declaring whose identity was more real.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: At the exact minute the day should
  end, Eamon braces for the familiar loss of sight and leverage. Nothing happens.
  The original body will hold them, and Nadira's sudden silence makes clear that
  Eamon now has what thirteen years taught them to call survival. Nadira then offers
  tomorrow so she can settle her affairs, although both understand tomorrow
  could become another coerced concession. Eamon recognizes that waiting for a
  less painful permission would merely make Nadira perform consent while unable to
  move. Eamon leaves before she must bargain again, surrendering sensation and
  control without pretending the body was never theirs.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: Nadira regains a body made stiff by a day of
  someone else's use and keeps the life she built, but now knows it began at the
  site of another person's dispossession. Eamon remains a bodiless living person
  able to possess again and has not solved the need for contact, movement, or a
  stable embodiment; the ethical injuries of earlier unwilling hosts are not
  erased. Nadira states that future entry, if any, will occur only after her spoken
  invitation and for a duration she chooses. Eamon can accept the boundary, but
  neither knows what relationship—or alternative body—can follow.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Eamon taking Nadira's movement before knowing
  her name and treating the body's old chipped tooth as a complete answer. End
  just after Eamon gives movement back: sight and taste vanish from the narration
  while Nadira flexes cramped fingers, drinks water, and speaks practical terms
  aloud so Eamon can still hear them faintly. The opening equates recognition
  with possession; the ending resolves the search by separating recognition
  from continued control.

## Beats

1. Eamon enters Nadira in the hallway at early morning and releases the prior host
   off-page. The stance is older and the shoulder unfamiliar, but the chipped
   tooth, healed toe, and reach of the arms produce immediate physical
   recognition: this is the body lost thirteen years ago.
2. Nadira addresses Eamon from inside the shared attention and demands release.
   Eamon says their name and claim; Nadira gives hers, establishes that she remains
   awake and remembering, and forces Eamon to admit that they intend to test the
   full day before leaving.
3. In Nadira's apartment, private body facts confirm Eamon's history without
   invalidating Nadira's present: an old scar and asymmetric ankle are where Eamon
   expects, while new calluses, corrective lenses, a tendon brace, and a food
   sensitivity belong to Nadira's thirteen years of care and work. Their split
   experience of sweet and sour establishes one minor possession effect and
   spoils the first attempt at breakfast.
4. Nadira recounts only what she knows: she woke in this body on the same morning
   Eamon was cast out, carrying no usable earlier memory, and chose a name and a
   life rather than waiting to become an explanation. Eamon has no memory of
   Nadira, the displacement, or a pursuer. Neither can turn matched dates into a
   mechanism.
5. Nadira insists on keeping a promise to finish a modest book repair in her
   downstairs workroom. Eamon wants to hide and examine the body, but follows
   her instructions rather than risk exposing her. Familiar hands prove clumsy
   at Nadira's craft; Eamon nearly cuts a prepared sheet incorrectly and learns that
   possessing muscles does not transfer thirteen years of judgment.
6. The repair proceeds through exact cooperation: Nadira supplies sequence and
   tolerances, Eamon slows their movements, and both manage the body's current
   wrist pain. They complete sound, ordinary work without a magical joining of
   memories or a sentimental fusion of skill.
7. Over the quieter afternoon, Eamon moves through Nadira's ordinary maintenance—a
   brace change, food, a short rest, cleaning paste from the bench—and sees how
   much of the recovered body is now a record of choices they did not make.
   Nadira asks about prior hosts; Eamon admits that some refused and were kept until
   the day ended anyway, and that refusing to learn their lives made departure
   easier.
8. In the final hour, they return to the workroom and prepare for the expected
   ejection without inventing safety they do not have. Eamon wants Nadira to promise
   access if the body rejects them; Nadira will promise only that they can speak
   after she has control. Each states what losing the body now would cost.
9. The exact twenty-four-hour boundary passes with no physical change. Eamon
   tests only by waiting and making one small deliberate movement; the body
   remains under their control. Nadira offers a further day to settle her affairs,
   and Eamon understands both the offered mercy and the coercion built into it.
10. Eamon voluntarily withdraws. Nadira retakes movement, checks the strained hand
    and drinks water before addressing Eamon's bodiless presence aloud. She sets
    a clear condition for any future visit: no entry without an invitation, and
    immediate departure when she asks. Eamon agrees from the narrowed,
    sense-poor state outside the body; the search has ended even though the
    problem of how Eamon will live has not.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Only life can use magic; systems and costs are local rather than
  universal; consent has no single metaphysical behavior but its ethical
  consequences matter; transformed, displaced, copied, or continuing people
  require mechanism-specific treatment; personhood cannot be reduced to an
  error or erased by an explanation; an underlying afterlife exists without
  deciding whether it is involved here.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Eamon, Nadira, their body, the one-day possession ability,
  shared-attention speech, taste inversion, the original-body exception, and
  the thirteen-year displacement are new and story-local. Establish no link to
  Nina and Anin, ghosts, reincarnation, the Mother-fragment, Tovan's branches,
  another transformation or memory system, or any artifact or institution.
  Nadira's origin, the cause of Eamon's ejection, why the body accepted Nadira, and
  whether Eamon can gain an unshared stable embodiment remain unknown. Treat the
  failed ejection as one observed result for this body, not a universal law of
  souls, bodies, ownership, consent, or continuation. The undated modern-like
  setting and thirteen-year span do not establish placement relative to any
  shared event.
