# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Dragons reach Stillfire Temple at life's end, where immortal keeper Alexstrasza receives final flames and carries their sensory memories. When young dragons crowd the sanctuary but refuse the rite and the name of their pursuer, she must discover why their living fires register as last ones and prevent mass death.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Alexstrasza guards consent, yet centuries of funerary ritual make her sort every need as a final one. Ceryth wants shelter without questioning the youngest into danger.
- Counterforce or complication: A moon-iron fracture beneath northern ice turns polar wind into vibration and auroral heat-shadow. It entrains these young dragons' narrow first-fire pitch, draws flame from their mouths, and tunes itself through sustained speech. Their concentration lets it follow south and resonate with Stillfire's last-fire patterns.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Alexstrasza. Final-flame memories surface as embodied temperature, color, vibration, scent, balance, or muscle impulse when present sensations touch them. They contain no thoughts or testimony; the youths choose what to reveal through speech, marks, movement, and flame-signals.
- Governing movement and time shape: Three winter days accumulate bodies, chores, and evidence. A true death establishes the rite; arrivals turn ceremony into refuge; a chosen demonstration identifies the threat; one auroral night contracts into coordinated work. Morning returns to labor and individual choices.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: A dragon yields a final flame only voluntarily at death. Alexstrasza carries nonverbal residue without consuming identity, restoring life, or gaining knowledge on demand. Stillfire's ice channels render heat variation as temporary color and tone. Irregular embodied patterns interrupt the resonance; names, oaths, and refusals are not magical.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move through rime thickness, ash weight, hot stone, and crowded air; let involuntary memory-impressions interrupt without becoming flashback summaries. Alexstrasza notices procedures before motives.
- Conversational texture: Mix ceremonial questions, clipped refuge logistics, ordinary complaints about food or sleeping space, and deliberate gaps. Wing, claw, heat, and scent signals supplement rather than translate every exchange.
- Rhetorical ownership: Alexstrasza reasons through sequence, capacity, and consent; Ceryth through headcounts and boundaries; Ulune through sensory comparison; Parrox through practical objections and sideways humor; Ygdrane through immediate bodily needs.
- Pressure behavior: Alexstrasza drops ritual completeness for operational choices. Ceryth repeats prohibitions, then delegates signals. Ulune gains precision as others lose fluency. Parrox's humor gives way to blunt task reports. The unnamed youths remain an uneven chorus.
- Anti-default: Avoid solemn lore speeches, uniformly poetic dragons, and polished reconciliation. Let understanding arise from misread chores, partial signals, and coordinated labor.

## Beats

1. Alexstrasza clears a flue, scrapes the basin, warms salt paste, and receives Ygdrane, an old dragon who chooses the rite. His final flame leaves upward snow, metallic scent, five breastbone pressures, and horizonless cold light. She records the anomaly without understanding it.
2. Ceryth arrives with young dragons, rejects the basin, and requests shelter; Alexstrasza honors both choices. More follow. Funeral alcoves become sleeping bays as ash, meltwater, waste trenches, fire-safe spacing, and hatchling food displace ceremony. The youths wake anyone breathing in five pulses but block questions requiring the pursuit's cadence.
3. Their conduct disproves a wish to die: none offers a final flame, and all protect one another. Ulune tracks vibrations in cups and floor ice; Parrox finds the flues changing pitch. Sensory fragments from several earlier elders answer present cues differently—a southward muscle-turn, moon-iron taste, heat casting a shadow—but no single memory explains the cause.
4. Ulune chooses a bounded demonstration: route marks in expendable frost, uneven wing taps, tail contact, throat-light, and resin scent showing what speech would smooth dangerously. Alexstrasza asks questions answerable without a name. Their evidence and old fragments identify the moon-iron resonance and why young fires, not seasoned ones, are vulnerable.
5. The auroral band reaches Stillfire. Crowding synchronizes the youths; living flame lifts toward the basin, whose channels respond as though a mass final rite has begun. Ceryth divides the refugees among chambers, Parrox manages vents and fuel, and Ulune maintains a changing intergroup signal while other youths improvise distinct responses. Alexstrasza must keep the roof flues open without commanding their breath.
6. Alexstrasza renders entrusted memory-patterns through the channels as changing heat, color, tone, and scent—not words or orders. The youths choose irregular breath and movement until the resonance loses its match and disperses upward. Morning finds them repairing channels, counting supplies, and sharing the counter-pattern through marks. Some remain; others choose to warn rookeries or investigate the fracture. Alexstrasza completes Ygdrane's funerary work after tending the living.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Memory mechanisms remain local and incomplete; personhood, consent, and refusal retain moral force. Existing dragon traits and systems elsewhere do not define Stillfire's dragons.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Alexstrasza's supplied name imports no external franchise history, powers, or relationships. Stillfire, final flames, first-fire resonance, and moon-iron are story-local and non-canon. The keeper cannot read minds, compel the rite, restore the dead, or extract exposition. The fracture's deeper origin and relation to Ersene, Qarsis, crown-era dragons, or other settings remain unresolved.
