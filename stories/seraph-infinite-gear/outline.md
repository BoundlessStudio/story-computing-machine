# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: The Seraph of the Infinite Gear descends as a civilization prepares to repeat its ancestral catastrophe. At the final gear she discovers another operator, then discovers his interventions include her living machinery. Deliver an actual civilizational rewind and an encounter whose apparent first meeting has a contested history.
- Focal pressure or attachment: She loves this civilization's audacity: its astronomers make artificial dawns, its singers address the sky without petitioning it. She also wants someone to attend to her rather than her arrival. A stranger's familiarity attracts her before its source becomes frightening.
- Counterforce or complication: Sovekh, an adult ceremonial singer, remembers seven three-day sequences. In one, she welcomed his company and offered to carry him beyond his homeland. Subsequent encounters differed. He has been rewinding her recollection and altering his approach to recover that welcome. His attachment and his coercion are both real.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through the Seraph. Admit bodily perception at immense scale; reveal Sovekh's history through speech and physical evidence. His claims about her former feelings remain testimony.
- Governing movement and time shape: A descent narrows into one prolonged confrontation inside the celestial mechanism, then opens into three days visibly running backward below. Earlier encounters enter through disputed recollections, never full flashbacks. Target prose: 2,500–4,000 words.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Her living body supplies magic; gold gears grow, heal, and transmit it. The final gear restores one geographically enclosed civilization to a fixed dawn three days earlier. The upper mechanism and its occupants remain exempt. A wheel in her wing can rewind her recollection, partly or fully, within that interval without reversing her body. Sovekh entered the upper mechanism before the anchor; neither operator has a duplicate below. Unable to power the great rewind, he erases her discoveries so she chooses it repeatedly. Outside lands and cosmic time remain unchanged.
- Dialogue promise: A magnificent visitation becomes an unequal reunion where practiced tenderness, authentic recollection, and coercion are difficult to separate.
- Dialogic medium: Speech across moving rings, then within a folded wing. Ordinary words become audible only when she quiets the machinery; receiving her attention matters materially.
- Dialogue engine: Sovekh anticipates questions and stages familiar moments. She changes their order and tests the mechanism, making remembered conversation cease to predict this encounter. Their dispute determines access to her body.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Majestic breadth with selective intimacy: a city's changing light, then one hand inside a feather. Sustained sentences follow distributed attention; shorten when attention becomes bodily and local.
- Conversational texture: Sovekh sustains an audience, offers irrelevant recollections, and becomes embarrassingly overfamiliar. She grants long hearings but interrupts attempted choreography. Include literal questions about touch and position.
- Rhetorical ownership: His singing lends timing, remembered cadence, and teasing; he mistakes successful performance for being understood. She owns ceremonial authority and spatial comparison, but cannot author his grief. Neither supplies the other's conclusions.
- Pressure behavior: He begins fluent, then repeats details that once mattered when their effect fails. Her public composure persists; she listens narrowly and becomes exact about what she actually experienced.
- Relationship movement: She wants freely given attention and fears requesting it under her immense authority. He wants renewed intimacy and cannot bear to ask without assurance. Evidence shifts him from privileged interpreter to someone she can hear without obeying.
- Anti-default: Avoid a weary deity and cheeky mortal trading corrective quips, followed by reassurance. Unequal experience permits attraction, sustained accusation, and unresolved claims without making every turn a verdict.

## Beats

1. Open with an artificial dawn rising beneath her descent. The civilization's rulers are repeating the stellar ignition that destroyed an ancestral capital. Approaching the final gear, she hears a returning phrase from inside a ring where no worshipper should stand. The great gear bears another hand's fresh turning marks.
2. Sovekh emerges with unearned ease. He could only rock the great gear through its slack; the marks prove access, not independent power. He knows where she will fold her wing and what catches her attention. Let his company become pleasurable before he anticipates something she has not decided to tell him.
3. She halts the mechanism to hear him. Under questioning, he admits repeated encounters and presents marks on the exempt framework. She finds evidence of handling on her recollection wheel; he admits turning it while she rested. Under her supervision, a tiny backward turn removes her immediately preceding question. His accurate repetition and her changed hand position corroborate the effect. She stops contact and takes control.
4. Give the central dispute room. Sovekh recounts the first welcome, an awkward shared interval, and a later rejection; his particulars exceed useful seduction. She challenges his selection and discovers omitted encounters in which she confronted this interference. Familiarity changes from possible proof of trust into evidence of rehearsal. Preserve genuine affectionate memories without treating earlier willingness as present consent.
5. With ignition approaching below, he offers the conversation that once won her promise. She pursues the account he keeps evading: what he did after her first refusal. His admission changes her decision about him. She breaks the wheel's exposed drive and seals it beneath healing gold, preserving memory while preventing another unauthorized turn. He remains alive, articulate, and capable of refusing her interpretation.
6. She turns the final gear herself. Show completed material reversal: launched fire contracts into its instrument, a dismantled festival rises, three nights cross the enclosed land. Outside constellations continue forward. She retains this encounter; Sovekh retains his incompatible intimacies. End after she returns him to restored ground without accepting his old promise as hers. Ignition is undone, its political cause remains, and her next intervention can use knowledge he previously removed. Leave the final exchange and image to the prose.
