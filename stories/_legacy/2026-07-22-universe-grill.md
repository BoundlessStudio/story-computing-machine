## Starting point supplied by the user

The working seed is a vast, deep-time science-fantasy universe in which civilizations rise, collapse, and become archaeology for later cultures. Magic, gods, and technology are all real, but no culture completely understands their boundaries. Machines become temples, rulers become gods, catastrophes become creation stories, and new states build themselves on mistaken versions of the past.

The central struggle is **memory versus myth**: who controls history, what price was hidden beneath survival, and whether people can break the cycle or merely become its next forgotten gods. The recurring human scale is outcasts, rebels, rulers, priests, keepers, and ordinary witnesses finding truths their societies need buried. The guiding sentence is:

> Every civilization eventually becomes archaeology, and every story reveals another layer of the universe that history tried to forget.

The portions of this seed supported by answered global rulings were promoted in
the one-time canon seed above. This paragraph remains provenance rather than an
authoritative substitute for `universe/premise.md`.

## What the corpus already agrees about

These are recurring signals, not canon facts:

- **A person can change without becoming a replacement.** Elaine remains Elaine after transformation; Anin becomes independently real through new experience; Elena is neither Daniel preserved unchanged nor Solara resurrected; Tovan and Orrin outgrow their designed purposes.
- **Memory is active, dangerous material.** It can be traded, copied, inherited, erased, distributed, preserved in witnesses, retained across rewritten time, and hidden inside deliberately inaccurate stories.
- **Names and records are acts of power.** Ravel controls complete identities. Duskkeeping anchors freely claimed names. Deepmarket prices relationships and memory. September erases matched records. A café story makes correct naming a human act of recognition without proving supernatural force.
- **Miracles leave bills.** Solar salvation destroys outer cities. A heartseed roots in the rescuer. Rewinding time preserves trauma. A divine inheritance destabilizes identity. Crossing a Gate leaves scars and a divided life.
- **The impossible becomes routine.** Families build locks and laundry systems; portals acquire licensing tables; gods audit contraband; temples preserve maintenance procedures; cafés and supermarkets remain important beside cosmic events.
- **Institutions mistake procedure for understanding.** Priests repeat degraded commands, Runners trust dead forum posts, adults weaponize Ravel's identity rules, and the Continuance Council converts an emergency into permanent authority.
- **Truth does not automatically produce justice.** Records can be hidden, purified, mistranslated, erased, or turned into a new total myth. Several stories insist that the record remain contestable.
- **Ordinary witness matters.** A notebook, cup, tally belt, scar, family meal, repaired gate, memorial name, or greenhouse can hold more moral truth than a monument.

# Archived decision queue

## Gate decisions

These questions control the meaning of most later answers.

### Q001 — Which source stories are selected as core works?

- **Question:** Are all nine stories selected for future shared-universe adaptation, or is this a candidate pool from which some works may remain standalone, apocryphal, or inspiration-only?
- **Answer shapes:** all nine; a named subset; tiers such as `core / compatible / standalone`; or thematic anthology with no literal continuity.
- **Safe default:** Treat every story as a candidate and promote none.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — *Daughter of the Sun* (`S01`) and *The Small Moon Rose First* (`S07`) are selected core adaptation sources, not yet canon. `S02`–`S06`, `S08`, and `S09` are inspiration and understanding only.

### Q002 — What does “shared universe” mean here?

- **Question:** How do the three core settings—Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world—share continuity: one physical universe across worlds/eras, one cosmos containing planes, a multiverse connected by Gates, or another arrangement?
- **Evidence:** `S07` explicitly connects Earth and Ravel through the Glass Gate. `S01` supplies a separate deep-time world but does not locate it relative to either one.
- **Safe default:** One cosmos containing distinct worlds or planes; their crossings and chronology are not yet unified.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world are the same physical world at different eras. Other dimensions and timelines may exist, but they are not required to connect these three settings.

### Q003 — Is Nell and Bash's Earth our Earth?

- **Question:** Is the Philadelphia/Massachusetts Earth in `S07` ordinary real-world Earth with one hidden crossing, an alternate Earth, or a later/earlier Earth inside the deep-time cycle?
- **Pressure:** This decides whether real geography and history remain binding and whether the Glass-Sea world can be Earth's remote past or future.
- **Safe default:** Contemporary real-world Earth, with the Glass Gate hidden from general knowledge.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — It is another era in the same world's deep-time cycle that resembles contemporary Earth, not necessarily our literal Earth. This modern setting is only a tiny fraction of the world's and universe's history.

### Q004 — Where is the Glass-Sea world relative to Earth and Ravel?

- **Question:** Is *Daughter of the Sun* set on future Earth, ancient Earth, another planet, another plane, an artificial world, or a location whose relationship to Earth should remain unknown?
- **Why now:** This decides whether its four-century history belongs on an Earth timeline and whether its ancient network can underlie the modern artifacts.
- **Safe default:** Another world in the same cosmos, relationship unrevealed.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — The Glass-Sea world is the same physical world long after Nell and Bash's modern-like era. Its chronological position relative to Ravel remains unresolved.

### Q005 — What kind of place is Ravel?

- **Question:** Is Ravel a physical planet, another plane, an artificial habitat, an alternate Earth, Earth's future/past, or a place its own inhabitants also misunderstand?
- **Evidence:** It has two moons, old civilizations, dead languages, programmable identity infrastructure, and a Gate that connects different Earth dates to one Ravel present.
- **Safe default:** A distinct world reached through a Gate; its physical or planar category remains unknown.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — By the Q002 ruling, Ravel is the same physical world in another era, not a separate planet, plane, artificial habitat, or alternate Earth. Its exact position in the chronology and how well its inhabitants understand their world's nature remain unresolved.

### Q006 — How visible is the supernatural?

- **Question:** On Earth, is Nell and Bash's disappearance treated as an isolated mystery, or do hidden institutions and prior returnees know that Gates and other worlds exist?
- **Evidence:** `S07` includes hospitals, police, therapy, and private investigators, but no established public Gate authority.
- **Safe default:** The public does not know; fragmentary private evidence and earlier lost travelers may exist.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Nell and Bash's modern-like era is a low-magic trough: magic does not meaningfully exist in ordinary life, and surviving accounts or phenomena are generally treated as myths and legends. Great ages of technology and magic have already risen and fallen; further cycles will pass before the gods rise again. Relic anomalies such as the Gate may survive without making the supernatural publicly understood. Whether any hidden institution possesses reliable knowledge remains unresolved.

### Q007 — How tightly should stories connect on the page?

- **Question:** Should links be invisible to characters, visible as recurring names/artifacts, discoverable by scholars, or eventually explicit through crossovers and a shared historical reveal?
- **Answer shapes:** thematic only; Easter-egg continuity; slow archaeological convergence; eventual ensemble crossover.
- **Safe default:** Slow archaeological convergence with no forced character crossover.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Use variable awareness. Some stories or characters may break beyond their local worldview and explicitly recognize or state that a larger setting exists; others should have no idea that they inhabit a much greater history. Connections need not be equally visible in every story.

### Q008 — What authority do the old story files have?

- **Question:** Should each source be imported substantially as historical canon, adapted selectively, or treated only as a mine for themes and images?
- **Pressure:** Several drafts contain copy errors, internal inconsistencies, or provisional reference sheets.
- **Safe default:** Selective adaptation after a story-by-story canon review.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — The existing source files are nonbinding seeds. *Daughter of the Sun* and *The Small Moon Rose First* belong to the project, but their present prose, outline, and lore do not need to be imported. Individual elements become authoritative only through later adaptation and promotion.

### Q009 — How much should the setting ever explain?

- **Question:** Should readers eventually learn a coherent underlying cosmology, or should every explanation remain culture-bound and partial even when the project bible knows more?
- **Answer shapes:** fully knowable backstage; partly knowable with hard limits; no final master explanation.
- **Safe default:** A consistent backstage reality with permanent unknowns and incomplete in-world models.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — The universe has one fully coherent underlying cosmology that the private project bible may eventually define. New stories may add to and extend that cosmology. In-world cultures and characters may still understand it only partially or incorrectly.

### Q010 — What must later lore never erase?

- **Question:** Should the following be locked as a craft rule: a cosmic explanation may deepen a story's anomaly but may not reduce its personhood, grief, consent, or moral choice to “only a machine error”?
- **Evidence:** This protects Elaine, Anin, Elena, Tovan, Orrin, Mae, and Aurelia from explanations that cancel their stories.
- **Safe default:** Yes.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Yes. A cosmic or technical explanation may deepen an anomaly, but it must never invalidate the affected person's identity, grief, consent, relationships, or moral choices by reducing them to “only a machine error.” This binding production constraint was promoted to `universe/style-guide.md` in the one-time canon seed.

### Q011 — Is every anomaly connected?

- **Question:** Do curses, gods, constructed minds, portals, names, time artifacts, wish events, spirits, and superhero powers share one substrate, several families of causes, or no required common origin?
- **Risk:** One origin creates elegant unity but can make the universe feel smaller and turn every story into a clue hunt.
- **Safe default:** Several interacting systems with occasional common ancestry.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Several distinct systems exist. They can interact, hybridize, and sometimes share ancestry, but no single origin is required to explain every anomaly.

### Q012 — Which work is the audience's doorway into the universe?

- **Question:** Should readers enter through *Daughter of the Sun* and learn the deep-time premise openly, or through *The Small Moon Rose First* and encounter the larger universe through one mysterious Gate?
- **Why now:** The entry point determines what readers experience as normal, miraculous, and hidden.
- **Safe default:** No flagship chosen yet.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Begin with multiple independent entry points and no official flagship. A larger work may later be designated or created as the primary doorway once a substantial body of stories exists.

## Cosmology and deep time

### Q013 — Is magic independently real everywhere?

- **Question:** Does the *Daughter of the Sun* rule apply universe-wide: technology cannot explain every god, spirit, oath, name, or rite?
- **Safe default:** Yes; individual effects may still combine magic with engineering.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — The underlying forces and systems commonly called magic are genuinely real and can exist independently of what a culture calls technology. There are many such systems, and they need not all be active at once or in every era. They can often substitute for, reproduce, or combine with systems described as technology; Q014 governs the labels rather than dividing them into objective kinds.

### Q014 — Are “magic,” “technology,” and “divinity” objective categories?

- **Question:** Does the project bible distinguish them precisely even when characters cannot, or are the boundaries themselves unstable?
- **Safe default:** They are real but overlapping categories; origin alone does not settle personhood or godhood.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — “Magic,” “technology,” and “divinity” are cultural labels imposed on real underlying systems, not fundamental categories in the private cosmology. One culture may see another's technology as magic, while another may use what outsiders call magic and understand it as technology. The project bible should describe underlying mechanisms and relationships without treating those labels as objective boundaries.

### Q015 — What makes a god a god?

- **Question:** Birth, worship, domain authority, immortality, soul, office, power, recognition, or sustained relationship?
- **Evidence:** Aurelia and the Time Goddess are presented as divine; Tovan is constructed but worshipped; Orla receives instructions; minor saints can lose contraband at cards.
- **Safe default:** No single test; cultures disagree, and construction does not disqualify divinity.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Divinity universally requires the continuing conjunction of worship, power, and authority. Losing all belief or worship ends godhood. Authority may be granted or taken and, rarely, assumed as a mantle. Power may be lost, taken, or given, but not by or to just any god; its transfer has constraints still to be defined. A being's origin, including construction, does not by itself grant or disqualify godhood.

### Q016 — Who constrains gods?

- **Question:** What enforces Divine Contract, assignments, Accords, domains, consent, and the limits that prevent powerful beings from simply fixing events?
- **Safe default:** Multiple institutions/laws, not one universal celestial government.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Gods may be constrained by cosmic laws and costs, dependence on worship/domain/authority, contracts and institutions, peer enforcement, and rival powers at different times. No single universal government governs every god, era, or system. Stories may emphasize different constraints, but their additions must remain compatible with the coherent shared cosmology and build on one another rather than conflict.

### Q017 — Is there a real afterlife?

- **Question:** Where do the dead go, why do they leave Earth with other visitors at summer's end, and why can Mae remember erased deaths?
- **Safe default:** Survival after death is real in some form, but cultures and gods possess only partial access.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — A universal underlying afterlife exists, but cultures experience and describe it differently. Ghosts, reincarnation, and digitally mediated continuations are also real possibilities across millions of years of technological and magical development. Their precise relationships to the underlying afterlife remain available for coherent story-by-story definition.

### Q018 — How many ancient civilizations matter?

- **Question:** Did one precursor seed most surviving systems, or have many civilizations independently risen, mixed magic with engineering, collapsed, and misread one another?
- **Safe default:** Many civilizations, with several technologies later copied across cultures.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Countless civilizations have risen, interacted, and collapsed. For most surviving systems, no original source remains recoverable; provenance is layered through copying, theft, trade, reinvention, and misattribution.

### Q019 — How deep is deep time?

- **Question:** Are the oldest recoverable layers thousands, millions, or billions of years old, and do the stories span planetary or stellar change?
- **Evidence:** The user seed says millions of years; the Time Goddess perceives oceans and stars changing; `S09` includes a pre-Bronze drowned kingdom.
- **Safe default:** Millions of years for civilizational layers; older cosmic history exists but is not yet dated.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Deep time has no fixed maximum; stories may reach as far backward or forward as the coherent cosmology permits. Millions of years is the normal working scale for civilizational layers, while greater spans should be used when a story requires them.

### Q020 — Is collapse cyclical, patterned, or merely recurrent?

- **Question:** Does a law or repeating failure drive civilizations toward centralization, myth, and collapse, or is “cycle” a historical pattern people can genuinely break?
- **Safe default:** Recurrent political and epistemic pattern, not predestination.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Each civilization may fall for different reasons. Causes include divine or cosmic events such as godly wars and predicted endings; natural catastrophes such as impacts, eruptions, and ice ages; civilizational failures such as war, overpopulation, lost knowledge, concentrated power, and institutional decay; and individual acts such as triggering a weapon, creating a plague, casting a spell, or awakening a destructive being. Causes may combine. The cycle is a pattern visible across deep time, not a single law that forces collapse.

### Q021 — Is living power plus engineered focus a universal rule?

- **Question:** Does the Crown's grammar explain Solara's armor, the time watch, heartseeds, and other relics, or only the old solar network?
- **Safe default:** Solar-network-specific until another source proves convergence.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Universal rule: **only LIFE can use MAGIC**. Nonliving devices may store, focus, transmit, or shape magic supplied through living agency, but cannot themselves act as magic users unless they qualify as alive. Whether a constructed mind, god, organism, or artifact counts as life is therefore cosmologically consequential.

### Q022 — Can belief create or alter gods and systems?

- **Question:** Does worship generate power, reshape existing beings, change remembered interfaces, or only affect human interpretation?
- **Safe default:** Belief can alter relationships and perhaps manifestations; it does not create every god from nothing.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Belief supplies the worship component required for divinity. If all belief and worship are lost, the being is no longer a god. Belief alone cannot create a god without the independent requirements of power and authority. No general rule that belief directly rewrites a god's nature or inherited systems is established.

### Q023 — Does desire have metaphysical weight?

- **Question:** Is Deepmarket's claim that the Realms “run on want” local market folklore, or a wider law behind wish events, predatory bargains, and grief-powered artifacts?
- **Safe default:** Local rule or partial truth, not a universal spell engine.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Desire has metaphysical weight only within particular systems, beings, bargains, or artifacts that use it. It is not a universal fuel or law behind all magic.

### Q024 — Must power always carry a cost?

- **Question:** Is cost an actual metaphysical conservation law, a pattern caused by imperfect tools and institutions, or a thematic preference rather than world physics?
- **Safe default:** Costs must be concrete, but they need not obey one cosmic accounting system.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — No universal cost is required; some power may be genuinely free. Free or costly power must still fit the coherent shared cosmology and obey the established rules of the particular system that produces it.

## Memory, identity, truth, and time

### Q025 — What is memory made of?

- **Question:** Can one underlying thing be stored in archives, inherited through light, bought at Deepmarket, copied into Anin, distributed across Tovan, retained by the dead, and erased by September?
- **Safe default:** Memory has material/metaphysical aspects, but different systems access different layers.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — “Memory” is a cultural umbrella for several unrelated phenomena. Recurring similarities hint that memory-like patterns can be reproduced across many substrates, but this does not establish one universal memory substance or guarantee that different systems access the same layer of a person.

### Q026 — What establishes personhood after transformation?

- **Question:** Is continuity grounded in memory, freely chosen name, body, relationships, agency, soul, pattern, or some combination?
- **Safe default:** No single component is sufficient; ongoing choice and recognized relationship carry special weight.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Personhood after transformation is a question for individual stories to explore rather than a universal checklist imposed in advance. Different mechanisms may yield different answers. Each treatment must remain compatible with the shared cosmology and with Q010's protection of identity, grief, consent, relationships, and moral agency.

### Q027 — Do names possess universal power?

- **Question:** Are Ravel's three-part identity, Duskkeeping's claimed names, angelic true names, Deepmarket full-name taboos, and café cup names expressions of one law or separate systems that rhyme?
- **Safe default:** Separate systems touching a deeper link between identity and recognition.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Names are usually symbolic and socially powerful rather than universally magical. Literal name-power is a rare exception produced when a particular technological, magical, legal, or cultural system binds identity or consequences to names.

### Q028 — Why does consent matter?

- **Question:** Is consent metaphysically required for some magic, ethically required but bypassable, or an innovation of specific traditions such as Duskkeeping?
- **Safe default:** It strengthens or legitimizes some systems but does not make coercion physically impossible.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Consent operates differently in every mechanism; there is no global metaphysical rule. Individual stories may determine whether a system requires, rewards, bypasses, or ignores consent, while treating the ethical consequences seriously and remaining consistent when that system recurs.

### Q029 — Can false records preserve truth?

- **Question:** Is Kellan's “tell it wrong” loophole unique to September, or a wider reason myth, mistranslation, jokes, and damaged inscriptions survive systems that erase exact history?
- **Safe default:** September-specific at first; deliberate false history is a recurring research hypothesis.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Apparently false or incompatible accounts may preserve genuine information by representing dreams, predictions, alternate-timeline traces, mythologized history, or other story-defined phenomena. No single universal loophole protects all false records, and these explanations should be established rather than used automatically to excuse contradictions.

### Q030 — Can falsehood also become a weapon?

- **Question:** What prevents protective myth from hardening into the next holy lie, and who gets to decide whether a record is safely distorted or politically falsified?
- **Safe default:** Nothing prevents it automatically; provenance and plural witnesses matter.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — Nothing inherently prevents a protective myth or altered record from becoming propaganda, dogma, or oppression. Agents must therefore track provenance, source authority, chronology, and causal consequences. Dreams, predictions, alternate timelines, and similar explanations may not be used as arbitrary patches that introduce nonsense, contradictions, or paradoxes.

### Q031 — What happens to copies and branches?

- **Question:** Are Anin, alternate Cals, Tovan branches, stored memories, and projected Mother-fragments new people, continuations, temporary instances, or case-by-case phenomena?
- **Safe default:** Case by case; new experience can create independent moral claims.
- **Ruling:** `ANSWERED 2026-07-22` — By Q026, copies, branches, stored memories, projections, and related phenomena are evaluated case by case by the story rather than forced into one universal ontological category. Their treatment must remain consistent with the specific mechanism and the Q010 personhood protection.

### Q032 — What are Tovan's local branches?

- **Safe default:** Diverging people with shared ancestry; the story's characters have not settled the law.
- **Ruling:** `DEFERRED 2026-07-22` — The current *Daughter of the Sun* files are nonbinding seeds under Q008. Decide the branches' nature if and when Tovan is adapted into an approved story; Q026 supplies the governing case-by-case approach.

### Q033 — What survives identity inheritance?

- **Question:** In Elena's case, are Mara's memories a soul, a fixed archive, embodied patterns, a conscious passenger, or material from which a new person emerged?
- **Safe default:** A new integrated person; Mara's continuing consciousness remains unknown.
