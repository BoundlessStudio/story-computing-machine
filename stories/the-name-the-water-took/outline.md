# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **The Name the Water Took**. An adult surfaces naked from a hidden jungle pool with practical knowledge but no name, past, or memory of arriving. Establish they were Braska, route-captain of an armed survey, why they entered deliberately, and what the evidence cannot recover.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Braska needs cover, safety, and facts, then weighs reaching for every fragment against preserving post-pool memories needed to judge evidence. Escaped guide Ioshka needs her seized seed wallet before Braska's former crew arrives.
- Counterforce or complication: Braska retains path-reading, knife work, command habits, and capture knots without their uses. These skills enable escape and justify Ioshka's distrust. She saw only Braska's late break with the survey. Two approaching surveyors know Braska's role but call the blankness a tactic.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Braska. Attention moves from temperature, injury, cover, paths, and improvised labels to inferences from hands, objects, reactions, and afterimages. No fragment exceeds Braska's former perception; Ioshka and the surveyors remain fallible.
- Governing movement and time shape: A four-hour circuit from pool to cover, along the damaged approach, and back ahead of the surveyors. Retrieval revises the evidence. Climax privately at the pool rim; end on the unmarked departure path without absolution or restoration.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Complete submersion in this one magical pool strips worn and carried objects plus autobiographical memory, leaving language, general knowledge, and practiced skills. Objects settle as pale lights around the bather's empty reflection. Hooking one out without reimmersion and letting it dry gives the bather one involuntary sensory-and-kinesthetic afterimage of its last deliberate use before entry. It supplies no new viewpoint, interpretation, or moral truth, then becomes ordinary. Full reimmersion also takes post-emergence memory. The pool is local and non-sentient.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Stay tactile and inferential: Braska notices exposure, leverage, spoor, knots, color under water, and jungle reactions before social meaning. Let vocabulary regain personal reference gradually without turning fragments lyrical or oracular.
- Conversational texture: Talk is interrupted by dressing, listening, hooking objects, moving under cover, and checking trail. Questions seek the next usable fact; ordinary warnings and tool requests can remain single-purpose.
- Rhetorical ownership: Braska owns immediate sensation, provisional labels, task questions, and deductions from mismatch. Ioshka owns plant, route, weather, and seed precision plus guarded eyewitness limits. The surveyors own rank assumptions, logistical shorthand, and blame-management.
- Pressure behavior: Braska becomes terser and more exact while still listening. Ioshka loses sequence, corrects herself, and substitutes gesture when speech risks exposing a route. The surveyors grow fluent but less responsive, repeating commands over contrary evidence.
- Anti-default: Avoid a serenely blank amnesiac, mystical-guide exposition, sequential memory playback, therapeutic confession, or polished debate over whether identity is chosen.

## Beats

1. Braska surfaces into heat, insect noise, and no personal referent; clothes and belt glow below. Under a root shelf, Ioshka turns away and offers a palm-fiber wrap. Braska dresses and checks injury before asking about identity. Ioshka supplies the name and pursuit warning, not a biography.
2. Braska instinctively chooses low-sign footing and reads pursuit spacing. A cut wrist cord with their reverse capture knot, survey marks, and map ash support Ioshka's account: the survey forced her to guide it; Braska freed her, destroyed the route, seized her confiscated seed wallet, and ran poolward.
3. At a rain shelter, Ioshka identifies the wallet's planting use. Fresh sign puts surveyors at the pool before dusk. She cannot abandon viable seed. Braska makes a hooked line and proposes circling back, knowing retrieval will not settle trust.
4. At the rim, object-lights shift around Braska's blank reflection. Boots and knife dry into disordered afterimages of marching the survey line, fastening Ioshka's restraint, then cutting it and the route cord. An engraved command disk supplies the ordinary name and rank; Ioshka's recognition supports it.
5. While Braska lengthens the hook, the surveyors arrive and call their former captain's blankness deception. Their accusations supply the missing link: Braska alone memorized the final approach, destroyed its copy, and deliberately submerged before recapture. Evidence converges without certainty about earlier motives.
6. The surveyors try to seize Ioshka and the line. Braska anticipates their formation. Using old knife and route skills, Braska frees the wallet, cuts their marker line, and opens a concealed root descent; Ioshka chooses the crossing order and carries the seed. The pool neither punishes nor endorses.
7. Beyond the rim, Braska closes a descent that permits reimmersion, preserving the new causal knowledge. At an unmarked fork, they hand Ioshka the wallet, take the exposed rear, and remove fresh survey marks as they travel. Name, culpable role, deliberate arrival, and protective act are verified; earlier motive and future relationship remain unresolved.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Memory needs a specific local mechanism; recoverable truth may remain partial. Names normally have social, not magical, force. Memory loss does not cancel personhood, consent, responsibility, or consequence.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Leave pool, survey, community, jungle, and era unconnected to established systems or places. The disk and recognition are ordinary evidence. The pool cannot reveal unseen events, motives, universal identity, or moral truth. Braska is an adult; practical cover and narrowed attention prevent voyeurism. Do not settle whether their earliest survey service was willing or coerced.
