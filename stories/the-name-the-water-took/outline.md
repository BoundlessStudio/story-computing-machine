# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: A naked young woman emerges from a hidden jungle
  pool unable to remember a name, a relationship, or a single event from her
  life. Waiting above the bank is sixty-one-year-old Eswen, who says the woman
  used to be her eighty-four-year-old mother, Tavira. The pool has delivered
  the youth Tavira knowingly sought: a healthy body near twenty-two, with
  language and practiced skills intact, purchased by the loss of every
  autobiographical memory and the clothes she wore into the water. Over one
  night, reveal who brought her, why she entered, what the pool has and has not
  preserved, and why this is not Tavira's first emergence. Resolve the promise
  by returning her most recent name and life as ordinary facts without magically
  restoring recognition: this young person accepts the consequences and
  records attached to Tavira, but neither she nor the story declares that a
  renewed body automatically recreates the old relationship.
- Intended reader experience: Begin with sensuous bodily alarm, let the apparent
  rescue acquire the uncanny proportions of a fountain-of-youth wish fulfilled
  exactly, and tighten into a private argument between a physically young
  mother and the daughter who now looks older. The late second-use revelation
  should enlarge the price rather than function as a trick solution. Leave the
  reader with real exhilaration at renewed strength, real grief for what is
  gone, and no easy instruction about whether Tavira continued, ended, or
  became someone adjacent to herself.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Relationship-led discovery organized by three sealed orientation
    packets—`BODY`, `PEOPLE`, and `WHY`—which old Tavira left for the person who
    emerged. The new Tavira chooses their order and may stop, while Eswen may
    answer, refuse, correct, or leave the written account standing. Each packet
    adds facts but makes the gap between information and remembered relation
    more consequential.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: Tavira attaches herself to Eswen because
    this stranger is her only living route toward a past, yet recoils from a
    maternal role she cannot feel and from the possibility that old Tavira
    arranged Eswen as a human memory device. She wants the name urgently while
    resisting any claim that the name decides who she must be.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: Not principally plot-led;
    movement comes from Tavira's changing attention to the person behind the
    testimony. Her immediate practical wants are clothes, safety, her name, and
    a coherent account before Eswen leaves at dawn. Her useful limit is an
    investigator's habit that survived as procedural skill: she tests every
    statement against pulse, handwriting, photographs, and scars. It protects
    her from surrendering agency to a stranger, but initially turns Eswen's
    lifetime with her into evidence to extract and cross-examine.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: The pool's price is irreversible in the observed case; facts can be
    supplied, but they do not cause recognition. Old Tavira's instructions
    assume that Eswen will teach the renewed woman back into her former life,
    while Eswen agreed only to wait, provide one bounded orientation, and guide
    her safely out of the jungle. Dusk makes the trail unsafe until morning,
    keeping them together without creating an emergency or an enemy.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Eswen wanted her aging mother alive and
    refused to let Tavira attempt the pool alone, but she also regards the
    immersion as a chosen departure that made Eswen carry their shared history
    by herself. She protects her remaining years, her right to grieve, and her
    freedom not to become a permanent archive. She keeps the promise she made,
    rejects borrowed intimacy, and retains the choice of future contact after
    Tavira changes how she asks.
  - Pressure or change created: Every answer gives Tavira a usable foothold and
    assigns Eswen another burden. Tavira can inherit records, property, bodily
    consequences, and other people's expectations; she cannot demand that
    Eswen manufacture the love, authority, or absolution those facts once
    carried.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Very close first-person present through
    Tavira after the emergence. Her vocabulary, general knowledge, and learned
    observation remain fluent, but personal associations are blank. Do not
    enter old Tavira's or Eswen's viewpoint; the prior life appears only through
    physical evidence, a brief letter, and Eswen's interested, incomplete
    testimony.
  - Time shape and compression: Continuous from late-afternoon emergence to
    shortly after dawn. Give the bank encounter, each packet choice, Eswen's
    refusal, and the second-use disclosure full scene space. Compress cooling,
    eating, and long intervals of reading through the changing jungle noise and
    the renewed body's unfamiliar appetite. Use no dramatized flashbacks and no
    magically recovered memory.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Let the reader share the initial blankness,
    then establish the apparent age inversion before supplying a name. `BODY`
    provides independently checkable facts: Tavira entered at eighty-four with
    a failing heart and emerged physically near twenty-two, without old scars
    or illness. `PEOPLE` identifies Eswen as her adult daughter through an
    ordinary chronology and photographs that Tavira can assess but not feel.
    `WHY` establishes informed choice, fear of an approaching ordinary death,
    and old Tavira's expectation that Eswen would reconstruct the relationship.
    Only after that conflict reveal records showing Tavira emerged from the
    same pool sixty-three years earlier, adopted `Tavira` afterward, and built
    the life that produced Eswen; the first-life name is absent from the
    surviving records, not metaphysically erased from the world. Never prove a
    soul theory or let the pool, documents, or name authenticate personhood.
  - Governing movement: Surface; search the water; inventory the young body;
    distrust the waiting stranger; accept bounded shelter; choose what can be
    checked; receive a name that feels inert; confront a daughter as fact;
    misuse familiarity; open the reason; discover recurrence; accuse; hear a
    refusal; stop extracting; ask what Eswen wants now; accept a limited route
    forward; leave at Eswen's pace.
- Speculative surplus: For several hours after immersion, a clear slick seals
  the skin so rain and wet cloth bead off and sweat cannot cool the renewed
  body. Eswen must keep Tavira in moving shade, fan her, and make her drink;
  mosquitoes also fail to settle on the film. The inconvenient protection
  fades before dawn and has no bearing on memory, identity, deserving youth,
  or whether the pool's price was worthwhile.
- Structural distinction: A post-miracle two-person chamber story: the bodily
  spectacle is already over, and three packets may be opened in chosen rather
  than chronological order around a low shelter table. Its decisive action is
  a change in the terms of one conversation, not a return to the pool, a public
  test, a pursuit, a hearing, a crowd conversion, or an institutional rewrite.
  The inverted-age dyad and recurrence disclosure distinguish the shape from a
  linear recovery quest or a single last-minute identity reveal.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: The `WHY` letter describes the
  first rejuvenation as proof that Tavira can come back and instructs Eswen to
  tell the emerged woman everything until she can resume their home. Eswen says
  plainly that she will not spend the rest of her older-looking life rebuilding
  a mother who chose not to remember her. Tavira first treats that refusal as
  withheld evidence. Then the prior-immersion records make the pattern visible:
  each renewed life depends on somebody else retaining what the pool removes.
  She recognizes that pressing Eswen for every private memory would make the
  daughter pay the price again. Tavira stops asking what she used to feel and
  asks what Eswen wants from the young stranger in front of her. Eswen chooses
  to provide the written facts once, guide her to the road, and decide later
  whether she wants a relationship.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: Tavira's youth and health are real; so are
  the prior life's obligations, Eswen's anger, the inaccessible memories, and
  the possibility that other people will make conflicting continuity claims.
  Tavira keeps `Tavira` as a practical address and takes the notebooks to read
  without using Eswen as their voice. She does not promise never to enter the
  pool again on behalf of a self who may one day fear death differently, and
  Eswen does not forgive, reclaim, or abandon her within the story.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Tavira feeling along the pool bottom for
  vanished clothes and then searching the same mute water for a name. End after
  dawn with the records in her own pack while Eswen names the distance she
  wants between them on the walk to the road; Tavira follows at that distance
  without asking for another piece of the past. The first movement demands a
  total answer from the water; the last accepts one bounded present action from
  a person who owes no total answer.

## Beats

1. Tavira surfaces naked in warm green water and reaches the bank on the strength
   of a body that feels startlingly young. Language, swimming, weather sense,
   and threat assessment remain, but every autobiographical question returns
   nothing. An older woman waiting beyond arm's reach offers a wrap, asks before
   touching her, and says she knows the name Tavira used.
2. The waiting woman, Eswen, checks Tavira's coherence and gets her beneath a
   nearby leaf-roof shelter before dusk. Tavira's skin repels rain and begins to
   overheat; Eswen handles the familiar aftereffect with shade, fanning, and
   measured drinks while refusing to answer an uncontrolled flood of questions.
   She places the three sealed packets on the table and states her dawn limit.
3. Tavira selects `BODY` first because its claims can be tested. It names her as
   Tavira, gives the previous day's age as eighty-four, records a failing heart
   and several scars, and predicts a healthy body near twenty-two. Her pulse,
   teeth, joints, and unmarked skin support the account. The written name causes
   no recognition, but she elects to be addressed by it for the night.
4. `PEOPLE` supplies a sparse family chronology and photographs of Tavira aging
   beside Eswen from infancy into her sixties. Eswen confirms that she is the
   daughter but refuses `Mother` as an automatic title. Tavira tries a childhood
   shortening found in a caption; Eswen stops her and explains that the private
   name belonged to a remembered relationship, not to the evidence packet.
5. Tavira delays `WHY` and interrogates Eswen instead. Eswen admits she guided
   Tavira to the pool because Tavira knew the price, was physically failing,
   and intended to attempt the route alone if refused. Eswen neither claims the
   choice was clean nor accepts blame for making it. Their disagreement remains
   active when Tavira finally breaks the last seal.
6. Old Tavira's letter frames renewal as survival and asks the emerged woman to
   resume home after Eswen restores the relevant memories in words. Its enclosed
   records disclose the deeper twist: Tavira herself first emerged young and
   blank sixty-three years earlier. `Tavira` was the name that life adopted;
   the first-life name is missing from the surviving packet. Youth has recurred,
   but autobiographical continuity has never been demonstrated.
7. Tavira accuses Eswen of helping someone arrange her destruction; Eswen
   answers that the woman who entered was her mother and the woman before her
   may be a continuation, a successor, or both, but neither uncertainty gives
   Tavira ownership of Eswen's memories. Eswen will guide her out and hand over
   the notebooks; she will not spend her remaining life narrating Tavira back
   into being.
8. Tavira notices that her surviving investigative competence is treating
   Eswen as one more instrument. She stops demanding emotional testimony and
   asks what Eswen wants now. They negotiate a narrow morning arrangement:
   directions and necessary health facts, a walk to the road with space between
   them, and no promise about the house or future contact. Tavira reads alone
   while Eswen sleeps.
9. At dawn the slick has faded and Tavira's renewed body is hungry, sore from
   the unfamiliar exertion, and still empty of yesterday. She packs the opened
   records, leaves the pool without testing it again, and follows Eswen toward
   the road at the distance Eswen chose. The story stops before reunion,
   settlement, legal claims, or a verdict on identity.

## Continuity

- Canon used: `Memory, myth, and recoverable truth`; `Memory, transformation,
  copies, and branches`; `Names and identity binding`; and the locked
  personhood protection. These allow partial ordinary evidence, require this
  memory effect to remain mechanism-specific, keep names socially rather than
  universally magical, and protect the emerged person's agency and Eswen's
  moral stakes.
- Boundaries and unknowns: The unnamed pool is a new, strictly local anomaly
  with no established relationship to Ravel identity-lines, Calvea's Calling
  the Name, Deepmarket memory tolls, Bellweather severance, the Fenwick watch,
  the Glass Gate, branch-person incidents, an existing water system, or any
  named jungle or basin. The story establishes only two recorded immersions by
  this person: full bodily renewal to a young-adult state, loss of worn clothes
  and autobiographical memory, preservation of general knowledge and practiced
  skills, and a temporary skin slick. Its origin, wider users, exact age target,
  limits, permanence, and effect on another person remain unknown. External
  records persist as fallible ordinary evidence and do not restore memory or
  decide whether the emerged Tavira is the same, a continuation, or a new
  person. Era, geography, first-life name, and the future of Tavira and Eswen's
  relationship remain unresolved.
