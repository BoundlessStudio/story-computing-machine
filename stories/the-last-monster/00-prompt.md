> [WP] you don’t know how long you been lost in this dungeon but you are losing your mind and just want to get out. You can’t tell what a monster or an adventurer anymore so you just been killing anything in sight. It gotten to a point where the dungeon monsters are now scared of you

## Story controls

- Working title: The Last Monster
- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV: Close second person
- Tense: Present
- Tone and genre: Claustrophobic dark fantasy with psychological horror and a humane emotional core
- Audience/content rating: Mature teen; sustained peril and violence without lingering graphic gore
- Required elements: A time-lost dungeon survivor; deteriorating perception; uncertainty over whether encountered beings are monsters or adventurers; indiscriminate lethal violence; dungeon creatures that have learned to fear the survivor; a concrete attempt to escape; a resolved ending
- Prohibited elements: Treating mental illness as innate evil; a synopsis or chapter-fragment ending; revealing that every event was merely a dream

## Assumptions

- The default story-room length of 2,500–4,000 words is appropriate.
- The second-person phrasing in the prompt is treated as a deliberate narrative invitation.
- The protagonist's confusion arises from prolonged dungeon exposure, deprivation, trauma, and any canon-compatible environmental pressures established by research.
- Low-impact setting details may be invented when they do not contradict current authority.
- The ending will resolve the immediate escape question while allowing emotional consequences to remain.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
