## Canon posture

The final story supports one unnamed, unplaced underground complex; one
long-trapped survivor; several unnamed kinds of dungeon inhabitants; one local
learned warning and evacuation response; a materially legible route to a
hillside drain; and one resolved escape followed by restraint and disclosure.
All are bounded to this story's observed place, people, and events.

The dungeon is ordinary nonliving architecture. Airflow, roots, grates, rope,
maps, weapons, lanterns, and marks are ordinary physical evidence and tools.
The story introduces no magic, altered chronology, living architecture,
objective monster taxonomy, universal dungeon ecology, or connection to any
established crossing, location, culture, species, faculty, or time mechanism.
