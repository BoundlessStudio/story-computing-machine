# Canon brief

No user ruling is required. The prompt is compatible with current authority when the dungeon, its inhabitants, and any perception-altering pressure are bounded local inventions rather than extensions of an established crossing system or universal “monster” taxonomy.

## Hard constraints

- The story occurs somewhere in the one persistent physical world shared across deep time. An unnamed fantasy era and region are safe; the dungeon need not connect to Ravel, Deepmarket, the Glass-Sea era, or any known location (`universe/premise.md` — `## The deep-time shared world`; `universe/locations.md` — `## The world across eras`).

- The prompt contract controls form and content: 2,500–4,000 words, close second person, present tense, claustrophobic dark fantasy and psychological horror with a humane core, and mature-teen non-graphic violence (`stories/the-last-monster/00-prompt.md` — `## Story controls`).

- The story must dramatize a time-lost dungeon survivor, deteriorating perception, inability to distinguish monsters from adventurers, indiscriminate lethal violence, creatures that have learned to fear the survivor, a concrete escape attempt, and a resolved immediate escape outcome (`stories/the-last-monster/00-prompt.md` — `## Story controls`; `## Completion tests`).

- The confusion must arise from prolonged exposure, deprivation, trauma, or a clearly bounded local environmental mechanism. It cannot frame mental illness as innate evil, erase the protagonist’s moral agency, or make violence consequence-free (`stories/the-last-monster/00-prompt.md` — `## Prohibited elements`; `## Assumptions`; `universe/style-guide.md` — `## Personhood and moral consequence`; `## Audience and content boundaries`).

- Violence and death may be sustained and consequential but must remain non-graphic, emphasizing fear, aftermath, choice, and responsibility rather than gore or spectacle (`universe/style-guide.md` — `## Audience and content boundaries`).

- The ending cannot declare the story a dream, dismiss the victims as unreal, or use an arbitrary hallucination reveal to patch causality. Evidence, chronology, and consequences must remain coherent even where victim identity stays uncertain (`stories/the-last-monster/00-prompt.md` — `## Prohibited elements`; `universe/style-guide.md` — `## Continuity and evidence discipline`).

- If the dungeon or an environmental force uses magic, only life may be the magic user. Nonliving architecture, traps, relics, doors, or mechanisms may store, focus, transmit, or shape living-supplied magic but cannot independently cast unless the story establishes that they are alive (`universe/rules.md` — `## Living agency and magic`).

- Any magical perception distortion, cost, desire response, consent behavior, time effect, or identity effect must be local and consistently demonstrated. Canon supplies no universal madness, memory, dungeon, monster, or time-loss mechanism (`universe/rules.md` — `## System categories`; `## Costs, desire, and local mechanisms`; `## Memory, transformation, copies, and branches`).

- “Monster” is not an established objective species or moral category. The admitted corpus uses the word only as moral or metaphorical framing, while canonical nonhuman beings receive story-specific agency and mechanisms. The new story may use “monster” as the protagonist’s unstable or cultural label, but must not establish that appearance or species determines guilt (`stories/daughter-of-the-sun/05-story.md` — `# Daughter of the Sun`; `stories/the-gentlest-terror/05-story.md` — `# The Gentlest Terror`; `stories/the-shape-of-mercy/05-story.md` — `# The Shape of Mercy`).

## Relevant established context

- The full manifest-admitted story corpus contains no literal dungeon, labyrinth, or adventurer precedent. That absence leaves the dungeon open for local invention; it does not establish a universal dungeon ecology or rule.

- `Realms` is the nearest location-and-escape precedent, but it is not a dungeon. The Queen Street seam, exit tokens, timed return windows, Deepmarket, living shops, memory toll, and route-stranding behavior are one bounded crossing system. Reusing any of those terms or mechanisms would create a continuity burden; superficial similarities such as tunnels, doors, hostile organisms, or being unable to return do not establish a connection (`universe/locations.md` — `## Queen Street crossing complex`; `## Deepmarket`; `universe/rules.md` — `## Queen Street living seam and route tokens`; `## Deepmarket desire pricing and stated memory toll`; `stories/realms/05-story.md` — `# Realms`).

- `The Small Moon Rose First` establishes a survivor who endured 617 days elsewhere while eleven seconds passed at the modern-like departure point. Nell returned half-starved, scarred, hypervigilant, sleeping beneath a table and later hiding food. This supports humane treatment of long-duration survival trauma, but the Glass Gate’s temporal-anchor behavior is explicitly local and cannot explain this dungeon without an intentional crossover (`stories/the-small-moon-rose-first/05-story.md` — `## Day 617`; `## February 18`; `## One Year After Nell Returned`; `universe/rules.md` — `## Witnessed Glass Gate operation`).

- `The Shape of Mercy` distinguishes a stormhart’s own terror, hunger, rage, and desire from an imposed command to kill. Fear can blur a frightened person’s judgment, but Phoebe’s ability to separate those impulses is her local beast-affinity faculty, not a perception power available to this protagonist (`stories/the-shape-of-mercy/05-story.md` — `# The Shape of Mercy`; `universe/rules.md` — `## Bellweather household faculties`; `## Garran's command-bonds and witnessed stormhart`).

- `The Gentlest Terror` establishes that a physically frightening creature may be gentle, social, and morally independent, and explicitly criticizes using a “monster’s face” as a political instrument. This is thematic precedent, not shared dragon or monster biology (`stories/the-gentlest-terror/05-story.md` — `# The Gentlest Terror`).

- `Daughter of the Sun` refuses to flatten a person responsible for mass death into a simple monster whose label removes grief or moral complexity. The new survivor may be terrifying and culpable without being reduced to innate evil or automatically absolved by impairment (`stories/daughter-of-the-sun/05-story.md` — `# Daughter of the Sun`).

## Conflicts or ambiguity

- **No present contradiction:** current canon does not prohibit a dungeon, local dungeon creatures, adventuring parties, survival-induced perceptual collapse, or a survivor feared by other inhabitants.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** canon defines no dungeon origin, ecology, intelligence hierarchy, adventurer culture, map logic, respawn rule, loot economy, or objective monster taxonomy. These may be invented locally but cannot be presented as already established universe-wide lore.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** canon supplies no environmental pressure that causes this protagonist’s confusion. Deprivation and trauma are already authorized prompt assumptions; any supernatural addition must be newly defined and bounded.

- **Omission, not contradiction:** the survivor’s actual elapsed time, age, identity, original party, number of victims, and the human/nonhuman status of any victim are unknown.

- **Conditional contradiction:** making an ordinary nonliving dungeon independently cast perception magic would conflict with the locked living-agency rule unless the dungeon is established as alive or the magic has a living source.

- **Conditional contradiction:** declaring the dungeon part of the Queen Street Realms, the Glass Gate network, Ravel, or another established system solely because it has doors, tunnels, time loss, or route failure would violate continuity and evidence discipline.

- **Conditional contradiction:** a final reveal that all violence was dreamed, simulated without consequence, or committed against unreal projections would violate the prompt and continuity requirements.

- The verified archived `S03` version of `Realms` contains additional details absent from promoted authority, including the Bone Orchard and broader Runner claims. It has `authority: none`; none of those details may be imported as canon (`sources/README.md` — `# Evidence records`; `sources/MANIFEST.json` — record `S03`; `sources/records/s03/Realms.md` — complete inert record).

## Unknowns

- The dungeon’s era, region, builders, original purpose, age, depth, geometry, current condition, and relationship to the surface.

- Whether the protagonist is merely unable to count elapsed time or has experienced an actual chronological anomaly.

- The protagonist’s name, age, gender, origin, profession, equipment, original companions, prior morality, and exact route into the dungeon.

- Whether deteriorating perception is entirely physiological and traumatic or includes a local toxin, organism, curse, magical effect, or sensory mismatch.

- The creature populations’ intelligence, languages, communities, diet, territorial behavior, and reasons for fearing the survivor.

- How creatures communicate or learn the warning, and whether their shared avoidance is observation, scent, sound, marks, rumor, or another local behavior.

- Which killed beings were monsters, adventurers, neither, or unknowable. The ending may preserve some uncertainty while still resolving escape.

- The physical exit mechanism, the survivor’s condition on reaching it, and whether anyone outside recognizes or restrains them.

## Safe local invention space

- Create an independent dungeon in an unnamed fantasy era of the shared world, with no crossover or cosmological exposition.

- Invent the dungeon’s architecture, local history, traps, ecology, factions, monster labels, adventuring practice, navigation hazards, and surface entrance. Keep all claims geographically and culturally local.

- Use deprivation, darkness, infection, sleep loss, malnutrition, isolation, trauma, or ordinary sensory ambiguity as the principal cause of confusion. A bounded biological or magical environmental effect is also safe if introduced early, given observable rules, and assigned a valid living source where magic is involved.

- Give dungeon creatures individual or communal agency. They may hide, redirect young, leave warnings, imitate corpses, abandon feeding grounds, or create escape corridors in response to the survivor. Their fear should be demonstrated through behavior rather than asserted by the narrator.

- Let “monster” and “adventurer” be unstable viewpoint categories. Concrete details—gear, posture, voices, wounds, hands, teeth, light, armor, or scent—may support multiple readings without an omniscient answer.

- Invent a material escape trail such as airflow, water gradient, root growth, discarded rope, repeated creature migration, bells, or changing masonry. Avoid borrowing Queen Street tokens, Glass Gate anchors, Gate Cant, Deepmarket pricing, or Ravel identity-lines.

- Resolve the escape while leaving emotional and moral consequences open. Reaching daylight, a surface gate, or an inhabited threshold is enough if the text clearly establishes whether the survivor exits.

## Concrete planning recommendation

Use a self-contained dungeon with no established-system crossover. Open after the survivor’s violence has already changed the ecology. Give the escape attempt one objective physical method—following airflow, water, or the creatures’ evacuation route—so the plot advances even as perception fails.

Build the midpoint around evidence that dungeon inhabitants coordinate around the survivor: abandoned nests, warning marks, deliberate silence, or bodies moved out of their path. In the climax, place one visibly terrified being between the survivor and the usable exit route. A pause, failed attack, or decision to follow rather than strike can provide the humane turn without proving what every earlier victim was. End with the immediate escape question answered and the survivor’s culpability, trauma, and uncertain victim record still real.

- **Drafting:** preserve close second-person present tense and keep externally verifiable detail distinct from the protagonist’s uncertain interpretation.

- **Drafting:** show the creatures’ learned fear through repeated causal behavior, not a late explanatory statement.

- **Drafting:** keep every violent encounter non-graphic and consequential. The protagonist’s impairment may explain judgment failure but must not turn killing into spectacle, proof of innate evil, or automatic absolution.

- **Continuity review:** verify that no dungeon feature has silently borrowed Queen Street, Deepmarket, Glass Gate, Ravel, Bellweather, or Ersene mechanics or terminology.

- **Continuity review:** if magic appears, identify its living user or source and the nonliving role of every focus, trap, door, or architectural mechanism.

- **Continuity review:** confirm that the immediate escape question is explicitly resolved and that no dream, simulation, or unreal-victim reveal erases consequences.

- **Name gates:** validate all character-facing names and aliases after planning and reconcile them after final review.
