# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: **Witch Cold**. Adult magical girl Nuala’s every feverish sneeze releases one random spell. Best friend Imogen must move her from a cluttered apartment to an empty basement washroom and through the fever peak. Deliver affectionate comic wonder, cumulative domestic consequences, and containment rather than cure.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Imogen knows Nuala’s preferred drink, working kit, and rose-and-cream magical outfit, but turns fear into over-packing and physical management. Nuala wants care without surrendering decisions about her body, route, or risk; broken speech does not reduce her craft or consent authority.
- Counterforce or complication: Each result changes the route or compromises supplies. Imogen’s tighter-is-safer belief creates single points of failure: one cramped room, exit, case, and water supply. The cold is a condition, not an antagonist; Nuala is never blamed.
- POV, distance, and information limit: First-person past through Imogen, attentive to logistics and best-friend knowledge. She reads Nuala’s gestures but cannot predict magic or feel her symptoms; only Nuala can diagnose residues and permit movement. Imogen’s certainty narrows her attention.
- Governing movement and time shape: Ninety minutes: arrival, failed containment, descent, cleared washroom, fever peak, settling. Movement contracts from clutter and stairs to one spacious private perimeter. Resolve through care and damage control, without public proof, confession, romance, or symbolic closure.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Every sneeze uses Nuala’s living magic to select, without pattern, emotional link, or subconscious influence, one of six effects within three metres and direct sight: (1) loose nonliving objects under five kilograms float for forty seconds; (2) one paper or textile item blooms to four times its volume for eight minutes; (3) two unfastened objects under ten kilograms swap; (4) one rigid object up to a metre across softens for thirty seconds, then hardens where left; (5) twenty litres of rain falls; or (6) one loose object under a kilogram makes seven copies for five minutes, after which only the original remains. Opaque barriers stop selection; impact, water, tears, stains, and deformation persist. Two to four seconds beforehand, identical coral pinlights and a descending three-note chime sharpen, indicating only imminence. Nuala cannot suppress, delay, aim, choose, or bias the release. She can move, consent, warn, diagnose, and plan. Cold, spells, signals, and objects are non-sentient and non-moral.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Imogen narrates through inventories, distances, closures, and contingencies, interrupted by tactile knowledge of Nuala and her handmade costume. Attention widens from objects to timing, clearance, and signals.
- Conversational texture: Ordinary fragments concern water, temperature, tissues, doors, and stairs. Congestion, chimes, and action break turns; sketches, counts, and colored cards replace speech.
- Rhetorical ownership: Imogen owns rapid logistics and overconfident risk summaries. Nuala owns precise aftereffect diagnosis, consent decisions, and terse craft corrections; the nurse addresses fever care only.
- Pressure behavior: Imogen speeds up, repeats, and seeks confirmation. Fever shortens Nuala’s clauses unevenly; difficult breathing stops speech, making Imogen wait for visual answers.
- Anti-default: Avoid manic-whimsy girl versus dour foil, quip chains, trope-aware jokes, apology loops, polished friendship speeches, or an ending exchange that explains the care already shown.

## Beats

1. Imogen arrives with one packed care case. Adult Nuala is exhausted in her charming practical working dress, not infantilized. A warning chime precedes a buoyancy spell lifting mugs, tissues, and medicine.
2. Imogen tapes cupboards and proposes the cramped bathroom. Nuala’s broken explanation and sketch identify its hard surfaces, exposed water, and sole exit as worse risks than open clearance. A bloom working fills the doorway with tissue-paper folds; it recedes, leaving torn supplies and delay.
3. A softening working deforms the doorframe as Imogen forces the case through. Nuala diagnoses its hardening time and refuses carrying. They choose the disused washroom: drain, few loose objects, two exits, opaque walls.
4. On the stairs, pinlights and chime serve only as a brace signal. An exchange separates the case; rain slicks the landing. Route work, not luck, recovers it. An unnamed nurse covers fever thresholds, hydration, dosing, and escalation signs only.
5. Imogen again centers one sealed case. Using colored cards, finger counts, and a floor sketch, Nuala separates water, medicine, towels, and tools beyond the three-metre perimeter for controlled handoff. Imogen accepts Nuala’s chosen position and keeps both exits usable.
6. At fever peak, copies crowd the drain, flowered cloth obstructs sight, buoyant gear cracks a light cover, and a basin guard hardens bent. Their brace routine contains effects. Imogen lets supplies take damage rather than grabbing Nuala; diagnoses shorten or become visual.
7. Dose, water, cooling cloth, and rest lengthen the intervals but do not cure a cold that may last days. After one final contained sneeze, Nuala sleeps by choice; Imogen stages the next drink outside range and starts a repair list and cleanup shift. Return upstairs remains later work.

- Boundaries and unknowns: Setting, witch cold, pool, signal, and recovery practice are local. Establish no link to existing magical-girl systems or any universal hierarchy, illness, cure, cost, consent, or transformation rule. No reference images were supplied.
