# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

## New characters or character facts

- **Miriam Velez (proposed):** A bicycle-and-transit app courier in an unnamed
  contemporary city and the sister of fifteen-year-old Mateo Velez. Their
  mother died in an ordinary road collision two years before the story.
  Miriam's fear of another loss led her to postpone shared pleasures and
  confuse control with care. After the Briar Hill crossing, she accepts
  reciprocal boundaries with Mateo, schedules a modest outing, and keeps it
  despite financial pressure and fear.
- **Mateo Velez (proposed):** Miriam's fifteen-year-old brother. He researches
  the local Briar Hill story, deliberately boards Route 0 using the same
  bargain and tests Miriam later follows, refuses a false image of their mother,
  and freely chooses the dawn return. Afterward, he accepts consequences for
  his unsafe secrecy and agrees that safety precautions do not constitute
  permission to disappear.
- Miriam and Mateo's unnamed mother (not a character-facing name) died in an ordinary
  road collision two years before the crossing. Before her death, she left a
  saved voicemail proposing a family outing to Briar Hill; the recording ends
  during her laughter. She does not return, speak from an afterlife, or acquire
  a continuing copy in this story.
- **Maude Dallow (proposed):** A silver-haired night-bus driver and living
  tender of the city end of the Route 0 crossing. She focuses her own green-lit
  magic through the nonliving bus, fare reader, wheel, and window frames; those
  objects do not act or make bargains independently. She states the crossing's
  rules, permits informed choices, and refuses to promise that the route will
  recur.
- **The Briar Host (proposed):** One living, person-like guardian rooted in
  Briar Hill. It tends the Hill end of this local crossing, manipulates its
  living hedge and roots, and projects bounded sensory and screen glamours from
  material received within its hedge. It is exacting but does not compel a
  rider's return, restore the dead, create a new answer from the dead, or turn
  arranged stored material into the absent person.

## New locations

- **Proposal — unnamed contemporary city:** A modern-like, low-magic city with
  municipal buses, a western terminal shelter, contactless fares, stop-code
  text responses, live-location sharing, app-based courier work, apartment
  towers, a canal, and ordinary late-night service. No real-world identity,
  date, wider magical society, or connection to another established location is
  asserted.
- **Proposal — Briar Hill:** An ordinarily mapped park entrance in or beside
  the city and the local anchor of the Briar Host's crossing. During the one
  witnessed crossing, it becomes accessible behind apartment towers by a
  thorn-lined route and living root steps. The story does not establish its
  origin or any connection to another crossing system.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Proposal — Route 0 crossing:** Route 0 is a bounded local crossing carried
  by a nonliving white bus and tended by living agents at both ends. On the one
  witnessed rainy last-service night, it overlays and rearranges fragments of
  contemporary city streets without establishing travel to another era, plane,
  or portal network.
- **Proposal — access bargain:** A rider boards by voluntarily canceling an
  ordinary destination the city already expects them to pursue, naming the
  person they seek, and paying the usual transit fare with an ordinary fare
  card. Both Mateo and Miriam satisfy these terms separately. A destination
  does not count as abandoned if the rider never chose it.
- **Proposal — three request-stop tests:** On the witnessed crossing, riders
  must enact generosity toward a stranded traveler, distinguish a real route
  by obtaining fresh outside information, and truthfully acknowledge the motive
  behind their journey. Miriam's choices cost scarce phone charge and the last
  power-bank capacity needed for tracking and civic messaging.
- **Proposal — false-exit deadline:** During Miriam's witnessed passage, Maude
  gives her six minutes to identify the real road among three copied exits.
  After those six minutes, the unresolved stop would become Miriam's
  destination. No broader timing rule or consequence for another crossing is
  established.
- **Proposal — stored-copy limit:** Within this crossing, the Briar Host can
  repeat maps, signs, screen material, photographs, recordings, and sensory
  impressions received or stored within its hedge. It cannot forge a new reply
  freely made outside the crossing after a rider enters. A current municipal
  stop-code response can therefore distinguish the real Briar Hill exit from
  copied ones.
- **Proposal — false-mother boundary:** The mother-shaped kitchen and voice are
  sensory glamours assembled from stored material, not a ghost, resurrection,
  continuing copy-person, afterlife contact, or newly chosen reply from Miriam
  and Mateo's mother. The authentic saved voicemail's traffic, correction,
  laughter, and unfinished ending expose the glamour's false certainty.
- **Proposal — consent and return:** The Briar Host can test a rider and open a
  route toward a shared unfinished promise, but it cannot choose a living
  rider's return or allow one rider to choose for another. The one promised
  return closes at sunrise. Mateo and Miriam board separately and freely after
  completing the last path together.
- **Proposal — practical cost:** The crossing does not reset ordinary
  consequences. Miriam loses her accepted courier block's guaranteed bonus,
  receives a warning that future offers may decrease, remains short on rent,
  and must take extra midday deliveries. Her bicycle still needs repair, both
  phones require recharging, and the siblings remain accountable for their
  choices.
- **Proposal — limited public evidence:** The one witnessed crossing leaves
  partial, ordinary-looking digital traces: a normal fare charge, intermittent
  location history at the terminal and an unlisted park access, dead phone
  batteries, a canceled courier block, and an ordinary municipal stop-code
  reply. Route 0 remains absent from the city's normal transit app. No cover-up,
  memory erasure, or general public knowledge of the crossing is established.
- **Proposal — leaf-punched seed-token:** After the siblings jointly choose the
  return, the Briar Host produces a flat brown token punched like a leaf. Maude
  describes it as completing beginnings rather than wishes. It remains inert
  at home and opens into a small green shoot only after Miriam and Mateo
  actually undertake their scheduled shared outing. This is a personal local
  gift, not a universal mechanism for promises or desire.
- **Explicit unknown — recurrence:** Only one Route 0 crossing and one dawn
  return are witnessed. Maude expressly refuses to turn that promise into a
  second one. Whether Route 0 ever appeared before, will appear again, or
  follows a repeatable rainy-night schedule remains unknown.
- **Explicit unknown — origin and wider scope:** The age, origin, creation, and
  larger history of Route 0, Maude's magic, the Briar Host, and the seed-token
  remain unknown. No shared ancestry with any other magical route, artifact, or
  crossing is proposed.

## Timeline events

- **Proposal — undated modern-like-era event:** Over one rainy night, Mateo and
  Miriam separately board Route 0, pass its three request stops, meet at Briar
  Hill, complete a final root-bridge test together, and freely take the sole
  promised dawn return. No calendar date or universal chronology coordinate is
  established.
- **Proposal — two weeks later:** Miriam and Mateo keep their scheduled,
  low-cost daytime outing by municipal bus. After they return, Miriam discovers
  that the planted leaf-punched token has split and sprouted. This shows a
  completed local gift without erasing grief, rent pressure, work consequences,
  or future responsibility.

## New glossary terms or aliases

  the Briar Host.
