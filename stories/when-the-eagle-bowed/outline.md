# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: While orphaned huntress Yavren dresses a mountain goat, a wild eagle lands within spear reach and bows. Living armor splits the mountain and three royal warrants activate. Yavren must remember being the executed last Eagle Queen, stop the armor from destroying the nesting slopes, and make every king's hunters believe the Queen died again without surrendering her present life.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Yavren wants her own mind, foster-sister Pelka alive, and their trapline back. The Queen's memories supply needed routes and command reflexes but bring obsolete geography and planned vengeance. Rejecting them loses the means to quiet the armor; accepting them whole lets a dead office consume current loyalties.
- Counterforce or complication: Three ridge detachments converge. The weir king believes death prevents a landslide; the ash king must bury memories that undermine his dynasty; the road king wants Yavren dead but the armor captured. Their tactics conflict. Pelka rejects both coronation and denial, forcing decisions through wounds, routes, and people below the slope.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Yavren, who reads spoor, wind, animals, and altered terrain but receives the Queen's past as intrusive sense, muscle expectation, and misplaced knowledge. Eagle and colony motives remain provisional until behavior repeats.
- Governing movement and time shape: An afternoon-to-dawn hunt in which Yavren shifts from quarry to tracker, climbing through three pursuit lines toward the threatened eyrie and an ancient molting cleft. End beyond the valley on practical travel, not restored court.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: The Eagle Queen was a nonhereditary mediator between wild eagles and talonmail, a living mineral-plated colony able to tunnel and sheath a bearer. Only eagle, bearer, and colony work magic; metal fittings merely focus it. The Queen refused three warlords' demand to deploy talonmail outside the valley, so they executed her, founded the crowns, and preserved kill warrants. Her soul later reincarnated as Yavren without bloodline selection. The colony retained bounded memory impressions from their earlier contact. A wild eagle's inherited nesting-distress bow before the returned soul closes the living signal; talonmail wakes, then contact presses those memories into Yavren. It follows heat, recognized movement, and raised-weapon threats, not speech. Without its old molt, it surfaces toward her, consumes exposed metal and limestone, and destabilizes the eyries.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move with a hunter's economy, noticing grit, feather angle, wind shadow, and load-bearing cracks before interpretation. Older sensory overlays interrupt rather than elevate the language.
- Conversational texture: Pelka uses unfinished practical questions and present tasks; soldiers trade terse range, warrant, and casualty information. Route and wound talk outnumbers lore.
- Rhetorical ownership: Yavren reasons from tracks; Pelka from shared recent particulars; weir forces calculate harm, ash forces invoke inheritance, and road forces inventory value. Queenly rhetoric remains intrusive pressure.
- Pressure behavior: Yavren loses nouns, shifts registers, and mistakes old routes for current ones. Pelka grows literal and demands one action at a time; soldiers understand one another less as orders conflict.
- Anti-default: Avoid courtly aphorisms, fluent reincarnation speeches, interchangeable tyrants, loyal-animal sentiment, chosen-one certainty, and a final exchange explaining what the physical deception establishes.

## Beats

1. At the kill, Yavren distinguishes the eagle's gesture from tameness or injury. She returns an unfamiliar hand posture; talonmail breaks through scree, and a ridge horn activates the warrants.
2. Pelka arrives before the riders. A plate's touch delivers the execution and an obsolete escape route. Yavren nearly follows it into a collapse, then finds a traverse from fresh goat tracks.
3. Weir archers evacuate households, ash hunters erase witnesses, and road men cast nets. Talonmail consumes raised metal and worsens the slope. Yavren stops a Queen-reflex from turning it on them.
4. The eagle keeps returning to a cracking eyrie. Yavren reads its bow as nesting distress, not allegiance. Its flight and Pelka's ridge knowledge reveal the ancient molting cleft.
5. Yavren allows talonmail to sheath her. Full memory reveals the Queen's planned revenge. Yavren retains the past but refuses its intention while Pelka grounds her with breath, rope, and immediate lives.
6. Yavren combines the old molt with a hunter's narrow crawl, slipping free and leaving a bloodied royal silhouette. The husk falls into an unreachable vent while the living core retracts and seals the fault.
7. The detachments witness the false death and armor's cessation. Conflicting aims prevent a joint search. The eagle returns to its nest; Yavren and Pelka cross an unnamed pass, planning food and the next route rather than a crown.

## Continuity

- Canon used: One deep-time world; only life uses magic; reincarnation is possible but locally defined; returned identity never erases present personhood or moral choice; truth does not ensure justice.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Covenant, talonmail, crowns, and trigger are local, establishing no universal bloodline, soul-recognition, animal obedience, or memory-restoration rule. Yavren is the continuing reincarnated soul; the stored impressions are evidence, not her soul. The agents certify a witnessed false death, ending immediate warrants without converting or overthrowing the kings.
