# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: A wild eagle bows to Tirra, an orphaned huntress on Harrowscar, and an empty suit of queen-metal begins dragging itself through the mountain toward her. The signal mirrors of Eyrpass Valley carry her description from court to court; before sunset, all seven kings renew the ancestral order to kill the last Eagle Queen, executed a century earlier, before memory makes the armor hers. Tirra must survive the converging hunt and voluntarily recover that queen's autobiographical memory. Her prior self was neither pure monster nor innocent icon: she imposed peace by force and once closed a pass while civilians were still escaping. Tirra accepts those acts as genuinely in her past without letting kings or relic define the self her present life made.
- Intended reader experience: A cold mountain pursuit rich in spoor, weather, unstable stone, and one spectacular impossible object, tightening into intimate dread as remembering makes escape easier and self-recognition harder. The return should feel real without becoming effortless empowerment, vindication, or coronation. End with earned survival, unwanted knowledge, and a live hunt.
- Generating force:
  - Mode: Plot-led three-spoor convergence. Tirra lays false trails around Harrowscar; royal trail warden Dajan reads across them; the armor ignores surface spoor and claws the shortest route through rock toward her living presence. Each crossing reverses hunter, quarry, and guide until all three tracks meet beneath the old execution ledge.
  - Focal attachment or contradiction: Tirra trusts the self she assembled without parents, pedigree, or permission, and she survives by increasing distance from anything that claims her. Yet the armor's direct pursuit is breaking the mountain above occupied winter camps, while the memory everyone fears may be the only way to halt it. Refusing the past protects her chosen self but leaves other people under the consequences; accepting it risks giving the kings' story and the old queen's violence a place inside her.
  - Plot-led wants and useful limit, if applicable: Tirra immediately wants to cross the lower passes before seven royal search lines close. Competing with survival, she wants to draw the armor away from the winter camps and stop its rockfalls. Her useful flaw is defensive self-sufficiency: she treats every approach as a snare and keeps relying on false trails after the armor proves it follows something inside her. That habit costs her the easiest escape and drives all three pursuers onto the same unstable shelf.
- Counterforce or complication:
  - Source: Seven royal search lines close the valley exits while the armor destabilizes slopes whenever Tirra flees downslope. It cannot explain itself, obey a guessed command, or distinguish an empty ridge from people sheltering below. Tirra has only conflicting songs and execution laws until contact unlocks precise sensory memory.
  - Credible inside logic, if a person: Dajan is an active pursuer, not a waiting convert. The inherited warrant protects seven small kingdoms from the return of a ruler whose armor once crushed a rebel pass, and present rockfalls make that danger immediate rather than ceremonial. He wants Tirra dead before she can command the suit, but he also refuses shots that would bury his own scouts or the camps below. He carries the fact Tirra most resists—that the old queen's worst order harmed people outside the royal rebellion—and retains the warrant after witnessing Tirra choose restraint.
  - Pressure or change created: Tirra cannot maximize distance, protect the camps, and avoid memory at once. Dajan makes simple claims of royal propaganda untenable; the kill order makes surrender untenable. Trapped together, remembering becomes both a route to survival and an encounter with culpability, not proof of rightful rule.
- Narrative design:
  - POV, tense, distance, and limitation: Close third-person past through Tirra, intimate with feathers, wind, broken lichen, boot pressure, and the panic of a trail reading her back. Memory first arrives as sensory mismatch, not explanatory vision; after recall, retain Tirra's hunting vocabulary rather than replacing it with regal diction. Do not enter the eagle, armor, kings, or Dajan. Give Dajan clipped route-and-casualty language and reveal priorities through whom he moves out of danger.
  - Time shape and compression: One continuous late-autumn day from the bow to nightfall. Render the first bronze claw, seven mirror orders, failed pass escape, turn away from the camps, Dajan's interception, chamber collapse, voluntary contact, memory, and escape. Compress elevation changes and royal replies through mirror light, horn calls, colder air, and worsening debris. No century-ago flashback scene or later political coda.
  - Information and reveal strategy: Tirra knows only that a bowing eagle and moving queen-metal invoke a death law. Show the empty articulated armor in partial glimpses while Dajan supplies the official charge. A gauntlet's brush yields a private place-memory that saves Tirra from a dead stair without settling identity. Deliberate hand-to-collar contact unlocks the execution, coercive reign, deadly pass order, and embodied restraint that can halt the suit. These are personal recognition, not public evidence. Leave the makers, afterlife interval, and the eagle's recognition unresolved.
  - Governing movement: Receive the bow; see bronze fingers split the cliff; run as seven mirrors answer; lay false spoor; discover the armor cutting straight through rock; brush its gauntlet and know a hidden stair; evade Dajan there; see stone threaten the winter camps; sacrifice the downhill escape and draw the suit inward; cross an unstable shelf between search line and buried route; fall with Dajan into the execution chamber; hear the old accusation; choose the open collar; remember; make the empty suit brace the roof, free Dajan, and halt instead of wearing it; escape through a forgotten drain while he resumes the hunt.
- Speculative surplus: Active queen-metal draws moisture into sharp feather-shaped frost on nearby iron. Bow rings seize, spearheads whiten, and buckles burn bare fingers, so mountain hunters wrap metal fittings in felt when old queen-work is rumored near. The frost gives proximity texture and inconvenience but cannot identify the Eagle Queen, carry memory, reveal intent, or decide how to command the armor.
- Structural distinction: A tightening predator/quarry braid of tracks laid, read, and forced through stone, meeting in a small buried chamber. Protecting the camps makes Tirra turn toward what pursues her. The decisive act is private contact and restraint, not battle before the kings, public proof, crowd conversion, institutional rewrite, or symbolic crowning.
- Decisive turn, deepening, or recognition: With the armor's collar open and Dajan trying to reach Tirra before she touches it, she chooses memory rather than receiving it by accident. She recognizes the execution ledge from below, remembers arranging the armor's living-keyed return, and remembers ordering the pass closure that Dajan named; the old culpability is not royal invention. She also recovers the physical command sequence that requires a living hand inside the collar. Tirra could let the armor close around her and clear Dajan by force. Instead she remains outside it, directs the empty suit to hold the failing roof long enough to free them both, then halts it half-emerged. She tells Dajan only that she remembers. His warrant remains; her refusal to kill him is a choice, not a demonstration that obliges him to believe she is harmless.
- Aftereffect or live uncertainty: The camps escape the immediate rockfall; the armor stops but is neither destroyed nor safely inaccessible. Tirra remembers who she was, how she died, what she did, and how to wake or restrain the suit, not an exhaustive lost history. All seven orders remain active, Dajan knows she can command the armor, and the kings may excavate Harrowscar. Whether she hides, confronts them, dismantles the armor, or claims the title remains open.
- Opening / ending relation: Open with Tirra reading the eagle's lowered head as an injured animal's defensive posture and reaching for a knife before the mountain answers. End after she escapes through a drain remembered by the queen but chooses her route by the huntress's wind sense; the eagle tears at the goat offal she left at dawn while Dajan's horns resume behind her. No second bow, coronation, or settled allegiance.

## Beats

1. On a solitary hunt above Eyrpass Valley, Tirra approaches a wild eagle that lands within spear reach. It lowers its head. Before she can decide whether the gesture is injury, threat, or absurdity, bronze fingers punch through Harrowscar and begin hauling an empty ancient suit toward daylight.
2. A ridge watcher sends the alarm. Tirra starts for the western pass while one royal mirror after another returns the kill pattern; by the seventh, Dajan has redirected patrols under fresh authority from every king. Keep them offstage but make the joint order unmistakable before Tirra remembers.
3. Tirra lays convincing false spoor. Dajan partly reads it; the armor shoulders through a rock rib and nearly catches her. A gauntlet brushes her wrist, producing one exact memory of a concealed stair. She uses it without accepting whose memory it is.
4. Dajan meets her at the stair and states the warrant's practical case: the Eagle Queen used this armor to close a pass on rebels and civilians together. Tirra escapes him, but frost-seized gear and the armor's breach send stone toward the occupied winter slopes.
5. Instead of taking the open descent, Tirra doubles back across Harrowscar to pull the armor toward the empty high face. Her reliance on another false traverse puts her, Dajan, and his approaching search line on a shelf already hollowed from below.
6. The shelf fails. Tirra and Dajan fall into the cramped chamber beneath the old execution ledge while his scouts remain cut off outside. He tries to finish the warrant before the armor breaks through; Tirra uses close hunting movement to disarm rather than kill him.
7. The armor opens its collar. Tirra chooses contact and remembers the execution, planned return, coercive reign, and pass order in concrete sensory detail. The recovered command is bodily and bounded, not a title anyone else can authenticate.
8. Tirra refuses both passive surrender and automatic crowning. With her living hand inside the collar, she makes the empty armor brace the collapsing chamber, frees Dajan, and commands it still. Dajan does not renounce the warrant; the unstable separation gives her minutes, not absolution.
9. Tirra takes a drainage cleft recalled by the queen but times her exit through her own knowledge of winds and horn gaps. The eagle remains wild, the kings remain enemies, and she carries away not a right to rule but both lives and the choice ahead.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The story occurs somewhere in the one deep-time physical world but need not locate its era relative to existing settings. The setting may call its systems magic or queen-work without establishing an objective category. Reincarnation is possible, memory mechanisms are story-specific, only living agency can actively use magic, and Tirra's continuing personhood and present choices must remain morally real. Historical evidence may be distorted and partial; memory does not automatically produce justice.
- Boundaries and unknowns: All proposed people, places, the seven unnamed kings, bowing-eagle recognition, and queen-metal armor are new and local, with no link to established queens, monarchies, valleys, mountains, creatures, armor, memory, soul, or name-workings. The armor is nonliving: Tirra and the wild eagle supply living activation while the suit shapes a preset tracking and mnemonic working rather than exercising agency. Contact neither creates Tirra's identity nor proves a universal reincarnation test, and command of the suit does not establish royal legitimacy. The eagle remains an unnamed wild animal whose motives and understanding are not translated. The builders, the soul's intervening century, the reign's full politics, and the armor's future remain unresolved.
