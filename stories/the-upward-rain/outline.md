# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: During the once-in-a-lifetime upward rain, rival adult explorers Anouk Verren and Kasim Orrel cross seven kilometres of exposed shelf expecting Namarra's royal precinct. The apparent palace proves an intentionally floodable reef-and-water commons built, repaired, then dismantled uphill by several coastal communities. This supports a coordinated Namarra and planned retreat, not the legend's single capital, king, or sudden divine drowning. Resolve survival, custody, and credit before the sea returns.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Anouk's disputed contour-and-strata reading puts the precinct at converging terraces; she needs professional vindication, but competition tempts a direct route and diagnostic removal. Kasim's coastal cooperative reconstructs a route from oral memory and masonry practice, both leading to the crown court. He needs that method credited, not mined for an appendix; custody concerns can harden into confirmation bias. Each fears sole-credit capture.
- Counterforce or complication: Distance, weather, soft mud, pressure-release gas, stranded pools, and filling channels compress the day. A route dispute costs time and a sensor; an interpretive dispute threatens the record. Jessa sees cloud movement, not their footing. Fragile ecology and masonry forbid shortcuts or trophies.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Alternating close third-person past, switching only at major route or custody choices. Anouk reads elevation, layers, loads, and uncertainty; Kasim reads wear, repair habits, sightlines, and taught routes. Neither knows the other's private stakes; Jessa is present only by radio. Narration does not settle an interpretation until both evidence streams support it.
- Governing movement and time shape: One daylight out-and-back transect: descent through older shorelines, a brief shared recording interval, then a return over the same markers as hazards reverse. Let earlier observations gain new meaning. Settle credit through field action, not public proof, reconciliation, or symbolic closure.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: From first light, seawater open to the sky beads upward into a fixed salt-cloud deck; sheltered pools and saturated sediment remain, while organisms strand. Lost hydrostatic weight vents gas, slumps mud, chills the shelf, and drives seaward wind. Downward drops mixed into the upward stream warn that lift is failing: rain fills old channels before the cloud releases a sheet return. Shore instruments provide warning, never control or an exact minute. The anomaly is local and cannot be prolonged.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Tactile narration moves by footing, distance, waterline, and competing notice. Anouk sorts layers and tolerances; Kasim follows joins, wear, and practiced routes. Keep wonder inside labor and bodily scale.
- Conversational texture: Use clipped navigation, radio time calls, sample refusals, recording corrections, breath-broken effort, ordinary credit grudges, and silence at evidence. Not every exchange interprets Namarra.
- Rhetorical ownership: Anouk frames reproducible alternatives and section relationships. Kasim reasons from route sequence, tool marks, repair order, and taught practice. Jessa owns countdowns, weather comparison, and safety authority.
- Pressure behavior: Fear makes Anouk overprecise, then numerical; dismissal makes Kasim withhold context, then offer only actionable correction. Jessa interrupts and requires acknowledgements. Exhaustion reduces both to task nouns, repetition, and useful silence.
- Anti-default: Avoid a quip duel, science-versus-legend debate, romantic turn, or polished joint thesis. Keep their methods distinct, partially disagreeing, and materially interdependent.

## Beats

1. At dawn Jessa verifies return markers and lines, duplicate recording, route ceiling, and mandatory turn signal. Anouk and Kasim leave with incompatible routes and custody expectations; all gear is abandonable.
2. Cold wind crosses mud, weed, pools, and wet channels. They detour around a nursery pool and report reachable stranded-life coordinates, attempting no shelf-wide rescue. Anouk's shortcut enters a pressure-release field; Kasim's late warning sounds proprietary, and a slump damages their probe.
3. Kasim's raised shell-gravel route exposes repeated repairs. Anouk sees the supposed avenue crossing several sediment ages; Kasim recognizes multiple masonry traditions. Together those observations weaken the single-palace reading.
4. At the crown court, stone cells, sluice sockets, layered shell-lime, and emptied building seats reveal tidal gardens and water-mixing basins. Six quarry-mark groups, dismantling scars, and an uphill haul line suggest shared upkeep and planned retreat. The supposed throne is a flow divider; monarchy, flood cause, and other ruins remain unknown.
5. Anouk wants a diagnostic piece; Kasim shows it still keys fragile lining and refuses removal. Kasim wants to follow the haul line; Anouk demonstrates they lack return margin. Each accepts the other's limit. They record context in place.
6. The cloud deck shears early. Jessa cuts their time as downward rain begins. A salt-fouled recorder fails, forcing one record from Anouk's paper sections, Kasim's route annotations, and the surviving camera.
7. They transmit mirrored files and checksums to Jessa and the coastal archive, embargo coordinates, and assign joint discovery credit with separate stratigraphy and route/material responsibility. Both retain copies; neither controls publication or removal alone. Agreement remains practical, not personal.
8. Gas closes the shortcut while rain fills old channels. Anouk identifies ribs above early flow; Kasim finds continuous footing. They abandon the empty sample case and damaged probe, use preplaced lines, and reach high ground before sheet return. End with archive confirmation as all three haul gear clear, without a public thesis.
