# A Penny to Boo

The request puts a maid on her day off. She decides to visit the city, a prince tags along, and he keeps telling people they are on a date. "You decide" and "tagging along" suggest the fun of a prince following a maid's lead for once. The two questions invite both a reason for wanting her company and a reason for declaring it a date in public. I read "to everyone" as an invitation to let the city react, with accumulating mischief and some room for flirtation. How well they know each other, what he means by a date, and what she wants once she finds out are open.

## Provisional direction

First person from Rilla, a palace maid headed to an outdoor puppet show in Saffron Square. The penny seats are where you get to shout back. She's been saving a heckle for the puppet prince's disastrous encounter with a goose.

- Cader catches up at the gate, out of breath. Rilla braces for an errand and is thrown when he asks where she's going and if he can come. She allows him as far as the square, curious what he's after. When the gatekeeper tries to send her back for Cader's cloak, he says they're on a date. The gatekeeper apologizes to her and lets them go. Rilla takes the startling claim for a one-time excuse.
- At the bun stall the seller reaches for the palace ledger. Cader repeats the line with obvious relish. Rilla insists on buying her own bun, while the seller calls to a neighboring stall about the prince and his sweetheart. By the time Rilla gets to the booth, the news has overtaken them. Cader confirms it to the puppeteer, who brings out chairs for the couple and offers a love duet in place of the riding scene. In front of the whole queue he asks Rilla what she thinks would please the prince.
- Rilla declines the chairs and pulls Cader aside. He lets slip the punch line of a joke she told another maid at the palace in his horse's voice. She had no idea he heard it. He wanted to spend the afternoon with her and hoped it might be a date. He liked saying so, especially when it made the gatekeeper take her at her word about her day off. He sees what the chairs have done and offers to go. Rilla offers him a place on the bench if he's game for the show she came to see.
- Rilla pays her penny and calls out to the puppet as the horse. Cader answers as the goose, exposing the detail the city never knew. He took a currant bun on parade, and the bird was after the bun sticking out of his pocket. Rilla drops her rehearsed line and asks the goose if it's taking commissions. They bargain in character over what it would take to make the puppet prince fall off again, and the puppeteer plays the riding scene around them. Rilla finds she's having more fun making the jokes with him than she ever did practicing them alone. When the show ends, she leads him back to the bun stall and asks him to share one. This time she makes the announcement.
