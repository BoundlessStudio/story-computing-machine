# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

## New characters or character facts

- **Irix** is fourteen, deaf and non-speaking, and the child of Talvi and the
  local archdemon Belphegor. Irix uses sign, a pocket slate, expression,
  diagrams, and self-built visual systems; the story establishes no cause for
  their disability and gives them no cure or voice substitute. Irix is an
  exceptional maker who can supply a shaping current locally called living
  magic and a needle of sealing heat from a fingertip. They begin by equating
  reliability with obedience, then learn to ask Knurl and Thimble for consent
  and accept their refusals, alterations, risks, and freely chosen friendship.
- **Belphegor** is Irix's archdemon parent and Talvi's partner. They use
  four-handed sign, have upper and lower eyes, work green fire, and are loving
  but extremely inert. `Archdemon` is only their local title; the story
  establishes no godhood, portfolio, hierarchy, folklore, or authority over
  other beings.
  After signing the wrong corner of Irix's guardian form and later failing to
  wake to support Irix on exhibition morning, Belphegor creates and accepts
  responsibility for the self-imposed couch consequence described below.
- **Talvi** is Irix's damned-soul parent and Belphegor's partner. She is fluent
  in Irix's signing, preserves Irix's childhood diagrams, and is loving but
  catastrophically unreliable at completing tasks. Her dispatch failures make
  Irix haul school equipment alone once, then face a second transport crisis
  without adult help; Irix solves that crisis collaboratively with Knurl and
  Thimble by converting the exhibit chassis into a walking cart. Talvi then
  establishes and owns an adult-managed transport arrangement rather than
  asking Irix to maintain her reminders. The cause, terms, and general meaning
  of her damnation remain unspecified.
- **Knurl** is a living, person-like friend constructed by Irix from cultured
  embermoss and a brass frame. Knurl chooses their own name from a labeled
  knurling tool; prefers precision, symmetry, bitter coal-sugar, and advance
  consent before handling; and communicates with hinged flags, pointing, and
  word tiles. Knurl spots material misalignment, helps redesign the relay and
  walking chassis, freely enters the exhibition, and chooses to straighten the
  cold-air shutter during the furnace incident.
- **Thimble** is a living, person-like friend constructed by Irix from cultured
  embermoss, felt, copper, and three flexible loops. Thimble chooses their own
  name and nests in the original thimble; prefers sweet ash, climbing, bright
  sails, improvisation, and selective touch; and communicates with colored
  panels, gestures, sails, and word tiles. Thimble freely shields evacuating
  students with a sail, is scorched, receives continuous care, keeps the
  original blackened sail beneath a deliberately mismatched patch, and chooses
  to return to school.
- **Ekri** is Irix's classmate. He initially rejects collaboration because he
  treats signed or written communication as an inconvenience. His
  living-current-powered flame-figure apparatus has a rushed feed latch whose
  failure triggers the furnace incident. Ekri follows Irix's visual instruction
  to close the cutoff, admits that he knowingly prioritized his finish, and
  begins restitution by documenting the failure and helping rebuild instead of
  receiving instant friendship.
- **Instructor Bexel**, also addressed as **Bexel**, runs the local exhibition.
  She signs competently but initially tolerates sound-only alerts and an
  inaccessible team-formation practice. During the incident she authorizes
  Irix's projected route and leads the evacuation. Afterward she recognizes
  Irix, Knurl, and Thimble as three collaborators, posts local access and
  personhood reforms, drafts the adult support agreement, and becomes its
  consenting school backup contact.

## New locations

- **Cinder Annex** is a bounded infernal school attended by Irix, Ekri, Knurl,
  and Thimble and staffed by Instructor Bexel. The story establishes it only as
  one local school; it is not presented as the school for all infernal,
  demonic, damned, dead, or constructed children.
- The Annex's **furnace hall** contains iron exhibition tables, floor grates, a
  connected furnace, an east-door evacuation route, a rear service passage, a
  furnace release grate, a feed-line cutoff, and a crooked cold-air shutter.
  These physical relationships enable the exhibition incident and evacuation.

## New factions or cultural facts

- The Cinder Annex holds a three-day annual **Aptitudes Exhibition**. At the
  time of this story, advanced-workshop eligibility requires collaborative
  work, teams lock three days before the exhibition, and a glass scoring seal
  makes the formal exhibition score void if it is broken before judging ends.
  Instructor Bexel can nevertheless recognize demonstrated aptitude and
  collaboration for advanced-workshop purposes after Irix deliberately
  sacrifices the formal score during the rescue.
- Before the furnace incident, the Annex's lesson bell and emergency alarm
  could be sound-only, and its collaboration requirement had no accessible
  adult-supervised team-formation path. A proposed solo accommodation would
  have let Irix exhibit while excluding them from the workshop criterion.
- After the incident, Bexel posts three local reforms: future event alarms must
  include both light and floor signals; team formation must include an
  accessible adult-supervised process; and living collaborators must be
  registered as participants and asked for consent.
- The exhibition record recognizes Irix, Knurl, and Thimble as three
  collaborators rather than one maker and two pieces of equipment. This is a
  local school decision, not a universal legal or ontological ruling about
  constructed people.
- Irix's household and the Cinder Annex use sign, slates, visible lamps, word
  tiles, colored panels, diagrams, and tactile floor signals as meaningful
  communication channels. The story does not establish a universal infernal
  sign language or accessibility standard.
- Talvi, Belphegor, and Bexel enter an adult-owned support agreement with Irix's
  consent. It provides standing conveyance for exhibition and workshop
  mornings, confirmation by an adult dispatcher, backup notice to Bexel,
  Bexel's authority to activate transport after a missed guardian task, and
  adult-to-adult reporting of failures. Irix is not responsible for designing,
  reminding, or maintaining this system.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Embermoss shaping:** In Irix's local craft, apparently dormant cultured
  embermoss is fed coal-sugar or sweet ash and grown through prepared brass,
  copper, and felt structures. Irix supplies a shaping current that their
  lessons call living magic and stops when their wrists tremble. The magic is
  supplied by a living maker; the growth that follows belongs to the culture.
- **Living-agency boundary:** Knurl and Thimble grow, metabolize food, learn,
  prefer, refuse, consent, improvise, suffer injury, maintain relationships,
  and make consequential choices. Their autonomous actions use bodies, tools,
  flags, sails, levers, and mechanisms. They do not independently cast magic.
  Their construction establishes their observed life and personhood in this
  story, but it does not define a universal test for life, minds, souls,
  ownership, or afterlife continuation.
- **Visual relay:** Irix's pressure strips detect vibration, heat, and load;
  receptive plates and a projector render the readings as colored geometry:
  blue for clear, yellow for strain, and red for danger. Knurl's rotated sensor
  spacing and Thimble's staggered sails keep overlapping signals distinct. A
  removable chassis power plate can enlarge the projection across the furnace
  hall. The relay is a communication and safety instrument, not a cure,
  speech/hearing substitute, or device that validates signing.
- **Exhibit and walking chassis:** Jointed supports that hold sensors against
  uneven floors can be turned downward, with the load plate moved forward,
  Thimble's sails balancing the case, and Knurl's weight shifts steering it.
  The resulting six-legged walking chassis transports the exhibit. Breaking
  its glass scoring seal and removing its power plate destroys the formal score
  but enables the emergency projection.
- **Local sealing heat:** Irix can produce a needle of heat from a fingertip
  sufficient to fix a paper hinge. With measured folds, that hinge can swing a
  signature placed in a form's decorated margin into the required guardian box
  without cutting the paper or changing the signed strokes. No broader rule for
  signatures, names, consent, or binding is established.
- **Ekri's flame-figure apparatus:** Living current pressed into a copper drum
  can be shaped into visible flame figures over the furnace mouth and produce
  strong physical pulses. In this incident, extra input through a knowingly
  rushed two-tooth latch opens the feed door, admits black ember-grain, raises
  pressure, and threatens ember spray through the release grate. Pulling the
  cutoff folds the flame figure into the furnace; opening the cold-air shutter
  then reduces the pressure.
- **Belphegor's couch consequence:** Belphegor can use their own green fire to
  stitch a narrow local connection between the household deadline slate and
  the couch. At a marked deadline the couch snaps upright and deposits
  Belphegor on their feet. Belphegor explicitly owns its consequence and
  maintenance. The mechanism is self-imposed furniture work, not universal
  contract magic, name-power, punishment, or authority over others.
- **Costs and vulnerabilities:** Shaping embermoss taxes Irix physically;
  Knurl's frame can be endangered by a loose bracket; Thimble's moss, felt, and
  sails can be scorched and require cooling and repair; and the relay's widest
  projection consumes the exhibit's sealed power plate. No universal cost rule
  is proposed.

## Timeline events

- **Undated Cinder Annex exhibition incident:** Three days after Bexel announces
  the collaborative-entry requirement, Irix arrives with Knurl and Thimble and
  demonstrates their jointly redesigned visual relay. Ekri's rushed furnace
  latch fails during his final flourish. Irix breaks the scoring seal to power
  a hall-scale evacuation map; Bexel reroutes students; Ekri closes the cutoff;
  Knurl opens the cold-air shutter; and Thimble shields the last students from
  ember spray, suffering a scorched sail. The hall clears without a stated
  human or construct death.
- **Immediate aftermath:** Bexel voids the formal exhibition score but credits
  Irix, Knurl, and Thimble with advanced-workshop aptitude and collaboration.
  Ekri accepts repair work, the Annex posts its three reforms, and Talvi,
  Belphegor, and Bexel establish the support agreement. On the following
  morning, the standing conveyance arrives and Knurl and Thimble independently
  choose to return to school with Irix.
- **Placement in the shared timeline:** None established. The event's era and
  relation to other stories remain unknown.

## New glossary terms or aliases

- **Aptitudes Exhibition:** The Cinder Annex's annual three-day student
  exhibition, locally tied to advanced-workshop eligibility.
- **Embermoss:** A cultured, food-metabolizing moss used by Irix as the living
  growth medium for Knurl and Thimble.
- **Coal-sugar / sweet ash:** Foods accepted by the embermoss constructs;
  Knurl prefers bitter coal-sugar and Thimble prefers sweet ash.
- **Living magic / living current:** Viewpoint-culture terms for shaping power
  supplied by living agents such as Irix or Ekri. They are not proposed as
  objective or universal categories.
- **Visual relay:** Irix's pressure-strip, plate, sail, and projection system
  for displaying vibration, heat, load, strain, and routes as colored light.
- **Scoring seal:** The exhibition's glass seal over an entry's chassis hinge;
  breaking it before judging ends voids that entry's formal score.
- **Walking chassis:** The collaboration-modified exhibit chassis whose jointed
  supports, load plate, sails, and weight shifts let it walk and carry the
  project.
- New aliases: None. The signed renderings in the story refer to the same
  registered identities and do not create separate sign-name glosses.

## Possible conflicts or retcons

- Known contradiction with authoritative universe notes: None. The story keeps
  magic dependent on living agency, treats personhood as morally consequential,
  does not call Belphegor a god, and does not universalize local infernal
  culture or mechanisms.
- Retcons to existing canon: None.
- Unresolved by design: whether Hell is a place, region, institution, cultural
  description, or something else; whether damnation is moral, legal, cultural,
  residential, or another status; and whether this family or school typifies
  any broader afterlife community.
- Unresolved by design: demonic and archdemonic hierarchy, bodies, powers,
  lifespans, reproduction, inheritance, authority, and folklore beyond this
  local Belphegor and family.
- Unresolved by design: the origin of Irix's deafness and non-speaking status,
  their metaphysical category, and the family's biological, legal, or
  metaphysical formation.
- Unresolved by design: whether embermoss ordinarily becomes living or
  person-like, what gives Knurl and Thimble their minds, whether they have
  souls, how broadly the craft works, whether it can create other people, and
  what relationship the friends may eventually have to the afterlife.
- Unresolved by design: the story's era, the location of the Cinder Annex
  relative to the physical world, and whether any portal or crossing connects
  it to another setting. No portal, copy, branch, reconstructed identity, or
  universal name-power appears.

## Recommended promotions

- Promote only a bounded, story-scale record of Irix, Talvi, this local version
  of Belphegor, Knurl, Thimble, Ekri, and Instructor Bexel, including their
  relationships, communication forms, demonstrated choices, and consequences.
- Promote the Cinder Annex as one local infernal school; its Aptitudes
  Exhibition and furnace-hall incident; and the three resulting access and
  living-collaborator reforms.
- Promote Irix's embermoss craft, sealing heat, visual relay, walking chassis,
  the demonstrated living-agency boundary, and their local costs without
  generalizing any mechanism into a universe-wide category.
- Promote the adult support agreement and Belphegor's self-imposed couch
  consequence only as family-scale accommodations with named owners and
  maintenance responsibilities.
- Do not promote claims about universal Hell, damnation, demon hierarchy,
  demonic parenthood, constructed souls, magic taxonomy, accessibility
  standards, portals, or timeline placement.

These are proposals for possible promotion only. This delta is not canon, and
no fact in it becomes authoritative without explicit user approval and the
separate canon-maintenance workflow.
