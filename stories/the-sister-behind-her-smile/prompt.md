# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] Your sister was an emotional, spoiled, conniving noble lady, yet deep down you still loved her. However, one day she suddenly changes, becoming much kinder. While the thought of her improving herself makes you happy, you can't help but feel there's something...off about her.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-18
- Avoid: none specified
