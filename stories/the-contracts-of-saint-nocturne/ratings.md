# Rating

- **Rating:** Teen
- **Content notes:** Non-graphic firearm combat, burns and other battle injuries; supernatural coercion during sleep, including a frightened child compelled to sing; threats of predation and abduction; inherited obligations and lifelong confinement; a brief obscene gesture.
- **Reason:** The finished prose and selected cover contain sustained supernatural menace and non-graphic violence that may be unsuitable for readers under thirteen. The cover depicts armed adult custodians and an unsettling six-eyed being without graphic injury. The rating reflects the intensity of the combat and coercion, rather than the story's ethical themes.
