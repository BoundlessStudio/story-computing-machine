# Outline

## Story

- Proposed reader-facing title: *The Inheritance Remembers*.
- Premise and central promise: The afternoon after Iven Darrow's funeral, her granddaughter Senra is packing food from the family house when Senra's mother, Meri, forgets a conversation they had minutes ago. The loss advances backward through every shared memory until Meri no longer recognizes Senra. Fulfill the prompt privately: the inherited Darrow curse has activated, its exact effect becomes legible before sunset, and Senra must face a living mother for whom their relationship has become unsupported testimony.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Senra has spent the funeral being the reliable daughter, remembering logistics and what Meri cannot bear to handle. She wants Meri to keep knowing her, but answers doubt with increasingly intimate proof. As recognition recedes, those details feel to Meri like access a stranger should not have. Senra's attempt to preserve their relationship unilaterally makes her more frightening.
- Counterforce or complication: No antagonist controls the loss. Meri retains judgment and event facts while losing Senra-specific autobiographical and relational memory from newest to oldest. Records and unrelated witnesses remain ordinary evidence, but cannot manufacture recognition. At dusk the backward erasure becomes complete. Blood relatives can form new memories of Senra afterward, but nothing restores what was removed.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close first-person past through Senra, attentive to domestic sequence and familiar shorthand. She cannot enter Meri's memory or treat one blank as proof. Keep Iven present only through objects, records, and Senra's recollections; no ghost, stored person, inherited omniscience, or complete family past appears.
- Governing movement and time shape: One narrowing afternoon in and around the unnamed family house. Each attempted proof reaches an older layer just as it becomes inaccessible: funeral logistics, recent adult care, adolescence, then childhood. Movement contracts from rooms full of belongings to the front threshold. The decisive action is Senra leaving when Meri asks, not a cure or family-wide conversion. Stop after nightfall permits one bounded future contact; no research montage, institution, or later cure.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: This local living bloodline mechanism passes dormant from a dead bearer to one descendant. Activation erases that bearer from every living blood relative's autobiographical memory, newest first, while leaving the bearer's identity and memory intact. Unrelated people and material records are unaffected; new post-activation memories can form. Its creator, selection criterion, dormant interval, and possible interruption remain unknown.
- Dialogue pressure: Senra wants Meri to recognize her before the loss reaches childhood and uses intimate shorthand as proof; she withholds how close she is to panic. Meri wants physical safety and claims she can verify without counterfeiting affection; she withholds the speed of the gaps. Senra's diction is sensory and associative, Meri's sequential and inventory-minded.

## Beats

1. Meri forgets a just-finished exchange while Senra clears the funeral table, then repeats a practical question without recognizing the repetition. Senra first blames exhaustion.
2. A phone thread remains visible, but Meri remembers arranging each task alone. An unrelated funeral volunteer confirms Senra was present; Meri accepts the fact without regaining the memory.
3. Senra tests progressively older shared details. Meri still knows the surrounding events but loses Senra's place in them as daylight retreats across the rooms. Senra's increasingly private prompts alarm her.
4. Iven's papers contain photographs of unrecognized Darrow adults and warnings copied without explanation. One prior bearer's note describes the backward order and post-dark limit; ongoing losses support it without making the note automatic truth.
5. Senra realizes the family did not merely forget an old definition: earlier bearers were removed from the only relatives expected to transmit it. She records the operative observations without claiming to know the curse's origin or purpose.
6. Meri forgets Senra's adolescence, then a documented childhood injury. Senra tries one last intimate memory as a key; Meri responds as a frightened homeowner confronted by a stranger with family secrets.
7. At the threshold, Meri asks Senra to leave while she verifies the documents. Senra gives up the house key and goes before force or outside authority becomes necessary, accepting that remaining recognizable cannot be extracted from Meri by evidence.
8. After dark, Meri does not recover the past. Because new memory now holds, she can remember the short, bounded contact she chooses with Senra that night. End with tomorrow's practical meeting uncertain rather than an instantly rebuilt relationship.

## Continuity

- Canon used: An independent contemporary-like entry point in the deep-time world. Reality remains coherent while family knowledge is incomplete. Only living agency uses magic, so the curse resides in the lineage rather than papers, photographs, or house. Records retain ordinary evidentiary force, and altered memory does not erase personhood.
- Boundaries and unknowns: This event establishes only the Darrow curse's witnessed activation and effects. It creates no universal law for blood, inheritance, forgetting, names, photographs, dusk, or family bonds, and no connection to the Fenwick watch, Deepmarket memory tolls, Sablewake's memory cost, Bellweather severance, or another memory mechanism. The curse's origin, reason, choice of Senra, prior activation count, dormant duration, and removability remain unresolved.
