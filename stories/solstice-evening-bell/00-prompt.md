# Prompt contract

## Verbatim writing prompt

> [WP] Solstice Evening Bell, First Monarch and queen of all magical girls, is the only being on Earth under the "ultrahuman" categorization. The invasion force is easily into the six-digit range. She stands alone. Write me a fight scene.

## Story controls

- Working title: Solstice Evening Bell
- Target length: 2,800–3,800 words
- POV: Close third person, centered on Solstice Evening Bell
- Tense: Past
- Tone and genre: Kinetic, defiant, and costly; epic magical-girl science fantasy
- Audience/content rating: Teen / PG-13; large-scale fantasy violence without gore
- Required elements: Solstice Evening Bell; the titles First Monarch and queen
  of all magical girls; her unique Earth classification as `ultrahuman`; an
  invading force clearly numbering at least 100,000; no allied combatants; a
  sustained, spatially intelligible fight whose choices reveal character; a
  decisive ending that resolves whether she can hold Earth; twelve crown
  chimes created through the willing, fatal final spells of the twelve most
  powerful magical girls then living; each chime's destruction carries the
  weight of destroying that woman's last surviving work; Solstice remains
  Earth's sole classified `ultrahuman` after the crown is gone.
- Prohibited elements: Surprise reinforcements, a hidden second ultrahuman,
  reducing the invasion to a vague background statistic, effortless victory,
  a last-minute power with no setup, mass civilian gore, trapped donor souls,
  treating the donors as interchangeable batteries, or ending Solstice's
  `ultrahuman` classification.

## Assumptions

- “She stands alone” means Solstice is the sole defender who fights the
  invasion. Remote civilian warnings or passive infrastructure may exist, but
  nobody else attacks, shields, heals, or materially augments her.
- The twelve donors died centuries before the battle. Their nonliving chimes
  are inherited equipment, cannot choose or act, and require Solstice's living
  agency. Their prior sacrifice is not a present ally or reinforcement.
- The donors' souls are not imprisoned in the crown. Each chime preserves only
  a final spell freely created while its donor was alive; destroying it erases
  that last surviving work, not the person or her afterlife.
- The prompt supplies new candidate-story facts, not pre-existing canon.
  Canon research must determine whether those facts conflict with authoritative
  universe notes; any compatible worldbuilding remains proposed until promotion.
- “Six-digit range” will be made concrete on the page as a count above 100,000.
- Solstice is an adult sovereign. “Magical girls” is an in-world tradition and
  title, not a statement that all members are minors.
- The scene may use brief memories or internal reflections, but the story's
  continuous present action is the battle.

## Completion tests

- The result is a complete short story, not a synopsis or chapter fragment.
- The central promise of the prompt is dramatized and resolved.
- The invasion count, battle geography, attrition, costs, and turning points
  remain legible through the full action sequence.
- Solstice wins or loses through an established capability and an intelligible
  tactical choice; the ending pays off her monarchy and her solitude.
- The ending confirms that the crown's techniques and all twelve donated final
  spells are permanently gone while Solstice remains Earth's only classified
  `ultrahuman`.
- The story passes canon and continuity review.
- Every character-facing name and alias is checked against `stories/NAMES.md`;
  any deliberate reuse has a documented narrative reason.
