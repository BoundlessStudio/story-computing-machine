# Proposed canon delta

This file proposes reusable shared-universe facts. It is not canon until the
user explicitly approves promotion and the facts are added to `universe/`.

## New characters or character facts

- **Solstice Evening Bell (proposed):** An adult sovereign whose royal hearing
  has endured for centuries. Her present living agency is required to wake and
  shape the nonliving crown, while its twelve chimes store final spells created
  by twelve living donors at the time of their deaths. Before and after the
  battle, Solstice is the only being on Earth entered in the era-local
  administrative category `ultrahuman`; her innate force can exceed a
  strategic weapon's while she retains the will and control to choose where it
  falls. The category does not mean she is Earth's only extraordinary being.
  - **Offices:** Solstice is the **First Monarch** and the **queen of all
    magical girls**. Formerly independent magical-girl circles created the
    monarchy and permitted one of their own to command the rest after eleven
    days of argument. There had been no monarch before Solstice and no
    successor after the circles faded. By the time of the invasion, history
    has left her with no living subjects.
  - **Twelve crown donors:** On the twelfth morning of the coronation dispute,
    the twelve most powerful magical girls then living willingly created the
    twelve orbiting chimes. Each adult donor drove her transformation past the
    point her body could survive and freely placed its final spell into silver.
    Their souls and minds are not stored in the crown; only their nonliving,
    magically charged final works remain. Solstice personally remembers each
    woman and the compact records explicit consent.
  - **Battle outcome:** Solstice defeats the invasion as Earth's sole
    combatant. Royal hearing ends when she sacrifices the twelfth and last
    orbiting tone; she then permanently destroys the crown's central resonator,
    its techniques, and the last residues of all twelve donated spells. Her
    innate output remains above the `ultrahuman` threshold, so the public
    exclusion-zone display still reports one. Her identity, classification,
    and historical offices endure.

## New locations

- **Unnamed western bay (proposed):** The battle site is an evacuated bay in
  the shared world's Earth era. Its reusable battle geography consists of an
  offshore sea shelf, a northern and southern breakwater at the bay mouth,
  empty harbor roads, and an eastern ridge separating the coast from inhabited
  land.
- **Bay-to-rupture battlespace (proposed):** A stationary rupture opens twenty
  kilometers above the western bay. The invasion descends from the rupture
  through cloud, the tropopause, and an autumn storm toward eight landing
  routes: two on the sea shelf, two on the breakwaters, and four along the
  eastern ridge. No proper local place name, country, city, literal real-world
  geography, or permanent portal site is established.

## New factions or cultural facts

- **Historical magical-girl circles (proposed):** Independent circles argued
  for eleven days before permitting Solstice, one of their own, to command the
  rest through a command compact. Their agreement made her the first monarch
  within this bounded tradition. The circles later faded, leaving no successor
  and no living subjects identified by the story. The story does not establish
  a large, public, present-day magical-girl population or a universal monarchy.
- **Coronation compact (proposed):** The circles' compact binds the operation
  of Solstice's crown and ends with the clause, “What the crown joins, the
  monarch may release.” After eleven days of debate, the twelve most powerful
  magical girls then living each gave explicit consent to a fatal final
  transformation that made one chime; their freely made spells, not their
  souls, were entrusted to Solstice. The circles feared that monarchy might
  surrender their power to one will, and Solstice promised that the crown would
  never own what they entrusted to it. This is a specific technical and
  political compact, not universal name-power, title-power, sacrifice-powered
  magic, or contract magic.
- **Hazard administration (proposed):** Unnamed public hazard bureaus maintain
  an era-local `ultrahuman` classification and exclusion-zone displays. The
  board shown in the story passively reports one classified asset both before
  and after the battle. It measures Solstice's innate force and control rather
  than possession of the crown and provides no targeting, defense, healing,
  tactical advice, or power.
- **Evacuation and nonintervention (proposed):** Solstice orders conventional
  batteries and aircraft behind the eastern ridge before the rupture opens.
  Evacuation arrows continue to mark the empty harbor roads, but no civilian,
  conventional force, observer, or public instrument materially assists her
  during the battle.

## New rules, capabilities, or costs

- **Solstice's thirteen-tone crown (proposed):** The crown consists of twelve
  orbiting silver chimes and a thumb-sized central resonator against Solstice's
  sternum. It is nonliving equipment. Each chime stores one final spell freely
  made by one of twelve living donors through a transformation their bodies did
  not survive. The donors' souls and consciousnesses passed on and are neither
  contained nor recalled. Solstice's living agency is required to wake, shape,
  and direct the stored magic; the crown cannot choose, act, use magic, or even
  make a spark independently.
- **Intact central resonator (proposed):** After the twelfth orbiting tone burns
  and royal hearing vanishes, the intact central resonator can still armor
  Solstice's retreat and, given time, shape smaller blows. It cannot defeat the
  synchronized 4,096-frame bridgehead before the next wave arrives. Solstice's
  innate output qualifies as `ultrahuman` independently of whether the
  resonator survives.
- **Royal hearing (proposed):** While orbiting chimes remain, Solstice can
  sense mass, position, and coordination-mesh pulses across the
  bay-to-rupture battlespace. She uses this faculty—not public instruments or
  an ally—to obtain the exact invasion count and track attrition. False mesh
  pulses can deceive it, each burnt frequency leaves a painful absence in it,
  and the faculty vanishes permanently when the last orbiting tone is lost.
- **Bell-step (proposed):** Solstice can move almost instantly along a straight
  line between two sounding chimes. A step cannot curve, so every lost chime
  reduces her routes: a broad lattice becomes a triangle, then one line, then
  no movement path.
- **Edict planes (proposed):** Two sounding chimes can hold a thin, fixed plane
  of destructive resonance between them. A plane destroys nonliving frames
  that cross it but cannot chase a target. Solstice therefore uses her own
  movement, fixed geography, gravity, updrafts, downdrafts, and the host's
  landing objectives to herd frames through the planes. The storm obeys
  ordinary physical laws rather than her magic.
- **Full chord and adaptive limit (proposed):** Aligning the chimes lets
  Solstice deliver a large resonant chord through a synchronized structure.
  Its first use destroys 32,768 frames by propagating through their mesh. The
  surviving mesh models its phase, interval, and delay, making a repeated
  chord largely ineffective and revealing the pattern of her power.
- **Burning a tone (proposed):** Solstice can overload a chime to expand an
  edict from a thin plane into a much broader destructive blade. The chime
  shatters; its donor's stable final spell is permanently erased; and its
  frequency is torn from Solstice's crown-bound neural pathways. The loss
  causes severe pain and cannot be healed or rebuilt. It does not kill or erase
  the donor again because no soul or mind is present. Broken chimes remain dead
  equipment but briefly retain a dying magical residue awakened through
  Solstice's living force.
- **Central release (proposed):** When the twelve burnt chime trails and
  Solstice's living body form a circuit through the invasion lattice, she can
  fulfill the compact's release clause by crushing the central resonator. The
  resulting destructive inverse of the full chord travels through her nerves,
  the chime residue, all 4,096 synchronized surviving frames, and their
  low-latency signal back to the rupture. Royal hearing has already ended with
  the twelfth orbiting tone. The release permanently tears away the central
  tone, consumes the twelve dying residues, disables the entire remaining host,
  and seals the rupture. It permanently ends the crown, royal hearing,
  bell-step, edict planes, and full chord, but does not reduce Solstice's innate
  force below the `ultrahuman` threshold.
- **Permanent crown cost (proposed):** The crown has no recharge, replacement,
  recovery, or healing mechanism in the story. All thirteen crown tones and all
  twelve donors' final works are gone at the end. Solstice retains her
  unharmonized innate power and `ultrahuman` classification but loses the
  crown's precision and every demonstrated crown technique. Pain, injury,
  sovereignty, willingness, and sacrifice do not operate as universal fuels;
  these consequences arise from this specific donation and crown mechanism.
- **Invasion frames (proposed):** The invasion host contains exactly 131,072
  roughly human-sized, nonliving autonomous technological shells with folding
  vanes. They use needle projectiles, high-speed collision attacks, and a
  low-latency coordination mesh. They are neither magical actors nor
  established persons, and the story describes them as destroyed, broken,
  disabled, or dark rather than dead.
- **Adaptive mesh (proposed):** The host's mesh redistributes the eight opening
  wedges into schools of sixty-four, learns resonant patterns after exposure,
  generates false mass pulses, coordinates sacrificial attacks on the crown,
  and later assembles the survivors into a lattice capable of stabilizing the
  rupture. Its synchronization makes it vulnerable to Solstice's resonance and
  finally conducts the central release back through the rupture.

## Timeline events

- **Undated western-bay invasion (proposed):** After the bay is evacuated and
  conventional forces withdraw, a rupture opens twenty kilometers above it.
  Exactly 131,072 frames emerge as eight wedges of 16,384. Solstice fights
  alone from the rupture to the two breakwaters and prevents all eight landing
  routes from becoming a bridgehead.
- **Exact attrition sequence (proposed):** The full chord destroys 32,768
  frames, leaving **98,304**. A repeated full chord then breaks fewer than one
  hundred additional frames; edict corridors and the first three burnt tones
  complete the reduction to **49,152** at the tropopause. Six more burnt
  tones, physical storm effects, and constrained landing geometry reduce it
  to **8,192**, organized as eight columns of 1,024. The final three orbiting
  tones destroy four columns and force the other four together, leaving
  **4,096**. The central release disables those remaining frames, bringing the
  count to **zero** and closing the rupture.
- **Immediate aftermath (proposed):** Dawn reaches an intact eastern ridge.
  No bridgehead remains, no second wave crosses the closed rupture, and Earth
  is held. Solstice survives with physical injuries but without any crown tone
  or royal hearing. Her raw power remains immense, alive, and controllable; the
  public display continues to report one `ultrahuman` asset in the exclusion
  zone.
- **Placement in the shared timeline (proposed):** The public technology,
  low-magic conditions, Earth label, and surviving anomaly place the event in
  the modern-like Earth era. Its exact civil date, Galactic Cycle coordinate,
  and relationship to other modern-like-era stories remain unknown. The event
  precedes the later Glass-Sea era and leaves the shared physical world intact
  and able to reach it.

## New glossary terms or aliases

- **`ultrahuman` (proposed):** A lowercase, era-local administrative category
  for a living individual who can exceed a strategic weapon's force while
  retaining the ability to choose where that force falls. Before and after this
  battle, Solstice is Earth's only entry in that specific category. Her innate
  force and agency satisfy it independently of the crown. The term is not an
  objective cosmological rank and does not mean “only magical,” “only
  superhuman,” or “only extraordinary being.”
- **Royal hearing (proposed):** Solstice's crown-mediated sensing of mass,
  position, and mesh pulses within the local battlespace.
- **Bell-step / bell-stepped (proposed):** Solstice's straight-line movement
  between sounding crown chimes.
- **Edict plane (proposed):** A fixed plane of destructive resonance held
  between two sounding chimes.
- **Full chord (proposed):** A large-volume resonant attack produced by aligned
  crown chimes and most effective against synchronized structures.
- **Central resonator (proposed):** The thirteenth crown focus against
  Solstice's sternum and the only crown tone originating with her; breaking it
  performs the irreversible central release without ending her innate
  `ultrahuman` output.
- **Rupture (proposed local usage):** The stationary seam through which this
  technological host invades and to which its mesh maintains a low-latency
  signal. Its origin and crossing system are unknown, and no alias or
  relationship to the Glass Gate is proposed.
- **New personal aliases:** None beyond Solstice's registered regnal titles.

## Possible conflicts or retcons

- Known contradiction with authoritative universe notes: None.
- Retcons to existing canon: None.
- **Continuity boundary — Earth:** This is the shared world's modern-like Earth
  era, not an assertion that the setting is literal real-world Earth. The event
  preserves the physical world, its inhabited regions, and its capacity to
  reach the later Glass-Sea era.
- **Continuity boundary — classification:** `ultrahuman` belongs to unnamed
  era-local hazard administration. Solstice's unique entry does not imply a
  universal authority, a complete census of extraordinary beings, or a
  cosmological category.
- **Continuity boundary — living agency:** Twelve living donors created and
  stored the chime spells through their fatal final transformations; during the
  battle, Solstice supplies the present living agency that wakes and directs
  them. Her crown cannot choose, act, or use magic independently. The invasion
  frames and mesh are nonliving technological systems and do not independently
  use magic.
- **Continuity boundary — personhood and afterlife:** The donors' willingness
  is explicit and morally consequential. Their minds and souls are not copied,
  trapped, summoned, erased, or killed again when a chime breaks. The universal
  underlying afterlife remains untouched; only each woman's nonliving final
  spell and surviving work are destroyed.
- **Continuity boundary — titles and divinity:** First Monarch and queen of all
  magical girls are offices created by a bounded historical compact. They
  establish neither godhood nor worship and confer no universal authority
  beyond the faded circle tradition.
- **Continuity boundary — crossing systems and imagery:** The rupture is a
  local, unexplained crossing. It is not the Glass Gate, and the story's bell,
  crown, silver, solstice, storm, seam, or release imagery does not imply a
  connection to another artifact, deity, person, faction, or mechanism.
- Unresolved by design: Solstice's birth, species, exact age, source of
  longevity, life before the circles, the crown's maker and material history,
  and the detailed capabilities and limits of her unharmonized innate power
  after losing the thirteen crown tones. Her continued life and
  `ultrahuman` classification are established rather than unresolved.
- Unresolved by design: the circles' names, size, geographic scope, individual
  members, reasons for forming or fading, and whether any magical-girl
  traditions exist elsewhere.
- Unresolved by design: the hazard bureaus' names, jurisdictions, measurement
  technology, and later treatment of Solstice's classification.
- Unresolved by design: the invaders' builders, origin, motive, destination,
  broader forces, and crossing technology; whether a later attack occurs; and
  whether any frame or debris is recovered.
- Unresolved by design: the bay's name and exact geography, the event's date,
  its Galactic Cycle coordinate, the extent of public knowledge, and its
  relationship to other stories.
