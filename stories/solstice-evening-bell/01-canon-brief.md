# Canon brief

## Hard constraints

- The prompt establishes candidate-story requirements, not existing setting
  facts. Only `LOCKED` and `CANON` universe entries, followed by stories whose
  index row says canon `yes`, are authoritative. No story in
  `stories/INDEX.md` is currently canon `yes`, so no finalized story adds
  binding facts here. (`universe/README.md` — “Authority order”;
  `stories/INDEX.md` — “Story index”)
- Earth, Ravel, and the Glass-Sea world are era-bound names for one persisting
  physical world, not separate planets or planes. If this battle is in the
  modern-like Earth era, the world must remain capable of reaching the much
  later Glass-Sea era; even a loss cannot annihilate the planet or make that
  established future impossible. (`universe/premise.md` — “The deep-time
  shared world”; `universe/locations.md` — “The world across eras”;
  `universe/timeline.md` — “Established era order”)
- “Earth” does not establish literal real-world Earth. The modern-like era
  resembles contemporary Earth, but its geography, history, calendars, and
  Galactic Cycle coordinate are unresolved. (`universe/glossary.md` —
  “Modern-like era”; `universe/timeline.md` — “Universal chronology”)
- Magic, technology, and divinity are cultural labels, not objective
  cosmological categories. “Magical girl” can be an in-world tradition, but
  Solstice's abilities need a coherent local mechanism rather than a universal
  magical-girl ruleset. (`universe/rules.md` — “System categories”;
  `universe/glossary.md` — “Magic, technology, and divinity”)
- Only life can use magic. Nonliving weapons, armor, ships, or infrastructure
  may store, focus, transmit, or shape magic supplied through living agency,
  but cannot independently be magic users unless established as alive. This
  applies to Solstice's equipment and to invasion machines.
  (`universe/rules.md` — “Living agency and magic”)
- Neither sacrifice, pain, desire, nor another cost is a universal power fuel.
  Because the prompt requires a costly fight, Solstice's costs, limits,
  recovery, and failure conditions must belong to her specific system and be
  set up before their decisive use. (`universe/rules.md` — “Costs, desire, and
  local mechanisms”)
- “First Monarch,” “queen,” and `ultrahuman` do not make Solstice a god. If the
  prose identifies her literally as a god, she must satisfy the continuing
  conjunction of worship, power, and authority; belief or title alone is
  insufficient. (`universe/rules.md` — “Divinity”)
- No universal hidden authority regulates extraordinary beings in every era.
  Any entity that defines or administers `ultrahuman` must be local to this
  era, culture, or institution unless later canon establishes wider scope.
  (`universe/premise.md` — “Knowledge and connection”)
- Names are normally symbolic and socially powerful, not inherently magical.
  Solstice's full name and regnal titles may have literal power only if this
  story defines a specific local binding mechanism. (`universe/rules.md` —
  “Names and identity binding”)
- Large-scale violence is allowed but must remain non-graphic and centered on
  character and consequence rather than suffering as spectacle. The prompt's
  PG-13/no-gore limit is consistent with the universe ceiling.
  (`universe/style-guide.md` — “Audience and content boundaries”;
  `stories/solstice-evening-bell/00-prompt.md` — “Story controls”)

## Useful established context

- The modern-like era is a low-magic trough after earlier ages of technology
  and magic and before later ages in which gods rise again. Ordinary life
  treats magic as myth or legend, while rare relic anomalies can survive. This
  can support Solstice as a singular exception, inherited officeholder, or
  bearer of a surviving local system. (`universe/timeline.md` — “Established
  era order”; `universe/glossary.md` — “Modern-like era”)
- Independent systems may interact, hybridize, imitate one another, or share
  ancestry; magic may reproduce or combine with effects another culture calls
  technology. The battle can therefore use science-fantasy interactions
  without giving Solstice and the invaders one universal origin.
  (`universe/rules.md` — “System categories”)
- Civilizational catastrophes may result from cosmic events, natural causes,
  institutions, or individuals, but collapse is not a predestined or
  synchronized cycle. The invasion can be a particular historical crisis.
  (`universe/premise.md` — “The deep-time shared world”)
- Cultures can hold incomplete or incorrect models of reality. An official
  `ultrahuman` assessment may be accurate within declared criteria without
  becoming the narrator's taxonomy for every power system.
  (`universe/premise.md` — “Knowledge and connection”)
- The project permits epic scale and magical-girl science fantasy; stories are
  joined by coherent world rules rather than a mandatory mood, scale, or
  awareness of deep history. (`universe/style-guide.md` — “Tonal range, scale,
  and connection”)
- The authoritative character and faction files contain no established
  Solstice, magical-girl institution, `ultrahuman` authority, or invasion
  faction. This absence is an unknown, not evidence that none can exist.
  (`universe/characters.md` — “Recurring characters”;
  `universe/factions.md` — “Factions and cultures”)

## Conflicts or ambiguity

- **Contradictions:** No direct contradiction between the prompt contract and
  current authoritative canon was found. No canon ruling is required before
  planning.
- **Low-magic Earth versus “all magical girls”:** If the battle occurs in the
  modern-like era, a large, public, routinely active global population of
  magical girls would strain the established low-magic trough in which ordinary
  life treats magic as legend. The title “queen of all magical girls” is
  required, but the prompt does not make that population numerous,
  contemporary, public, Earthbound, or still active. Define a narrow compatible
  scope instead of silently overturning the era. (`universe/timeline.md` —
  “Established era order”; `stories/solstice-evening-bell/00-prompt.md` —
  “Verbatim writing prompt”)
- **Classification scope:** “The only being on Earth under the `ultrahuman`
  categorization” establishes a unique classification, not Earth's only
  magical, superpowered, divine, nonhuman, or dangerous being. Its criteria,
  issuer, reliability, jurisdiction, and treatment of the invaders are
  unspecified. Preserve that narrow claim. (`universe/premise.md` — “Knowledge
  and connection”)
- **Earth's identity:** The prompt's Earth is compatible with the modern-like
  label but does not import literal present-day nations, cities, dates,
  technology, or orbital infrastructure. Those would be candidate inventions.
  (`universe/locations.md` — “The world across eras”;
  `universe/glossary.md` — “Modern-like era”)
- **Two titles:** Canon does not say what Solstice was first to rule, whether
  either title is hereditary or earned, whether “queen” is political, ritual,
  martial, or personal authority, or what “all” includes. Their relationship
  must be defined locally and consistently.
- **Name versus office:** It is unknown whether “Solstice Evening Bell” is a
  birth name, transformation identity, regnal name, translated epithet, or a
  combination. Any answer is new story lore and cannot produce automatic
  name-power. (`universe/rules.md` — “Names and identity binding”)
- **Invasion origin:** Other dimensions or timelines may exist but are not
  required, and crossing rules are system-specific. An extraterrestrial,
  extradimensional, time-crossing, or same-world origin is possible only as a
  newly defined local mechanism. It must not be linked to the Glass Gate or
  another system through resemblance alone. (`universe/premise.md` — “The
  deep-time shared world”; `universe/rules.md` — “Portals and crossing systems”
  and “Artifacts, motifs, and folklore”)

## Unknowns

- Solstice's origin, species, ordinary identity, relationships, reign,
  succession, transformation process, abilities, equipment, limits, wounds,
  and reason for fighting alone are **Unknown**. Only her adult status is a
  prompt assumption.
- The magical-girl tradition's origin, membership, era, visibility,
  governance, powers, age demographics, relationship to Solstice, and whether
  any members remain alive are **Unknown**.
- The definitions and recognizing authorities of “First Monarch,” “queen of
  all magical girls,” and `ultrahuman` are **Unknown**. `Ultrahuman` is not an
  established cosmological taxonomy.
- The battle's exact era, Galactic Cycle, civil date, country, city, terrain,
  altitude, civilian situation, and relationship to any other story are
  **Unknown**.
- The invasion force's origin, name, species or construction, motive, command
  structure, exact count, weapons, magic status, transport, formation, and
  intended fate for Earth are **Unknown**. The contract establishes only a
  clearly six-digit force and requires a count above 100,000.
- Whether the invaders are living persons, constructed minds, remote devices,
  copies, or a mixture is **Unknown**. Their ontology and moral status must be
  coherent if consequential.
- Public knowledge of magic, Solstice, the invasion, and `ultrahuman` is
  **Unknown**.
- Any warning network, evacuation system, automated infrastructure, observer,
  and reason it cannot fight are **Unknown**. Passive systems are permitted by
  the prompt but cannot attack, shield, heal, or materially augment Solstice.
- The outcome and long-term consequences are **Unknown**. Later physical-world
  continuity is established if this is the modern-like era, but canon does not
  decide whether Solstice holds a territory, prevents occupation, survives, or
  preserves the current civilization.

## Safe invention space

- Define `ultrahuman` as a narrow, era-local administrative, military,
  scientific, or cultural category with explicit criteria. Its uniqueness
  should apply to classification, not expand into a universal claim about all
  extraordinary beings.
- Create one coherent local power system for Solstice, with a small legible
  capability set, targeting and range rules, operational limits, and
  consequences. Seed every decisive application before payoff. Equipment may
  combine magic and technology subject to living agency.
- Establish a bounded local history for “First Monarch” and “queen of all
  magical girls,” plus a compatible reason Solstice is the sole combatant. If
  “all” is legal, honorific, historical, or restricted to a tradition, make
  that scope clear rather than evasive.
- Invent the battle site, approach vector, objective, defensive geometry,
  environmental hazards, and passive warning or evacuation infrastructure.
  Keep real-world resemblance as texture, not literal historical identity.
- Invent the invasion faction, units, exact count above 100,000, command
  structure, weapons, vulnerabilities, and arrival mechanism. Any crossing
  should be independent of the Glass Gate unless future approved canon says
  otherwise.
- A relic from an earlier high-magic/high-technology age can explain Solstice's
  rarity, but keep its origin local or uncertain. Do not connect it to existing
  artifacts, gods, or cultures merely through sun, bell, crown, time, or glass
  imagery.
- Resolve the battle decisively while preserving the later physical world. A
  costly victory, containment, occupation, retreat, capture, or civilizational
  wound can fit; total planetary erasure in the modern-like era cannot.
- Add unnamed civilians, remote observers, memories, prior officeholders, or
  noncombatant institutions as needed, provided none materially assists
  Solstice. Clear every character-facing name through `stories/NAMES.md`.
- Record every reusable invention as proposed material in `06-canon-delta.md`;
  none becomes canon merely by appearing in this candidate story.

## Name constraints

- **Solstice Evening Bell / Solstice:** Neither `Solstice Evening Bell` nor
  `Solstice` has an exact or alias match in `stories/NAMES.md`; both are
  available for this prompt-required identity. Register every form used,
  including `Evening Bell` or `Bell` if either appears alone as her label.
  (`stories/NAMES.md` — “Rules”; “Character name registry”)
- **Close Bell match:** `Bell` is not a reserved form, but the Bellweather family
  appears across six candidate identities: Adrian, Evelyn, Garran, Lady Rowena,
  Marcus, and Phoebe Bellweather. Using `Bell` alone would create a conspicuous
  lexical near-match despite not being an exact collision. Prefer `Solstice` or
  `Evening Bell`; intentional use of `Bell` needs explicit distinct-identity
  and reader-disambiguation notes. (`stories/NAMES.md` — “Character name
  registry,” Bellweather entries)
- **First Monarch:** No exact registry form matches `First Monarch`. Treat it as
  an alias of Solstice, not a second person. If shortened to `the Monarch` or
  `Monarch` as a proper label, register those forms. Existing role identities
  `King Othran`, `the Hollow Regent` / `Regent`, and `Queen Aurelia` are distinct
  and have no established connection to Solstice. (`stories/NAMES.md` —
  “Character name registry,” relevant title entries)
- **queen of all magical girls:** No exact registry form matches this title.
  Register the title and any article/capitalization variant used as an alias.
  Avoid `Queen` alone. Do not borrow `Sun Queen`, `Burning Queen`, `Goddess of
  the Sun`, `Goddess`, or `Mother`; those forms belong to the distinct legacy
  identity Queen Aurelia. (`stories/NAMES.md` — “Character name registry,”
  Queen Aurelia / the Goddess)
- **Time and solar imagery:** `Solstice` does not establish a time or sun
  domain. `Time`, `the Goddess of Time`, and `Goddess of Time` belong to a
  distinct legacy deity. Crown, sun, time, and goddess language must not imply
  identity with that figure or Queen Aurelia. (`stories/NAMES.md` — “Character
  name registry,” The Goddess of Time and Queen Aurelia entries)
- **`ultrahuman`:** This is a category, not a registered name. Keep it lowercase
  as supplied when used as a class. If `the Ultrahuman` becomes a proper epithet
  for Solstice, register it as another alias of the same identity.
- **Additional names:** No prompt-supplied form collides with the unresolved
  legacy set. Do not give new identities the unresolved forms `Lena`, `Mara`,
  `Nisha`, `Pell`, or `Voss`. Search every personal name, codename, username,
  named construct, person-like ship, animal companion, invader commander, and
  title used as a name. (`stories/NAMES.md` — “Known unresolved legacy
  collisions”)
- These are production-memory constraints, not canon. They prevent accidental
  identity implications but do not place any registered legacy or candidate
  character in Solstice's continuity. (`stories/NAMES.md` — “Rules”)

## Required checks after drafting

- Confirm Earth is not asserted to be literal real-world Earth, and the result
  preserves the physical world's ability to reach the later Glass-Sea era.
- Confirm an exact or tightly bounded force count of at least 100,000 appears
  on-page and remains legible through attrition rather than becoming a vague
  background statistic.
- Trace geography, fronts, altitude, distances, formation changes, objectives,
  and elapsed time. Every tactical turn and the ending must follow from
  established positions and capabilities.
- Verify Solstice is the sole defender: no ally, deity, civilian, automation,
  remote operator, infrastructure, or apparent death grants attacks, shields,
  healing, targeting, power, or material augmentation.
- Inventory every Solstice capability and decisive tactic. Each must be set up
  before use, obey stable rules, and sustain a costly, non-effortless fight.
- Check every magical actor or autonomous magical device against the
  living-agency rule. Identify its living source or establish its own
  life/personhood.
- Search every use of `ultrahuman`. It must remain the exact unique Earth
  classification and must not drift into “only magical being,” “only
  superhuman,” “only god,” or a universal category.
- Search `god`, `goddess`, `divine`, and `worship`. Metaphor or cultural address
  should be clear; literal godhood must satisfy worship, power, and authority.
- Verify both regnal titles refer consistently to Solstice, are locally
  defined, and do not imply a universal monarchy or an unsupported large public
  magical-girl population during the low-magic trough.
- Ensure the decisive ending resolves whether Solstice holds Earth while
  preserving later continuity. Capture every classification, institution,
  power-system, invasion, battle, and outcome fact in the proposed canon delta.
- Do not use bells, solstices, suns, crowns, time, gods, portals, or glass as
  evidence of connection to an existing person, system, faction, or era.
- Keep violence PG-13 and non-graphic, distinguish destruction from confirmed
  deaths, and preserve moral/personhood consequences if invaders or constructs
  are living.
- Re-run the story-scoped name check after planning and after final editing.
  Ensure the registry contains `Solstice Evening Bell`, `Solstice`, both
  prompt-required titles as used, and every additional character-facing alias;
  inspect especially `Bell`/Bellweather, Queen Aurelia's aliases, the Goddess
  of Time, `King Othran`, and `the Hollow Regent`.

## Revision addendum — 2026-07-26

- The user-directed crown origin is compatible with **Living agency and
  magic** if each donor creates her final spell while alive, the nonliving
  chime only stores it, and Solstice supplies the present living agency that
  wakes and directs it. The crown must never act or use magic independently.
  (`universe/rules.md` — “Living agency and magic”)
- Willing death is a property of this crown's creation mechanism, not a new
  universal rule that sacrifice fuels magic. Consent must remain explicit and
  its ethical consequence must not be sentimentalized away.
  (`universe/rules.md` — “Costs, desire, and local mechanisms”)
- The donors' souls must not be trapped, copied, consumed, or erased by chime
  destruction. The universal underlying afterlife remains intact; what the
  battle destroys is each woman's nonliving final spell and last surviving
  work. This preserves their personhood and the moral reality of their choices.
  (`universe/rules.md` — “Afterlife and continuation”;
  `universe/style-guide.md` — “Personhood and moral consequence”)
- Solstice's `ultrahuman` classification may remain after the crown is
  destroyed if the story explicitly establishes that her innate living force
  and control—not possession of the crown—satisfy the era-local criteria. The
  revision does so and retains the narrow classification scope.
  (`universe/premise.md` — “Knowledge and connection”)
- The twelve donors remain unnamed in this story. Concrete personal memories
  do not require registry entries unless the prose turns a description into a
  stable title used as a character-facing name.
