# Outline

## Story

- Proposed reader-facing title: *The Brackwater Circuit*.
- Premise and central promise: Each spring tide, Levara Quist, head priest of Holdfast, and Ambel Tarn, head priest of Farwake, circle Brackwater Reach's reef in a two-person skiff to renew their opposing gods' truce. After seven years of formal rivalry, each boards privately ordered to win the other's conversion before dawn. During the wet circuit, professional attention becomes mutual love. Resolve that promise plainly: they reject conversion as its proof, complete the truce, and return to opposed offices with one future meeting chosen but no peace between gods or congregations.
- Focal attachment or contradiction: Levara genuinely loves Holdfast, her office, and the harbor families who trust her, yet anticipates Ambel's arguments more than any sanctioned pleasure. She handles desire like a covenant problem and tests him instead of admitting what she wants. Ambel values Farwake's defense of departure; he wants Levara's uncoerced regard, not rescue or a love that makes him conquered ground.
- Counterforce or complication: Holdfast and Farwake are living local gods with exclusive jurisdictions, worship, power, and authority. Each treats the rival head priest's conversion as strategic gain. Their mirrored orders poison curiosity: once Ambel notices Levara's doctrinal tests, every kindness looks tactical. The falling tide limits deliberation, but no storm or public emergency replaces the relational choice.
- POV and information limit: Close third-person past through Levara. She knows harbor work and Holdfast's command but cannot hear Farwake or know Ambel's faith, order, attraction, or answer until he reveals them. Keep the gods exterior beyond bounded messages and effects; grant neither omniscience.
- Governing movement and time shape: One dusk-to-dawn circuit passes from Holdfast's inner water through the contested mouth, around Farwake's reef, and back, alternating practical and divine leverage. Render the command handoff, fouled line, mirrored-order disclosure, decisive argument, and final liturgy; imply earlier years through work habits, not flashbacks.
- Operative speculative constraint: Holdfast stills water inside a properly tied mooring; Farwake strengthens an outward current beneath a deliberately launched craft. Their powers overlap only here and interfere if invoked together, so the priests control alternating stretches at spring tide. Neither god detects truth, compels consent, reads minds, or rewards love. Holdfast-blessed rope resists mildew but crusts with salt; Farwake-blessed loose objects creep seaward.
- Structural distinction and ending boundary: Use a circular working journey and two-person conversation, not public debate, rescue, proof, or reform. After exposing the gods' mirrored tactic, each priest relinquishes leverage, acknowledges love, and refuses defection while preserving honest disagreement. Waiting crews interrupt them on return; love has begun, while sanction, tenure, disclosure, and compromise remain unsettled.

## Beats

1. At the public launch, establish ceremonial hostility, divine effects, and Levara's secret order. Beyond listeners, wet gear and familiar route corrections expose their ordinary working ease.
2. In Holdfast's water, Levara probes Ambel's faith under cover of reviewing truce language. He notices she is listening for surrender rather than understanding.
3. At the mouth, Levara releases the securing line and trusts Ambel's command. They clear a foul through coordinated work, not rescue; attraction sharpens while secrecy distorts it.
4. At the reef, Ambel reveals Farwake's recruitment order. Levara discloses its mirror and admits she briefly imagined conversion removing their barrier. He refuses to become captured ground; she rejects his suspicion that devotion is only fear.
5. Returning, sustained conversation changes commitment. They distinguish love from recruitment, acknowledge it mutually, surrender usable confidences, and choose one ordinary meeting without promising safety or office.
6. They complete the honestly adversarial liturgy. Crews take the boat into separate jurisdictions before dawn; there is no announcement, crowd response, or thematic summation.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Levara counts knots, distances, clauses, and maintenance; measured syntax loosens as Ambel escapes her categories. Keep sea detail tactile, not a constant romance metaphor.
- Conversational texture: Move among liturgy, procedure, complaints about wet clothes and food, and sustained argument. Use pauses, corrections, and partial understanding without paired speeches.
- Rhetorical ownership: Levara owns covenant distinctions and conditional logic. Ambel reasons from voyages and particular people, testing claims against consequences. Crews use brief task language.
- Pressure behavior: Levara overqualifies and answers implications. Ambel drops anecdotes, repeats boundaries, and lets silence stand. Each must restate what was heard before pressing on.
- Anti-default: Avoid symmetrical philosopher-priests, uniformly polished agendas, or gods as personality types. Wet work, unequal fluency, sincere faith, and refused conversion prevent a tidy theological merger.

## Continuity

- Era and connections: Use an unlocated later age; connect neither place, god, nor rite to established cultures, Gates, deities, seas, or chronology.
- Godhood: Preserve godhood as continuing worship, bounded power, and local authority. This is one local system, and living agency supplies its power.
- Content boundary: Both lovers are unambiguously adult; keep intimacy non-explicit and consent free of conversion or divine coercion.
