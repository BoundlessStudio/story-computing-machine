# Outline

## Story

- Title and central promise: **A Question for the Sky**. After eight centuries of being taught the world below is lifeless, radiator mechanic Sairu finds a child's question shining across surface terraces in maintenance-label language. Complete a cautious visual correspondence with Piru and decide who controls future contact.
- Focal attachment and want: Sairu trusts mechanisms that answer the hand. She wants to answer before weather closes the sightline without making Piru's home a surveillance target. Her useful flaw is treating proof as permission until the return signal sets a boundary.
- Counterforce and complication: Supervisor Orvek protects the station's heat margin and accountable logs; archivist Lassan accepts only what the syntax supports. Brief passes, cloud, sparse vocabulary, thermal load, and automatic geolocation constrain them. Neither colleague remains loyal to the lifeless-world lesson once the evidence holds.
- POV and information limit: Close third past through Sairu, attending to hot spots, hinge lag, orbital timing, and block shapes. No surface viewpoint. She cannot infer Piru's culture, population, or motives beyond patterns and moving heat traces; Lassan identifies grammar and provenance, not intention.
- Governing movement and time shape: Three usable night passes across thirty hours, separated by decoding, repair, and cooling. Discovery becomes a partial reply, then a bounded exchange. End by leaving the next opportunity for the surface to initiate, without broadcast, crowd conversion, or institutional settlement.
- Operative constraint: Living tenders direct a local current through kilometer-wide star-reed grids, releasing stored sunlight as green-white and infrared radiance. The work chills them; repetition bleaches useful crop. Reeds focus but do not choose. Sairu flips radiator vanes between emissive and shielded faces for a surface heat-eye frame to read. Closed vanes store dangerous heat, enforcing short packets and full cooling. Darkness, angle, line of sight, and thick cloud limit both channels. Vanes, optics, labels, and records are inert.

## Beats

1. On an exterior inspection, Sairu sees a terrace grid ask whether a worker lives above. A repeated correction and a self-marker identify the author as a child named Piru. Moving heat traces establish living operators, not their number.
2. Before applying the contact tag that triggers survey, Sairu takes the frames to Lassan. Lassan separates certain words from guessed spacing and establishes descent from service labels without explaining how the language survived.
3. Orvek catches the optical hold. Sairu presses for acknowledgment, Lassan narrows the claim, and Orvek calculates cooling and surveillance consequences. She authorizes one low-resolution packet, with an abort threshold and no locator request.
4. During the second pass, Sairu times vanes while Orvek calls heat margins and Lassan checks order. A lagging hinge forces Sairu to shorten the reply to living confirmation and a receipt request. Cloud prevents an answer; all three must cool and repair.
5. Waiting tests what each treats as evidence. Sairu repeats certainty, Orvek retreats into procedure as the excursion enters the log, and Lassan refuses denial or grand conclusions. A confirmed-contact tag would auto-center more optics on Piru's grid.
6. The third pass brings receipt, a simple count showing several tenders, and a concrete restriction assembled from label terms: no location mark or unsummoned search. Piru's imperfect revision clears an ambiguity. Sairu accepts that an answer grants no ownership of later contact.
7. Orvek places the anomaly under a legitimate safety hold and accepts audit. Lassan records only verified language and the restriction. Sairu acknowledges the boundary and offers a surface-initiated return window. The terraces confirm receipt and darken; she disables auto-centering and opens the vanes.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Move by calibration, recurrence, and correction. Notice gradients, delays, geometry, and the gap between pattern and inference; let wonder arrive through material observation.
- Conversational texture: Station talk ranges from clipped work calls to interrupted private argument. Visual messages are sparse, concrete, revised, and label-bound, not polished letters.
- Rhetorical ownership: Sairu reasons through feedback; Orvek through thresholds, responsibility, and logs; Lassan through provenance and bounded claims; Piru through categories, counts, and operational limits.
- Pressure behavior: Sairu repeats and overcommits, Orvek compresses into procedure, and Lassan restates uncertainty. During vane work, abstraction gives way to timing; Piru corrects ambiguity across passes.
- Anti-default: Avoid eloquent rebels trading cosmic assurances, a doctrinaire villain, a code-lore lecture, committee-transcript rhythm, or a final truth maxim. Costly turn-taking and respected limits make contact meaningful.

## Continuity

- Canon used: Station and surface share the deep-time world. Living people originate the bounded star-reed magic; reeds only focus it, while devices, labels, and records have no agency or truth authority.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Era placement remains unresolved. The local doctrine's origin, believers, and wider planet remain unknown. The completed exchange establishes only Piru, several tenders, and one grid—not a planetwide population, government, or mandate.
