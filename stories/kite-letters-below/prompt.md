# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] An orbital civilization has taught its citizens for eight centuries that the earth below is lifeless. A heat-radiator mechanic finds a newly handmade cloth kite tangled in the station’s vanes, carrying a child’s question written in a language preserved only in maintenance labels. The mechanic begins an improvised correspondence with the planet below through discarded supply capsules.

## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: broadly accessible unless specified
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-08
- Avoid: none specified
