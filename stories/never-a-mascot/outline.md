# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: A small rooster in battered articulated armor marches beside the kingdom's host and becomes its joke, then its lucky mascot. Across seven years, the armorer who helped start that story keeps repairing him while missing evidence that he follows neither banner. When the host's last three soldiers witness his enormous dawn form, his action—not a declaration—reveals a war of his own against armies burning the east road.
- Focal pressure or attachment: The armorer, now the surviving sergeant, has used the mascot legend to steady recruits and dignify her care. She wants to obey the host's last order to burn the road and make its deaths count, yet also save the wounded surgeon and scout. Respecting the rooster means admitting her cherished account edited out his choices.
- Counterforce or complication: Pitch lines, smoke-hidden travelers, archers, a collapsing causeway, and the surgeon's limited mobility close the window. The rooster clears civilians and animals from both armies' fires; the royal order seals that route. No commander remains to change it or own the result.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through the armorer-sergeant. She reads rivets, arrow angles, loads, and heat damage, but folklore distorts allegiance. She cannot enter the rooster's mind or see his decisive crossing. Agency emerges from refusals, routes, targets, and costs.
- Governing movement and time shape: A comic muster, three compressed repair returns across seven years, then one continuous half-hour from final rout to sunrise. Recurrence changes the armor's meaning. Keep the climax on an open burned approach before only three witnesses; end with wound care and route choice, not acclaim, official correction, or thematic coda.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Once per dawn, direct first light lets the rooster enlarge voluntarily for at most nine minutes. He gains mass and strength, not flight, healing, knowledge, invulnerability, or command. Growth drains heat and blood. His jointed armor unfolds mechanically; foreign objects stay unchanged. Deep arrows must be removed before he shrinks. Consent governs aid. This is one living being's capacity, not a rule for birds or armor.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Material, close, cumulative; notice armor fit, soot, road grades, and the gap between useful story and evidence. Compress years through repairs, then slow dawn into weight, position, and cost.
- Conversational texture: Muster chatter mixes easy cruelty with work estimates; veteran talk is interrupted, practical, and worn, with room for ordinary requests and mistaken answers.
- Rhetorical ownership: The armorer reasons through fit, duty, and repair; the scout owns bearings and corrected counts; the surgeon owns bodily thresholds. Officers speak in possession. The silent rooster argues through choices.
- Pressure behavior: The armorer's explaining and commands shorten into listening. The scout repeats uncertainties. Pain makes the surgeon slower but more exact. Smoke and manual work cause missed replies without universal inarticulacy.
- Anti-default: Avoid quippy company banter, equally polished moral voices, or a noble animal whose pain exists to improve humans; no chronicler, inspirational speech, or final maxim.

## Beats

1. At muster, the young armorer patches the rooster's functional armor while soldiers laugh and attach royal color. He removes it, refuses baiting grain, and takes the road's verge outside the column. She recognizes experienced workmanship but joins the joke.
2. Compress campaigns through repairs he initiates and pays for with scavenged fittings. Each return bears damage from elsewhere. Soldiers make his appearances lucky; he never camps, answers a call, or keeps insignia.
3. Later evidence includes both armies' fletching and pitch, cut cage wire, and east-road wear. The armorer protects the mascot story because it steadies recruits. Their practical trust is real; her account of it is not.
4. Seven years on, the host breaks. Its armorer-sergeant, scout, and wounded field surgeon shelter beside a prepared pitch line. Their last order requires ignition before dawn, though smoke hides possible travelers. Debate fuse time, carrying capacity, and uncertain observation rather than abstractions.
5. The small, injured rooster crosses toward the fire wagons and refuses redirection. The sergeant chooses among burning the road, abandoning one companion, or delaying; she cuts the fuse but keeps the spark.
6. Keep his crossing beyond sight. Reversed fire, broken harnesses, shots, and escaping figures reveal purposeful action. The scout identifies fire from both sides; the armorer finally connects his old wounds to attacks on any incendiaries threatening this road.
7. At first light he emerges enormous from the burning field, arrows in his back and dawn behind, dragging a blazing wagon beam off the causeway until the last travelers pass. He spends his interval without posing, saluting, or approaching the standard.
8. The survivors remove arrows before he shrinks: the surgeon directs, scout watches time and field, armorer works through familiar plates, and his movements govern consent. Recognition lands through reversed service and independent targets. Afterward, he chooses the east fork; they carry their surgeon along the same road while discussing water, pace, and weight, not ownership or legend.

## Continuity

- Canon used: The coherent deep-time world permits an unnamed, undated fantasy kingdom and local living magic. The rooster controls his finite change. Repetition distorts truth without making folklore proof.
- Boundaries and unknowns: Link no existing kingdom, faction, era, war, species, system, artifact, or noun. Leave origin and war politics outside the frame. The armor merely accommodates living change. One rooster's agency establishes nothing universal about animals.
