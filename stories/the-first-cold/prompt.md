# Prompt

[WP] For three centuries, the colossal furnace towers have kept humanity's last colony alive on a world swallowed by eternal winter. Their fires have never faltered, their mechanisms have never failed, and no one remembers who built them. But when a routine maintenance crew discovers a human heartbeat pulsing deep within one tower's foundations, they uncover something impossible: the towers are alive.
