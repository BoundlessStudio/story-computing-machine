# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: After a tabletop roleplaying session, Reniya hears a formless adviser stop her from naming the friends' route home; beneath their mill room, silver root grows toward that destination. In interrupted stages, the adviser explains that it and friends play a cosmic game: one made this peril, while the others must guide Reniya and her companions to discover and solve it. Reniya's TTRPG comparison genuinely surprises the voice. Resolve the route-seed before morning market talk activates it; never imply that people or world are fictional.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Reniya runs games and wants to turn uncertainty into rules, but risks preferring a clean cosmic answer to her friends' evidence. Mason Veysa will not risk the hillside catchment on an unseen speaker's authority. Courier Ludek recognizes the danger in ordinary route talk and can refuse both Reniya and the voices. Friendship permits disagreement without consensus about the players.
- Counterforce or complication: Each of three guiding voices may answer only two direct questions, report only what it observed of the creator's move, and cannot perceive underground structure, local idiom, or mortal thought. Their descriptions overlap imperfectly. Questions run out as the seed nears the catchment wall; cutting it while charged releases a destructive root toward its last destination. The creator remains absent.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Reniya, who alone hears the voices while holding a cold splinter shed by the growth. She relays them imperfectly. Neither she nor the reader can verify their nature, motives, or game beyond clues tested against matter. Veysa's and Ludek's judgments appear through speech, refusal, expertise, and action.
- Governing movement and time shape: One recursive night from mill room through lanes and orchard terraces to a circular kiln trench above the catchment. Small speech-tests turn claims into evidence; changing ground repeatedly revises the plan. The decisive turn belongs to mortals coordinating around terrain the voices cannot see. End after they lift the inert seed and check the catchment, handling physical aftermath as contact fades—no public proof, score, institutional turn, or thematic summation.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: A nonliving route-seed, charged once by its living cosmic maker, converts audible, sincere route intentions within half a mile into metallic growth along the imagined course. Thought, questions, lies, past travel, and disembodied guidance do not feed it or alter choice. New root cannot cross its cooled seam; a closed course knots the finite charge around the seed and exposes it for cutting. Breaking it charged releases growth toward the last destination. This mechanism is story-local.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Practical close narration moves from dice and habitual routes to cracks, loads, soil, and shifting ground. Reniya spots patterns quickly but revises when matter resists.
- Conversational texture: Friends use shorthand, questions, corrections, work instructions, and tired humor; cosmic explanation arrives through interruption and challenge, never lecture.
- Rhetorical ownership: Reniya reasons by rules and probabilities; Veysa by fracture and accountable risk; Ludek by routes, overheard habits, and exceptions. The voices respectively favor turn structure, permissions, and spatial conditions.
- Pressure behavior: Reniya relays too tersely and confidently. Veysa shifts from argument to exact imperatives. Ludek loses humor and repeats evidence until heard. The voices hedge, challenge premises, or overproduce branches.
- Anti-default: Avoid grand omniscient voices, interchangeable clever companions, or obedience to a meta-quest proving Reniya special.

## Beats

1. While the friends pack their game, the first voice stops Reniya from naming their homeward route; silver filament answers the aborted intention. Reniya's questions produce the required formless-voice, cosmic-game, created-peril, guided-companions, TTRPG-comparison, and surprised-recognition sequence amid Veysa's and Ludek's demand for proof.
2. Veysa exposes the filament. Ludek recalls earlier jumps following stated destinations. A harmless controlled statement causes matching growth, establishing mechanism and Reniya's fallible relay. They investigate instead of taking the voice's preferred direction.
3. Following cooled seams through terraces, they ration questions among three voices. Answers establish stored charge, self-crossing limits, and cutting danger, but misread the unseen catchment. Veysa finds stressed masonry; Ludek realizes market traffic will supply hundreds of routes.
4. At the buried kiln ring, Veysa refuses the voices' excavation plan. Reniya yields when Ludek identifies an old service course that circles back. They open the dry spillway and mark a loop from separate sightlines without naming a destination.
5. The friends coordinate sincere route descriptions as roots change footing. Veysa adjusts for the wall; Ludek halts a phrase pointing downhill; the last cosmic answer confirms only the crossing condition. Their revised plan knots the growth, exposes the seed, and vents its final fracture into the spillway. Veysa cuts the discharged core.
6. They check the catchment and recover the inert seed. The voices differ over the result, but their answers are spent and contact fades. Keep the final companion exchange practical as they carry the core and choose which damage needs attention first.
