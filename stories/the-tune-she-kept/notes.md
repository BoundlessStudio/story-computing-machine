## 2026-10-11 — Starting direction

The writer chose a fiddler and mother whose joyous Fae night costs two hundred mortal years. An old recording lets her hear the little daughter she remembers as a fully adult woman, with musical choices and a life she never witnessed. The coordinator asked what the living singer wants independently, what staying costs beyond a symbolic refusal of the Fae, and how the ending can remain an encounter rather than make archival work cure grief. The writer owns the provisional direction and prose. No supplied originals; no shared-universe additions proposed.
