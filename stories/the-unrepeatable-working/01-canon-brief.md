## Hard constraints

1. Only life can use magic. Any board apparatus, brass node, rail, written record, recording, resonator, or laboratory instrument may store, conduct, focus, measure, or shape living-supplied magic but may not independently choose, consent, originate power, or cast. [Source: `universe/rules.md`, `## Living agency and magic`]

2. “Magic” is a cultural label, not an objective universal category. The discovery may be new to this culture, institution, or documented practice, but the story must not claim it is the first such phenomenon anywhere in the world’s deep history. [Source: `universe/rules.md`, `## System categories`; `universe/glossary.md`, `## Magic, technology, and divinity`; `universe/premise.md`, `## The deep-time shared world`]

3. The new effect must remain locally defined. Neither desire, sacrifice, pain, consent, speech, silence, names, uncertainty, nor institutional recognition is a universal magical fuel. Any causal role for these elements belongs only to the demonstrated mechanism. [Source: `universe/rules.md`, `## Costs, desire, and local mechanisms`]

4. Names are not universally magical. If a registered spell name, phrase, or vocal form closes the working, that behavior must be an engineered or locally discovered binding—not true-name metaphysics or a rule that speech inherently commands reality. [Source: `universe/rules.md`, `## Names and identity binding`; `universe/glossary.md`, `## Gate Cant`; `universe/rules.md`, `## Calvea's dusk working and Calling the Name`]

5. Similar voice-, breath-, name-, brass-, consent-, or severance-based effects do not establish shared ancestry with Gate Cant, Calvea’s Calling the Name, Ersene calls, the Morrow supper, the Fenwick watch, or any other system. [Source: `universe/rules.md`, `## Artifacts, motifs, and folklore`; `universe/style-guide.md`, `## Continuity and evidence discipline`]

6. Disability must never be framed as magical, intellectual, ethical, or personal deficiency. A voice-dependent local practice may create an access barrier, but it must not imply that speaking people possess more living agency or that non-speaking people are incomplete magic users. Canon already establishes Irix as a deaf, non-speaking, fluent communicator and living magic user without cure or speech substitute. [Source: `universe/style-guide.md`, `## Personhood and moral consequence`; `universe/characters.md`, `## Irix`; `stories/the-friends-i-built/05-story.md`, `# The Friends I Built`]

Candidate continuity requires that she remain a mute shaper who communicates fluently through sign, slate, expression, and diagrams and whose inability to supply ordinary audible words is neither cured nor bypassed. Its process should distinguish reproducing an effect from explaining its ultimate cause; require controls, disclosed uncertainty, and living-participant limits; and remain capable of being corrected by evidence. Canon’s broader evidence discipline rejects convenient certainty and treats records as fallible evidence rather than automatic truth. [Source: `universe/premise.md`, `## Memory, myth, and recoverable truth`; `universe/rules.md`, `## Records, falsehood, and historical traces`; `universe/style-guide.md`, `## Continuity and evidence discipline`]

## Recommended safe prequel architecture

The strongest compatible connection is an early discovery of the single-working precursor that the later unnamed college develops into callwright practice.

The accidental effect may be the first locally documented separation of a working across two living participants:

- one living practitioner prepares and powers an incomplete effect;
- another living participant’s chosen, contemporaneous audible call closes or releases it;
- neither participant alone performed the witnessed accident in the way the board initially assumes;
- inert apparatus carries or marks the interaction but supplies no agency;
- controlled repetition establishes bounded conditions while leaving the underlying reason uncertain.

This satisfies “genuinely new” as a new local capability: power/direction and release can be distributed between living agents. It remains an accident because the discoverer initially cannot identify which element—living breath, timing, authorization, acoustic contact, registered form, or their combination—made the first occurrence possible.

A credible board demonstration can test a compact matrix:

- recorded or stored sound: fails;
- written, signed, or merely thought label: fails to close this specific mechanism without invalidating those channels as communication;
- wrong timing or broken sound path: fails;
- fresh living caller without authorization: fails or is safely inert;
- fresh authorized caller using the bounded live form: succeeds;
- different trained shaper or caller: optional limited replication, proving the effect is not a chosen-one trait.

The final intellectual resolution need not solve the cosmology. It can establish an honest operational claim: “these conditions reproduce the effect; we do not yet know why.” The dramatic resolution should also assign credit accurately to every living participant rather than letting the institution call a caller equipment or the discoverer a solitary genius.

## Direct continuity options

### Preferred low-risk option

Use a new discoverer and an earlier research board attached to, or ancestral to, the candidate’s unnamed callwright college. The board’s surviving report becomes the institutional seed for the later one-shaper/one-caller/one-working practice. This provides explicit prequel continuity while leaving all candidate characters’ earlier biographies open.

### Viable character-linked option

A younger Dorram may be the first-person discoverer, provided he is explicitly the same candidate identity. His accident could reveal the distributed release principle, while the board rewards a later self-called reproduction and helps create the institutional bias toward self-sufficiency that he still carries into Kethra’s examination. This can enrich his later willingness to say, “The evidence corrected me,” without transferring Kethra’s lattice authorship or rescue achievement to him.

If this option is selected:

- do not establish an exact age or interval unless needed;
- do not make Dorram the inventor of the voiced lattice;
- preserve his later ability to act as both shaper and caller;
- make any institutional lesson provisional rather than universal;
- register the reuse as one recurring identity, not an unrelated duplicate.

Kethra and Ulan need not appear. Their absence is safest unless the chosen chronology can support them without inventing ages or earlier relationships.

## Candidate-continuity limits

The prequel may safely originate or foreshadow:

- a primitive single-cell or single-pattern activation;
- the recognition that shaping and calling are distinct necessary contributions;
- the requirement for present living agency;
- a crude live acoustic path or inert conducting focus;
- a board report that later becomes callwright teaching material;
- an early institutional mistake that later practices attempt to correct.

It must not pre-establish:

- Kethra’s voiced lattice or three-cell maximum;
- Kethra’s exact multi-caller architecture;
- `Weightward`, `Roofstay`, `Airclear`, `Crown Release`, or `Safe Descent` unless they are explicitly treated as later developments;
- Kethra’s examination-tower rescue;
- her archmage office result;
- her post-rescue caller roster;
- the mature **yes**, **wait**, and **stop** procedure as Kethra’s finalized safety practice;
- any cure, magical compensation, or origin for Kethra’s muteness.

Terms such as `shaper`, `caller`, `cell`, `call-link`, `voiced lattice`, and `speaking rail` remain candidate-local vocabulary. The prequel may show earlier terminology or the origin of only those terms intentionally selected for the same local system. ## Conflicts and omissions

### Contradictions

No contradiction with current `LOCKED` or `CANON` authority was found. A bounded living-agent, voice-mediated precursor is compatible with existing system diversity, living-agency rules, and local name-binding limits.

### Omission, not contradiction

Current canon does not establish:

- this board of magical researchers;
- the callwright college or its era and geography;
- the origin of callwright practice;
- a universal scholarly method for magical discoveries;
- why a living audible form could close this local pattern;
- the precise chronology between this prequel and `voice-of-silence`;
- Dorram’s earlier career, if reused;
- whether the later examination panel descends directly from this board.

These are omissions and therefore available for bounded local invention. They are not evidence for global rules.

### Candidate dependency, not authority conflict

The prequel relationship relies on a non-canon candidate. This is not a contradiction and does not require a user ruling because the prompt explicitly authorizes the dependency. It does require later continuity rechecks and must not be described as established canon.

## Unknowns to preserve unless the story locally answers them

- The ultimate cosmological reason living sound affects this mechanism.
- Whether breath, acoustic vibration, conscious articulation, authorization, timing, or their conjunction is causal.
- The outer limits of caller species, distance, amplification, language, accent, volume, and substitution.
- Whether the first effect could recur outside this college’s local system.
- The discoverer’s wider historical importance.
- The exact age, founders, governance, and later fate of the board.
- The exact interval before Kethra’s examination.
- Whether the board’s report remained accurate, was revised, or became institutional myth.

Any local answer should be limited to what the demonstration establishes. Missing evidence must not become a universal law.

## Safe local invention space

The story may invent:

- one unnamed or newly named fantasy-era locality;
- an earlier board, laboratory, archive, hearing chamber, and research procedure;
- new researchers, callers, technicians, rivals, and witnesses;
- a modest demonstration effect distinct from Kethra’s later five named spells;
- crude inert apparatus and measurable physical controls;
- local terminology preceding the mature callwright vocabulary;
- bounded fatigue, breath, concentration, heat, pain, or material failure;
- procedural politics, professional credit disputes, and institutional incentives;
- a locally scoped reason the board demands immediate demonstration;
- an ending in which operational reproducibility is established while ultimate causation remains open.

The effect should be bounded enough to avoid effortless omnipotence. A narrow physical or informational result is safer than transformation of reality at large.
