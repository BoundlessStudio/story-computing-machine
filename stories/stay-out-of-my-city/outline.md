# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Warren Dovett overpowers Brackenport's warring heroes and villains, refuses recruitment, and threatens both to stay out. Over nine years, it becomes the world's most peaceful and crime-free city. Its freedom and safety are real.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Warren and his daughter Elvi swim off the ferry steps, argue about films, and know their neighbors. At nineteen she is leaving for a marine-biology degree elsewhere. He wants to celebrate without being left behind.
- Counterforce or complication: Hero Candescent and villain Mordant see a recruitment opportunity. Candescent needs help defending other cities; Mordant wants protection for his crew. Elvi wants her father's company at the coach, without an escort. Warren keeps answering worry with capabilities.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third past through Warren; externally legible powers, intimately familiar streets. Elvi's responses may correct his reading of her mood. No access to other minds.
- Governing movement and time shape: A founding confrontation when Elvi is ten; two compressed intervening encounters; dwell on departure morning at nineteen. Time expands before an outward journey. No political coda.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Warren can arrest and redirect the macroscopic movement of visible matter and energy, from a falling bag to an entire attacking fleet. Restraining bodies preserves breathing and circulation. His living faculty requires sight and intention; he cannot read minds, observe through walls, or compel feelings. Credible deterrence lets ordinary civic life flourish without continuous magical policing. No compulsory sacrifice or hidden price.
- Dialogue promise: Superpowered recruitment meets stubborn local attachment; familiar father-daughter teasing becomes an awkward farewell. Preserve irritation, vanity, impatience, and affection alongside the threat's genuine force.
- Dialogic medium: An initially amplified harbor ultimatum, recurring face-to-face approaches, household talk, offered or rejected practical help, and a final conversation beside a waiting coach.
- Dialogue engine: Both factions keep trying to turn Warren's refusal into allegiance. Elvi needs him to distinguish a request for reassurance from a request to eliminate uncertainty; her corrections change how he accompanies her.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Warm, observant, occasionally awed; give impossible scale full sentences before attending to embarrassing particulars. Warren's familiarity supplies intimacy without making everyone else ridiculous.
- Conversational texture: Recruitment has competing performances; family talk has interruptions, remembered preferences, unnecessary reminders, and failed jokes. Let single-purpose speech stand.
- Rhetorical ownership: Candescent owns strategic argument and earned public authority. Mordant performs confidence and needles competitors. Warren notices people and objects before principles. Elvi's humor comes from knowing him; she does not become an autonomy lecturer.
- Pressure behavior: Warren repeats packing questions; Candescent becomes insistent; Mordant loses decorative language when physically overmatched. Elvi's annoyance falters when departure becomes immediate.
- Relationship movement: Candescent wants cooperation but dislikes admitting helplessness; Mordant wants protection but conceals fear. Warren wants Elvi to stay without burdening her with the request. Elvi wants encouragement but finds admitting her own fear embarrassing. Their final exchange permits shared sadness without changing her plans.
- Anti-default: Avoid three fluent professionals negotiating an ethical operating procedure, and avoid revealing that safety was imprisonment. Neither faction learns the story's lesson for the reader.

## Beats

1. **The first warning.** A hero-villain battle reaches the harbor. Warren halts attacks, suspends both forces over unoccupied water, and makes each acknowledge his ultimatum before releasing them outward. He refuses both offers of membership. Elvi waits with their swimming things; power and attachment appear together.
2. **The peace takes.** Two encounters years apart show recruitment becoming elaborate inducement. Candescent proposes affiliation; Mordant offers unnecessary protection. Neither faction establishes a base; criminal networks retreat, and ordinary prevention and mutual trust gain room. Establish the eventual absence of crime directly through unguarded daily life. Residents may complain, disagree, or leave. Elvi grows less impressed by her father's reputation.
3. **The packed room.** Warren has supplied redundant luggage help without asking what Elvi wants. Their familiar packing dispute gives way to her request that he come to the coach as her father. She chose her course for fieldwork and distance, without secret distress.
4. **One last offer.** Both emissaries wait beyond the inland boundary with armed escorts. Candescent offers consequential work abroad; Mordant offers advantage and protection for Elvi's journey. Each advances their entourage to dominate the encounter. Before either crosses, Warren arrests both formations, lifts transports and flying escorts above their empty staging ground, and turns them outward. His renewed threats are explicit and credible. They withdraw without conversion or alliance. The road remains peaceful.
5. **The ordinary goodbye.** Elvi punctures Warren's renewed escort offer with a childhood embarrassment they both remember. Humor cannot hide that both are frightened. He responds to what she needs instead of proposing another capability. Allow an awkward, interrupted hug and a second attempt at parting; leave the actual words open. She leaves on the ordinary coach. Stay with Warren through separation, without a power display to reverse it.
