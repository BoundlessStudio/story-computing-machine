# Prompt

## Prompt

> [WP] A young relic hunter enters a forbidden desert labyrinth to prove she deserves a place among the great explorers, relying on an uncanny talent for making shots everyone else calls impossible—bouncing arrows off stone, threading gaps she cannot see, striking targets around corners. But when her small expedition uncovers something vast and ancient beneath the desert, her gift begins escalating beyond reason, until distance, walls, dimensions, and eventually reality itself seem to become nothing more than obstacles her arrows can find a way around.
