# Outline

## Story

- Premise and central promise: Young relic hunter Ivara enters the forbidden Azhra Labyrinth with Tressa and Mazin to join renowned explorers. Her trick shooting wakes a city-sized route lattice. Escalate from ricochet and blind gap to sealed chambers, incompatible spaces, and an arrow leaving available reality to strike the labyrinth's unreachable exterior.
- Focal pressure or attachment: Ivara needs Tressa's record to make her talent count. She treats retreat as disbelief and resists admitting that her greatest target must be assembled from her companions' perceptions.
- Counterforce or complication: Each shot teaches the unstable lattice another route while degrading Ivara's direction sense. Water and arrows are finite; Tressa rejects guesses, while Mazin wants survival and payment, not greatness.
- POV, distance, and information limit: Close third-person past through Ivara, rendering trajectory through draw weight, stone grain, wind, and impact. The builders, purpose, and nature of apparent dimensions remain unknown.
- Governing movement and time shape: One descent and reversal, afternoon to underground night to dawn. Each shot changes geometry and trust. The buried climax targets the structure's outside; a surface aftermath resolves proof without institutional judgment.
- Speculative rule or ordinary-world constraint: Ivara begins with uncanny living calculation through ordinary arrows. The nonliving lattice focuses a released arrow toward her held target but cannot choose or act alone. Routes linger and combine across incompatible enclosures; accumulation leaks matter, disorients her, and destabilizes the maze. Hitting the exterior shut-stone collapses them, and the effect stays at Azhra.
- Dialogue promise: Teasing, evidentiary squabbles, bad spatial descriptions, embarrassed fear, clipped calls, and listening that matters more than certainty.
- Dialogic medium: Speech, shouts through walls, ledger diagrams, chalk, rope tugs, hand angles, taps, interrupted replies, and arrow impacts.
- Dialogue engine: Ivara needs described targets and witnesses; Tressa needs observable results; Mazin notices heat, dust, and slope. Incomplete calls alter Ivara's target, changing routes, danger, credit, and access.

## Voice

- Narrative texture: Follow Ivara's tactile geometry, noticing usable edges before scenery; sun, sand, cloth, breath, bow tension, and impact keep impossibility physical. Syntax may double back as topology breaks, but causality stays clear.
- Conversational texture: Talk begins needling and ordinary, arguing over credit, water, and wording. Underground it fragments into shouted corrections, repeated landmarks, poor gestures, and practical interruption.
- Rhetorical ownership: Ivara owns intuitive comparisons, defensive humor, and evasive boasts; Tressa owns evidence, dry authority, misreading of performance, and belated willingness to listen; Mazin owns complaints, accidental jokes, irritated affection, and first admission of confusion.
- Pressure behavior: Ivara outruns qualifiers; Tressa over-specifies until separation strips her language down; Mazin repeats sensory facts, loses sequence, then falls silent. Coordination requires pauses and correction, not shared eloquence.
- Relationship movement: Ivara wants Tressa's endorsement and Mazin's loyalty but cannot request trust. Tressa wants shared credit but cannot ask without seeming to claim Ivara's feat. Mazin wants them to choose him over the find but fears sounding cowardly. Exchanges shift leverage from performer to witnesses.
- Anti-default: Avoid interchangeable archaeologists calmly explaining a device. Keep pettiness, bad explanation, distraction, physical comedy, and disputed observation; coordinated attention should land through action, not a teamwork speech.

## Beats

1. In a sandstone slot, brown-haired Ivara braces in weathered green-and-brown layers and draws at close quarters. She ricochets an arrow around a lip to trip the entrance latch. Tressa records what she saw; Mazin questions whether they can close it.
2. Ivara threads a blind gap to release a shutter. Below, immense rings continue beyond lamplight. Her first around-corner shot returns through a sealed wall; the lattice answers with a maze-wide vibration. Tressa urges retreat, but Ivara wants an undeniable find.
3. Another arrow emerges in an inaccessible chamber, leaving cold air and sand flowing between spaces. Mapped turns disagree, and Mazin becomes stranded across a partition. Excitement turns into blame over whether Ivara's proof causes the danger.
4. Voices, taps, ledger marks, and poor descriptions supply partial targets. Arrows cross rooms with different dust, gravity, or momentary skies. A shot releases Mazin but multiplies routes and erodes Ivara's distance sense; scarcity prevents casual experiments.
5. At the central hollow, inward-curving corridors and reliefs suggest a shut-stone on the lattice's exterior. Tressa supplies orientation, Mazin the desert-facing stone's remembered heat and texture. Ivara must hold their composite description while the argument over credit remains unresolved.
6. Ivara fires at the outside of the structure enclosing them. The arrow passes wall, distance, incompatible space, and available reality. Impact arrives from every direction; routes collapse, ordinary weight returns, and a sand-choked stair reconnects. Their climb remains strenuous, not triumphal.
7. At dawn, Tressa and Mazin dispute the record. The arrow drops from an unwatched direction, dusted with outer stone. Ivara leaves it until both witnesses see its landing. Relic and ledger support her claim; admission remains unseen.
