## Constraints

- Target length: 2,500–4,000 words
- POV, tense, and distance: derive from what the story should reveal, distort,
  conceal, or make intimate unless specified
- Tone and audience: adult R+ crime noir; hard profanity, moral darkness, and
  story-bearing graphic violence and injury with visible consequences.
- Content notes: imprisonment, organized crime, aging, coercion, violence, and
  death. Criminal methods remain non-instructional; sexual intimacy, if any,
  fades to black.
- Required: a complete story that resolves its central promise
- Required continuation: Elspeth from `stories/empire-within/story.md`, titled
  *Nobody Spends Her Name*. Preserve her established identity and the prior
  events while telling a new story after her capture and almost sixty years
  of imprisonment. The prior package remains unchanged.
- Continuity: Elspeth was twenty-three in the earlier story; phones, cars, and
  earpieces already existed. Her unfamiliar modern world must plausibly follow
  that starting point. Ordinary aging and isolation need credible treatment.
- Craft profile: prospective-2026-08-23
- Avoid: reducing the mafia premise to better management or a moral lesson;
  treating her as unfamiliar with technology already present in the first story.
