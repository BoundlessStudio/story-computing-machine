# Provisional direction

A singer's spirit heart has become a piece of the night sky. People gather under it, point out their own colours, and tell stories about what her songs helped them survive. Their love is real; the intimacy they claim with her is not. She has begun looking up for the small yellow of her sister, with whom she has barely spoken since her first hit included a few seconds of the sister's voice from a childhood kitchen recording: an ordinary moment of the sister helping her sing. Fans turned the unknown girl on the record into a character who belonged to them too.

On the night of an anniversary broadcast, the singer has brought the full recording, intending to let the audience hear her own unguarded voice from before fame reshaped it. The tape also contains much more of her sister than the hit did. Her sister arrives to ask her not to play it, release more of her voice, or keep retelling that childhood care as the singer's public origin story. The singer wants the one public chance to offer something of herself that was not designed for strangers. Refusing costs her that hope, while the industry inconvenience stays in the background. In their encounter, she keeps consulting the sky for evidence that the sister still loves her. The sister catches the glance before the singer answers. Their relation does not fit in the visible colour; the singer must attend to the particular request in front of her.

Before the sister's visit, a fan tells the singer, truthfully, what the song meant to them; that encounter complicates her decision without becoming a second ending. Afterward, she stops the new use of the recording. The original hit and its immense heart cannot be recalled. A possible ending stays with a small, incomplete exchange with the sister, in which the singer attends to what is actually said instead of what can be seen above them.

The image's lone figure and immense painted heart guide the scale and texture, but the story should let the sky be beautiful as well as burdensome. The draft may find a more particular form or ending than this plan.
