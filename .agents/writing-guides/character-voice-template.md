# Character voice template

<!-- Planning aid for fictional dialogue. This file does not establish canon. -->

Use one compact card for each major speaker before drafting. Put the useful
answers in the story-specific style profile in `02-story-plan.md`; do not create
a new production artifact or complete fields that do not distinguish the
speaker.

Voice is conversational behavior, not a collection of catchphrases. Derive the
card from the character's history, work, culture, relationships, immediate
safety, and story pressure.

### Conversational logic

- **Usually wants from an exchange:** Information, compliance, reassurance,
  admiration, distance, forgiveness, time, control, connection, or something
  more specific.
- **Refuses to say directly:**
- **Assumes without explaining:**
- **Default tactic:** Joke, challenge, appease, lecture, bargain, deflect,
  confess, ask, withdraw, change the subject, or a character-specific action.
- **Tactic after resistance:** What changes when the first approach fails.
- **Conversational failure mode:** How this person mishears, overexplains,
  evades, provokes, freezes, concedes too quickly, or answers the wrong need.

### Sound on the page

- **Pacing and sentence shape:** Terse or expansive; fragments or complete
  clauses; linear or parenthetical; quick reply or considered delay.
- **Diction and image sources:** Words supplied by work, region, generation,
  faith, hobby, class, household, or private history.
- **Precision:** What they name exactly, and what they keep vague.
- **Humor:** Whether they joke, what kind, with whom, and what happens when a
  joke fails.
- **Silence and interruption:** What makes them stop, overlap, repeat, or wait.
- **Never by default:** A word, construction, disclosure, or polished insight
  this character would resist even when it is true.

## Plan compression

The plan does not need this entire worksheet. For each major speaker, retain the
few constraints that will change actual lines:

> **Name — dialogue profile:** default want and tactic; sentence shape and
> vocabulary source; avoided truth; pressure shift; relationship-specific
> exception; one thing the speaker would not naturally say.

For every dialogue-bearing scene, also state the incompatible conversational
aims and at least one likely tactic change. Do not prewrite signature lines for
the prose writer to reproduce.
