# Writing guides

<!-- Repository-level production guidance. These files do not establish canon. -->

This package turns the universe's broad prose defaults into practical craft
guidance without expanding `universe/style-guide.md` into a drafting manual.
Use the smallest relevant guide while planning, drafting, editing, or reviewing
a story:

- `writing-guide.md` — scene movement, viewpoint, description, exposition,
  rhythm, imagery, pacing, openings, endings, and restraint.
- `voice-guide.md` — the shared way of looking, story-specific narrative voice,
  distance, emotional register, wit, lyricism, and tonal range.
- `dialogue-guide.md` — speaker differentiation, subtext, conversational
  conflict, ethical and technical language, banter, tags, beats, and speech
  texture.
- `character-voice-template.md` — a compact planning card for deriving each
  major speaker's conversational logic from background, relationship, and
  pressure.
- `dialogue-review-checklist.md` — an operational scene and manuscript pass for
  speaker distinction, competing intentions, subtext, exposition, banter, and
  earned conversational closure.

## Precedence and authority

Current canon and the binding constraints in `universe/style-guide.md` always
apply. Within those boundaries, use this order for story-specific craft
decisions:

1. The user's explicit request, supplied prose sample, and captured controls in
   `00-prompt.md`.
2. Deliberate style decisions recorded in `02-story-plan.md`.
3. These shared craft guides.

The guides are defaults, not a reason to flatten every story into one narrator.
A specific, intentional effect may depart from them when the prompt and plan
support it. They are repository-level agent instructions, not in-world facts,
canon authority, or guarded story handoff inputs.

## Stage use

- **Planning:** record a compact story style profile: dominant voice qualities,
  narrative distance, dialogue pressures, image sources, and any intentional
  departures from the defaults. Use `character-voice-template.md` for major
  speakers and compress the resulting cards into the story plan.
- **Drafting and final editing:** read the three craft guides and the planned
  voice cards. Use `dialogue-review-checklist.md` as a revision lens, not as a
  visible formula in the prose.
- **Review:** flag a craft issue when it is repeated or materially weakens
  character distinction, scene movement, clarity, emotional force, or prompt
  fulfillment. Use `dialogue-review-checklist.md`, but do not fail a story for
  one isolated construction that works in context.
