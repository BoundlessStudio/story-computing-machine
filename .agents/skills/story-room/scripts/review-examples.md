# Diagnostic examples

These two teaching stories are excluded from withheld calibration. Examples
teach a reading operation, not a requirement to find the same defect elsewhere.
Never copy their finding into a target without target evidence.

## State across distance — The Inheritance Remembers

A passenger slaps the paint rig's dashboard while the other driver's voice
comes through its speaker. Later she takes a flashlight from that other driver
inside his van and eventually steps down from the van. Reconstruct her last
established vehicle and locate the transfer. An unshown transfer needed to make
the handoff possible is a specific continuity concern. Quote both establishing
and conflicting passages, and name the missing transition.

Preserve: an explicitly shown transfer at a stop resolves this concern. Do not
require narration of every routine movement, or infer a failure merely because
an object is used again later.

## Completed state versus next action — The Mercy Circuit

The test is still running when an inspector arrives. The technician says
"Already local-zeroed." The inspector says "Say when." Only at "Now" does the
glyph vanish. Determine whether local zero and cancelling authorization are
established as different operations. If the prose establishes no distinction,
the completed-state claim and subsequent shutdown conflict. If it does establish
that distinction, preserve the exchange and cite it. Do not invent separate
circuits merely to make the lines fit.

Preserve: "Whose coat?" / "Mine." is ordinary shifting speaker reference. A
pronoun is not broken merely because it refers to different people in different
mouths. A finding needs a real ambiguity or contradiction in context.

## Patterns need more than a tally

For voice, compare each speaker with their own earlier speech before comparing
speakers with each other. Two short replies do not establish convergence; show
how a consequential exchange loses previously established differences in
attention, reasoning or response to pressure. A deliberately synchronized joke
can work. For endings, identify the meaning already conveyed and what a later
line adds. An emotional response, changed perspective or purposeful refrain is
not automatically redundant. Keep evidence and preference separate.
