# Short-story writing

Use for the prose pass or one finding-driven revision of a current four-file
story. The prompt is authoritative. The outline is useful design intent, not a
contract; depart from it when the story improves without breaking the prompt or
continuity.

Write only `stories/<slug>/story.md`. Default to 2,500–4,000 words unless the
prompt says otherwise. Apply `universe/style-guide.md` and the continuity
boundaries already distilled into the outline. Use targeted authority searches
only when the outline exposes a genuine unknown.

## Story movement

- Put a specific person under immediate pressure. A premise becomes a story
  when it forces choices with consequences.
- Give each scene a concrete want, resistance, and change in knowledge,
  leverage, commitment, danger, relationship, or available choices.
- Earn causal transitions. Compress travel, repetition, routine, and background
  unless their accumulation is the point.
- Resolve the prompt's central promise. End on the last meaningful action,
  decision, or changed image rather than a summary of the theme.

## Viewpoint and world

- Filter detail through the viewpoint character's work, history, needs, and
  blind spots. Keep narrative distance deliberate and avoid unmarked viewpoint
  drift.
- Prefer concrete nouns, active verbs, material consequences, and details that
  affect action or reveal the observer.
- Introduce speculative systems through use, cost, disagreement, maintenance,
  or aftermath. Canon is a constraint, not an exposition quota.
- Trust behavior and consequence to carry emotion and ethics when explanation
  would flatten them.

## Voice and dialogue

- Derive voice from the prompt, genre, viewpoint, and social world rather than
  applying one shared narrator to every story.
- Give important speakers incompatible conversational aims and distinct tactics,
  assumptions, diction sources, and failure modes. Let tactics change under
  resistance.
- Use dialogue as action, not as a relay for lore or project policy. Preserve
  subtext, imperfect listening, silence, and directness when each is earned.
- Ration polished banter, aphorisms, generic gestures, familiar synthetic
  metaphors, one-line emphasis, and em dashes.

## Final pass

Before returning, check prompt fulfillment, causal clarity, viewpoint, repeated
cadences, speaker distinction, spatial clarity, setup/payoff, and the ending.
In revision mode, fix only assigned blocking findings; do not restart the story,
rewrite the prompt or outline, or create a draft/final duplicate.
