# Short-story writing

Use for one WRITE, blocking-finding REVISE, or explicitly authorized BUNDLE EDIT
assignment. Before acting, read [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md#canon-lock-and-story-editing)
for editability, scope, and worktree rules. Write only the assigned current
`story.md` or bundle `05-story.md`, preserving frontmatter and layout.
A replacement arrives as a clean CREATE scaffold; never retrieve removed
creative artifacts. Default to 2,500–4,000 words unless the prompt says otherwise.

## Inputs

Read the complete recorded prompt and
[universe README](../../../universe/README.md), then the
[style guide](../../../universe/style-guide.md#story-craft-defaults).
The prompt is acceptance authority; historical amendments remain context and
the last recorded craft profile applies. For current format, read the outline
and any profile-required Voice capsule as drafting intent, not prose to imitate.
For BUNDLE EDIT, use existing inputs and only the authorized change context;
no current-format outline or Voice capsule is required. Never open previous
stories or Voice capsules; collection comparison belongs to the reviewer.

Use `creative-writing-craft` as the primary reference: prose and scene resources
plus at most one materially relevant genre resource. Use `dialogue` during
meaningful dialogue revision and `prose-style` when language needs diagnosis.
These references do not introduce stages: ignore their diagnostic-only refusals,
optional scripts, user-question/output-directory instructions, separate reports,
and persistence. Generic mandates for hidden agendas, verbal tics, compulsory
subtext, simultaneous dialogue functions, punchy sentences, or isolated
paragraphs do not override prompt, policy, assignment, or this adapter.

## Draft and revise

Choose the actual generating force, focal attachment, viewpoint, time shape,
and information order using the style guide. Write the complete story directly.
Let discoveries supersede advisory outline choices when prompt and continuity
remain sound. Use the Voice capsule as a story-specific constraint, not a
source of catchphrases, accents, cosmetic differences, or compulsory conflict.

During dialogue drafting, fix the observable event, identify what the speaker
notices and wants, choose the literal or legibly marked mode, write the line,
and establish what the listener can recover before writing the response.
Never draft a punchline, correction, aphorism, or retort first and manufacture
a setup. Apply the style guide's semantic dialogue gate before rhetorical
polish; when one word seems wrong, recheck the exchange instead of merely
substituting a synonym. Meaningful non-spoken contact is dialogue too.

After complete prose exists, revise that same file at four scales:

1. **Whole story:** prompt promise, actual generating force, focal attachment,
   principal agency, and an ending grown from the whole.
2. **Movement and information:** cause, chronology, space, operative limits,
   knowledge, omissions, repetition, and transitions. Remove explanations that
   repeat what action and reaction already establish.
3. **Dialogue scenes:** apply the active policy's coherence gate first, then
   Voice intent, speaker distinction, reasoning/relationship movement, and
   decisive/final exchanges. Preserve the policy's distinct line-specific
   coherence and scene-wide convergence thresholds. Evaluate proposed repairs
   against the exchange's action and narration, not just isolated lines.
4. **Language and sound:** viewpoint filtering, paragraph movement, sentence
   rhythm, specificity, imagery, and speaker-owned language. Repair generic
   gestures, artificial metaphors, accidental echoes, and mechanical emphasis.

These are in-place craft judgments, not quotas, automatic bans, or saved passes.
In REVISE mode, address only assigned blocking findings and indispensable
surrounding action/narration. For BUNDLE EDIT, make only the authorized localized
change and its necessary seams; do not modernize unrelated prose or migrate
the package. Do not edit outlines, reviews, canon, or other story files.
Return a one-sentence change report after saving; create no draft copy,
checklist, craft report, or additional artifact.
