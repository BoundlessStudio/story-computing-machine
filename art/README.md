# Preserved story art

These are preserved artworks rescued from the retired Pages snapshot. The site had the only surviving full-size copy of 753 character sheets; they now live at `characters/<story-slug>/<character-id>.webp`. Its small thumbnails were browsing copies and were removed. Selected replacement paintings that existed only in Pages now live at `landscapes/<story-slug>/<image-id>.png` (36) and `interiors/<story-slug>/<image-id>.png` (105). Other landscape and interior source paintings remain in their story packages; some were excluded from the selected collection. `selection-notes.json` keeps the concise exclusion reasons and links each corrected painting to the source it replaced, without hash or capture machinery. The filenames identify the story and subject; text on a character sheet provides its displayed name.

The user's later collection direction for character and location art is Canadian Group of Seven inspired oil painting: broad, rhythmic colour and value shapes; varied directional opaque strokes; quieter connecting passages; selective substantial impasto; simplified figures and architecture; and story-specific natural or otherwise motivated light. Keep shadows connected and allow darkness and lost edges. Avoid glossy faces or lenses, uniform bevelled strokes, theatrical rim light, and automatic orange-blue contrast. Use actual paintings, such as `stories/a-beginner-of-considerable-age/art/landscapes/01-apple-orchard-hillside.png` and `stories/friends-of-the-night/art/landscapes/05-the-stream-after-ash.png`, as visual references when this collection style is requested. Story evidence controls identity, clothing, setting, and light.

These pictures are interpretations, not shared-universe authority. New `[WP]` art belongs with the story that prompted it; no gallery capture or image inventory is required.
