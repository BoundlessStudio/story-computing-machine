# Story art and selection notes

Art belongs in its story directory. Covers are `stories/<slug>/title-image.jpg`; supplied original images retain their names under `stories/<slug>/art/references/`; selected character sheets are under `art/characters/`; and original location paintings remain under `art/landscapes/` and `art/interiors/`. Selected corrected location paintings are separate files under the relevant story's `art/landscapes/selected/` or `art/interiors/selected/`. `selection-notes.json` records which painting was excluded or corrected and points to both original and selected files. These notes are provenance for choosing an image, not another publication process.

The 34 canon-locked stories cannot receive new package files under the current [story rules](../AGENTS.md). Their 162 rescued images remain here under `characters/`, `landscapes/`, and `interiors/` until the user explicitly rules on art-only additions. The reference image named in `stories/a-seat-for-the-sun/prompt.md` has not yet been recovered; do not substitute a generated image for it.

The user's later collection direction for character and location art is Canadian Group of Seven inspired oil painting: broad, rhythmic colour and value shapes; varied directional opaque strokes; quieter connecting passages; selective substantial impasto; simplified figures and architecture; and story-specific natural or otherwise motivated light. Keep shadows connected and allow darkness and lost edges. Avoid glossy faces or lenses, uniform bevelled strokes, theatrical rim light, and automatic orange-blue contrast. Use actual paintings, such as `stories/a-beginner-of-considerable-age/art/landscapes/01-apple-orchard-hillside.png` and `stories/friends-of-the-night/art/landscapes/05-the-stream-after-ash.png`, as visual references when this collection style is requested. Story evidence controls identity, clothing, setting, and light.

These pictures are interpretations, not shared-universe authority. New `[WP]` art belongs with the story that prompted it; no gallery capture or image inventory is required.
